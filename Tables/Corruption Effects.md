|   6   | **Complete Corruption.** The creature dies and becomes one with the pleurossa. The pleurossa network gains a number of Hit Points equal to a roll of the creature's Hit Dice. Alternatively, if the creature is a floral dragon, it becomes a corrupted floral dragon as detailed in the Corrupted Floral Dragon Template. |
