#### Flense
*Level 8 Necromancy*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a scalpel)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
You target a creature you can see within range, using necromantic force to slice the skin from its body. Make a ranged spell attack against that creature. On a hit, the target takes 8d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. On each of your turns until the spell ends, you can take a Magic action to force the same target to make a Constitution saving throw, taking 8d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. The spell ends if the target is ever outside the spell's range, if the target is reduced to 0 Hit Points, or if you can't see it.

**Classes:** Cleric, Wizard
