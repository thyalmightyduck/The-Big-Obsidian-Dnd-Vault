#### Ode to Wrath
*Level 4 Enchantment*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
You begin a poetic chant that is magically accompanied by a trio of pounding drums beating an angry rhythm. For the duration, an aura radiates from you in a 30-foot Emanation. While in the aura, you and your allies each add your spellcasting ability modifier to damage rolls made with melee weapons.

**Classes:** Bard
