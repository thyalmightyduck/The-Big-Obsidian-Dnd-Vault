#### Fist of the Frost Jarl
*Level 4 Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of water melted from an icicle)
- **Duration:** Special
---
A fist of solid ice filling a 5-foot Cube hurtles from you in a direction you choose in a 120-foot Line. Each creature in the Line makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d8 Cold damage. On a successful save, a creature takes half damage.

You may choose for one creature of Huge size or smaller that fails its save to have the Grappled condition, which has an escape DC equal to your spell save DC. A creature that ends its turn Grappled by the fist takes 2d8 Cold damage. When a creature escapes the grapple, the fist shatters.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 4th. If cast using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the spell ignores Resistance to Cold damage.

**Classes:** Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
