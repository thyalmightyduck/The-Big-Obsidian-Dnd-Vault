#### Skaldic Scolding
*Level 4 Enchantment*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take in response to taking damage from a creature that you can see within 30 feet of yourself
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
The creature that damaged you is bombarded with a barrage of scathing insults and must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 4d10 Psychic damage and has the Incapacitated condition until the start of your next turn. If the creature fails the save by 5 or more, it has the Stunned condition instead. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell level above 4th.

**Classes:** Bard
