#### Storm Maiden's Edge
*Level 4 Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute
---
You touch a weapon and imbue it with the power of the storm. Until the spell ends, the chosen weapon deals an extra 2d8 Lightning damage on a hit.

A creature hit by the weapon must make a Strength saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On a failed save, the target is pushed back 10 feet. If the target fails the Strength save by 5 or more, it has the Prone condition.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The amount of extra Lightning damage dealt by the weapon increases by 1d8 for every two spell slot levels above 3rd.

**Classes:** Paladin, Ranger
