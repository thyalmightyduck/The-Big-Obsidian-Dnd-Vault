#### Angrboda's Fury
*Level 4 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
When you cast this spell, you open yourself up to become a conduit of the rage of the giant goddess **Angrboda**. One creature within range makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target becomes Vulnerable to one type of damage of your choice. If the creature has Immunity to the damage type you selected, it loses its Immunity and takes damage as normal, but it does not have Vulnerability to the damage type you chose.

The creature can repeat the Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending this spell on a success.

**Classes:** Cleric, Sorcerer, Wizard
