#### Regenerative Hull
*Level 5 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
You touch a vehicle. At the start of each of your turns, the vehicle regains a number of Hit Points equal to twice your spellcasting modifier. A vehicle must have 1 HP or more to be affected.

**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
