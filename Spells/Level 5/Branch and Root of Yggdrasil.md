#### Branch and Root of Yggdrasil
*Level 5 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (branch, leaf, or bark from a World Tree)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
All trees are intrinsically linked to Yggdrasil. When you cast this spell and touch a tree, you and up to eight willing creatures in contact with that tree are transported to a random location on Yggdrasil. If you are touching a World Tree found on the mortal plane (such as those found in the Northlands) when you cast the spell, you can specify a target destination on Yggdrasil in general terms, and you appear in or near that destination, as determined by the GM.

Using this magic requires a sacrifice from the caster. After casting this spell, your Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to a roll of one of your Hit Dice until you complete a Long Rest.

**Classes:** Druid
