#### Bound Fortunes
*Level 5 Necromancy*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (two small spindles bound together with thread)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
---
You touch another willing creature and bind your fates together until the spell ends. For the duration, if the target is within 60 feet of you and makes an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw, you may use your Reaction to allow the target to reroll and use the new result. The target can do the same to you. Each time you do this, your, and only your Hit Point maximum is reduced by 1d4, and the same is true of the target. If this reduces your Hit Point maximum to 0, you die. This reduction is removed when the spell ends.

**Classes:** Cleric
