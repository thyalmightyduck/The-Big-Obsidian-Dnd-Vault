#### Arcane Invigoration
*Level 2 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You sacrifice your life force to weave a mystic defense. Roll one or two of your unexpended Hit Point Dice, and gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to the roll's total plus your spellcasting ability modifier. Those dice are then expended.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The number of unexpended Hit Point Dice you can roll increases by one for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes:** Magus
