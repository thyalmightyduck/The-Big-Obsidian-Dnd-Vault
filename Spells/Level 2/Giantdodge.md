#### Giantdodge
*Level 2 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** 1 minute
---
Increase your Speed by 20 feet. You gain a +2 Bonus to your AC against attacks made by Large or larger creatures, and you do not provoke Opportunity Attacks from them.

**Classes:** Ranger
