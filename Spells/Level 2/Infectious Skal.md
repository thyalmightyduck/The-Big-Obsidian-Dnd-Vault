#### Infectious Skal
*Level 2 Enchantment*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
You sing a boisterous drinking song that compels others to stop what they are doing and sing along. A creature that can hear you within the spell's range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the Charmed condition until the spell ends. While the creature is Charmed, it is Friendly toward you, and it takes no actions other than to sing along with the song. For the duration of the song, you may use a Bonus Action to target a new creature that is within 30 feet of you or of another creature singing the song. You can affect a total number of creatures equal to your Spellcasting ability modifier at any one time, adding them progressively. A targeted creature must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become Charmed by you and begin singing along. If any of the singers other than you is attacked during the song, the song stops with a loud discordant note, and the spell ends for everyone affected.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** If you cast this spell with a 3rd or higher-level spell slot, you can affect 2 creatures each time you take a Bonus Action to spread the song.

**Classes:** Bard
