#### Phantasmal Feint
*Illusion Cantrip*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** S, M (a toy weapon)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You lash out with the toy used in this spell's casting. Make a melee spell attack against one creature of your choice within range. On a hit, you deal 1d6 Psychic damage and the next attack with a weapon or Unarmed Strike against the target before the start of your next turn has Advantage.
***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).

**Classes:** Bard, Magus, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
