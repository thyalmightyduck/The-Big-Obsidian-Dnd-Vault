#### Force Blast
*Evocation Cantrip*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 180 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You unleash a bolt of mystical power at a creature or an object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Force damage.
***Cantrip Upgrade.*** This damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).

**Classes:** Artificer, Sorcerer, Wizard
