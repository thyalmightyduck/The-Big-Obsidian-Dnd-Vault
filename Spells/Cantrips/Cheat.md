#### Cheat
*Divination Cantrip*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S, M (a weighted die)
- **Duration:** 1 round
---
You twist your fingers and fate seems to follow suit. Until the end of your next turn, you can reroll any ability check you make to play nonmagical games of skill and must use the new roll. Therefore, this spell could influence a game of poker, but not the result of a *Deck of Many Things*.

**Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
