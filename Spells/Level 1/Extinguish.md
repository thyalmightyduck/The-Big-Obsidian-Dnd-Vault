#### Extinguish
*Level 1 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You extinguish all magical and nonmagical flames of your choice in a 60-foot Cube originating from you. Creatures made partially or completely of fire take 1d12 Force damage.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** For each spell slot above 1st that you use to cast this spell, the cube increases by 10 feet and affected creatures take an additional 1d12 Force damage.

**Classes:** Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
