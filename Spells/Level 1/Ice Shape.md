#### Ice Shape
*Level 1 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a thread)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You touch a section of ice no more than 5 feet in any dimension and form it into any shape you like. For example, you could shape the ice into a weapon, statue, or coffer, or you could make a small passage through the ice that is 5 feet thick. You could also shape ice to seal a narrow cavern opening shut. Any object you create is crude but functional, but finer mechanical detail isn't possible.

**Classes:** Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
