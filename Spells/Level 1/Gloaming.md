#### Gloaming
*Level 1 Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, M (a piece of smoked glass)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
Magical gloom spreads from a point you choose within range to fill a 50-foot-radius sphere for the duration. The fading twilight spreads around corners. A creature with darkvision can see through this darkness, and any normal light can illuminate it. Ranged attacks made through the gloaming have disadvantage. If the point you choose is an object being worn or carried, the effect emanates from the object and moves with it. Completely covering the source of the gloaming with an opaque object, such as a bowl or a helm, blocks the darkness.

If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of light created by a spell of 2nd level or higher, the effect is dispelled.
