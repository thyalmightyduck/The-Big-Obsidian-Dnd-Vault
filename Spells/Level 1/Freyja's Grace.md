#### Freyja's Grace
*Level 1 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take after failing a saving throw.
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
Reroll the saving throw as a Charisma save, regardless of the original save type.

**Classes:** Bard, Paladin, Sorcerer, Warlock
