#### Ensorcelled Strike
*Level 1 Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
As part of casting this spell, you make an Unarmed Strike or attack with a weapon against a creature. On a hit, you deal damage as normal and the target is ensorcelled until the end of your next turn. While a creature is ensorcelled, it has Disadvantage on saving throws against spells, and spell attacks against it have Advantage. A target is no longer ensorcelled if it fails a saving throw against a spell or is hit by a spell attack.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** If the spell slot is level 2+, the attack deals an additional 1d6 Force damage for each slot level above 1.

**Classes:** Magus
