#### Speechmaster's Rune
*Level 1 Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a Tiny stone)
- **Duration:** 1 minute
---
You place a magical effect into the stone used as the spell's material component, creating a visible rune etched into the stone. Any creature holding the stone has Advantage on Charisma checks.

**Classes:** Wizard
