#### Coldheart
*Level 1 Necromancy*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You cause a supernatural chill to fall upon one Beast or Humanoid that you can see within range. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it receives 1 Exhaustion level.

At the end of its next turn, the target must make another Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature receives 1 Exhaustion level. Then the spell ends. Exhaustion applied by this spell is not removed by the end of the spell.

**Classes:** Druid
