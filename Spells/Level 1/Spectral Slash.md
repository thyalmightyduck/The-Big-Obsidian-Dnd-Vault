#### Spectral Slash
*Level 1 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** 20 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a melee weapon worth 1 SP+)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You send forth a spectral copy of yourself to strike down your foe. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within 20 feet of you. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 damage of your weapon's damage type.

You can then spend your action to move up to 20 feet in a straight line towards the target, streaking through a spectral trail, and take the Attack action against it. To use this action, you must attack with the melee weapon used for the casting of this spell.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 and the distance traveled by 10 feet for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes:** Paladin, Ranger
