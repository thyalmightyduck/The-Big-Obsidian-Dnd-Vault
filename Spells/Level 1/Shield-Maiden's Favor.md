#### Shield-Maiden's Favor
*Level 1 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when you or an ally takes damage
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
A spiritual echo of a Valhallan shield-maiden appears and interposes herself between the triggering attack and its target, then the shieldmaiden vanishes. The triggering attack's damage is reduced by 1d10 plus your spellcasting modifier.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage reduction increases by 1d10 for each spell level above 1st.

**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Paladin, Ranger
