# The Magus Class 2024 (TMC24)
#### Towerfall Tactic
*Level 1 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** [[Reaction]], which you take when you take damage from an attack
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** S, M (a Shield worth 1+ GP)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You gain 5 [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] and the creature that made the triggering attack must make a Strength [[saving throw]]. On a failure, the creature gains the [[Prone|Prone condition]] and it cannot right itself to end the condition until after the end of the current turn. These [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] vanish if any remain after 1 minute.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] increase by 5 for each spell slot level above 1.

**Feats:** [[Boon of Magic School Mastery]]
