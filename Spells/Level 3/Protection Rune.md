#### Protection Rune
*Level 3 Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute
---
You touch a creature, creating a visible rune etched into its skin. The creature has Resistance to a damage type of your choice.

**Classes:** Cleric, Sorcerer, Wizard
