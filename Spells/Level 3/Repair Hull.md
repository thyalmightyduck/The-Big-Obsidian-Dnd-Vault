#### Repair Hull
*Level 3 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (iron nails)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
A vehicle you touch regains a number of Hit Points equal to 4d12 plus your spellcasting ability modifier. The target vehicle must have 1 HP or more to be affected.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d12 for each spell level above 3rd.

**Classes:** Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
