>## Death Cap
>*Tiny Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 50 (20d4)
>- **Speed** 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___

>- **Saving Throws** Wis +2
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —

>***False Appearance.*** While a death cap remains motionless and isn't possessing a creature, the cap is indistinguishable from an ordinary object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Crush.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Bludgeoning damage, and the death cap attaches to the target. While attached, the death cap can't attack creatures other than the target, but it has Advantage on attack rolls. The death cap's Speed becomes 0, it can't benefit from any bonus to its Speed, and it moves with the target.  
>A creature can take an action to remove the attached death cap with a successful DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check. The death cap can detach itself using 5 feet of movement.  
>
>***Possession.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature the death cap is touching. *Failure:* The target is possessed by the death cap. The death cap attaches to the target, as with its Crush attack, and the target loses control of its body. The death cap retains its mental ability scores, Immunities, and senses, but otherwise uses the target's statistics, gaining access to the target's knowledge, class features, proficiencies, and languages. The death cap can possess one target at a time and can't use its Crush attack while possessing a target.  
>The possessed target deals one extra die of damage with melee weapons. At the start of its turn, the death cap can grant the target Advantage on melee attack rolls until the start of its next turn. Attack rolls against the target have Advantage for the same duration.  
>A creature can detach the death cap only if the target has the Grappled, Restrained, or Incapacitated conditions. The attempt provokes an Opportunity Attack from the target. If the death cap is detached, the possession ends.  
>Every 24 hours, the death cap drains one of the target's Hit Dice, which it regains only after the possession ends. If the target has no Hit Dice, it dies. After losing a Hit Die in this way, the target can repeat the save, ending the possession on a success. The possession also ends if the target drops to 0 Hit Points, the death cap is turned, or the death cap ends the possession as a Bonus Action. When the possession ends, the death cap falls to the ground in an unoccupied space within 5 feet and the target is immune to the death cap's Possession for 24 hours.  
>
> ##### Other Slayers
>
>While a cap appears to be the favored form of these horrid creatures, stories abound about other forms, from cloaks that seem more patient than the cap form to gauntlets or even weapons, more interested in political power than pure slaughter. These different types of death caps keep their host around for extended periods, sometimes even years, which means the death cap either doesn't feed on the host or keeps them alive with some form of necromancy.
>
>Some necromancers and priests educated in the dark arts either scoff at such tales or warn that the cap form might be a lesser form of this item-haunting undead.
