>## Reborn Vladimir von Drakken
>*Medium Undead (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 22
>- **Hit Points** 475 (50d8 + 250)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +11 (21)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|21 (+5)|21 (+5)|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|22 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +11, Int +10, Wis +9, Cha +12
>- **Skills** Athletics +12, Insight +9, Intimidation +12, Perception +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic, Poison
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 17 (XP 18,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Ascendant Transformation.*** When Vladimir von Drakken drops to 0 Hit Points, his body explodes in a shower of gore and he reappears in an unoccupied space within 10 feet as Ascendant Vladimir von Drakken. His Initiative doesn't change.  
>
>***Aura of Dread.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of Vladimir von Drakken takes 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Bloodthirsty.*** Vladimir von Drakken has Advantage on attack rolls against a target with the Bloodied or Bleeding condition.  
>
>***Daywalker.*** Vladimir von Drakken doesn't possess any Vampire Weaknesses from the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** Vladimir von Drakken regains 30 Hit Points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 Hit Point remaining.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** Vladimir von Drakken can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Vladimir von Drakken always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On his turn, Vladimir von Drakken can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Vladimir von Drakken takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Bloodmourne.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 27 (6d6 + 6) Force damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (4d4). In addition, Vladimir von Drakken can apply one of the following martial maneuvers to the target:
>- **Advance.** Vladimir von Drakken pushes the target 15 feet, then moves up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the target without provoking Opportunity Attack action.
>- **Hamstring.** The target has the Immobilized condition until the end of its next turn.
>- **Overpower.** The target has the Staggered condition until the end of its next turn.
>- **Claws.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 20 (4d6 + 6) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 20).
>
>***Bite (1/Round).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 49 (14d6) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Weakened condition (DC 20 Constitution save ends). Vladimir von Drakken regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt. A Humanoid killed by this damage rises on its next turn as a Vampire Spawn under Vladimir von Drakken's control.  
>
>***Dominate (1/Round).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature Vladimir von Drakken can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition until the end of its next turn. While Charmed, the target obeys Vladimir von Drakken's commands and can't take Reactions. Failure by 5 or More: The Charmed condition lasts 24 hours or until Vladimir von Drakken is reduced to 0 Hit Points. Each time the target takes damage, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. *Success:* The target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll against Vladimir von Drakken.  
>
>***Wing Blast (1/Round).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 15-foot Emanation originating from Vladimir von Drakken. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away from Vladimir von Drakken and has the Prone condition. *Failure or Success:* Before or after using this Epic Action, Vladimir von Drakken can move up to half his Fly Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Shadow Step (2/Round).*** Vladimir von Drakken teleports to an unoccupied space he can see within 60 feet that is in Dim Light or Darkness. He has Advantage on the next melee attack he makes before the end of this round.  
>
>***Shape-Shift (1/Round).*** Vladimir von Drakken shape-shifts into a Tiny bat (Speed 5 ft., Fly Speed 30 ft.) or a Medium cloud of mist (Speed 5 ft., Fly Speed 20 ft. [hover]), or he returns to his vampire form. Anything he is wearing transforms with him.  
>While in bat or mist form, Vladimir von Drakken can't speak. His game statistics, other than his size and Speed, are unchanged, but he can't use Epic Actions other than Shape-Shift and Epic Resistance. While in mist form, Vladimir von Drakken can't speak or manipulate objects, and loses his Aura of Dread. He is weightless and can enter an enemy's space and stop there. If air can pass through a space, the mist can do so, but it can't pass through liquid. He has Resistance to all damage.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Vladimir von Drakken chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on him. He can use this action even if he would otherwise be unable to take actions, his actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters his game statistics.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Spell-Reflecting Strike.*** *Trigger:* Vladimir von Drakken makes a successful saving throw against a spell, or a spell attack misses him. *Response:* Vladimir von Drakken can choose another creature (including the spellcaster) he can see within 120 feet of him. The spell targets the chosen creature instead of Vladimir von Drakken. If the spell forces a saving throw, the chosen creature makes its own save. If the spell was an attack, the attack roll is rerolled against the chosen creature.
