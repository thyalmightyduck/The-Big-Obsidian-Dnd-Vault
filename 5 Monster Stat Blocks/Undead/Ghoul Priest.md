>## Ghoul Priest
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 52 (7d8 + 21)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Chain Shirt*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Deathly Teeth.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 25 (4d10 + 3) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the start of the ghoul priest's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Reanimate.*** The ghoul priest chooses the corpse of a ghoul it can see within 60 feet. The ghoul returns to life with 5 Hit Points and stands up.
