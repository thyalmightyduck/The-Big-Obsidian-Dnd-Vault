___
>## Mud Zombie
>*Large Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d10 + 16)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|3 (-4)|8 (-1)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +1
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the mud zombie remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an oozing pile of corpse parts.  
>
>***Formless.*** The mud zombie can move through an opening large enough for a Small creature without squeezing.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The mud zombie can grapple up to two creatures at a time.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Split.*** *Trigger:* The mud zombie is Medium or larger, has at least 10 Hit Points, and takes 10 damage at once. *Response:* The mud zombie splits into two new mud zombies. Each new zombie has Hit Points equal to half the original zombie's (round down). New zombies are one size smaller than the original zombie.
