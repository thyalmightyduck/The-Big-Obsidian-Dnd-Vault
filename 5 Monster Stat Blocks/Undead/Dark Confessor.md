>## Dark Confessor
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 153 (18d8 + 72)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Wis +8
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Insight +8, Persuasion +6, Religion +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Poison
>- **Gear** *Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The vampire regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes three attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Bite, Unholy Light, or Spellcasting.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15).  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The vampire casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17, +9 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Guidance*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Bane*, *Darkness*, *Disguise Self*  
>1/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Spirit Guardians*  
>
>***Spiritual Maul.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Force damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away from the vampire.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 12 (3d6 + 2) Necrotic damage, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>***Unholy Light (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 10-foot radius, 40-foot high Cylinder centered on a point the vampire can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage plus 14 (4d6) Fire damage, and the target has the Staggered condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Blood Rebuke.*** *Trigger:* A creature the vampire can see within 60 feet of it deals damage to it. *Response—**Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (4d4). *Success:* Half damage only.
