>## Living Nightmare
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 50 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands all languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The living nightmare can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The living nightmare makes two Nightmare Strikes.  
>
>***Nightmare Strike.*** Melee or Ranged Spell Attack:  
>+5, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of the living nightmare's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The living nightmare casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Minor Illusion*, *Silent Image*  
>3/day each: *Major Image*, *Phantasmal Force*  
>1/day each: *Confusion*, *Phantasmal Killer*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Nimble Escape.*** The living dream takes the Disengage or Hide action.
