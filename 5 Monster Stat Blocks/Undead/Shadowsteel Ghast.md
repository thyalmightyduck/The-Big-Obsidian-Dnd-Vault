___
>## Shadowsteel Ghast
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 118 (17d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +5, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Two languages known in life
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The ghast has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Shadowsteel Coat.*** The ghast is coated in metal armor made of Shadowsteel.  
>
>***Shadowsteel Miasma.*** Any creature of the ghast's choice that starts its turn within 30 feet of the ghast must roll 1d4 and subtract the result from any attack roll or saving throw it makes until the start of its next turn. Only one miasma can affect a given creature at a time. A creature that rolls a natural 20 on any attack roll or saving throw while in a Shadowsteel Miasma is immune to the miasma of any shadowsteel ghast for 1 hour.  
>
>***Turning Defiance.*** The ghast and any shadowsteel ghouls within 30 feet of it have Advantage on saving throws against effects that turn undead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ghast makes two Claw attacks. It can replace one attack with Grave Surge.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. A cursed target must make an Escalation Check. If a target drops to 0 hit points due to this damage and isn't already cursed, it is afflicted with a random curse of the GM's choice from the Grim Hollow Player's Guide.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The ghast casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>2/day: *Bestow Curse*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (3/Day).*** The ghast casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Magic (5/Day).*** The ghast casts *Counterspell* or *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
