>## The Crimson Abbot
>*Large Undead (Paladin, Vampire); Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 85 (9d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Fly 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +9, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Athletics +9, Insight +7, Intimidation +7, Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Necrotic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Half Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Honor Lost.*** If a creature takes a Bonus Action to present a memento or recite a reminder of the abbot's past and succeeds on a DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check, the abbot has Disadvantage on the next D20 Test he makes before the start of the creature's next turn.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the abbot fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The abbot can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Sunlight Hypersensitivity.*** The abbot takes 20 Radiant damage if he starts his turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, he has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The abbot makes two Sanguine Blade attacks. He can replace one attack with a use of Blood Call.  
>
>***Sanguine Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d10 + 6) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Blood Call.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature other than a Construct or Undead within 30 feet. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage, and the target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by the damage taken. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The abbot regains Hit Points equal to the damage taken. A Humanoid reduced to 0 Hit Points by this damage and then buried rises the following sunset as a **Crimson Clergy** under the abbot's control.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The abbot casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Command*, *Disguise Self* (can appear as a Medium Humanoid, 24-hour duration), *Thaumaturgy*  
>1/day: *Word of Recall* (to lair only)  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* The abbot is hit by an attack roll while holding a weapon. *Response:* The abbot adds 3 to his AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Bloody Strike.** The abbot moves up to half his Speed and makes one Sanguine Blade attack.
>- **Castigate.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition and repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
  After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.
>- **Overwhelm.** The abbot casts *Command*. He can't take this action again until the start of his next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Crimson Abbot's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Fresh Blood.** Within 1 mile of the lair, spilled blood  doesn't dry out or coagulate, instead remaining fresh for 24 hours.
>- **Incinerate Heresy.** Within 1 mile of the lair, when an enemy of the abbot's takes Fire damage, that creature starts burning until the end of its next turn.
>
>If the Crimson Abbot dies or moves his lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
