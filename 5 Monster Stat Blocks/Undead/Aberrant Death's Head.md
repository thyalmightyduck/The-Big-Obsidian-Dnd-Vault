>- **Hit Points** 24 (7d4 + 7)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)

>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12

>***Flyby.*** The death's head doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.
