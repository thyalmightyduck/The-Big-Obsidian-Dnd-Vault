>## Disembodied Psyche
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 117 (18d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +8, Wis +7, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Deception +8, Insight +7, Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common; telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The psyche can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The psyche makes two Electroshock attacks. It can also use Hypnotize, if available.  
>
>***Electroshock.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 35 (10d6) Lightning damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Dazed condition until the end of the psyche's next turn.  
>
>***Hypnotize (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature of the psyche's choice in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the psyche. *Failure:* The target has the Incapacitated condition (save ends). While Incapacitated, the target has the Immobilized condition.  
>
>***Mental Possession.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the psyche can see within 5 feet. *Success:* The target is immune to this psyche's Mental Possession for 24 hours. *Failure:* The target is possessed by the psyche; the psyche disappears, and the target has the Incapacitated condition and loses control of its body. The psyche now controls the body, but the target retains awareness. The psyche can't be targeted by any attack, spell, or other effect, except ones that specifically target Undead. The psyche's game statistics are the same, except it uses the possessed target's Speed, as well as the target's Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution modifiers.  
>The possession lasts until the body drops to 0 Hit Points or the psyche leaves as a Bonus Action. Whenever the target takes damage, it repeats the save, ending the possession on a success. When the possession ends, the psyche appears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the target, and the target is immune to this psyche's Mental Possession for 24 hours.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Teleport.*** The psyche teleports to an unoccupied space within 60 feet.
