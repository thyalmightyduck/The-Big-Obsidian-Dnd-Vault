___
>## Moonshine
>*Tiny Undead, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 22 (5d4 + 10)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|1 (-5)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Detect Life (see below), Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Detect Life.*** The moonshine magically senses the direction of creatures up to 60 feet away that aren't Constructs or Undead.  
>
>***Difficult Terrain.*** It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>***Ephemeral.*** The moonshine can't wear or carry anything.  
>
>***Illumination.*** The moonshine sheds Bright Light in a 15-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 15 feet.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The moonshine can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The moonshine makes two Gloomy Touch attacks.  
>
>***Gloomy Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Siphon Life (2/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11, one creature that isn't a Construct or Undead the moonshine can see within 5 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Stunned condition until the end of the moonshine's next turn, and the moonshine or a creature of its choice within 30 feet regains 3 (1d6) Hit Points.
