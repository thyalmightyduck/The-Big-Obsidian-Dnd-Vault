>## Corpse Walker
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 82 (11d8 + 33)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Wis +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren't silvered
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Cursed Gaze.*** A Humanoid that starts its turn within 30 feet of the corpse walker is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, with Disadvantage unless it is averting its eyes from the corpse walker. On a success, it ends the effect on itself and is immune to Cursed Gaze for 24 hours.  
>If the target fails two saves, it is cursed with disturbing thoughts, and can't benefit from Short or Long Rest until the curse is removed.  
>Unless surprised, the target can avert its eyes to avoid the corpse walker's Cursed Gaze. If it does, it can't see the corpse walker until the start of its next turn. If the target looks at the corpse walker in the meantime, it is immediately subjected to Cursed Gaze.  
>
>***Sunlight Hypersensitivity.*** The corpse walker takes 20 Radiant damage when it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.  
>
>***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the corpse walker to 0 Hit Points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 plus the damage taken unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. *Success:* The corpse walker drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The corpse walker makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the corpse walker regains Hit Points equal to that amount. The reduction lasts until the target finishes a Long Rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its Hit Point maximum to 0. An Evil Humanoid slain in this way rises 24 hours later as a corpse walker.
