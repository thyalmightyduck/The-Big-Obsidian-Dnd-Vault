___
>## Pesta
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5
>- **Skills** Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Hazy Stealth.*** The pesta has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks near heat shimmers or in dusty or misty air. In these conditions, the pesta can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The pesta can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>***Plague Stench.*** A creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of the pesta is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* 5 (2d4) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage plus 9 (2d8) Poison damage.  
>
>***Plague Cloud (Recharge after a Short or Long Rest).*** The pesta creates a 20-foot radius Sphere of yellowish mist centered on a point within 60 feet. The mist spreads around corners and renders its area Lightly Obscured. The mist remains for 1 minute, until the pesta loses Concentration, or until it is dispersed by a strong wind.  
>A creature that enters the mist for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition and is infected with the pesta's disease (usually Sewer Plague). At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the Poisoned condition (but not the disease) on a successful save. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
> ##### Variant: Weeping Pesta
>
>Some pestas are the undead victims of the Weeping Pox, carrying that disease. Those diseased by such a pesta's Plague Cloud or Poisoned by its aura are infected with the Weeping Pox. A humanoid infected with this disease must repeat a DC 15 Constitution saving throw every 24 hours, increasing its Exhaustion level by one on a failure. This Exhaustion can't be reduced until the disease is cured by magic.
>
>Until the first level of Exhaustion sets in, however, the victim has sharpened senses. When making an ability check or saving throw against anything other than the disease, they can add a d4 to ability checks or saving throws. A slight fever and overexcitement are the only signs this condition comes from the Weeping Pox.
