>## Wretched Driver
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Athletics +4
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The wretched driver makes two Rapier attacks. It can replace one attack with a Cursed Shout.  
>
>***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Cursed Shout.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, two creatures within 30 feet of the wretched driver that can hear it. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the start of the wretched driver's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Angry Driving.*** The wretched driver controls a vehicle. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, each other creature on the same vehicle as the wretched driver. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.
