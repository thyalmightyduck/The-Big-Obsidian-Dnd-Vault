>## Zealot of Morrigan
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d8 + 56)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Dex +3, Con +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +11, Intimidation +4, Perception +7, Survival +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blood Frenzy.*** The vampire has Advantage on melee attack rolls against a creature with the Bloodied condition.  
>
>***Blood Shield.*** Whenever the vampire damages a creature that has blood, the vampire gains 15 Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The vampire regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes three attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Bite.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 5) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 21).  
>
>***Bite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 15 (3d6 + 5) Necrotic damage. The target's Hit Point Maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to that amount.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Morrigan's Fury (1/Short Rest).*** The vampire howls at the moon, gaining Resistance to all damage except Acid and Radiant damage for 1 minute or until it has the Incapacitated condition. As part of this Bonus Action, the vampire moves up to half its Speed toward a Hostile creature without provoking Opportunity Attack action.
