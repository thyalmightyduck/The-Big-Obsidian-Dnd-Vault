___
>## Weeping Lord
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 52 (7d8 + 21)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|17 (+3)|9 (-1)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +5, Wis +2
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands the languages it spoke in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Command Weeping Zombies.*** The weeping lord directs weeping zombies within 120 feet of it using a form of limited telepathy.  
>
>***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the weeping zombie to 0 Hit Points, it makes a Constitution saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken), unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. On a success, the weeping zombie drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>***Weeping Pox.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, a Humanoid that comes within 5 feet of the weeping zombie. *Success:* The target is immune to Weeping Pox for 24 hours. *Failure:* The target contracts Weeping Pox. The target repeats the save every 24 hours, suffering an Exhaustion level on a failure. This Exhaustion can't be removed until the disease is cured with magic.  
>Until the first Exhaustion level sets in, the target has sharpened senses. Whenever the target makes an ability check or saving throw against anything other than the disease, it rolls a d4 and adds the result to its roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is subjected to Weeping Pox.  
>
>***Grave Miasma.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 50 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage. The target is subjected to Weeping Pox and can't regain Hit Points until the start of the weeping lord's next turn.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The weeping lord casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Constitution as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Chill Touch* (5th level)  
>3/day: *Ray of Sickness*  
>1/day: *Blindness/Deafness*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Shield (3/Day).*** The weeping lord casts *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability from Innate Spellcasting.
