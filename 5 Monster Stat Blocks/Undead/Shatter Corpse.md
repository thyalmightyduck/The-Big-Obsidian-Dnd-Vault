___
>## Shatter Corpse
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 45 (6d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|3 (-4)|6 (-2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +0
>- **Immunities** Poison, Piercing; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** Understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Glass Spikes.*** A creature that makes a melee attack against the shatter corpse takes 3 Piercing damage. The creature knows this before making the attack, and can impose Disadvantage on its attack roll to avoid the damage.  
>
>***Loud.*** Creatures have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to hear the approach of a shatter corpse.  
>
>***Turn Resistance.*** The shatter corpse has Advantage on saving throws against any effect that turns undead.  
>
>***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the shatter corpse to 0 Hit Points, it must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken), unless the damage is Bludgeoning, Radiant, or from a Critical Hit. On a success, the shatter corpse drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Volley of Glass (Recharge 6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 5 (2d4) Slashing damage. *Success:* Half damage.
