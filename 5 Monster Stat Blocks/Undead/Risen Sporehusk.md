>## Risen Sporehusk
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 32 (5d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|3 (-4)|6 (-2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire, Radiant
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 15 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 7
>- **Languages** Understands Common plus one other language it knew but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The sporehusk regains 5 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the sporehusk takes Fire or Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function on the sporehusk's next turn. The sporehusk dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Sunlight Weakness.*** While in sunlight, the sporehusk has Disadvantage on D20 Tests.  
>
>### Actions
>***Grab.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 11).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. (must target a creature Grappled by the sporehusk). *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.
