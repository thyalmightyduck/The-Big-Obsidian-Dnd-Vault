>## Vessel Shard
>*Tiny Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 81 (18d4 + 36)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +6, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands one national language and three others but can't speak, telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Spirit Absorption.*** When a creature dies or is destroyed within 30 feet of the vessel shard, the vessel shard absorbs part of the departing spiritual energy, regaining 10 Hit Points or the dead creature's Hit Point maximum, whichever is less.  
>
>***Turn Resistance.*** The vessel shard has Advantage on saving throws against any effect that turns undead.  
>
>***Unhallowed Aura.*** Unless the vessel shard has the Incapacitated condition, it emits a 30-foot Emanation of desecration. Undead creatures of its choice in the Emanation roll 1d4 and add that number to attack rolls and saving throws. Halve the number of Hit Points a living creature regains while within the Emanation.  
>A Humanoid that dies in the Emanation reanimates as a **Zombie** at the start of its next turn, provided enough of its body is intact. The vessel shard controls this **Zombie**.  
>
>### Actions
>***Sepulchral Touch.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or ranged 120 ft. *Hit:* 28 (8d6)) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Restrained condition until the end of the vessel shard's next turn, and each creature of the shard vessel's choice within 5 ft. of the target takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Putrefy (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in the vessel shard's Unhallowed Aura. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage, and the target takes another 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the ongoing damage on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Shattered Spirits (1/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in the vessel shard's Unhallowed Aura. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the target is cursed. While cursed, the target can't regain Hit Points and its Hit Point maximum is reduced by the amount of Necrotic damage taken until the curse ends. It also suffers one of the following delusions (roll 1d6), or a similar delusion of the GM's choice:  
>
| 1d6 | Delusion                                                           |
|:---:|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
|  1  | Only I matter. Others are disposable.                              |
|  2  | All other creatures should serve me.                               |
|  3  | Living beings are weak and not to be trusted.                      |
|  4  | No risk is too great for my personal glory.                        |
|  5  | I must never put myself in danger of dying.                        |
|  6  | To survive, I must eat only little creatures that are still alive. |
>
>Every 24 hours, the target repeats the save, ending the curse on a success. *Failure:* The target's Hit Point maximum is further reduced by 10 (3d6). If this effect kills the target, it rises as a zombie under the vessel shard's control. The zombie approaches the vessel shard until it is in the vessel shard's Unhallowed Aura, attacking any creature that prevents it from doing so.  
>The curse can be removed by effects that turn undead or end possession. If the effect calls for a saving throw, the curse has a +4 bonus on its save. The curse can also be removed by keeping the target on hallowed ground and in Bright Light for 24 hours, but the curse compels the target to escape these conditions if possible.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Consume Spirit.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, one Undead creature the vessel shard can see within 30 feet (this is considered an effect that turns undead). *Failure:* The target loses 10 Hit Points and is destroyed if this effect reduces it to 0 Hit Points. The vessel shard regains Hit Points equal to the amount the target lost.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Arcane Shield ((Recharge 6)).*** *Trigger:* The vessel shard is hit by an attack or fails a Dexterity saving throw. *Response:* The vessel shard adds 5 to its AC against that attack or to the result of its saving throw.
