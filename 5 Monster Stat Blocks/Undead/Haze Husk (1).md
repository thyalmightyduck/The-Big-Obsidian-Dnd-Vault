>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Bludgeoning damage.

>### Reactions
>***Death Burst.*** *Trigger:* The haze husk is reduced to 0 Hit Points by damage that isn't Radiant or from a Critical Hit. *Response—**Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the haze husk. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. Failure by 5 or More: The target gains one Contamination level.
