___
>## Soman Vampire Berserker
>*Medium Undead, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +4
>- **Skills** Intimidation +5, Perception +4, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Radiant
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Gear** *Longsword*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Ostoy and one other language
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Evasion.*** If the vampire is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, the vampire instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails.  
>
>***Feeding Frenzy.*** When the vampire regains Hit Points from its Bite, it has Advantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Forbiddance.*** The vampire can enter a residence only with an invitation from one of that home's residents. If the vampire enters a house uninvited and involuntarily, it takes 1d10 Psychic damage at the end of each of its turns while it remains there.  
>
>***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the vampire has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes one Bite attack and two Longsword attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the vampire regains Hit Point equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage, or 9 (1d10 + 4) Slashing damage if wielded with two hands.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Teleport (Recharge 5–6).*** The vampire disappears in shadowy mist and teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Horrifying Revelation.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 30 feet of the vampire deals damage to it. *Response:* *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. If the target succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to this vampire's Horrifying Revelation for 24 hours.
