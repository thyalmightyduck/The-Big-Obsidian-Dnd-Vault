>## Ravenous Ghoul
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The ghoul has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the ghoul's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Rancid Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The ghoul moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see.
