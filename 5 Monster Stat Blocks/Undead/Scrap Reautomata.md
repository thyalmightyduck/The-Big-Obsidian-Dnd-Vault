>## Scrap Reautomata
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|7 (-2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** Understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Combustion.*** While the reautomata has the Burning condition, its Speed becomes 50 feet and its melee weapon attacks deal an extra 10 (3d6) Fire damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The reautomata makes two attacks.  
>
>***Rusty Weapon.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Ignition (Recharges after a Short Rest).*** The reautomata has the Burning condition (2d6 Fire).
