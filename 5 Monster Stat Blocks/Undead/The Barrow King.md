___
>## The Barrow King
>*Large Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 414 (36d10 + 216)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +16 (26)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|24 (+7)|14 (+2)|22 (+6)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|24 (+7)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Wis +11
>- **Skills** Intimidation +14, Perception +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, Stunned
>- **Gear** *Plate Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 22 (XP 41,000, or 50,000 in lair; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day, or 5/Day in Lair).*** If the king fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The king has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Marshall Undead.*** Undead creatures of the king's choice (excluding himself) in a 60-foot Emanation originating from him have Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws. He can't use this trait if he has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Name of Rest.*** The king has AC 10 against attack rolls made with a weapon inscribed with the runic form of his original name, and the king has Disadvantage on attack rolls made against a creature holding such a weapon.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The king makes three Soul Blade attacks. He can replace one attack with a use of Life Drain.  
>
>***Soul Blade.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 5 ft. or range 40/80 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d6 + 7) Slashing damage plus 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage. *Hit or Miss:* The blade magically returns to the king's hand immediately after a ranged attack.  
>
>***Life Drain.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 22, one creature within 5 feet. *Failure:* 16 (2d8 + 7) Necrotic damage, and the target's Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the damage taken.  
>A Humanoid slain by this attack rises 24 hours later as a **Wight** under the king's control, unless the Humanoid is restored to life or its body is destroyed.  
>
>***Blade Storm (Recharge 5–6).*** The king's blade flies in a deadly arc around him. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature of the king's choice in a 20-foot Emanation originating from him. *Failure:* A creature takes 35 (10d6) Force damage and has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Undying Mount (Recharge 5–6).*** The king casts *Phantom Steed*, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability. The conjured steed takes no damage from areas of effect. The spell ends early if the king casts it again.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* The king is hit by an attack roll while holding a weapon or shield. *Response:* The king adds 7 to his AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Death Grasp.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 22, one creature the king can see within 100 feet. *Failure:* A ghostly, skeletal arm pulls the target up to 40 feet closer to the king, and the target's Speed is halved until the end of its next turn.
>- **Grim Charge.** The king moves up to his Speed (or commands his mount to move up to its Speed) and makes one Soul Blade attack.
>- **Lord's Call.** The king magically summons two **Wights** into unoccupied spaces he can see within 30 feet of himself.
  The wights obey the king and take their turn immediately after him on the same Initiative count. A wight vanishes after 1 hour, when it or the king dies, or when the king uses this action again. A creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of a wight must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or have the Frightened condition until the start of its next turn. The king can't use this action again until the start of his next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Barrow King's lair is changed by his presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Empire of Desecration.** A *Hallow* spell cast within 1 mile of the lair fails, but any resources used to cast it aren't wasted.
>- **Necrotic Shroud.** When a living creature within 1 mile of the lair regains Hit Points as part of finishing a Long Rest, it can only regain up to half its Hit Point maximum.
>
>If the Barrow King dies or moves his lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
