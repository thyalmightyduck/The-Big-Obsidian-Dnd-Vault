___
>## Kindled Mouruge
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d8 + 56)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Holy Water Vulnerability.*** The kindled mouruge takes double damage from *Holy Water*.  
>
>***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the kindled mouruge to 0 Hit Points, it must make a Constitution saving throw (DC of 5 plus the damage taken) unless the damage is Radiant or Cold, or from a Critical Hit. On a successful save, the kindled mouruge drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The kindled mouruge makes two attacks, using Bite or Slam in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Soul Fire.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Soul Fire (Recharge 4–6).*** *Melee  or Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or ranged 30 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) Fire damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. If the target isn't a Construct, it ignites. Until a creature takes an action to douse the flames, the target takes 4 (1d8) Fire damage plus 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage at the end of each of its turns.
