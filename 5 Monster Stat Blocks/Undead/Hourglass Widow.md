___
>## Hourglass Widow
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 340 (40d8 + 160)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|19 (+4)|18 (+4)|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|22 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +9, Con +10, Int +10, Wis +9, Cha +11
>- **Skills** Arcana +10, Athletics +9, Insight +9, Investigation +10, Perception +15
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Force, Lightning, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 25
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 20 (XP 25,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Accelerate Through Time (2/Day).*** The widow takes an extra Action on its turn.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If the widow fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Master of Time.*** The widow chooses whether it is affected by effects that modify time or change its speed. The widow can affect creatures frozen in a *Time Stop* spell and other stasis-like effects.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The widow regains 25 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If it takes Fire or Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the widow's next turn. The widow's body is destroyed only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Turn Resistance.*** The widow has Advantage on saving throws against any effect that Turns Undead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The widow makes three attacks, using Disintegrating Touch and Withering Gaze in any combination.  
>
>***Disintegrating Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 34 (8d6 + 6) Force damage.*Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20. *Failure:* The target's Speed is reduced by 15 feet (to a minimum of 0 feet). At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. The effects of multiple Disintegrating Touches are cumulative.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The widow casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20, +12 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Eldritch Blast* (four beams), *Mage Armor** (included above), *Mage Hand*  
>3/day each: *Blight*, *Disintegrate*, *Haste*, *Slow*, *See Invisibility*  
>1/day each: *Eyebite*, *Foresight**, *Freedom of Movement*, *Power Word Stun*, *Teleport*, *Time Stop*, *Wall of Force*  
>
>*The widow casts these spells before combat.  
>
>***Withering Gaze.*** A creature within 30 feet that the widow can see takes 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage. A creature can only be affected by this attack once per round.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Expeditious Retreat.*** The widow casts *Expeditious Retreat* using the spellcasting ability in Innate Spellcasting, then takes the Dash action.  
>
>***Misty Step (3/Day).*** The widow casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability in Innate Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Shield (3/Day).*** The widow casts *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Innate Spellcasting.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the hourglass widow can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The hourglass widow regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Eldritch Blast.** The widow casts *Eldritch Blast*.
>- **Sudden Rush.** The widow teleports to an unoccupied space within 30 feet of it. It can make a Disintegrating Touch attack before or after it teleports. The widow can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Frozen in Time.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the widow. *Failure:* The target has the Paralyzed condition until the end of its next turn. *Failure or Success:* The widow can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
