>## Striga
>*Large Undead (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 57 (6d10 + 24) plus 57 (6d10 + 24) per player character
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Con +7, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Perception +7, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Blood Frenzy.*** The striga has Advantage on melee attack rolls against a creature with the Bloodied condition.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The striga is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. It may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Necrotic Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the Striga takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The striga regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the striga takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The striga can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The striga has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>### Actions
>***The striga always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the striga can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the striga takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Necrotic Bite (1/Round).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 24 (4d6 + 6) Necrotic damage. The target's Hit Point Maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the striga regains Hit Points equal to that amount.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 24 (4d6 + 6) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** The striga touches a delerium shard and regains 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the striga has used a delerium shard in this way, it can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Blood Howl (1/Round).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the striga that can hear it. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Jinxed (1d6) and Immobilized conditions (save ends both). *Success:* Half damage, and the target is immune to the striga's Blood Howl for 24 hours.  
>
>***Charge.*** The striga moves up to 40 feet in a straight line, moving through the spaces of other creatures. Each creature whose space the striga passes through is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* The target is pushed 10 feet away from the striga and has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The striga chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The striga can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Retaliation.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 15 feet of the striga deals Radiant damage to it. *Response:* The striga makes a Claws attack against that creature.
