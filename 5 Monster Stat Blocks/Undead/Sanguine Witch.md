>## Sanguine Witch
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 136 (16d8 + 64)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Deception +7, Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic, Poison, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft. (unimpeded by magical Darkness), Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The vampire regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes two attacks and uses Feast, if available. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Blood Drain.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 21 (5d6 + 4) Necrotic damage, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to the damage dealt. The target has the Weakened condition (DC 15 Constitution save ends).  
>
>***Feast (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the vampire. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage plus 14 (4d6) Psychic damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The vampire regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The vampire casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Armor* (included above)  
>2/day each: *Darkness*, *Fear*, *Hold Person*, *Inflict Wounds*, *Ray of Enfeeblement*, *Suggestion*  
>1/day: *Bestow Curse*, *Dominate Person*, *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Vampiric Touch*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Arcane Denial.*** *Trigger:* A creature the vampire can see within 60 feet casts a spell of level 3 or lower. *Response—**Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The spell fails, but no resources are wasted.
