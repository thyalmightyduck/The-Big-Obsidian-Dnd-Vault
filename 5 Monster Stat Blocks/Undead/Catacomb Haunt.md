___
>## Catacomb Haunt
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** see Elemental Weakness
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Elemental Weakness.*** The catacomb haunt has Vulnerability to one of the following damage types: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder. A creature can take the Study action to study the haunt, discovering the Vulnerability with a successful DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception) check.  
>If the haunt takes damage it has a Vulnerability to, it loses its Resistences, Immunities (except to the Poisoned condition), Incorporeal Movement trait, and Fly Speed. At the end of each of its turns, the haunt makes a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, regaining its lost abilities on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The catacomb haunt can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>***Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** The catacomb haunt deals an extra 17 (5d6) damage when it hits a target with an attack and has Advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the haunt that doesn't have the Incapacitated condition and the haunt doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Escape.*** The catacomb haunt takes the Disengage action.
