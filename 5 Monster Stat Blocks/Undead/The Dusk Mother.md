>## The Dusk Mother
>*Large Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 315 (30d10 + 150)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 50 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +16 (26)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|1 (-5)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|24 (+7)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +8, Wis +9, Cha +14
>- **Skills** Intimidation +14, Perception +9, Stealth +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Understands all but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 24 (XP 62,000, or 75,000 in lair; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Death's Shroud.*** The mother always has the Invisible condition. Blindsight, Truesight, and other effects that allow a creature to see Invisible creatures don't reveal the mother. A creature that has been reduced to 0 Hit Points at least once in its life can see her normally.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The mother can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. She takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if she ends her turn inside an object.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day, or 5/Day in Lair).*** If the mother fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The mother has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Steadfast Matron.*** Undead creatures of the mother's choice in a 60-foot Emanation originating from her (excluding herself) have Advantage on saving throws. She can't use this trait if she has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Undeniable Guilt.*** At the start of her turn, if the mother can see a possession once owned by her living children, or if she can see or hear the spirits of her children, she has the Incapacitated condition until the start of her next turn, and she must move as far from the object or spirits as possible.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mother makes three Shears attacks.  
>
>***Shears.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 25 (4d8 + 7) Force damage. If the target is a creature and is hit with two or more Shears attacks in the same turn, it gains 1 Exhaustion level.  
>
>***Mournful Tolling (1/Day).*** The mother focuses her wail into her bell. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the mother (Constructs, Undead, and creatures that have ever been raised from the dead succeed automatically). *Failure:* 77 (14d10) Psychic damage that ignores the target's *Resistance*, if any, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of the mother's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Soul Doll.*** The mother targets the corpse of a creature she killed within the last minute. The target's soul is drawn out and transforms into a ceramic doll of the creature. The doll is a Tiny object with AC 15, HP equal to half the creature's maximum Hit Points, Vulnerability to Thunder damage, and Immunity to Poison damage.  
>The soul can dimly see and hear its surroundings, and creatures with telepathy can communicate with the soul.  
>If the doll is destroyed, the soul arises from the doll as a Shadow under the mother's control. The creature can't be returned to life unless its soul is freed from the doll by a *Wish* spell, or by destroying the shadow it spawned.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Drift.** The mother moves up to her Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. When she enters a creature's space during this move, that creature takes 16 (3d10) Cold damage and has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn. A creature takes this damage only once per turn. The mother can't take this action again until the start of her next turn.
>- **Endless Keening.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature without the Deafened condition in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the mother (Constructs, Undead, and creatures that have ever been raised from the dead succeed automatically). *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Necrotic damage that ignores the target's *Resistance*, if any. Failure by 5 or More: The creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The mother can't take this action again until the start of her next turn.
>- **Snip.** The mother makes one Shears attack.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Dusk Mother's lair is changed by her presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Enduring Dirge.** Within 1 mile of the lair, creatures with an Intelligence score higher than 4 who can speak at least one language absently sing nursery rhymes or funeral songs without intending to.
>- **Thoughts of Loss.** Whenever a living creature within 1 mile of the lair finishes a Short Rest, it must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw or it gains no benefit from the rest.
>
>If the Dusk Mother dies or moves her lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
