>## Ascendant Vladimir von Drakken
>*Large Undead (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 21
>- **Hit Points** 125 (10d10 + 70) plus 125 (10d10 + 70) per player character
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +11 (21)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|27 (+8)|19 (+4)|25 (+7)|19 (+4)|19 (+4)|23 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +11, Con +14, Int +11, Wis +11, Cha +13
>- **Skills** Athletics +15, Insight +11, Intimidation +13, Perception +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic, Poison
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 21 (XP 33,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Aura of Dread.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of Vladimir von Drakken takes 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Daywalker.*** Vladimir von Drakken doesn't possess any Vampire Weaknesses from the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>***Reborn Transformation.*** When he finishes a Long Rest, Vladimir von Drakken reverts to his Reborn Vladimir von Drakken form.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** Vladimir von Drakken regains 30 Hit Points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 Hit Point remaining.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If Vladimir von Drakken is reduced to 0 Hit Points or dies, he drops to 1 Hit Point instead unless he is killed by a creature with the blood of House von Drakken within them.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** Vladimir von Drakken can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Vladimir von Drakken always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On his turn, Vladimir von Drakken can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Vladimir von Drakken takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Bloodmourne.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 35 (6d8 + 8) Force damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (4d4).
>- **Claws.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 26 (4d8 + 8) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 23).
>
>***Bite (1/Round).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 23, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 70 (20d6) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Weakened condition (DC 22 Constitution save ends). Vladimir von Drakken regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt. A Humanoid killed by this damage rises on its next turn as a Vampire Spawn under Vladimir von Drakken's control.  
>
>***Soul Rending (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 23, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 54 (12d8) Necrotic damage. Failure by 5 or More: Double damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Terrifying Glare.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 23, one creature Vladimir von Drakken can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 25 (6d6 + 4) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. Failure by 5 or More: While Frightened, the target has the Paralyzed condition.  
>
>***Wing Blast (1/Round).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 23, each creature in a 15-foot Emanation originating from Vladimir von Drakken. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away from Vladimir von Drakken and has the Prone condition. *Failure or Success:* Before or after using this Epic Action, Vladimir von Drakken can move up to half his Fly Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Aerial Maneuver (1/Round).*** Vladimir von Drakken moves up to his Fly Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Vladimir von Drakken chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on him. He can use this action even if he would otherwise be unable to take actions, his actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters his game statistics.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Blood Rage (1/Day).*** *Trigger:* Vladimir von Drakken becomes Bloodied. He can use this Reaction even if he has the Incapacitated condition or is otherwise unable to take Reactions. *Response:* All conditions and spells on Vladimir von Drakken end unless he chooses to be affected by them, and he can move up to his Fly Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action and ignoring Difficult Terrain. Vladimir von Drakken recharges and immediately uses Soul Rending.
