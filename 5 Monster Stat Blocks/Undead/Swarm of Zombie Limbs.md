>## Swarm of Zombie Limbs
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|1 (-5)|5 (-3)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 7
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and it can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny chunk of flesh. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Rotten Flesh.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Necrotic damage, or 5 (1d6 + 2) Necrotic damage if the swarm is Bloodied, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.
