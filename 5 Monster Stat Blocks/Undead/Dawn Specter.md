>## Dawn Specter
>*Medium Undead, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d8)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Fire, Lightning, Thunder; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
>- **Immunities** Cold, Poison, Radiant; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Any languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Ethereal Sight.*** The dawn specter can see 60 feet into the Ethereal Plane when it is on the Material Plane, and vice versa.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The dawn specter can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Radiant Touch.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (4d6 + 3) radiant damage.  
>
>***Etherealness.*** The dawn specter enters the Ethereal Plane from the Material Plane, or vice versa. It is visible on the Material Plane while it is in the Border Ethereal, and vice versa, yet it can't affect or be affected by anything on the other plane.  
>
>***Glorious Visage.*** Each non-undead creature within 60 feet of the dawn specter that can see it must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed for 1 minute. A charmed target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the charmed condition on itself on a success. If a target's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the target is immune to this dawn specter's Glorious Visage for the next 24 hours.  
>
>***Possession (Recharge 6).*** One humanoid that the dawn specter can see within 5 feet of it must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or be possessed by the dawn specter; the dawn specter then disappears, and the target is incapacitated and loses control of its body. The dawn specter now controls the body but doesn't deprive the target of awareness. The dawn specter can't be targeted by any attack, spell, or other effect, except ones that turn undead, and it retains its alignment, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, and immunity to being charmed and frightened. It otherwise uses the possessed target's statistics, but doesn't gain access to the target's knowledge, class features, or proficiencies.  
>The possession lasts until the body drops to 0 hit points, the dawn specter ends it as a bonus action, or the dawn specter is turned or forced out by an effect like the *dispel evil and good* spell. When the possession ends, the dawn specter reappears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the body. The target is immune to this dawn specter's Possession for 24 hours after succeeding on the saving throw or after the possession ends.
