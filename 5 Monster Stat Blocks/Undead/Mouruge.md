>## Mouruge
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 45 (6d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|7 (-2)|13 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Perception +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the mouruge to 0 Hit Points, it must make a Constitution saving throw (DC of 5 plus the damage taken) unless the damage is Radiant or Cold, or from a Critical Hit. On a successful save, the mouruge drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mouruge makes one Bite attack and one Slam attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage.
