___
>## Speaking Skull
>*Tiny Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 1 (2d4 - 4)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 5 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|2 (-4)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, History +4, Medicine +4, Nature +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Immunities** Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the skull remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary humanoid skull.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Disparaging Remarks.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature the skull can see within 10 feet. *Failure:* 5 (2d4) Psychic damage, and the target has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Guiding Words.*** *Trigger:* A creature the skull can see within 10 feet makes an ability check. *Response:* The target can roll 1d4 and add the result to that check.
