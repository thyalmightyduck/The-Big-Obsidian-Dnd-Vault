___
>## Skeleton Mage
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Perception +3
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Fire Lance.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 25 (4d10 + 3) Fire damage, and the target has the Burning condition (1d10 Fire).  
>
>***Shocking Arc.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 30 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 3) Lightning damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Shocked condition (save ends).  
>
>***Thunder Blast (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cube originating from the skeleton mage. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away from the skeleton mage. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Teleport.*** The skeleton mage teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 60 feet.
