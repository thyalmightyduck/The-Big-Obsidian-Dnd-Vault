>## Shadowsteel Ghoul
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|9 (-1)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +2
>- **Skills** Perception +2
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** One language known in life
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The Shadowsteel ghoul has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Shadowsteel Coat.*** The ghoul is coated in metal armor made of Shadowsteel.  
>
>***Shadowsteel Hunger.*** The Shadowsteel ghoul has Advantage on attack rolls against creatures carrying or wearing Shadowsteel.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Shadowsteel ghoul makes two Claw attacks.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage. A cursed target must make an Escalation Check. If this damage reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points and it isn't already cursed, roll 1d4 to determine which curse it is afflicted with: 1–2 *Curse of Damned Aging*, 3–4 *Curse of Foul Blight*.
