___
>## Meld
>*Large Undead, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d10 + 42)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Ethereal Sight.*** The meld can see 60 feet into the Ethereal Plane when it is on the Material Plane and vice versa.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The meld can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object. The meld can also interact with any liquid surface as if it was solid ground.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The meld makes four Withering Slam attacks. It can replace two attacks with a use of Conflicted Soul.  
>
>***Etherealness.*** The meld cast the *Etherealness* spell, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability. The meld is visible on the Material Plane while on the Border Ethereal and vice versa, but it can't affect or be affected by anything on the other plane.  
>
>***Withering Slam.*** *Melee Attack:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Conflicted Soul.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature the meld can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target suffers the effect of the *Confusion* spell (no Concentration required to maintain the spell).
