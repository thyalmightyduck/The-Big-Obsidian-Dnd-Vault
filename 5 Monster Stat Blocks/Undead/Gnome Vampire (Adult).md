>## Gnome Vampire (Adult)
>*Small Undead, Any Evil Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 180 (24d6 + 96)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +14 (24)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Int +7, Wis +7, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, History +7, Nature +7, Perception +7, Stealth +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** The languages known in life
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Lair).*** If the vampire fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The vampire regains 20 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight. If the vampire takes Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Speak with Beasts.*** The vampire can communicate with Beasts as if they shared a language.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Turn Resistance.*** The vampire has Advantage on saving throws against any effect that turns undead.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the following flaws:  
>*Forbiddance.* The vampire can't enter a residence without an invitation from an occupant.  
>*Stake to the Heart.* The vampire dies if a weapon that deals Piercing damage is driven into the vampire's heart while it has the Incapacitated condition in its resting place.  
>*Sunlight Hypersensitivity.* The vampire takes 20 Radiant damage when it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes two Claw attacks and uses Bite. It can replace one Claw attack with a use of Trip.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature and both Claw attacks hit, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or that has the Charmed, Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage. The target's Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to that amount. A Humanoid reduced to 0 Hit Points by this damage and then buried rises the following sunset as a vampire spawn under the vampire's control.  
>
>***Trip.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature within 5 feet of the vampire. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Command Beasts.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, up to three Beasts that can hear or see the vampire within 30 feet of it. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition. While Charmed, the target sees the vampire as a leader to be heeded and protected, following its verbal commands and acting in its interest even when left without a command.  
>Each time the vampire or the vampire's allies do anything harmful to a target, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. Otherwise, the effect lasts 24 hours or until the vampire dies, is on a different plane of existence than the target, or takes a Bonus Action to end the effect.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** If the vampire isn't in sunlight, it shape-shifts into a Medium or smaller beast (Fly or Swim Speed 30 ft.) or a Medium cloud of mist (Speed 5 ft., Fly Speed 20 ft. [hover]), or it returns to its vampire form. Anything it is wearing transforms with it.  
>While in beast form, the vampire can't speak. Its game statistics, other than its size and Speed, are unchanged. If its beast form lacks claws, its Claw attacks become Slam attacks that deal Bludgeoning damage instead of Slashing damage.  
>While in mist form, the vampire can't take any Actions, speak, or manipulate objects. It is weightless and can enter an enemy's space and stop there. If air can pass through a space, the mist can do so, but it can't pass through liquid. It has Resistance to all damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Misty Escape.*** *Trigger:* The vampire drops to 0 Hit Points outside its resting place. *Response:* The vampire uses Shape-Shift to become mist. If it can't use Shape-Shift, it is destroyed.  
>While it has 0 Hit Points in mist form, the vampire dies if it takes damage three times or Radiant damage once. It can't return to its vampire form, and it must reach its resting place within 2 hours or be destroyed. Once in its resting place, it returns to its vampire form and has the Paralyzed condition until it regains any Hit Points. It regains 1 Hit Point after spending an hour there.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, the vampire can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The vampire regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
>
>- **Move.** The vampire moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
>- **Claw.** The vampire makes a Claw attack.
>
>### Lair Actions
>Adult and ancient vampires form a supernatural bond with their lair, allowing them lair actions. On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), such vampires can take a lair action to cause one of the following effects. The vampire can't use the same effect two rounds in a row:
>- A **Swarm of Insects** forms within 60 feet of the vampire. The vampire commands the swarm, which takes its turn just after the vampire. The swarm of insects remains until it is dispersed or until the vampire drops to 0 Hit Points.
>- Thorny vines or bloody tenatcles erupt from the ground in a 30-foot radius Sphere centered on a point the vampire chooses within 120 feet of the vampire. This area is Difficult Terrain for creatures of the vampire's choice. When the impediments appear, each creature of the vampire's choice in the area must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or gain the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) and Restrained while grappled in this way. The plants or tentacles retreat into the ground when the vampire drops to 0 Hit Points.
>- 30-foot-radius Emanation. The mist spreads around corners, and its area is Heavily Obscured for all creatures except the vampire. It lasts until the vampire uses a Bonus Action to dismiss it, the vampire drops to 0 Hit Points, or a wind of moderate or greater speed (at least 1d4 * 10 miles per hour) disperses it.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The power of an adult or ancient vampire's curse changes the region surrounding the vampire's lair, creating any of the following effects:
>- Biting insects and their larvae increase noticeably in population within 1 mile of the lair.
>- Unnerving natural sounds—twigs breaking, leaves crunching, trees creaking, and so on—are louder within 1 mile of the lair.
>- Plants within 500 feet of the lair are tougher, twisted, and any thorns or thistles they have are longer and more painful when they prick.
>- The air within 500 feet of the lair is noticeably colder and smells faintly of blood. This cooling of the air makes mist more likely around the lair.
>
>If the vampire dies, these effects linger for one week, then dissipate over 1d6 days.
