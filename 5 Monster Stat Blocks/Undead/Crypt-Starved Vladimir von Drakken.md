___
>## Crypt-Starved Vladimir von Drakken
>*Medium Undead (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 76 (9d8 + 36) plus 76 (9d8 + 36) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|19 (+4)|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Int +8, Wis +7, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Athletics +10, Insight +7, Intimidation +10, Perception +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic, Poison
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Aura of Dread.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of Vladimir von Drakken takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Reborn Transformation.*** When Vladimir von Drakken consumes the blood of six living creatures of level or challenge rating 10 or higher, he transforms into Reborn Vladimir von Drakken. His Initiative doesn't change. If he is destroyed before he can feed sufficiently, he must begin the process again when he rejuvenates.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** Vladimir von Drakken regains 30 Hit Points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 Hit Point remaining.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If Vladimir von Drakken is destroyed in his crypt-starved form, he crumbles into ash and reforms 1d10 days later with all of his Hit Points. His new body appears within his crypt in the Brachenwald. Casting Hallow on the druidic shrine prevents his new body from returning so long as the spell remains in effect.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>### Actions
>***Vladimir von Drakken always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On his turn, Vladimir von Drakken can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Vladimir von Drakken takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Bloodmourne.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 26 (6d6 + 5) Force damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (4d4).
>- **Claws.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18).
>
>***Bite (1/Round).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 40 (10d6 + 5) Necrotic damage. The target's Hit Point Maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and Vladimir von Drakken regains Hit Points equal to that amount. A Humanoid killed by this effect and then buried in the ground rises the following night as a Vampire Spawn under Vladimir von Drakken's control.  
>
>***Terrifying Glare.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 18, one creature Vladimir von Drakken can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. Failure by 5 or More: While Frightened, the target has the Paralyzed condition.  
>
>***Shadow Step (1/Round).*** Vladimir von Drakken teleports to an unoccupied space he can see within 60 feet that is in Dim Light or Darkness. He has Advantage on the next melee attack he makes before the end of this round.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Vladimir von Drakken chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on him. He can use this action even if he would otherwise be unable to take actions, his actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters his game statistics.
