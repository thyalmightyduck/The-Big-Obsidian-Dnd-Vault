>## Shadow Spirit
>*Medium Undead, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11 + the spell's level
>- **Hit Points** 50 (Assassin only) or 40 (Infiltrator only) + 10 for each spell level above 5
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 40 ft. (Infiltrator only)
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals your Proficiency Bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals your Proficiency Bonus
>___
>***Incorporeal Passage.*** The spirit can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. If it ends its turn inside an object, it is shunted to the nearest unoccupied space and takes 1d10 Force damage for every 5 feet traveled.  
>
>***Shadow Stealth.*** The spirit may take the Hide action as a Bonus Action when in Dim Light or Darkness.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The spirit makes a number of attacks equal to half this spell's level (round down).  
>
>***Chill Touch (Infiltrator Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, Reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d8 + 3 + summonSpellLevel Cold damage.  
>
>***Deathly Bolt (Assassin Only).*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, range 150 ft. *Hit:* 2d4 + 3 + summonSpellLevel Cold damage. If the spirit was hidden when it made the attack, the attack deals an additional 2d4 Cold damage.
