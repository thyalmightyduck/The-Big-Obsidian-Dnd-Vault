___
>## Skeleton Cannoneer
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|8 (-1)|13 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Dead Firearms.*** The cannon and pistol a skeleton cannoneer uses are nonfunctional as ranged weapons for a creature other than a skeleton cannoneer. For the skeleton cannoneer, the pistol never needs to be reloaded.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pistol Club.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Dead Pistol.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 30/90 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.  
>
>***Dead Cannon.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Sphere centered on a point within 500 feet of the skeleton cannoneer but no closer than 50 feet to it. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The skeleton cannoneer can't use Dead Cannon again until it takes an action to load it.  
>Alternatively, the skeleton cannoneer can target the cannon itself. Doing so causes the cannon to explode in a 15-foot Emanation, destroying it and increasing the damage dealt by 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage.
