>## Crooked Man
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 221 (26d8 + 104)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|18 (+4)|19 (+4)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Deception +9, Perception +7, Stealth +9
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Aversion to Self-Sacrifice.*** The crooked man takes 20 Necrotic damage if he can see a creature willingly take damage or gain a condition in place of someone else, or to someone else's benefit.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The crooked man has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The crooked man makes two Crooked Claw attacks, and he can replace one attack with a use of Snap Limb. He can also use *Confusion* if available.  
>
>***Confusion (1/Day).*** The crooked man casts *Confusion*, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17).  
>
>***Crooked Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Slashing damage plus 21 (6d6) Psychic damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16) from one of two claws. Until the grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***Snap Limb.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19, one creature the crooked man is grappling, and that has limbs that can break. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Force damage, and the bones (or equivalent) in one of the target's limbs (crooked man's choice) breaks, causing an appropriate effect below. A broken limb heals in 40 days, or when the creature regains any Hit Points from magic or Regeneration. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Broken Arm.*** Disadvantage on attack rolls. Can't hold anything or manipulate objects with the limb.  
>
>***Broken Leg.*** Speed halved. If the creature takes the Dash action, it must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or have the Prone condition.  
>
>***Broken Wing.*** Fly Speed reduced to 0.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Blinking Teleport.*** The crooked man has the Invisible condition for 1 minute and teleports to an unoccupied space he can see within 60 feet. The condition ends immediately after the crooked man deals any damage.
