___
>## Gegazol
>*Gargantuan Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 22
>- **Hit Points** 499 (27d20 + 216)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 40 ft., Fly 80 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|27 (+8)|14 (+2)|26 (+8)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|23 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Int +10, Wis +9
>- **Skills** Perception +9
>- **Immunities** Cold, Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Draconic
>- **Challenge** 24 (XP 62,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Ice Walk.*** Gegazol can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check. Difficult Terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost her extra movement.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day or 5/Day in Lair).*** If Gegazol fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** Gegazol has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If destroyed while her soul vessel remains intact, Gegazol's unconscious body rejuvenates within 5 feet of the soul vessel, where she regains 78 (12d12) Hit Points each day. When she loses the Bloodied condition, she regains consciousness.  
>
>***Stench.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of Gegazol is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 23. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to Gegazol's Stench for 1 hour.  
>
>***Turning Immunity.*** Gegazol and Undead within 120 feet of her are immune to effects that Turn Undead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Gegazol uses Corrupting Tail, then makes three Rend attacks.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 21 (2d12 + 8) Force damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Corrupting Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 60 ft. *Hit:* 21 (4d6 + 8) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Poison damage, and the target gains 1 Exhaustion level.  
>
>***Breath of Long Night (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 23, each creature in a 90-foot Cone. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Cold damage and 45 (10d8) Necrotic damage, and the target suffers 1 Exhaustion level until the end of Gegazol's next turn. *Failure by 5 or More:* The target also has the Blinded condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, Gegazol can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Geggzol regains all expended uses at the start of each of her turns.
>
>- **Daemon Sight.** Gegazol gains Truesight with a range of 120 feet until the end of her next turn, then takes the Search action.
>- **Pounce.** Gegazol moves up to half its Speed, and it makes one Rend attack. Gegazol can't use this action again until the start of her next turn.
>- **Tail Swipe.** Gegazol makes a Corrupting Tail attack.
>- **Wings of Winter.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 23, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from Gegazol. *Failure:* The target takes 17 (2d8 + 8) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Cold damage and has the Prone condition. *Failure or Success:* Gegazol then flies up to half her Fly Speed. Gegazol can't use this action again until the start of her next turn.
>
>### Lair Actions
>Gegazol's lair has gained a life of its own, thanks in part to the influence of the daemon that corrupts her mind and spirit. Once per round, the daemonic presence in the lair causes an effect from the list below.
>- Tendrils of necrotic power rise and reach for living creatures within the lair. Each living creature in the lair must attempt a DC 21 Constitution saving throw or take 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage and gain one Exhaustion level until Initiative count 20 on the next round.
>- Gegazol crashes her wings upon the ground, and rime spreads in a sphere with a 120-foot radius around her. This sphere spreads around corners and creates Difficult Terrain made of ice. Creatures in that area must make a DC 21 Constitution saving throw. Those who fail take 18 (4d8) Cold damage and have the Grappled condition (escape DC 21). On a success, the creature takes half the damage and isn't grappled. The Difficult Terrain disappears on Initiative count 20 on the next round.
>- Gegazol creates an opaque wall of black ice on a solid surface she can see within 120 feet of her. The wall is made up of twenty contiguous 10-foot square panels that are each 2 feet thick. When the wall appears, each creature in its area is pushed 5 feet out of the wall's space, appearing on whichever side of the wall the creature wants. Each 10-foot section of the wall has AC 5, 60 Hit Points, Vulnerability to Fire damage, and Immunity to Acid, Cold, Necrotic, Poison, and Psychic damage. The wall disappears when Gegazol uses this Lair Action again or when she dies.
