___
>## Druid of the Sanguine Rite
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 187 (25d8 + 75)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Con +7, Wis +9
>- **Skills** Nature +6, Survival +9
>- **Gear** *Hide Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Druidic plus any three languages
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Blood Sacrifice.*** A creature with the Restrained condition also has the Helpless condition against the vampire's attacks.  
>
>***Plasma Absorption.*** Each time a creature takes damage from the Bleeding condition while within 30 feet of the vampire, the vampire regains Hit Points equal to the damage dealt.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The vampire regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes three attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Draining Bite or Spellcasting.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15).  
>
>***Bloodthorn Whip.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 30 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 5) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (4d4).  
>
>***Draining Bite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Weakened condition (save ends).  
>
>***Bloodroots (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the vampire. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Piercing damage, and the target has the Restrained condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The vampire casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17, +9 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Druidcraft*  
>2/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Dominate Beast*, *Entangle*, *Faerie Fire*, *Pass Without Trace*, *Spike Growth*  
>1/day each: *Antilife Shell*, *Contagion*, *Create Undead* (as an action), *Insect Plague*, *Mass Suggestion*, *Scrying*
