___
>## Scream Thief
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The scream thief can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Life Drain.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, its Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to half the damage taken.  
>
>***Silence the Living (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, a Humanoid within 30 feet of the scream thief. *Failure:* 19 (3d12) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition. While Frightened, the target can't vocalize a sound of any kind. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Fragment the Spirit.*** *Trigger:* A creature is killed by the scream thief. *Response:* The target's spirit rises as 3 (1d6) psychic fragments in the nearest unoccupied space to its corpse. The fragments are under the scream thief's control.
