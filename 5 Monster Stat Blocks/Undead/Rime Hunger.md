___
>## Rime Hunger
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 30 (4d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|3 (-4)|6 (-2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +0
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Cold, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** Understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fed Fortitude.*** For 1 minute after the rime hunger regains Hit Points, when damage reduces the rime hunger to 0 Hit Points, it can make a Constitution saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken), unless the damage is Fire, Radiant, or from a Critical Hit. On a successful save, the rime hunger drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12). The rime hunger can only grapple one target at a time.  
>
>***Sapping Grasp.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature Grappled by the rime hunger. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Cold damage, and the rime hunger regains Hit Points equal to half the Cold damage dealt. *Success:* Half damage only.
