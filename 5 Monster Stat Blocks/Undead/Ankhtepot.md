>## Ankhtepot
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 202 (27d8 + 81)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +8, Wis +11
>- **Skills** History +8, Perception +11, Religion +8
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 17 (XP 18,000, or 20,000 in lair; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Darklord Restoration.*** If Ankhtepot dies, he revives with all his Hit Points in 1d10 weeks somewhere in Har'Akir.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Domain).*** If Ankhtepot fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** Ankhtepot has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Ankhtepot makes four Negative Energy Burst attacks. He can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Power Word Stun*.  
>
>***Negative Energy Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 26 (6d6 + 5) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Ankhtepot casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19):  
>
>At will: *Dispel Magic*, *Divination*, *Mage Hand* (hand is Invisible), *Power Word Stun*  
>1/day each: *Animate Dead*, *Sunburst*  
>
>***Touch of Death (Recharge 6).*** Ankhtepot touches one creature within 5 feet. If the creature has 80 Hit Points or fewer, it dies. Otherwise, it takes 52 (8d12) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Ankhtepot can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Ankhtepot regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Death Strike.** Ankhtepot makes one Negative Energy Burst attack.
>- **Hand of Fate.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 19, one Large or smaller creature Ankhtepot can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Force damage, and the target is pushed or pulled up to 20 feet straight away or toward Ankhtepot. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* Ankhtepot can't take this action again until the start of his next turn.
>- **Sands of Time.** Ankhtepot teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space he can see. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 19, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from Ankhtepot at his destination space. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Necrotic damage, and the target magically ages 10 years. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* Ankhtepot can't take this action again until the start of his next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>Har'Akir is altered by Ankhtepot's presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Blistering Sun.** Any outdoor area of Bright Light in Har'Akir is an area of extreme heat (see the *Dungeon Master's Guide*).
>- **Closing the Borders.** While in his domain, Ankhtepot can close or open the borders to Har'Akir at will. While the borders are closed, whirling sands fill the Mists surrounding Har'Akir. A creature that enters the Mists for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there takes 2d6 Slashing damage.
>- **Control Undead.** Undead of CR 5 or lower in Har'Akir have the Charmed condition and obey Ankhtepot's orders.
>
>If Ankhtepot dies, these effects end immediately.
