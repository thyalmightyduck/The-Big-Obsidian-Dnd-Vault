>## Wretched Reveler
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +4, Deception +4, Performance +4
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Dart*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The wretched reveler makes two attacks, using Dart or Tankard in any combination.  
>
>***Tankard.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Dart.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Body Moving.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature within 30 feet of the wretched reveler that can hear it singing. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition until the start of the wretched reveler's next turn. While Charmed, the target uses its movement to dance in place without leaving its space, imposing Disadvantage on its attack rolls and Dexterity saving throws. Other creatures have Advantage on attack rolls against the target while it dances.  
>The wretched reveler can stop singing at any time, ending the effect. The effect ends early if the wretched reveler is killed or has the Incapacitated condition, or if the target can't hear the wretched reveler.
