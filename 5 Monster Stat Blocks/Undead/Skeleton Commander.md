___
>## Skeleton Commander
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +3
>- **Skills** Insight +3, Perception +3
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skeleton commander makes two Pike attacks. It can replace one attack with Warlord Strike even if it is not recharged.  
>
>***Pike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Warlord Strike (Recharge 5–6).*** The skeleton commander chooses an Undead ally of CR 3 or less that can hear and understand it, commanding it to attack a creature the skeleton commander attacked this turn. The ally can use its Reaction to make an attack against the designated creature, adding the skeleton commander's Proficiency Bonus to the damage roll.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Brandish Standard.*** *Trigger:* An Undead the skeleton commander can see within 30 feet makes an attack roll or saving throw. *Response:* The skeleton commander brandishes the tattered banner on its pike. The triggering creature can roll 1d4 and add the result to its roll, provided it can see the skeleton commander and isn't already benefiting from Brandish Standard.
