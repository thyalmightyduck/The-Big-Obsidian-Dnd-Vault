>## Ebon Knight Steed
>*Large Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 68 (8d10 + 24)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Fly 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands one language the ebon knight speaks but can't speak, telepathy 1 mile (with its ebon knight only)
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Aura of Smoke.*** The ebon knight steed is surrounded by sulfurous black fumes. A creature more than 5 feet from the ebon knight steed has Disadvantage on attack rolls against it.  
>
>***Dissipate.*** When the ebon knight steed is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it disappears, leaving no corpse.  
>
>***Turning Bond.*** While within 30 feet of its **ebon knight**, the ebon knight steed can't be turned unless the same effect also turns the knight.  
>
>### Actions
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Trample.*** The ebon knight steed makes a Hooves attack against a creature that has the Prone condition.
