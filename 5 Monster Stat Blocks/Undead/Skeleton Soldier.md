___
>## Skeleton Soldier
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Shield*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Poison-Tipped Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shield Slam.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 5 feet of the skeleton soldier. *Failure:* The target is pushed 5 feet away from the skeleton soldier and has the Prone condition.
