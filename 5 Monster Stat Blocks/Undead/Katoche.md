___
>## Katoche
>*Huge Undead, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 127 (15d12 + 30)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands all languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The katoche can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The katoche makes three Bewildering Strikes.  
>
>***Bewildering Strike.*** *Melee  or Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Psychic damage, and the target is affected as if by the *Confusion* spell until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Separate.*** *Trigger:* The katoche becomes Bloodied. *Response:* The katoche splits into one **Living Dream** and one **Living Nightmare**.  
>Each new creature has Hit Points equal to half the katoche's, rounded down.
