>## Lich Troll
>*Large Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 171 (18d10 + 72)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +9, Int +8, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +8, History +8, Insight +6, Perception +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Giant, the languages it knew in life and Giant
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (2/Day).*** If the lich troll fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The lich troll regains 15 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the lich troll takes Acid or Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function until the start of the lich troll's next turn. The lich troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lich troll makes three attacks, using Claw, Frost Beam, or Grave Beam in any combination.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Cold damage.  
>
>***Frost Beam.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d8) Cold damage, and the target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of the lich troll's next turn.  
>
>***Grave Beam.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 60 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d10)) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the end of the lich troll's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The lich troll casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Mage Hand*, *Magic Missile* (level 3 version), *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Animate Dead*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Dispel Magic* (level 4 version), *Haste*, *Invisibility*, *Mirror Image*  
>1/day each: *Blight*, *Dimension Door*, *Cloudkill*, *Create Undead*, *Scrying*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (2/Day).*** The lich troll casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Magic (4/Day).*** The lich troll casts *Counterspell* (level 4 version) or *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 2. Immediately after another creature's turn, the lich troll can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The lich troll regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Evasive Attack.** The lich troll uses *Misty Step* and makes a Claw, Frost Beam, or Grave Beam attack. If it is out of uses of *Misty Step*, it just makes the attack.
