___
>## Crawling Grave
>*Large Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +5, Wis +2
>- **Damage Resistances** Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands all languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Lethal Overrun.*** If the crawling grave enters the space of a creature that is dead or has the Incapacitated condition, it picks up the creature. The creature moves with the crawling grave , has a Speed of 0 that cannot be increased, and has Disadvantage on Death Saving Throw. A creature that loses the Incapacitated condition or is revived stops moving with the crawling grave. A creature within 5 feet of the crawling grave can take an action to pull a creature or corpse free of the crawling grave with a successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check.  
>At the end of the crawling grave's second turn after picking up a corpse, the corpse breaks apart and the crawling grave regains Hit Points equal to half that corpse's former Hit Point maximum or 20 Hit Points, whichever is fewer. The crawling grave expels anything the corpse was wearing or carrying.  
>
>***Swarmlike.*** The crawling grave can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the crawling grave can move through any opening large enough for a Small creature.  
>
>***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the crawling grave to 0 Hit Points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 plus the damage taken unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. *Success:* The crawling grave drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The crawling grave makes two Slam attacks  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition. If the crawling grave is Bloodied, it deals 8 (1d10 + 3) Bludgeoning damage and doesn't impose the Prone condition.
