>## Ferryman
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., 40 ft. with Coffin Boat
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +3, Cha +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, and Prone (with Coffin Boat)
>- **Gear** *Hooded Lantern*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Coffin Boat.*** The ferryman rides an open coffin on a swell of swamp water, allowing it to travel with a Speed of 40 feet over any surface, including liquids, ignoring Difficult Terrain. The coffin can hold two other Medium creatures. Two Small creatures or four Tiny creatures count as one Medium creature.  
>While riding the coffin, the ferryman has Immunity to the Prone condition, and the coffin moves with the ferryman unless the ferryman has the Incapacitated condition. The coffin has AC 15; HP 25; Immunity to Necrotic, Poison, and Psychic damage.  
>If the coffin is destroyed, the ferryman can magically create a new one using a 10-minute ritual.  
>
>***Payment.*** The ferryman must peacefully offer a ride in its boat to a creature that takes the Influence action to offer it 3 CP. If that creature is cursed by the ferryman, the curse ends. If the boat is full, the ferryman must return to offer the creature a ride once its current passengers leave the boat. If the creature or its allies damage the ferryman or force it to make a saving throw, this effect ends and the creature can't use that ferryman's Payment trait again.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The ferryman regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns if it has at least 1 Hit Point.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ferryman makes two Lantern Pole attacks and uses Hexed Lantern if available.  
>
>***Lantern Pole.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Hexed Lantern (1/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature of the ferryman's choice in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* The creature is cursed for 1 minute and must subtract 1d4 from D20 Tests.
