___
>## Fate Cat
>*Tiny Undead, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d4 + 40)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|3 (-4)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Con +6, Wis +7, Cha +2
>- **Skills** Perception +9, Stealth +5, Survival +9
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Frightened, Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Stunned
>- **Senses** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Understands all language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The fate cat regains 5 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the fate cat takes Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the fate cat's next turn. The fate cat's body is destroyed only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** When the fate cat's body is destroyed, its soul lingers. After 24 hours, the soul inhabits and animates another cat corpse on the same place of existence and regains all of its Hit Points. While the soul is bodiless, a *Wish* spell can force the soul into the afterlife and prevent it from returning.  
>
>***Sin Tracker.*** The fate cat knows the distance and direction of any creature that has wronged it, even on another plane of existence. If the creature the fate cat is tracking dies, the fate cat dies as well.  
>
>***Turn Immunity.*** The fate cat is immune to effects that Turn Undead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The fate cat can make two Claw attacks.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The fate cat casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Bestow Curse*, *Invisibility*, *Minor Illusion*  
>1/day each: *Contagion*, *Teleport*, *Flash Fever*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Magical Retaliation.*** The fate cat casts *Counterspell* or *Hellish Rebuke* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
