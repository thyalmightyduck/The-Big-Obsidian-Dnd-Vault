___
>## White Wraith
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** HP 60 (8d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|9 (-1)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +5
>- **Skills** Perception +6, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Fire, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Cold, Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blizzard Stalker.*** Inclement weather (such as fog, snow or wind) doesn't impede the wraith's vision, and it has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks it makes in such conditions.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The wraith can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Life Drain.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 (with Advantage during the first round of each combat), reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) Necrotic damage plus 16 (3d10) Cold damage. If the target is a creature, its Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken.  
>
>***Freezing Gale (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Cold damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Create Walker (2/Day).*** The wraith targets a Humanoid corpse within 10 feet of itself that died from either Cold damage or exposure to extreme cold. The target's remains animate as either a Skeleton or a Zombie (depending on the state of the corpse, as determined by the GM) that has Immunity to Cold and Necrotic damage and shares the wraith's Blizzard Stalker trait. The animated creature is under the wraith's control. The wraith can have no more than ten animated creatures under its control at a time.
