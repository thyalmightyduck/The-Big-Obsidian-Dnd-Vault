>## Ghoul Lord
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Mace*, *Plate Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Blood Scent.*** The ghoul lord has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to Search for creatures who are not Constructs or Undead. It can automatically detect any creature that isn't a Construct or Undead within 10 feet of it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ghoul lord makes two attacks.  
>
>***Soulbinding Mace.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Jinxed condition (1d6; save ends).  
>
>***Abyssal Pull.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the target is pulled 15 feet toward the ghoul lord.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shield Slam.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 5 feet of the ghoul lord. *Failure:* The target is pushed 10 feet away from the ghoul lord and has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Unyielding.*** *Trigger:* The ghoul lord is subjected to an effect that would move it, cause it to have the Prone condition, or both. *Failure:* The ghoul lord is neither moved nor has the Prone condition.
