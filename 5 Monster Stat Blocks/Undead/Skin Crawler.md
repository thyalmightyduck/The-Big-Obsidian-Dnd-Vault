___
>## Skin Crawler
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Grappled, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 20 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amorphous Form.*** The skin crawler can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skin crawler makes two Claw attacks and one Bind attack.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Bind.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (3d8 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and Disadvantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws, and the skin crawler can't Bind another target.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Crush and Consume.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature Grappled by the skin crawler. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the skin crawler regains an equal number of Hit Points. If this damage kills the target, the skin crawler assumes its form. The skin crawler's game statistics are the same in each form. It reverts to its true form if it dies.
