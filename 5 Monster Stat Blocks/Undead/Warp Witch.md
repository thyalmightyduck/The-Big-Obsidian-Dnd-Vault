>## Warp Witch
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d8)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Fire, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Cold, Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The warp witch can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Balefire Lantern.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 21 (4d8 + 3) Cold damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the end of the warp witch's next turn.  
>
>***Psychic Scream.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition (save ends).  
>
>***Possession.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, one Humanoid the warp witch can see within 5 feet. *Failure:* The target is possessed by the Warp Witch. The witch disappears, and the target has the Incapacitated condition and loses control of its body. The witch controls the target's body but doesn't deprive the target of awareness. The witch can't be targeted by any attack, spell, or other effect, except ones that turn undead. The witch retains its alignment, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, and Immunity to the Charmed and Frightened conditions. It otherwise uses the possessed target's game statistics, but doesn't gain access to the target's knowledge, class features, or proficiencies.  
>The possession lasts until the body drops to 0 Hit Points, the witch ends it as a Bonus Action, or the witch is turned or forced out by an effect like the *Dispel Evil and Good* spell. When the possession ends, the witch reappears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the body. If the target succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to this warp witch's Possession for 24 hours.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Mutate Host.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature the warp witch is possessing. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level and a mutation.
