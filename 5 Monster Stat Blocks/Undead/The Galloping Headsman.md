>## The Galloping Headsman
>*Large Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 220 (21d10 + 105)
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +15 (25)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|17 (+3)|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +8, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Athletics +12, Intimidation +10, Perception +8
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Damage dealt by a creature holding the headman's original skull
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Common; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 18 (XP 20,000, or 22,000 in lair; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Binding Skull.*** The headsman has Vulnerability to any damage dealt by a creature holding the headsman's original skull, and it has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls made against that creature.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Lair).*** If the headsman fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The headsman has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The headsman makes two Headsman's Axe attacks and one Hooves attack.  
>
>***Headsman's Axe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 19 (2d12 + 6) Slashing damage plus 16 (3d10) Fire damage. If the target is a creature with at least one head and the headsman rolled a 20 on the attack roll, the target must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or it is decapitated and dies if it can't survive without a head. A target is immune to this effect if it takes none of the damage, has Legendary Actions, or is Huge or larger. Such a creature takes an extra 28 (8d6) Force damage from the hit.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 24 (4d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the headsman moved at least 20 feet immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage and has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Flaming Skull (Recharge 5–6).*** The headsman hurls its skull, after which it reappears in the headsman's hand. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 90-foot-long, 10-foot-wide Line. *Failure:* 63 (14d8) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The target starts burning.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Move.** The headsman moves up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
>- **Rancorous Gaze.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 18, one creature the headsman can see within 90 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition and repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. *Failure or Success:* The headsman can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Relentless Execution.** The headsman makes one Headsman's Axe attack with Advantage. If the target has the Frightened condition, treat a 19 on the d20 as a 20.
  The headsman can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Galloping Headsman's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Encroaching Doom.** Within 6 miles of the lair, an air of doom clings to creatures. Creatures have Disadvantage on saving throws made to avoid or end the Frightened condition that aren't caused by the Galloping Headsman.
>- **Interminable Journey.** Paths warp and stretch within 6 miles of the lair. In that area, travel pace outside of combat is halved.
>
>If the Galloping Headsman dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
