___
>## Flesh Mournling
>*Large Undead (Brute), Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 95 (10d10 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of their creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Horrid Sob.*** When an enemy who can hear the mournling starts their turn within 30 feet of the mournling, the enemy must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of the mournling until the start of their next turn. A creature who succeeds on their saving throw is immune to the Horrid Sob of all flesh mournlings for 24 hours.  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** The mournling is immune to any power, spell, or effect that would alter their form.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mournling makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is frightened of the mournling, the target falls prone.  
>
>***Agonizing Wail (Recharge 5–6).*** The flesh mournling screams with unbearable pain in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area who can hear the mournling must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature's eyes water uncontrollably, they take 16 (3d10) psychic damage, and they can't see anything more than 5 feet away until the end of their next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and suffers no other effect.
