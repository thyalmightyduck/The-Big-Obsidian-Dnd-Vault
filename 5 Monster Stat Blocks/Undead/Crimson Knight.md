___
>## Crimson Knight
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +7, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Intimidation +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Poison
>- **Gear** *Battleaxe*, *Half Plate Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft. (unimpeded by magical Darkness), Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The vampire regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes three attacks. It can replace one attack with a Bite.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15).  
>
>***Crimson Axe.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 30/90 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d12 + 4) Force damage, and the target suffers one of the following effects of the vampire's choice. *Hit or Miss:* After being thrown, the axe returns to the vampire's hand.  
>
>***Crackling Smite.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Thunder damage, and the target has the Shocked condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Unholy Smite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the end of the vampire's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Wrathful Smite.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Necrotic damage, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Charge.*** The vampire moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see. If it ends this movement within 5 feet of the creature, it has Advantage on the next attack roll it makes against that creature before the end of its turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Unbridled Fury.*** *Trigger:* A creature hits the vampire with an attack. *Response:* The vampire makes a Crimson Axe attack against the attacker.
