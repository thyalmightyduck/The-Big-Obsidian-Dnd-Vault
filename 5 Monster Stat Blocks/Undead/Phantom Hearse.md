___
>## Phantom Hearse
>*Huge Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 110 (13d12 + 26)
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Breathless Warding.*** A creature holding its breath has the Invisible condition to the hearse. If the creature takes damage while holding its breath, it must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or let out its breath and become visible to the hearse until the start of the creature's next turn, when it can hold its breath again.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The hearse can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hearse makes two Ghostly Coachman attacks. It can replace one of the attacks with a use of Trample, if available.  
>
>***Ghostly Coachman.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Cold damage. If the target is a creature, it can't regain Hit Points until the start of the hearse's next turn.  
>
>***Trample (Recharge 5–6).*** The hearse moves in a straight line up to its Speed. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature whose space the hearse enters during this action. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Cold damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Tolling Bell (1/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature of the hearse's choice that it can see in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the hearse. *Failure:* 44 (8d10) Psychic damage, and the creature's Speed is halved until the start of the hearse's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Entomb.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature Grappled by the hearse (it can have up to three creatures entombed at a time). *Failure:* The target is entombed in a coffin in the hearse, and the Grappled condition ends.  
>An entombed target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the hearse, and at the start of each of the hearse's turns it takes 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage and gains 1 Exhaustion level.  
>If the hearse takes 20 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside a coffin, the hearse must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or release all entombed creatures, each of which falls in a space within 5 feet of the hearse and has the Prone condition. If the hearse dies, any entombed creature falls to the ground with the Prone condition in the hearse's former space.
