>## Jespaith
>*Gargantuan Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 279 (18d20 + 90)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|25 (+7)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Radiant
>- **Immunities** Acid, Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Corruption Aura.*** The jespaith corrupts a 20-foot Emanation, turning it into Difficult Terrain even after the jespaith moves on. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, a creature that starts its turn in the Emanation. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. *Failure or Success:* The target takes 5 (2d4) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Poison damage.  
>
>***Rock Breaker.*** The jespaith can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrow Speed, but it leaves behind rubble rather than a tunnel.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The jespaith deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Turn Immunity.*** The jespaith has Immunity to effects that Turn Undead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The jespaith makes two Slam attacks. It can replace one attack with Engulf.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 23 (3d10 + 7) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 20). The jespaith can grapple up to four creatures at once.  
>
>***Engulf.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 20, each Huge or smaller creature Grappled by the jespaith. *Failure:* The grapple ends and the target is pulled into the jespaith's body. The target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, is suffocating, and has Total Cover from effects that originate outside the jespaith. At the start of each of the jespaith's turns, the target takes 23 (3d10 + 7) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) Acid damage. The jespaith can hold only one Huge creature, two Large creatures, or eight Medium or smaller creatures at a time.  
>The target can escape the jespaith by taking an action and succeeding on a DC 20 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. Escaping the jespaith costs 10 feet of movement and ejects the target into a space of its choice within 5 feet of the jespaith. If the jespaith dies, the target no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape without using an action or making a check.  
>If the target dies while engulfed, its body is broken up and the jespaith keeps anything the target was wearing or carrying inside of it.  
>
>***Spit (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 90-foot long, 10-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Acid damage plus 18 (4d8) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage.
