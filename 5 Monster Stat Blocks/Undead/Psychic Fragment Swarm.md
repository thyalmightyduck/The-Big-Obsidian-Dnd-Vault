>## Psychic Fragment Swarm
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|3 (-4)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Can repeat one phrase it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The swarm can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>***Maddening Voices.*** The swarm constantly emits a babble of words and phrases spoken in life by its many undead spirits. A creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the swarm is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The creature has the Stunned condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The creature is immune to Maddening Voices for 24 hours.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gainTemporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Withering Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage, or 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage if the swarm has the Bloodied condition.
