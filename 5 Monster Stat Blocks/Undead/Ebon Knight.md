___
>## Ebon Knight
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 135 (18d8 + 54)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't silvered
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Lance*, *Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life, telepathy 1 mile (with its steed only)
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of Smoke.*** The ebon knight is surrounded by sulfurous black fumes. A creature more than 5 feet from the ebon knight has Disadvantage on attack rolls against it.  
>
>***Mounted Tactics.*** The ebon knight and its steed act on the same Initiative. The steed acts after the ebon knight and has no restrictions on its actions. The ebon knight can force an attack that targets the steed to target the ebon knight instead. If the ebon knight's steed is destroyed, the ebon knight is dismounted but lands on its feed in a space of its choice within 5 feet of where the mount died.  
>
>***Turning Bond.*** While within 30 feet of its mount, the ebon knight has Advantage on saving throws against being turned. Also, the knight can't be turned unless the same effect also turns the steed.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ebon knight makes two Lance attacks.  
>
>***Lance.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 10 (1d12 + 4) Piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage, and if the ebon knight is mounted, the target has the Prone condition. If the ebon knight moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 6 (1d12) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Summon Steed (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** An **Ebon Knight Steed** bound to the ebon knight appears in an unoccupied space the ebon knight can see within 30 feet. When summoned, this steed is restored to its Hit Point maximum. The ebon knight can dismiss the steed as an action, causing it to disappear. If the knight enters an encounter riding the steed, this action has already been expended.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Set Lance.*** *Trigger:* A creature enters the knight's reach while it wields a lance. *Response:* The knight makes a Lance attack.
