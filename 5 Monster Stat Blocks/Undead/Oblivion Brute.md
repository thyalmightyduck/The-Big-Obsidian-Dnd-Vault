>## Oblivion Brute
>*Large Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d10 + 24)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +8
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Radiant
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Sunlight Weakness.*** While in sunlight, the oblivion brute has Disadvantage on D20 Tests.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The oblivion brute uses Unbearable Howl, then makes a Soul Drain attack.  
>
>***Soul Drain.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a living creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target loses 1 Hit Die. Hit Dice lost in this way return at the end of a Short Rest, and can be spent to regain Hit Points during that rest as normal.  
>If this effect reduces the target's number of available Hit Point Dice to fewer than 0, the target has the Unconscious condition. At the end of each of its turns, the Unconscious target repeats the save, awakening on a success. If an oblivion stalker kills the creature while it has no Hit Dice, the target's soul is lost to the realm of shadow and can't be revived without mighty magic, such as a *Wish* or Divine Intervention.  
>
>***Unbearable Howl (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, each living creature in a 30-foot Cone that isn't Deafened. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. While Frightened, the target's Speed is halved. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. If the target succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to the Unbearable Howl of all oblivion brutes for 24 hours.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shadow Stealth.*** In Dim Light or Darkness, the oblivion brute can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action.  
>
> ##### Variant: Customizing Oblivion Stalkers
>
>- Since oblivion stalkers can drain almost any living creature's energy, and they can take just about any shape. Some oblivion stalkers retain the natural abilities of their original form. For instance, an oblivion stalker that was once a wolf might retain the Pack Tactics trait. Similarly, a boar could possess a version of Charge, draining its prey while adding Slashing damage and the possibility of knocking their victim Prone.
>- Keep one special ability that makes sense for a creature of the stalker's former form without affecting the Challenge of the stalker. Avoid bestowing an oblivion stalker any ability that is magical, grants the stalker a ranged attack, or expands its Resistances or Immunities.
