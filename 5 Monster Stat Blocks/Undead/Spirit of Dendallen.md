___
>## Spirit of Dendallen
>*Huge Undead (Wizard), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 225 (18d12 + 108)
>- **Speed** 0 ft. (see the Incorporeal Tether trait)
>- **Initiative** +17 (27)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|23 (+6)|22 (+6)|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +10, Int +13, Wis +11, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Arcana +20, Deception +17, History +13, Insight +11, Medicine +11, Perception +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Truesight 15 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Common plus three other languages; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 21 (XP 33,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Attuned Thrall.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 21, the creature that attuned to three Remnants of Dendallen (if the creature has Immunity to the Charmed condition or can't be possessed, it can still be affected, but it makes its saves against this effect with Advantage). *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition, becoming the host to the Spirit of Dendallen. On the spirit's turn, it can issue verbal or telepathic commands to the target (no action required). The target does its best to obey on its turn. If it completes an order and doesn't receive further direction from the spirit, it acts and moves as it likes, seemingly unaware of or unbothered by the presence of the spirit. The spirit can also command the target to take a Reaction but must take its own Reaction to do so.  
>The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect and dismissing the spirit on a success. The DC for this save decreases when the spirit drops to certain Hit Point thresholds, reducing by 1 at 150 HP (DC 20), 100 HP (DC 19), and 50 HP (DC 18).  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** The spirit can't shape-shift.  
>
>***Incorporeal Tether.*** The spirit can't move on its own. Instead, as the spirit's host moves, the spirit is moved with it (remaining centered on the host), even through other creatures and objects. The spirit can occupy another creature's space and vice versa.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the spirit fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The spirit has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Persistent Tendrils.*** If the spirit doesn't have any tendrils at the end of its turn, one Tendril of Dendallen is created, as if by the Create Tendrils action.  
>
>***Unstable Conduit.*** If the spirit's host dies, the spirit is destroyed in an explosion of necromantic energy, and the host is disintegrated into gray dust, leaving behind any Remnants of Dendallen it was wearing or carrying. The host can be revived only by a *True Resurrection* or a *Wish* spell. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the host. *Failure:* 33 (6d10) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The spirit makes two attacks, using Slam or Decaying Ray in any combination. It can (A) replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Magic Missile* (level 2 version) or (B) replace two attacks with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Blight* (level 9 version).  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +13, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Force damage plus 11 (2d10) Acid damage.  
>
>***Decaying Ray.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +13, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 21. *Failure:* The target gains 1 Exhaustion level. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, taking 5 (1d10) Necrotic damage on a failure or losing all Exhaustion levels caused by this effect on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.  
>
>***Create Tendrils (Recharge 5–6).*** The spirit creates 1d4 + 2 Tendrils of Dendallen, each appearing in unoccupied spaces the spirit can see on solid surfaces within 150 feet of itself. The spirit can have no more than six tendrils at a time. The tendrils take their turns immediately after the spirit on the same Initiative count, and they obey the spirit. A tendril remains for 1 minute or until it's reduced to 0 Hit Points.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The spirit casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 21):  
>
>At will: *Bestow Curse*, *Darkness*, *Dispel Magic*, *False Life* (level 3 version), *Hold Person* (level 3 version), *Mage Hand*, *Magic Missile* (level 2 version)  
>2/day each: *Blight* (level 9 version), *Finger of Death*, *Cloudkill*, *Telekinesis*, *Wall of Force*  
>1/day each: *Circle of Death*, *Prismatic Spray*, *Weird*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Magic.*** The spirit casts *Counterspell* or *Shield* in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the spirit of dendallen can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The spirit of dendallen regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Accursed Magic.** The spirit uses Spellcasting to cast *Bestow Curse*, *False Life* (level 3 version), or *Hold Person* (level 3 version). The spirit can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Command Tendrils.** Up to two Tendrils of Dendallen the spirit can see each either make a Grasp attack or use *Teleport*. The spirit can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Sweep.** The spirit sweeps out with its massive arm. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 19, each creature in a 20-foot Cone. *Failure:* 23 (3d12 + 4) Force damage, and the target is pushed up to 15 feet away from the spirit and has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
