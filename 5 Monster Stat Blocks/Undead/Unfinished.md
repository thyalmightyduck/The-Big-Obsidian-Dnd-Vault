___
>## Unfinished
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 130 (20d8 + 40)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 50 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Wis +4, Cha +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands all languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Semi-Corporeal.*** Parts of the unfinished fade in and out of the Material Plane. The unfinished has Advantage on saves to avoid or end the Grappled, Prone, and Restrained conditions. It makes another save at the beginning of its turns for an effect that causes any of these conditions, whether it normally allows a saving throw or not.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The unfinished makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 17 (3d8 + 4) Necrotic damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it is pushed 10 feet and has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The unfinished casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Hand*  
>3/day each: *Arcane Hand* (level 9 version), *Telekinesis*
