>## Hive Haunt
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 28)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +3
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Necrotic, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny insect.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hive haunt makes a Bite attack and a Sting attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage, or 4 (1d8) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage if the swarm has the Bloodied condition. The target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the swarm regains an equal number of Hit Points. The reduction lasts until the target finishes a Long Rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its Hit Point maximum to 0.  
>
>***Sting.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 5 (2d4) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage, or 4 (1d8) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage if the swarm has the Bloodied condition.*Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. While Poisoned, the target has the Bloodied condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.
