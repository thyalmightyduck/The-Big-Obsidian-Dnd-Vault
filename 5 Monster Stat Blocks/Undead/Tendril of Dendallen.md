___
>## Tendril of Dendallen
>*Large Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 37 (5d10 + 10)
>- **Speed** 0 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|2 (-4)|6 (-2)|3 (-4)|

>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Psychic, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Poisoned, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Immovable.*** The tendril can't move or be moved except via teleportation or interplanar travel.  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** The tendril can't shape-shift.  
>
>***Necromantic Demise.*** The tendril disappears if the Spirit of Dendallen is destroyed or dismissed.  
>
>***Will of Dendallen.*** The tendril has a telepathic link with the spirit and the spirit's host while it's on the same plane of existence as them. The tendril doesn't provide cover to enemies against attacks and effects that pass through its space.  
>
>***Grasp.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Force damage plus 4 (1d8) Acid damage. If the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) and is pulled up to 5 feet toward the tendril. The tendril can have only one target Grappled in this way at a time.  
>
>***Fling.*** The tendril throws a Medium or smaller creature Grappled by it to a space it can see within 60 feet of itself that isn't in the air. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, the creature thrown and each creature in the destination space. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Teleport.*** The tendril teleports up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space it can see on a solid surface.
