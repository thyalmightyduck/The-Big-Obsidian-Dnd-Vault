>## Psychic Fragment
>*Tiny Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 3 (1d4 + 1)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|3 (-4)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|2 (-4)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 0 (XP 10; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The psychic fragment can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>***Mimicry.*** The psychic fragment can mimic a single word or phrase it spoke in life. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful DC 11 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Withering Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) Necrotic damage.
