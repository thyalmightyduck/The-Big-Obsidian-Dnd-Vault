___
>## Helpful Hand
>*Tiny Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 5 (2d4)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Spider Climb.*** The hand can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Finger Jab.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1 Bludgeoning damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Latch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* The hand attaches itself to the target. While latched, the hand cannot attack. Instead, at the start of each of the hand's turns, the latched creature takes 2 (1d4) Necrotic damage. A creature within 5 feet can take an action to detach the hand with a successful DC 13 Strength check. The hand can also detach itself at any time during its turn (no action required).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Helper.*** The hand takes the Help action.
