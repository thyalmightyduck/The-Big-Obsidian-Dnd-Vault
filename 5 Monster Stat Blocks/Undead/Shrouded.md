___
>## Shrouded
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Corrupting Mist.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, a creature that starts its turn in the shrouded's space. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The soul of a Humanoid that dies from this damage becomes a new shrouded at the start of the shrouded's next turn and takes its turn on that Initiative.  
>The Humanoid can't be returned to life until the new shrouded is destroyed.  
>
>***Graveyard Warding.*** The shrouded can't move through a space with a barrier or line of iron, such as a wrought iron fence or a line of iron filings. This impassible barrier rises to a height of 1 mile above the iron.  
>
>***Mist Form.*** The shrouded can enter another creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without spending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Variation.*** The shrouded has one of three variants, which determines a unique action option: Devourer, Reaper, or Whisperer.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The shrouded makes two melee attacks.  
>
>***Corrupting Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Consuming Bite (Devourer Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). At the start of a Grappled target's turn, it takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the shrouded regains Hit Points equal to that amount.  
>
>***Scythe (Reaper Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, it can't regain Hit Points until the start of the shrouded's next turn.  
>
>***Toxic Thoughts (Whisperer Only).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature that isn't a Construct or Undead in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the shrouded. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the start of the shrouded's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
