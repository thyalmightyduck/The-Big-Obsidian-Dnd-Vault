___
>## Festerhulk
>*Huge Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 207 (18d12 + 90)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|25 (+7)|9 (-1)|20 (+5)|3 (-4)|6 (-2)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +2
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned, Stunned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands Common and Giant but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Fungal Step.*** The festerhulk ignores Difficult Terrain caused by plants or fungi.  
>
>***Spore Cloud.*** At the end of each of the festerhulk's turns, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the festerhulk takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage and has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn. A Giant or Humanoid killed by this damage rises as a new creature 24 hours later: Festerhulk (if a Giant), **Zombie** (if a Humanoid).  
>
>***Sunlight Hypersensitivity.*** The festerhulk takes 10 Radiant damage if it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The festerhulk makes two Fetid Slam attacks and uses Slime Mold if available.  
>
>***Fetid Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. Hit:20 (2d12 + 7) Bludgeoning damage plus 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Slime Mold (1/Day).*** The festerhulk disgorges a mass of ravenous slime mold into an unoccupied space within 20 feet. This mass uses the Black Pudding stat block except it is a Plant, and its size is Medium. The mass acts on the festerhulk's Initiative. It, and any masses it Split into, dries up and dies in 1 hour.
