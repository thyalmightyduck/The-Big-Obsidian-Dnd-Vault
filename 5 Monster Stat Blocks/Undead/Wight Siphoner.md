___
>## Wight Siphoner
>*Medium Undead (Minion), typically Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 10
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** The languages they knew in life
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 140; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Minion.*** If the siphoner takes damage from an attack or as the result of a failed saving throw, their hit points are reduced to 0. If the siphoner takes damage from another effect, they die if the damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum; otherwise they take no damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Grasping Resurrection (Group Attack).*** *Melee Spell Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 necrotic damage. If three or more siphoners joined the attack, they can restore the remains of a destroyed Undead minion within 15 feet of the target. That Undead minion returns to life with all their hit points. If five or more siphoners joined the group attack, they can either choose to increase the range of this effect from 15 to 30 feet or to restore two Undead minions instead of one.
