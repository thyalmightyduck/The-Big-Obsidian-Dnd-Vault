>## Grasping Shadow
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 1 (1d4)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Poison, Radiant, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands the languages it knew in life but mostly just screams
>- **Challenge** 0 (XP 10; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The shadow can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spectral Grasp.*** The shadow causes one creature it can see within 5 feet to have the Grappled condition. A target that takes an action to escape automatically ends the grapple.  
>
>***Death Row.*** The shadow moves up to its Speed, pulling a creature it has Grappled with it.
