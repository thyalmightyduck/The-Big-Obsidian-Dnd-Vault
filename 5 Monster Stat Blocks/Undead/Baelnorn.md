___
>## Baelnorn
>*Medium Undead (Wizard), Neutral Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 255 (34d8 + 102)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +15 (25)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|21 (+5)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +17, History +11, Insight +8, Perception +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Lightning, Radiant
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** All
>- **Challenge** 18 (XP 20,000, or 22,000 in lair; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Lair).*** If the baelnorn fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The baelnorn has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The baelnorn makes three attacks, using Arcane Burst or Paralyzing Touch in any combination. It can replace two attacks with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Lightning Bolt* (level 4 version).  
>
>***Arcane Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 31 (4d12 + 5) Force damage.  
>
>***Paralyzing Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 5) Cold damage, and the target has the Paralyzed condition until the start of the baelnorn's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The baelnorn casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Dispel Magic*, *Invisibility*, *Lightning Bolt* (level 4 version), *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Dimension Door*, *Scrying*  
>1/day each: *Clone*, *Guards and Wards*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell.*** The baelnorn casts *Counterspell* in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, the baelnorn can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The baelnorn regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Frightening Glance.** The baelnorn casts *Fear*, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting. The baelnorn can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.  
>
>- **Overwhelming Radiance.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the baelnorn; Undead have Disadvantage on this save. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Radiant damage. *Success:* Half damage.
>- **Recuperative Teleport.** The baelnorn teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see and regains 11 (2d10) Hit Points. The baelnorn can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
