___
>## Lake Dredger
>*Huge Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 157 (15d12 + 60)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|4 (-3)|12 (+1)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Depicted Demise.*** The lake dredger takes 20 Force damage at the start of its turn if it can see a creature holding a created depiction (such as a drawing, painting, or carving) of the lake dredger.  
>
>***Silt Stealth.*** The lake dredger has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made when in an area with aquatic plants or seaweed.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lake dredger makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 24 (4d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Swallow.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18, one Large or smaller creature Grappled by the lake dredger (the lake dredger can only have one creature swallowed at a time). *Failure:* The lake dredger swallows the target, which is no longer Grappled. While swallowed, the creature has the Restrained condition, has Three-Quarters Cover against attacks and other effects outside the lake dredger, and takes 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage at the start of each of the lake dredger's turns.  
>As an action, the swallowed creature can escape through the lake dredger's ribcage. The creature takes 11 (2d10) Piercing damage and emerges in a space within 5 feet of the lake dredger. If the lake dredger dies, a swallowed creature is no longer Restrained and can escape from the corpse by using 15 feet of movement, exiting Prone.
