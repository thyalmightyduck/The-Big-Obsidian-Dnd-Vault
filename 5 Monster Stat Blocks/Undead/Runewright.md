___
>## Runewright
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 143 (26d8 + 26)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +9
>- **Skills** Arcana +9, Intimidation +8, Perception +7, Persuasion +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The runewright has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The runewright makes three Rune Blast attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Runic Gaze.  
>
>***Rune Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Radiant damage (the runewright's choice based on the rune being used).  
>
>***Runic Gaze.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17 One creature the runewright can see with 60 feet. *Failure:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target has the Charmed or Frightened condition (the runewright's choice) until the start of the runewright's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The runewright casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Disguise Self*, *Identify*, *Water Breathing*  
>2/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Locate Object*, *Speak with Dead*  
>1/day each: *Dimension Door*, *Fire Shield*, *Freedom of Movement*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Spellcasting.*** The runewright casts the *Misty Step* spell.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sun Rune.*** *Trigger:* The runewright is hit by an attack roll. *Response:* The attacking creature must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be Blinded until the end of its next turn.
