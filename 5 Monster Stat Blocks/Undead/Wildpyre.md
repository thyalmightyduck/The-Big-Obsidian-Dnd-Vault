___
>## Wildpyre
>*Medium or Large Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|4 (-3)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +7, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Fire, Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The wildpyre can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>***Quenching Water.*** Any water affects the wildpyre as if it were Holy Water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The wildpyre makes two Blazing Rend attacks and uses Flame Bind if available.  
>
>***Blazing Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Necrotic damage plus 7 (2d6) Fire damage, and the target begins burning. If the target is a creature no larger than the wildpyre, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Flame Bind (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature the wildpyre can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Restrained condition. At the end of each of the target's turns, it repeats the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success. During the target's turn, it can choose to end the Restrained condition by taking 6 (1d12) Fire damage plus 6 (1d12) Necrotic damage.
