>## Wretched Master of Ceremonies
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 97 (15d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Insight +5, Perception +5, Persuasion +7
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Turning Defiance.*** The wretched master of ceremonies and any Undead within 30 feet of it have Advantage on saving throws against effects that turn undead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The wretched master of ceremonies makes two Whip attacks. It can replace one attack with Macabre Direction.  
>
>***Disguise Self.*** The wretched master of ceremonies casts *Disguise Self* (save DC 15), requiring no components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability.  
>
>***Whip.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller, the master moves the target to another unoccupied space within the whip's reach.  
>
>***Macabre Direction.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature of the wretched master of ceremonies' choice within 30 feet that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition until the start of the master's next turn. The master can use the target's Reaction, forcing it to make a weapon attack or cast a cantrip at a target of the master's choice.  
>
>***Jubilant Thralls (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15, each Humanoid of the wretched master of ceremonies' choice within 30 feet of it that can hear it (creatures fighting the master have Advantage). *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition. While Charmed, the target has the Incapacitated condition, its Speed is reduced to 0, and it has Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to perceive creatures other than the master.  
>Each time the target takes damage, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. Otherwise, the effect ends after 1 hour or if the master is destroyed or on a different plane of existence than the target. The master can end the effect early as a Bonus Action.  
>If the target remains Charmed for 1 minute, it no longer has the Incapacitated condition and regains its Speed. However, it remains Charmed, regarding the master as someone to be heeded and protected. It takes the master's requests and actions in the most favorable way possible. The target protects the master from those trying to harm it, but takes great risks only if bravery or foolhardiness is part of its normal nature.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step.*** The wretched master of ceremonies casts *Misty Step*, requiring no components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability.
