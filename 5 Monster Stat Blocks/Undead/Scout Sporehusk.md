___
>## Scout Sporehusk
>*Small or Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 32 (5d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|11 (+0)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire, Radiant
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Leather Armor*, *Longbow*, *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Blindsight 15 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 7
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The sporehusk regains 5 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If it takes Fire or Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function on the sporehusk's next turn. The sporehusk dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Sunlight Weakness.*** While in sunlight, the sporehusk has Disadvantage on D20 Tests.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The scout makes two attacks, using Shortsword and Longbow in any combination.  
>
>***Grab.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 11).  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. (must target a creature Grappled by the sporehusk). *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) Piercing damage.
