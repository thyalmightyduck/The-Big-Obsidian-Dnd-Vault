>## Indentured Spirit
>*Medium Undead, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 13 (3d8)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___

>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Fire, Lightning, Thunder; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
>- **Immunities** Cold, Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The spirit can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
