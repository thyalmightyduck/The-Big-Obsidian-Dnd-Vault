>## Hazeblood Vampire
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 45 (6d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|8 (-1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The vampire is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The vampire may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The vampire has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the vampire's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes two attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Contaminated Bite.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 5) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13).  
>
>***Contaminated Bite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 17 (4d6 + 3) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** The vampire touches a delerium shard and regains 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the vampire has used a delerium shard in this way, it can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The vampire moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see.
