___
>## Skeleton Archer
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|8 (-1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Quiver*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Chillsteel Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Cold damage. Critical *Hit:* The target has the Frozen condition (DC 10).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Steady Aim.*** The skeleton archer grants itself Advantage on its next ranged attack roll this turn. It can use this Bonus Action only if it hasn't moved during this turn, and after it uses this Bonus Action, its Speed becomes 0 and can't increase until the end of this turn.
