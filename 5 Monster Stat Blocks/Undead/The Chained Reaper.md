>## The Chained Reaper
>*Gargantuan Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 201 (13d20 + 65)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +7, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Nature +4, Perception +7, Survival +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Orc
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Ancestral Shame.*** If a creature that can speak Orc within 30 feet of the reaper takes an action to read aloud the names carved in the stone on the reaper's back, the reaper takes 20 Radiant damage.  
>
>***Ethereal Sight.*** The reaper can see 60 feet into the Ethereal Plane when it is on the Material Plane.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The reaper can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the reaper fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The reaper makes one Balefire Scythe attack and one Chain attack, and it uses either Chain Slam or Fling.  
>
>***Balefire Scythe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Necrotic damage, and the target begins burning. It takes Necrotic damage instead of Fire damage while burning in this way.  
>
>***Chain.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) from one of four chains.  
>
>***Chain Slam.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature Grappled by the reaper. *Failure:* 9 (1d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the start of the reaper's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Etherealness.*** The reaper casts the *Etherealness* spell, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability. The reaper is visible on the Material Plane while on the Border Ethereal and vice versa, but it can't affect or be affected by anything on the other plane.  
>
>***Fling.*** The reaper throws a Large or smaller creature Grappled by it to a space it can see within 30 feet of itself that isn't in the air. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, the creature thrown and each creature in the destination space. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Balefire Scythe.** The reaper makes one Balefire Scythe attack. The reaper can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Drift.** The reaper moves up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
>- **Lantern Flare.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature of the reaper's choice in a 20-foot Emanation originating from it. *Failure:* The target has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Chained Reaper's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Chained Vitality.** A creature that isn't a Construct or Undead that finishes a Long Rest within 1 mile of the lair must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have its Hit Point maximum reduced by 5 (to a minimum of 1 HP).
>- **Falling Shroud.** Thin mist arises within 1 mile of the lair. Undead with the Incorporeal Movement trait in that area have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
>
>If the Chained Reaper dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
