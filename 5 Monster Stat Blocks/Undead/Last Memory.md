>## Last Memory
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|1 (-5)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Eternal Torment.*** If a last memory dies, it reappears 1d4 days later, unleashing a howling scream that can be heard up to 300 feet away.  
>
>***Silhouette.*** The last memory can only move along contiguous flat surfaces, such as walls or floors. It cannot fly or swim and must always be in contact with a surface. Additionally, the last memory ignores all natural Difficult Terrain and can climb without making an ability check.  
>
>***Unusual Nature.*** The last memory doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.  
>
>### Actions
>***Sorrowful Touch.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 30 ft. *Hit:* 12 (3d6 + 2) Psychic damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Anguish (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 10, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the last memory that can hear it. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage and the target becomes Jinxed 1d6 (save ends).
