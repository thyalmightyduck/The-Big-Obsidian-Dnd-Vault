>## Night Blade
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 117 (18d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +11 (21)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Con +5
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +8, Deception +6, Perception +7, Stealth +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Poison
>- **Gear** *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Bloodthirsty.*** The vampire has Advantage on attack rolls against a target with the Bloodied or Bleeding condition.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The vampire regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes two attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Bite.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 5) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16).  
>
>***Spectral Weapons.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 100/400 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Force damage, and the target suffers one of the following effects of the vampire's choice (the vampire can't select the same option more than once per turn):  
>- **Poison.** The target takes an extra 9 (2d8) Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition (save ends, *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16).
>- **Open Wounds.** The target has the Bleeding condition (3d6).
>- **Mindmuddler.** The target has the Bewildered condition (save ends, *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16).
>
>***Bite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 15 (3d6 + 5) Necrotic damage, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Meld Into Shadow.*** In Dim Light or Darkness, the vampire casts *Invisibility* on itself, requiring no components and using Charisma as its spellcasting ability.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Spectral Dodge.*** *Trigger:* The vampire takes damage. *Response:* The vampire gains Resistance to that damage, then moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.
