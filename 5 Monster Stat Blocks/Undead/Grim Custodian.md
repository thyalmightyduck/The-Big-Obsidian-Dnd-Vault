___
>## Grim Custodian
>*Medium Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +7, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Deception +10, Insight +5, Perception +5, Persuasion +10
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The vampire regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes two attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Bite or Spellcasting.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15).  
>
>***Bite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Necrotic damage, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The vampire casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Charm Person*, *Command*, *Disguise Self*, *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*  
>2/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *Invisibility*, *Nystul's Magic Aura*, *Major Image* (level 4 version), *Nondetection*, *Suggestion*  
>1/day each: *Animate Dead*, *Arcane Eye*, *Mislead*, *Seeming*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shadow Step.*** The vampire teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 60 feet.
