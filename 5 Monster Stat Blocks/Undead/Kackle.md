___
>## Kackle
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 67 (15d8)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Gear** *Hooded Lantern*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The kackle can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>***Lantern Vulnerability.*** The kackle's lantern has AC 15 and Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage. Any damage the lantern takes deals half that amount of Force damage to the kackle.  
>
>### Actions
>***Lantern Flame.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 90 ft. *Hit:* Varies by lantern color (kackle's choice):  
>
>***Blue.*** 7 (1d6 + 4) Cold damage, and the target has the Restrained condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Green.*** 20 (3d10 + 4) Necrotic damage, and the target's Speed is 0 until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Purple.*** 10 (1d12 + 4) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Red.*** 15 (2d10 + 4) Fire damage, and the target starts burning, taking 5 (1d10) Fire damage instead of the regular burning damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Flickering Teleport.*** The kackle teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. The kackle can then change its lantern color, and it can create the following—*Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the kackle's destination space. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) damage of the same type as the current Lantern Flame color. *Success:* Half damage.
