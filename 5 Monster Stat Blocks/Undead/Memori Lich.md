___
>## Memori Lich
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 266 (28d8 + 140)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Con +11, Wis +8
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Athletics +11, Intimidation +8, Perception +8
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** One or two languages
>- **Challenge** 19 (XP 22,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Action Surge (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The memori lich can take an extra Action on its turn.  
>
>***Fast Healing.*** While Bloodied, the memori lich regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. This trait doesn't function while the lich has 0 Hit Points.  
>
>***Immutable.*** No effect can alter the memori lich's form.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day, or 5/Day in Lair).*** If the memori lich fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If destroyed while its memento skull remains intact, the memori lich reforms in 2d4 days, regaining all of its Hit Points. Its new body appears within 5 feet of the memento skull.  
>
>***Superior Critical.*** The memori lich scores a Critical Hit on a roll of 18, 19, or 20 on the d20.  
>
>***Turn Resistance.*** The memori lich is immune to effects that Turn Undead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The memori lich uses Stunning Presence and makes four Greatsword attacks. It can replace one attack with Wall of Fire.  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Slashing damage and 11 (2d10) Force damage.  
>
>***Stunning Presence.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 120-foot Emanation originating from the memori lich that is aware of it. *Failure:* The target has the Stunned condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. If the target succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to the memori lich's Stunning Presence for 24 hours.  
>
>***Wall of Fire (Recharge 5–6).*** The memori lich casts *Wall of Fire* (level 6 version; spell save DC 16). This special casting of the spell doesn't require Concentration and lasts until the start of the memori lich's next turn.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, the memori lich can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The memori lich regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
>
>- **Attack.** The memori lich makes a Greatsword attack.
>- **Move.** The memori lich moves up to its Speed.
>- **Binding Curse.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the memori lich can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target is cursed. While cursed, the target can't move more than 30 feet from the memori lich and takes an extra 5 (1d10) Force damage from the memori lich's attacks. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. The effect ends early if the memori lich dies or has the Incapacitated condition. If the target dies while cursed, the memori lich regains 20 Hit Points. *Failure or Success:* The memori lich can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Siphon Vitality (Recharge 5–6).** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the memori lich. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage. The memori lich can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing a memori lich's lair takes on the horrible and powerful essence of its master, creating the following effects:
>- When the memori lich become Bloodied, the ground within 120 feet of the creature shakes violently. Each creature on the memori lich's choice in that area must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.
>- All creatures within a mile of the lair only regain half as many Hit Points from magical healing.
>- When a creature of the memori lich's choice becomes Bloodied, ghostly hands emerge from the ground. The creature must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving throw or have the Grappled condition (escape DC 18). Once the creature succeeds on the saving throw or escapes, the hands disappear.
