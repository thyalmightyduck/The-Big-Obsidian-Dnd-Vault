___
>## Bog Body
>*Medium Undead (Ambusher), typically Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 22 (3d8 + 9)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +2, Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Acid, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands the languages they knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Acidic Blood.*** A creature who deals piercing or slashing damage to the bog body while within 5 feet of them takes 3 (1d6) acid damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, they are grappled (escape DC 13). Until this grapple ends, the bog body can't make a Slam attack against another target.  
>
>***Sling Mud.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) acid damage.  
>
>***Submerge.*** If the bog body is in swampy terrain, they can submerge themself beneath the water and mud. While submerged, the bog body is heavily obscured.  
>If the bog body is grappling a target when they submerge, the target submerges with them. While submerged, the target is heavily obscured, restrained, and unable to breathe, and takes 4 (1d8) acid damage at the start of each of their turns.  
>A bog body can resurface as a bonus action. When they do so, a target they have grappled also resurfaces.
