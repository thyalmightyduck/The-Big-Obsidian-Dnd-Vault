___
>## Vampire Spawn
>*Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 115 (14d8 + 52)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Wis +3
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +3, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** The languages known in life
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The vampire regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns if it has at least 1 Hit Point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has the following flaws:  
>*Forbiddance.* The vampire can't enter a residence without an invitation from an occupant.  
>*Running Water.* The vampire takes 20 Acid damage when it ends its turn in running water.  
>*Stake to the Heart.* The vampire dies if a weapon that deals Piercing damage is driven into the vampire's heart while it has the Incapacitated condition.  
>*Sunlight Hypersensitivity.* The vampire takes 10 Radiant damage when it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes two Claw attacks and uses Bite.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) from one of two claws.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or that has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. *Failure:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. The target's Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to that amount.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Deathless Agility.*** The vampire takes the Dash or Disengage action.
