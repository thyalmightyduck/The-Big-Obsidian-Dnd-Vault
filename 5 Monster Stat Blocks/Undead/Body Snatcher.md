___
>## Body Snatcher
>*Large Undead*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 136 (16d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|7 (-2)|14 (+2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +6
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Clinging Shadows.*** A creature in Dim Light or Darkness has Disadvantage on ability checks to escape the body snatcher's grapple. Each time a creature attempts to escape the body snatcher's grapple while it is in Dim Light or Darkness, the creature takes 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage. If this Necrotic damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, the target is stabilized but has the Paralyzed condition for 1 hour, even if it regains Hit Points.  
>
>***Sunlight Weakness.*** The body snatcher takes 10 (3d6) Radiant damage if it ends its turn in sunlight, and has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks while in sunlight.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The body snatcher makes four Grabbing Hands attacks.  
>
>***Grabbing Hands.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). Until this grapple ends, the body snatcher can't use this hand against another target. The body snatcher has four hands, each of which can grapple one target.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shadow Teleport.*** The body snatcher teleports up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space that it can see that is in Dim Light or Darkness. It can bring any Medium or smaller creature it is grappling, teleporting that creature to an unoccupied space it can see within 5 feet of its destination that is also in Dim Light or Darkness. An unwilling creature can make a DC 13 Charisma saving throw to resist being teleported. On a successful save, neither the creature nor the body snatcher *Teleport*.
