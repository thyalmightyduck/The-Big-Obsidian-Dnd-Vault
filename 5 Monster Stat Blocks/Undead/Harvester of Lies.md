___
>## Harvester of Lies
>*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 130 (20d8 + 40)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Wis +7, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +9, Insight +7, Perception +7, Stealth +13
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Coat of Lies*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Coat of Lies.*** While wearing their specially prepared coat, the harvester's AC includes its Charisma modifier (included above) and it can use its Innate Spellcasting.  
>
>***Hear Lies.*** The harvester hears all lies uttered within 1 mile of it. By maintaining Concentration, the harvester can sense the liar's distance and direction.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The harvester can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Trackless.*** The harvester leaves no tracks to indicate where it has been or where it is heading.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The harvester makes three Claw attacks. It can replace one attack with Sever Tongue.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Slashing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17).  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The harvestercasts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components except its *Coat of Lies* and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Detect Thoughts*, *Zone of Truth*  
>3/day each: *Hold Person*, *Invisibility*, *Silence*  
>1/day each: *Levitate*, *Tongues*  
>
>***Sever Tongue.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft., one Humanoid Grappled by the harvester. *Hit:* 33 (8d6 + 5) Slashing damage.*Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target's tongue is ripped out. The target can't speak or cast spells with Verbal components. A creature with Immunity to Slashing damage, Legendary Resistance, Legendary Actions, or the ability to speak without a tongue has Immunity to this effect.  
>A tongue can be reattached within 1 hour of being severed with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check, or can be regrown using magic that replaces lost limbs.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (3/Day).*** The harvester casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
