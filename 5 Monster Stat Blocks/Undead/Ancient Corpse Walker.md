>## Ancient Corpse Walker
>*Large Undead (Shapechanger), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 171 (18d10 + 72)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren't silvered
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Cursed Gaze.*** A Humanoid that starts its turn within 30 feet of thecorpse walker is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, with Disadvantage unless it is averting its eyes from the corpse walker. On a success, it ends the effect on itself and is immune to Cursed Gaze for 24 hours. If the target fails two saves, it is cursed with disturbing thoughts, and can't benefit from Short or Long Rest until the curse is removed.  
>Unless surprised, the target can avert its eyes to avoid the corpse walker's Cursed Gaze. If it does, it can't see the corpse walker until the start of its next turn. If the target looks at the corpse walker in the meantime, it is immediately subjected to Cursed Gaze.  
>
>***Spore Calling.*** When the corpse walker rolls Initiative, Undead allies within 120 feet come to its aid. Moderate or stronger wind can disperse these spores before they are effective.  
>
>***Spore Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the corpse walker to 0 Hit Points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 plus the damage taken unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. *Success:* The corpse walker drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>When it drops to 0 Hit Points, the corpse walker transforms into its Spore Cloud form. If it can't transform, it is destroyed. The spores of this cloud deal half their normal damage and can't animate corpses. The corpse walker can't revert to its true form until it has at least 1 Hit Point. It must reach its resting place within 2 hours or be destroyed. Once in its resting place, it reverts to its normal form and has the Paralyzed condition. After 1 hour, it regains 1 Hit Point and loses the Paralyzed condition.  
>If the corpse walker takes damage as a cloud, it makes Constitution saving throws as described above, remaining at 0 Hit Points on a success. If it fails or can't make the saving throw, it is destroyed.  
>
>***Sunlight Hypersensitivity.*** The corpse walker takes 10 Radiant damage when it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The corpse walker makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the corpse walker regains Hit Points equal to that amount. The reduction lasts until the target finishes a Long Rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its Hit Point maximum to 0. An Evil Humanoid slain in this way rises 24 hours later as a corpse walker loyal to the ancient corpse walker.  
>
>***Spore Cloud.*** If the corpse walker isn't in sunlight, it can shape-shift into a cloud of spores or revert to its true form. Anything it is wearing or carrying shape-shifts with it. In cloud form, the corpse walker can't take Actions besides this one and can't speak or manipulate objects. The cloud is weightless, has a Flying Speed of 20 feet, can Hover, and can enter a Hostile creature's space and stop there. The cloud spreads around corners. If air can pass through a space, the cloud can do so without without expending extra movement to do so, but it can't pass through water. It has Advantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws, except those against wind. The cloud has Immunity to nonmagical damage other than from sunlight.  
>A creature that starts its turn in the cloud is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Poison damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage. The corpses of Beasts and Humanoids that the cloud passes over rise 24 hours later as zombies loyal to the corpse walker. If the corpse was an Evil Humanoid, it rises as a corpse walker loyal to the ancient corpse walker. The cloud can animate each corpse once.
