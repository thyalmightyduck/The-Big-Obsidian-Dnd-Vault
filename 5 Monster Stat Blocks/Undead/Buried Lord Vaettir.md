___
>## Buried Lord Vaettir
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Con +5, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Intimidation +5, Perception +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Longsword*, *Longbow*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Truesight 30 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** The languages it knew in life
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Aura of Authority.*** While in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the buried lord, the buried lord's allies have Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws, provided the buried lord doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Grave Goods Sense.*** The buried lord can pinpoint the location of creatures wearing or carrying one of the vættr's personal items or grave goods within 60 feet of it, and the vættr can sense the general direction of its personal items and grave goods within 1 mile of it.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** A destroyed buried lord vættr gains a new body in 24 hours, regaining all its Hit Points and becoming active again. The new body forms in a random unoccupied space within 100 feet of where it fell. This trait doesn't function if the vættr is put to rest (typically by returning its remains to its burial mound).  
>
>***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the buried lord vaettir has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The buried lord vaettir makes two Longsword or two Longbow attacks.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 150/600 feet., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Corpse Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13 Each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.  
>
>***Disorienting Gaze.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13 One creature the buried lord can see within 30 feet of it. *Failure:* For 1 minute, the target has the Incapacitated condition and moves in a random direction whenever it moves. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. *Success:* The target has Immunity to this buried lord's Disorienting Gaze for 24 hours.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The buried lord vaettir casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>2/day each: *Bane*  
>1/day each: *Enlarge/Reduce*, *Gaseous Form*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hunter (2/Day).*** The buried lord vaettir casts *Hunter's Mark*.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* The buried lord vaettir is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon or shield. *Response:* The buried lord vaettir adds 2 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
