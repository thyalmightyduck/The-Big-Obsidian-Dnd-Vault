___
>## Centaur Skeleton
>*Large Undead, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 45 (6d10 + 12)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|9 (-1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Pike*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Charge.*** If the centaur moves at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a pike attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 10 (3d6) piercing damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The centaur skeleton makes two attacks: one with its pike and one with its hooves, or two with its longbow.  
>
>***Pike.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.  
>
> ##### Variant: Centaur Skeleton Customization
>
>Some centaur skeletons have the following additional feature at the GM's discretion.
>
>***Spirit Away.*** The centaur skeleton and up to three creatures within 5 feet of it magically enter the Ethereal Plane from the Material Plane, or vice versa. An unwilling creature that succeeds on a DC 13 Charisma saving throw can resist this effect.
>
>**Source:** *TDCSR*, page 256
