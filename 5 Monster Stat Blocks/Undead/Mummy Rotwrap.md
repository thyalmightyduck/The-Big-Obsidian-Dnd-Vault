>## Mummy Rotwrap
>*Medium Undead (Minion), typically Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 11
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** The languages they knew in life
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 220; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Minion.*** If the rotwrap takes damage from an attack or as the result of a failed saving throw, their hit points are reduced to 0. If the rotwrap takes damage from another effect, they die if the damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum; otherwise they take no damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Words of Ancient Doom (Group Attack).*** *Melee  or Ranged Spell Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 4 necrotic damage, and if three or more rotwraps joined the attack, the target can't speak or use telepathy until the end of their next turn.
