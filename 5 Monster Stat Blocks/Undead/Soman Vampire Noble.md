>## Soman Vampire Noble
>*Medium Undead, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Deception +8, Intimidation +8, Perception +6, Persuasion +8
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Radiant
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Poison
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Rapier*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Ostoy and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Brute.*** A melee weapon deals one extra die of its damage when the vampire hits with it (included in the attacks).  
>
>***Evasion.*** If the vampire is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, the vampire instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails.  
>
>***Guarded Mind.*** The vampire's emotions and thoughts can't be read.  
>
>***Vampire Weaknesses.*** The vampire has these weaknesses:  
>- **Forbiddance.** The vampire can enter a residence only with an invitation from one of that home's residents. If the vampire enters a house uninvited and involuntarily, it takes 1d10 Psychic damage at the end of each of its turns while it remains there.
>- **Stake to the Heart.** If a weapon that deals Piercing damage if driven into the vampire's heart while it has the Incapacitated condition in its resting place, the vampire has the Paralyzed condition until the weapon is removed.
>- **Sunlight.** The vampire takes 10 Radiant damage if it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, the vampire has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes four attacks, using Rapier or Dagger in any combination. It can replace one attack with Bite.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage, and the vampire regains Hit Point equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage, or 22 (4d8 + 4) Piercing damage if the attack was made with Advantage.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Charm (Recharge 5–6).*** The vampire casts *Charm Person*, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16). The Charmed target is a willing recipient of the vampire's Bite, the damage of which doesn't end the spell. When the spell ends, the target is unaware it was Charmed by the vampire.  
>
>***Creatures of the Night (1/Day).*** Provided it isn't in direct sunlight, the vampire magically calls 5 (2d4) **Swarms of Bats** or **Swarm of Rats**. While outdoors, the vampire can call 5 (2d4) **Wolves** instead. The called beasts arrive in 2 (1d4) rounds, acting as allies of the vampire and obeying its spoken commands. The beasts remain for 1 hour, until the vampire dies, or until the vampire dismisses them as a Bonus Action.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Teleport (Recharge 5–6).*** The vampire disappears in shadowy mist and teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Horrifying Revelation.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 30 feet of the vampire deals damage to it. *Response:*—*Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. If the target succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to this vampire's Horrifying Revelation for 24 hours.
