>## Runa Banasár
>*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 21
>- **Hit Points** 380 (40d8 + 200)
>- **Speed** 60 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Con +12, Wis +10, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Arcana +8, Athletics +12, Intimidation +10, Perception +10
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Coldrazor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** Valikan
>- **Challenge** 23 (XP 50,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Brute.*** A weapon deals two extra dice of its damage when Runa hits with it (included in her attack).  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** Runa is immune to any effect that would alter her form.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If Runa fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If destroyed while her memento skull remains intact, Runa's body reforms in 2d4 days, regaining all her Hit Points. Her new body appears within 5 feet of the memento skull.  
>
>***Turn Immunity.*** Runa is immune to effects that Turn Undead.  
>
>***Unarmored Defense.*** When not wearing armor, Runa's Armor Class equals 10 + her Dexterity modifier + her Constitution modifier. She can use a *Shield* while gaining this benefit.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Runa uses Stunning Presence and makes four attacks with *Coldrazor*. She can use Wall of Fire in place of one attack.  
>
>***Coldrazor (+3 Handaxe).*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d6 + 8) Slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) Cold damage. Critical Hit: 32 (7d6 + 8) Slashing damage plus 24 (8d6) Cold damage. *Hit or Miss:*  Coldrazor returns to Runa's hand immediately after being thrown  
>
>***Stunning Presence.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 120-foot Emanation originating from Runa that is aware of her. *Failure:* The target has the Stunned condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. If the target succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to Runa's Stunning Presence for 24 hours.  
>
>***Wall of Fire (Recharge 5–6).*** Runa casts *Wall of Fire* (level 9 version, spell save DC 18). This special version of the spell lasts until the start of Runa's next turn and doesn't require Concentration.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Rampage.*** *Trigger:* Runa reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points with a Coldrazor attack. *Response:* Runa can move up to her Speed and makes a Coldrazor attack. Special: Runa regains her Reaction before and after using this ability.  
>
>***Retaliate.*** *Trigger:* Runa takes damage from a creature within 5 feet of her. *Response:* Runa makes a melee weapon attack against that creature.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Runa can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Runa regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Attack.** Runa makes a *Coldrazor* attack.
>- **Move.** Runa moves up to her Speed.
>- **Curse of Cowardice.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 18, one Stunned creature Runa can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target is cursed. While Stunned, the target has Disadvantage on saving throws and takes an extra 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage from Runa's weapon attacks. This effect ends when the creature is no longer Stunned. *Failure or Success:* Runa can't use this action again until the start of her next turn.
>- **Siphon Vitality (Recharge 6).** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from Runa. *Failure:* 39 (6d12) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage.
