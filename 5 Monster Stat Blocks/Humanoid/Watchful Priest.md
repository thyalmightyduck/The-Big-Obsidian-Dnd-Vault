___
>## Watchful Priest
>*Medium Humanoid, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 60 (11d8 + 11)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Insight +5, Intimidation +5, Perception +5, Persuasion +5, Religion +3
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** One regional language plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Enraging Sacrifice.*** When the priest dies, each ally within 20 feet who can see the priest gains 10 Temporary Hit Points and a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls for 1 minute.  
>
>***Zealous Aura.*** The priest radiates an aura of zeal in a 20-foot Emanation originating from it. Each ally in the area has Advantage on Initiative checks, and their successful attacks deal an extra 1d4 Radiant damage. This aura becomes inactive if the priest has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The priest makes two Castigating Word attacks.  
>
>***Castigating Word.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (2d6) Radiant damage, and one ally of the priest within 20 feet of the target can take a Reaction to make a melee attack.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The priest casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *Prestidigitation*  
>1/day each: *Bless*, *Calm Emotions*, *Charm Person*, *Healing Word*, *Heroism*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Righteous Morale (1/Day).*** The priest chooses up to three other creatures it can see within 20 feet of it. Each target regains 4 (1d8) Hit Points and has  Immunity to the Charmed and Frightened conditions until the start of the priest's next turn.
