>## Sahuagin Claw of Sha'argon
>*Large Humanoid, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 178 (17d10 + 85)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|14 (+2)|21 (+5)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +9, Dex +6, Con +9, Wis +8
>- **Skills** History +6, Perception +12, Religion +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 22
>- **Languages** Aquan, Common, Sahuagin
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Blood Frenzy.*** The sahuagin has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that doesn't have all its Hit Points.  
>
>***Limited Amphibiousness.*** The sahuagin can breathe air and water, but it must be submerged at least once every 4 hours to avoid suffocating outside water.  
>
>***Shark Telepathy.*** The sahuagin can magically control sharks within 120 feet of itself, using a special telepathy.  
>
>***Toughened Carapace.*** Any Critical Hit against the sahuagin becomes a normal hit.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The sahuagin makes three attacks, using Crushing Claw or Cutter Claw in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Crushing Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) from the Crushing Claw.  
>
>***Cutter Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 24 (3d12 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The sahuagin casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):  
>
>At will: *Guidance*, *Mending*, *Resistance*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Banishment*, *Command* (level 3 version), *Flame Strike*, *Hold Person* (level 3 version)  
>1/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Freedom of Movement*, *Harm*
