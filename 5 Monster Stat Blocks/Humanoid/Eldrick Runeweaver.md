___
>## Eldrick Runeweaver
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30) plus 75 (10d8 + 30) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|22 (+6)|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Dex +5, Con +8, Int +11, Wis +9, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +16, History +16, Perception +14, Persuasion +12
>- **Damage Resistances** Damage from Spells
>- **Gear** *Robe of the Archmagi*, *Staff of Power*
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 24
>- **Languages** All
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Academy Rings (1/Day).*** Eldrick can cast a spell from Spellcasting without expending a use of it.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** Eldrick has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Warding Aura.*** Eldrick projects a 30-foot Emanation of warding magic. The first time on each turn that a creature within the Emanation deals damage or casts a spell on Eldrick, that creature takes 14 (4d6) Force damage and is pushed 10 feet away from Eldrick.  
>
>### Actions
>***Eldrick always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On his turn, Eldrick can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Eldrick takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Greater Spellcasting (1/Round).*** Eldrick casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 21, +13 to spell attacks):  
>
>1/day each: *Forcecage*, *Globe of Invulnerability*, *Maze*, *Prismatic Spray*, *Prismatic Wall*, *Teleport*  
>
>***Prismatic Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 27 (4d10 + 6) Cold, Fire, or Lightning damage (Eldrick's choice).  
>
>***Spellcasting (1/Round).*** Eldrick casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 21, +13 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Sending*  
>2/day each: *Bigby's Hand* (Eldrick may use an Epic Action to control the hand once per round), *Banishment*, *Dimension Door*, *Ice Storm*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Scrying*, *Wall of Force*  
>
>***Teleport.*** Eldrick teleports to an unoccupied space he can see within 60 feet.  
>
>***Chain Lightning (Recharge 5–6).*** Eldrick casts *Chain Lightning*, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Dispel Magic (1/Round).*** Eldrick casts *Dispel Magic*, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Eldrick chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on him. Eldrick can use this action even if he would otherwise be unable to take actions, his actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters his game statistics.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell.*** Eldrick casts *Counterspell* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability from Spellcasting.
