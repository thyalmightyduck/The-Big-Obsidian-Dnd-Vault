___
>## Wechselkind Cutpurse
>*Small Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 18 (4d6 + 4)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Perception +3, Sleight of Hand +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Gear** *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Sylvan and one other language
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Artificial Form.*** The wechselkind has Advantage on saving throws against the Poisoned condition and is immune to disease. It doesn't need to eat, drink, or breathe.  
>
>***Childish Agility.*** The wechselkind can move through the space of any creature larger than it.  
>
>***Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** The wechselkind deals an extra 3 (1d6) damage when it hits a target with a weapon attack and has Advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the wechselkind that doesn't have the Incapacitated condition and the wechselkind doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Faerie Glamour.*** The wechselkind casts *Disguise Self* (save DC 11), requiring no components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability. When it does so, it can disguise itself only as the child it was created to replace.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The wechselkind takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
