___
>## Lion Guard
>*Medium Humanoid (Catfolk), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17 (Unarmored Defense)
>- **Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Con +4, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Survival +5
>- **Gear** *Glaive*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common and one other
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Cat's Paws.*** When the Lion Guard hits a creature with an opportunity attack, that creature's speed becomes 0 until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Cat-like Reflexes.*** The Lion Guard can take a reaction each turn in combat.  
>
>***Instinct.*** The Lion Guard cannot be surprised while it is conscious.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Lion Guard makes two attacks.  
>
>***Glaive.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sudden Strike.*** When a creature the Lion Guard can see moves into their reach, the Lion Guard makes one attack against the creature.
