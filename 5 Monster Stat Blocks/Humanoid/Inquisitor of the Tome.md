>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 88 (16d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|19 (+4)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +7, Wis +6, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Arcana +10, History +7, Nature +7, Religion +10
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Longsword*

>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3

>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The inquisitor makes three Force Blade attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Otiluke's Resilient Sphere* or *Telekinesis* if available.  
>
>***Force Blade.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) Force damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it is pushed up to 10 feet straight away from the inquisitor.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The inquisitor casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Dispel Magic*, *Mage Armor* (included in AC), *Mage Hand* (the hand is Invisible), *Sending*  
>1/day each: *Otiluke's Resilient Sphere*, *Telekinesis*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Implode (Recharge 4–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the inquisitor can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Force damage, and the target has the Prone condition and is pulled up to 20 feet straight toward the Sphere's center. *Success:* Half damage only.
