___
>## Laneshi Necromancer
>*Medium Humanoid (Laneshi), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 71 (11d8 + 22)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Medicine +6, Religion +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Holy Symbol*, *Shield*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Laneshi plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The laneshi necromancer can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Death Strike (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** When the laneshi necromancer hits with a melee attack, the target takes 27 (5d10) Necrotic damage in addition to the attack's effects. A Humanoid slain by this damage rises at the start of the necromancer's next turn as a zombie under the necromancer's command.  
>
>***Duality of Spirit.*** The laneshi necromancer has Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws and spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Necrotic Mastery.*** Necrotic damage dealt by the laneshi necromancer ignores Resistances.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The laneshi necromancer makes two attacks, using Spear or Ghostly Hand in any combination.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Ghostly Hand.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d10) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the end of the laneshi necromancer's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The laneshi necromancer casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Lesser Restoration*, *Light*, *Mending*, *Spare the Dying*, *Speak with Animals*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Animate Dead*, *Bane*, *Enhance Ability*, *Guiding Bolt* (level 3 version), *Hold Person*, *Silence*  
>1/day each: *Blight*, *Control Water*, *Death Ward*, *Greater Restoration*, *Harm*, *Spirit Guardians*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Healing Word (3/Day).*** The laneshi necromancer casts *Healing Word*, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
