___
>## Cultist of Myrkul
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 204 (24d8 + 96)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|20 (+5)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Int +9, Wis +8, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +9, Insight +8, Religion +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Holy Symbol*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Nearer to the Dead.*** While Bloodied, the cultist has Immunity to the Frightened and Poisoned conditions.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The cultist makes three Necrotic Burst attacks.  
>
>***Necrotic Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 27 (5d10) Necrotic damage and the target can't regain Hit Points until the end of the cultist's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The cultist casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17):  
>
>At will: *Speak with Dead*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>1/day each: *Animate Dead*, *Circle of Death*
