>## Lucretia Mathias
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 68 (15d8) plus 68 (15d8) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___

>|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|24 (+7)|19 (+4)|

>- **Saving Throws** Con +5, Int +9, Wis +12, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Insight +17, Perception +17, Persuasion +14, Religion +14
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Contamination|Monstersofdrakkenheim
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 27
>- **Languages** All
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Divine Grace.*** Creatures in the Emanation that target Lucretia with an attack or a harmful spell must first make a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature must choose a new target or lose the attack or spell. This effect doesn't protect Lucretia from area effects, such as the explosion of a *Fireball*. If Lucretia makes an attack, casts a spell that affects an enemy, or deals damage to another creature, this trait ends until the start of her next turn.  
>
>***Holy Aura.*** Lucretia projects a 60-foot Emanation of holy power. At the start of each of her turns, she can choose the aura's effect, which applies to creatures of her choice in the Emanation:  
>
>***Pacifying Presence.*** A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation has the Weakened condition until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Prescient.*** Lucretia can't be surprised while she is conscious.  
>
>***Sanctified by Falling Fire.*** When Lucretia dies, her soul is pulled into the *delerium crystal* embedded in her chest.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** Lucretia Mathias is immune to the Contamination condition.  
>
>***Unarmored Defense.*** While Lucretia is wearing no armor and wielding no shield, her AC includes her Wisdom modifier (included above).  
>
>***Undeniable Faith.*** Lucretia automatically succeeds on Constitution saving throws to maintain Concentration on her spells.  
>
>***Wrathful Flame.*** A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation takes 10 (3d6) Radiant damage plus 10 (3d6) Fire damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Greater Spellcasting (1/Round).*** Lucretia casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20, +12 to spell attacks; spells other than cantrips are cast at level 9):  
>
>1/day each: *Gate*, *Greater Restoration*, *Heal*, *Heroes' Feast*, *Resurrection*, *Word of Recall*  
>
>***Lucretia always takes her turn on Initiative count 20.*** On her turn, Lucretia can use her movement and interaction as normal. However, she does not take an action or Bonus Action on her turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Lucretia takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Pacifying Touch.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature within 5 feet of Lucretia. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, the target is stabilized but has the Unconscious condition for 1 hour, even after regaining Hit Points.
>- **Sacred Flame.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature within 60 feet of Lucretia. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Radiant damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
>
>***Divine Intervention (1/Day).*** Lucretia chooses any spell which doesn't require a Reaction to cast. As part of this Epic Action, she casts that spell without Material components. If she uses this Epic Action to cast the *Wish* spell, she can't use this Epic Action for the next 1d4 days.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Lucretia chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting her and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on her. Lucretia can use this action even if she would otherwise be unable to take actions, her actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters her game statistics.  
>
>***Spellcasting (1/Round).*** Lucretia casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20, +12 to spell attacks; spells other than cantrips are cast at level 9):  
>
>At will: *Guidance*, *Light*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Aid*, *Bless*, *Banishment*, *Dispel Magic*, *Flame Strike*, *Hold Person*, *Mass Cure Wounds*, *Sending*  
>1/day each: *Commune*, *Death Ward*, *Divination*, *Scrying*, *Speak with Dead*
