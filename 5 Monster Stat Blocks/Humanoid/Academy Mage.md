>## Academy Mage
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 63 (14d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, History +5
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Academy Rings (1/Day).*** The mage can cast a spell from Spellcasting without expending a use of it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mage makes two Prismatic Blast attacks.  
>
>***Prismatic Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Cold, Fire, or Lightning damage (mage's choice).  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The mage casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Invisibility*  
>1/day each: *Fireball*, *Slow*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (Recharge 5–6).*** The mage casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability from Spellcasting.
