___
>## Llanna Deyern
>*Small Humanoid (Wechselkind), Chaotic Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 16 (3d6 + 6)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +4, Arcana +4, Perception +2
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison, Psychic
>- **Gear** *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Lower Bürach, Elvish, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Artificial Form.*** Llanna has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition and has Immunity to disease. She does not need to eat, drink, sleep, or breathe  
>
>***Defense Mechanism.*** When Llanna is reduced to 0 Hit Points, she uses Unchain the Beast, if able. At the end of the duration, she has the Unconscious condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack..*** Llanna makes two attacks using Dagger.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Faerie Glamour.*** Llanna casts *Disguise Self*, appearing as a fleshand-blood half-elf child.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Unchain the Beast (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** Llanna transforms into the Clockwork Horror for 1 minute. Her Hit Points are replaced by those of the clockwork horror until the duration ends.
