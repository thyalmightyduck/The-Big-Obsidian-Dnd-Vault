___
>## Free Swords Captain
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 161 (19d8 + 76)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|20 (+5)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +9, Dex +9, Con +8, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +9, Insight +7, Perception +7
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Longsword*, *Plate Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Any four languages
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Arms Expert.*** The captain can don or doff its *Shield* whenever it changes weapons.  
>
>***Field Commander.*** Allies within 60 feet who can see or hear the captain have Advantage on all saving throws.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The captain makes three attacks using Longsword or Longbow in any combination.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Tactical Advance.*** Each Free Swords ally within 30 feet of the captain can take a Reaction to move up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Tactical Response.*** *Trigger:* The captain or a Free Swords ally that the captain can see is hit by an attack roll. *Response:* The captain commands one Free Swords ally within 90 feet who can see or hear the captain to use its Reaction to move up to its Speed and make one Weapon Attack against the attacking creature.
