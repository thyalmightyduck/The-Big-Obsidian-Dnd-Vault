___
>## Wandering Druid
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 84 (13d8 + 26)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +4, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Animal Handling +7, Insight +7, Medicine +7, Perception +7
>- **Gear** *Handaxe*, *Hide Armor*, *Shield*, *Shortbow*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Primal Strike.*** Once on each of its turns, when the druid hits a creature with an attack, it deals an extra 1d8 Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage (chosen on a successful hit).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The druid makes three attacks, or it casts a spell.  
>
>***Fang and Claw (Wild Shape Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (4d6 + 3) Radiant damage, and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Hand Axe (Humanoid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (2d6 + 1) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Short Bow (Humanoid Form Only).*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting (Humanoid Form Only).*** The druid casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Barkskin* (included in AC), *Detect Magic*, *Elementalism*, *Guidance*, *Message*, *Speak with Animals*  
>2/day each: *Call Lightning*, *Cure Wounds*  
>1/day each: *Blight*, *Revivify*  
>
>***Verdant Bolt.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, range 90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Wild Shape.*** The druid shapeshifts into a beast form or back to humanoid form. Its game statistics are the same in each form, except where listed. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying is absorbed into its body when it shifts into beast form. When the druid shifts into a beast form, it gains 18 Temporary Hit Points.
