### Info
>Dressed in plain gray robes tied off with a frayed rope, and wearing little else but a pair of old sandals, these pilgrims carry little on them and have given up their possessions for a simple life dedicated to the Falling Fire.

The prophetic words of Lucretia Mathias have inspired hundreds of commonfolk to make their way to Drakkenheim. Their motives and reasons are varied: some seek meaning and purpose in life which they find in the righteous calling of the Testament of the Falling Fire. Others are devout adherents to the Faith of the Sacred Flame who are disillusioned by what they see as the corruption and meddling of the clergy. A few travel to join their friends and family members who have already taken up with the Falling Fire.

#### Combat Tactics

Untrained and unprepared for combat, most pilgrims can only operate as a mob, using their numbers to overwhelm threats. Faith strengthens their resolve, and their morale holds fast whilst their leaders spurn them to holy purpose.

---

#### Followers of the Falling Fire

The Followers of the Falling Fire are a religious sect who believe the meteor signaled a celestial prophecy. These fanatics consider delerium a holy sacrament. Called to pilgrimage by the prescient testament of their leader, Lucretia Mathias, zealous masses of devout commonfolk now journey to the Crater's Edge to take their place in a divine plan.

##### Combat Tactics

Falling Fire pilgrims give up their possessions and wealth and often carry very little on them. For the most part, common Falling Fire devotees are not equipped to fight. Most members try to avoid combat. Preferring a peaceful approach, they are more reactionary in their tactics and hope most situations do not come to bloodshed. However, Drakkenheim is a dangerous city, and monsters and bandits have attempted to thwart the efforts of the devout worshippers often. Pilgrims have been known to take up clubs, daggers, and any mundane weapons they can find to defend themselves, and often groups of traveling pilgrims are accompanied by armored zealots and spellcasting divine leaders who are ready to protect their flock.

“_There comes a time in every man's life where he wants more. Delerium in your chest is the wrong way._”
