___
>## Preceptor
>*Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 104 (16d8 + 32)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|13 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Animal Handling +5, Arcana +9, History +9, Investigation +9, Medicine +5, Nature +9
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Any five languages
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Academic Specialty.*** The Preceptor has Advantage on all ability checks using their chosen specialty and gains Resistance to a type of damage.  
>- Biothurge, Master of Warbreed: Medicine, Poison
>- Blastwright, Master of Machines: Arcana, Fire
>- Decosopher, Master of Alchemy: Nature, Acid
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Preceptor makes three Scalpel attacks. It can replace one Scalpel attack with an Acid Flask, Incendiary Bomb, or Neurotoxic Cloud.  
>
>***Scalpel.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d4 + 5) Slashing damage, plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Acid Flask (Decosopher).*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Acid damage and 7 (2d6) Acid damage at the end of the target's next turn.  
>
>***Incendiary Bomb (Blastwright).*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Fire damage and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Neurotoxic Cloud (Biothurge).*** Targets in a 20-ft. Cone take 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage and have the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. If the target succeeds on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, it takes half damage and is not Poisoned.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The Preceptor casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17, +9 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*  
>1/day each: *Acid Arrow*, *Animate Dead*, *Banishment*, *Detect Magic*, *Dispel Magic*, *Enhance Ability*, *Fireball*, *Levitate*, *Magic Circle*, *Misty Step*, *Summon Dragon*, *Unseen Servant*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Impromptu Golem (Biothurge, 1/Day).*** The biothurge turns a twisted mass spare bodies parts into a ****Zombie****. The zombie lasts until the end of the biothurge's next turn or until it is destroyed. The biothurge can use a Bonus Action to extend the duration for 1 round.  
>
>***Latent Decoction (Decosopher, 1/Day).*** The decosopher activates a potion stored in its blood stream. The decosopher is affected by its choice of: *Haste, Healing (Superior), Speed, or Water Breathing*.  
>
>***Magitek Interference (Blastwright, 1/Day).*** The blastwright enjoins a magical item or golem to cease functioning until the beginning of the blastwright's next turn. If the item is held or is a creature, a successful DC 17 Intelligence saving throw resists the interference.
