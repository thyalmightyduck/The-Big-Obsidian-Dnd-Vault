___
>## Old Ways Chosen
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 88 (16d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Performance +6, Religion +2
>- **Gear** six *Daggers*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Animal Mask.*** The chosen wears the mask of an animal, imbuing it with the strength of the old gods. When a creature is grappling the chosen, the creature can remove the mask as an action, or as an attack during the Attack action, with a successful DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check. Without its mask, the chosen can't cast spells, its Concentration on any existing spells ends, and the save DC of Song of the Old Ways is reduced by 4.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The chosen makes three Ritual Dagger attacks.  
>
>***Ritual Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Radiant or Necrotic damage (the chosen's choice).  
>
>***Songs of the Old Ways (Recharge 5–6).*** The chosen sings one of the following songs:  
>
>***Blessing of the Harvest.*** Each creature of the chosen's choice in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the chosen regains 14 (4d4 + 4) Hit Points.  
>
>***Song of Sacrifice.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 30 feet. *Failure:* 40 (9d8) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Paralyzed condition until the end of the chosen's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting (Requires Animal Mask).*** The chosen casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components other than its Animal Mask and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Druidcraft*, *Minor Illusion*  
>2/day each: *Entangle*, *Scorching Ray* (level 4 version)  
>
>***Wicker Chant.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature of the chosen's choice in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the chosen. *Failure:* 31 (9d6) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Jump (Requires Animal Mask).*** The chosen casts *Jump*, using the same spell components and spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
