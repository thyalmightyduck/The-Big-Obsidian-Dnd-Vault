>## Master Plague Doctor
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Wizard), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 135 (18d8 + 54)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Medicine +5
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Healer's Kit*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Any three languages
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Conditioned Immunity.*** The plague doctor has Immunity to disease and Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.  
>
>***Good Medicine (2/Day).*** The plague doctor carries a concoction that, if consumed as an action, restores 22 (5d8) Hit Points and cures any disease.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Bad Medicine.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 10- foot Sphere centered on a point the plague doctor can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. While Poisoned, the target takes 9 (2d6 + 2) Acid damage at the start of each of its turns and 14 (4d6) Poison damage each time it takes an action. The target can take an action to repeat the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The plague doctor casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Boil Blood*, *Detect Magic*, *Grease*, *Light*, *Sleep*  
>2/day each: *Bestow Curse*, *Fiend Flesh*, *Gaseous Form*, *Gust of Wind*, *Stinking Cloud*  
>1/day each: *Blight*, *Cloudkill*, *Confusion*, *Finger of Death*
