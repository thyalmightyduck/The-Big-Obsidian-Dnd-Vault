___
>## Academy Grandmaster
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 195 (30d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 10 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Int +9, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +13, History +13, Insight +7, Perception +7
>- **Senses** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common and three other languages
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Academy Rings (1/Day).*** The mage can cast a spell from Spellcasting without expending a use of it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mage makes four Prismatic Blast attacks.  
>
>***Prismatic Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Cold, Fire, or Lightning damage (mage's choice).  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The mage casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17, +9 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Find Familiar*, *Light*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Cone of Cold*, *Dimension Door*, *Dispel Magic*, *Fireball*, *Greater Invisibility*, *Hold Monster*, *Sending*  
>1/day each: *Chain Lightning*, *Maze*, *Prismatic Spray*, *Teleport*, *Time Stop*, *Wall of Force*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (Recharge 5–6).*** The mage casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability from Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Contingency (1/Day).*** *Trigger:* The mage would become Incapacitated from a spell. *Response:* The mage casts *Dispel Magic* (level 5 version) targeting itself.
