___
>## Gennifer Weathermay-Foxgrove
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Lycanthrope); Lawful Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Investigation +4, Medicine +5, Stealth +4
>- **Gear** *Component Pouch*, *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic, Infernal
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Suppressed Lycanthropy.*** If reduced to 0 Hit Points, Gennifer succumbs to lycanthropy. Her statistics are replaced by those of the Werewolf stat block, but she retains her alignment; personality; Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores; proficiencies; and languages known. She then immediately revives in her hybrid form with all her Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Gennifer makes two attacks, using Blessed Dagger or Radiant Wisp in any combination.  
>
>***Blessed Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Radiant Wisp.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 3) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Esoteric Ward (Recharge 6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Emanation originating from Gennifer. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Radiant damage. If the target is an Undead creature, it has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Gennifer casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *Mending*  
>1/day each: *Locate Animals or Plants*, *Magic Circle*, *Protection from Poison*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Occult Aid (2/Day).*** Gennifer casts *Healing Word*, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
