>## Qar, Master of Jackals
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Skirmisher); Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 71 (11d8 + 22)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +3, Dex +7, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +7, Athletics +3, Perception +7, Stealth +7
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Evasion.*** If Qar is subjected to an effect that allows him to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, he instead takes no damage if he succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if he fails.  
>
>***Exploit Opening (3/Day).*** When Qar makes an attack, he has advantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Supernatural Resistance.*** Qar has advantage on saving throws against powers, spells, and other supernatural effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Qar makes three Unarmed Strike attacks. If he hits the same creature with all three attacks, the target is dazed until the end of their next turn.  
>
>***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Iron Ring (2/Day).*** If not in the Cyst, Qar teleports to the Cyst. If in the Cyst, he teleports to a place he has visited.  
>
>### Reactions
>***No Survivors.*** When an enemy Qar can see within 40 feet of him drops to 0 hit points, Qar moves up to his speed. If he ends that movement within 5 feet of an enemy, he can make an Unarmed Strike against them.  
>
>***Voice Stealer (3/Day).*** When an enemy Qar can hear within 30 feet of him finishes casting a spell with a verbal component, Qar attempts to steal their voice. The enemy must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw or be unable to speak for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn).
