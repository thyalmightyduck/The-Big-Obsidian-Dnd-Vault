___
>## Exorcist
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Con +7, Int +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Intimidation +5, Medicine +6, Religion +7
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Greatsword*, *Half Plate Armor*, *Heavy Crossbow*, *Quiver*
>- **Senses** Truesight 10 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Common
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blessed Weapons.*** When the Exorcist hits with any weapon, the weapon unleashes holy fire, causing the target to have the Burning condition (4d6 Radiant; included in the attacks below).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The exorcist makes two attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Grasping Ghost.  
>
>***Radiant Sword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage, and the target has the Burning condition (4d6 Radiant).  
>
>***Blessed Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target has the Burning condition (4d6 Radiant).  
>
>***Grasping Ghost.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature the exorcist can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage, and the target is moved 30 feet in a horizontal direction of the exorcist's choice. At the end of this movement, the target has the Immobilized condition until the end of the exorcist's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Expel Evil (1/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each Celestial, Fey, Fiend, and Undead that can see and hear the exorcist. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened and Incapacitated conditions and must move as far from the exorcist as it can on its turns. The effect lasts for 1 minute or until the target takes damage, the exorcist has the Incapacitated condition, or the exorcist dies.  
>
>***Last Rites (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature the exorcist can see within 60 feet that isn't a Celestial or Construct (Fiends and Undead have Disadvantage). *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Radiant damage. If the target is a Fiend or Undead, it instead takes 64 Radiant damage and is turned to ash if the effect reduces it to 0 Hit Points. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Spiritual Healing.*** One creature the exorcist can see regains 35 10d6 Hit Points. Once a creature benefits from Spiritual Healing, it can't do so again until it finishes a Short or Long Rest.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Negate Spell (3/Day).*** *Trigger:* A creature the exorcist can see within 60 feet is casting a spell. *Response:* If the spell is level 3 or lower, the spell fails, but any spell slots or charges aren't wasted.
