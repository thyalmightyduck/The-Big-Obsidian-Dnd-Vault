>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)

>- **Initiative** +2 (12)

>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Keldar makes two attacks using Longsword or Handaxe in any combination. She can replace one of her attacks with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Thunder damage.  
>
>***Handaxe.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 5 (2d4) Thunder damage. Creatures within 5 feet of the target take 1 Thunder damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Keldar casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as her spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +5 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Thunderclap*, *True Strike*, *Vicious Mockery*  
>1/day each: *Blindness/Deafness*, *Color Spray*, *Healing Word*, *Hideous Laughter*, *Hold Person*, *Thunderwave*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Percussive Shove.*** Kelda's drum emits a blast of percussive force. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13 Each creature in a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. *Failure:* Target is pushed away from Kelda to the end of the line.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Thunderous Rebuke.*** *Trigger:* A creature Keldar can see makes an attack roll against her. *Response:* *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13 The triggering creature. *Failure:* Triggering creature takes 1d6 Thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away from Keldar.
