>### Actions
>***Poison Needle.*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage, and the target must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.  
>
>***Medical Treatment (3/Day).*** The doctor touches another creature. The target magically regains 20 (4d8 + 2) hit points, and the doctor ends either one disease or one condition afflicting it. The condition can be blinded, deafened, paralyzed, or poisoned.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Diagnosis.*** The doctor takes the Help action.
