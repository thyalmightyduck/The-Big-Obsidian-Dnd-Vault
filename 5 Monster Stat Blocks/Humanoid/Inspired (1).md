>## Inspired

>- **Hit Points** 40 (9d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)

>- **Skills** Deception +7, Insight +2, Persuasion +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common, Quori

>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2

>***Dual Mind.*** The inspired has Advantage on Wisdom saving throws.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The inspired makes two Crysteel Dagger attacks. It can replace one attack with *Vicious Mockery*.  
>
>***Crysteel Dagger.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Force damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The inspired casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Mage Hand*, *Vicious Mockery*  
>1/day each: *Charm Person*, *Dissonant Whispers*, *Hex*, *Hold Person*, *Mage Armor* (included in AC)  
>
> ##### Variant: Quori Vessel
>
>The inspired are vessels for quori spirits, allowing the quori to manifest a portion of their power while the vessel is possessed. An inspired can gain benefits depending on the type of quori possessing it. The quori also gains access to the inspired's knowledge and features.
