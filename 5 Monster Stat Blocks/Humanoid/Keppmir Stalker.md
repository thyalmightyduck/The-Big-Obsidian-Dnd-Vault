>## Keppmir Stalker
>*Medium Humanoid (Keppmir), Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +4, Perception +3, Stealth +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Gear** *Spear*, *Breastplate*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Valikan
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Ambusher.*** In the first round of combat, the keppmir stalker has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has Surprised.  
>
>***Heated Body.*** A creature other than a keppmir that ends its turn grappling or Grappled by the keppmir stalker takes 2 Fire damage.  
>
>***Hold Breath.*** The keppmir stalker can hold its breath for 30 minutes.  
>
>***Water Breathing.*** The keppmir stalker can breathe only underwater.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The keppmir stalker makes two attacks, using Gore or Spear in any combination.  
>
>***Gore.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 96 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, or 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage if the spear is wielded in two hands.
