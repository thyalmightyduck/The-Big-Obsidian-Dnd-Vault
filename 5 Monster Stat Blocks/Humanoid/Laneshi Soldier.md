___
>## Laneshi Soldier
>*Medium Humanoid (Laneshi), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Animal Handling +3, Athletics +4, Perception +3
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Net*, *Pike*
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Laneshi plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The laneshi soldier can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The laneshi soldier makes two melee Pike attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Net.  
>
>***Beast Whispers.*** The laneshi soldier casts *Speak with Animals*, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability.  
>
>***Pike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Net.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 11, one Large or smaller creature the laneshi soldier can see within 15 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Restrained condition. The target can take an action to free itself or another creature trapped by a Net with a successful DC 10 Strength (Athletics) check. Dealing 5 Slashing damage to the Net (AC 10) frees the target and destroys the Net.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Skirmisher.*** The laneshi soldier takes the Disengage action.
