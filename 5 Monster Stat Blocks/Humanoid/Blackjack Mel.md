>## Blackjack Mel
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Insight +4, Perception +2, Persuasion +7, Sleight of Hand +7, Stealth +7
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Evasion.*** If Blackjack Mel is subjected to an effect that allows him to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, he instead takes no damage if he succeeds on the saving throw and only half damage if he fails, provided he doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Knife.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 30 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** Blackjack Mel takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Uncanny Dodge.*** *Trigger:* A creature Blackjack Mel can see deals damage to him with an attack. *Response:* Blackjack Mel halves the damage he takes from that attack.
