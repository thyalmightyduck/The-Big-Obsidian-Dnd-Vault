___
>## Bolgrim Boar-Scar
>*Medium Humanoid (Human), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Deception +3, Perception +4, Stealth +4
>- **Gear** *Handaxe*, *Holy Symbol*, *Longsword*, *Mitrhal Splint Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Retribution of Ragnarok.*** When Bolgrim dies, runes engraved on his chest explode. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12 Each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from Bolgrim. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Bolgrim makes three attacks, using Longsword, Handaxe, or Necrotic Ray in any combination.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Slashing damage if the target is marked.  
>
>***Handaxe.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Slashing damage if the target is marked.  
>
>***Necrotic Ray.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d12 +2) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, it is spiritually marked with a necrotic rune until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Bolgrim casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Light*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Command*, *Cure Wounds*  
>1/day each: *Hold Person*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* Bolgrim is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon. *Response:* Bolgrim adds 2 to his Armor Class against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
