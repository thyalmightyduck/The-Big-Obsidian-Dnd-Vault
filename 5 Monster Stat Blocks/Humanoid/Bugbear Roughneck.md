___
>## Bugbear Roughneck
>*Medium Humanoid (Bugbear, Brute); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (*hide armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Stealth +4, Survival +3
>- **Immunities** Frightened
>- **Gear** *Handaxe*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Goblin
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Bugbear's Inspiration.*** Allied Humanoids within 15 feet of the roughneck who can see or hear them have advantage on Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack (Battle Fury Only).*** The roughneck makes two Handaxe attacks.  
>
>***Handaxe.*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.  
>
>***Bugbear Battle Cry (1/Day).*** Each ally within 15 feet of the roughneck who can hear and understand them gains advantage on attack rolls until the start of the roughneck's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Battle Fury (1/Day).*** The roughneck enters a furious state that lasts for 1 minute. While in this state, the roughneck can take the Multiattack action on their turn, and they have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from mundane attacks.
