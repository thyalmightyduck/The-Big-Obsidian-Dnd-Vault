>## Gnoll Brute
>*Medium Humanoid (Gnoll), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Gnoll
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Instinctive.*** The gnoll brute has Advantage on Initiative rolls and on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed and Frightened conditions. Being surprised doesn't impose Disadvantage on the gnoll brute's Initiative rolls.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gnoll brute makes one Bite attack and one Claws attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage. If the gnoll brute moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 4 (1d8) Piercing damage and, if it is Large or smaller, has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampage.*** After reducing a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, the gnoll brute moves up to half its Speed and makes a Bite attack.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Shared Rampage.*** *Trigger:* An ally within 100 feet that the gnoll brute can see uses Rampage. *Response:* The gnoll brute uses Rampage also.
