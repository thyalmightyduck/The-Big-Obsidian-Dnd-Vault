>## Time Raider Archon
>*Medium Humanoid (Time Raider, Minion); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (*studded leather*)
>- **Hit Points** 14
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Kuran'zoi
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 140; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Minion.*** If the archon takes damage from an attack or as the result of a failed saving throw, their hit points are reduced to 0. If the archon takes damage from another effect, they die if the damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum; otherwise they take no damage.  
>
>***Psychic Scar.*** The archon is immune to any effect that would sense their emotions, read their thoughts, reveal they are lying, or reveal their alignment or location.  
>
>### Actions
>***Brutal Flail (Group Attack).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 bludgeoning damage. If two or more archons joined the attack, the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be dazed until the end of their next turn. The DC for this saving throw equals 10 plus the number of archons who joined the attack.  
>
>***Blaster (Group Attack).*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, range 30/90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 radiant damage.
