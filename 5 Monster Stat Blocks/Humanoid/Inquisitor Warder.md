___
>## Inquisitor Warder
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +7, Investigation +4, Perception +7, Religion +4
>- **Gear** *Chain Mail*, *Shield*, *Warhammer*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Ward.*** When the inquisitor warder casts a spell that causes creatures to regain Hit Points, the warder can choose one of the spell's targets and end the effects of one spell affecting it. The effect must be from a spell of equal or lower level to the warder's healing spell.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The inquisitor warder makes three Warhammer attacks.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The inquisitor warder casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Lesser Restoration*, *Light*, *Sacred Flame*, *Spare the Dying*  
>2/day each: *Cure Wounds* (level 4 version), *Dispel Magic*, *Guiding Bolt* (level 4 version), *Remove Curse*  
>1/day each: *Greater Restoration*, *Mass Cure Wounds*, *True Seeing*  
>
>***Warhammer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Force damage, or 13 (3d8) Force damage if the target is maintaining Concentration on a spell.
