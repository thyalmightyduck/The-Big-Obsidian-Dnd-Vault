___
>## Blightworm Warlock
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 71 (11d8 + 22)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Perception +3
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blightworm Whispers.*** The blightworm warlock has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Stunned, and Unconscious conditions.  
>
>***Spell Siphon (1/Day).*** When the blightworm warlock successfully interrupts a spell using its Counterspell Reaction, it doesn't expend the Reaction and copies the spell. Until the warlock uses this trait again, it can cast the copied spell using its Spellcasting action, provided the spell is level 5 or lower.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack..*** The blightworm warlock makes two Eldritch Burst attacks.  
>
>***Eldritch Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 19 (3d10 + 3) Force damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The blightworm warlock casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*  
>3/rest: *Consume Mind*, *Contact Other Plane*, *Creeping Touch*, *Dimension Door*, *Shatter*, *Suggestion*, *Vampiric Touch*  
>
>3/Short Rest (Level 5 Versions)  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Serpent Tongue (1/Short Rest).*** The blightworm warlock casts *Serpent Tongue* (level 5 version), using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>1/rest: *Serpent Tongue*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell (1/Short Rest).*** The blightworm warlock casts *Counterspell* (level 5 version) in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>1/rest: *Counterspell*
