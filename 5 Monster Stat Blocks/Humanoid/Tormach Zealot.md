>## Tormach Zealot
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +4, Insight +3, Intimidation +4, Performance +4, Persuasion +4, Religion +3
>- **Gear** *Scale Mail*, *Whip*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Two national languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Spellcasting.*** The Tormach zealot casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *True Strike*, *Vicious Mockery*  
>1/day each: *Bane*, *Faerie Fire*, *Heroism*, *Thunderwave*  
>
>***Whip.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Slashing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it is pushed 5 feet or has the Prone condition (zealot's choice).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Blood-Soaked Inspiration.*** After dealing damage, the Tormach zealot chooses one creature within 60 feet that can see and hear it. The target can roll 1d6 and add the result to one D20 Test it makes before the end of its next turn.
