___
>## Goblin Warrior
>*Small Humanoid (Goblin, Skirmisher); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (*leather armor*, *shield*)
>- **Hit Points** 9 (2d6 + 2)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +4, Stealth +4
>- **Gear** *Shortbow*, *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common, Goblin
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Crafty.*** The warrior doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when they move out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.  
>
>***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Fleet Foot.*** When a creature within 5 feet of the warrior misses them with a melee attack, the warrior can move up to half their speed.
