___
>## Skald
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 97 (15d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Deception +7, History +5, Perception +4, Performance +7, Persuasion +7
>- **Gear** *Leather Armor*, *Shortbow*, *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skald makes two Shortword or two Shortbow attacks.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, range 80/320 feet., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The skald casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Message*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Heroism*, *Hideous Laughter*  
>1/day each: *Enthrall*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Heroic Oration (Recharge 5–6).*** One creature of the skald's choice within 30 feet of it that can hear it has Advantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the start of the skald's next turn.
