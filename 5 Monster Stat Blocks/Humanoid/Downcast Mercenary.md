>## Downcast Mercenary
>*Medium Humanoid (Downcast), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 82 (11d8 + 33)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +4
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +4, Religion +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Gear** *Greatsword*, *Half Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Celestial and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Action Surge (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The downcast mercenary can take an extra action on its turn.  
>
>***Indomitable (2/Day).*** If the downcast mercenary fails a saving throw, it can reroll the saving throw and use the new roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The downcast mercenary makes three Greatsword attacks  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage and 4 (1d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Thaumaturge.*** The downcast mercenary casts *Thaumaturgy*, using Charisma as its spellcasting ability.
