>## Sanctified Knight
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +3, Persuasion +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Radiant
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Contamination|Monstersofdrakkenheim
>- **Gear** *Longsword*, *Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common and one other language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Sanctified by Falling Fire.*** When the knight dies, its soul is pulled into the *delerium crystal* embedded in its chest.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** The knight is immune to the Contamination condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The knight makes three Longsword attacks.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage, or 9 (1d10 + 4) Slashing damage if wielded with two hands, plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Lay on Hands (3/Day).*** The knight touches another creature. The target regains 11 (2d8 + 2) Hit Points and is cured of one disease or the Poisoned condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Deny the Witch (3/Day).*** *Trigger:* A creature the knight can see within 60 feet of it casts a spell. *Response:* If the spell is level 3 or lower, the spell fails, but no resources are wasted.
