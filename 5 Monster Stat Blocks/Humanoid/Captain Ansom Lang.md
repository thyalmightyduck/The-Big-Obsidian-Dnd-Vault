>## Captain Ansom Lang
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 130 (20d8 + 40)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +10, Athletics +5, Perception +8, Stealth +10, Survival +5
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Quiver*, *Shortsword*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Evasion.*** If Ansom is subjected to an effect that allows him to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, he instead takes no damage if he succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if he fails, provided he doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Ansom makes four attacks.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the end of Ansom's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Flurry of Blades.*** Ansom makes two Shortsword attacks.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* A creature Ansom can see hits him with an attack. *Response:* Ansom adds 3 to his AC against that attack, potentially causing it to miss.
