>## Human Mercenary
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Retainer); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (medium armor)
>- **Hit Points** Nine times their level (number of d12 Hit Dice equal to their level)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str 3+PB, Dex +PB, Con 2+PB, Int +PB, Wis +PB, Cha +PB
>- **Skills** Athletics +3 plus PB, Medicine +0 plus PB, Perception +0 plus PB
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10 plus PB
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals the mentor's bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals the mentor's bonus
>___
>### Actions
>***Signature Attack (Halberd).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  3 plus PB to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d10 plus PB slashing damage. Beginning at 7th level, the mercenary can make this attack twice, instead of once, when they take the Attack action on their turn.  
>
>***3rd Level: Battlefield Medicine (3/Day).*** As an action, the mercenary restores PBd8 hit points to a creature they can see within 5 feet of them.  
>
>***5th Level: Exploit Opening (3/Day).*** The mercenary takes the Attack action, making each attack with advantage and dealing an extra PBd4 slashing damage on a hit.  
>
>***7th Level: Halberd Master (3/Day).*** When a creature enters a space within the mercenary's reach, the mercenary uses a reaction to make a signature attack with advantage against that creature. If this attack hits, it deals an extra PBd6 slashing damage.
