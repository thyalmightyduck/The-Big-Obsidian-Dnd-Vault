>## Wechselkind Glamourist
>*Small Humanoid (Wizard), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d6 + 10)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Arcana +4, Deception +4, Perception +2, Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Gear** *Rapier*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Sylvan and one other language
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Artificial Form.*** The wechselkind has Advantage on saving throws against the Poisoned condition and is immune to disease. It doesn't need to eat, drink, or breathe.  
>
>***Childish Agility.*** The wechselkind can move through the space of any creature larger than it.  
>
>***Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** The wechselkind deals an extra 17 (5d6) damage when it hits a target with a weapon attack and has Advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the wechselkind that doesn't have the Incapacitated condition and the wechselkind doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Faerie Glamour.*** The wechselkind casts *Disguise Self* (save DC 12), requiring no components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability. When it does so, it can disguise itself only as the humanoid child it was created to replace.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The wechselkind casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12, +4 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Hand* (the hand is invisible), *Minor Illusion*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Charm Person*, *Color Spray*, *Silent Image*  
>1/day each: *Blur*, *Invisibility*, *Suggestion*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The wechselkind takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
