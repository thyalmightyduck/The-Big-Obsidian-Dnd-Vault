___
>## Lizardfolk Grunt
>*Medium Humanoid (Lizardfolk, Minion); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 7
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Gear** *Javelin*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 20; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The grunt can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Minion.*** If the grunt takes damage from an attack or as the result of a failed saving throw, their hit points are reduced to 0. If the grunt takes damage from another effect, they die if the damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum; otherwise they take no damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite (Group Attack).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1 piercing damage, and the target is grappled by each grunt who joined the attack (escape DC 10 plus the number of grunts grappling; on a success, the target escapes every grunt grappling them). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the grunts who joined the attack can't bite another target.  
>
>***Javelin (Group Attack).*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1 piercing damage.
