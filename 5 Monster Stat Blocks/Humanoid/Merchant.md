___
>## Merchant
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +3, Perception +3, Persuasion +5
>- **Gear** *Padded Armor*, *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Trader's Eye.*** The merchant has Advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks to determine if goods are of poor quality or a forgery, and it has Advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks to recognize when a creature is attempting to sell it poor quality or forged goods.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The chosen makes two attacks, using Shortsword or Quip in any combination.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Quip.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Psychic damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Best Bargain.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13 A creature the merchant can see within 30 feet of it. *Failure:* The target is Charmed until the start of the merchant's next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Call for Aid (1/day).*** *Trigger:* A creature hits the merchant with an attack while in a populated area. *Response:* On Initiative count 20 of the following round, 1d4 **bandits** or 1d4 **guards** (the merchant's choice) arrive, acting as allies of the merchant and defending it to the best of their abilities. The called allies remain for 1 hour, until the merchant dies, or until the merchant dismisses them as a Bonus Action.
