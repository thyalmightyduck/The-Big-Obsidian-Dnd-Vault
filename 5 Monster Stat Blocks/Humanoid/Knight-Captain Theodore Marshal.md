>## Knight-Captain Theodore Marshal
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 94 (11d8 + 44) plus 94 (12d8 + 44) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Dex +5, Con +9, Int +6, Wis +7, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Animal Handling +7, Athletics +10, Insight +7, Persuasion +9
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Holy Avenger Longsword*, *Shield*, *Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Aura of Righteousness.*** Theodore projects a 30-foot Emanation of radiant power. Allies in the Emanation have Advantage on saving throws. A Hostile creature that starts its turn in the Emanation takes 14 (4d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Theodore always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On his turn, Theodore can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Theodore takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Holy Avenger Longsword.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +13, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (2d12 + 5) Slashing damage, and the target suffers one of the following effects of Theodore's choice.
>- **Divine Smite.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Radiant damage. *Success:* Half damage.
>
>***Searing Smite.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Fire damage, and the target has the Burning condition (4d6 Fire). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Thunderous Smite.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed up to 10 feet away from Theodore and has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Shield.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +13, reach 5 ft or range 30/90 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Dazed condition until the end of its next turn. *Hit or Miss:* After being thrown, the shield returns to Theodore's hand.  
>
>***Charge (1/Round).*** Theodore moves up to his Speed toward a Hostile creature he can see. He gains Advantage on the next weapon attack he makes this round.  
>
>***Call the Sacred Flame (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foot-high Cylinder centered on a point within 60 feet of Theodore. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Radiant damage plus 21 (6d6) Fire damage, and the target has the Blinded condition (save ends).  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Theodore chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on him. Theodore can use this action even if he would otherwise be unable to take actions, his actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters his game statistics.
