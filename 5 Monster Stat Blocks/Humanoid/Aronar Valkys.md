>## Aronar Valkys
>*Medium Humanoid (Half-Elf, Support); Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20 (*plate*, *shield*)
>- **Hit Points** 130 (20d8 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|26 (+8)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|26 (+8)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Con +5, Wis +11
>- **Skills** Insight +11, Perception +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Necrotic
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic, Elvish
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Charm Resistant.*** Aronar has advantage on saving throws he makes to avoid or end the charmed condition on himself.  
>
>***Delusions of Grandeur.*** Whenever Aronar is affected by an effect (such as the *hold person* spell) that allows him to make a saving throw at the end of his turn, he can also repeat that saving throw at the start of his turn. Additionally, whenever Aronar succeeds on a saving throw against an effect, he ends that effect on himself and any ally within 30 feet of him under the same effect.  
>
>***Spellcasting (Utility).*** In addition to any other spells in this stat block, Aronar can cast the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19):  
>
>At will: *augury* +, *thaumaturgy* A  
>1/day each: *hallow* +, *raise dead* +  
>
>***Sun Eater's Grace (3/Day).*** When Aronar is within 30 feet of Athmia and casts a spell that restores hit points to another creature, Athmia regains the same number of hit points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Aronar makes three Divine Mace attacks, or he makes one Divine Mace attack and one God Complex attack.  
>
>***Divine Mace.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (1d6 + 8) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the start of their next turn.  
>
>***God Complex (3/Day; 3rd-Level Spell).*** *Ranged Spell Attack:*  +11 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 28 (8d6) necrotic damage, and the next attack roll made against this target before the end of Aronar's next turn has advantage.  
>
>***Cure Wounds (3/Day; 3rd-Level Spell).*** Aronar touches a creature and the target regains 21 (3d8 + 8) hit points.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Vainglory (3/Day; 3rd-Level Spell).*** A creature of Aronar's choice within 60 feet of him regains 15 (3d4 + 8) hit points.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sun Eater's Soul (3/Day).*** When a creature Aronar can see within 30 feet of him is hit with an attack, he can halve the attack's damage dealt to them.
