>## Infernal Contractor
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Evil Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 19 (3d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Insight +5, Persuasion +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire
>- **Gear** *Chain Shirt*, *Rapier*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Infernal and one other language
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Fire damage.  
>
>***Locate Signee.*** The infernal contractor senses the direction of any creature that has signed one of its contracts, provided it is on the same plane of existence. If the creature is moving, the contractor knows the direction of its movement. Effects that protect the target from Divination work against this action.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Infernal Brand (Recharge 6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature the infernal contractor can see within 60 feet. A creature that signed a contract with the contractor has Disadvantage. *Failure:* The target is cursed. The target must roll a d4 and subtract the result from each D20 Test it makes. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. The effect ends early if the contractor uses this Bonus Action again or the target enters hallowed ground.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Infernal Luck.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 30 feet makes a D20 Test. *Response:* The contractor forces the target to reroll the D20 Test and use the lower roll. The target is then immune to this contractor's Infernal Luck for 24 hours.
