>## Trapper
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Nature +5, Perception +5, Stealth +4, Survival +7
>- **Gear** *Hide Armor*, *Greataxe*, three *Hunting Traps*, *Shortbow*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Traps (3/Day).*** *Trigger:* The first time an enemy with a Passive Perception of 12 or lower comes within 30 feet of the trapper or the trapper shoves the creature 5 feet. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target takes 7 (2d6) Piercing damage and has the Restrained condition. A creature can use its action to make a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check, freeing itself or another creature within its reach on a success. Each failed check deals 1d4 Piercing damage to the trapped creature.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The trapper makes two attacks, using Greataxe or Short Bow in any combination.  
>
>***Greataxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d12 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shove.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 13, one Large or smaller creature within 5 feet of the trapper. *Failure:* The creature is pushed 5 feet directly away from the trapper.
