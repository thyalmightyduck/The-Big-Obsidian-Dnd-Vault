___
>## Belligerent Brawler
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 105 (14d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +6, Con +4
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Intimidation +6
>- **Gear** *Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The brawler makes two attacks, using Grasping Strike or Punch Down in any combination.  
>
>***Grasping Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16).  
>
>***Punch Down.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Brace Up.*** The brawler gains 13 (3d8) Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Combo.*** The brawler makes one Grasping Strike or Punch Down attack.  
>
>***Enraging Insult.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature that can see or hear the brawler within 30 feet. *Failure:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Psychic damage, and the target has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than the brawler until the start of the brawler's next turn.
