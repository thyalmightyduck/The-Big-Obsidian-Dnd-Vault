___
>## Ogresh Champion
>*Medium Humanoid (Ogresh), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +3, Perception +3, Performance +4, Persuasion +4
>- **Gear** *Chain Mail*, *Holy Symbol*, *Shield*, *Warhammer*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Any three languages
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***A Friendly Ear (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** After speaking to a non-Hostile creature for 1 minute, the ogresh champion can subject it to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition for 1 hour. It is unaware of being Charmed even after the effect ends.  
>
>***Powerful Build.*** The ogresh champion counts as one Size larger for the purpose of determining its carrying capacity.  
>
>***Smite.*** When the ogresh champion hits with a melee attack, it can expend a spell from Spellcasting to deal extra radiant damage. The extra damage is 9 (2d8) for a 1/Day spell.  
>
>***Takes One to Know One.*** The ogresh champion has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spellcasting.*** The ogresh champion casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>1/day each: *Bless*, *Cure Wounds*, *Sanctuary*  
>
>***Warhammer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.
