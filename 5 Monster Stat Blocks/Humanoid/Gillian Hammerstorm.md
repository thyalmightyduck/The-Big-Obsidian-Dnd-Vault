___
>## Gillian Hammerstorm
>*Medium Humanoid (Dwarf), Chaotic Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 52 (7d8 + 21)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Dex +2, Con +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Insight +3, Perception +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Gear** *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Castinellan, Dwarvish, one other language
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Dwarven Resilience.*** Gillian has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.  
>
>***Heavy Hitter.*** Gillian's Unarmed Strike score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.  
>
>***Innate Toughness.*** Gillian can add her Constitution modifier to her AC instead of her Dexterity modifier (included above).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Gillian makes two Unarmed Strike, or three Unarmed Strike if she has the Bloodied condition.  
>
>***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Now I'm Angry (Recharges after a Long Rest).*** *Trigger:* Gillian becomes Bloodied. *Response:* Gillian gains Temporary Hit Points equal to half her Hit Point maximum, which last for 10 minutes. While she has these Temporary Hit Points, she has Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
