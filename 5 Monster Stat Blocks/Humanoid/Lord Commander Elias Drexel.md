>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d8 + 40) plus 85 (10d8 + 40) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)

>***Intimidating Aura.*** Elias projects a 30-foot Emanation of imposing command. An ally that start its turn in the Emanation has Advantage on the first attack roll it makes before the start of its next turn. A Hostile creature that starts its turn in the Emenation takes 10 (3d6) Thunder damage and is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to Elias's Intimidating Aura for 24 hours.  
>
>### Actions
>***Elias always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On his turn, Elias can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Elias takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Lord Commander's Blade.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 25 (4d8 + 7) Slashing damage, and Elias can apply one of the following martial masteries to the target:
>- **Wound.** The target has the Bleeding condition (4d4).
>- **Topple.** The target has the Prone condition.
>- **Sap.** The target has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the start of start of Elias's next turn.
>- **Longbow.** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +12, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 25 (4d8 + 7) Piercing damage, and Elias can apply one of the following martial masteries to the target:
>- **Push.** If the target is Large or smaller, it is pushed up to 10 feet away from Elias.
>- **Slow.** The target has the Slowed condition (DC 15 Constitution save ends).
>
>***Command.*** Elias chooses one ally he can see. If the ally can see and hear him, it can use its Reaction to make one weapon attack with Advantage. On a hit, the attack deals an extra 10 (3d6) damage.  
>
>***Flare (1/Round).*** Elias chooses a point he can see within 60 feet and fires at flare at it, signaling the Hooded Lantern's bombardment. Elias recharges his Artillery Strike. He can only use this Epic Action if it's the first Epic Action he has taken this round.  
>
>***Artillery Strike (Recharge Special).*** Elias can only use this Epic Action if it's the last Epic Action he takes this round. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 20-foot radius Sphere centered on the point Elias chose with his Flare Epic Action. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) Fire damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Elias chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on him. Elias can use this action even if he would otherwise be unable to take actions, his actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters his game statistics.
