___
>## Outlaw
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Animal Handling +3, Intimidation +4, Sleight of Hand +5
>- **Gear** *Blackpowder Pistol*, *Firearm Bullet*, *Rapier*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Rider's Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** The outlaw deals an extra 10 (3d6) damage when it hits a target with a weapon attack and has Advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the outlaw that doesn't have the Incapacitated condition and the outlaw doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack roll. The outlaw can also deal this extra damage if it has moved 20+ feet this turn before making the attack, including on a mount.  
>
>### Actions
>***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Blackpowder Pistol.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 25/100 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Mobile.*** Both the outlaw and its mount takes the Dash or Disengage action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Uncanny Riding.*** *Trigger:* A creature the outlaw can see hits it or its mount with an attack. *Response:* The outlaw halves the attack's damage against itself or its mount.
