___
>## Boreas's Chosen
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|19 (+4)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Intimidation +6, Survival +5
>- **Immunities** Cold
>- **Gear** *Leather Armor*, *Shield*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Ice Walk.*** The chosen moves across and climbs icy surfaces without needing to make an Ability Check. Additionally, snow and ice are not Difficult Terrain for the chosen.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The chosen makes two Spear attacks.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Cold damage.  
>
>***Breath of the North Wind (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14 Each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Cold damage, and the target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn. A creature failing the saving throw by 5 or more has the Petrified condition, as it is entombed in ice until the end of its next turn instead. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Freezing Blood.*** *Trigger:* The chosen is hit by an attack. *Response:* Creatures within 5 feet of the chosen take 4 (1d8) Cold damage.
