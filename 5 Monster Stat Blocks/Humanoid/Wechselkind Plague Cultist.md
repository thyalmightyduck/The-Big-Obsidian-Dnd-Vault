>## Wechselkind Plague Cultist
>*Small Humanoid (Warlock), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 82 (15d6 + 30)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +8, Deception +6, Perception +4, Stealth +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Gear** *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Sylvan and one other language
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Artificial Form.*** The wechselkind has Advantage on saving throws against the Poisoned condition and is immune to disease. It doesn't need to eat, drink, or breathe.  
>
>***Childish Agility.*** The wechselkind can move through the space of any creature larger than it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack..*** The wechselkind makes three attacks, using Dagger or Force Ray in any combination.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Force damage.  
>
>***Force Ray.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d10) Force damage.  
>
>***Faerie Glamour.*** The wechselkind casts *Disguise Self*(save DC 11), requiring no components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability. When it does so, it can disguise itself only as the humanoid child it was created to replace.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The wechselkind casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Poison and Disease*, *Invisibility*, *Mage Armor* (included above)  
>3/day: *Blight*, *Blindness/Deafness*, *Contagion*, *Death Ward*, *Dispel Magic*, *False Life*, *Gaseous Form*, *Hold Monster*, *Ray of Sickness*  
>
>3/Day (Level 5 Versions)  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (2/Day).*** The wechselkind casts *Misty Step* using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Misty Step (2/Day).*** The wechselkind casts *Counterspell* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
