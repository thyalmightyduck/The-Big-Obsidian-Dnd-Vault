>## Grudgel Strategist
>*Medium Humanoid (Grudgel), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Insight +3, Perception +3
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Halberd*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Grudgel and two national languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Weapon Bond.*** The grudgel strategist can only be disarmed if it is dead or has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The grudgel strategist makes two Halberd attacks. It can replace one attack with Shooting Star.  
>
>***Halberd.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Slashing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it is pushed 5 feet to another unoccupied space within the grudgel strategist's reach or has the Prone condition (grudgel's choice).  
>
>***Shooting Star.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) Cold damage, and the target can't take Reactions until the start of the grudgel strategist's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The grudgel strategist casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>1/day each: *Color Spray*, *Mirror Image*, *Sleep*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Center (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The grudgel strategist gains Advantage on the next attack roll or ability check it makes before the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Magical Movement (2/Day).*** The grudgel strategist casts *Expeditious Retreat* or *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Battlefield Control.*** *Trigger:* A creature moves into the grudgel strategist's reach. *Response:* The grudgel strategist makes an Opportunity Attack against the triggering creature.  
>
>***Shield (2/Day).*** The grudgel strategist casts *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
