___
>## Zealot
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Intimidation +3
>- **Gear** *Greatsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Unshakeable Faith (1/Day).*** When the zealot would fail a saving throw, it can reroll the save with Advantage. It must use the new roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The zealot makes two Greatsword attacks.  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***For the Flame (1/Day).*** Allies of the zealot within 60 feet of it that can hear it gain Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the start of the zealot's next turn.
