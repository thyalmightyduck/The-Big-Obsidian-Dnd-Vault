>## Lyrandar Scion
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Khoravar), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 60 (11d8 + 11)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +3, Dex +5
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Lightning, Thunder
>- **Gear** *Wand*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The scion makes two Thunderbolt attacks and uses Spellcasting.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The scion casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Elementalism*, *Gust of Wind*, *Thunderwave*  
>
>***Thunderbolt.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 30 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Lightning damage plus 9 (2d8) Thunder damage. Critical *Hit:* The creature also has the Deafened condition for 1 minute.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Jump (2/Day).*** The scion casts *Jump*, requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Feather Fall (2/Day).*** The scion casts *Feather Fall* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
