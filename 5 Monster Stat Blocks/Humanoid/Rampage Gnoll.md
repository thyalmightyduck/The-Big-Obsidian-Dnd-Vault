>## Rampage Gnoll
>*Medium Humanoid (Gnoll), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +4, Athletics +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Gnoll
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Instinctive.*** The rampage gnoll has Advantage on Initiative rolls and on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed and Frightened conditions. Being surprised doesn't impose Disadvantage on the rampage gnoll's Initiative rolls.  
>
>***Rampage Aura.*** A hostile creature that ends its turn within 10 feet of the rampage gnoll is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target must use its Reaction to make a melee attack using a weapon or cantrip against a creature of the rampage gnoll's choice. The target moves up to half its Speed if necessary to reach the creature, taking the safest possible path. *Success:* The target is immune to the Rampage Aura of all rampage gnolls for 1 hour.  
>
>***Skilled Brute.*** A weapon deals one extra die of its damage when the rampage gnoll hits with it (included in the attacks).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The rampage gnoll makes three attacks, only one of which can be a Bite attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Wing Spike.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampage.*** After reducing a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, the rampage gnoll moves up to half its Speed and makes a Bite attack.
