>- **Immunities** Poisoned

>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Dendallen makes four attacks, using Staff or Chilling Ray in any combination. He can (A) replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Magic Missile* or (B) replace two attacks with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Blight* (level 5 version).  
>
>***Staff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) Force damage. If the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Necrotic damage, and the target has Disadvantage on any ability check or saving throw that uses Strength or Constitution. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.  
>
>***Chilling Ray.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Cold damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Dendallen casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17):  
>
>At will: *Bestow Curse*, *Darkness*, *Dispel Magic*, *Hold Person*, *Mage Armor* (included in AC), *Mage Hand*, *Magic Missile*  
>2/day each: *Blight* (level 5 version), *Circle of Death*, *Cloudkill*, *Telekinesis*, *Wall of Force*  
>1/day each: *Finger of Death*, *Prismatic Spray*, *Weird*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Magic (3/Day).*** Dendallen casts *Counterspell* or *Shield* in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
