___
>## Venomous Gnoll
>*Medium Humanoid (Gnoll), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|6 (-2)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +3
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Gnoll
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The venomous gnoll makes two attacks, only one of which can be a Bite attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage and 2 (1d4) Poison damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage and 2 (1d4) Poison damage.  
>
>***Spine Spike.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage and 2 (1d4) Poison damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampage.*** After reducing a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, the venomous gnoll moves up to half its Speed and makes a Bite attack.
