___
>## Radlee Thugram
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Soldier); Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (*plate*)
>- **Hit Points** 130 (20d8 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|24 (+7)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Con +5, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Athletics +10, Intimidation +4, Perception +6, Survival +6
>- **Gear** *Halberd*, *Longbow*
>- **Senses** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Exploit Opening (3/Day).*** When Radlee makes an attack, she has advantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Sure-Footed.*** Radlee has advantage on saving throws against being moved against her will or knocked prone.  
>
>***Tactical Vigilance.*** Radlee has advantage on initiative checks.  
>While she isn't incapacitated, Radlee and each ally within 30 feet of her who can see or hear her can't be surprised.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Radlee makes three Halberd or three Longbow attacks. Alternatively, she makes one Halberd attack and uses Backswipe.  
>
>***Halberd.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (1d10 + 7) slashing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.  
>
>***Backswipe.*** Radlee swings her halberd's shaft at a creature within 5 feet of her. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or take 16 (3d10) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protector.*** When a creature Radlee can see within 10 feet of her attacks one of her allies, Radlee can make a Halberd attack against the attacker.
