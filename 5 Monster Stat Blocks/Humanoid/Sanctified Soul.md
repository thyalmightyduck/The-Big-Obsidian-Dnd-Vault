>***Sanctified by Falling Fire.*** When the sanctified soul dies, its soul is pulled into the *delerium crystal* embedded in its chest.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** The sanctified soul is immune to the Contamination condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Quarterstaff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Bludgeoning damage, or 5 (1d8 + 1) Bludgeoning damage if wielded with two hands.  
>
>***Guiding Bolt.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The sanctified soul casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Faerie Fire*, *Flame Strike*  
>1/day each: *Wall of Fire*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Flash of Light.*** *Trigger:* A creature the sanctified soul can see makes an attack roll. *Response:* The sanctified soul imposes Disadvantage on the attack roll.
