>## Inquisitor of the Mind Fire
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 104 (16d8 + 32)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|21 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +6, Wis +6, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Insight +6, Perception +6
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Silvered Longsword*
>- **Senses** Truesight 30 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The inquisitor makes three Silvered Longsword attacks or uses Mind Fire three times. It can replace one attack or one use of Mind Fire with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Hold Monster* if available.  
>
>***Silvered Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the start of the inquisitor's next turn. Critical *Hit:* If the target is a shape-shifted creature, it takes an additional 4 (1d8) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Inquisitor's Command (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature of the inquisitor's choice that it can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition until the start of the inquisitor's next turn. On the Charmed target's turn, the inquisitor can telepathically control the target's move and make the target take the Attack action (the inquisitor chooses the target), the Dash action, or no action.  
>
>***Mind Fire.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the inquisitor can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 9 (1d8 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the start of the inquisitor's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The inquisitor casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Dispel Magic*, *Mage Hand* (the hand is Invisible), *Sending*  
>1/day each: *Hold Monster*, *Modify Memory*
