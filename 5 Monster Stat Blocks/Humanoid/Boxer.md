>## Boxer
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +8
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The boxer makes three attacks, using Grasping Strike or Punch Down in any combination.  
>
>***Grasping Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16).  
>
>***Punch Down.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Brace Up.*** The boxer gains 18 (4d8) Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Combo.*** The boxer makes one Grasping Strike or Punch Down attack.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Cross Counter.*** *Trigger:* The boxer is hit by a melee attack made by a creature. *Response:* The boxer reduces the damage it takes by 18 (4d8) and deals that much Bludgeoning damage to the target if it is within 5 feet.
