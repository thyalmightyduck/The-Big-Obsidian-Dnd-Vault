___
>## Tormach Cultist
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 16 (3d8 + 3)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Intimidation +2
>- **Gear** *Handaxe*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** One national language
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Tormach cultist uses Frightening Scream and makes two Handaxe attacks.  
>
>***Handaxe.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Frightening Scream.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 10, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the cultist that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the end of the cultist's next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to the Frightening Scream of all Tormach cultists for 24 hours.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampage.*** When the Tormach cultist reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, it can move up to half its Speed and make a melee attack.
