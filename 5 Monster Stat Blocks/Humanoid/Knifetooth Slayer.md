___
>## Knifetooth Slayer
>*Medium Humanoid, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|5 (-3)|13 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +3
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Survival +3
>- **Gear** *Greatsword*, *Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Brutal Attacks.*** When the Knifetooth slayer scores a Critical Hit with a melee weapon attack, it deals an extra die of damage. This damage die isn't doubled.  
>
>***Reckless.*** At the start of its turn, the Knifetooth slayer can gain Advantage on melee weapon attack rolls until the start of its next turn, but attack rolls against it have Advantage until then.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Knifetooth slayer makes two Greatsword attacks. If it misses with either attack, it can use Bite.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.
