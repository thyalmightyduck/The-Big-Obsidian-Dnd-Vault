>- **Hit Points** 105 (14d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)

>***Spellcasting.*** The justice is a 3rd-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 13). It has the following paladin spells prepared:  
>

>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The justice makes two attacks.  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.
