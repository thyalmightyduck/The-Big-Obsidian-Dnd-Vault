>## Claunt Mamona
>*Small Humanoid, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 38 (7d6 + 14)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +4, Cha +4
>- **Skills** Deception +4, History +4, Perception +3, Persuasion +4, Sleight of Hand +3
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Gear** *Blackpowder Pistol*, *Pouch*, *Breastplate*, *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Charneault, Castellan, Ostoy, Higher Bürach
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Lasting Curse.*** If Claunt is restored to his original form by a *Remove Curse* spell or similar magic, he returns to his porcine form the next time he finishes a Long Rest. Only a *Wish* spell, or slaying the spellcaster that cursed him, can permanently remove the curse.  
>
>### Actions
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Blackpowder Pistol.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3 to hit, range 25/100 ft. *Hit:* 6 (2d4 + 1) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Fire damage.  
>
>***Satchel of Sorcerous Sales.*** Claunt reaches into his satchel, dropping in any number of gold pieces to withdraw a nonmagical item with a value equal to or less than the deposited sum. *Gold* deposited into the satchel goes directly into Gorodyn's infernal treasury. In addition to nonmagical items, Claunt can also purchase the following potions from his satchel: *Potion of Climbing* (50 GP), *Potion of Healing* (25 GP), *Potion of Greater Healing* (100 GP), *Potion of Resistance* (200 GP), and *Potion of Waterbreathing* (200 GP). Only Claunt can attune to this item.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Enlightened Self-Interest.*** Claunt takes the Dash or Disengage action.
