___
>## Downcast Apostate
>*Medium Humanoid (Downcast, Cleric); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Deception +8, Intimidation +8, Perception +5, Persuasion +8, Religion +5, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Gear** *Arcane Focus*, *Morningstar*
>- **Senses** Truesight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Celestial and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The downcast apostate makes two attacks, using Morningstar or Holy Flame in any combination.  
>
>***Morningstar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Force damage plus 18 (4d8) Necrotic or Radiant damage (apostate's choice).  
>
>***Holy Flame.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the downcast apostate can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 22 (5d8) Radiant damage. Cover grants no benefit against this saving throw.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The downcast apostate casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Alter Self*, *Invisibility*, *Light*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*  
>4/rest: *Cure Wounds*, *Dimension Door*, *Dispel Magic*, *Flame Strike*, *Fly*, *Greater Restoration*, *Guiding Bolt*, *Insect Plague*, *Lesser Restoration*, *Suggestion*, *Tongues*, *Vampiric Touch*, *Wall of Fire*  
>1/day: *Glibness*, *Mass Suggestion*, *Plane Shift*, *Power Word Kill*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell (2/Day).*** The downcast apostate casts *Counterspell* (level 5 version) in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability in Innate Spellcasting.
