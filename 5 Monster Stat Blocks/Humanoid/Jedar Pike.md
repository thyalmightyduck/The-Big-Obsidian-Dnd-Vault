>## Jedar Pike
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Support); Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (*half plate*)
>- **Hit Points** 27 (5d8 + 5)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +4, Cha +4
>- **Skills** Insight +4, Perception +4, Religion +2
>- **Gear** *Warhammer*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Celestial, Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Exploit Opening (3/Day).*** When Jedar makes an attack, he has advantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Spellcasting (Utility).*** In addition to any other spells in this stat block, Jedar can cast the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *light* A, *thaumaturgy* A  
>1/day: *augury* +  
>
>### Actions
>***Warhammer.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) necrotic damage.  
>
>***Nameless Reach (2/Day; 1st-Level Spell).*** *Ranged Spell Attack:*  +4 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) necrotic damage, and the next attack roll made against this target before the end of Jedar's next turn has advantage.  
>
>***Animate Bones (1/Day).*** Jedar animates three **decrepit skeleton**s, who appear in unoccupied spaces Jedar can see within 30 feet of him. The skeletons act immediately after Jedar on the same initiative count and follow his verbal orders.  
>If Jedar dies, the skeletons are destroyed.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bone Shield.*** Jedar destroys a decrepit skeleton under his control and chooses a creature he can see within 60 feet of him. The target gains 7 (2d6) temporary hit points.
