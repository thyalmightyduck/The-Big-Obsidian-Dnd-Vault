___
>## Shield-Maiden
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +4
>- **Skills** Intimidation +3, Perception +3
>- **Gear** *Javelin*, *Longsword*, *Studded Leather Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The shield-maiden makes three attacks, using Longsword or Javelin in any combination. The shield maiden can replace one attack with Shield Bash.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Javelin.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Shield Bash.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 12 One creature within 5 feet that the shield-maiden can see. *Failure:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Call to Arms (Recharge 6).*** Up to three creatures of the shield-maiden's choice that are within 30 feet of her and can hear her gain Advantage on attack rolls until the start of the shield-maiden's next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Interpose Shield.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 5 feet of the shield-maiden is hit by a melee attack roll. *Response:* The target adds 2 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss. If the attack misses, the shield-maiden can make one weapon attack against the attacker.
