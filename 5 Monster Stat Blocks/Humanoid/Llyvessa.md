>## Llyvessa
>*Medium Humanoid (Elf, Artillery); Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (*studded leather armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 88 (16d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 35 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|22 (+6)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +9, Nature +5, Perception +6, Stealth +9, Survival +6
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Scimitar*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Elvish, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Deadly Sharpshooter.*** Attacking at long range with a ranged weapon doesn't impose disadvantage on Llyvessa's attack rolls.  
>
>***Elf Glamour (1/Day).*** When a creature targets Llyvessa with an attack, power, or spell, the creature must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by her until the end of her next turn (no action required).  
>
>***Mind Link.*** Llyvessa can communicate telepathically with any Amethyst Knife boss regardless of distance, provided they are on the same plane of existence.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Llyvessa makes two Scimitar or Longbow attacks. Alternatively, she makes one Longbow attack and uses Psychic Shot, if available.  
>
>***Scimitar.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d6 + 6) slashing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +9 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) piercing damage.  
>
>***Psychic Shot (Recharge 5–6).*** Llyvessa makes a Longbow attack that ignores cover, and which can pass through mundane solid objects up to 5 feet thick that aren't being worn or carried.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Jaunt (1/Day; 3rd-Order Power).*** Llyvessa or one willing creature within 5 feet of her teleports to an unoccupied space Llyvessa can see within 30 feet of her.  
>
>### Reactions
>****Control Projectiles (3/Day; 2nd-Order Power).*** When a creature Llyvessa can see within 60 feet of her makes a ranged weapon attack, she gives the creature advantage or disadvantage on the attack roll (her choice).  
>
>***Shared Condition.*** When Llyvessa is blinded, charmed, dazed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, or stunned, she chooses a willing Amethyst Knife boss within 60 feet of her who doesn't already have the condition or is immune to it. The chosen creature then gains the condition for the duration, and Llyvessa loses the condition. She can use this reaction even while incapacitated.
