___
>## Disembodied Arcanist
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 88 (16d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, Investigation +6, Perception +4
>- **Gear** *Arcane Focus*, *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Coded Speech.*** The disembodied arcanist can speak in such a way that creatures it chooses hear a hidden meaning, but all others hear only what the arcanist says. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 14, any creature that hears the arcanist's coded speech. *Success:* The target knows the arcanist's speech held a hidden message, but not what that message was.  
>
>***Occultation.*** Any creature of the disembodied arcanist's choice that interacts with it for fewer than 10 minutes is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target forgets its interaction with the arcanist.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The disembodied arcanist makes three attacks, using Dagger or Frost Blast in any combination.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage and 4 (1d8) Lightning damage.  
>
>***Frost Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) Cold damage, and the target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of the disembodied arcanist's next turn.  
>
>***Fade Away (Recharges after a Long Rest).*** The disembodied arcanist enters the Ethereal Plane from the Material Plane. It can't affect or be affected by anything on the Material Plane. The effect lasts for 1 minute or until the arcanist uses a Bonus Action to end it.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The disembodied arcanist casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Fog Cloud*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Gust of Wind*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Magic Missile*, *Phantasmal Force*, *Vampiric Touch*  
>1/day each: *Blink*, *Blur*, *Phantasmal Killer*, *Phantom Steed*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Feather Fall.*** The disembodied arcanist casts *Feather Fall* in response to that spell's trigger, uusing the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting and targeting only itself.  
>
>***Shield (2/Day).*** The disembodied arcanist casts *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
