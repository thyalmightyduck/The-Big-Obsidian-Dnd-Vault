___
>## Jarl
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 112 (15d8 + 45)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +6
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Perception +4, Persuasion +6
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Longsword*, *Scale Mail*, *Shield*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, plus three other languages.
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of Command.*** The jarl and Friendly creatures within 10 feet of it can't be Charmed or Frightened while the jarl isn't Incapacitated.  
>
>***Mental Fortitude.*** The jarl has Immunity to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts. In addition, it has Advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain a creature's intentions or sincerity.  
>
>***Resilient Leader (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** If the jarl fails a save, it can reroll the die and must use the new roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The jarl makes three attacks, using Longsword and Longbow in any combination.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Force damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, range 150/600 feet., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Force damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Loyal Heroism.*** The jarl inspires up to two Friendly creatures it can see within 30 feet of it. Each target has Advantage on the next attack roll or save it makes before the start of the jarl's next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Evade.*** *Trigger:* The jarl is Bloodied and is hit by an attack. *Response:* The jarl adds 3 to its AC against that attack. If that causes the attack to miss, the jarl can move up to half its Speed away from the attacker without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
