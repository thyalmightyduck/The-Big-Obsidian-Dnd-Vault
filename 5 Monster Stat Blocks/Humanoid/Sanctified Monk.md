>## Sanctified Monk
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 66 (12d8 + 11)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|19 (+4)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Insight +4, Religion +4, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Radiant
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Contamination|Monstersofdrakkenheim
>- **Gear** *Dart*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Sanctified by Falling Fire.*** When the monk dies, its soul is pulled into the *delerium crystal* embedded in its chest.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** Sanctified Monk is immune to the Contamination condition.  
>
>***Unarmored Defense.*** While the monk is wearing no armor and wielding no shield, its AC includes its Wisdom modifier (included above).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The monk makes three Unarmed Strike attacks or five Dart attacks.  
>
>***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Dart.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Flame Step.*** The monk teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 60 feet that is in Bright Light. It gains Advantage on its next melee attack.
