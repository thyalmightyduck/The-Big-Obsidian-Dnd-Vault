___
>## Viking
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|9 (-1)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Con +4
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Intimidation +2
>- **Gear** *Battleaxe*, *Chain Shirt*, *Shortbow*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The viking has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the viking's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The viking makes two attacks, using Battleaxe and Shortbow in any combination.  
>
>***Battleaxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3 to hit, range 80/320 feet., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 1) Piercing damage.
