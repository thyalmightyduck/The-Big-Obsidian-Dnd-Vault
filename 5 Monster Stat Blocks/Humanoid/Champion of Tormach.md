>## Champion of Tormach
>*Large Humanoid (Any Race), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Insight +3, Intimidation +4, Perception +3
>- **Gear** *Battleaxe*, *Splint Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Two national languages
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Protection Aura.*** The champion of Tormach emits a 10-foot Emanation of protection. While the champion doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, each creature of the champion's choice in the Emanation gains a +2 bonus to saving throws. A creature can benefit from only one such aura at a time.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The champion of Tormach makes two Battleaxe attacks.  
>
>***Battleaxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The champion of Tormach casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>2/day each: *Bane*, *Magic Weapon*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hunter's Mark (2/Day).*** The champion of Tormach casts *Hunter's Mark*, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Misty Step (1/Day).*** The champion of Tormach casts *Misty Step*, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Smite.*** When the champion of Tormach hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, it can expend a spell from its Spellcasting. The spell has no effect, and the attack deals an extra 9 (2d8) Necrotic or Radiant damage.
