>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Laurie makes two attacks, using Gossamer or Light Crossbow in any combination.  
>
>***Gossamer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Light Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d8 + 1) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Deflect Blow.*** *Trigger:* An attack roll hits Laurie or a creature she can see within 5 feet. *Response:* The triggering attack's damage is reduced by 5 (1d10).
