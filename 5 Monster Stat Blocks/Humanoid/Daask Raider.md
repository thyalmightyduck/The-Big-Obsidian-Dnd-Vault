>## Daask Raider
>*Medium Humanoid (Gnoll), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Perception +2
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common, Gnoll
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The raider has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the raider's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pummel.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Smackback.*** *Trigger:* The raider takes damage from a creature within 5 feet. *Response:* The raider makes one Pummel attack, targeting the triggering creature.
