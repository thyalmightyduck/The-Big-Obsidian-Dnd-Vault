>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Petra makes three attacks.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the end of Petra's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** Petra takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Skirmisher.*** *Trigger:* A hostile creature Petra can see ends its turn within 5 feet of her. *Response:* Petra moves up to her Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.
