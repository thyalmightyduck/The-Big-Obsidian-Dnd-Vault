>## Void Pirate Squib
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 66 (12d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Gear** *Leather Armor*, *Scimitar*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Captain's Orders.*** While within 60 feet of a void pirate captain, the void pirate has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed and Frightened conditions.  
>
>***From Another World.*** The void pirate doesn't have the standard Aberrant Traits other creatures in the Space Between Worlds have.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The void pirate makes two Void Cutlass attacks or uses its Sturdy Net.  
>
>***Void Cutlass.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Sturdy Net.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 15/30 ft. *Hit:* The target has the Restrained and Defenseless conditions. A creature can take an action to free itself or another creature in a net with a successful DC 14 Strength check. Dealing 10 Slashing damage to the net (AC 12) frees the target without harming it and destroys the net.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Man the Cannons.*** The void pirate loads an artillery weapon, such as a cannon or ballista.
