___
>## Mutagenist
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7, Int +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Medicine +5
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Noxious Blood.*** When a creature hits the mutagenist with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it, the creature takes 10 (3d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mutagenist makes two Scalpel attacks and uses Forced Mutation.  
>
>***Scalpel.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (4d6).  
>
>***Forced Mutation.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature the mutagenist can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage, and the target gains a randomly determined mutation. The mutation lasts for 1 minute, and afterwards, any lost body parts are restored. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The mutagenist casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Alter Self*  
>2/day: *Enhance Ability*  
>1/day: *Polymorph*  
>
>***Transmogrifying Elixir (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The mutagenist injects itself with a transmogrifying elixir. The mutagenist transforms, using the Grotesque Gargantuan stat block for their altered form. The mutagenist reverts to its normal form after 1 hour or if its transmogrified form is reduced to 0 Hit Points.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Anatomical Precision.*** The mutagenist gains Advantage on Scalpel attacks until the start of its next turn, as long as it has not moved this turn. The mutagenist's Speed becomes 0 until the start of its next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Desperate Transformation.*** *Trigger:* The mutagenist is reduced to 0 Hit Points. *Response:* The mutagenist uses Transmogrifying Elixir, if available.
