___
>## Free Swords Battlemage
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +5
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Athletics +5, Insight +2, Perception +2
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Longbow*, *Longsword*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Any three languages
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Arms Expert.*** The Veteran can don or doff its *Shield* whenever it changes weapons.  
>
>***Group Discipline.*** The Veteran has Advantage on all saving throws if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet and both the Veteran and the ally don't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Individual Skill.*** The first time on a turn that the Veteran deals damage with an attack, the attack deals an additional 1d8 damage if the Veteran has no allies within 5 feet of it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Veteran makes two Longsword or Longbow attacks.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Spellcasting.*** The Battlemage casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material component and using Intelligence as its spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13).  
>
>1/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Fireball*, *Haste*
