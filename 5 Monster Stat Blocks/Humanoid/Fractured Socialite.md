___
>## Fractured Socialite
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 22 (3d8 + 9)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +3, Intimidation +3
>- **Gear** *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Reckless (in Fractured Rage Only).*** At the start of its turn, the fractured socialite can gain Advantage on melee weapon attacks until the start of its next turn, but attack rolls against it have Advantage for the same duration.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack (in Fractured Rage Only).*** The fractured socialite makes two Cane attacks.  
>
>***Cane.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Fractured Rage (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The fractured socialite becomes enraged, swelling in size and distorting facial features to an unrecognizable state. This transformation lasts for 1 hour or until the socialite has the Incapacitated condition. The socialite gains 10 Temporary Hit Points, which last until the transformation ends. While enraged, the socialite can roll 1d4 and add the result to each Strengthbased D20 Test and damage roll it makes.
