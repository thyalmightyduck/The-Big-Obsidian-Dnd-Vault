___
>## Champion of Filth
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Non-Good Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 28)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +3
>- **Skills** Intimidation +5
>- **Gear** *Longsword*, *Shield*, *Splint Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blighted Aura.*** When a creature within 10 feet of the champion of filth makes a D20 Test, it must roll 1d4 and subtract the result from its roll. This effect doesn't stack with other Blighted Auras or the *Bane* spell.  
>
>***Divine Health.*** The champion of filth is immune to disease.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The champion of filth makes two Longsword attacks.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The champion of filth casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>2/day each: *Detect Poison and Disease*, *Ray of Enfeeblement*  
>1/day each: *Blindness/Deafness*, *Hold Person*
