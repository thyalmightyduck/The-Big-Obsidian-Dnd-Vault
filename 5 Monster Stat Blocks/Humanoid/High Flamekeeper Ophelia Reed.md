>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Ophelia makes two Spiritual Weapon attacks. She can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Ophelia casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Guidance*, *Light*, *Spare the Dying*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Aura of Vitality*, *Banishment*, *Bless*, *Dispel Magic*, *Flame Strike*, *Greater Restoration*, *Mass Cure Wounds*  
>1/day each: *Heal*, *Raise Dead*, *Sunbeam*  
>
>***Spiritual Weapon.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 30 ft. *Hit:* 25 (6d6 + 4) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Healing Word (3/Day).*** Ophelia casts *Healing Word* using the spellcasting ability from Spellcasting.
