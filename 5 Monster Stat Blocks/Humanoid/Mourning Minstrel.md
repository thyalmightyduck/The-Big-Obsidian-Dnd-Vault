>## Mourning Minstrel
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 60 (11d8 + 11)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Perception +4, Performance +5
>- **Gear** *Musical Instrument*, *Rapier*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Death Note.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +5, range 30 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d8) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The minstrel casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Dancing Lights*, *Minor Illusion*, *Speak with Dead*  
>2/day each: *Faerie Fire*, *Hideous Laughter*, *Shatter*  
>1/day each: *Animate Dead*, *Blindness/Deafness*, *Invisibility*, *Major Image*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Danse Macabre (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The minstrel plays a magical requiem. Until the end of its next turn, Undead of the minstrel's choice within 30 feet that can see or hear the minstrel rolls 1d4 and adds the result to each ability check and saving throw it makes. This effect doesn't stack with others that grant similar bonuses, such as the *Bless* spell.  
>As a Bonus Action on subsequent turns, the minstrel can extend the requiem until the end of its next turn.
