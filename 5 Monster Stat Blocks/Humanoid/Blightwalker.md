>## Blightwalker
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Non-Good Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 180 (24d8 + 72)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5
>- **Skills** Intimidation +7
>- **Gear** *Longsword*, *Shield*, *Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Blighted Aura.*** When a creature within 10 feet of the blightwalker makes a D20 Test, it must roll 1d6 and subtract the result from its roll. This effect doesn't stack with other Blighted Auras or the *Bane* spell.  
>
>***Blighted Contact.*** A creature that takes Necrotic damage from the blightwalker is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target is afflicted with a rotting disease, taking 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>
>***Divine Health.*** The blightwalker is immune to disease.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The blightwalker makes three Longsword attacks.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage, and the target is subjected to Blighted Contact.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The champion of filth casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>2/day each: *Blindness/Deafness*, *Detect Poison and Disease*, *Hold Person*, *Ray of Enfeeblement*  
>1/day each: *Bestow Curse*, *Blight*, *Stinking Cloud*
