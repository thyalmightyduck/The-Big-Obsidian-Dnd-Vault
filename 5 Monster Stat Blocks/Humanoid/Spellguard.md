___
>## Spellguard
>*Medium Humanoid, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 105 (14d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Athletics +7, Perception +4
>- **Gear** *Flail*, six *Javelins*, *Plate Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Superior Magic Resistance.*** The spellguard has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. Spell attack rolls have Disadvantage against the spellguard.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The spellguard makes two attacks, using Force Flail or Javelin in any combination.  
>
>***Force Flail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Force damage.  
>
>***Javelin.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Defensive Divination.*** *Trigger:* A creature the spellguard can see hits an ally within 5 feet of the spellguard with an attack roll, or an ally within 5 feet of the spellguard fails a saving throw against a spell or other magical effect. *Response:* The spellguard rolls 1d20 and chooses whether to use that roll in place of the d20 rolled for the attack roll or saving throw.
