# Morte’s Planar Parade (MPP)
___
## Fated Shaker
*Small or Medium [[Humanoid]], Any Alignment*
___
- **[[Armor Class]]** 13 (16 with *mage armor*)
- **[[Hit Point|Hit Points]]** 76 (17d8)
- **[[Speed]]** 30 ft.
- **[[Initiative]]** +3 (13)
___

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|
___
- **[[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]]** Int +5, Wis +6
- **[[Skill|Skills]]** [[Insight]] +6, [[Investigation]] +5, [[Perception]] +6
- **Senses** [[Passive Perception]] 16, Passive Insight 16, Passive Stealth 13
- **Languages** [[Common]] plus two more languages
- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
- **[[Proficiency Bonus]]** +3
___
### [[Action|Actions]]
***Multiattack.*** The shaker makes two Golden Rod or Radiant Bolt attacks.  

***Golden Rod.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) radiant damage.  

***Radiant Bolt.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:*  +6 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 3) radiant damage.  

***Spellcasting.*** The shaker casts one of the following spells, requiring no material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  

At will: *[[mage armor]]*, *[[mage hand]]*  
1/day each: *[[enlarge reduce]]*, *[[fly]]*, *[[suggestion]]*  

### [[Bonus Action|Bonus Actions]]
***Commanding Words.*** The shaker speaks magical words to order a creature it can see within 30 feet of itself. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom [[saving throw]] or be affected by one of the following effects (choose one or roll a d4):  
- **1-2: Grovel.** The target takes 14 (4d6) psychic damage, drops whatever it is holding, and has the [[Prone|prone condition]].
- **3-4: Cower.** The target takes 10 (3d6) psychic damage and has the [[Frightened|frightened condition]] until the end of its next turn.

- - -
### Info
Fated shakers are bullies who shake down those indebted to the faction's tax collectors and evictors. These enforcers use their magic to intimidate and subjugate those who try to stand up to them.
