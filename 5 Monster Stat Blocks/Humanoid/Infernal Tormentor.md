>## Infernal Tormentor
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Evil Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 97 (13d8 + 39)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Intimidation +5, Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire
>- **Gear** *Greatsword*, *Half Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Infernal and one other language
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The infernal tormentor makes two Greatsword attacks. It can replace one attack with Infernal Leash.  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing plus 7 (2d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Infernal Leash (Recharge 6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature the infernal tormentor can see within 30 feet. A creature that has signed a fiendish contract has Disadvantage on this save. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Fire damage. The target is pulled 10 feet toward the tormentor and cursed for 1 minute or until the tormentor uses this action again. The curse also ends if the target enters hallowed ground.  
>While cursed, the target can't move more than 30 feet from the tormentor and takes an extra 7 (2d6) Fire damage from the tormentor's Greatsword attacks. As a Bonus Action, the tormentor can move the target up to 10 feet to any unoccupied space within 30 feet of the tormentor. If the space is dangerous, the target repeats the save before moving, ending the effect on a success.
