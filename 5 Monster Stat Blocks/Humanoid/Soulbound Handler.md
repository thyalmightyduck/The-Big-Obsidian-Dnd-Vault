___
>## Soulbound Handler
>*Medium Humanoid, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16 (Fiendish Audacity, *shield*)
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Con +5, Wis +4, Cha +4
>- **Gear** *Morningstar*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common, Infernal
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fiendish Adacity.*** The handler's AC includes its Charisma modifier.  
>
>***Fiendish Fury.*** The handler deals an extra 5 (1d10) damage when it hits with a melee weapon attack (included in the attacks).  
>
>***Fiendish Vigour.*** The handler gains 5 temporary hit points at the start of each of its turns. If the handler takes radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the handler's next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The handler makes two attacks with its Morningstar or Tommybow.  
>
>***Morningstar.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 3) piercing damage.  
>
>***Brittlebone Splinterspray Tommybow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., ., two creatures within 5 feet of each other. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) piercing damage. Reload (3).  
>
>### Reactions
>***Guardian.*** When a creature within 5 feet of the handler is the target of an attack, the handler can choose to become the target of the attack instead of the original target.
