___
>## Abjurer Archmage
>*Medium Humanoid (Wizard), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 198 (36d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +15, History +10, Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed
>- **Gear** *Staff*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus five other languages
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The archmage has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The archmage makes three Arcane Burst attacks.  
>
>***Arcane Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. or range 150 ft. *Hit:* 27 (4d10 + 5) Force damage.  
>
>***Banishing Blast (Recharge 6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Force damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition and is transported to a harmless demiplane until the start of the archmage's next turn, at which point it reappears in the space it left or in the nearest unoccupied space if that space is occupied. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The archmage casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18):  
>
>At will: *Arcane Lock*, *Detect Magic*, *Mage Armor* (included in AC), *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Fireball* (level 7 version), *Nondetection*, *Otiluke's Resilient Sphere*  
>1/day each: *Mind Blank* (cast before combat), *Teleport*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Fortify (Recharge 4–6).*** The archmage magically bolsters itself. The archmage gains 40 Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Magic Disruption (2/Day).*** The archmage casts *Dispel Magic* or *Globe of Invulnerability*, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell (3/Day).*** The archmage casts *Counterspell* in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
