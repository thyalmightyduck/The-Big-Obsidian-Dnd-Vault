___
>## Shaman
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 84 (13d8 + 26)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +3, Medicine +5, Perception +5
>- **Gear** *Hide Armor*, *Quarterstaff*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The shaman makes three attacks, using Ritual Staff or Cursed Bolt in any combination.  
>
>***Ritual Staff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Cursed Bolt.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The shaman casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Druidcraft*, *Elementalism*, *Guidance*, *Mending*  
>2/day each: *Cure Wounds*, *Entangle*, *Fog Cloud*  
>1/day each: *Flaming Sphere*, *Plant Growth*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Inspire Ferocity (Recharge 4–6).*** The shaman inspires ferocity in up to three creatures it can see. Those targets have Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of the shaman's next turn and gain 10 Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Minor Elemental Absorption (3/Day).*** *Trigger:* The shaman takes Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage. *Response:* The shaman gives itself Resistance to that instance of damage.
