>## Ebon Syndicate Elite Assassin
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 162 (25d8 + 50)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +13 (23)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Int +6
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Insight +7, Perception +11, Sleight of Hand +9, Stealth +13
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Heavy Crossbow*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Thieves' Cant, plus three other languages
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Customized Poison.*** The assassin prepares and applies specialized poisons to its weapons. The first time each turn that the assassin deals weapon damage to a creature, choose one of the following:  
>- **Abduction.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Unconscious condition for 1 minute. The condition ends if the target takes damage or a creature within 5 feet of it takes an action to wake it. *Success:* The target has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn.
>- **Annihilation.** The target takes 28 (8d6) Poison damage.
>- **Debilitation.** The target takes 17 (5d6) Poison damage, and has the Poisoned condition until the start of the assassin's next turn.
>- **Suffocation.** The target has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. While Poisoned in this way, the target can't speak or breathe.
>
>***Evasion.*** If the assassin is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, the assassin instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the save and only half damage if it fails. It can't use this trait if it has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The assassin makes three attacks using its Daggers or one attack using its Heavy Crossbow.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (4d4 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Heavy Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 27 (4d10 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The assassin takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Smoke Bomb (2/Day).*** *Trigger:* The assassin takes damage from another creature. *Response:* The assassin creates smoke in a 20-foot-radius Sphere. The area becomes Heavily Obscured for 1 minute or until dispersed by a strong wind. As part of this Reaction, the assassin can move up to its Speed and take the Hide action.
