___
>## Pathogenist
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 213 (25d8 + 100)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|19 (+4)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Int +8, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +8, Medicine +10, Nature +8, Perception +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Laboratory Adaptation.*** The pathogenist is immune to disease.  
>
>***Mutated Strains.*** The pathogenist's attacks and effects ignore the target's *Resistance* or Immunity to Poison damage and Immunity to the Poisoned condition and disease.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The pathogenist makes two Poison Needle attacks.  
>
>***Poison Needle.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 35 (10d6) Poison damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, it is stabilized and has the Poisoned condition for 1 hour, even if it regains Hit Points. While Poisoned, the target has the Paralyzed condition.  
>
>***Pestilent Infection (Recharge 5–6).*** The pathogenist infects one creature it can see within 30 feet with a magical disease. At the start of each of its turns, the target is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage, and the target gaines one Exhaustion level. Third Success: The disease ends.  
>
>***Plague Wind (1/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 22 (5d8) Poison damage plus 23 (5d8) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* If the target has 25 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it dies and its flesh turns to ash, leaving a weathered skeleton.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Heightened Tolerance.*** The pathogenist ends the Charmed or Frightened condition on itself.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Outbreak.*** *Trigger:* A creature the pathogenist can see that is infected by its Pestilent Infection is reduced to 0 Hit Points or dies. *Response:* The pathogenist spreads the disease to another creature it can see within 30 feet of the infected creature.
