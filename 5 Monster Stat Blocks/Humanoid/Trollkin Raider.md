>## Trollkin Raider
>*Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|9 (-1)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Con +4
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Intimidation +2, Survival +3
>- **Gear** *Leather Armor*, *Longbow*, *Shield*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The trollkin regains 5 hp at the start of its turn. If the trollkin takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the trollkin's next turn. The trollkin dies only if it starts its turn with 0 hp and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The trollkin makes two attacks, using Spear and Longbow in any combination.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3 to hit, range 150/600 feet., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aim.*** The raider has Advantage on the next ranged attack roll it makes during the current turn.
