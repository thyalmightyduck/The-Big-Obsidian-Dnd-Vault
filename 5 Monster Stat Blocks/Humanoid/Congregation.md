___
>## Congregation
>*Gargantuan swarm of Medium Humanoids*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 170 (20d12 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|7 (-2)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Dispersal.*** When the congregation is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it leaves behind 2d4 Pilgrims in its place.  
>
>***Mob.*** The congregation can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the congregation can move through any opening large enough for a Medium Humanoid. The congregation can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The congregation makes one attack against each creature in its space.  
>
>***Torches.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* The target has the Burning condition (4d6 Fire).  
>
>***Pitchforks.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 28 (8d6) Piercing damage, or 14 (4d6) Piercing damage if the congregation has the Bloodied condition. The target has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Trample.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature in the congregation's space that has the Prone condition. *Failure:* 17 (2d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. *Success:* Half damage.
