___
>## Silver Order Paladin
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +3, Persuasion +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Damage from Spells
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** three *Javelins*, *Longsword*, *Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common and one other language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Holy Weapons.*** When the paladin hits with any weapon, the weapon deals an extra 9 (2d8) Radiant damage (included in the attack).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The paladin makes two attacks.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Force damage, or 9 (1d10 + 4) Force damage if wielded with two hands, plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Javelin.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Force damage plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Lay on Hands (3/Day).*** The paladin touches another creature. The target regains 11 (2d8 + 2) Hit Points and is cured of one disease or the Poisoned condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Deny the Witch (3/Day).*** *Trigger:* A creature the paladin can see within 60 feet casts a spell. *Response:* If the spell is level 3 or lower, the spell fails, but no resources are wasted.
