>## Vulkoori Venom Priest
>*Medium Humanoid (Elf), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +3, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Religion +2, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Gear** *Holy Symbol*, *Quarterstaff*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish, Giant
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fey Ancestry.*** Magic can't put the priest to sleep.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spellcasting.*** The priest casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Dancing Lights*, *Ray of Sickness*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>1/day each: *Cure Wounds*, *Hold Person*, *Protection from Poison*  
>
>***Venom Staff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the priest's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Intensify Poison.*** Each creature with the Poisoned condition within a 30-foot Emanation originating from the priest takes 3 (1d6) Poison damage.
