___
>## Eldritch Trickster
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 68 (15d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, History +5
>- **Gear** *Psychic Blade*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The trickster makes two Psychic Blade attacks.  
>
>***Psychic Blade.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 17 (4d6 + 3) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The trickster casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Disguise Self*, *Invisibility*, *Major image*, *Mirror Image*, *Sleep*, *Suggestion*  
>1/day each: *Dominate Person*, *Gaseous Form*, *Hypnotic Pattern*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The trickster takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
