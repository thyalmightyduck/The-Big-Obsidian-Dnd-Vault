___
>## Frost-Afflicted
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Intimidation +6, Survival +5
>- **Immunities** Cold; Petrified
>- **Gear** *Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Burning Ice.*** A creature with Resistance to Cold damage doesn't have Resistance to the Cold damage dealt by the frost-afflicted. A creature with Immunity to Cold damage is unaffected by this trait.  
>
>***Iced Skin.*** A creature that hits the frost-afflicted with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 4 (1d8) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The frost-afflicted makes two attacks, using Frigid Punch or Frost Bolt in any combination.  
>
>***Frigid Punch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 5 (2d4) Cold damage.  
>
>***Frost Bolt.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d10 + 2) Cold damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Frigid Flare (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12 Each creature within 5 feet of the frost-afflicted. *Failure:* The target takes 4 (1d8) Cold damage, and its speed is reduced by 10 until the end of the frost-afflicted's next turn.
