___
>## Caithas
>*Medium Humanoid (Tiefling, Artillery); Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20 (*studded leather*)
>- **Hit Points** 104 (16d8 + 32)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|26 (+8)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|26 (+8)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +11, Con +5, Wis +11
>- **Skills** Perception +11, Stealth +11, Survival +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Fire, Necrotic
>- **Gear** *Scimitar*
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic, Infernal
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Forest Defense.*** When Caithas is in forest terrain and is targeted by a ranged attack by an attacker he can see, the attack has disadvantage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Caithas makes three Scimitar or three Scaled Longbow attacks.  
>
>***Scimitar.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d6 + 8) slashing damage.  
>
>***Scaled Longbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +11 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (1d8 + 8) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. If the target is a creature who hasn't attacked Caithas since the end of his last turn, he has advantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Manifestation (Recharge 5–6).*** Caithas calls on his connection with Qazyldrath, and the dragon manifests in Caithas's space and exhales dark energy in a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) acid damage plus 18 (4d8) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Additionally, that area is filled with magical darkness for 1 minute. A creature with darkvision can't see through this darkness, and supernatural light can't illuminate it. The manifestation of Qazyldrath then disappears.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Gaze of the Decay (1/Day).*** Caithas chooses a creature he can see within 60 feet of him, magically marking them for 24 hours. While Caithas and the marked creature are on the same plane of existence, he always knows the distance and direction to them. Additionally, a marked creature can't hide from him, and if the marked creature becomes invisible, they gain no benefit from that condition against Caithas.
