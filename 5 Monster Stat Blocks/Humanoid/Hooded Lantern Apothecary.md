>## Hooded Lantern Apothecary
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Medicine +4, Nature +5
>- **Gear** *Hand Crossbow*, *Leather Armor*, *Quiver*, *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Hand Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Medical Treatment (3/Day).*** The apothecary touches another creature. The target regains 20 (4d8 + 2) Hit Points, and the apothecary ends one disease or condition affecting the target, choosing from the Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, and Poisoned conditions.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The apothecary casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12, +4 to spell attacks):  
>
>1/day each: *Aid*, *Lesser Restoration*, *Protection from Poison*, *Purge Contamination*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Adrenaline Boost.*** One ally within 5 feet of the apothecary can use its Reaction to move up to its Speed and make an attack.
