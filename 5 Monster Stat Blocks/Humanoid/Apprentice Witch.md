___
>## Apprentice Witch
>*Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +2, Deception +5, Nature +2, Perception +3
>- **Gear** *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The witch makes two attacks, using Dagger or Eldritch Burst in any combination.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Eldritch Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., or range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Force damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The witch casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Barkskin* (included in AC), *Druidcraft*, *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*  
>2/day: *Bane*, *Hex*  
>1/day: *Augury*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (1/day).*** The witch casts *Misty Step*.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Reflexive Hex.*** *Trigger:* A creature the witch can see within 60 feet makes an ability check, attack roll or saving throw. *Response:* The target must roll a d4 and subtract the number rolled from the triggering roll.
