>## Abree Simoni
>*Medium Humanoid (Human), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|9 (-1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Cha +4
>- **Skills** Insight +1, Intimidation +4, Investigation +3, Perception +1
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Holy Symbol*, *Longsword*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Castinellan plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Smite.*** When Abree hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, she can expend a spell from her Spellcasting action. The spell has no effect, and the attack deals an extra 9 (2d8) Radiant damage for a 2/Day spell or 18 (4d8) Radiant damage for a 1/Day spell.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Abree makes two attacks, using Dagger or Vile Spit in any combination.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Abree casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>2/day each: *Command*, *Cure Wounds*  
>1/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *Find Steed*, *Knock*  
>
>***Vile Spit.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature Abree can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* 2 (1d4) Poison damage and the target Blinded until the end of its next turn.   
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hunter's Mark (2/Day).*** Abree casts *Hunter's Mark* using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Inquisitor's Eye (Recharges after a Long Rest).*** Abree grants herself Advantage on Intelligence (Investigation), Wisdom (Insight), and Wisdom (Perception) checks for 10 minutes.
