>## Lizardfolk Scaletooth
>*Medium Humanoid (Lizardfolk, Brute); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +4, Survival +2
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The scaletooth can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The scaletooth makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, the scaletooth can move them 5 feet in any direction or grapple them (escape DC 12). Until this grapple ends, the scaletooth can't make a Tail attack against another target.  
>
>***Rending Bite.*** The scaletooth makes a Bite attack with advantage against one creature they have grappled, dealing an extra 3 (1d6) piercing damage on a hit.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Reptilian Escape (1/Day).*** If the scaletooth fails a Strength or Dexterity saving throw, they lose their tail and succeed instead. They regrow their tail when they finish a long rest.
