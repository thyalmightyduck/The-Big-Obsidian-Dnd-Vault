___
>## Warlord
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 112 (15d8 + 45)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Dex +5, Con +6, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Intimidation +6, Perception +5
>- **Gear** *Battleaxe*, *Chain Mail*, *Longbow*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The warlord makes three attacks, using Battleaxe or Longbow in any combination.  
>
>***Battleaxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Command.*** The warlord orders one ally within 60 feet that can see and hear the warlord. The ally can immediately use its Reaction to make a melee or ranged weapon attack against a target the warlord chooses.  
>
>***Intimidate.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14 One creature the warlord can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target has Disadvantage on attack rolls until the start of the warlord's next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Reflexive Strike.*** *Trigger:* The warlord is hit by an attack roll. *Response:* The warlord can make one Battle Axe attack against the attacker. On a hit, the attacker must succeed on a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check or have the Prone condition.
