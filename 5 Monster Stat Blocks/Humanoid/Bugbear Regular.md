___
>## Bugbear Regular
>*Medium Humanoid (Bugbear, Minion); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (*leather armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 8
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Goblin
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 40; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Bodyblock.*** When a Medium or smaller enemy is within 5 feet of three or more regulars who aren't prone or incapacitated, that enemy can't see beyond a range of 15 feet.  
>
>***Bugbear's Encouragement.*** Allied Humanoids within 5 feet of the regular who can see or hear them gain a cumulative +1 bonus to Wisdom saving throws.  
>
>***Minion.*** If the regular takes damage from an attack or as the result of a failed saving throw, their hit points are reduced to 0. If the regular takes damage from another effect, they die if the damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum; otherwise they take no damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bullyclub (Group Attack).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1 bludgeoning damage.
