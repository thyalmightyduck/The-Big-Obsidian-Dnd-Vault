>## Athmia Valkys
>*Medium Humanoid (Half-Elf, Skirmisher); Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20 (*studded leather*)
>- **Hit Points** 117 (18d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|26 (+8)|14 (+2)|26 (+8)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +11, Int +11, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Insight +4, Perception +7, Stealth +14
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Necrotic
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic, Elvish
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Charm Resistant.*** Athmia has advantage on saving throws she makes to avoid or end the charmed condition on herself.  
>
>***Evasion.*** If Athmia is subjected to an effect that allows her to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, she instead takes no damage if she succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if she fails.  
>
>***Mental Assault (1/Turn).*** When Athmia hits a creature with her Soul Blade attack, she can assault their mind. The target must succeed on a DC 19 Intelligence saving throw or expend one spell slot of the highest level available with no effect. A creature who fails this saving throw but can't expend a spell slot must instead spend 3 of their Hit Dice with no effect, or if they can't, take an extra 10 (3d6) psychic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Athmia makes two Soul Blade attacks.  
>
>***Soul Blade.*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +11 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 25 (5d6 + 8) psychic damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of Athmia until the end of her next turn.  
>
>***Psychic Frenzy (1/Day).*** Athmia clouds the minds of her foes.  
>Each enemy within 60 feet of her must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or be dazed until the start of her next turn. Athmia gains 5 temporary hit points for each creature who fails this save.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** Athmia takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.  
>
>***Sun Eater's Gift (3/Day).*** While within 30 feet of Aronar, Athmia swaps places with him if he is willing.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sun Eater's Mind (3/Day).*** When a creature Athmia can see within 60 feet of her makes a spell attack against her or casts a spell that requires her to make a saving throw, she can pierce their mind, and the next attack she makes against that creature is automatically a critical hit.
