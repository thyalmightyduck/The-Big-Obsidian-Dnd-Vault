___
>## Celestial Celebrant
>*Medium Humanoid (Celestial), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Religion +2
>- **Gear** *Chain Mail*, *Mace*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Celestial and one other language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Mace.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 13 (3d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The celestial celebrant casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *Sacred Flame*, *Spare the Dying*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Bless*, *Calm Emotions*, *Cure Wounds* (level 2 version), *Lesser Restoration*, *Sanctuary*, *Silence*  
>1/day each: *Beacon of Hope*, *Death Ward*, *Guardian of Faith*, *Revivify*, *Spirit Guardians*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Celestial Magic (3/Day).*** The celestial celebrant casts *Healing Word* (level 2 version) or *Spiritual Weapon*, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Succor (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Trigger:* The celestial celebrant or a creature it can see within 60 feet takes damage. *Response:* The celebrant casts *Cure Wounds* on the triggering creature.
