>## Monastery of the Distressed Body Monk
>*Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Non-Good Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12 (*leather armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 9 (2d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +2, Religion +2
>- **Gear** *Scimitar*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Any one language (usually Common)
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Dark Devotion.*** The monk has advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.  
>
>### Actions
>***Hooked Polearm.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape DC 10). Until this grapple ends, the monk cannot use its polearm on another target.
