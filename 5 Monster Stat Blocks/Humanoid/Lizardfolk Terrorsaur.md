___
>## Lizardfolk Terrorsaur
>*Medium Humanoid (Lizardfolk, Skirmisher); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (natural armor; 16 while flying)
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 50 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +3, Stealth +5
>- **Gear** *Glaive*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Aerial Agility.*** While the terrorsaur is flying, their AC is 16.  
>
>***Amphibious.*** The terrorsaur can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Running Start.*** The terrorsaur can use only their flying speed if they first either fall 10 feet without hitting the ground or walk 10 feet in a straight line on that turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The terrorsaur makes one Bite attack and one Glaive attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.  
>
>***Glaive.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage.  
>
>***Blowgun (Recharge 6).*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 1 piercing damage plus 17 (5d6) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn).  
>
>### Reactions
>***Reptilian Escape (1/Day).*** If the terrorsaur fails a Strength or Dexterity saving throw, they lose their tail and succeed instead. They regrow their tail when they finish a long rest.  
>
>***Skulking Retreat.*** If the terrorsaur takes damage from a melee attack, they can move up to half their speed.
