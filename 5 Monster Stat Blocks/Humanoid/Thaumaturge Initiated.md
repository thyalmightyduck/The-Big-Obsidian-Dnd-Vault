___
>## Thaumaturge Initiated
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 81 (18d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +7, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Deception +3, Perception +4, Stealth +5
>- **Gear** *Quarterstaff*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 150 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** One regional language plus three other languages
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Disguise Other.*** When the initiated casts *Disguise Self*, it can choose to have it affect up to 3 additional creatures (including Hostiles) that it can see within 60 feet. It can choose the same or different disguises for each affected creature. When cast in this manner, the effect lasts for 1 minute or until the initiated chooses to dismiss it from that creature.  
>
>***Personalized Arcane Burst.*** Each initiated is specialized in one pair of damage types from the following list:  
>- Acid and Poison
>- Bludgeoning and Necrotic
>- Cold and Fire
>
>When the initiated uses the Arcane Burst action, it can choose to change the attack's damage type from Force to one of the two selected damage types.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The initiated makes three Arcane Burst attacks.  
>
>***Arcane Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Force damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The initiated casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Darkvision* (included in Senses), *Disguise Self*, *Mage Armor* (included in AC)  
>2/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Lightning Bolt*  
>1/day each: *Greater Invisibility*, *Hypnotic Pattern*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Malignant Transposition (3/Day).*** The initiated creates clouds in a 5-ft radius Sphere in its space and in up to three additional spaces it can see within 60 feet. The Spheres are Heavily Obscured except to the initiated. The Spheres last until the start of the initiate's next turn, or they can be dispersed by a strong wind.  
>When the clouds appear, the initiated can target one creature in one cloud with the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The creature swaps places with the initiated. The initiated can also teleport themselves to an unoccupied space within one of the other clouds.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Arcane Reflection (3/Day).*** The initiated casts *Shield* in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting. If the triggering attack misses and it is a spell or Magical Effect, the initiated can immediately end the *Shield* spell to have the triggering attack target another creature within range of the spell's originator.
