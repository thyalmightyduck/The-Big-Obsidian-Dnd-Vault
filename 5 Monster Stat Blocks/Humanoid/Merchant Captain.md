___
>## Merchant Captain
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Cha +7
>- **Skills** Insight +4, Perception +4, Persuasion +7
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, plus three other languages
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Trader's Eye.*** The merchant has Advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks to determine if goods are of poor quality or a forgery, and it has Advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks to recognize when a creature is attempting to sell it poor quality or forged goods.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The merchant captain makes four attacks, using Shortsword or Quip in any combination.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Quip.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The merchant captain casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Comprehend Languages*, *Mage Hand*, *Mending*  
>3/day each: *Calm Emotions*, *Enthrall*, *Heroism*  
>1/day each: *Confusion*, *Freedom of Movement*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Fast Talk.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15 A creature the merchant captain can see within 30 feet of it. *Failure:* The target has Disadvantage on the next attack roll or save it makes before the start of the merchant captain's next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Banter (4/day).*** *Trigger:* A creature within 30 feet of the merchant captain fails an attack roll, ability check or saving throw. *Response:* The creature rerolls the triggering roll and must use the new result.
