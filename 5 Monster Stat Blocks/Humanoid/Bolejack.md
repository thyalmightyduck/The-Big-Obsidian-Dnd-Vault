___
>## Bolejack
>*Medium Humanoid (Any Species), Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 32 (5d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Intimidation +4, Nature +2, Survival +3
>- **Gear** *Battleaxe*, *Handaxe*, *Hide Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** One national language, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Dryad Protection.*** The bolejack has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects that don't come from an allied dryad.  
>
>***Dryad's Stride.*** Moving through nonmagical Difficult Terrain, as well as magical Difficult Terrain created by an allied dryad, costs the bolejack no extra movement.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The bolejack makes one Battleaxe attack and one Handaxe attack.  
>
>***Battleaxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Force damage.  
>
>***Handaxe.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Force damage.  
>
>***Axe Sweep (Recharge 5–6).*** The bolejack makes a Battleaxe attack against two adjacent creatures within its reach, using the same attack roll for both targets. A Medium or smaller creature hit by either attack has the Prone condition.
