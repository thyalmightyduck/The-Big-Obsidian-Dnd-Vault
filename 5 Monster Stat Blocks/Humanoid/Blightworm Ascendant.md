>## Blightworm Ascendant
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Evil Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 104 (22d8 + 44)
>- **Speed** 35 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +8, Perception +5
>- **Gear** *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Blightworm Rebirth.*** When the blightworm ascendant dies, a **Blightworm** emerges in a space within 5 feet of the ascendant's corpse at the start of its next turn. (The blightworm is part of the ascendant's Challenge Rating.)  
>
>***Blightworm Whispers.*** The blightworm ascendant has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Stunned, and Unconscious conditions.  
>
>***Spell Siphon (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** When the blightworm ascendant successfully interrupts a spell using its *Counterspell* Reaction, it doesn't expend the Reaction and copies the spell. Until the ascendant uses this trait again, it can cast the copied spell using its Spellcasting action, provided the spell is level 5 or lower.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack..*** The blightworm warlock makes two Eldritch Burst attacks.  
>
>***Eldritch Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 26 (4d10 + 4) Force damage, and the blightworm ascendant can push the target up to 10 feet away from the ascendant in a straight line.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The blightworm warlock casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Disguise Self*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*  
>3/rest: *Consume Mind*, *Contact Other Plane*, *Creeping Touch*, *Dimension Door*, *Finger of Death*, *Shatter*, *Suggestion*, *Vampiric Touch*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Serpent Tongue (1/Short Rest).*** The blightworm warlock casts *Serpent Tongue* (level 5 version), using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>1/rest: *Serpent Tongue*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell (2/Short Rest).*** The blightworm warlock casts *Counterspell* (level 5 version) in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>2/rest: *Counterspell*
