___
>## Inquisitor of the Sword
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 120 (16d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +5, Wis +7, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Insight +7, Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Silvered Longsword*
>- **Senses** Truesight 30 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Metabolic Control.*** At the start of each of its turns, if the inquisitor has at least 1 Hit Point, it regains 10 Hit Points and can end one condition on itself.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The inquisitor makes three Silvered Longsword attacks.  
>
>***Silvered Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Psychic damage. Critical *Hit:* If the target is a shape-shifted creature, it takes an additional 4 (1d8) Slashing damage. *Hit or Miss:* The inquisitor can teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The inquisitor casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Dispel Magic*, *Mage Hand* (the hand is Invisible), *Sending*  
>1/day each: *Dimension Door*, *Fly*, *Greater Invisibility*
