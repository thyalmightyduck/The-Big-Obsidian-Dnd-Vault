>## Keppmir
>*Medium Humanoid (Keppmir), Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 11 (2d8 + 2)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +3, Perception +2, Stealth +2
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Gear** *Spear*, *Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Valikan
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Heated Body.*** A creature other than a keppmir that ends its turn grappling or Grappled by the keppmir takes 1 fire damage.  
>
>***Hold Breath.*** The keppmir can hold its breath for 30 minutes.  
>
>***Water Breathing.*** The keppmir can breathe only underwater.  
>
>### Actions
>***Gore.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Piercing damage, or 5 (1d8 + 1) Piercing damage if the spear is wielded in two hands.
