>## Kobold Tiro
>*Small Humanoid (Kobold, Minion); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12 (*shield*)
>- **Hit Points** 4
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|8 (-1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 5; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Minion.*** If the tiro takes damage from an attack or as the result of a failed saving throw, their hit points are reduced to 0. If the tiro takes damage from another effect, they die if the damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum; otherwise they take no damage.  
>
>***Shield? Shield!.*** The tiro gains a +1 bonus to AC while within 5 feet of at least one ally who also has this trait, is wielding a shield, and isn't incapacitated or prone. To benefit from this trait, the tiro must be wielding a shield.  
>
>***Testudo.*** When the tiro finishes a long rest, they choose a non-minion creature to be their commander. While a tiro who isn't incapacitated or prone is within 5 feet of their commander, the commander gains a cumulative +1 bonus to AC (maximum bonus of +5).  
>
>### Actions
>***Pugio (Group Attack).*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1 piercing damage.
