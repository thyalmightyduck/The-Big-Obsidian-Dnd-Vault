___
>## Fractured Noble
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 93 (11d8 + 44)
>- **Speed** 30 ft. (40 ft. in fractured rage)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|19 (+4)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Deception +5, Intimidation +5
>- **Gear** *Chain Shirt*, *Longsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Instinctive.*** The fractured noble has Advantage on Initiative rolls. Unless it has the Incapacitated condition, the fractured noble makes Initiative rolls normally even when surprised, but it must enter Fractured Rage on its first turn.  
>
>***Brutal Attacks (in Fractured Rage Only).*** When the fractured noble scores a Critical Hit with a melee weapon attack, it deals an additional die of damage.  
>
>***Reckless (in Fractured Rage Only).*** At the start of its turn, the fractured noble can gain Advantage on melee weapon attacks until the start of its next turn, but attack rolls against it have Advantage for the same duration.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The fractured noble makes two Longsword attacks. In Fractured Rage, it makes three melee attacks instead.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Fractured Rage (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The fractured noble becomes enraged, swelling in size and distorting facial features to an unrecognizable state. This transformation lasts for 1 hour or until the socialite has the Incapacitated condition. The noble gains 30 Temporary Hit Points, which last until the transformation end. While enraged, the socialite can roll 2d4 and add the result to each Strength-based D20 Test and damage roll it makes.
