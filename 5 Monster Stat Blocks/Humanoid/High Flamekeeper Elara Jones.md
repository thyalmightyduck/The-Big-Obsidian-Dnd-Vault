>*Medium Humanoid (Human), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (*chain shirt*)
>- **Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +5, Religion +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Radiant
>- **Gear** *Spear*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Any two
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Spellcasting.*** Elara casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as their spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *guidance*, *light*, *spare the dying*, *thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *bless*, *healing word*, *lesser restoration*  
>1/day each: *aid*, *dispel magic*, *spirit guardians*  
>
>### Actions
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +2 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) piercing damage, or 4 (1d8) damage when used in two hands to make a melee attack.  
>
>***Searing Light.*** Elara speaks a prayer as they project a radiant beam at one creature it can see within 30 feet of it. The target must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the target suffers 14 (4d6) radiant damage and is blinded until the start of Elara's next turn. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage and is not blinded.  
>
>***Radiant Flames.*** Elara channels divine power into a burst of holy fire. Creatures of Elara's choice within 30 feet of it must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed saving throw, each target takes 16 (3d10) radiant damage, or half as much on a successful save. Magical darkness in the area is dispelled.
