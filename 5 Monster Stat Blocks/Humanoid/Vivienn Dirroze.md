___
>## Vivienn Dirroze
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Brute); Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (*plate*)
>- **Hit Points** 152 (16d8 + 80)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|26 (+8)|11 (+0)|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|24 (+7)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +11, Con +8, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +3, Athletics +11, Deception +10, Intimidation +10
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Necrotic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing during draconic rage
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Exploit Opening (3/Day).*** When Vivienn makes an attack, she has advantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Reckless.*** At the start of her turn, Vivienn can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls made during her turn, but attack rolls made against her have advantage until the start of her next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Vivienn makes three Godslayer Axe attacks.  
>
>***Godslayer Axe.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (1d12 + 8) slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) necrotic damage. If the target is a Celestial, they take an extra 18 (4d8) necrotic damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Draconic Rage (3/Day).*** Vivienn roars with the might of dragons, entering a rage that lasts for 1 minute or until she is incapacitated. While raging, she gains the following benefits:  
>- Her size becomes Large.
>- She has a +2 bonus to weapon damage rolls.
>- She has advantage on Strength ability checks and saving throws.
>- She has resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
>
>***Ravenous Bite (Draconic Rage Only).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (1d12 + 8) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or be frightened of Vivienn for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn).
