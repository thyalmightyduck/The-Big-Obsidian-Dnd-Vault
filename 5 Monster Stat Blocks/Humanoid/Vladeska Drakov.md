___
>## Vladeska Drakov
>*Medium Humanoid (Human), Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 170 (20d8 + 80)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Perception +5
>- **Immunities** Frightened
>- **Gear** *Pike*, six *Spears*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Giant, Orc
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400, or 10,000 in lair; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Darklord Restoration.*** If Vladeska dies, she revives with all her Hit Points in 1d10 weeks somewhere in Falkovnia.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Domain).*** If Vladeska fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Vladeska makes two Pike attacks. She can replace one attack with an Impaling Spear attack.  
>
>***Impaling Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target is impaled on Vladeska's spear. While impaled, the creature has the Restrained condition and takes 3 (1d6) Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. The target or a creature within 5 feet of it can take an action to remove it from the spear.  
>
>***Pike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Artillery Fire (1/Day).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 20-foot-radius, 60-foot-high Cylinder centered on a point Vladeska can see within 300 feet. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Bludgeoning damage plus 13 (3d8) Fire damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Redirect Attack.*** *Trigger:* A creature Vladeska can see makes an attack roll against her. *Response:* Vladeska chooses a Small or Medium ally within 5 feet of herself. She and that ally swap places, and the ally becomes the target of the attack instead.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Vladeska can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Vladeska regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Advance.** Vladeska moves up to half her Speed and makes one Pike attack.
>- **Dreadful Impaling.** Vladeska makes an Impaling Spear attack. If the attack hits and the target is a creature, each ally of the target within 30 feet is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. *Failure or Success:* Vladeska can't take this action again until the start of her next turn.
>- **Order Ballista.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature Vladeska can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 14 (4d6 + 3) Piercing damage, and the target's Speed is halved until the end of its next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>Falkovnia is altered by Drakov's presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Authoritarian Regime.** Drakov's allies have Advantage on Wisdom saving throws.
>- **Closing the Borders.** Drakov can't close or open the borders of her domain. Falkovnia's borders are usually closed, but they open one week every month starting on the night of the new moon.
>- **Endless War.** Whenever a creature other than Drakov or her allies finishes a Long Rest in the domain, roll 1d20. On a 1, the creature doesn't remove any Exhaustion levels as a result of finishing that rest, and the creature gains 1 Exhaustion level.
>
>If Drakov dies, these effects end immediately.
