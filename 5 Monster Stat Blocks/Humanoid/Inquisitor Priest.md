___
>## Inquisitor Priest
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +5, Investigation +3, Perception +5, Religion +3
>- **Gear** *Ring Mail*, *Shield*, *Warhammer*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Ward.*** When the inquisitor priest casts a spell that causes creatures to regain Hit Points, the priest can choose one of the spell's targets and end the effects of one spell affecting it. The effect must be from a spell of equal or lower level to the priest's healing spell.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spellcasting.*** The inquisitor priest casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Light*, *Sacred Flame*, *Spare the Dying*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Cure Wounds*, *Guiding Bolt*, *Protection from Evil and Good*  
>1/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Remove Curse*  
>
>***Warhammer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) Bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) Force damage, or 9 (2d8) Force damage if the target is maintaining Concentration on a spell.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Spiritual Weapon (1/Day).*** The inquisitor priest casts the *Spiritual Weapon* spell, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
