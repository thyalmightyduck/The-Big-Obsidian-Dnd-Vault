>## Draven Malas
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Controller); Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20 (*studded leather*)
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d8 + 56)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|26 (+8)|18 (+4)|26 (+8)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +11, Con +7, Int +11
>- **Skills** Arcana +11, Investigation +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Necrotic
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Exploit Opening (3/Day).*** When Draven makes an attack, he has advantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Profane Sacrifice (1/Turn).*** When Draven hits a creature with a melee attack, he can sacrifice his vitality to empower the attack. The target's speed drops to 0 until the start of his next turn, and Draven takes 5 (1d10) psychic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting (Utility).*** In addition to any other spells in this stat block, Draven can cast the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19):  
>
>At will: *alarm* +, *mage hand* A  
>1/day each: *glyph of warding* +, *scrying* +  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Draven makes three Gloom Dagger attacks.  
>
>***Gloom Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +11 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (1d4 + 8) piercing damage plus 7 (3d4) necrotic damage, and Draven can move the target up to 10 feet horizontally. If the damage from this attack reduces a creature to 0 hit points, Draven regains a use of Blood of the Dragon.  
>
>***Blood of the Dragon (1/Day).*** Draven boils the blood of up to three creatures he can see within 60 feet of him. Each target takes 33 (6d10) necrotic damage and must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn) and knocked prone.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Creeping Dread.*** Draven chooses a creature he can see within 30 feet of him in an area of dim light or darkness. The creature must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or move their speed in a direction chosen by Draven. A creature with advantage on saving throws against being frightened has advantage on this saving throw, and a creature who can't be frightened automatically succeeds on it.
