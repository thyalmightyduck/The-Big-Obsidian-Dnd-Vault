>## Clockwork Horror
>*Medium Humanoid, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 35 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +6, Con +6
>- **Skills** Athletics +9, Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Force, Lightning, Necrotic, Piercing, Poison, Radiant, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Elvish, Lower Bürach, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Artificial Form.*** The clockwork horror has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition and has Immunity to disease. It does not need to eat, drink, sleep, or breathe.  
>
>***Chain the Beast.*** When the clockwork horror is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it transforms back into **Llanna Deyern**.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The clockwork horror makes a Gore attack and two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Gore.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.
