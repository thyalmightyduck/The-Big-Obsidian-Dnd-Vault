>## Silver Order Squire
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 22 (4d8 + 4)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Gear** *Quiver*, *Shield*, *Shortbow*, *Shortsword*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***For the Flame! (1/Day).*** The squire can take an additional Multiattack action on its turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The squire makes two attacks.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Helping Hand.*** The squire grants a creature within 5 feet of them Valor (1d4) until the end of that creature's next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Look Out!.*** *Trigger:* An ally within 5 feet of the squire is hit by an attack. *Response:* The squire takes the attack's damage instead.
