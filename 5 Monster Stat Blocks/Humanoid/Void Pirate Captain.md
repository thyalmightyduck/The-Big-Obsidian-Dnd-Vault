___
>## Void Pirate Captain
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Int +4, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Perception +8, Sleight of Hand +9, Stealth +9
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***From Another World.*** The void pirate doesn't have the standard Aberrant Traits other creatures in the Space Between Worlds have.  
>
>***Keen Eye, Sharp Wit.*** The void pirate has Advantage on Initiative rolls.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The void pirate makes three attacks.  
>
>***Void Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Force damage.  
>
>***Pirate Pistol.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bark Orders.*** One void pirate squib or quartermaster within 60 feet of the void pirate can use its Reaction to move up to half its Speed or make an attack.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Captain's Command (2/Day).*** *Trigger:* A Friendly creature within 30 feet of the void pirate fails an attack roll. *Response:* If the creature can hear the void pirate, it can reroll the attack roll and use the higher result.
