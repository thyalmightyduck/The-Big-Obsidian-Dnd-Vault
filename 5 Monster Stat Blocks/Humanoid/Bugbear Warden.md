___
>## Bugbear Warden
>*Medium Humanoid (Bugbear, Retainer); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (medium armor)
>- **Hit Points** Eight times their level (number of d10 Hit Dice equal to their level)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +PB, Dex 3+PB, Con +PB, Int +PB, Wis 1+PB, Cha +PB
>- **Skills** Perception +1 plus PB, Stealth +3 plus PB, Survival +1 plus PB
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11 plus PB
>- **Languages** Common, Goblin
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals the mentor's bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals the mentor's bonus
>___
>### Actions
>***Signature Attack (Spiked Bow).*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  3 plus PB to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2d6 plus PB piercing damage. Beginning at 7th level, the warden can make this attack twice, instead of once, when they take the Attack action on their turn.  
>
>***3rd Level: Halting Shot (3/Day).*** As an action, the warden makes a signature attack. On a hit, the target's speed is reduced to 0 until the end of their next turn.  
>
>***5th Level: Presence of Inspiration (3/Day).*** As a bonus action, the warden inspires each ally within 30 feet of them who can hear them, granting them advantage on attack rolls until the start of the warden's next turn.  
>
>***7th Level: Enraged Barrage (1/Day).*** As an action, the warden makes a signature attack. Each ally within 30 feet of them who can see them can use a reaction to make a weapon attack, cast a cantrip with a casting time of 1 action, or manifest a 1st-order power with a manifestation time of 1 action.
