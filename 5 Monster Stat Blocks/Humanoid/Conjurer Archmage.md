___
>## Conjurer Archmage
>*Medium Humanoid (Wizard), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 225 (41d8 + 41)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +15, History +10, Perception +7
>- **Gear** *Crystal*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus five other languages
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The archmage has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The archmage makes four Arcane Burst attacks.  
>
>***Arcane Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. or range 150 ft. *Hit:* 27 (4d10 + 5) Force damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The archmage casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Find Familiar*, *Mage Armor* (included in AC), *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*, *Unseen Servant*  
>2/day: *Dimension Door*  
>1/day each: *Incendiary Cloud*, *Lightning Bolt* (level 8 version), *Plane Shift*, *Teleport*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Benign Transposition (3/Day).*** The archmage teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space that it can see. If it instead chooses a space within range that is occupied by a willing creature, they both teleport, swapping places.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Magic (3/Day).*** The archmage casts *Counterspell* or *Shield* in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
