___
>## Time Raider Armiger
>*Medium Humanoid (Time Raider, Soldier); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16 (*breastplate*)
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Con +4, Int +5
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Athletics +5, Intimidation +2, Perception +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, Kuran'zoi
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Psychic Scar.*** The armiger is immune to any effect that would sense their emotions, read their thoughts, reveal they are lying, or reveal their alignment or location.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The armiger makes two attacks using Blaster, Serrated Saber, or both.  
>
>***Serrated Saber.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, and the target has disadvantage on their next attack roll made before the start of the armiger's next turn.  
>
>***Blaster.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, range 30/90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) radiant damage.  
>
>***Concussive Slam (1st-Order Power).*** The armiger rains down telekinetic force on a creature they can see within 30 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or take 7 (3d4) force damage and be knocked prone.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sickening Sympathy (2nd-Order Power).*** When another creature the armiger can see within 30 feet of them is hit with an attack, the armiger floods the attacker's mind with the nauseous pain of their target. The attacker must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of their next turn.
