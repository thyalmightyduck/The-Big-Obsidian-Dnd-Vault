___
>## Dreamer Snoop
>*Medium Humanoid (Dreamer), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 11 (2d8 + 1)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +3, Investigation +4, Perception +2, Stealth +3
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Dreamspeak, Dwarven, and one other language
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Dreamwalking.*** While asleep, the dreamer snoop can reach out and touch the dreams of others within 1 mile. After finishing a Long Rest during which the snoop slept, the snoop gains one skill or tool proficiency until it finishes another Long Rest.  
>
>***Dreamwalking Snoop.*** When the dreamer snoop uses Dreamwalking, it can choose a creature it has seen before within 1 mile and cast *Detect Thoughts* (spell save DC 12) once on it, acquiring information as if talking to the target. This use of Dreamwalking provides only this benefit.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Psychic damage.
