___
>## Academy Outcast
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +3, Deception +5
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common and one other language
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The outcast makes two Shadow Bolt attacks.  
>
>***Shadow Bolt.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the start of the outcast's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The outcast casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Darkness*, *Disguise Self*, *Suggestion*  
>1/day each: *Bestow Curse*, *Dimension Door*, *Fear*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Web*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Summon Demon (1/Day).*** The outcast magically summons three **Dretches** or one **Quasit**. The summoned creatures appear in an unoccupied space within 60 feet of the outcast, whom they obey. They take their turns immediately after the outcast. The creatures remain for 1 hour, until the outcast dies, or until dismissed by the outcast as a Bonus Action.
