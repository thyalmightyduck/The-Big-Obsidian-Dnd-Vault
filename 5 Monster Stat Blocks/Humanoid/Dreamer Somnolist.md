___
>## Dreamer Somnolist
>*Medium Humanoid (Dreamer), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 27 (5d8 + 5)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +4, Perception +4, Performance +5
>- **Gear** *Shortsword*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Dreamspeak, Dwarven, and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Dreamwalking.*** While asleep, the dreamer somnolist can reach out and touch the dreams of others within 1 mile. After finishing a Long Rest during which the somnolist slept, the somnolist gains one skill or tool proficiency until it finishes another Long Rest.  
>
>***Dreamwalking Shaping.*** When the dreamer somnolist uses Dreamwalking, it can choose a willing creature within 1 mile. The somnolist enters the target's dreams and helps the target according to its desires. The somnolist can cast spells within the dream, but the creature knows the general effect and must willingly fail any saving throw. The target awakens with Heroic Inspiration. This use of Dreamwalking provides only these benefits.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dreamer somnolist makes two attacks, using Shortsword or Scathing Thoughts in any combination.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Scathing Thoughts.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, one target within 60 feet. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Psychic damage, and the target has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The dreamer somnolist casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Disguise Self*, *Message*, *Minor Illusion*  
>2/day each: *Calm Emotions*, *Phantasmal Force*, *Sleep*  
>1/day each: *Major Image*
