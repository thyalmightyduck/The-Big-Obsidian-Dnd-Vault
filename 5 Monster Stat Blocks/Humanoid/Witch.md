___
>## Witch
>*Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 136 (21d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Cha +8
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Deception +8, Nature +4, Perception +6
>- **Gear** *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common plus three other languages
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The witch makes three attacks, using Dagger or Eldritch Burst in any combination.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Eldritch Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., or range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (4d6 + 4) Force damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The witch casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):  
>
>At will: *Bane*, *Barkskin* (included in AC), *Druidcraft*, *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*  
>2/day: *Augury*, *Charm Person*, *Hex*, *Invisibility*  
>1/day: *Blight*, *Polymorph*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Spellcasting.*** The witch casts *Misty Step*.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Retributive Vines.*** *Trigger:* The witch takes 5 or more damage from an attacker it can see. *Response:* Vines grow out of the ground to wrap around the attacker. The target has the Restrained condition until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Reflexive Hex.*** *Trigger:* A creature the witch can see within 60 feet succeeds on a D20 Test. *Response:* The target must roll a d6 and subtract the number rolled from the triggering roll.
