___
>## Missionary
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 66 (12d8 + 11)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +5, Persuasion +3, Religion +4
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Quarterstaff*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Unshakeable Faith (1/Day).*** When the missionary would fail a saving throw, it can reroll the save with Advantage. It must use the new roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Quarterstaff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Bludgeoning damage, or 5 (1d8 + 1) Bludgeoning damage if wielded with two hands.  
>
>***Sacred Fire.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The missionary casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Bless*, *Dispel Magic*  
>1/day: *Spirit Guardians*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Healing Word (3/Day).*** The missionary casts *Healing Word* using the spellcasting ability from Spellcasting.
