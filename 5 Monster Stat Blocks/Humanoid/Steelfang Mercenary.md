___
>## Steelfang Mercenary
>*Medium Humanoid (Human Shapechanger), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (*hide*)
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +4
>- **Gear** *Battleaxe*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Any two
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The mercenary has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.  
>
>***Steelfang's Curse.*** If bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage reduces the mercenary to 0 hit points, it drops to 1 hit point instead unless the damage was from a silvered weapon.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mercenary makes two battleaxe attacks or two claw attacks, and one bite attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage. If the target is a humanoid, it must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be cursed with werewolf lycanthropy.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage.  
>
>***Battleaxe.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage if used with two hands.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Change Shape.*** The mercenary magically transforms into a wolf, or back into its true form, while retaining their game statistics. This transformation ends if the mercenary is reduced to 0 hit points or uses a bonus action to end it.
