>***Exploit Opening (3/Day).*** When Athenodorus makes an attack, he has advantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Spellcasting (Utility).*** In addition to any other spells in this stat block, Athenodorus can cast the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *light* A, *mending* A  
>1/day each: *divination* A, *glyph of warding* +  
>
>### Actions
>***Star of Iron.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, they are hexed until the end of their next turn. Whenever a creature hexed in this way makes an attack roll or saving throw, they must roll a d4 and subtract the number rolled from the attack roll or saving throw.  
>
>***Iron Ring (2/Day).*** If not in the Cyst, Athenodorus teleports to the Cyst. If in the Cyst, he teleports to a place he has visited.  
>
>***Unholy Fire (2/Day; 3rd-Level Spell).*** Athenodorus calls unholy black flames from the depths at a point he can see within 90 feet of him. Each enemy in a 15-foot-radius, 30-foot-high cylinder centered on that point must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 14 (4d6) fire damage plus 14 (4d6) necrotic damage and is moved up to 15 feet horizontally. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't moved.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Blessing of Ajax (2/Day).*** Athenodorus chooses a creature he can see within 30 feet of him. The target regains 14 (2d8 + 5) hit points and Athenodorus can end one of the following conditions affecting the target: blinded, charmed, dazed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, or stunned.
