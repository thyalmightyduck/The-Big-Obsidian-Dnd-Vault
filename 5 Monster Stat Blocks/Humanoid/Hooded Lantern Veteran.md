>## Hooded Lantern Veteran
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 52 (7d8 + 21)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Perception +6, Stealth +7, Survival +6
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Quiver*, *Shortsword*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The veteran makes two attacks.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the end of the veteran's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The veteran casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12, +4 to spell attacks):  
>
>1/day each: *Cure Wounds*, *Fog Cloud*, *Pass Without Trace*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Quarry.*** The veteran casts *Hunter's Mark*, using the spellcasting ability from Spellcasting.  
>
>***Quick Ascent.*** The veteran moves up to 30 feet in a straight line in any direction, provided it ends its turn adjacent to a solid surface.
