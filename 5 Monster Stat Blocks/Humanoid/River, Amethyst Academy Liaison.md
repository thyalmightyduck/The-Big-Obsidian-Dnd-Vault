___
>## River, Amethyst Academy Liaison
>*Medium Humanoid (Tiefling)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 83 (15d8 + 15)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +4, Int +7, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Arcana +10, Insight +5, Investigation +10, Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common and three other languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Academy Rings (1/Day).*** River can cast a spell from Spellcasting without expending a use of it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Force Missile.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 28 (8d6) Force damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** River casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Find Familiar*, *Light*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *Dimension Door*, *Dispel Magic*, *Identify*, *Invisibility* (level 4 version), *Sending*  
>1/day each: *Scrying*, *Rary's Telepathic Bond*, *Teleportation Circle*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (Recharge 5–6).*** River casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability from Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***River's Contingency.*** *Trigger:* River becomes Bloodied. *Response:* River casts *Mislead* and teleports to an unoccupied space she can see within 30 feet.
