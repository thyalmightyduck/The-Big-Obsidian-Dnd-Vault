___
>## Birdfolk Thug
>*Medium Humanoid (Any Birdfolk), Any Non-Good Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (*leather armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 27 (5d8 + 5)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Intimidation +2
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Heavy Crossbow*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Birdfolk, understands Auran but can't speak it
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Bandit Fighting.*** When the thug hits with a melee weapon attack, it can spend a bonus action to either take the Disengage action or attempt to trip the target of its attack. A creature it attempts to trip must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.  
>
>***Glide.*** When falling at least 10 feet, the thug can spend a reaction to fly up to its speed in one direction as it descends. It lands in an unoccupied space at the end of its movement, and takes no falling damage. The thug can't glide while carrying heavy objects, heavy weapons, or shields (though it can drop any held items as part of its reaction).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The thug makes two melee attacks.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 feet, one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d2 + 2) piercing damage.  
>
>***Heavy Crossbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage.
