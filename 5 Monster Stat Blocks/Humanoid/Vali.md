>## Vali
>*Medium Humanoid (Elf, Ambusher); Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16 (*leather armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 71 (13d8 + 13)
>- **Speed** 35 ft., Climb 35 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +7, Cha +2
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +7, Perception +9, Stealth +9
>- **Gear** *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Cloak and Dagger.*** While Vali is hidden, he doesn't give away his location if he makes a weapon attack and misses.  
>
>***Deft-Footed.*** Vali leaves no tracks or other traces of his presence unless he wants to.  
>
>***Elf Glamour (1/Day).*** When a creature targets Vali with an attack, power, or spell, the creature must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by him until the end of his next turn (no action required).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Vali makes two Shortsword attacks, or he makes two Waning Moon attacks and uses Waning Moon Fade, if available.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) piercing damage.  
>
>***Waning Moon.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d8 + 5) piercing damage. If the target is a creature who can't see Vali, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) force damage.  
>
>***Waning Moon Fade (1/Day).*** Vali becomes invisible for 1 minute and takes the Hide action. This invisibility ends if Vali makes an attack, casts a spell, or manifests a power.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Nimble Escape.*** Vali takes the Disengage or Hide action.
