>## Witch Owl Cultist
>*Medium Humanoid (Any Species), Any Evil Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 18 (4d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +2
>- **Skills** Athletics +2, Deception +3, Perception +2
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** One national language and one other language
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** The witch owl cultist deals an extra 3 (1d6) damage when it hits a target with a weapon attack and has Advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the cultist that doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, and the cultist doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Nimble Escape.*** The witch owl cultist takes the Disengage or Hide action.
