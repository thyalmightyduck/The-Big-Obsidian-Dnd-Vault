___
>## Mist Wanderer
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +3, Insight +5, Medicine +5
>- **Gear** *Orb*, *Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Death Curse.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the wanderer when it dies. *Failure:* The target is cursed. While cursed, the target has the Blinded and Deafened conditions.  
>
>***Mist Walker.*** The wanderer can travel safely through the Mists to reach a specific domain it knows by name. The wanderer still can't bypass domain borders closed by a Darklord's will.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The wanderer makes two Radiant Burst attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Mind Spike* if available.  
>
>***Radiant Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 3) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The wanderer casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *Mage Hand*  
>1/day each: *Augury*, *Detect Magic*, *Mind Spike*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Evil Eye (1/Day).*** The wanderer casts *Hold Person*, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
