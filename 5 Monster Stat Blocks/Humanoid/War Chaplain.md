___
>## War Chaplain
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +4, Cha +2
>- **Skills** Medicine +4, Perception +4, Persuasion +4
>- **Gear** *Holy Symbol*, *Longsword*, *Ring Mail*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The chosen makes two attacks, using Longsword or Radiant Bolt in any combination.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Radiant Bolt.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d10 + 2) Radiant damage.  
>
>***War God's Healing (Recharge 5–6).*** One ally within 30 feet of the chaplain regains 10 (3d6) Hit Points.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Divine Orders.*** The war chaplain commands an ally within 30 feet. The target can use its Reaction to make one attack against a creature the chaplain attacked this round.
