>## Monster Hunter
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +3, Dex +5, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Investigation +4, Nature +4, Survival +6
>- **Gear** *Handaxe*, *Leather Armor*, *Scimitar, Silver*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Faith.*** When the monster hunter rolls a 1 on the d20 of an ability check or saving throw, it can reroll the die, and it must use the new roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The monster hunter makes two attacks, using Handaxe or Silver Knife in any combination.  
>
>***Handaxe.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage, plus 10 (3d6) Slashing damage if the attack roll was made with Advantage.  
>
>***Silver Knife.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage, plus 10 (3d6) Slashing damage if the attack roll was made with Advantage.  
>
>***Wooden Stake.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aim.*** The monster hunter gives itself Advantage on the next attack roll it makes during the current turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Save Ally.*** *Trigger:* An ally within 5 feet has the Grappled condition. *Response:* The ally can immediately make an escape roll with Advantage.
