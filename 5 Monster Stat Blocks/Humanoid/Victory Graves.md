>## Victory Graves
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Skirmisher); Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (*studded leather*)
>- **Hit Points** 88 (16d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|22 (+6)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Int +5
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +9, Athletics +6, Deception +4, Intimidation +4, Stealth +9
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, thieves' cant, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Exploit Opening (3/Day).*** When Victory makes an attack, she has advantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Kinetic Buildup.*** When Victory hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, her speed increases by a cumulative 5 feet and she gains one additional reaction until the start of her next turn.  
>
>***Mind Link.*** Victory can communicate telepathically with any Amethyst Knife boss regardless of distance, provided they are on the same plane of existence.  
>
>***Psionic Wound (3/Day).*** When Victory hits a creature with a Mindrazor attack, she can attempt to inflict a psionic wound (no action required). The target must succeed on a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw or become vulnerable to psychic damage for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Victory makes three Mindrazor attacks and one Cheap Shot attack.  
>
>***Mindrazor.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (1d8 + 7) piercing damage.  
>
>***Cheap Shot.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d4 + 6) bludgeoning damage, and the target must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, Victory can choose for the target to either be knocked prone or gain one of the following conditions until the end of the target's next turn: blinded, dazed, or deafened.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Shared Condition.*** When Victory is blinded, charmed, dazed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, or stunned, she chooses a willing Amethyst Knife boss within 60 feet of her who doesn't already have the condition or is immune to it. The chosen creature then gains the condition for the duration, and Victory loses the condition. She can use this reaction even while incapacitated.
