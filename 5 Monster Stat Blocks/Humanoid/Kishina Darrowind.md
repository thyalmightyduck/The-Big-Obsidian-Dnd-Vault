>## Kishina Darrowind
>*Medium Humanoid (Half-Elf, Skirmisher); Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19 (pathfinder's boots)
>- **Hit Points** 121 (22d8 + 22)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|24 (+7)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|24 (+7)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Dex +10, Wis +10
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +10, Athletics +5, Insight +10, Perception +10
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Charm Resistant.*** Kishina has advantage on saving throws she makes to avoid or end the charmed condition on herself.  
>
>***Light Landing.*** Kishina takes no damage from falling.  
>
>***Standing Leap.*** Kishina's long jump is up to 45 feet and her high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Kishina makes four Unarmed Strike attacks or three Throwing Knife attacks.  
>
>***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (1d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage, or 20 (3d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage on a critical hit.  
>
>***Throwing Knife.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +10 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d4 + 7) piercing damage.  
>
>***Painful Sprint (Recharge 6).*** Kishina moves up to her speed without provoking opportunity attacks, hitting a pressure point on each enemy she can see who she passes within 5 feet of during this move. Each target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throwing or be poisoned until the start of their next turn. A creature who fails this save by 5 or more also falls prone.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Throw.*** Kishina attempts to throw a Large or smaller enemy she can see within 5 feet of her. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or be moved up to 15 feet away from her and be knocked prone.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Shield (3/Day; 1st-Level Spell).*** When Kishina is targeted by an attack or a *magic missile* spell, she conjures an invisible barrier of force. Until the start of her next turn, she gains a +5 bonus to AC, including against the triggering attack, and takes no damage from magic missile.
