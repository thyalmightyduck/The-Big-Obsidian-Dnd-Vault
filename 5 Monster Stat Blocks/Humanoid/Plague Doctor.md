>## Plague Doctor
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Wizard), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Medicine +3
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Healer's Kit*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Any three languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Good Medicine (1/Day).*** The plague doctor carries a concoction that, if consumed as an action, restores 13 (3d8) Hit Points and cures any disease.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Poison damage.  
>
>***Bad Medicine.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 10- foot Sphere centered on a point the plague doctor can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. While Poisoned, the target takes 3 (1d6) Poison damage each time it takes an action. The target can take an action to repeat the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The plague doctor casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Light*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Boil Blood*, *Grease*, *Sleep*  
>1/day each: *Acid Arrow*, *Bestow Curse*, *Gust of Wind*, *Stinking Cloud*
