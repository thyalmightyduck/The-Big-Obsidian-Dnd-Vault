>### Actions
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) Slashing damage.

>### Bonus Actions
>***Battle Draught (1/Day).*** The marauder consumes an unstable chemical compound. For the next minute, the marauder gains one of the following abilities: Resistance to spell damage; Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage; or 2 (1d4) extra damage with weapon attacks.
