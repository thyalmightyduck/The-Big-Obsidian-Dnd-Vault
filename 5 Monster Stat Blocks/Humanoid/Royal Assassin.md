>## Royal Assassin
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 143 (22d8 + 44)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +12 (22)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +8, Deception +8, Perception +9, Sleight of Hand +12, Stealth +12
>- **Gear** *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Death Strike.*** When the assassin reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, the creature immediately dies.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The assassin makes three Poison Weapon attacks.  
>
>***Poisoned Weapon.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) Poison damage. Once per turn, the assassin can apply one of the following additional effects to the target:  
>- **Silent Scream.** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Bewildered condition (save ends). While Bewildered, the target is unable to speak.
>- **Sap Strength.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition (save ends). While Poisoned, the target also has the Weakened condition.
>- **Sluggish Sinews.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Dazed condition (save ends). While Dazed, the target also has the Slowed condition.
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The assassin takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
