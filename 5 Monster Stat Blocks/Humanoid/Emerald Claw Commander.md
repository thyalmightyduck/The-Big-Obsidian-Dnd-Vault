>## Emerald Claw Commander
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +4, Wis +3
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Intimidation +4, Perception +3
>- **Gear** *Flail*, five *Javelins*, *Plate Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, Dwarvish
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Aura of Authority.*** While in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the commander, the commander and its allies have Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws, provided the commander doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The commander makes two attacks, using Flail or Javelin in any combination.  
>
>***Flail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage—plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage if the commander is Bloodied—and the target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of the commander's next turn.  
>
>***Javelin.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage—plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage if the commander is Bloodied.
