>## Knifetooth Warlord
>*Medium Humanoid, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 97 (15d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Intimidation +5, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Shield*, *Studded Leather Armor*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** One national language
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Knifetooth warlord makes three Spear attacks.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Warlord Strike (Recharge 5–6).*** The Knifetooth warlord makes two Spear attacks, then it commands up to three other Knifetooth Horde members of CR 4 or lower to attack. Each affected creature can a Reaction to make an attack against a creature the warlord attacked this turn. The target adds the warlord's proficiency bonus to the damage roll of this attack.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Brutal Inspiration.*** The Knifetooth warlord lets out a battle cry. Knifetooth Horde members who can see or hear the warlord gain Advantage on saving throws until the start of the warlord's next turn.
