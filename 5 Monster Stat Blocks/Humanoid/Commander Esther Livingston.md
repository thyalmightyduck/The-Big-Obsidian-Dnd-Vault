>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hshe, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage if used wsheh two hands.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hshe, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.  
>
>***Heavy Crossbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +3 to hshe, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d10 + 1) piercing damage.
