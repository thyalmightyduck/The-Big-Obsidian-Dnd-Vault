___
>## Yggdrasili Crown
>*Medium Humanoid, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 97 (15d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Perception +5
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Lightning
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aetheric Aura.*** When a creature within 10 feet of the crown takes Psychic damage, it takes an extra 5 (2d4) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Assimilation.*** A creature that drops to 0 Hit Points within 60 feet of the cloud is subjected to the following effect. Assimilation can penetrate barriers, but 2 feet of rock, 2 inches of metal, a thin sheet of lead, or a barrier of force blocks it. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target becomes an **Yggdrasili Drone**. If the target is near multiple yggdrasili, it makes a single saving throw against the highest DC among them.  
>
>***Psychic Static.*** Attempts to read the crown's thoughts fail. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15, a creature that tries to read the crown's thoughts. *Failure:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Psychic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The crown makes two Slam attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Psychic Whip.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Psychic Whip.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature the crown can sense within 120 feet. *Failure:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Mind Bomb (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 20-foot radius Sphere centered on a point the crown can sense within 120 feet. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Psychic damage, the target can't take Reactions, and each time the target makes an ability check or saving throw, it must roll a d4 and subtract the result from its roll. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only.
