___
>## Ogresh Sage
>*Medium Humanoid (Ogresh), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 66 (12d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, History +5, Insight +4, Investigation +5, Perception +4, Persuasion +4
>- **Gear** *Quarterstaff*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Any five languages
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***A Friendly Ear (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** After speaking to a non-Hostile creature for 1 minute, the ogresh sage can subject it to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition for 1 hour. It is unaware of being Charmed even after the effect ends.  
>
>***Powerful Build.*** The ogresh sage counts as one Size larger for the purpose of determining its carrying capacity.  
>
>***Studied Defense.*** The ogresh sage adds its Intelligence modifier to its AC instead of its Dexterity modifier (included above).  
>
>***Takes One to Know One.*** The ogresh sage has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The sage makes two Quarterstaff attacks.  
>
>***Quarterstaff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Bludgeoning damage, or 5 (1d8 + 1) Bludgeoning damage if wielded with two hands.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Analyze Enemy.*** The ogresh sage begins analyzing a creature it can see within 30 feet. If it maintains Concentration for 1 minute, it can add its Intelligence bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls it makes against the target.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sage Words.*** *Trigger:* A creature the ogresh sage can see makes an attack roll or ability check. *Response:* If the target can hear and understand the ogresh sage, it gains Advantage on the roll.
