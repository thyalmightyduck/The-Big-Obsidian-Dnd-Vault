>## Time Raider Vertex
>*Large Humanoid (Time Raider, Support); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (psionic power armor)
>- **Hit Points** 153 (18d10 + 54)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|9 (-1)|16 (+3)|19 (+4)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +6, Int +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +10, Perception +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Common, Kuran'zoi, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Psychic Scar.*** The vertex is immune to any effect that would sense their emotions, read their thoughts, reveal they are lying, or reveal their alignment or location.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vertex makes two Psionic Slam attacks.  
>
>***Psionic Slam.*** *Melee Power Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage, and the next attack roll against the target made before the start of the vertex's next turn has advantage.  
>
>***Split Space (2/Day).*** The vertex chooses a point in a precise location they have seen (in person or otherwise) on any plane of existence. A shimmering 10-foot-diameter portal leading to that location appears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the vertex. Each creature who touches the portal is instantly teleported to the nearest unoccupied space at the chosen location. The portal lasts until the vertex dies, uses this action again, dismisses the portal as an action, or is transported by the portal.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Invigorated March (3rd-Order Power).*** The vertex emits invigorating psionic energy. Each time raider within 60 feet of the vertex can use their reaction to move up to half their speed.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Kinetic Defense Field.*** When another Medium or smaller creature the vertex can see within 120 feet of them takes damage, that damage is reduced by 15.
