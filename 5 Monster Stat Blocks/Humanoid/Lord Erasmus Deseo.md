>## Lord Erasmus Deseo
>*Medium Humanoid (Human, Support); Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (*plate*)
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +5, Wis +2
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, History +4, Intimidation +3
>- **Gear** *Greatsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Exploit Opening (3/Day).*** When Erasmus makes an attack, he has advantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Master Tactician.*** At the start of a combat encounter, Erasmus chooses when he acts in the initiative order after all other creatures have rolled initiative.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Erasmus makes two Greatsword attacks. He can replace one attack with a use of I'm Right Here.  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, they have disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than Erasmus until the end of their next turn.  
>
>***I'm Right Here.*** Erasmus taunts a creature he can see within 5 feet of him who can hear him. The creature must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or use their reaction, if available, to attack Erasmus. If the creature attacks, an ally within 30 feet of Erasmus who isn't incapacitated can make a weapon attack against the creature (no action required).  
>
>***Iron Ring (2/Day).*** If not in the Cyst, Erasmus teleports to the Cyst. If in the Cyst, he teleports to a place he has visited.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Mark.*** Erasmus chooses a creature he can see within 60 feet of him and marks them until the start of his next turn. The first time one of Erasmus's allies deals damage to a marked creature on each turn, the creature takes an extra 3 (1d6) damage.  
>
>***Reposition.*** Erasmus and each ally within 60 feet of him who can hear him moves up to 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
