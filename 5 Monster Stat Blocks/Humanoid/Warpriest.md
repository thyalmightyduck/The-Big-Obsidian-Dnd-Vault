___
>## Warpriest
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 142 (19d8 + 57)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Medicine +7, Perception +7, Religion +3
>- **Immunities** Frightened
>- **Gear** *Chain Mail*, *Holy Symbol*, *Shield*, *Warhammer*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The warpriest makes three attacks, using Blessed Warhammer or Divine Flame in any combination.  
>
>***Blessed Warhammer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 13 (3d8) Radiant or Necrotic damage (warpriest's choice).  
>
>***Divine Flame.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (4d6 + 4) Radiant or Necrotic damage (warpriest's choice)  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The warpriest casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Guidance*, *Light*, *Spare the Dying*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day: *Command*, *Cure Wounds*, *Hold Person*, *Spiritual Weapon*  
>1/day: *Guardian of Faith*, *Protection from Energy*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Divine Aid (3/Day).*** The warpriest casts *Bless*, *Dispel Magic*, *Healing Word*, or *Lesser Restoration*.  
>
>### Reactions
>***War Blessing.*** *Trigger:* A creature the warpriest can see within 30 feet hits with a melee weapon attack. *Response:* The triggering weapon deals an extra 9 (2d8) Radiant or Necrotic damage (warpriest's choice).
