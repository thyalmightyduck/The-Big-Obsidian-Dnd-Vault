___
>## Silver Order Recruit
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Longsword*, *Quiver*, *Shield*, *Shortbow*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***For the Flame! (3/Day).*** The recruit can take an additional Multiattack action on its turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The recruit makes two attacks using its Longsword or Shortbow in any combination.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) Slashing damage if wielded with two hands.  
>
>***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Shield Bash.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* The target is pushed 5 feet away from the recruit and has the Staggered condition (save ends).  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* The recruit is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon. *Response:* The recruit adds 2 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
