___
>## Archmage Penumbra
>*Medium Humanoid (Tiefling), Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17 (*robe of the archmagi*)
>- **Hit Points** 225 (30d8 + 90)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|25 (+7)|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +9, Int +13, Wis +10
>- **Skills** Arcana +13, History +13, Perception +10, Persuasion +9
>- **Damage Resistances** damage from spells
>- **Gear** *Staff of the Magi*
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** All
>- **Challenge** 20 (XP 25,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Special Equipment.*** Each Academy Director wears a *robe of the archmagi* and a *ring of spell storing*, and carries a *staff of the magi*, and a *ring of spell storing*. They carry 2d6 legendary spell scrolls and potions. Bonuses from this equipment are included in the stat block.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Penumbra fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** Penumbra has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Penumbra uses octarine blast twice. They can replace one use of arcane blast with Spellcasting instead.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Penumbra casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as their spellcasting ability (spell save DC 23, +15 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *mage hand*, *minor illusion*, *prestidigitation*, *dispel magic*, *sending*  
>3/day each: *Bigby's hand*, *invisibility*, *detect thoughts*, *project image*, *scrying*, *teleport*, *wall of force*, *telekinesis*  
>1/day each: *time stop*, *forcecage*, *reverse gravity*, *prismatic wall*, *maze*, *mind blank*, *simulacrum*, *clone*, *demiplane*  
>
>***Staff of the Magi.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Octarine Blast.*** Penumbra chooses fire, cold, lightning, acid, or poison damage, and hurls a beam of energy at a creature or object they can see. The creature or object must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw. The target takes 35 (10d6) damage of the chosen type on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Arcane Step.*** Penumbra magically teleports to an unoccupied space within 120 feet of them.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Arcane Denial.*** Penumbra targets one creature it can see within 60 feet of it that is casting a spell. If the spell is 5th level or lower, the spell fails, but any spell slots or charges are not wasted.  
>
>***Ultimate Contingency (1/Day).*** When targeted by an attack or spell, or included in the area of the spell, Penumbra can cast any spell in the Core Rules of 8th level or lower without any components, interrupting (and potentially avoiding) the triggering effect. The spell's normal casting time changes to 1 reaction.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Archmage can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Archmage regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Octarine Blast.** Penumbra uses its octarine blast attack.
>- **Arcane Step.** Penumbra uses its arcane step bonus action.
>- **Cast a Spell (Costs 3 Actions).** Penumbra casts a spell.
