___
>## Celestial Campaigner
>*Medium Humanoid (Celestial), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Intimidation +4, Perception +6, Religion +3
>- **Gear** *Longsword*, *Plate Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Celestial and one other language
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***War Blessing.*** A weapon deals two extra dice of its damage when the celestial campaigner hits with it (included in the attack).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The celestial campaigner makes two Longsword attacks.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) Slashing damage, or 19 (3d10 + 3) Slashing damage if wielded with two hands.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The celestial campaigner casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Guidance*, *Light*, *Sacred Flame*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Bless*, *Divine Favor*, *Guiding Bolt* (level 2 version), *Hold Person*, *Magic Weapon*  
>1/day each: *Daylight*, *Dispel Magic*, *Freedom of Movement*, *Stoneskin*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Celestial Magic (3/Day).*** The celestial celebrant casts *Shield of Faith* or *Spiritual Weapon*, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protection.*** *Trigger:* A creature the celestial campaigner can see within 5 feet makes an attack against a creature other than the campaigner. *Response:* The campaigner imposes Disadvantage on the triggering attack.
