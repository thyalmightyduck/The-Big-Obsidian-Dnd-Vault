>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The villager militia makes two attacks, using Dagger, Spear, or Torch in any combination.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Torch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Fire damage.
