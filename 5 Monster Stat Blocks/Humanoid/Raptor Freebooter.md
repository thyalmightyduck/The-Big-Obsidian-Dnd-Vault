___
>## Raptor Freebooter
>*Small Humanoid (Raptor), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (*leather*)
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d6 + 20)
>- **Speed** 25 ft., Swim 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +2, Perception +5, Persuasion +4
>- **Gear** *Hand Crossbow*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Birdfolk, understands Auran but can't speak it
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Confidence.*** The freebooter adds its Charisma modifier to its initiative rolls.  
>
>***Glide.*** When falling at least 10 feet, the freebooter can spend a reaction to fly up to its speed in one direction as it descends. It lands in an unoccupied space at the end of their movement, and takes no falling damage. The freebooter can't glide while carrying heavy objects, heavy weapons, or shields (though it can drop any held items as part of its reaction).  
>
>***Patient.*** When the freebooter takes a readied action, it has advantage on the first attack roll, skill check, or ability check it makes as a part of that action.  
>
>***Sneak Attack.*** Once per turn, the freebooter deals an extra 3 1d6 damage when it hits a target with a weapon attack and it has advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the freebooter that isn't incapacitated and the freebooter doesn't have disadvantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Talons.*** The freebooter has advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks to climb any surface its talons could reasonably grip.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The freebooter makes two saber attacks and one talons attack or two attacks with its hand crossbow.  
>
>***Saber.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.  
>
>***Talons.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.  
>
>***Hand Crossbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.
