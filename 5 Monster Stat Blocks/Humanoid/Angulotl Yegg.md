___
>## Angulotl Yegg
>*Small Humanoid (Angulotl, Retainer); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (light armor)
>- **Hit Points** Seven times their level (number of d8 Hit Dice equal to their level)
>- **Speed** 25 ft., Climb 20 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +PB, Dex 3+PB, Con +PB, Int +PB, Wis 2+PB, Cha +PB
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +3 plus PB, Athletics +0 plus PB, Stealth +3 plus PB, Survival +2 plus PB
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Angulotl, Common
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals the mentor's bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals the mentor's bonus
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The angulotl yegg can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Signature Attack (Poison Knife).*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  3 plus PB to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d4 plus PB piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage. Beginning at 7th level, the yegg can make this attack twice, instead of once, when they take the Attack action on their turn.  
>
>***3rd Level: Death from Above! (3/Day).*** As a bonus action, the yegg jumps up to their walking speed without provoking opportunity attacks. If they leap over an enemy during this jump, the yegg can make a signature attack with advantage against that enemy. On a hit, this attack deals an extra PB piercing damage.  
>
>***5th Level: Lay Low (3/Day).*** When the yegg hits a creature with a signature attack while hidden, the yegg can use their reaction to remain hidden.  
>
>***7th Level: Excoriating Acid (3/Day).*** As an action, the yegg throws a flask of acid at a point they can see within 20 feet of them. The flask shatters, and each creature within 10 feet of that point must make a DC 10 plus PB Dexterity saving throw, taking PBd10 acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
