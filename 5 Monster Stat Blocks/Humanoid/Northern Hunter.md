>## Northern Hunter
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 171 (18d8 + 90)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +9, Insight +7, Perception +7, Survival +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Frightened
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Longbow*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common, plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Arctic Tracker.*** The Northern hunter has Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track creatures in forest, cold grassland, and glacial terrain. In addition, Difficult Terrain composed primarily of snow or ice doesn't cost the Northern hunter extra movement.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Northern hunter makes four attacks, using Longbow or Spear in any combination. The hunter can choose to deal Force damage instead of the weapon's normal damage type.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage. The target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of the hunter's next turn. Multiple reductions are not cumulative.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage. The target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of the Northern Hunter's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Slayer (3/Day).*** The next attack the Northern hunter makes ignores all Resistances and Immunities to damage.  
>
>***Swift Hunter.*** The Northern hunter moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see.  
>
>### Reactions
>***No Escape.*** *Trigger:* A creature the Northern hunter can see within 40 feet of it moves into a space more than 10 feet away from it. *Response:* *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16 The hunter flings a bola, tangle of rope, or similar object at the creature. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.
