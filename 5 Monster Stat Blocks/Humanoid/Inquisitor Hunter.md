___
>## Inquisitor Hunter
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 19 (3d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Investigation +3, Perception +3, Religion +3, Survival +3
>- **Gear** *Chain Shirt*, *Longbow*, *Quiver*, *Arrows (20)*, *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Inquisitor's Awareness.*** The inquisitor hunter can spend 1 minute maintaining Concentration to learn whether any arcane or innate spellcasters, Aberrations, Fey, and Fiends are within 1 mile. This trait doesn't reveal the creatures' locations or numbers.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The inquisitor hunter makes two Shortsword attacks, provided it has a Shortsword in each hand.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Psychic damage, or 5 (2d4) Psychic damage if the target is maintaining Concentration on a spell.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, ranged 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Psychic damage, or 5 (2d4) Psychic damage if the target is maintaining Concentration on a spell.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The inquisitor hunter casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 11):  
>
>1/day each: *Detect Magic*, *Protection from Evil and Good*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hunter's Mark (1/Day).*** The inquisitor hunter casts *Hunter's Mark* using the same spellcasting ability as in Spellcasting.
