___
>## Silver Order Cavalier
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +4, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Animal Handling +4, Athletics +7, Perception +4, Persuasion +5
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** three *Javelins*, *Lance*, *Shield*, *Splint Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common and one other language
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Holy Weapons.*** When the cavalier hits with any weapon, the weapon deals an extra 9 (2d8) Radiant damage (included in the attack).  
>
>***Skilled Rider.*** While mounted, the cavalier can force an attack that targets its mount to target the cavalier instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The cavalier makes two attacks.  
>
>***Lance.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 (with Disadvantage against a target within 5 feet), reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 10 (1d12 + 4) Force damage plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Javelin.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Force damage plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Summon Steed (1/Day).*** The cavalier magically summons a Griffon, which appears in an unoccupied space within 60 feet of the cavalier and obeys its commands. The steed takes its turn immediately before or after the cavalier. It remains until it or the cavalier dies, or the cavalier dismisses it as a Bonus Action.
