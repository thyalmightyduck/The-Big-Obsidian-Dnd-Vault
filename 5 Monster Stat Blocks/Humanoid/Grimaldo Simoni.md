___
>## Grimaldo Simoni
>*Medium Humanoid (Human), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +5, Wis +3
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Intimidation +1, Investigation +5, Stealth +4
>- **Gear** *Arcane Focus*, *Dagger*, *Spellbook*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Castellan and one other language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Bloody Brilliant.*** Grimaldo has a pool of seven d12s. Whenever he casts a sangromancy spell that calls for the expenditure of a Hit Die, he can spend an equal number of these d12s instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Grimaldo makes two Necrosis attacks.  
>
>***Bejeweled Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Necrosis.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the start of Grimaldo's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Grimaldo casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Message*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Alter Self*, *Burning Hands*, *Hold Person*  
>1/day each: *Dark Sacrament*, *Reanimate*, *Sanguine Poppet*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Blood Magic (4/Day).*** Grimaldo casts *Blood Rush* (level 2 version), *Crimson Lash*, or *Wilting Smite* using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
