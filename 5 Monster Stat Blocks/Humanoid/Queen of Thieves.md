___
>## Queen of Thieves
>*Medium Humanoid (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 23
>- **Hit Points** 83 (15d8 + 15) plus 83 (15d8 + 15) per player character
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|13 (+1)|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +10, Con +6, Int +10, Wis +8, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +10, Arcana +10, Deception +14, Insight +8, Perception +8, Persuasion +9, Sleight of Hand +10, Stealth +15
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Gear** *+3 Rapier*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Common, Dwarvish, Elvish, Undercommon
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Canny Defense.*** While the Queen of Thieves is wearing light or no armor and wielding no shield, her AC includes her Intelligence modifier (included above).  
>
>***Masked Identity.*** The Queen of Thieves is immune to any effect that would sense her emotions or read her thoughts, as well as any divination spell that she refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain her intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Memory Thief.*** Creatures are not aware when the Queen of Thieves reads their thoughts or imposes the Charmed condition on them. Additionally, when a spell cast by the her that causes creatures to become Charmed ends, she can make affected creatures lose their memories. Each creature must succeed on a DC 18 Intelligence saving throw or forget up to 8 hours of the time spent Charmed by her. These memories can be restored with a *Heal* spell or similar magic.  
>
>***Vexing Aura.*** The Queen of Thieves projects a 30-foot Emanation of bewitching magic. At the start of each of her turns, she can choose the aura's effect, which applies to creatures of her choice in the Emanation:  
>- **Enchanting Presence.** A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation has the Charmed condition until it ends its turn outside the Emanation. If she deals damage to the creature, the condition ends, and that creature is immune to her Enchanting Presence for 24 hours.
>- **Mocking Laughter.** A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation takes 14 (4d6) Psychic damage.
>
>### Actions
>***The Queen of Thieves always takes her turn on Initiative count 20.*** On her turn, she can use her movement and interaction as normal. However, she does not take an action or Bonus Action on her turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, she takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***+3 Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +13, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (1d8 + 8) Piercing damage plus 35 (10d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Cutting Words.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 18, one creature within 120 feet of the Queen of Thieves. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Psychic damage, and the target has Disadvantage on the next D20 Test it makes before the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Dash.*** The Queen of Thieves moves up to her Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Misdirection (1/Round).*** The Queen of Thieves becomes Invisible and teleports to an unoccupied space she can see within 60 feet. At the same time, an illusory double of her appears where she was standing. The illusory double, as well as the Invisible condition, end if the Queen of Thieves attacks or casts a spell, or after 1 minute.  
>
>***Resistance Resistance.*** The Queen of Thieves chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting her and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on her. She can use this action even if she would otherwise be unable to take actions, her actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters her game statistics.  
>
>***Spellcasting (1/Round).*** The Queen of Thieves casts one of the following spells, requiring no Verbal or Somatic components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18, +10 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Thoughts*, *Disguise Self*, *Invisibility*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Suggestion*  
>2/day each: *Charm Monster*, *Dimension Door*, *Dominate Person*, *Gaseous Form*, *Greater Invisibility*, *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Major Image* (level 4 version), *Mirror Image*, *Scrying*, *Sending*, *Sleep*  
>1/day each: *Feeblemind*, *Mass Suggestion*, *Project Image*, *Seeming*
