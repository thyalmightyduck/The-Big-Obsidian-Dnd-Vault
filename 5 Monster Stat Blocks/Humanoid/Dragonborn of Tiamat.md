___
>## Dragonborn of Tiamat
>*Medium Humanoid, typically Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (*plate*)
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d8 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +7, Wis +4, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Intimidation +6, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Frightened
>- **Gear** *Greataxe*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (1/Day).*** If the dragonborn fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragonborn makes two Greataxe attacks.  
>
>***Greataxe.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (1d12 + 5) slashing damage plus 13 (3d8) necrotic damage.  
>
>***Necrotic Breath (Recharge 6).*** The dragonborn exhales shadowy fire in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 36 (8d8) necrotic damage and is frightened of the dragonborn for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't frightened. A frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
