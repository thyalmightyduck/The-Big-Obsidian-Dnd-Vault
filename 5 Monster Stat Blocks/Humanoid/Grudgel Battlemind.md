___
>## Grudgel Battlemind
>*Medium Humanoid (Grudgel), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 28)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Insight +5, Perception +5
>- **Gear** *Longsword*, *Plate Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Grudgel and three national languages
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Action Surge (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The grudgel battlemind takes an extra Action on its turn.  
>
>***Weapon Bond.*** The grudgel battlemind can be disarmed only if incapacitated or dead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The grudgel battlemind makes three weapon attacks or casts one cantrip and makes one weapon attack.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The grudgel battlemind casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Message*  
>1/day each: *Color Spray*, *Fly*, *Gust of Wind*, *Haste*, *Longstrider*, *Sleep*, *Spider Climb*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Center (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The grudgel battlemind gains Advantage on the next attack roll or ability check it makes before the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Misty Step (2/Day).*** The grudgel battlemind casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Battlefield Control.*** *Trigger:* A creature moves into the grudgel strategist's reach. *Response:* The grudgel strategist makes an Opportunity Attack against the triggering creature.  
>
>***Shield (2/Day).*** The grudgel battlemind casts *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
