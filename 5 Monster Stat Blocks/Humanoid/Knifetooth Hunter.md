>## Knifetooth Hunter
>*Medium Humanoid, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 22 (4d8 + 4)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Perception +3, Stealth +5, Survival +3
>- **Gear** *Handaxe*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** The Knifetooth hunter deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage when it hits a target with a weapon attack and has Advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the hunter that doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, and the hunter doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack roll.  
>
>### Actions
>***Handaxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage, or 3 (1d6) Slashing damage if the attack was made as a Bonus Action.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Devious Warrior.*** The Knifetooth hunter takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action. If it has a handaxe in each hand, it can instead make a *Handaxe* attack.
