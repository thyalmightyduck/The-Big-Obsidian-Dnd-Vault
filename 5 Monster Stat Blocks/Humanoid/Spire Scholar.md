>## Spire Scholar
>*Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12 (15 with *mage armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, History +6
>- **Gear** *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Spellcasting.*** The scholar is an 8th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following wizard spells prepared:  
>
>Cantrips (at will): *light*, *mage hand*, *minor illusion*, *ray of frost*  
>1st level (4 slots): *comprehend languages*, *mage armor*, *magic missile*, *shield*  
>2nd level (3 slots): *hold person*, *misty step*, *mirror image*  
>3rd level (3 slots): *blink*, *dispel magic*, *lightning bolt*  
>4th level (2 slots): *control water*  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
