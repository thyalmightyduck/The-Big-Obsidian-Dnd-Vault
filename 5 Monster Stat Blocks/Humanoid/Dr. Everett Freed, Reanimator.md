>***Special Immunities.*** Dr. Everett Freed, Reanimator is immune to the Weakened condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Reanimator makes two Electric Jolt or Necrotic Blast attacks.  
>
>***Electric Jolt.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 35 (10d6) Lightning damage, and the target has the Shocked condition until the end of the Reanimator's next turn.  
>
>***Necrotic Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 36 (8d8) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Weakened condition until the end of the Reanimator's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The Reanimator casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17, +9 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *False Life*, *Gentle Repose*  
>2/day each: *Animate Dead*, *Death Ward*, *Hold Person*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Raise Dead*  
>
>***Touch of Death (1/Day).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 200 Poison damage. This damage ignores *Resistance* and Immunity to Poison damage and can't be reduced or prevented in any way. If the target is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it automatically fails death saving throws until it finishes a Long Rest. A Heal or *Greater Restoration* spell ends this effect early.  
>
>***Reanimate (Recharge 5–6).*** The Reanimator targets the corpse of one of its destroyed creations it can see within 30 feet. The target returns to life with 45 (10d8) Hit Points. The target has Resistance to all damage and Advantage on saving throws until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Creator's Command.*** The Reanimator chooses a creature it can see that was reanimated or created by it. The target can use its Reaction to make an attack with Advantage or move up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Bodyguard.*** *Trigger:* A creature the Reanimator can see targets it with an attack or spell that targets only the reanimator. *Response:* The Reanimator chooses one of its creations within 10 feet and switches spaces with it. The creation becomes the target instead.
