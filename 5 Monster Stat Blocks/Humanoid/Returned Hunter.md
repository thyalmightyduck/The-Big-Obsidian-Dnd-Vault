___
>## Returned Hunter
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 71 (13d8 + 13)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +3, Deception +3, Investigation +3, Perception +4, Survival +4
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Shortswords*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Sylvan and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fey Touched.*** The returned hunter has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed and Frightened conditions and magic that would impede its movement or put it to sleep.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The returned hunter makes three Shortsword attacks or two Longbow attacks.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 150/600 ft. Hit:7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The returned hunter casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Minor Illusion*, *Speak with Animals*  
>2/day each: *Charm Person*, *Cure Wounds*  
>1/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Nondetection*, *Pass Without Trace*, *Silence*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hunter Magic (3/Day).*** The returned hunter casts *Hunter's Mark* or *Misty Step*, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Vanish.*** The returned hunter takes the Hide action.
