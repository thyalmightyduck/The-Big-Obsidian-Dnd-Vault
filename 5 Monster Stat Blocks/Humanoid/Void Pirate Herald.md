>## Void Pirate Herald
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 113 (15d8 + 45)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Int +6, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, Perception +4
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***From Another World.*** The void pirate doesn't have the standard Aberrant Traits other creatures in the Space Between Worlds have.  
>
>***Magical Chaos.*** When the void pirate or a creature within 30 feet of it is targeted by a spell, roll a d6. On a 4-5, an Arcane Anomalies triggers on the caster. On a 6, the spell fails and an Arcane Anomalies triggers on the caster.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The void pirate makes three Void Blast attacks.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The void pirate casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Chromatic Orb*, *Faerie Fire*, *Heat Metal*  
>1/day each: *Arcane Eye*, *Evard's Black Tentacles*, *Fly*  
>
>***Void Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 19 (3d10 + 3) Force damage, and the target is knocked 10 feet away from the void pirate.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Summon Elemental (1/Day).*** The void pirate magically summons an **Entropic Flame**, **Walking Geode**, **Living Deep Haze**, or **Animated Delerium Sludge**. The elemental appears in an unoccupied space within 60 feet of the void pirate, whom it obeys. It takes its turn immediately after the void pirate. It lasts for 1 hour, until it or the void pirate dies, or the void pirate dismisses it as a Bonus Action.
