>### Actions
>***Fiery Burst.*** *Melee Spell  or Ranged Weapon Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) Fire damage, and the Knifetooth firebrand can make the flames leap to another target within 20 feet. The second target takes 3 (1d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Firebrand Aura (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The Knifetooth firebrand surrounds itself with an aura of violet flame, shedding Bright Light in a 10-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 10 feet. These flames increase the firebrand's AC by 1.  
>Whenever a creature within 10 feet of the firebrand hits it with an attack, the flames leap to the attacker, dealing 5 (2d4) Fire damage to it. Violet flame outlines a creature struck by this fire, so the creature can't have the Invisible condition until it leaves the aura. This aura lasts for 10 minutes, until the firebrand dies or has the Incapacitated condition, or until the firebrand ends it as a Bonus Action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Terrifying Flames.*** *Trigger:* The Knifetooth firebrand's Firebrand Aura deals Fire damage to a creature. *Response:* *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, the damaged creature. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn.
