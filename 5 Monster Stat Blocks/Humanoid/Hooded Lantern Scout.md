>## Hooded Lantern Scout
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 32 (5d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +5, Survival +5
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Quiver*, *Shortsword*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The scout makes two attacks.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the end of the scout's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Quarry.*** The scout casts *Hunter's Mark*, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability.
