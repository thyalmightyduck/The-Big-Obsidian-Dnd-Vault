___
>## Inquisitor Judge
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Lawful Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 112 (15d8 + 45)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +6, Investigation +5, Perception +6, Religion +5
>- **Gear** *Plate Armor*, *Greatsword*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Aura of Clarity.*** While conscious, the inquisitor judge emits a 10- foot Emanation of clarifying magic. Each creature in the area of the judge's choice can't have the Blinded or Frightened condition, and the judge chooses whether creatures and objects in the area can have the Invisible condition.  
>
>***Smite.*** Once per turn, when the inquisitor judge hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, it can expend a spell from its Spellcasting action. The spell has no effect, and the attack deals an extra 9 (2d8) Radiant damage for a 2/Day spell or 18 (4d8) Radiant damage for a 1/Day spell.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The inquisitor judge makes three Greatsword attacks.  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing Slashing plus 4 (1d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The Inquisitor Judge casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Protection from Evil and Good*  
>2/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *Guiding Bolt* (level 5 version), *Lesser Restoration*  
>1/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Remove Curse*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Brand Heretic (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The inquisitor judge brands one creature it can see within 30 feet as a heretic. For 1 minute, the inquisitor judge's attacks against the heretic score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Punish Heretic.*** *Trigger:* A creature starts its turn within 5 feet of the inquisitor judge. *Response:* The inquisitor judge can make a Greatsword attack against the creature.
