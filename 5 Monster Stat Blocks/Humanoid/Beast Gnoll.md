>- **Hit Points** 9 (2d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)

>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Spine Spike.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampage.*** After reducing a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, the gnoll moves up to half its Speed and makes a Bite attack.
