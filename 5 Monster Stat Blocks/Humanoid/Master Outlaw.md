___
>## Master Outlaw
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 110 (20d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 35 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +7, Animal Handling +5, Intimidation +6, Perception +5, Sleight of Hand +7
>- **Gear** *Blackpowder Pistol*, *Firearm Bullet*, *Rapier*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Any three languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Opening Shot.*** If the master outlaw hits a target with a ranged weapon attack during the first round of combat, it has Advantage on the next attack roll it makes against the same target before the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Rider's Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** The master outlaw deals an extra 21 (6d6) damage when it hits a target with a weapon attack and has Advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the master outlaw that doesn't have the Incapacitated condition and the master outlaw doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack roll. The master outlaw can also deal this extra damage if it has moved 20+ feet this turn before making the attack, including on a mount.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The master outlaw makes two attacks, using Rapier or Blackpowder Pistol in any combination.  
>
>***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Blackpowder Pistol.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 25/100 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Mobile.*** Both the master outlaw and its mount take the Dash or Disengage action.  
>
>***Quick Loader.*** The master outlaw loads a bullet into its Blackpowder Pistol or another ranged weapon with the Loading property.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Uncanny Riding.*** *Trigger:* A creature the master outlaw can see hits it or its mount with an attack. *Response:* The master outlaw halves the attack's damage against itself or its mount.
