___
>## Eldritch Priest
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Cleric), Any Non-Good Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Religion +2
>- **Gear** *Chain Shirt*, *Greatclub*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Greatclub.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) Bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) Cold damage.  
>
>***Psychic Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d6) Psychic damage, and the target has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Prophecy of Doom (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 15-foot radius Sphere centered on a point within 120 feet. *Failure:* The target is subjected to a random effect from the Eldritch Effects table. All targets are subjected to the same effect. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats its save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>**Cannot render "statblock"—entity was not preloaded (Eldritch Effects)!**
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The priest casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12, +4 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Bane*, *Hideous Laughter*, *Sleep*  
>1/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *See Invisibility*
