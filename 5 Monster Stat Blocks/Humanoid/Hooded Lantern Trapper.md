>## Hooded Lantern Trapper
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 30 (4d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4
>- **Skills** Stealth +6
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Heavy Crossbow*, *Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Powder Keg.*** If the trapper takes damage from a critical hit, it must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or the attack ruptures their alchemical supplies and gunpowder. Each creature within 15 feet of the trapper (including the trapper) is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Trapper's Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 60/120 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) Piercing damage, and the target is subjected to one of the following effects of the trapper's choice:  
>- **Bola Shot.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.
>- **Tranquilizer Shot.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target has the Dazed condition until the end of its next turn.
>
>***Net Shot (1/Day).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target has the Immobilized condition (save ends).  
>
>***Set Trap (5/Day).*** The trapper sets a trap in a space within 5 feet of it. A creature that moves within 5 feet of the trap immediately sets it off, affecting each creature in a 5-foot radius centered on the trap. The trap can be one of the options listed for the Trapper's Crossbow or one of the following additional options:  
>- **Fire Trap.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target has the Burning condition (1d8).
>- **Shock Trap.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target has the Shocked condition until the end of its next turn.
>- **Frost Trap.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target has the Frozen condition (DC 10).
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Smoke Bomb (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The trapper casts *Fog Cloud* without the need for components, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability.
