>## Berserker Trollkin
>*Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 136 (16d8 + 64)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Intimidation +3, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Bryntroll*, *Chain Shirt*, *Handaxe*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Bloodied Frenzy.*** While Bloodied, the trollkin has Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The trollkin regains 5 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the trollkin takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the trollkin's next turn. The trollkin dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The trollkin berserker makes four attacks, using Freezing Bryntroll or Freezing Handaxe in any combination.  
>
>***Freezing Bryntroll.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (1d12 + 4) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Cold damage. Once per round, if the berserker hits with its bryntroll, it can make a melee attack roll with the weapon against a second creature within 5 feet of the first, and that is within reach. On a hit, the second creature takes 13 (2d12) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Freezing Handaxe.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 feet., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Cold damage.
