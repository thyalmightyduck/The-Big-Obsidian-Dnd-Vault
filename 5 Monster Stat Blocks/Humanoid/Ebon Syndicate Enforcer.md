>## Ebon Syndicate Enforcer
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Dex +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +4, Stealth +5
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Fighting Chain*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Thieves' Cant, plus three other languages
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The enforcer makes two attacks using their Dagger and Fighting Chain in any combination.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Fighting Chain.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12). The enforcer can only have one creature grappled by its fighting chain.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Strangle.*** The enforcer deals 4 (1d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage to a target Grappled by its *Fighting Chain*, and the target is unable to speak until the start of the enforcer's next turn or until it is no longer Grappled.
