# **<u>Lore</u>** 
A follower of the long-dead necromancer Dendallen, Hawkren is a clumsy but powerful wizard who was expelled from the Spire for studying forbidden necromancy shortly after being granted a teaching position. Although they are uncertain, rumors have spread that Hawkren is attempting to return the spirit of Dendallen to power by collecting the late necromancer's items, which are said to remain with his previous servants.
## Hawkren and Ogden
Hawkren Bloodstone and Ogden Fellweaver are both followers of Dendallen, a body-possessing necromancer turned quasi-lich and leader of power-hungry cultist fanatics
