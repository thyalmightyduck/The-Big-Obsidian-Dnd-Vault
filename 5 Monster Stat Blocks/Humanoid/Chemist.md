>***Critical Condition.*** A creature that rolls a natural 1 on a saving throw against the chemist's attacks or effects takes maximum damage from the attack or effect.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** Chemist is immune to the Burning condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The chemist makes one Caustic Grip attack and uses either Chemical Spray or Explosive Concoction, if available.  
>
>***Caustic Grip.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 24 (8d4 + 4) Acid damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17). Until the grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition and the chemist can't use Caustic Grip against another target. At the end of each of its turns, the target takes 24 (8d4 + 4) Acid damage.  
>
>***Chemical Spray.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Acid, Cold, Fire, or Lightning damage (the chemist's choice). *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Explosive Concoction (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 20-foot radius Sphere centered on a point within 120 feet of the chemist. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Acid, Cold, Fire, or Lightning damage (the chemist's choice), and the target has the Burning condition (4d6, same damage type). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Acid Rain (1/Day).*** The chemist conjures a storm of acid rain in a 20-foot radius, 20-foot tall Cylinder centered on a point it can see within 120 feet. Exposed flames in the area are doused. Chemical pools accumulate in the area and emit thick fumes, rendering it Difficult Terrain and Lightly Obscured. The storm and the pools evaporate after 1 minute.  
>A creature that enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* 30 (12d4) Acid damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Emergency Dose.*** The chemist administers a restorative poultice to itself or a creature it can see within 5 feet that isn't a Construct or Undead. The target regains 35 10d6 Hit Points. Once a creature benefits from Emergency Dose, it can't do so again until it finishes a Short or Long Rest.
