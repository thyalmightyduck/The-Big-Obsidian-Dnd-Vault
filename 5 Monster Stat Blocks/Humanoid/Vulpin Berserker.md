>## Vulpin Berserker
>*Medium Humanoid (Vulpin), Any Chaotic Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (*hide armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 67 (9d8 + 27)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +3
>- **Gear** *Greataxe*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Birdfolk, Vulpin
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Evasive.*** The berserker adds its Intelligence modifier as a bonus to its Dexterity saving throws (already included in the stat block).  
>
>***Reckless.*** At the start of its turn, the berserker can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against it have advantage until the start of its next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.  
>
>***Greataxe.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d12 + 3) slashing damage.
