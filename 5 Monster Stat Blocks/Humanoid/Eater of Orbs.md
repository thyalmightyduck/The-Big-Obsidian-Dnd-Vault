>## Eater of Orbs
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Demon), Any Evil Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 84 (13d8 + 26)
>- **Speed** 35 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Insight +4, Intimidation +7, Perception +4, Performance +7, Stealth +6
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Gear** *Rapier*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Any three languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Fiendish Palaver.*** If the eater has consumed a Humanoid's eye, the eater can apply its Proficiency Bonus to any Charisma check to impersonate that Humanoid. If the Proficiency Bonus already applies, the eater gains Expertise instead.  
>
>***Fiendish Precision.*** When the eater doesn't have Disadvantage on a melee weapon attack and hits the target, the eater deals 3 extra dice of the weapon's damage (included in the attack).  
>
>***Fiendish Sight.*** Magical Darkness doesn't impede the eater's Darkvision.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The eater makes two Rapier attacks.  
>
>***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage, or 16 (3d8 + 3) Piercing damage if the eater doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack. Critical Hit: The eater extracts one of the target's eyes.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The eater shape-shifts into the Humanoid whose eye it last ate, or reverts to its true form. Its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The eater casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Charm Person*, *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Calm Emotions*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Disguise Self*, *Hideous Laughter*, *Knock*, *Suggestion*  
>1/day each: *Confusion*, *Fear*, *Nondetection*, *Tongues*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The eater takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action  
>
>### Reactions
>***Uncanny Dodge.*** *Trigger:* A creature the eater can see hits it with an attack. *Response:* The eater halves the attack's damage.
