___
>## Downcast Sangromancer
>*Medium Humanoid (Downcast, Wizard); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 135 (18d8 + 54)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7, Wis +6, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Investigation +5, Perception +6, Religion +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Gear** *Arcane Focus*, *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Celestial and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The downcast sangromancer make two attacks, using Dagger or Holy Flame in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Holy Flame.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature the downcast sangromancer can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 27 (5d10) Radiant damage. Cover grants no benefit against this saving throw.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The downcast sangromancer casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Message*  
>2/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *Enhance Ability*, *Guiding Bolt*, *Reanimate*, *Vampiric Touch*  
>1/day each: *Blight*, *Circle of Scarlet*, *Mortality*, *Sunbeam*, *Finger of Death*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Blood Rush (2/Day).*** The downcast sangromancer casts *Blood Rush*, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Blood Wings.*** The downcast sangromancer manifests wings of blood and gains a Fly Speed of 30 feet. The wings remain until the sangromancer dies, has the Incapacitated condition, or dismisses them as a Bonus Action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell (2/Day).*** The downcast sangromancer casts *Counterspell* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Theft of Vitae (2/Day).*** The downcast sangromancer casts *Theft of Vitae* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
