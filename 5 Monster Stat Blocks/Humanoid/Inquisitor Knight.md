>## Inquisitor Knight
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +4, Investigation +3, Perception +4
>- **Gear** *Chain Mail*, *Javelin*, *Longsword*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Brave.*** The inquisitor knight has Advantage on saving throws made to avoid or end the Frightened condition.  
>
>***Discerning.*** The inquisitor knight has Advantage on saving throws against illusions.  
>
>***Mark.*** When the inquisitor knight hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, it Marks the target until the end of the inquisitor knight's next turn. The Mark ends early if the knight has the Incapacitated condition or dies, or another creature Marks the same target. While the Marked creature is within the inquisitor knight's reach, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than the inquisitor knight.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The inquisitor knight makes two attacks, using Longsword or Javelin in any combination.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) Slashing damage if wielded with two hands. The inquisitor knight Marks the target.  
>
>***Javelin.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage. If the attack was a melee attack, the inquisitor knight Marks the target.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Marked Control.*** *Trigger:* A creature Marked by the inquisitor knight moves while within 5 feet of the inquisitor knight. *Response:* The inquisitor knight makes a Longsword attack against the triggering creature. *Hit:* In addition to the Longsword damage, the creature's Speed becomes 0 until the end of its turn.
