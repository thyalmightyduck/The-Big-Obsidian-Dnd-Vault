___
>## Flamekeeper
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Insight +6, Religion +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Radiant
>- **Gear** *Chain Shirt*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common and one other language
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) Piercing damage, or 4 (1d8) Piercing damage if wielded with two hands.  
>
>***Searing Light.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature the flamekeeper can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Radiant damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the start of the flamekeeper's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Radiant Flames.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature of the flamekeeper's choice in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the flamekeeper. *Failure:* 16 (3d10) Radiant damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* Magical Darkness in the area is dispelled.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The flamekeeper casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Guidance*, *Light*, *Spare the Dying*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Bless*, *Healing Word*, *Lesser Restoration*  
>1/day each: *Aid*, *Dispel Magic*, *Spirit Guardians*
