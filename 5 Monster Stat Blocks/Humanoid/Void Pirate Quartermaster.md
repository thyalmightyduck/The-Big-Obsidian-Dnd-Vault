___
>## Void Pirate Quartermaster
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 84 (13d8 + 26)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Captain's Orders.*** While within 60 feet of a void pirate captain, the void pirate has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed and Frightened conditions.  
>
>***Duel to the Death.*** Once per turn, if the Void Pirate is within 5 feet of an enemy, and no other creatures are within 5 feet of them, the void pirate can deal an extra 10 (3d6) Slashing damage with their Voidblade attack.  
>
>***From Another World.*** The void pirate doesn't have the standard Aberrant Traits other creatures in the Space Between Worlds have.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The void pirate makes two attacks.  
>
>***Voidblade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 4) Force damage.  
>
>***Boltgun.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Piercing damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bark Orders.*** One void pirate squib within 60 feet of the void pirate can use its Reaction to move up to half its Speed or make an attack.
