___
>## Alienist
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 180 (25d8 + 75)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|19 (+4)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7, Int +8, Wis +8, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +8, Deception +7, Insight +8, Medicine +8, Perception +8, Persuasion +7
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed
>- **Gear** *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Telepathy 30 ft. Any six languages
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Lie Detection.*** The alienist always knows if it hears a lie, and has Advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks.  
>
>***Unmatched Intellect.*** The alienist adds its Intelligence modifier to its Initiative rolls.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The alienist makes three Mind Thrust attacks.  
>
>***Mind Thrust.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 26 (4d10 + 4) Force damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet in any horizontal direction.  
>
>***Mass Manipulation (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16, up to six creatures within 60 feet of the alienist that can see and hear it. *Failure:* The target must use its Reaction to move up to its Speed and make a weapon attack or cast a cantrip. The alienist chooses the direction of the movement and the target of the attack or cantrip.  
>
>***Nerve Gas (1/Day).*** The alienist creates a 10-foot radius Sphere of psychoactive fog centered on a point it can see within 60 feet. The fog spreads around corners and renders its area Heavily Obscured. It lasts for 1 minute or until a strong wind disperses it.  
>A creature that enters the fog for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there is subjected to the following effect. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The alienist casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to spell attacks):  
>
>2/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *Suggestion*  
>1/day each: *Modify Memory*, *Telekinesis*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Teleport.*** The alienist teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 60 feet.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Mind Over Matter.*** *Trigger:* The alienist takes damage. *Response:* The alienist gains Resistance to the triggering damage.
