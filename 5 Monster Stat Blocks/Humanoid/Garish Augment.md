>## Garish Augment
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Any Evil Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 104 (11d8 + 55)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Wis +3
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Athletics +6, Medicine +3, Perception +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Poison, Psychic
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Deep Speech or Infernal plus one national language
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Augmented Movement.*** The garish augment has two other Speeds:Burrowing (20 feet), Climbing (30 feet), or Swimming (30 feet).  
>
>***Augmented Spit.*** TThe garish augment's Spit attack deals Acid, Necrotic, or Poison damage, chosen when the garish augment was created.  
>
>***Augmented Strikes.*** The garish augment's Strike attack deals Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage, chosen when the garish augment was created.  
>
>***Jumper.*** The garish augment jumps twice as far as normal.  
>
>***Unnatural Presence.*** Effects that detect Aberrations or Fiends can detect the garish augment.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The garish augment makes three attacks, using Strike or Spit in any combination.  
>
>***Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) damage of the type from Augmented Strikes.  
>
>***Spit.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) damage of the type from Augmented Spit.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* A creature that garish augment can see hits it with an attack. *Response:* The garish augment adds 2 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
