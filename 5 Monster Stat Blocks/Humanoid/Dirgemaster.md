___
>## Dirgemaster
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 93 (17d8 + 17)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Perception +5, Performance +7
>- **Gear** *Musical Instrument*, *Rapier*, *Breastplate*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Any three languages
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Death Marked.*** A creature that takes damage from the dirgemaster's attacks or spells can't regain Hit Points until the end of the dirgemaster's next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Counterspell (3/Day).*** The dirgemaster casts *Counterspell* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Death Knell.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +7, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8)) Psychic damage, or 26 (4d12) Psychic damage if the target has the Bloodied condition.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The dirgemaster casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Dancing Lights*, *Hideous Laughter*, *Major Image*, *Speak with Dead*  
>2/day each: *Animate Dead*, *Blindness/Deafness*, *Faerie Fire*, *Shatter* (level 3 version)  
>1/day each: *Animate Objects*, *Confusion*, *Eyebite*, *Greater Invisibility*, *Insect Plague*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Macabre Dirge (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The dirgemaster plays a magical requiem. Each creature of the dirgemaster's choice that can see and hear it must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, that creature must subtract 1d4 whenever it makes a D20 Test for one minute. If the target is an allied Undead or Construct, it adds the result to its roll. Otherwise, it subtracts the result from its roll. A creature with Immunity to the Frightened condition is unaffected. This effect doesn't stack with others that grant similar bonuses or penalties, such as the *Bless* and *Bane* spells.
