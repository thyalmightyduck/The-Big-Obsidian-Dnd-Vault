>## Huskarl
>*Small or Medium Humanoid, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Con +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +4, Intimidation +3, Perception +4
>- **Gear** *Battleaxe*, *Chain Mail*, *Javelin*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The huskarl makes two attacks, using Battle Axe and Javelin in any combination.  
>
>***Battle Axe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Javelin.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Interpose.*** *Trigger:* An ally within 5 feet of the huskarl takes damage. *Response:* The huskarl takes the damage instead.
