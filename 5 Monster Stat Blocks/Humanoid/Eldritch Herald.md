___
>## Eldritch Herald
>*Small or Medium Humanoid (Cleric), Any Non-Good Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 104 (16d8 + 32)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +6, Religion +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Gear** *Breastplate*, *Handaxe*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Otherworldly Calm.*** The herald has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed and Frightened conditions, and its thoughts can't be read. A creature that attempts to read the herald's thoughts is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The herald makes three attacks, using Handaxe or Psychic Burst in any combination.  
>
>***Handaxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage plus 6 (2d6) Cold damage.  
>
>***Psychic Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Prophecy of Doom (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 15-foot radius Sphere centered on a point within 120 feet. *Failure:* The target is subjected to a random effect from the Eldritch Effects table. All targets are subjected to the same effect. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats its save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>**Cannot render "statblock"—entity was not preloaded (Eldritch Effects)!**
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The herald casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Light*, *See Invisibility*, *Sleep*, *Thaumaturgy*, *Tongues*  
>2/day each: *Bane*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Hideous Laughter*, *Hold Person*, *Wrack*  
>1/day each: *Bestow Curse*, *Confusion*, *Contact Other Plane*, *Death Ward*, *Dream*, *Phantasmal Killer*, *Spirit Guardians*, *True Seeing*
