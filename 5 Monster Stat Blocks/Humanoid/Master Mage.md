___
>## Master Mage
>*Medium Humanoid*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 83 (15d8 + 15)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +6, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, History +6, Investigation +6, Perception +4
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common and three other languages
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Academy Rings (1/Day).*** The mage can cast a spell from Spellcasting without expending a use of it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mage makes three Prismatic Blast attacks.  
>
>***Prismatic Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Cold, Fire, or Lightning damage (mage's choice).  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The mage casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Light*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*, *Unseen Servant*  
>2/day each: *Fly*, *Ice Storm*, *Lightning Bolt*  
>1/day each: *Banishment*, *Wall of Force*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell (Recharge 5–6).*** The mage casts *Counterspell* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability from Spellcasting.
