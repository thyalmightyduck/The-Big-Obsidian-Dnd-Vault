>## Rudolph van Richten
>*Medium Humanoid (Human), Lawful Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 105 (14d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, Medicine +7, Perception +7, Religion +6, Sleight of Hand +5
>- **Gear** *Hand Crossbow*, *Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Common, Elvish, Infernal
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Shield of Erasmus.*** While Bloodied, Rudolph has Resistance to Force, Poison, and Psychic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Rudolph makes three attacks, using Silver Sword Cane or Hand Crossbow in any combination.  
>
>***Silver Sword Cane.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage plus 5 (2d4) Radiant damage. Critical *Hit:* The target has the Poisoned condition until it finishes a Long Rest. It makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw at end of each hour, ending the effect on a success.  
>
>***Hand Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Repel Evil (Recharge 5–6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature of Rudolph's choice in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of Rudolph's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Occult Aid (2/Day).*** Rudolph casts *Cure Wounds*, *Dispel Evil and Good*, or *Sanctuary*, using Wisdom as the spellcasting modifier (spell save DC 15).
