>- **Languages** —

>___
>***Amphibious.*** The hafgufa spawn can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Seagoing Lurker.*** The hafgufa spawn has Advantage on Stealth checks when underwater.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hafgufa spawn makes four Tentacle attacks. It can replace one attack with Bite.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Piercing damage. The hafgufa spawn has Advantage on this attack roll against a creature Grappled by one of its tentacles.  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) from one of four tentacles, and the hafgufa spawn can pull the target up to 10 feet straight toward itself.  
>
>***Reeking Mist (1/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13 Each creature in a 20-foot Cube. *Failure:* Target has the Incapacitated condition for 10 minutes. At the end of each of its turns, a target can repeat the saving throw, ending the condition on a success.
