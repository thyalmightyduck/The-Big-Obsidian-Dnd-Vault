___
>## Elder Hurrock
>*Medium Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|8 (-1)|18 (+4)|5 (-3)|11 (+0)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7
>- **Skills** Perception +3
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Thunder
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't adamantine
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands Dwarvish and Terran but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Harrowed Tunneler.*** The elder hurrock can burrow through solid rock at its Borrow Speed and leaves a 6-foot diameter tunnel in its wake. The tunnel is Difficult Terrain.  
>
>***Rubble Walker.*** Difficult Terrain composed of rock, rubble, or similar debris doesn't cost the elder hurrock extra movement.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elder hurrock makes one Axe Arm attack, one Beard attack, and one Shield Arm attack.  
>
>***Axe Arm.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Beard.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Shield Arm.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* A creature the elder hurrock can see hits it with a melee attack while its shield arm is free. *Response:* The elder hurrock adds 4 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
