___
>## Scarapit
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 59 (7d10 + 21)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 20 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|3 (-4)|8 (-1)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +1, Stealth +4, Survival +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Immunities** Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Ambusher.*** In the first round of a combat, the scarapit has advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has surprised.  
>
>***Keen Smell.*** The scarapit has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Noxious Resilience.*** The scarapit can breathe normally in any environment, and it has advantage on saving throws made against harmful gases and vapors (such as *cloudkill* and *stinking cloud* effects, inhaled poisons, and the breath weapons of some dragons).  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The scarapit can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Subterranean Explorer.*** While the scarapit is underground, it always knows how far it is from the surface, it can't be surprised while it isn't incapacitated, and it has advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made to track creatures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage, or 21 (4d8 + 3) piercing damage if the target is surprised, and the target must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 9 (2d8) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the poison damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target is stable but poisoned for 1 hour, even after regaining hit points, and is paralyzed while poisoned in this way.
