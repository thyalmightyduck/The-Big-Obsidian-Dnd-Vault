>## Lord of the Feast
>*Large Monstrosity (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 66 (7d10 + 28) plus 66 (7d10 + 28) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Wis +9
>- **Skills** Athletics +9, Perception +14, Stealth +14, Survival +9
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 24
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Aura of Hunger.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the Lord of the Feast takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Close-Quarters Hunter.*** Being within 5 feet of a Hostile creature doesn't impose Disadvantage on the Lord of the Feast's ranged attack rolls.  
>
>***Hunter's Sense.*** The Lord of the Feast has Advantage on attack rolls against a Bloodied creature.  
>
>***Predatory Senses.*** The Lord of the Feast has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks. He can pinpoint the position of any creature within 120 feet of him with the Invisible condition.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The Lord of the Feast regains 20 Hit Points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 Hit Point remaining.  
>
>### Actions
>***The Lord of the Feast always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the Lord of the Feast can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the Lord of the Feast takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Bite.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level.
>- **Claws.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (4d4).
>- **Poison Arrow.** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) Poison damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition (save ends).
>- **Ensnaring Arrow.** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target has the Restrained condition (DC 17 Dexterity saving throw ends).
>- **Harpoon Arrow.** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage, and if the target is Huge or smaller, it is pulled up to 20 feet toward the Lord of the Feast.
>
>***Reposition.*** The Lord of the Feast moves up to his Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Call of the Hunt (1/Round).*** The Lord of the Feast releases a blood-curdling howl that can be heard all across Drakkenheim. All garmyr in the city heed this call and rush towards the Lord of the Feast's current position. Each garmyr within 60 feet who can see and hear the Lord of the Feast can use its Reaction to move up to half its Speed and make one melee or ranged attack with Advantage.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The Lord of the Feast chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on him. The Lord of the Feast can use this action even if he would otherwise be unable to take actions, his actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters his game statistics.
