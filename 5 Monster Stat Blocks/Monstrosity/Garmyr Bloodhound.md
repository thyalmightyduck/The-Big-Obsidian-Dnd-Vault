>## Garmyr Bloodhound
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 82 (11d8 + 33)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|19 (+4)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +9, Stealth +7, Survival +9
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Quiver*, *Studded Leather Armor*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Kill Shot.*** The garmyr's ranged attacks deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a Bloodied creature.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The garmyr has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the garmyr's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Skilled Hunter.*** The garmyr's ranged attacks ignore Half and Three-Quarters Cover.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The garmyr makes three attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage, or 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage if wielded with two hands to make a melee attack.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the start of the garmyr's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hunter's Quarry.*** The garmyr casts *Hunter's Mark*, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Skirmisher.*** *Trigger:* A Hostile creature ends its movement within 5 feet of the garmyr. *Response:* The garmyr moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.
