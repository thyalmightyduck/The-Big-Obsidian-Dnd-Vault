>## Nyarlathotep
>*Huge Monstrosity (Mythos Deity), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 273 (26d12 + 104)
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|26 (+8)|17 (+3)|19 (+4)|22 (+6)|19 (+4)|24 (+7)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +12, Wis +10, Cha +13
>- **Skills** Arcana +12, Deception +16, Insight +10, Investigation +12, Perception +10
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Darkvision 240 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** All
>- **Challenge** 19 (XP 22,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Defeat from Victory.*** When Nyarlathotep drops to 0 Hit Points or would be killed outright, he is not slain. Instead, his body ripples with energy as it undergoes a horrific transformation. He returns to whatever strange realm he dwells in as his defeated form is torn apart by unseen forces. Mythos creatures with a combined CR of up to 20 emerge from the pile of gore, occupying the nearest, empty space. These creatures attack those who were hostile to Nyarlathotep and fight to the death.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If Nyarlathotep fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** Nyarlathotep has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Poisoned Words, Rotten Soul.*** A creature that succeeds in a Wisdom (Insight) check to detect one of Nyarlathotep's lies takes 66 (12d10) Psychic damage and cannot communicate with other creatures for 3d6 minutes. A creature killed by this trait appears to have died of a heart attack or similar cause.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Nyarlathotep makes two Rending Claw attacks and two Piercing Talon attacks.  
>
>***Rending Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, Reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d8 + 8) Slashing damage and the target gains the Prone condition.  
>
>***Piercing Talon.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, Reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (1d10 + 8) Piercing damage. Prone creatures have Vulnerability to this attack's damage.  
>
>***Horrid Tongue Lash (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 24, creatures of Nyarlathotep's choice within 20 ft. of it; *Failure:* The target takes 66 (12d10) acid damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** Nyarlathotep shape-shifts into a Small or Medium Humanoid, or it returns to this form. Other than his size, his game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment he is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Nyarlathotep casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20):  
>
>At will: *Charm Monster*, *Dispel Magic*, *Invisibility*, *Misty Step*, *Pass without Trace*  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Nyarlathotep can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Nyarlathotep regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Swift Claw.** Nyarlathotep makes a Rending Claw attack.
>- **Incite Delirium.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature within 120 ft. of Nyarlathotep of its choice; *Failure:* The target uses its Reaction to make a weapon attack against a target of Nyarlathotep's choice.
>- **Relentless Horror (Recharge 5–6).** Nyarlathotep chooses one of the following options:
  - It teleports up to 200 ft. to an unoccupied space.
  - It immediately ends one spell or condition affecting it.
  - It ends one spell or magical effect of its choice within 120 ft. of it.
