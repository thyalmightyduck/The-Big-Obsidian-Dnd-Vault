___
>## Plasmid
>*Large Monstrosity, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 127 (15d10 + 45)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|17 (+3)|5 (-3)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The plasmid can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Receptive Telepathy.*** The plasmid is considered a shark for the purpose of being magically controlled by any sahuagin's Shark Telepathy trait.  
>
>***Solid State (Object Form Only).*** When formed into an inanimate object, the plasmid has an AC of 18 and regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. However, the plasmid's Speed becomes 0 in this form and it cannot attack. It can be used as an item or a set of *Artisan's Tools* when it is in this form.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The plasmid makes three Vicious Pseudopod attacks.  
>
>***Vicious Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Adaptive Shape-Shift (Amorphous Form Only).*** When in its true, amorphous form, the plasmid gains one of the following benefits until it uses this action again:  
>- **Truesight.** The plasmid gains Truesight with a range of 30 feet.
>- **Blindsight.** The plasmid gains Blindsight with a range of 60 feet.
>- **Speed.** The plasmid's Speed and Swim Speed increase to 30 feet.
>- **Extra Damage.** The plasmid's Vicious Pseudopod deals an extra 1d6 Acid damage on a hit.
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shape-Shift.*** The plasmid shape-shifts to resemble a Medium or Small object while retaining its game statistics, or it returns to its true amorphous form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.
