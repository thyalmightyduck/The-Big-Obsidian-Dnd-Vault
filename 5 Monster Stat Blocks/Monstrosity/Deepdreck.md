___
>## Deepdreck
>*Medium Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|9 (-1)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Tremorsense 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Explosive Death.*** The deepdreck explodes when it dies. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 15-foot Emanation originating from the deepdreck. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Fragrance Aversion.*** The deepdreck has Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws while within 10 feet of burning incense or a creature wearing Perfume.  
>
>***Tunneler.*** The deepdreck can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrow Speed and leaves a 5-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The deepdreck makes two attacks, using Claw or Putrid Emission in any combination.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Putrid Emission.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the start of the deepdreck's next turn.
