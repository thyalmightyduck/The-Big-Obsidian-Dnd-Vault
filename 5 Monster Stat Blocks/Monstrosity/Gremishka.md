___
>## Gremishka
>*Tiny Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 31 (7d4 + 14)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 30 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The gremishka has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite (Individual Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Force damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Magic Allergy (Individual Form Only; 1/Day).*** *Trigger:* A creature within 30 feet of the gremishka casts a spell. *Response:* The gremishka regains 15 Hit Points, then becomes a **Medium swarm of Tiny creatures**.
