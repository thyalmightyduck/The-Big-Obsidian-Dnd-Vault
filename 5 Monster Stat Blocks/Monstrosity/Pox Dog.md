>***Pack Tactics.*** The pox dog has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the dog's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Weeping Pox.*** A Humanoid that comes within 5 feet of the pox dog is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target is infected with a disease called the Weeping Pox. *Success:* The target is immune to the disease for 24 hours.  
>An infected target initially has sharpened senses and a sense of wellbeing. Whenever the target makes an ability check or a saving throw against something other than the Weeping Pox, it can roll a d4 and add the result to its roll.  
>Every 24 hours, the target makes another save. *Failure:* The disease's benefits end, and the target suffers a level of Exhaustion. This Exhaustion can't be reduced until the disease is cured. The Weeping Pox can only be cured by magic.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Piercing damage, and the target is subjected to Weeping Pox.
