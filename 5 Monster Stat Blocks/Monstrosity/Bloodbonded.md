___
>## Bloodbonded
>*Medium Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 66 (12d8+12), or 78 +24 if missing a leg
>- **Speed** 30 ft. (or 10 ft. if missing a leg)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +3, Wis +0
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Insight +0, Persuasion +6
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Psychic
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Radiant
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Mace*, *Studded Leather Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** Celestial, Infernal, one national language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Resolute Followers.*** Allies of the bloodbonded within 60 feet of it have Advantage on Charisma and Wisdom saving throws.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The bloodbonded makes one Mace attack and one Blood Boil attack.  
>
>***Mace.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Blood Boil.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature the bloodbonded can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Fire damage. The bloodbonded must be able to speak to use Blood Boil.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bloody Inspiration.*** The bloodbonded causes an ally within 30 feet to enter a state of enraged bloodlust for 1 minute. The ally gains 5 Temporary Hit Points, Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, and Vulnerability to Psychic damage. The ally has Advantage on attack rolls, but creatures have Advantage on attack rolls against the ally.  
>
>***Rage Bomb (2/Day).*** The bloodbonded infuses an ally affected by Bloody Inspiration with fatal fury, causing it to foam at the mouth with blood. The ally explodes in a shower of superheated blood. Each creature within 10 feet of the exploding ally is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Loyal Followers.*** *Trigger:* The bloodbonded is hit with an attack that targets only the bloodbonded. *Response:* An ally within 5 feet takes the damage instead.
