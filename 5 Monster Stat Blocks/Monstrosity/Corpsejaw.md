>*Medium Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 18 (4d8)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___

>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|

>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Adamantine Jaws.*** The corpsejaw's Bite attack is considered adamantine, scoring a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage. Critical Hit: If the target is wearing nonmagical armor, the armor takes a permanent and cumulative -1 penalty to the AC it offers. Armor reduced to an AC of 10 is destroyed.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature and the corpsejaw moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition and the corpsejaw can make a Bite attack against it as a Bonus Action.
