___
>## Garmyr Thaumaturge
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 105 (14d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +3, Perception +7
>- **Gear** *Spear*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of the Blood Moon.*** The garmyr projects a 30-foot Emanation of carnage. Creatures have the Bloodied condition while within the Emanation, even if they have more than half of their Hit Points remaining. This effect ends if the garymyr is Incapacitated.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The garmyr has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the garmyr's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The garmyr makes three attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage, or 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage if wielded with two hands to make a melee attack.  
>
>***Blood Call.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (2d4).  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The garmyr casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Armor* (included above)  
>2/day each: *Bane*, *Bless*, *Crusader's Mantle*  
>1/day each: *Dispel Magic*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Feast.*** The garmyr makes a Bite attack against a Bloodied target.
