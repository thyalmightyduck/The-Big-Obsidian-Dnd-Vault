___
>## Boog
>*Large Monstrosity (Brute), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Intimidation +2
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Boog Charge.*** If Boog moves at least 10 feet straight toward a Medium or smaller creature and then hits them with a Gore attack on the same turn, the target is impaled on Boog's horn and is grappled (escape DC 15). While grappled in this way, the target is restrained and takes 3 (1d6) piercing damage at the start of each of their turns, and Boog can't make a Gore attack against another target.  
>
>***Boog Rampage.*** If Boog sees another Abomination reduced to 0 hit points, Boog rampages for 1 minute. While rampaging, Boog's melee attacks deal an extra 2 damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Boog makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (1d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage or 12 (1d10 + 7) if rampaging.  
>
>***Gore.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) piercing damage or 10 (1d6 + 7) if rampaging.  
>
>***Boog's Ball.*** Boog throws a 3-inch-radius magic ball at a point they can see within 60 feet of them. If that point is 30 feet or less from Boog, the ball grows to a 5-foot radius. If that point is more than 30 feet from Boog, the ball grows to a 10-foot radius. Each creature in the ball's area when it reaches that point must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage. After the ball reaches that point, it shrinks back to its 3-inch radius and teleports into the hand Boog used to throw it.
