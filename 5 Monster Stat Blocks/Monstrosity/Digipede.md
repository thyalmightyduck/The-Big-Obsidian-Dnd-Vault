>___

>- **Hit Points** 135 (14d12 + 56)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)

>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Con +7
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Lightning
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3

>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The digipede makes one Bite attack and two Talons attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d6 + 6) Slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) Lightning damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Shocked condition until the end of the digipede's next turn.  
>
>***Eye Beam (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 60-foot long, 5-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Lightning damage, and the target has the Slowed condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. *Second Failure:* The target has the Immobilized condition. *Third Failure:* The target has the Paralyzed condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
