>## Faith Finch
>*Tiny Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 1 (1d4 - 1)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|1 (-5)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|2 (-4)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 0 (XP 10; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Faith Cough.*** A Humanoid that touches the faith finch or takes damage from its Talons attack is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target is infected with a disease called Faith Cough. *Success:* The target is immune to the disease for 24 hours.  
>An infected target has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and Constitution saving throws. Every 24 hours, the target repeats its save. The disease ends after the target succeeds on seven saves.  
>
>***Inspired Presence.*** A creature that succeeds on a DC 15 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check can coax a faith finch to land on them. A person a faith finch lands on gains Heroic Inspiration.  
>
>### Actions
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1 Slashing damage, and the target is subjected to Faith Cough.
