>## Hafgufa
>*Gargantuan Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 217 (14d20 + 70)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___

>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +9
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The hafgufa can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Seagoing Lurker.*** The hafgufa has Advantage on Stealth checks when underwater.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The hafgufa deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hafgufa makes four Tentacle attacks. It can replace one attack with its Bite.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 28 (4d10 + 6) Piercing damage. The hafgufa has Advantage on this attack roll against a creature grappled by one of its tentacles.  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10 to hit, reach 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (1d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Huge or smaller creature, the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18) from one of four tentacles, and the hafgufa can pull the target up to 25 feet straight toward itself.  
>
>***Reeking Mist (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17 Each creature in a 40-foot Cube. *Failure:* The target has the Incapacitated condition for 10 minutes. At the end of each of its turns, a target can repeat the saving throw, ending the condition on a success.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Swallow.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 18 One Large or smaller creature within 5 feet that is Grappled by the hafgufa (it can have up to six creatures swallowed at a time). *Failure:* The target is swallowed, and the Grappled condition ends. A swallowed creature has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, it has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the hafgufa, and it takes 28 (8d6) Acid damage at the start of each of the hafgufa's turns.  
>If the hafgufa takes 40 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the hafgufa must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, each of which falls in a space within 10 feet of the hafgufa and has the Prone condition. If the hafgufa dies, any swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape from the corpse using 20 feet of movement, exiting Prone.
