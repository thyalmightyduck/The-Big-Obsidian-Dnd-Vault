>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)

>- **Skills** Stealth +3
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Death Flash.*** The invisiboar emits a bright flash of light when it dies. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the invisiboar. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.  
>
>***Invisible.*** The invisiboar always has the Invisible condition unless it's in the area of an *Antimagic Field* spell or similar effect.  
>
>### Actions
>***Gore.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the boar moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) Piercing damage and has the Prone condition.
