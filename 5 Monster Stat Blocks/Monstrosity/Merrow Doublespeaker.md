___
>## Merrow Doublespeaker
>*Medium Monstrosity, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 67 (9d8 + 27)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Stealth +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Primordial (Aquan)
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The merrow can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Mimicry.*** The merrow can mimic voices and animal sounds. A creature that hears the mimicries can tell they are imitations with a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The merrow makes three Spined Tail attacks. It can replace one of these attacks with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Command*.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The merrow casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Command*, *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*  
>
>***Spined Tail.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Piercing damage.
