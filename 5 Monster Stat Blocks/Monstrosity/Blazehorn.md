>## Blazehorn
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d10 + 16)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|2 (-4)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the blazehorn moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) Bludgeoning damage and, if it is Large or smaller, has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Firebomb (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature and object in a 5-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point within 30 feet. *Failure:* 7 (3d4) Fire damage and the target starts burning. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Defensive Launch.*** *Trigger:* The blazehorn becomes Bloodied. *Response:* The blazehorn recharges its Firebomb and uses it immediately. It targets the source of the damage that gave it the Bloodied condition, if possible.
