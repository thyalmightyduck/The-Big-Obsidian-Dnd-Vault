>## Mind Leech
>*Tiny Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 20 (8d4)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Aquatic Camouflage.*** The leech has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in aquatic terrain.  
>
>***Memory Bank.*** The leech has three memory banks in its body. When encountered, roll 1d3 to determine how many memory banks are filled with grey matter. The rest are empty.  
>
>### Actions
>***Attach.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* The leech attaches to the target. While attached, the leech doesn't attack, and moves with the target, using none of the leech's movement. The leech can detach itself using 5 feet of movement, and detaches immediately if its blood bags are filled or if the target dies.  
>A creature within 5 feet can take an action to detach the leech with a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check. *Failure:* The leech is still detached, but the target has the Bleeding condition (2d4). Failure by 5 or More: The leech isn't detached and the target takes 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Brain Drain.*** The leech can use this Bonus Action only when it is attached to a creature. That creature is subjected to the following effect. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target's Intelligence score is reduced by 1d4, and one of the leech's memory banks is filled. If this effect reduces the target's Intelligence score to 0, it has the Unconscious condition until it regains at least 1 point of Intelligence. Otherwise, the reduction lasts until the creature finishes a Short or Long Rest.  
>
>***Thought Transfusion.*** The leech can use this Bonus Action only when it is attached to a creature and has at least one filled memory bank. The attached creature gains Advantage on Intelligence-based D20 Tests for 1 minute, and the leech empties one of its filled memory banks.
