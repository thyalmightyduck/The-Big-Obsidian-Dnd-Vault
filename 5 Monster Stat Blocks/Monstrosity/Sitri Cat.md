>***Seven Shades of Sitri.*** A Humanoid that touches the Sitri cat or takes damage from its Claws attack is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target is infected with a disease called the Seven Shades of Sitri. *Success:* The target is immune to the disease for 24 hours.  
>An infected target is overwhelmed with carnal urges. When the target interacts with a non-Hostile Humanoid, it has the Charmed condition until the Humanoid or its allies harm the target. Every 24 hours, the target repeats the save, ending the Charmed condition but not the disease on a success. The disease ends after the target succeeds on five saves.  
>
>### Actions
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1 Slashing damage, and the target is subjected to the Seven Shades of Sitri.
