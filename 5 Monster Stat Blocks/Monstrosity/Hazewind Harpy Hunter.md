>## Hazewind Harpy Hunter
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 22 (4d8 + 4)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Perception +6
>- **Gear** *Longbow*, *Quiver*, *Studded Leather Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The harpy makes two attacks.  
>
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aerial Maneuver.*** The harpy takes the Dash or Disengage action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Chorus.*** *Trigger:* The harpy is within the area of another harpy's song. *Response:* The harpy joins in on the song, choosing a creature within 30 feet. The target has Disadvantage on its next saving throw against the song.
