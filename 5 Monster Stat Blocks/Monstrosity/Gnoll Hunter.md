### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The gnoll makes two Bite, Spear, or Longbow attacks.  

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.  

***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, or 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage when used with two hands to make a melee [[Attack Action|attack]].  

***Longbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage, and the target's speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.  

### [[Bonus Action|Bonus Actions]]
***Rampage.*** After the gnoll reduces a creature to 0 [[Hit Point|hit points]] with a melee [[Attack Action|attack]] on its turn, the gnoll moves up to half its speed and makes a Bite [[Attack Action|attack]].
