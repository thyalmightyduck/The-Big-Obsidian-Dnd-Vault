___
>## Hazewind Harpy Valkyrie
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 84 (13d8 + 26)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Con +5
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +7, Athletics +6
>- **Gear** *Half Plate Armor*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Pluck.*** The harpy doesn't need to spend extra movement to drag a Medium or smaller creature Grappled by it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The harpy makes three attacks.  
>
>***Grasping Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). Until the grapple ends, the harpy can't use its Grasping Talons on another target.  
>
>***Barbed Javelin.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Lightning damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aerial Maneuver.*** The harpy takes the Dash or Disengage action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Chorus.*** *Trigger:* The harpy is within the area of another harpy's song. *Response:* The harpy joins in on the song, choosing a creature within 30 feet. The target has Disadvantage on its next saving throw against the song.
