___
>## Inkwalker
>*Small Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 22 (5d6 + 5)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +4
>- **Immunities** Acid; Prone
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Mimics sounds and voices it has heard
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Adhesive.*** The inkwalker adheres to anything that touches it. A Large or smaller creature adhered to the inkwalker has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). Ability checks made to escape this grapple have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Ambusher.*** In the first round of combat, the inkwalker has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has Surprised.  
>
>***Grappler.*** The inkwalker has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature Grappled by it.  
>
>***Mimicry.*** The inkwalker can mimic sounds it has heard, including voices and animal noises. A creature that hears the sound it makes can tell they are imitations with a successful Wisdom (Insight) check opposed by the inkwalker's Charisma (Deception) check.  
>
>***Wall Walker.*** The inkwalker can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Acid damage.  
>
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is subjected to Adhesive.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The inkwalker shape-shifts into a CR 1/2 or lower beast Beast or back into its true, amorphous shape. Its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying is transformed.
