___
>## Bondbeast
>*Small Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 84 (13d6 + 39)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Desperate Attacks.*** The bondbeast has Advantage on attack rolls if its bonded spellcaster is within 10 feet and is Bloodied.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The bondbeast makes two Bite attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the start of the bondbeast's next turn. While Poisoned in this way, the target has Disadvantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects originating from the bondbeast's bonded spellcaster, and spell attack rolls made against the Poisoned target by the bonded spellcaster have Advantage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The bondbeast takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Deflection.*** *Trigger:* An attack roll hits the bondbeast's bonded spellcaster while the bonded spellcaster is within 10 feet of the bondbeast. *Response:* Reduce the triggering attack's damage by 8 (1d10 + 3).
