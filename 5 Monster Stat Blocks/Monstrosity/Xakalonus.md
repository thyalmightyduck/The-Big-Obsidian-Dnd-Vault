___
>## Xakalonus
>*Medium Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 105 (14d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|9 (-1)|12 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands Draconic but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Absorption.*** If a xakalonus succeeds on a saving throw against a damaging magical effect and takes no damage, it regains 11 (2d10) Hit Points. Alternatively, if the xakalonus has all of its Hit Points, the next successful melee attack by the xakalonus deals an additional 11 (2d10) Force damage.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The xakalonus has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The xakalonus makes one Bite attack and one Claws attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Sonic Bark (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the xakalonus that can hear it (other xakalonus and the **Hraptnon** are immune). *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Thunder damage and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn.
