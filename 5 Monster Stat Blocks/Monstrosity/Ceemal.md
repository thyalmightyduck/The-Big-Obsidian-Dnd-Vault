___
>## Ceemal
>*Medium Monstrosity (Support), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +4, Int +5, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Medicine +6, Nature +5, Perception +6, Stealth +8, Survival +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Octopodan Camouflage.*** Ceemal can attempt to hide even when they are only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain, falling snow, mist, or other natural phenomena.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Ceemal makes two Fillet Knife or two Acid Spit attacks.  
>
>***Fillet Knife.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, another Abomination within 5 feet of the target regains hit points equal to half the damage dealt.  
>
>***Acid Spit.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, range 20/40 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) acid damage, and the target is poisoned until the start of Ceemal's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***To Me, Sweetie (3/Day).*** Ceemal chooses an ally within 30 feet of them who can hear them. The ally can use their reaction to move up to their speed toward Ceemal without provoking opportunity attacks.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Wail of Loss.*** When another Abomination who Ceemal can see is reduced to 0 hit points by an enemy within 60 feet of Ceemal, Ceemal releases a psychic wail. The enemy must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) psychic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
