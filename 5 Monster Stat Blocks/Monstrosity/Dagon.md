___
>## Dagon
>*Gargantuan Monstrosity (Mythos Deity), Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 23
>- **Hit Points** 468 (24d20 + 216)
>- **Speed** 60 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +16 (26)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|12 (+1)|29 (+9)|22 (+6)|19 (+4)|27 (+8)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +17, Dex +8, Con +16, Int +13, Wis +11, Cha +15
>- **Skills** Arcana +13, Insight +11, Nature +13, Perception +11
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Darkvision 240 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** All
>- **Challenge** 22 (XP 41,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Amphibious.*** Dagon can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If Dagon fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***One With the Sea.*** When Dagon drops to 0 Hit Points or would be killed outright, it instead transforms into a swirling body of water. At the start of each of Dagon's turns, it gains one recovery point but otherwise cannot use actions or legendary actions other than Relentless Horror. When it gains its fourth recovery point, its maximum Hit Points return to normal if they have been reduced and it regains all lost Hit Points. It reforms its physical body in a space of its choice within 10 miles of its location. It then loses all recovery points.  
>If Dagon is the target of a *Banishment* spell cast with a 9th-level spell slot while in this cloud form, it automatically fails its saving throw and is instead sent to the deepest corner of the ocean where it slumbers for 6d10 years.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** Dagon deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Spectral Waves.*** Storm-tossed waves formed of magical force swirl around Dagon in a 120-foot Emanation originating from him. Creatures without a Swim Speed treat this area as Difficult Terrain and take 10 Force damage if they end their turn in this area. Flying creatures that end their turn in this area must make DC 24 Strength saving throws, gaining the Prone condition on a failed save.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Dagon makes four Slashing Claw attacks.  
>
>***Slashing Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +17, Reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 21 (2d10 + 10) Slashing damage and the target is pushed up to 10 feet away from Dagon and gains the Prone condition.  
>
>***Hungry Tide (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 24, creatures of Dagon's choice within 120 ft. of it; *First Failure:* The target takes 11 (2d10) Force damage, and gains the Restrained condition until the end of its next turn, when it repeats the save. *Second Failure:* The target takes 4d20 (22) Force damage its Speed is reduced to 0 and cannot be increased until it uses an action to make a successful DC 24 Strength (Athletics) check.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Dagon can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Dagon regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Disdainful Swipe.** Dagon makes a Slashing Claw attack.
>- **Storm Tossed Seas.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 25, creatures within 120 ft. of Dagon of its choice; *Failure:* Dagon moves the target up to 30 feet and the target gains the Prone condition.
>- **Relentless Horror (Recharge 5–6).** Dagon chooses one of the following options:
  - It teleports up to 200 ft. to an unoccupied space.
  - It immediately ends one spell or condition affecting it.
  - It ends one spell or magical effect of its choice within 120 ft. of it.
