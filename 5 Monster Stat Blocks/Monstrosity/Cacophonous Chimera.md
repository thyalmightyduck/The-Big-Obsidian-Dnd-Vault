>## Cacophonous Chimera
>*Huge Monstrosity (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 54 (4d12 + 28) plus 54 (4d12 + 28) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|15 (+2)|25 (+7)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +12, Int +6, Wis +8, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Perception +8
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Common
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Cacophonous Chorus.*** The cacophonous chimera creates a 30-foot Emanation of noise. A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation that can hear it takes 10 (3d6) Thunder damage.  
>
>***Keen Sight and Smell.*** The cacophonous chimera has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight or smell.  
>
>### Actions
>***The cacophonous chimera always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the cacophonous chimera can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the cacophonous chimera takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Claws and Talons.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d10 + 6) Slashing damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Prone condition.
>- **Contaminated Bite (1/Round).** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target gains a Contamination level.
>
>***Lamprey Tail.*** Melee Attack Roll:+11, reach 30 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Piercing damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (2d4).  
>
>***Aerial Maneuver (1/Round).*** The cacophonous chimera flies up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Cacophony (1/Round).*** The cacophonous chimera's three heads sing a discordant song. The effect of the song varies based on which of the chimera's head is taking the lead. Each time the cacophonous chimera uses this Epic Action, it can't use the same effect it chose last round.  
>
>***Bewildering Chitter (Ratling).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 300-foot Emanation originating from the chimera that can hear it. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Bewildered condition (save ends).  
>
>***Chilling Howl (Garmyr).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 300-foot Emanation originating from the cacophonous chimera that can hear it. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Cold damage, and the target has the Immobilized condition (save ends).  
>
>***Staggering Screech (Harpy).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 300-foot Emanation originating from the cacophonous chimera that can hear it. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Staggered condition (save ends).  
>
>***Wing Blast.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature within 15 feet of the cacophonous chimera. *Failure:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away from the chimera. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The cacophonous chimera chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The cacophonous chimera can use this Epic Action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Crescendo of Chaos (1/Day).*** *Trigger:* The cacophonous chimera becomes Bloodied. It can take this Reaction even if it has the Incapacitated condition or is otherwise unable to take Reactions. *Response:* All conditions and spells on the cacophonous chimera immediately end unless it chooses to be affected by them, and it can move up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action and ignoring Difficult Terrain.  
>It then unleashes a blast of thunderous power. Creatures within 300 feet of it who can hear it are subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* 17 (5d6) Thunder damage plus 17 (5d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Prone condition and can't stand until the end of its next turn.
