>## Apex
>*Large Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 210 (20d10 + 100)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Climb 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +15 (25)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Dex +10, Con +10, Int +10, Wis +10, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +10, Athletics +10, Intimidation +10, Medicine +10, Nature +10, Perception +10
>- **Senses** Blindsight 20 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 16 (XP 15,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The Apex regains 20 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the Apex takes Necrotic damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn. The Apex dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Standing Leap.*** The Apex's Long Jump is up to 50 feet and its High Jump is up to 30 feet, with or without a running start.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Apex makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 27 (4d10 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Lightning Bolt.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 120-foot long, 5-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Lightning damage, and the target has the Shocked condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Fire Blast.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) Fire damage, and the target and each creature within 10 feet of it is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* The target has the Burning condition (4d8 Fire).  
>
>***Cold Snap.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, one creature the Apex can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Cold damage, and the target has the Frozen condition (DC 18). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Rapid Adaptation.*** *Trigger:* The Apex takes damage that isn't Necrotic or Psychic. *Response:* The Apex gains Immunity to that damage type until it is reduced to 0 Hit Points or finishes a Short or Long Rest.
