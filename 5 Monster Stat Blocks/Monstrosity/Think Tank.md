>## Think Tank
>*Large Monstrosity (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 100 (8d10 + 56) plus 100 (8d10 + 56) per palyer character
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 5 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|24 (+7)|22 (+6)|18 (+4)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +13, Int +12, Wis +10, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Arcana +12, Insight +10, Medicine +10, Nature +12, Perception +10
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 1 mile
>- **Challenge** 19 (XP 22,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The Think Tank is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The Think Tank may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Life Support System.*** The Think Tank is protected by three machine nodes that make up its Life Support System. The nodes are positioned in a triangle, each no further than 30 feet apart from the rest. Each node is a Large object with AC 20, 25 Hit Points per player character, and Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage. The Think Tank can't move or be moved more than 60 feet away from any two of its Life Support System nodes.  
>
>***Nightmare Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 120 feet of the Think Tank takes 14 (4d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Shield Generator.*** As long as at least one machine node in the Life Support System remains, the Think Tank's Immunities can't be ignored except by effects caused by level 9 spells or artifacts. If all nodes are destroyed, the Think Tank loses its Immunities.  
>
>***Telepathic Eavesdropping.*** The Think Tank can project its consciousness to see and hear any creature or location within 5 miles as if it had cast *Scrying* (no action required). It can read the thoughts of creatures in this area, communicate with them telepathically, and listen in on telepathic communications which occur between other creatures in the region. It remains aware of its own surroundings while doing so. It cannot observe the area within a Private Sanctum spell or locations and creatures protected by magic which blocks divination or scrying sensors.  
>
>### Actions
>***The Think Tank always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the Think Tank can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the Think Tank takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Contaminated Blast.** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 10 ft., or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 32 (4d12 + 6) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20. *Failure:* The target gains a Contamination level.
>- **Telekinetic Grip.** The Think Tank reaches out with its mind to hold one creature or object it can see within 120 feet of it in its psychic grasp. If the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 20. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Force damage, and the target is moved up to 30 feet in any direction and has the Restrained condition until it escapes or the Think Tank has the Incapacitated condition. The Think Tank can hold up to two creatures or objects in its grip at one time. On its turn, a held creature can take an action to repeat the save, escaping the grip on a success.
  If the target is an object weighing 1,000 pounds or less that isn't being worn or carried, it is telekinetically moved up to 30 feet in any direction. The Think Tank can also exert fine control on objects with its telekinesis, such as manipulating surgical tools or opening a door or a container.
>- **Pluck the Puppet Strings.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature within 120 feet of the Think Tank. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage, and the target must use its Reaction to move up to its Speed directly toward a creature of the Think Tank's choice. If the target ends this movement within reach of the chosen creature, it must make a melee weapon attack against that creature with Advantage.
>
>***Mind Cage (1/Round).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature the Think Tank can see within 120 feet. *Success:* 33 (6d10) Psychic damage. *Failure:* 33 (6d10) Psychic damage, and the target perceives itself to be trapped within a nightmare conjured forth from the depths of its own psyche until it escapes. While trapped, the target has the Immobilized, Blinded, and Deafened conditions. On its turn, the target can take an action to repeat the save. Each time it does, it takes 33 (6d10) psychic damage. On a success, the effect ends for it.  
>
>***Unbind Gravity (1/Round).*** The Think Tank controls gravity in a 200-foot Cube centered on itself until the end of the next round. Each time the Think Tank uses this Epic Action, it decides which direction gravity pulls within the area. All creatures and objects that aren't somehow anchored to the ground in the area fall in the chosen direction until they reach the edge of the area. A creature can make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw to grab onto a fixed object it can reach, avoiding the fall on a success.  
>If some solid object (such as a ceiling) is encountered in this fall, falling objects and creatures strike it just as they would during a normal downward fall. If an object or creature reaches the edge of the area without striking anything, it remains there, oscillating slightly, for the duration.  
>Alternatively, the Think Tank can decide to prevent gravity from acting within the area. When it does, creatures within the area without a Fly Speed levitate in midair, and must move by pushing or pulling against a fixed object or surface within reach (such as a wall or a ceiling), which allows them to move as if they were climbing. Unattended objects float around randomly.  
>If the Think Tank doesn't use this Epic Action during the next round, the effect ends and affected objects and creatures fall back down.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The Think Tank chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The Think Tank can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
