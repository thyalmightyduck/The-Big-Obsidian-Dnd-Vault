___
>## Rabenhex
>*Small Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 10 (3d6)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|3 (-4)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|13 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** The rabenhex doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Slashing damage. If the target is cursed by a rabenhex's Hex, it takes an extra 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hex (Recharges when the Hex Ends).*** The rabenhex places a curse on a creature it can see within 60 feet. The target has Disadvantage on attack rolls against rabenhexes and hags. The curse lasts for 1 minute, until the target drops to 0 Hit Points, or until the rabenhex attacks a different creature, has the Incapacitated condition, or dies. If the rabenhex is Invisible, using this trait doesn't reveal its location.
