___
>## Oracle of the Rat God
>*Small Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 110 (20d6 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Int +5, Wis +8
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Insight +8, Perception +8, Religion +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Poison, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The ratling has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Death Toll.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the ratling can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Weakened condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The ratling casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):  
>
>1/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Divination*, *Eyebite*, *Scrying*, *Spirit Guardians*  
>
>***Squeak of Doom (1/Day).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 16, each Hostile creature in a 60-foot Emanation originating from the ratling that can hear it. *Failure:* 55 (10d10) Psychic damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Ratling Cunning.*** The ratling takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Tail Barbs.*** *Trigger:* A creature the ratling can see within 60 feet succeeds on a D20 Test. *Response:* The ratling forces the triggering creature to reroll the D20 Test and use the lower roll.
