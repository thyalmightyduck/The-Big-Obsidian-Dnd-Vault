___
>## Sightless Agony
>*Medium Monstrosity (Shadowsteel Curse), Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 187 (22d8 + 88)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +9, Con +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** All languages known before the curse
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Shared Agony.*** When a Sightless Agony takes damage, it emits a wave of energy, causing each creature within 5 feet of it to take an amount of Psychic damage equal to half the triggering damage (rounding down).  
>
>***Stunted Flight.*** When a Sightless Agony chooses to fly, it must land by the end of its turn or have the Prone condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Sightless Agony makes one Bite attack, two Claw attacks, and one Tail attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Piercing damage plus 14 (2d8 + 5) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage plus 14 (2d8 + 5) Psychic damage. If both Claw attacks hit the same creature, the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17). Checks made to escape the grapple have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage plus 18 (3d8 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Wave of Agony (1/Day).*** *Trigger:* The Sightless Agony becomes Bloodied. *Response:* *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature within a 20-foot Emanation. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Psychic damage. *Success:* Half damage.
