___
>## Groundling Slayer
>*Medium Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +7, Survival +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Gear** six *Daggers*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The groundling makes two attacks, using Claw or Dagger in any combination.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13) from one of two paws.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Go to Ground.*** *Trigger:* The groundling is hit by an attack roll while it is on the ground. *Response:* The groundling halves the damage (round down) it takes from that attack.
