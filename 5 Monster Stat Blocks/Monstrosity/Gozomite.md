___
>## Gozomite
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Deep Speech
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The gozomite has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the gozomite's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The gozomite can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gozomite makes two Bite attacks. It can replace on attack with Stinger.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Stinger.*** Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target is injected with hundreds of gozomite eggs that hatch after 2d4 rounds. If the target takes Fire damage, the eggs die before hatching.  
>After the eggs hatch, the target takes 14 (4d6) Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns as the gozomitelings feast on its flesh. A creature can take an action to kill or remove the gozomitelings using an open flame or a weapon that deals Piercing or Slashing damage, dealing 10 damage to the target in the process, or a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check that deals no damage.  
>A creature reduced to 0 Hit Points by the gozomitelings dies. Its corpse is devoured by the gozomitelings after 1 round, leaving behind only its equipment and bones.
