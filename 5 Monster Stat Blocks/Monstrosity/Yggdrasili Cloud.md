>## Yggdrasili Cloud
>*Huge Monstrosity, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|19 (+4)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Grappled, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Assimilation.*** A creature that drops to 0 Hit Points within 30 feet of the drone is subjected to the following effect. Assimilation can penetrate barriers, but 2 feet of rock, 2 inches of metal, a thin sheet of lead, or a barrier of force blocks it. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target becomes an **Yggdrasili Drone**. If the target is near multiple yggdrasili, it makes a single saving throw against the highest DC among them.  
>
>***Cloud.*** The cloud can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the cloud can move through any opening large enough for air to enter, but it can't enter or move through liquid.  
>
>***Psychic Static.*** Attempts to read the cloud's thoughts fail. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13, a creature that tries to read the cloud's thoughts. *Failure:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Psychic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The cloud uses Psychic Thrash twice.  
>
>***Psychic Thrash.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature the cloud can sense within 60 feet, plus one creature in the cloud's space. *Failure:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Psychic damage. *Failure by 5 or More:* The target has the Incapacitated condition and repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. The effect ends early if the creature takes damage or a creature takes an action to shake it from its stupor.
