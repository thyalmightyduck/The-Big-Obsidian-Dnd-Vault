___
>## Stygian Bat
>*Large Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d10 + 20)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|4 (-3)|13 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** The stygian bat doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The stygian bat makes one Bite attack and one Claw attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage. If the bat has Advantage on the attack, it deals an extra 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage, and the target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the bat flew 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition.
