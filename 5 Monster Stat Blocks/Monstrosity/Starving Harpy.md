___
>## Starving Harpy
>*Medium Monstrosity (Minion), typically Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 8
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 40; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Chorus of Death.*** If the starving harpy hears a harpy's Alluring Song, they can start harmonizing (no action required). The starving harpy continues harmonizing until the song ends, until they choose to stop harmonizing, or until they become deafened or incapacitated. For each starving harpy harmonizing, the range of the song increases by 50 feet, and the save DCs for the singing harpy's Alluring Song action and Beckon bonus action both increase by 1 (maximum DC increase of 5).  
>
>***Echolocation.*** The harpy can't use their blindsight while deafened.  
>
>***Minion.*** If the harpy takes damage from an attack or as the result of a failed saving throw, their hit points are reduced to 0. If the harpy takes damage from another effect, they die if the damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum; otherwise they take no damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Grasping Talons (Group Attack).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1 piercing damage, and if the target is a creature, the harpies can move them 5 feet in any direction. If the attack was made by three or more harpies, they can move the target 10 feet instead of 5.
