>## Body Snatcher
>*Medium Monstrosity (Shadowsteel Curse), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 123 (19d8 + 38)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Cha +3
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Deception +7, Stealth +8
>- **Immunities** Lightning
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** All languages known before the curse, and all languages known by its host
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Electric Invigoration.*** Whenever a Body Snatcher would take Lightning damage, it absorbs the energy and instead gains Temporary Hit Points equal to half the damage, rounded down. The Body Snatcher also immediately recharges its Electric Torrent ability.  
>
>***Skin Sack.*** The Body Snatcher is physically indistinguishable from its host and cannot be detected by nonmagical means. If the Body Snatcher is reduced to 0 Hit Points, and it has been inside its current host for less than 24 hours, the host regurgitates it then has the Unconscious condition and 1 Hit Point.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Body Snatcher makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Invade.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15; one Humanoid creature Grappled by the Body Snatcher. *Failure:* The Body Snatcher departs its current host and moves into the Grappled creature. It then regains all Hit Points and recharges Electric Torrent. If it had been inside its previous host for less than 24 hours, that creature has the Unconscious condition and 1 Hit Point. Otherwise, the previous host body becomes a lifeless skin sack.  
>
>***Electric Torrent (Recharge 5–6).*** The Body Snatcher unleashes a large electric pulse. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each other creature within 20 feet of the Body Snatcher. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Lightning damage. *Success:* Half damage.
