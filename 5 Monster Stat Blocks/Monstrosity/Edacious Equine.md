___
>## Edacious Equine
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 22 (3d10 + 6)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|2 (-4)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The edacious equine uses Three-Eyed Gaze and makes one Hooves attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Three-Eyed Gaze.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature within 30 feet that can see the edacious equine and be seen by it. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition until the end of the equine's next turn. While Charmed, the creature has the Restrained condition. *Success:* The target is immune to any equine's Three-Eyed Gaze for 24 hours.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Charmed Bite.*** If the edacious equine hits a Charmed target with a Hooves attack, it can make a Bite attack against the target as a Bonus Action.
