___
>## Vitebriate
>*Medium Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 52 (7d8 + 21)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Proficient in up to three skills from its past life
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** Languages it knew previously
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Unnatural Life.*** The vitebriate suffers none of the frailty of old age.  
>
>***Vitebriance.*** While on the same plane of existence as a creature Charmed by it, the vitebriate can reduce the target's Hit Point maximum by 1 each day. This reduction lasts until the Charmed condition ends and the target rests for 7 days without this reduction.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vitebriate makes two Dagger attacks.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Dominate Person.*** The vitebriate casts *Dominate Person* (save DC 13), requiring no components and using Charisma as its spellcasting ability. Once the vitebriate chooses a target, it can't target other creatures with *Dominate Person* for 24 hours. The spell doesn't require Concentration, but ends early if the vitebriate is killed or knocked Unconscious against its will (trancing or sleeping doesn't end the spell). If the target has been Charmed for at least 1 hour, it no longer repeats the save when it takes damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Life Shield (1/Day).*** *Trigger:* The vitebriate takes damage. *Response:* The triggering damage is instead dealt to a creature Charmed by the vitebriate. Any damage in excess of the creature's remaining Hit Points is dealt to the vitebriate.
