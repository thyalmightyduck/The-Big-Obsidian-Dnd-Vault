>## Fzeg
>*Medium Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 187 (22d8 + 88)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +14 (24)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Perception +14, Stealth +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 24
>- **Languages** Any four languages
>- **Challenge** 16 (XP 15,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The fzeg has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the fzeg fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The fzeg regains 20 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point. If the fzeg takes Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the fzeg's next turn.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The fzeg can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The fzeg uses its Howl, then makes one Bite attack and two Rend attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 17 (5d6) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. A Humanoid slain by the fzeg's Bite rises the following night as a fzeglaich under the fzeg's control.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Howl.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature within 300 feet of the fzeg that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. If a creature succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the fzeg's Howl for 24 hours or until it gets the Stunned condition from the fzeg.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The fzeg shape-shifts into a Small or Medium Humanoid or back into its true hybrid form. It can transform its body parts as part of its attacks and chooses whether the change remains after the attack. Other than its Size, its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the fzeg can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The fzeg regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Move.** The fzeg moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
>- **Claw.** The fzeg makes a Rend attack.
>- **Incite.** The fzeg chooses an ally with a Challenge Rating of 10 or lower it can see within 60 feet that has a Bite attack. If the target can see and hear the fzeg, it can use its Reaction to move up to half its Speed and make a Bite attack.
>- **Bite.** The fzeg makes a Bite attack. The fzeg can't use this Legendary Action again until the start of its next turn.
