___
>## Bloated Gastromorph
>*Gargantuan Monstrosity (Shadowsteel Curse), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 217 (14d20 + 70)
>- **Speed** 25 ft., Climb 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|7 (-2)|21 (+5)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +9, Con +9, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** All languages known before the curse
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The Bloated Gastromorph regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the Bloated Gastromorph takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function on the Bloated Gastromorph's next turn. The Bloated Gastromorph dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The Bloated Gastromorph can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Bloated Gastromorph makes three Claw attacks. It can replace one attack with a Tongue attack.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Slashing damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) and is moved up to 5 feet toward the Bloated Gastromorph.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 27 (4d10 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>If the Bloated Gastromorph takes 40 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, it regurgitates all swallowed creatures at the end of that turn, each of which falls in a space within 10 feet of the gastromorph and has the Prone condition. If the Bloated Gastromorph dies, any swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape from the corpse using 15 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.  
>
>***Tongue (Recharge 5–6).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 25 ft., one target. *Hit:* 31 (4d12 + 5) Acid damage. A creature that is hit by this attack is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target has the Paralyzed condition and is moved up to 20 feet toward the gastromorph. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Swallow.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17, one Large or smaller creature Grappled by the gastromorph (it can have up to two creatures swallowed at a time). *Failure:* The target is swallowed by the gastromorph, and the Grappled condition ends. A swallowed creature has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the gastromorph, and taks 21 (6d6) Acid damage at the start of each of the gastromorph's turns.
