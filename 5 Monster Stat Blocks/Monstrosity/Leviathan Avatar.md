___
>## Leviathan Avatar
>*Huge Monstrosity, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 + the spell's level
>- **Hit Points** 70 + 10 for each spell slot level above 7
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Swim 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|6 (-2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands the languages you know
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0)
>
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The spirit can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The spirit makes a number of Rend attacks equal to half this spell's level (round down).  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  your spell attack modifier to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d10 + 5 + summonSpellLevel Piercing damage.  
>
>***Roar.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC equals your spell save DC, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the start of your next turn. *Success:* No effect.
