___
>## Ratling Warrior
>*Small Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 5 (2d6 - 2)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|15 (+2)|8 (-1)|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +6
>- **Gear** *Pouch*, ten *Sling Bullets*, *Sling*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The ratling has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The ratling has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the ratling's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Sling.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Ratling Cunning.*** The ratling takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
