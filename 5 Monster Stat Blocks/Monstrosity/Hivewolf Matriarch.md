>## Hivewolf Matriarch
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 45 (6d10 + 12)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|9 (-1)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Hive Connection.*** The hivewolf matriarch is connected to other hivewolves within 300 feet by a hivemind, which they use to share information. If the hivewolf matriarch is aware of a threat, all hivewolves within 300 feet are also aware of the threat. This effect can penetrate barriers, but 2 feet of rock, 2 inches of any metal other than lead, or a thin sheet of lead or barrier of force blocks it.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The hivewolf matriarch has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the hivewolf matriarch's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) Acid damage.  
>
>***Acid Spray (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Acid damage. *Success:* Half damage.
