___
>## Hag-Blighted
>*Medium Monstrosity, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|9 (-1)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** The languages known before the curse
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Immutable Form.*** The hag-blighted has Immunity to any spell or effect that would alter its form.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hag-blighted makes one Bite attack and one Slam attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Spew Bile (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Acid damage. *Success:* Half damage.
