___
>## Glacier Bulette
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 103 (9d10 + 54)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|11 (+0)|22 (+6)|2 (-4)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Ice Walk.*** The bulette can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check. Additionally, Difficult Terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost it extra movement.  
>
>***Steam Aura.*** At the end of each of the bulette's turns, each creature of the bulette's choice in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the bulette takes 5 (1d10) Fire damage unless the bulette has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Tunneler.*** The bulette can burrow through solid ice and leaves a slick, 10-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The bulette makes two Bite attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d12 + 4) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Steam Eruption (Recharge 5–6).*** If the bulette is burrowed no more than 20 feet deep in solid ice, it spends 5 feet of movement to burrow directly upward, emerging from the ice and jumping to a space within 5 feet of where it emerged. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in the space where the bulette emerged or its destination space. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Fire damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The target is pushed to the nearest unoccupied space within 5 feet of where the bulette emerged or its destination space.
