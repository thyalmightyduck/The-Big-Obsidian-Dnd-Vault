>## Plague Carrier
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 45 (7d8)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Coughing Fits.*** The target is unable to speak until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Fatigue.*** The target's Speed is reduced by 5 feet until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Plague Symptoms.*** The plague carrier is host to a disease created by a pathogenist, chosen from the options below. A creature infected by this disease is subjected to the chosen effect at the start of each of its turns. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage, and the target suffers the disease's effect. Third Success: The disease ends.  
>
>***Shivers.*** The target shakes uncontrollably. Each time it fails its saving throw, it drops whatever it is holding.  
>
>### Actions
>***Festering Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +2, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d8) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target is infected with the plague carrier's Plague Symptoms.  
>
>***Bile Spreader.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage, and the target is infected with the plague carrier's Plague Symptoms. *Success:* Half damage only.
