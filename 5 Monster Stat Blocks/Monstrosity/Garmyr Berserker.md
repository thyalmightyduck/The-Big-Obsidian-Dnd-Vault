>## Garmyr Berserker
>*Large Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 114 (12d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|5 (-3)|13 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Perception +8
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** two *Battleaxes*, *Half Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Reckless.*** At the start of its turn, the garmyr can gain Advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against it have Advantage until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Scent of Blood.*** The garmyr's melee attacks deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a Bloodied creature.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The garmyr makes three Battleaxe attacks. It can also Howl, if available.  
>
>***Battleaxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it is pushed up to 10 feet straight away from the garmyr. Critical *Hit:* The target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Howl (Recharge 6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, each Hostile creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the garmyr that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition (save ends).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The garmyr takes the Dash action to move toward a Hostile creature it can see.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Retaliate.*** *Trigger:* A creature hits the garmyr with a melee attack. *Response:* The garmyr makes a Battleaxe attack against that creature.
