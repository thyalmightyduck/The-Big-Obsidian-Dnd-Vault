>## Lupine
>*Large Monstrosity, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 104 (11d10 + 44)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Con +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Perception +6, Survival +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Gear** *Battleaxe*, *Longbow*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 90 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common (can't speak in wolf form), Primordial
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Rage.*** The lupine can give itself Advantage on its first D20 Test each turn. If it does so and fails that D20 Test, it's overcome with rage. On each of its turns while raging, the lupine attacks the nearest creature it can see. If no creature is near enough to move to and attack, its rage ends. A lupine's ally, if within 60 feet of the raging lupine, can try to calm it by taking an action to make a DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check; the lupine must be able to hear its ally. If this check succeeds, the lupine ceases its rage.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The lupine regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the lupine takes Fire damage or damage from silver objects, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn. The lupine dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lupine makes two attacks, using Battleaxe, Rend, or Longbow in any combination. If it hits with a Rend attack, the lupine can make one Bite attack against the same target as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Battleaxe (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (1d10 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Bite (Wolf or Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Rend (Wolf or Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Longbow (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Horrifying Howl (Recharge 6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature within 60 feet that can hear the howl and isn't a lupine. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition and repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The lupine shape-shifts into a Large wolf-humanoid hybrid form or a Medium wolf, or it returns to its true humanoid form. Its game statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.
