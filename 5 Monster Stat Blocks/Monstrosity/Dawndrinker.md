>## Dawndrinker
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 95 (10d10 + 40)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|19 (+4)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire
>- **Immunities** Radiant
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Darkvision 240 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Light Sense.*** The dawndrinker can sense any light source within 120 feet. This sense penetrates most barriers, but it is blocked by 3 feet of wood or dirt, 1 foot of stone, or 1 inch of metal.  
>
>***Radiant Absorption.*** When the dawndrinker would take Radiant damage, it instead takes no damage and regains Hit Points equal to the Radiant damage dealt.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dawndrinker makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Cold damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition until the end of the dawndrinker's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Drink Light.*** The dawndrinker drinks light, extinguishing nonmagical light sources within 30 feet. Magical lights in the area created by spells of level 3 or lower, or by creatures of CR 7 or lower, are dimmed: Bright Light is reduced to Dim Light, and Dim Light is extinguished. An extinguished magical light is dispelled, or suppressed for 1 minute if the light source is continuous.  
>The dawndrinker can extinguished stronger magical lights by succeeding on a Constitution check with a DC of 10 plus the spell's level or 5 plus the creature's CR, whichever is lower.  
>
>***Shadow Cunning.*** In Dim Light or Darkness, the dawndrinker can take the Disengage or Hide action as a Bonus Action.
