___
>## Spell Leech
>*Tiny Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 20 (8d4)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Aquatic Camouflage.*** The leech has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in aquatic terrain.  
>
>***Spell Glands.*** The leech has a spell gland, which can store up to ten levels of spells. When encountered, roll 1d10 to determine how many spell levels it has stored.  
>
>### Actions
>***Attach.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* The leech attaches to the target. While attached, the leech doesn't attack, and moves with the target, using none of the leech's movement. The leech can detach itself using 5 feet of movement, and detaches immediately if its blood bags are filled or if the target dies.  
>A creature within 5 feet can take an action to detach the leech with a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check. *Failure:* The leech is still detached, but the target has the Bleeding condition (2d4). Failure by 5 or More: The leech isn't detached and the target takes 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Spell Drain.*** The leech can use this Bonus Action only when it is attached to a creature. The attached creature is subjected to the following effect. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Bewildered condition until the start of the leech's next turn. If the target has spell slots, the leech drains one randomly determined spell slot which can be no higher than level 3. It then fills its spell glands with the same number of spell levels.  
>
>***Arcane Transfusion.*** The leech can use this Bonus Action only when it is attached to a creature and has at least one level worth of spells in its Spell Glands. The leech empties any number of spell levels from its Spell Glands, and the creature it is attached to regains an equal number of spell levels worth of spell slots.
