>## Merrow Haranguer
>*Medium Monstrosity, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Performance +7, Persuasion +7
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common, Primordial (Aquan)
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The merrow can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The merrow makes three attacks, using Coral Scepter and Poisoned Longbow in any combination.  
>
>***Coral Scepter.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Poisoned Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Invective.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, up to three creatures the merrow can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage, and the creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of the merrow's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
