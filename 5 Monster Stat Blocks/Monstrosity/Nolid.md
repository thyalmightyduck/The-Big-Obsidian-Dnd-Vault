>## Nolid
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d10 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|2 (-4)|11 (+0)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +3
>- **Immunities** Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The nolid has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.  
>
>***Painkiller.*** While the nolid is Poisoned, it gains Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The nolid uses its Startling Howl, then makes one Bite attack and one Claws attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Startling Howl.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 30- foot Emanation originating from the nolid that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the end of the nolid's next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to the Startling Howl of all nolids for 24 hours.
