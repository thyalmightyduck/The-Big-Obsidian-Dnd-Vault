>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___

>- **Hit Points** 66 (12d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___

>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|5 (-3)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|

>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skretch makes two attacks.  
>
>***Beak.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spittle.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 120/300 ft., one target directly underneath the skretch. *Hit:* 15 (6d4) Acid damage.  
>
>***Expel Waste (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 10-foot radius Sphere directly beneath the skretch. *Failure:* 20 (8d4) Acid damage, and the target has the Burning condition (4d4 Acid). Each time the target takes damage from this condition, it must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or gain one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.
