>## Hungerer Snake
>*Small Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 27 (5d6 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|2 (-4)|12 (+1)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +5
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Blood Frenzy.*** The hungerer snake has Advantage on melee attack rolls against a creature missing any of its Hit Points.  
>
>***Tears of the Hungerer.*** A Beast or Humanoid that takes damage from the hungerer elk is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target contracts a magical disease called Tears of the Hungerer. *Success:* The target is immune to the disease for 24 hours.  
>An infected target decreases its Hit Point maximum by 2 (1d4) every 24 hours. If this effect reduces its Hit Point maximum to 0, the target dies and becomes a **Zombie** with this trait. Tears of the Hungerer can only be cured by magic.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, the target. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Tail Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The hungerer snake moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see.
