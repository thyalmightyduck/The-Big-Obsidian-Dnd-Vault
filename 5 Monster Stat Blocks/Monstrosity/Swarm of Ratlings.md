___
>## Swarm of Ratlings
>*Huge swarm of Small Monstrosities*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 100 (20d10 - 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 15 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|17 (+3)|8 (-1)|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Chittering Mass.*** The swarm has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature in its space.  
>
>***Scampering Survivors.*** When the swarm is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it leaves behind 1d4 **Ratling Warriors** in its space.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Small ratling. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The swarm makes one Bite attack against each creature in its space.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 28 (8d6) Piercing damage, or 14 (4d6) Piercing damage if the swarm has the Bloodied condition. If the target is a creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Scurrying Horde.*** The swarm takes the Dash action.
