___
>## Verminous Abomination
>*Large Monstrosity (Shadowsteel Curse), Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 120 (16d10 + 32)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +6, Con +5
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +7
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** All languages known before the curse
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Tunneler.*** The Verminous Abomination can burrow through solid rock at half its Speed and leaves a 10-foot-wide, 10-foot-high tunnel in its wake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Verminous Abomination makes two attacks: one with its Bite and one with its Claws.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Poison damage and have the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Birth Ratlings (Recharge 5–6).*** The Verminous Abomination spawns 1d4+2 **Swarm of Rats** within 5 feet of it. The swarms may immediately move and use actions. On later turns, they move and take actions on the same Initiative as the Verminous Abomination.
