___
>## Awakened Chapped Brute
>*Large Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 126 (12d10 + 60)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Perception +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Blinded
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Understands one language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The chapped brute has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Sure-Footed.*** The chapped brute has Advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made to avoid or end the Prone condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The chapped brute makes two Tentacle attacks.  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition, and the chapped brute can't use that tentacle against another target. The chapped brute has two -tentacles, each of which can grapple one target.  
>
>***Tentacle Slam.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16, one or two creatures Grappled by the chapped brute. *Failure:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of the chapped brute's next turn. *Success:* Half damage.
