___
>## Ratling Guttersnipe
>*Small Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 35 (10d6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Int +3
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Perception +4, Stealth +7
>- **Gear** *Hand Crossbow*, *Quiver*, ten *Bolts*, *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The ratling has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The ratling has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the ratling's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Contaminated Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage. Failure by 5 or More: The target also gains one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Hand Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Ratling Cunning.*** The ratling takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
