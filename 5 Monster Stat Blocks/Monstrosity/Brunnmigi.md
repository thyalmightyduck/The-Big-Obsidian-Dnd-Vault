___
>## Brunnmigi
>*Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 22 (3d10 + 6)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +3
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** passive Perception 14, Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands Common and the Northern Tongue but cannot speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The brunnmigi makes two Claw attacks.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage plus 2 Poison damage.  
>
>***Befouled Spittle.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Poison damage, and the target is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. The target can make a save whenever it completes a Long Rest to end this condition.
