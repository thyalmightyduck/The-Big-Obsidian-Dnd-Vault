___
>## Chiropteran Behemoth
>*Huge Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 21
>- **Hit Points** 287 (25d12 + 125)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 120 ft.
>- **Initiative** +17 (27)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|5 (-3)|20 (+5)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +11, Stealth +17
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Understands one language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 20 (XP 25,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Flyby.*** The behemoth doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks while it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If the chiropteran behemoth fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The behemoth has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Potent Weapons.*** The behemoth's attacks ignore Resistances.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The behemoth gains 20 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the behemoth is exposed to an Undoing Elixir, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn. The behemoth dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The behemoth makes a Bite attack and two Claw attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 33 (6d8 + 6) Force damage. If the behemoth has Advantage on the attack roll, it deals an extra 17 (5d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 20 (4d6 + 6) Force damage. Critical Hit: 48 (12d6 + 6) Force damage, and if the target dies, its head or another vital body part is severed or destroyed. If the behemoth moved at least 20 feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Shriek (Recharge 6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 19, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 45 (7d12) Psychic damage, and the target has the Paralyzed condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Darkness Aura.*** The behemoth creates magical darkness in a 40-foot radius Emanation until the start of its next turn. A creature with Darkvision can't see through this darkness and nonmagical light can't illuminate it. A light-producing spell of level 5 or lower is dispelled if its area overlaps the darkness's area.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the chiropteran behemoth can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The chiropteran behemoth regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Detect.** The chiropteran behemoth makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
>- **Claw.** The behemoth makes a Claw attack.
>- **Swoop.** The behemoth moves up to half its Speed and makes a Claw attack. The behemoth can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Wing Buffet.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 15-foot Emanation originating from the behemoth. *Failure:* 20 (4d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away. *Failure by 5 or More:* The target also has the Prone condition.*Failure or Success:* The behemoth flies up to half its Flying Speed, and can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
