>## Uncontained Growth
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d8 + 56)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|11 (+0)|19 (+4)|1 (-5)|7 (-2)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +7
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Absorbing Flesh.*** A creature reduced to 0 Hit Points by the growth is absorbed into its body and dies. When it absorbs a creature, the growth's size increases by one category. If the growth finishes a Long Rest and hasn't absorbed a body since its last Long Rest, its size decreases by one category.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The growth makes a number of attacks based on its size: two (Medium), three (Large), four (Huge) or five (Gargantuan).  
>
>***Assimilating Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Necrotic damage, and if the target is smaller than the growth, it is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target is swallowed by the growth. A swallowed creature has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover from attacks and effects outside the growth, and takes 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage and the start of each of the growth's turns. The growth regains Hit Points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage dealt, and excess healing increases its Hit Point Maximum.  
>If the growth takes 20 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the growth must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which land with the Prone condition in unoccupied spaces within 10 feet of the growth. If the growth dies, a swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape from the corpse using 20 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Adaptable Flesh.*** *Trigger:* The growth takes damage from a spell or attack. *Response:* The growth halves the damage it takes from the triggering spell or attack.
