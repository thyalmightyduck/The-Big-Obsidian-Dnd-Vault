___
>## Rat Prince
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 66 (12d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|19 (+4)|13 (+1)|9 (-1)|15 (+2)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Athletics +5, Perception +6, Stealth +8
>- **Gear** *Hand Crossbow*, *Leather Armor*, *Quiver*, ten *Bolts*, two *Shortswords*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The Rat Prince has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The Rat Prince has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the Rat Prince's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Rat Prince makes three attacks: two with his Shortswords and one with his Contaminated Bite.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Contaminated Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level.  
>
>***Hand Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Ratling Cunning.*** The Rat Prince takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sacrificial Pawn.*** *Trigger:* The Rat Prince is targeted by an attack or spell that targets only him. *Response:* The Rat Prince redirects the attack or spell to another ratling within 5 feet of him, causing the ratling to become the target instead.
