___
>## Malrgotr
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 68 (8d10 + 24)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|11 (+0)|17 (+3)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Frightened, Stunned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Bristles.*** A creature that hits the malrgotr with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 4 (1d8) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The malrgotr makes one Gore attack and one Hooves attack.  
>
>***Gore.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Glorious Flare (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13 All creatures in a 15-foot radius around the malrgotr. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Radiant damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn.
