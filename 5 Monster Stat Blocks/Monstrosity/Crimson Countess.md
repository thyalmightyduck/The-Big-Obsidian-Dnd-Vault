___
>## Crimson Countess
>*Medium Monstrosity (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12) plus 39 (6d8 + 12) per player character
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|21 (+5)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|11 (+0)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +8, Perception +6
>- **Immunities** Lightning, Thunder
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of Storms.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the Crimson Countess takes 10 (3d6) Lightning damage.  
>
>***Pluck.*** The Crimson Countess doesn't need to spend extra movement to drag a Medium or smaller creature Grappled by it.  
>
>### Actions
>***The Crimson Countess always takes their turn on Initiative count 20.*** On their turn, the Crimson Countess can use their movement and interaction as normal. However, they do not take an action or Bonus Action on their turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the Crimson Countess takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Grasping Talons.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d4 + 5) Piercing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15).
>- **Lightning Javelin.** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Lightning damage.
>
>***Aerial Acrobat.*** The Crimson Countess flies up to her Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Frightful Screech (1/Round).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature within 120 feet of the Crimson Countess that can hear them. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition (save ends). While Frightened, the target must take the Dash action and move away from the Crimson Countess by the safest available route on each of its turns unless there is nowhere to move.  
>
>***Lightning Strike (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creatures in a 60-foot long, 5-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Lightning damage, and the target has the Shocked condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The Crimson Countess chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on them. The Crimson Countess can use this action even if they would otherwise be unable to take actions, their actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters their game statistics.
