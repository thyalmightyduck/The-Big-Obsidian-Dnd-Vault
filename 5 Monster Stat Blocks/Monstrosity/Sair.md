___
>## Sair
>*Medium Monstrosity (Artillery), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Int +6
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Perception +2, Survival +2
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** Sair doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when they fly out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.  
>
>***Psionic Bolt (1st-Order Power).*** *Ranged Power Attack:*  +6 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) psychic damage. If the target is Large or smaller, they are pushed 5 feet away from Sair.  
>
>****Mind Scream (1/Day; 3rd-Order Power).*** Sair psionically pierces the mind of each enemy within 30 feet of them. Each target must make a DC 14 Intelligence saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) psychic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature reduced to 0 hit points by this damage is automatically stabilized.
