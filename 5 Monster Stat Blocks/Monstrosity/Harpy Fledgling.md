>## Harpy Fledgling
>*Medium Monstrosity (Harpy, Retainer); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (light armor)
>- **Hit Points** Seven times their level (number of d8 Hit Dice equal to their level)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str 3+PB, Dex +PB, Con 1+PB, Int +PB, Wis +PB, Cha 1+PB
>- **Skills** Athletics +3 plus PB, Performance +1 plus PB
>- **Senses** Blindsight 15 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals the mentor's bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals the mentor's bonus
>___
>***Echolocation.*** The harpy can't use their blindsight while deafened.  
>
>### Actions
>***Signature Attack (Talons).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  3 plus PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2d4 plus PB slashing damage. Beginning at 7th level, the fledgling can make this attack twice, instead of once, when they take the Attack action on their turn.  
>
>***3rd Level: Wind Sweep (3/Day).*** As a bonus action, the fledgling moves up to their flying speed without provoking opportunity attacks. If they end this movement within 5 feet of an enemy they can see, the fledgling can make a signature attack against them.  
>
>***5th Level: Food Grab (3/Day).*** When the fledgling hits a Medium or smaller creature with a signature attack, the target is also grappled (escape DC 10 plus PB). The fledging then moves up to their fly speed without provoking opportunity attacks (no action required). While grappling the target, the fledgling can't attack another creature.  
>
>***7th Level: Harpy Song (1/Day).*** As an action, the fledgling starts singing for 1 minute. Each creature within 60 feet of the fledgling who can hear them and who understands Common or Giant must succeed on a DC 10 plus PB Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by the fledgling until they can't hear the fledgling singing anymore. While charmed in this way, a creature can't willingly harm any of the fledgling's allies.
