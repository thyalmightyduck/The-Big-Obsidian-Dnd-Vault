___
>## Garmyr Warrior
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 22 (4d8 + 4)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|13 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Gear** *Hide Armor*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The garmyr has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the garmyr's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The garmyr makes two attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage, or 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage if wielded with two hands to make a melee attack.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Feast.*** The garmyr makes a Bite attack against a Bloodied target.
