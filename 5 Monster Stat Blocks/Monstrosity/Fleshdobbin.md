___
>## Fleshdobbin
>*Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 104 (11d10 + 44)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|7 (-2)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Intimidation +6, Perception +5
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified
>- **Gear** *Chain Mail*, *Glaive*
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Bridle Binding.*** If a creature puts a bridle on the fleshdobbin (treat as securing *Manacles*), the fleshdobbin has the Charmed condition while it wears the bridle, and it can't take the bridle off on its own.  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** The fleshdobbin can't shape-shift.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The fleshdobbin makes one Claw attack and one Wailing Halberd attack.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Wailing Halberd.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 10 (1d10 + 5) Slashing damage plus 5 (1d10) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Shriek (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Psychic damage. In addition, the target has the Frightened condition and repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* A creature the fleshdobbin can see hits it with a melee attack roll while the fleshdobbin is wielding a Melee weapon. *Response:* The fleshdobbin adds 3 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
