___
>## Ithjar
>*Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 90 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|20 (+5)|17 (+3)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +8, Perception +6
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Slippery Serpent.*** The ithjar has Advantage on ability checks and saving throws to avoid or end the Grappled, Paralyzed, or Restrained conditions.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ithjar makes two Bite attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Tail.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the ithjar can't make Tail attacks against other creatures.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Writhing Escape.*** *Trigger:* The ithjar becomes Grappled. *Response:* The ithjar makes a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check to escape.
