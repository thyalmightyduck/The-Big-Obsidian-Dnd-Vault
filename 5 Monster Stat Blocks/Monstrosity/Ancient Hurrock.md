___
>## Ancient Hurrock
>*Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 168 (16d10 + 80)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Burrow 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|7 (-2)|20 (+5)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +9
>- **Skills** Perception +8
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Thunder
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't adamantine
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Tremorsense 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Understands Dwarvish but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Harrowed Tunneler.*** The ancient hurrock can burrow through solid rock at its Borrow Speed and leaves a 6-foot diameter tunnel in its wake. The tunnel is Difficult Terrain.  
>
>***Rubble Walker.*** Difficult Terrain composed of rock, rubble, or similar debris doesn't cost the ancient hurrock extra movement.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The ancient hurrock deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ancient hurrock makes one Beard attack and two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Beard.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17). When a creature escapes the ancient hurrock's beard, it takes 9 (2d8) Piercing damage. The ancient hurrock can grapple up to two creatures with its beard, but once its beard is full, it can only make Beard attacks against those creatures.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If the ancient hurrock moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) Bludgeoning damage and, if it is Large or smaller, has the Prone condition.
