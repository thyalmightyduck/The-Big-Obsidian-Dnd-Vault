>## Weremammoth
>*Small or Medium Monstrosity (Lycanthrope), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 133 (14d8 + 70)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., 40 ft. (hybrid form only), 50 ft. (mammoth form only)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|24 (+7)|10 (+0)|21 (+5)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Cha +0
>- **Skills** Perception +7
>- **Gear** three *Spears +7s*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common (can't speak in mammoth form)
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Adapted.*** The weremammoth can tolerate temperatures of 0 degrees Fahrenheit or lower without being subjected to the effects of extreme cold.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The weremammoth makes three attacks, using Spear or Trample in any combination. It can replace one attack with a Gore attack.  
>
>***Gore (Mammoth or Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d8 + 7) Piercing damage. If the target is a Humanoid, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target is cursed. If the cursed target drops to 0 Hit Points, it instead becomes a Weremammoth under the GM's control and has 10 Hit Points. *Success:* The target is immune to this weremammoth's curse for 24 hours.  
>
>***Spear (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d6 + 7) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Trample (Mammoth or Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (2d10 + 7) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the weremammoth moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shape-Shift.*** The werewolf shape-shifts into a Large mammoth-humanoid hybrid or a Huge mammoth, or it returns to its true humanoid form. Its game statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.
