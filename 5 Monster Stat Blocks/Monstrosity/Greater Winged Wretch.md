>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The winged wretch makes three Rend attacks.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Thunderous Shriek (Recharge 5–6).*** The winged wretch emits a cacophonous wave of energy. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 17 (5d6) Thunder damage, and the target has the Deafened condition for 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only.
