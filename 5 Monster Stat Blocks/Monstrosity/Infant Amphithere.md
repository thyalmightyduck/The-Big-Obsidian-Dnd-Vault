___
>## Infant Amphithere
>*Small Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 22 (5d6 + 5)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 40 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Perception +2
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The amphithere can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) poison damage.  
>
>***Poison Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** The young amphithere exhales a blast of stinging venom in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature reduced to 0 hit points by this attack remains stable but is poisoned for 1 hour, even after regaining hit points. If a creature has total cover from the young amphithere, it is not affected by the breath attack.
