>## Yggdrasili Drone
>*Medium Monstrosity, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 18 (4d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +2
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Perception +2
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Lightning
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Senses** Blindsight 15 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Assimilation.*** A creature that drops to 0 Hit Points within 15 feet of the drone is subjected to the following effect. Assimilation can penetrate barriers, but 2 feet of rock, 2 inches of metal, a thin sheet of lead, or a barrier of force blocks it. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target becomes an **Yggdrasili Drone**. If the target is near multiple yggdrasili, it makes a single saving throw against the highest DC among them.  
>
>***Psychic Feedback.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the drone when it takes Psychic damage. *Failure:* The target takes the same amount of Psychic damage. If the target is near multiple yggdrasili, it makes a single saving throw against the highest DC among them and takes only the highest amount of Psychic damage.  
>
>***Psychic Static.*** Attempts to read the drone's thoughts fail. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13, a creature that tries to read the drone's thoughts. *Failure:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Psychic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Psychic Lash.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature the drone can sense within 30 feet. *Failure:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Psychic damage.
