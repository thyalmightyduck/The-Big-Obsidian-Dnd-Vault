>## Rat Crown-Prince
>*Medium Monstrosity (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 45 (6d8 + 24) plus 45 (6d8 + 24) per player character
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|23 (+6)|16 (+3)|9 (-1)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +14, Athletics +10, Perception +11, Stealth +14
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The Rat Crown-Prince has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Verminous Aura.*** The Rat Crown-Prince emits a 30-foot Emanation of verminous contamination. A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***The Rat Crown-Prince always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On his turn, The Rat Crown Prince can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the Rat Crown-Prince takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Contaminated Bite (1/Round).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d4 + 6) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level.  
>
>***Delerium-Infused Blades.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. or range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Necrotic damage, and the target suffers one of the following effects of the Rat Crown-Prince's choice (he can't select the same option more than once per round):  
>- **Poison.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Acid Burn.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Acid damage, and the target has the Burning condition (3d6 Acid). *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Mindkiller.** If the target is maintaining Concentration on a spell, it takes an extra 25 (5d10) Psychic damage unless it chooses to end its Concentration.
>
>***Call Ratlings (1/Round).*** 2d4 **Ratling Warriors** appear, burrowing up from below unoccupied spaces within 60 feet of the Rat Crown-Prince. The warriors have 1 Hit Point each and remain until killed.  
>
>***Meld into Shadow (1/Round).*** The Rat Crown-Prince has the Invisible condition until he makes an attack.  
>
>***Swift Stride.*** The Rat Crown-Prince moves up to his Speed.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The Rat Crown-Prince chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on him. The Rat Crown-Prince can use this action even if he would otherwise be unable to take actions, his actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters his game statistics.
