>## Groundling Grunt
>*Medium Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 45 (7d8 + 14)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +2, Stealth +4, Survival +2
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Gear** *Sling*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13) from one of two paws.  
>
>***Sling.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Go to Ground.*** *Trigger:* The groundling is hit by an attack roll while it is on the ground. *Response:* The groundling halves the damage (round down) it takes from that attack.
