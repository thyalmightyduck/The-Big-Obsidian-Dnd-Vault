___
>## Blood Tracker
>*Large Monstrosity, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 95 (10d10 + 40)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|13 (+1)|18 (+4)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +9, Survival +9
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Understands one language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blood Master.*** The blood tracker can be bound to an Evil creature by eating the creature's blood daily for 13 days. While the master is within 100 feet of the blood tracker, the two can communicate telepathically. As an action while the blood tracker is within 300 feet, the master can use the tracker's senses until the start of the master's next turn. The master has the Blinded and Deafened conditions during this time.  
>
>***Blood Tracker.*** The blood tracker has Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track creatures with blood. If such a creature is missing any of its Hit Points, magic can't prevent the blood tracker from tracking it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** blood tracker makes one Bite attack and uses Blood Bay.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Blood Bay.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 60 feet (with Disadvantage if it is missing any Hit Points). *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Thunder damage, or 19 (3d12) Thunder damage if the target is missing any Hit Points, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of the blood tracker's next turn. While Frightened, the target's Speed is 0 and can't increase.
