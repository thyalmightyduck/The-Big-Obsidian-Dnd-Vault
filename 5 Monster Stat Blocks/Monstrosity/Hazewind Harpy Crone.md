>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)

>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|

>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Perception +6

>### Actions
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Crone Songs.*** The harpy sings a magical melody in a 60-foot Emanation, choosing one of the following effects. Allies of the Crone are unaffected.  
>
>***Breakneck Hymn.*** Each creature in the Emanation that can hear the harpy has Vulnerability to Bludgeoning damage from falling until the start of the harpy's next turn.  
>
>***Disarming Tune.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in the Emanation that can hear the harpy. *Failure:* The target has the Weakened condition until the start of the harpy's next turn.  
>
>***Mesmerizing Melody.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in the Emanation that can hear the harpy. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the start of the harpy's next turn.  
>
>***Piercing Notes.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in the Emanation that can hear the harpy. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Thunder damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aerial Maneuver.*** The harpy takes the Dash or Disengage action.
