___
>## Bloated Hive
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 82 (11d8 + 33)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Frightened
>- **Senses** Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Disrupt the Hive.*** When the bloated hive dies, it immediately spawns a **Swarm of Contaminated Insects** in its space.  
>
>***Insect Swarm.*** The bloated hive emits a 10-foot Emanation of buzzing insects. A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Bloated Hive makes two attacks.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Spawn Swarm (Recharge 5–6).*** The bloated hive creates a **Swarm of Contaminated Insects**, which appears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet. The swarm takes its turn immediately after the bloated hive.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Distracting Swarm.*** The bloated hive causes its Insect Swarm to fly in the face of a creature within 5 feet of it. The target can't take Reactions until the end of the bloated hive's turn.
