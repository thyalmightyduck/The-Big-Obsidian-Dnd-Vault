___
>## Styrkvisnar
>*Medium Monstrosity, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 110 (13d8 + 52)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 40 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +5
>- **Skills** Deception +3, Perception +8, Stealth +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Any three languages (can't speak in Hybrid Form)
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Adaptable Warrior.*** The styrkvisnar has proficiency with all weapons.  
>
>***Hold Breath.*** The styrkvisnar can hold its breath for 30 minutes.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (1/Day).*** If the styrkvisnar fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The styrkvisnar makes three attacks, using Bite, Talons, Tail, or Unarmed Strikes in any combination.  
>
>***Bite (Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Tail (Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Talons (Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Thunderous Roar (Recharge 6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Thunder damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The roar is audible over 1 mile.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The styrkvisnar shape-shifts into a Medium Humanoid or back into its true form, which is a hybrid of eagle, seal, and wolf. Its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the styrkvisnar can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The styrkvisnar regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Attack.** The styrkvisnar uses Bite, Tail, Talon, or Unarmed Strike.
>- **Move.** The styrkvisnar moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
