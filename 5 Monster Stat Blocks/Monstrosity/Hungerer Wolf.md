>## Hungerer Wolf
>*Medium Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 16 (3d8 + 3)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___

>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +4
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The hungerer wolf has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Tears of the Hungerer.*** A Beast or Humanoid that takes damage from the hungerer elk is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target contracts a magical disease called Tears of the Hungerer. *Success:* The target is immune to the disease for 24 hours.  
>An infected target decreases its Hit Point maximum by 2 (1d4) every 24 hours. If this effect reduces its Hit Point maximum to 0, the target dies and becomes a **Zombie** with this trait. Tears of the Hungerer can only be cured by magic.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Piercing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The hungerer wolf moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see.
