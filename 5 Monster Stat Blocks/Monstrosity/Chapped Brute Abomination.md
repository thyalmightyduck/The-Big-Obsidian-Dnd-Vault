___
>## Chapped Brute Abomination
>*Huge Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 161 (14d12 + 70)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +10, Perception +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 90 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Adaptive Hide.*** After taking damage, the chapped brute gains Resistance to that type of damage until it takes another type of damage.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The chapped brute has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Unstable Life.*** At the start of the chapped brute's turn, roll 1d20. The abomination regains Hit Points equal to any even result and loses Hit Points equal to any odd result. This trait can't reduce the chapped brute below 1 Hit Point.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The chapped brute makes two Tentacle attacks.  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition, and the chapped brute can't use that tentacle against another target. The chapped brute has four tentacles, each of which can grapple one target.  
>
>***Engulf.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature Grappled by the chapped brute. *Failure:* The grapple ends and the target is pulled into the chapped brute's body. The target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, is suffocating, and has Total Cover from effects that originate outside the chapped brute. At the start of each of the chapped brute's turns, the target takes 14 (4d6) Acid damage. The chapped brute can have one Large creature or up to four Medium or smaller creatures engulfed at a time.  
>The target can escape the chapped brute by taking an action and succeeding on a DC 18 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. Escaping the chapped brute costs 10 feet of movement and ejects the target into a space of its choice within 5 feet of the chapped brute. If the chapped brute dies, the target no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape without using an action or making a check.  
>If the target dies while engulfed, the chapped brute doesn't roll for Unstable Life at the start of its next turn, instead regaining 20 Hit Points. The targets body breaks up inside the chapped brute and everything the creature was wearing or carrying is expelled.
