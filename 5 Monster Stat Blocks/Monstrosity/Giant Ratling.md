___
>## Giant Ratling
>*Large Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The giant ratling has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The giant ratling makes two Claw attacks and one Bite attack. It can replace up to two attacks with uses of Hurl Ratling.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage. Critical *Hit:* The target has the Staggered condition until the start of the giant ratling's next turn.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d12 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Hurl Ratling.*** The giant ratling picks up a willing Medium or smaller ratling ally or corpse within 5 feet. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature the giant ratling can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The hurled ratling takes the same damage and lands with the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Enrage (Recharge 5–6).*** The giant ratling gains 15 Temporary Hit Points. Until the Temporary Hit Points are lost, the giant ratling has Advantage on Strength-based D20 Tests.
