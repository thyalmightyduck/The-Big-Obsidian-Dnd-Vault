___
>## The Jinxed Leviathan
>*Gargantuan Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 21
>- **Hit Points** 437 (25d20 + 175)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Swim 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +16 (26)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|14 (+2)|25 (+7)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|21 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +17, Con +14, Wis +8
>- **Skills** Perception +8, Stealth +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Force, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Understands Draconic but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 23 (XP 50,000, or 62,000 in lair; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The leviathan can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day, or 5/Day in Lair).*** If the leviathan fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Returned Curse.*** If the leviathan curses a creature holding an idol of Snake Eye Jack blessed by one of his devoted followers, the creature is unaffected by the curse. The leviathan then takes 20 Radiant damage and has Disadvantage on D20 Tests until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The leviathan makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +17, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 23 (2d12 + 10) Piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) Force damage. The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 20), and until the grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +17, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 23 (3d8 + 10) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition and is pushed up to 20 feet straight away from the leviathan.  
>
>***Accursed Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature in a 120-foot-long, 10-foot-wide Line. *Failure:* 78 (12d12) Necrotic damage, and the target is cursed. The cursed target has Disadvantage on D20 Tests and can't regain Hit Points. The target can end the curse early if it voluntarily fails a saving throw forced by one of its enemies. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Swallow.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 25, one Large or smaller creature Grappled by the leviathan (it can have up to four creatures swallowed at a time). *Failure:* The target is swallowed and no longer Grappled. A swallowed creature has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the leviathan, and takes 24 (7d6) Force damage at the start of each of its turns.  
>If the leviathan takes 50 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the leviathan must succeed on a DC 25 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, each of which falls in a space within 10 feet of the leviathan and has the Prone condition. If the leviathan dies, any swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape from the corpse using 15 feet of movement, exiting Prone.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Jinxed Hide.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 60 feet of the leviathan hits it with an attack roll or targets it with a spell. *Response:* The triggering creature must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or become cursed until the end of its next turn. The cursed creature has Disadvantage on D20 Tests and can't regain Hit Points.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Devour.** The leviathan uses Swallow.
>- **Swamp Surge.** The leviathan causes a swell of muddy water and grasping roots. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the leviathan. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target's Speed is 0 until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The leviathan can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Tail Sweep.** The leviathan moves up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks and makes one Tail attack.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Jinxed Leviathan's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Hanging Hex.** Within 1 mile of the lair, when a creature other than the Jinxed Leviathan rolls a 1 or a 20 on a D20 Test, the next D20 Test the creature makes has Disadvantage, and it can't have Advantage.
>- **Murky Waters.** Within 1 mile of the lair, swamp water is cloudy and shows fleeting faces in the murk. Creatures in the water have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
>
>If the Jinxed Leviathan dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
