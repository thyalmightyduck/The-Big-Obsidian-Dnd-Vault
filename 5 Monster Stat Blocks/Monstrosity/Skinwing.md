>## Skinwing
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 68 (8d8 + 32)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +2
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Disturbing Bleating.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the skinwing is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Staggered condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to the Disturbing Bleating of all skinwings for 24 hours.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The skinwing has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the skinwing's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skinwing makes one Bite attack and one Talons attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.
