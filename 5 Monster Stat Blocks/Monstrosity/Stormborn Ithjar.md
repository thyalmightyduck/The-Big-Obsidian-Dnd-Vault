___
>## Stormborn Ithjar
>*Huge Monstrosity, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 189 (18d12 + 72)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 120 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|20 (+5)|19 (+4)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +9, Perception +9
>- **Immunities** Lightning, Thunder
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Slippery Serpent.*** The stormborn ithjar has Advantage on ability checks and saving throws to avoid or end the Grappled, Paralyzed, or Restrained conditions.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The stormborn ithjar makes three Bite attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Tail.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) Lightning damage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Thunder damage, and if the target is Larger or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the stormborn ithjar can't make Tail attacks against other creatures.  
>
>***Storm Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Lightning damage plus 27 (6d8) Thunder damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The discharge of this breath is audible for 1 mile outdoors or 300 feet indoors.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Writhing Escape.*** *Trigger:* The stormborn ithjar becomes Grappled. *Response:* The stormborn ithjar makes a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check to escape.
