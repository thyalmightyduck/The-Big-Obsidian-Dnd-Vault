>## Living City
>*Gargantuan Monstrosity (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 70 (4d20 + 28) plus 70 per player character (4d20 + 28)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Climb 5 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|1 (-5)|25 (+7)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +0, Int +7, Wis +6, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Stealth +0
>- **Immunities** Grappled, Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common; telepathy 10 ft.
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Digestive Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn in the living city's space takes 10 (4d4) Acid damage. At the start of its turn, the living city decides whether its Digestive Aura is active until the start of its next turn (no action required).  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the living city remains motionless in its Structural Form, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary structure or vehicle, such as a house or a sailing ship.  
>
>***No Exit.*** When it rolls for Initiative, the living city reflexively closes the windows, doors, and other egress points on the outside of its body, preventing creatures inside it from leaving its space.  
>A creature in the living city's space that attempts to leave its space using teleportation or planar travel is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 20. *Failure:* 27 (5d10) Psychic damage. *Failure or Success:* The teleportation or planar travel fails.  
>
>***Structural Form.*** The living city can spend 10 minutes in Concentration to shape-shift into a Gargantuan structure or back into its true, amorphous form. Its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>While in the form of a structure, the living city takes up its entire space and creates floors, walls, doorways, and windows within its space which resemble and behave like normal structures, while leaving behind open space within it to create rooms. Creatures can pass through the open spaces and stop there as if moving through a normal structure.  
>The walls and floors of the living city can be damaged and thus breached. Each 5-foot-by-5-foot section has AC 15 and 30 Hit Points. Reducing a section to 0 Hit Points destroys it and deals 30 damage to the living city. A living city can only take this damage once per turn.  
>In addition, the living city extrudes pieces of itself inside its space which resemble furnishings and everyday objects. The extrusions appear, feel, sound, and behave exactly like the mimicked objects. A thin, slimy tendril connects any furnishings or objects to the body of the living city. The tendril has AC 15 and 1 Hit Point. If the tether is severed or destroyed, the simulacra melts into goo, but does no harm to the living city.  
>
>### Actions
>***The living city always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the living city can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the living city takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Grasping Tendril.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed or pulled 10 feet (the living city's choice).  
>
>***Haunting Whispers.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature in the living city's space. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition (save ends).  
>
>***Shifting Form.*** The living city alters the composition of a 10-foot-by-10-foot area inside it, creating walls, doors, windows, stairs, or furnishings. It can choose to make the area Difficult Terrain. Creatures inside the living city who are not directly observing the shift must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom (Perception) check to notice the change.  
>If the living city creates a wall that cuts through another creature's space when it appears, the creature is pushed to one side of the wall (the living city's choice). If a creature would be surrounded on all sides by the wall (or the wall and another solid surface), that creature can make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a success, it can use its Reaction to move up to its speed so that it is no longer enclosed by the wall.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The living city chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The living city can use this Epic Action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
