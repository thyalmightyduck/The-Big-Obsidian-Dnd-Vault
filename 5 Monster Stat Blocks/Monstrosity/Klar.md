___
>## Klar
>*Medium Monstrosity (Skirmisher), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 52 (7d8 + 21)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +6, Con +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Deception +5, Persuasion +5, Survival +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** Klar can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Mimicry.*** Klar can mimic animal sounds. A creature who hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>***Standing Leap.*** Klar's long jump is up to 40 feet and their high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Klar makes one Bite attack and one Mantis Claws attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.  
>
>***Mantis Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 f., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, they are grappled (escape DC 14). While grappled in this way, the target is restrained. Moving while grappling a Medium or smaller target doesn't halve Klar's speed. Klar can have only one target grappled in this way at a time.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bellowing Roar (1/Day).*** Klar lets loose a terrifying roar.  
>Each creature within 30 feet of Klar who can hear them must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of Klar for 1 minute. If a creature frightened in this way takes damage, they can repeat this saving throw, ending the effect on themself on a success.  
>
>***Kangaroo Leap.*** Klar jumps up to half their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
