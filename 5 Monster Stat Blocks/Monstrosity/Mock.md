>## Mock
>*Tiny Monstrosity, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 9 (2d4 + 4)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|4 (-3)|13 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Acid; Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Adhesive (Object Form Only).*** The mock adheres to anything that touches it. The mock has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature adhered to it. The target or a creature within 5 feet of it can detach the mock as an action.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Acid damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shape-Shift.*** The mock shape-shifts to resemble a Tiny object while retaining its game statistics, or it returns to its true blob form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.
