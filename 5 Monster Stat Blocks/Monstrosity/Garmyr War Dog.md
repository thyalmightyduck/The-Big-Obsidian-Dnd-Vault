>***Pack Tactics.*** The war dog has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the war dog's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Feast.*** The war dog makes a Bite attack against a Bloodied target.
