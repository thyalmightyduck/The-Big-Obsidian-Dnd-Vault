>## Crater Wurm
>*Gargantuan Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 247 (15d20 + 90)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Burrow 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|28 (+9)|7 (-2)|22 (+6)|1 (-5)|8 (-1)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +11, Wis +4
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Tunneler.*** The crater wurm can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrowing Speed and leaves a 10-foot diameter tunnel in its wake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The crater wurm makes a Bite attack, then makes Bite attacks against each creature adjacent to the first target.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 35 (4d12 + 9) Piercing damage, and if it is Large or smaller, the target is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 22. *Failure:* The target is swallowed by the wurm. The target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover against effects originating from outside the wurm, and takes 36 (8d8) Necrotic damage at the start of each of the wurm's turns. A creature that takes this Necrotic damage must also succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or gain one Contamination level.  
>If the wurm takes 30+ damage on a single turn from a creature inside it, it must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which land in spaces within 10 feet of the wurm with the Prone condition. If the wurm dies, a swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape from the corpse using 20 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.  
>
>***Breach.*** The wurm moves up to its Speed, entering the spaces of Large or smaller creatures. Each creature whose space it enters is subjected once to the following effect. At the end of its movement, the wurm can make one Bite attack against a Prone creature as a Bonus Action. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 22. *Success:* The target is pushed 5 feet away from the wurm into a space of its choice. If the target chooses not to move, it suffers the effects of a failure. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away from the wurm and has the Prone condition.
