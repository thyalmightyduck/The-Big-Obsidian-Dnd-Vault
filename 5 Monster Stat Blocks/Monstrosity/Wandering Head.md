___
>## Wandering Head
>*Large Monstrosity, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 95 (10d10 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|9 (-1)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +5, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Deception +4, Insight +4, Persuasion +4
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Any four languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Off Balance.*** The wandering head has Disadvantage on ability checks and saving throws made to avoid or end the Prone condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The wandering head makes one Bite attack and one Slam attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Counterspell (2/Day).*** The wandering head casts *Counterspell* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The wandering head casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Identify*, *Light*, *Mage Hand*, *Mending*  
>2/day each: *Arcane Lock*, *Charm Person*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Illusory Script*  
>1/day each: *Fabricate*, *Remove Curse*, *Suggestion*
