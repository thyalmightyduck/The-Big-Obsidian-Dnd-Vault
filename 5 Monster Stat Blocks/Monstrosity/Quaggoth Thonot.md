___
>## Quaggoth Thonot
>*Medium Monstrosity, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 67 (9d8 + 27)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Undercommon
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Bloodied Fury.*** While Bloodied, the quaggoth has Advantage on attack rolls.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The quaggoth makes two Claw attacks.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 5 (2d4) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The quaggoth casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Mage Hand* (the hand is Invisible), *Minor Illusion*  
>2/day: *Mind Spike*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Psionic Defense (3/Day).*** The quaggoth casts *Feather Fall* or *Shield* in response to the spell's trigger, requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
