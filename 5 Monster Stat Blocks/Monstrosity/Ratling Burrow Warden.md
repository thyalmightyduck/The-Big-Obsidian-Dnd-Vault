___
>## Ratling Burrow Warden
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Con +3
>- **Skills** Athletics +4, Perception +2
>- **Gear** *Chain Shirt*, *Shield*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The ratling has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The ratling has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the ratling's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Poison-Tipped Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shield Shove.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within reach of the ratling. *Failure:* The target is pushed 5 feet away from the ratling and has the Prone condition.
