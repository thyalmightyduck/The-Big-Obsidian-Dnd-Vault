>## Maw Vermin
>*Huge Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 161 (14d12 + 70)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|13 (+1)|20 (+5)|2 (-4)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Con +9, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Necrotic, Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The maw vermin has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The maw vermin makes one Bite attack and three Claw attacks, and it can use Grasping Maw or Glob Launcher.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Piercing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it is swallowed. A swallowed creature has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, and has Total Cover from effects originating outside the maw vermin. At the start of each of the maw vermin's turns, a swallowed creature takes 10 (3d6) Acid damage and must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or gain one Contamination level.  
>The maw vermin can swallow up to three creatures at a time. If the maw vermin takes 20+ damage in a single turn from a creature inside it, the maw vermin must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, each of which land in spaces within 10 feet of the maw vermin with the Prone condition. If the maw vermin dies, a swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape from the corpse using 10 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) Slashing damage, and if the target is Huge or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16). Until the grapple ends, the maw vermin can't use this Claw on another target. The maw vermin has three Claws.  
>
>***Grasping Maw.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 18, one Medium or smaller creature the maw vermin can see within 15 feet. *Failure:* The target is pulled into an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the maw vermin.  
>
>***Glob Launcher.*** The maw vermin coats a swallowed creature in acidic goo and launches it at a point within 120 feet of it. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature within 10 feet of the launched creature as it lands (the launched creature automatically fails). *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Acid damage, and the target has the Burning condition (4d6 Acid). While Burning, the target has the Restrained condition.
