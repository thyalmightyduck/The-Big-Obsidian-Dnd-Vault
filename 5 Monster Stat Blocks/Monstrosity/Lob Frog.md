>## Lob Frog
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Poison Skin.*** When a creature within 5 feet of the lob frog hits it with a melee attack, that creature takes 3 (1d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Standing Leap.*** The lob frog's long jump is up to 30 feet and its high jump is up to 20 feet, with or without a running start.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage, and the target has the Distracted condition.  
>
>***Throw Frogspawn.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) Acid damage. If the target is a creature, the egg sticks to the target's body, melding with its flesh. A creature within 5 feet of the egg can take an action to remove the egg, dealing 10 (3d6) Acid damage to the target in the process. If the egg is still attached to the target at the end of its next turn, the target is infected with a disease and has the Sickened condition until the disease ends. The disease can't be cured except by magic.  
>Every hour, the target is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level. Contamination levels gained in this way can't be removed until the disease is cured. If the target suffers a Monstrous Transformation, it dies and its flesh falls away, birthing a Lob Frog.
