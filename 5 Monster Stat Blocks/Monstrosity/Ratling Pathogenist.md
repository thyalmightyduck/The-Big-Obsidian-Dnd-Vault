>## Ratling Pathogenist
>*Small Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 110 (20d6 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, Deception +1, Stealth +7
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The ratling has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Plaguebringer Aura.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, a creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of the ratling. *Failure:* 5 (1d10) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Filthy Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Infection (3/Day).*** The ratling causes one creature it can see within 60 feet to be infected with a disease. At the start of each of its turns, the target is subjected to the following effect. Constitution SavingThrow: DC 14. *Failure:* 13 (2d12) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage. After three successes, the disease ends. *Failure or Success:* A *Lesser Restoration* spell ends the disease early.  
>
>***Metastasize.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 15 feet that is infected with a disease. *Failure:* The target has one of the following conditions (the ratling's choice): Addled, Clumsy, or Enfeebled (save ends). The condition immediately ends and is replace by a new condition if the ratling uses Metastasize on the target again. *Failure or Success:* 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampant Sickness.*** The ratling spreads its Infection from an infected target to another creature adjacent to that target.
