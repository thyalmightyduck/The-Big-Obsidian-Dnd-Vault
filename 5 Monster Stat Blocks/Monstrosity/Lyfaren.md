>## Lyfaren
>*Gargantuan Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 216 (16d20 + 48)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Burrow 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|6 (-2)|17 (+3)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Immunities** Petrified, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 20 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Earthbound.*** The lyfaren must stay surrounded by earth. If the lyfaren starts its turn with its entire Gargantuan body removed from the ground, it takes 36 (8d8) Force damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lyfaren uses Bite, and if it has a creature grappled, it uses Swallow.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 42 (8d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18).  
>
>***Swallow.*** The lyfaren swallows a creature Grappled by it. While swallowed, the target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover against attacks and other effects originating from outside the lyfaren, and the target takes 27 (6d8) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of the lyfaren's turns. A lyfaren can have up to four Large or smaller creatures swallowed at a time.
