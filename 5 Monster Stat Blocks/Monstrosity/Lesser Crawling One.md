>## Lesser Crawling One
>*Medium Monstrosity (Mythos), Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 59 (9d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +4, Int +6, Wis +3
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, Insight +3, Perception +3
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 other languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The lesser crawling one has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Relentless Evil.*** When a lesser crawling one is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it falls into a pile of rotting flesh, worms, and maggots. If it takes at least 15 Fire damage before the end of its next turn, the lesser crawling one is permanently destroyed. It can still be targeted by spells and effects that target creatures. Otherwise, the lesser crawling one reforms in 2d6 days. The new body appears in an unoccupied space within the tomb it was originally buried in.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slimy Grasp.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, Reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 1) Necrotic damage, and a Medium or smaller creature has its Speed reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn as it is covered in slime and maggots.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The lesser crawling one casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Darkness*, *Hold Person*, *Invisibility*, *Magic Missile*, *Ray of Sickness*  
>1/day: *Bestow Curse*, *Dispel Magic*, *Fear*, *Shatter*  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 1. Immediately after another creature's turn, the lesser crawling one can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The lesser crawling one regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **By My Command.** The lesser crawling one chooses up to three willing Undead or Mythos creatures it can see. Each of the chosen creatures can take a Reaction to move up to its Speed, and each creature gains Advantage on the next attack roll it makes before the lesser crawling one's next turn.
>- **Squirming Escape.** The lesser crawling one teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
