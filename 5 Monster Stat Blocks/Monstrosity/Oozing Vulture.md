>## Oozing Vulture
>*Medium Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +6, Stealth +3
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Blood Frenzy.*** The oozing vulture has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature missing any of its Hit Points.  
>
>***Flyby.*** The oozing vulture doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.
