>## Dread Skeljaskra
>*Gargantuan Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 186 (12d20 + 60)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 15 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|6 (-2)|20 (+5)|4 (-3)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Con +9
>- **Immunities** Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Siege Monster.*** The dread skeljaskra deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dread skeljaskra makes two attacks, using Rend, Burning Spit, or Spiked Tail in any combination.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 33 (6d8 + 6) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Burning Spit.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10 to hit, range 90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 20 (4d6 + 6) Poison damage plus 10 (3d6) Fire damage, and the target has the Blinded and Poisoned conditions until the start of the skeljaskra's next turn.  
>
>***Spiked Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 33 (5d10 + 6) Piercing damage, and the target is pushed 20 feet.  
>
>***Crushing Roll (Recharge 5–6).*** If the dread skeljaskra begins its turn in its Armored Ball form, it can roll up to 60 feet. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16 Each creature in a 60-foot Line that is 15 feet wide. *Failure:* 32 (5d12) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Armored Ball.*** *Trigger:* The dread skeljaskra takes 30 or more damage. *Response:* The skeljaskra curls into a ball until the start of its next turn. While this effect lasts, the creature has Immunity to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, and it has the Incapacitated condition.
