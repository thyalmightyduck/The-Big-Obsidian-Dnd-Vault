___
>## Shardmaul Manticore
>*Large Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d10 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|7 (-2)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The manticore makes two Claw attacks and one Stinger attack  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage. If the manticore flies 30+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition and the manticore can make a Bite attack against it as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Stinger.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 24 (7d6) Necrotic damage. Failure by 5 or More: The target also gains one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Wing Shards (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the manticore (flying creatures have Disadvantage). *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Piercing damage, and if the target is flying, it has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
