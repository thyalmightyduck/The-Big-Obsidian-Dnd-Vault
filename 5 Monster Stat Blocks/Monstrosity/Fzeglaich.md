___
>## Fzeglaich
>*Medium Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 135 (18d8 + 54)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Wis +3
>- **Skills** Perception +6, Stealth +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** One language
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The fzeglaich has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The fzeglaich regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point. If the fzeglaich takes Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the fzeglaich's next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The fzeglaich makes three Rend attacks. It can replace one attack with Bite.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage, and the fzeglaich regains Hit Points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage dealt. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampage.*** After reducing a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, the fzeglaich moves up to half its Speed and makes a Bite attack.
