>- **Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., 50 ft. in hybrid and kindred form, Climb 30 ft. in hybrid and kindred form
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)

>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The werebear makes three Claw attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Bite  
>
>***Bite (Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is a Humanoid, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target is cursed. If the cursed target drops to 0 Hit Points, it gains the Lycanthrope Transformation. *Success:* The target is immune to this werebear's curse for 24 hours.  
>
>***Claw (Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The werebear shape-shifts into a bear-humanoid hybrid, a bear, or back into its true form, which is humanoid. Any armor it is wearing merges into its hybrid form.  
>The werebear's kindred bear form is a Large bear. In this form, it can speak to and understand bears, can't take any Actions that require hands, and adds its Constitution modifier to any saving throw it makes. The werebear gains 25 Temporary Hit Points the first time it assumes its bear form each day. In bear form, it uses the same attacks as the Hybrid form.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The werebear casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Animal Friendship*, *Druidcraft*, *Guidance*, *Mending*, *Shillelagh*  
>2/day each: *Conjure Animals*, *Dispel Magic*, *Faerie Fire*, *Hold Person*, *Lesser Restoration*, *Plant Growth*  
>1/day each: *Arboreal Curse*, *Commune with Nature*, *Freedom of Movement*, *Ice Storm*, *Tree Stride*, *Wall of Thorns*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Healing Word (3/Day).*** The werebear casts *Healing Word* using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
