>## Medusa Headstrong
>*Medium Monstrosity (Medusa, Retainer); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (medium armor)
>- **Hit Points** Seven times their level (number of d8 Hit Dice equal to their level)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +PB, Dex 3+PB, Con 1+PB, Int +PB, Wis 1+PB, Cha +PB
>- **Skills** Perception +1 plus PB, Survival +1 plus PB
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11 plus PB
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals the mentor's bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals the mentor's bonus
>___
>### Actions
>***Signature Attack (Snake Bite).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  3 plus PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d10 plus PB piercing damage. Beginning at 7th level, the headstrong can make this attack twice, instead of once, when they take the Attack action on their turn.  
>
>***3rd Level: Snake Spit (3/Day).*** As a bonus action, the headstrong spits venom at the eyes of a creature within 5 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 10 plus PB Dexterity saving throw or be blinded until the end of their next turn.  
>
>***5th Level: Venomous Bite (3/Day).*** When the headstrong hits a creature with a signature attack, the headstrong injects the target with venom. The target must make a DC 10 plus PB Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes PBd6 poison damage and is poisoned until the start of the headstrong's next turn. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage and isn't poisoned.  
>
>***7th Level: Stone Gaze (3/Day).*** As an action, the headstrong fires energy beams from their eyes at a creature they can see within 30 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 10 plus PB Constitution saving throw or begin to turn to stone and be restrained.  
>A target restrained in this way must repeat the saving throw at the end of their next turn. On a successful save, the effect ends. On a failed save, the creature is petrified for 1 hour or until they are restored by a *cure ailment* power of 4th order or higher, a *greater restoration* spell, or a similar supernatural effect.
