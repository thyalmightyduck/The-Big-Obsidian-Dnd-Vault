>## Chitter
>*Medium Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 67 (9d8 + 27)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|9 (-1)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Running Leap.*** As long as the chitter has moved 20+ feet this turn, it has a high jump and long jump of 30 feet.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The chitter makes two Sharpened Claws attacks and one Acrid Bite attack. It can also use Chitter, if available.  
>
>***Acrid Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Sharpened Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Chitter (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature within 120 feet of the chitter that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Dazed condition until the end of the chitter's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The chitter moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see. If it ends this movement adjacent to the creature, it gains Advantage on attack rolls against that creature until the end of its turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Slow Fall.*** *Trigger:* The chitter takes falling damage and doesn't have the Incapacitated condition. *Response:* The triggering damage is reduced to 0.
