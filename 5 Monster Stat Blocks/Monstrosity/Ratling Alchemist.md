___
>## Ratling Alchemist
>*Small Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 70 (20d6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +7, Perception +5, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Poison
>- **Gear** *Quiver*, ten *Bolts*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The ratling has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The ratling has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the ratling's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ratling makes three Repeating Crossbow attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Repeating Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d12 + 4) Piercing damage, plus 10 (3d6) damage of one of the following types (the ratling's choice each time it attacks): Acid, Cold, Fire, or Poison.  
>
>***Delerium Bomb (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 10-foot radius Sphere centered on a point the ratling can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Fire damage plus 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage. *Failure or Success:* An Arcane Anomalies occurs in the area.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Ratling Cunning.*** The ratling takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Covering Fire.*** *Trigger:* A creature reduces an ally the ratling can see to 0 Hit Points. *Response:* The ratling makes a ranged attack against that creature.
