>## Nolidir
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 97 (13d10 + 26)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|4 (-3)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +4
>- **Immunities** Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Painkiller.*** While the nolidir is Poisoned, it gains Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The nolid uses its Terrifying Howl, then makes one Bite attack and one Claws attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Terrifying Howl.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 60- foot Emanation originating from the nolid that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save (with Disadvantage if it can see the nolidir), ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. If the target succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to the Terrifying Howl of all nolidirs for 24 hours.
