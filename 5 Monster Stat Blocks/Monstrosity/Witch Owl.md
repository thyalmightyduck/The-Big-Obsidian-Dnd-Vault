___
>## Witch Owl
>*Medium Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +2, Perception +5, Stealth +3, (stealth 5 while flying)
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands one national language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** The witch owl doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>***Mimicry.*** The witch owl can mimic owl sounds and Humanoid voices. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they're imitations with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The witch owl makes two attacks: one with its Bite and one with its Talons.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (2d4 + 1) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Humanoid, it is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target is cursed to become a witch owl.
