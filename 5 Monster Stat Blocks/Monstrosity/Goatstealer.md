>## Goatstealer
>*Small Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 22 (4d6 + 8)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +6, Survival +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The goatstealer has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the goatstealer's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Stench.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, any creature that starts its turn in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the goatstealer. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to the Stench of all goatstealers for 1 hour.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12). Until the grapple ends, at the start of each of the goatstealer's turns, the target takes 3 (1d6) Poison damage.
