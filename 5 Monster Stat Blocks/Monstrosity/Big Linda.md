___
>## Big Linda
>*Huge Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 168 (16d12 + 48)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|25 (+7)|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|2 (-4)|12 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Immunities** Acid
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Regeneration.*** When Big Linda starts her turn in an area of the Deep Haze, she regains 10 Hit Points. Big Linda dies only if she starts her turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If Big Linda is destroyed and her corpse is left within an area of the Deep Haze for 1 hour, she is revived with all of her Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Big Linda makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 33 (4d12 + 7) Piercing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 19). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and Big Linda can't Bite another target.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Acid Spew (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 63 (14d8) Acid damage. *Success:* Half damage.
