___
>## Swarm of Gremishkas
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Monstrosities, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 31 (7d4 + 14)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 30 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The gremishka has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Swarm (Swarm Form Only).*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Swarming Bites (Swarm Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (3d6 + 2) Piercing damage, or 9 (3d4 + 2) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied, plus 7 (2d6) Force damage. If the target is attuned to any magic items, its Attunement to one item (DM's choice) ends.
