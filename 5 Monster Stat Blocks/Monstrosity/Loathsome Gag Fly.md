>## Loathsome Gag Fly
>*Small Monstrosity*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 27 (5d6 + 10)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Digestive Enzymes.*** The gag fly has Advantage on attack rolls against a Burning target.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The gag fly can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Proboscis.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Acid Spray (Recharge 6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 5 (2d4) Acid damage, and the target has the Burning condition (2d4 Acid). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Bloat Burst.*** *Trigger:* The gag fly is reduced to 0 Hit Points or takes damage from a Critical Hit. *Response—**Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the gag fly. *Failure:* 5 (2d4) Acid damage, and the target has the Burning condition (2d4 Acid).
