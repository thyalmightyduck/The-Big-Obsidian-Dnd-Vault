>## Skeljaskra
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 66 (7d10 + 28)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|6 (-2)|18 (+4)|4 (-3)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Dex +6, Con +6
>- **Immunities** Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Siege Monster.*** The skeljaskra deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skeljaskra makes two attacks, using Rend, Burning Spit, or Tail in any combination.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Burning Spit.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Poison damage, and the target has the Blinded and Poisoned conditions until the start of the skeljaskra's next turn.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d8 + 5) Piercing damage, and the target is pushed 5 feet.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Armored Ball.*** *Trigger:* The skeljaskra takes 10 or more Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage from a single source. *Response:* The skeljaskra curls into a ball until the start of its next turn. While this effect lasts, the creature has Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage and has the Incapacitated condition.
