>## Swarm of Sarcopikes
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Monstrosities, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 49 (9d8 + 9)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Cold, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Cold Aura.*** At the end of each of the swarm's turns, each creature that isn't a sarcopike in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the swarm takes 4 (1d8) Cold damage.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Water Breathing.*** The swarm can breathe only underwater.  
>
>### Actions
>***Swarm of Bites.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Cold damage, or 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Cold damage if the swarm is Bloodied.
