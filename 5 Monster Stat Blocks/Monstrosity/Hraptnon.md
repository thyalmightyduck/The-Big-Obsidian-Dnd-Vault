___
>## Hraptnon
>*Gargantuan Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 264 (16d20 + 96)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 20 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|17 (+3)|22 (+6)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +11, Dex +9, Con +12, Wis +9
>- **Skills** Athletics +11, Perception +9
>- **Immunities** Spell damage, magical weapon damage; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Stunned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Draconic
>- **Challenge** 18 (XP 20,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If the hraptnon fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The hraptnon has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Undying.*** Unless the hraptnon's Stillborn Heart is removed from its pool, the hraptnon's death is temporary. One of its countless **Xakalonus** offspring grows into the hraptnon in 24 hours. The hraptnon loses its Immunities if the Stillborn Heart is removed from the pool.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hraptnon makes three attacks, using Bite, Claw, or Spine in any combination.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Piercing damage.*Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20. *Failure:* The target has the Incapacitated condition until the end of the hraptnon's next turn.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 27 (4d10 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Spine.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +11, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) Piercing damage. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 20. *Failure:* The target has the Prone and Bloodied condition until it takes an action to remove the spine with a successful DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Spawn Xakalonus.*** *Trigger:* The hraptnon takes 20 points of damage or more from a single attack. *Response:* The hraptnon spawns a **Xakalonus** in the nearest unoccupied space. The **Xakalonus** shares Initiative with the hraptnon.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the hraptnon can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The hraptnon regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Claw.** The hraptnon makes a Claws attack.
>- **Baneful Howl.** The hraptnon casts *Bane* (level 3 version; spell save DC 20). The hraptnon can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Life Drain.** One **Xakalonus** within 30 feet of the hraptnon dies. The hraptnon regains Hit Points equal to half the xakalonus's remaining Hit Points when it died. The hraptnon can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
