___
>## Plague Carrion
>*Large Monstrosity (Shadowsteel Curse), Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 247 (26d10 + 104)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +9, Wis +8
>- **Skills** Perception +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Acid, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** All languages known before the curse
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Cloud of Blight.*** A miasma surrounds the Plague Carrion in a 20-foot Emanation. Each time another creature starts its turn or enters the area, it gains a -1 penalty to damage rolls. Multiple instances of this penalty stack, but a creature can't gain it more than once each round. The penalty is removed when the creature finishes a Quick, Short, or Long Rest.  
>
>***Death Burst.*** When the Plague Carrion dies, it explodes in a cloud of sickening gas. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation centered on the Plague Carrion. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Poison damage, and the creature's Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to half the Poison damage taken (rounding down). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>The Hit Point maximum reduction lasts until the creature finishes a Short or Long Rest.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Plague Carrion makes one Bite attack and one Plague Touch attack, or two Plague Beam attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 20 (4d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Plague Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 49 (10d8 + 4) Poison damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to half the Poison damage taken (rounding down).  
>
>***Plague Beam.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the Plague Carrion's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The Plague Carrion casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material component and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>1/day: *Contagion*, *Insect Plague*
