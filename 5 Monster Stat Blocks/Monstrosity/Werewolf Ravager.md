___
>## Werewolf Ravager
>*Small or Medium Monstrosity (Lycanthrope), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 28)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., 50 ft. in hybrid form
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|9 (-1)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +6, Dex +6
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from silvered attacks
>- **Immunities** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't silvered; Charmed, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Shortsword*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Grappler.*** The werewolf has Advantage on attack rolls against Grappled targets. Its attacks deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage against a creature it has Grappled.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The werewolf makes two melee attacks, using Claw or Shortsword in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Bite.  
>
>***Bite (Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition. If the target is a Humanoid, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target is cursed. If the cursed target drops to 0 Hit Points, it gains the Lycanthrope Transformation. *Success:* The target is immune to this werewolf's curse for 24 hours.  
>
>***Claw (Hybrid Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*    
>+6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) if it is Medium or smaller. It can grapple one creature at a time, and it cannot use Claw attacks against other targets when grappling a creature.  
>
>***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The werewolf shape-shifts into a wolf-humanoid hybrid or back into its true form, which is humanoid. Any armor it is wearing merges into its hybrid form.
