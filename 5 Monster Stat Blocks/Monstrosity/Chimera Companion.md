# Flee, Mortals!
___
## Chimera Companion
*Large Monstrosity (Companion), Unaligned*
___
- **[[Armor Class]]** 13 plus PB (natural armor)
- **[[Hit Point|Hit Points]]** 7 + seven times caregiver's level (number of d8 [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Dice]] equal to their caregiver's level)
- **[[Speed]]** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
- **[[Initiative]]** +1 (11)
___

|   STR   |   DEX   |   CON   |  INT   |   WIS   |  CHA   |
| :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :----: | :-----: | :----: |
| 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 5 (-3) | 12 (+1) | 8 (-1) |
___
- **[[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]]** Con +2 plus PB
- **Skills** [[Athletics]] +3 plus PB
- **Immunities** [[Frightened]]
- **Senses** [[Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[Passive Perception]] 11, Passive Insight 11, Passive Stealth 11
- **Languages** —
- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals the caregiver's bonus)
- **[[Proficiency Bonus]]** equals the caregiver's bonus
___
### Actions
***Signature Attack (Bite).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  3 plus PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d6 plus PB piercing damage.  

***1st Level: Ram's Defense (2 Ferocity).*** The chimera makes a signature attack. On a hit, the attack imposes [[disadvantage]] on the target's next attack roll made before the start of the chimera's next turn.  

***3rd Level: Lion's Leap (5 Ferocity).*** The chimera moves up to half their speed without provoking [[Opportunity Attack|opportunity attacks]]. If they end this movement on the ground, each creature within 5 feet of them must succeed on a DC 10 plus PB Strength [[saving throw]] or be knocked [[prone]].  

***5th Level: Volcanic Blow (8 Ferocity).*** The chimera spits an explosion of fire at a point they can see within 60 feet of them. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius [[sphere]] centered on that point must make a DC 10 plus PB Dexterity [[saving throw]], taking PBd6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.  

### Reactions
***Protective Absorption (Recharges after a Short or [[Long Rest]]).*** When the chimera's caregiver takes damage while within 60 feet of the chimera, half the damage taken (rounded up) is transferred to the chimera.
