___
>## Hungerer Elk
>*Large Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 32 (5d10 + 5)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|2 (-4)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Tears of the Hungerer.*** A Beast or Humanoid that takes damage from the hungerer elk is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target contracts a magical disease called Tears of the Hungerer. *Success:* The target is immune to the disease for 24 hours.  
>An infected target decreases its Hit Point maximum by 2 (1d4) every 24. If this effect reduces its Hit Point maximum to 0, the target dies and becomes a **Zombie** with this trait. Tears of the Hungerer can only be cured by magic.  
>
>### Actions
>***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage. If the hungerer elk moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit and the target is Large or smaller, the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The hungerer elk moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see.  
>
>***Trample.*** The hungerer elk can make a Stomp attack against a Prone target as a Bonus Action.
