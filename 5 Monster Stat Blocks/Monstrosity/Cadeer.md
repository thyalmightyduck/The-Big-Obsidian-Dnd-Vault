___
>## Cadeer
>*Small Monstrosity, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 22 (5d6 + 5)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Investigation +5, Perception +2
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands four languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Sense.*** Given 1 minute of Concentration, the cadeer can sense magic within 3 miles outdoors and 300 feet underground. Using this sense, the cadeer senses only significant sources of magic, such as powerful magical beings or items, the of other planes, and strong ongoing spells. The cadeer has Advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) and Wisdom (Perception) checks to discern magic.  
>
>### Actions
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The cadeer casts *Detect Magic* or *Identify*, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13). The cadeer can also cast any spell sorted in its antlers at the same level it was previously cast.  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Identify*  
>
>***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the cadeer has a spell stored in its antlers, it deals an extra 2 (1d4) Force damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Spell Absorption.*** *Trigger:* The cadeer is the target of (or is included in the area of) a spell of level 2 or lower. *Response:* The cadeer can choose to absorb the spell with its antlers. The spell has no effect and the cadeer stores the spell in its antlers. When the cadeer stores a spell, any spell previously stored in the antlers dissipates harmlessly.
