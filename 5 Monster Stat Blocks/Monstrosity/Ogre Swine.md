>## Ogre Swine
>*Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 51 (6d10 + 18)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|9 (-1)|16 (+3)|4 (-3)|14 (+2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Understands one language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Relentless (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** If the ogre swine takes 10 damage or less that would reduce it to 0 Hit Points, it is reduced to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>***Stench.*** A creature other than an ogre or ogre swine that starts its turn within 5 feet of the ogre swine is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to the Stench of all ogre swine for 1 hour.  
>
>### Actions
>***Tusk.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 2 (1d4) Poison damage. If the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target is infected with Sewer Plague. *Success:* The target is immune to being infected by all ogre swine for 24 hours.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Blood Aggression.*** The ogre swine takes the Dash action, moving toward a creature missing any of its Hit Points.
