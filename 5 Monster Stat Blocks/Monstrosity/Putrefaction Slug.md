>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) Acid damage.  
>
>***Spit.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 10/30 ft. Hit:7 (2d6) Acid damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. First *Failure:* The target has Vulnerability to Bludgeoning damage. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature that takes an action to pour water on the spit grants the target Advantage on its saving throws. Subsequent Failures: The target takes 3 (1d6) Acid damage.
