___
>## Doom Boar
>*Medium Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 22 (4d8 + 4)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|2 (-4)|9 (-1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Reckless.*** At the start of its turn, the doom boar can gain Advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against it have Advantage until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Two-Headed.*** The doom boar has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on saving throws to avoid or end the Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Stunned, or Unconscious conditions.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The doom boar makes two Tusk attacks.  
>
>***Tusk.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage. If the doom boar moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) Slashing damage and, if it is Large or smaller, has the Prone condition.  
>If the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 9. *Failure:* The target is cursed, suffering a random effect from the *Bestow Curse* spell. After 1 minute, the target can repeat the save, ending the effect on itself on a success but extending the curse's duration to 24 hours on a failure.
