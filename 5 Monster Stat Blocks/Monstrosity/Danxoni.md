>## Danxoni
>*Huge Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 168 (16d12 + 64)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 20 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|2 (-4)|8 (-1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +3
>- **Skills** Perception +3
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Parasitic Release.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the danxoni when it dies. The Emanation spreads around corners. *Failure:* The target is infected with Sewer Plague.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The danxoni makes four Claws attacks and one Bite attack.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Acid damage.  
>
>***Symbiont Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Acid damage plus 7 (2d6) Piercing damage and 14 (4d6) Poison damage. *Failure by 5 or More:* The target is infected with Sewer Plague. *Success:* Half damage only.
