>## Grym-Mage Tyrannosaur
>*Huge Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (natural armor; 16 with *barkskin*)
>- **Hit Points** 147 (14d12 + 56)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|6 (-2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Cha +6
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Enraged Casting (Recharge 4–6).*** While the tyrannosaur has half of its hit points or fewer, it can cast one of its innate spells as a bonus action, even if the normal casting time for that spell is an action.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The tyrannosaur's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit with spell attacks). The tyrannosaur can innately cast the following spells, requiring only somatic components:  
>
>At will: *barkskin* *, *eldritch blast*, *false life*  
>2/day each: *hellish rebuke*, *inflict wounds*, *misty step*, *pass without trace*, *spike growth*  
>
>*The tyrannosaur casts barkskin before combat  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The tyrannosaur has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The tyrannosaur makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its tail. It can't make both attacks against the same target.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 32 (4d12 + 6) piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape DC 16). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the tyrannosaur can't bite another target.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.
