___
>## Boneflayer
>*Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 94 (9d10 + 45)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Perception +6, Survival +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Sylvan; understands Common but can't speak it
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Flyby.*** The boneflayer doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>***Thunderous Ward.*** When the boneflayer hears a drum or gong, or takes Thunder damage, it has Disadvantage on D20 Tests until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The boneflayer makes one Beak attack and one Talons attack.  
>
>***Beak.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) from both talons. While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Swoop (Recharge 5–6).*** If the boneflayer has a creature Grappled, the boneflayer flies up to half its Fly Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks and drops that creature.
