>## Corn Dolly
>*Tiny Construct, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 38 (11d4 + 11)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +1
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Husk Body.*** The corn dolly can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Uneasy Visage.*** The strange, childlike inked face unnerves onlookers. While a creature is holding the corn dolly, creatures within 10 feet of it have Disadvantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks made against the creature.  
>
>### Actions
>***Husk Cut.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1 Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Blank Stare.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature the corn dolly can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Sowing Dread (2/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature of the corn dolly's choice in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Silent Protector (Recharge after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Trigger:* A creature within 60 feet fails a saving throw against an effect that doesn't deal damage. *Response:* The corn dolly suffers the effects of the failure instead of the creature.
