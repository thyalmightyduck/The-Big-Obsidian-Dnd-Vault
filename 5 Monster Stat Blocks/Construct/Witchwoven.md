>*Tiny Construct, Neutral*

>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 21 (6d4 + 6)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|

>- **Skills** Nature +4, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Deafened, Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Cursed Scratch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a creature, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls until the start of the witchwoven's next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Bond.*** *Trigger:* A creature the witchwoven can see within 60 feet takes damage. *Response:* The creature gains Resistance to the triggering damage, and the witchwoven takes 10 (3d6) Force damage.
