>## Dolly
>*Small Construct, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d6 + 10)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +6
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Immutable Form.*** Dolly can't shape-shift.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Dolly uses Creepy Voice and makes one Touch attack.  
>
>***Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (2d4) Psychic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the Restrained condition until the end of Dolly's next turn.  
>
>***Creepy Voice.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature in a 15-foot Emanation originating from Dolly that can hear it. *Failure:* 5 (2d4) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of Dolly's next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to becoming Frightened by Dolly's Creepy Voice for 1 hour, but can still take damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Crying Escape.*** *Trigger:* The Grappled or Restrained condition ends on Dolly, or Dolly is released from the toymaker's bag. *Response:* Dolly crawls up to 15 feet, stands up, and uses Creepy Voice.
