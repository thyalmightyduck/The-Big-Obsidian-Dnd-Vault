>## Pactbreaker
>*Large Construct, Chaotic Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 157 (15d10 + 75)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|8 (-1)|20 (+5)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't adamantine
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Understands Celestial and Infernal but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The pactbreaker has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Pact Breaking.*** When the pactbreaker deals damage to a creature bound to a contract or pact with an Evil entity, the creature is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target can't use features, powers, and spells associated with their pact until the end of the pactbreaker's next turn. This effect ends the target's Concentration on any such effects.  
>
>***Pact Sense.*** The pactbreaker can sense creatures who have entered a pact with an Evil entity from up to 1,000 feet away. The pactbreaker knows the creature's distance and direction, and if it is moving, the pactbreaker knows the direction of its movement. Any effect that blocks Divination magic blocks this trait.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The pactbreaker makes two attacks, using Reactive Weapon or Eldritch Leash in any combination.  
>
>***Reactive Weapon.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Force damage plus 11 (2d10) Psychic damage. On subsequent hits against the same target, the damage type changes if the target would be harmed more by another damage type: Force damage can become Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing, and Psychic damage can become Radiant or Necrotic.  
>
>***Eldritch Leash.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +7, range 300 ft. *Hit:* 29 (4d12 + 3) Force damage, and if the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target's Flying Speed is reduced to 0 and can't increase for 1 minute. If the target is flying, it safely descends at a rate of 60 feet per round until it reaches the ground or the effect ends. If the target has a natural Speed rather than one provided by a spell or magical effect, it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. *Failure by 5 or More:* The target falls normally.  
>
>***Adjustable Form.*** The pactbreaker changes its appearance, though it always appears to be a Construct. Alternatively, it ends one effect that is altering its form.
