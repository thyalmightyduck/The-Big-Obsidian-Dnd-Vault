___
>## Gore Drinker
>*Huge Construct, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 190 (20d12 + 60)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +11, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Athletics +16
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Greatclub*, *Shield*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands Common and Giant but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 16 (XP 15,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Blood Frenzy.*** The gore drinker has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that doesn't have all its Hit Points.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the gore drinker fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The gore drinker has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gore drinker makes two attacks, using Greatclub or Slam in any combination.  
>
>***Greatclub.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 19), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends. The gore drinker can grapple up to three creatures at a time with its corpse parts.  
>
>***Bilious Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Acid damage, and the target has the Blinded and Poisoned conditions until the end of the gore drinker's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the gore drinker can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The gore drinker regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Dodge and Strike.** The gore drinker moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks and makes one Greatclub or Slam attack.
>- **Drink Blood.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19, one creature Grappled by the gore drinker. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the gore drinker regains Hit Points equal to that amount. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The gore drinker can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Fling.** The gore drinker throws a Medium or smaller creature it has Grappled to a space on the ground that the gore drinker can see within 30 feet of itself. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 19, the creature thrown and each creature in the destination space. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Failure or Success:* The gore drinker can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
