>## Kingkiller Hydra
>*Gargantuan Construct (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 92 (8d12 + 40) plus 92 (8d12 + 40) per player character
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___

>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Con +11, Wis +8, Cha +10
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Poisoned, Stunned, Unconscious, Burning|Monstersofdrakkenheim
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Tremorsense 300 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 18 (XP 20,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Corrosive Aura.*** The area within 30 feet of the hydra is filled with corrosive fumes and is Lightly Obscured. A creature that starts its turn in the fumes takes 20 (8d4) Acid damage.  
>
>***Head Count.*** The hydra can't make more Bite or Spit Acid attacks each round than it has heads, and each head can only be used to make one Bite or Spit Acid attack each round.  
>
>***Multiple Heads.*** The hydra has five heads. Whenever the hydra takes 25 damage or more in a single turn, one of its heads dies. The hydra dies if all of its heads are dead.  
>
>***Reactive Heads.*** For each head the hydra has beyond one, it gets an extra Reaction that can be used only for Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** The hydra is immune to the Burning (Acid only) condition.  
>
>***Wakeful.*** While the hydra sleeps, at least one of its heads is awake.  
>
>### Actions
>***The hydra always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the hydra can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the hydra takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 26 (4d8 + 8) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spit Acid.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +14, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 20 (8d4) Acid damage, and the target has the Burning condition (8d4 Acid). While Burning, the target has the Blinded condition.  
>
>***Grow Heads.*** The hydra can only perform this Epic Action if one or more of its heads died this round, unless the head was killed by Fire damage. The hydra grows two heads, and regains 20 Hit Points.  
>
>***Hydra Fury (1/Round).*** The hydra makes one Bite attack or one Spit Acid attack for each head that hasn't yet made an attack this round.  
>
>***Trample (1/Round).*** The hydra moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action. During this movement, it can move through the spaces of Huge or smaller creatures. Each creature the hydra moves over is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 22. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Tail (1/Round).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 34 (4d12 + 8) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed up to 20 feet away from the hydra.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The hydra chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The hydra can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
