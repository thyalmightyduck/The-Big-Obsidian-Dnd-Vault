>## Vedaja
>*Tiny Construct, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 33 (6d6 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +3, Perception +3, Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands the language of its creator plus one other language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Immutable Form.*** A vedaja's form can't be altered.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The vedaja has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The vedaja makes two Hand attacks.  
>
>***Hand.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning or Piercing damage (the vedaja's choice).  
>
>***Terrifying Glare.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11 One creature the vedaja can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the end of the vedaja's next turn. While Frightened, the target has the Paralyzed condition.
