___
>## Stitcher
>*Large Construct*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 200 (16d10 + 112)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|20 (+5)|24 (+7)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +6, Wis +8
>- **Skills** Medicine +12, Perception +8
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Hold Still, Please.*** The stitcher has Advantage on attack rolls against a Grappled, Immobilized, or Slowed creature.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The stitcher makes two Scissorhand attacks and one Sewing Needle attack. It can replace any of these attacks with Attach Limb.  
>
>***Scissorhand.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Slashing damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (4d4).  
>
>***Sewing Needle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target has a body part bound and sewn up (stitcher's choice):  
>
>***Eyes.*** The target has the Blinded condition.  
>
>***Mouth.*** The target cannot speak and cannot cast spells with a verbal component.  
>
>***Limbs.*** The target has the Restrained condition.  
>A creature within 5 feet can take an action to remove the threads with a successful DC 17 Wisdom (Medicine) check, ending the effect. *Failure:* The threads are removed but the target takes 11 (2d10) Slashing damage. Failure by 5 or More: The target takes 11 (2d10) Slashing damage and the threads aren't removed.  
>
>***Attach Limb.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature the stitcher can see within 10 feet (Grappled, Slowed, Restrained, Incapacitated, and Immobilized creatures have Disadvantage). *Failure:* The target makes a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw at the start of each of its turns. On a success, it controls the limb until the start of its next turn. Otherwise, the limb is uncontrolled for the same duration. The target suffers one of the following effects depending on the attached limb:  
>- **Head.** While controlled, the target has Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma-based D20 Tests. While uncontrolled, the target can control its action or its movement on its turn, but the head controls the other. On a failure by 5 or more, the head chooses which to control instead of the target.
>- **Arm.** While controlled, the target can take an extra action on its turn, choosing from the Attack (one weapon attack only) or Utilize actions. While uncontrolled, the extra arm makes one melee weapon attack against the target at the start of its turn. The arm has a +9 attack bonus and deals 14 (3d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage on a hit.
>- **Leg.** While controlled, the target can take an extra action on its turn, choosing from the Dash or Disengage actions. While uncontrolled, the leg forces the target to move up to its Speed in a random direction at the start of its turn. If this movement forces the target toward a hazard or precipice, the host can repeat the saving throw. On a successful save, it stops its movement and has the Prone condition before entering the hazard.
  An attached limb can be attacked (AC 20, 20 Hit Points, Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage). A creature within 5 feet can take an action to remove the limb with a successful DC 17 Wisdom (Medicine) check or DC 15 Strength check. When removed by any means other than a successful Wisdom (Medicine) check, the target takes 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage and has the Bleeding condition (4d4).
