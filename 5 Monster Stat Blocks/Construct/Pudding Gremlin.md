___
>## Pudding Gremlin
>*Tiny Construct, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 21 (6d6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Death Burst.*** The pudding gremlin explodes when it dies. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the pudding gremlin. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** If the pudding gremlin doesn't move on its turn, it has Advantage when taking the Hide action.  
>
>### Actions
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft, *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Fire Breath (Recharge 6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature in a 15 ft Cone. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Create Gremlin (1/Day).*** If the pudding gremlin is within 10 ft of an ordinary pudding, it can turn the ordinary pudding into another pudding gremlin. This new pudding gremlin appears in an unoccupied space within 10 ft of the original pudding gremlin and acts as its ally.
