>## Mageguard Sphere
>*Tiny Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 42 (12d4 + 12)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|4 (-3)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The mageguard sphere has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Spell Storing.*** A spellcaster bonded to the mageguard sphere can cause it to store one spell of level 2 or lower. To do so, the bonded spellcaster must cast the spell on the sphere while within 5 feet of it. The spell has no effect but is stored within the sphere. Any previously stored spell is lost when a new spell is stored. The sphere can cast the spell stored with any parameters set by the original caster, requiring no spell components and using the caster's spellcasting ability. The stored spell is then lost.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mageguard sphere makes two Force Burst attacks.  
>
>***Force Burst.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Force damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Magical Redirection.*** *Trigger:* A spell or other magical effect targets the bonded spellcaster while the bonded spellcaster is within 10 feet of the mageguard sphere. *Response:* The mageguard sphere becomes the target instead. If the redirected spell or magical effect used a spell slot, the mageguard sphere also regains 1 Hit Point per spell slot.
