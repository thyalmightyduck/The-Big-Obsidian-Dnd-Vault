___
>## Innocent Harvester
>*Small Construct*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 36 (8d6 + 8)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Stealth +7
>- **Immunities** Poison; Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Normal Semblance.*** The harvester has Advantage on Charisma (Deception) and Dexterity (Stealth) checks to pass itself off as an ordinary Small Humanoid such as a Human urchin or a Halfling.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The harvester makes two Surgical Claw attacks.  
>
>***Surgical Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage, and the target has the Bleeding condition (2d4).  
>
>***Syringe (1/Day).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* If this Poison damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, it is stabilized but has the Poisoned condition for 1 hour, even if it regains Hit Points. While Poisoned, the target has the Paralyzed condition.  
>
>***Latch (Recharge 5–6).*** The harvester moves up to its Speed. If it ends this movement within 5 feet of a Medium or larger creature, it can make one Surgical Claw attack against that creature. *Hit:* The harvester attaches to the target. While attached, the harvester has Advantage on attack rolls against the target. The attached harvester moves with the target, using none of the harvester's movement. The harvester can detach itself by using 5 feet of movement on its turn. A creature within 5 feet, including the target, can take an action to detach the harvester with a successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The harvester takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
