>## Reautomata Wrecker
>*Large Construct*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 190 (20d8 + 100)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|25 (+7)|10 (+0)|21 (+5)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +5, Wis +8, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +12, Perception +8
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Siege Monster.*** The reautomata deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The reautomata makes one Drill attack and one Wrecking Ball attack.  
>
>***Drill.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 21 (4d6 + 7) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Wrecking Ball.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 21 (4d6 + 7) Bludgeoning damage. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 20. *Failure:* The target has the Dazed condition (save ends) and the Prone condition.  
>
>***Destructive Rampage (Recharge 6).*** The reautomata moves up to its Speed in a straight line, moving through the space of any Medium or smaller creature on its path. The first time its enters each creature's space during this move, it can make a Drill or Wrecking Ball attack against that creature.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Overdrive Engine (1/Day; Bloodied Only).*** The reautomata's Destructive Rampage recharges, and it gains Immunity to all damage and conditions until the end of its next turn. For the duration, the reautomata automatically succeeds on all saving throws and Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution-based ability checks and has Advantage on attack rolls. It can't be forced to move or affected by spells of level 8 or lower unless it chooses to be. Features and effects that ignore Resistances and Immunities, such as a Vorpal Weapon, don't function against the reautomata unless those effects are caused by a level 9 spell or an artifact.  
>At the end of its next turn, the reautomata dies and explodes. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the reautomata when it dies. *Failure:* 20 (8d4) Acid damage plus 36 (8d8) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage.
