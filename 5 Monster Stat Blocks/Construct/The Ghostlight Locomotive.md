>## The Ghostlight Locomotive
>*Gargantuan Construct, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 94 (7d20 + 21)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +2, Cha +2
>- **Skills** Athletics +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands all but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Crooked Switch (1/Day).*** When the locomotive is Bloodied, it regains all expended uses of Legendary Resistance and enters a malevolent form for 12 hours. In this form, it is Chaotic Evil and can use its Multiattack action.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (2/Day).*** If the locomotive fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack (Crooked Switch Only).*** The locomotive makes two Rail Slam attacks.  
>
>***Rail Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed up to 10 feet straight away.  
>
>***Spirit Exhaust (Recharge 6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Ghostly Rails.** The locomotive moves up to half its Speed in a straight line without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
  A Large or smaller creature whose space it passes through has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) from the spectral rails.
>- **Haunting Whistle.** The locomotive blows its wailing steam whistle. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 30 feet. *Failure:* 4 (1d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. *Failure or Success:* The locomotive can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Ghostlight Locomotive's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Ghastly Shroud.** When a creature that isn't Undead finishes a Short or Long Rest within 1 mile of the locomotive, the creature must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or any time it regains Hit Points, it only regains half the amount. This effect ends when the creature next finishes a Long Rest or receives a *Lesser Restoration* spell or similar magic.
>- **Spirit Calling.** Within 1 mile of the locomotive, Hostile **Shrouded** and **Specters** are abundant and drawn to any creature that attacks the Ghostlight
  Locomotive or that tries to keep pace with, climb, or enter the *Ghostlight Express*.
>
>If the Ghostlight Locomotive dies, these effects end immediately.
