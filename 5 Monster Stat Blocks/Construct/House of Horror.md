>## House of Horror
>*Gargantuan Construct, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 217 (14d20 + 70)
>- **Speed** 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|5 (-3)|16 (+3)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Cha +3
>- **Skills** Athletics +11, Deception +8
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands Common plus one other language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Construction.*** Creatures can enter the house's space and navigate it as a normal building. The house makes attacks from any surface or object that isn't being worn or carried within its space. It doesn't have Disadvantage on ranged attack rolls due to an enemy being within 5 feet of it.  
>If a creature deals 30 or more damage in a single turn to a 5-foot section of the house's structure, the house must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or a 5-foot opening appears in that space until the start of the creature's next turn.  
>
>***Exorcism Susceptibility.*** If the house is targeted by a *Dispel Evil and Good* spell, it has the Stunned condition. At the end of each of its turns, it can make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw, ending the condition on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. As a Magic action, a creature inside the house that throws a flask of Holy Water against the house and succeeds on a DC 20 Charisma check affects the house as above.  
>
>***Soul Trap.*** A Humanoid that dies within the house has its soul trapped there until the house dies. The creature can't be returned to life by any means short of a *True Resurrection* or *Wish* spell.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The house makes three attacks, using Structure Slam or Object Fling in any combination, and it uses Agonized Souls.  
>
>***Structure Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Force damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16). The house can grapple up to two creatures at a time, using animated furniture, objects, or fixtures such as stair railings.  
>
>***Object Fling.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +11, range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d4 + 6) Bludgeoning or Piercing damage (house's choice). If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Agonized Souls.*** The house's stolen souls torment a victim. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, a creature within 10 feet of the house. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Alter Structure.*** The house can reshape itself to change the location of walls, doors, floors, and other features of its construction. When one of these features appears or disappears, a creature within 5 feet of a changing feature can make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw. On a successful save, the creature chooses which side of a feature it ends up on, or it moves to the nearest unoccupied space to avoid falling.
