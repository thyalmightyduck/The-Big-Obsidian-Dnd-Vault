>## Inkfester
>*Large Construct (Shapechanger), Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d10 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Death Burst.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the inkfester when it dies. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Flat Form.*** The inkfester's normal form is a two-dimensional pattern of runes on a flat surface. Until it moves or takes an action, the inkfester is indistinguishable from ink on a surface.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The inkfester can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Water Weakness.*** If the inkfester is doused in water or ends its turn submerged in water, it takes 3 (1d6) Acid damage, or 6 Acid damage if the water is soapy.  
>
>### Actions
>***Fearful Glyphs.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, up to two creatures within 30 feet that can see the inkfester. *Failure:* 8 (2d6 + 1) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Consuming Mist.*** The inkfester takes on the form of a cloud of dark ink. In this form, the inkfester has a Fly Speed of 20 feet and can Hover, and it can occupy a Hostile creature's space. It also has Advantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws and Immunity to nonmagical damage other than from acid or water. Creatures that share a space with the inkfester can't breathe.  
>A creature that starts its turn in the inkfester's space is subjected to the following effect. *Strength or* *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Acid damage, and if the target is Larger or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>A creature within 5 feet of the inkfester can take an action to pull free a creature or object in its space with a successful DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check. Whether it succeeds or not, the creature takes 3 (1d6) Acid damage.
