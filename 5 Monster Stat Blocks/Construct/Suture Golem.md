>## Suture Golem
>*Large Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|5 (-3)|12 (+1)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Slashing from attacks that aren't adamantine
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Immutable Form.*** The suture golem can't shape-shift.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The suture golem has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The suture golem makes two Slam attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Suture Whip.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (4d4 + 3) Force damage.  
>
>***Suture Whip.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Force damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) and is pulled into an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the suture golem. Until the grapple ends, the suture golem can't make Suture Whip attacks against another target.  
>If the suture golem hits a Medium or smaller target with Suture Whip while the target has the Grappled condition and is within 5 feet of the suture golem, the target is pulled into the suture golem's space and sutured to its body. The grapple ends and the target has the Restrained condition. While Restrained, the target moves with the suture golem, and the suture golem can't make attacks against it. It can escape from the Restrained condition in the same fashion as the Grappled condition, but has Disadvantage on its check to do so.  
>The suture golem can have up to two Medium or smaller creatures sutured to its body at a time. While it has two creatures trapped in this way, its Speed is halved. If the suture golem dies, the sutures dissolve, freeing the creatures.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Suture Constrict.*** Each creature Restrained by the suture golem takes 8 (2d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.
