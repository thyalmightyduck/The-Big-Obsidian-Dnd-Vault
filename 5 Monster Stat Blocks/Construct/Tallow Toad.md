___
>## Tallow Toad
>*Tiny Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 17 (5d4 + 5)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|2 (-4)|9 (-1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Death Burst.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the tallow toad when it dies. *Failure:* 3 (1d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Illumination.*** The tallow toad sheds Dim Light in a 10-foot radius.  
>
>***Water Weakness.*** If the tallow toad is doused in water or ends its turn submerged in water, it takes 3 (1d6) Acid damage, or 6 Acid damage if the water is soapy.  
>
>### Actions
>***Tongue of Flame.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 2) Fire damage.  
>
>***Spit.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Fire damage. The target takes half the original damage again at the end of its next turn unless a creature uses an action to remove the hot wax first.
