___
>## Poppet
>*Tiny Construct, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 21 (6d4 + 6)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +2
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't adamantine or silvered
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft. (unimpeded by magical Darkness), Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** One national language, telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the poppet remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary doll.  
>
>***Turning Susceptibility.*** The poppet can be turned as if it were Undead. However, it can't be destroyed by effects that Turn Undead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Embrace.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage. If the target has the Frightened condition, the attack deals 14 (4d6) Psychic damage instead.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Eerie Laugh.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the poppet that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save (with Disadvantage if it can see the poppet), ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. *Failure by 5 or More:* While Frightened, the target's Speed is halved. *Failure or Success:* If the target succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, the target is immune to the Eerie Laugh of all poppets for 24 hours.
