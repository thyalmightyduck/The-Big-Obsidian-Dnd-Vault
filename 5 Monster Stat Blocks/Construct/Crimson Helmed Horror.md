>## Crimson Helmed Horror
>*Medium Construct, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20 (*plate armor*, *shield*)
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren't adamantine
>- **Immunities** Force, Necrotic, Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Stunned
>- **Gear** *Longsword*
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The helmed horror has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Spell Immunity.*** The helmed horror is immune to *burning hands*, *fireball*, and *scorching ray*.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The helmed horror makes two longsword attacks.  
>
>***Flaming Longsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) fire damage, or 9 (1d10 + 4) fire damage if used with two hands.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Conjure Sword.*** As a bonus action on its first turn in combat, the helmed horror conjures a longsword-sized blade of fire, which appears in its free hand. This sword disappears in a cloud of smoke when the helmed horror drops to 0 hit points.
