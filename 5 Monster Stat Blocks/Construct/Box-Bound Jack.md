___
>## Box-Bound Jack
>*Small Construct, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d6 + 10)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Athletics +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Immutable Form.*** Box-Bound Jack can't shape-shift.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Box-Bound Jack make two Cymbal attacks.  
>
>***Cymbal.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) Thunder damage.  
>
>***Dissonant Clash (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature in a 15-foot Emanation originating from Box-Bound Jack. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Thunder damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Box Jumper.*** Box-Bound Jack jumps without provoking Opportunity Attacks. Box-Bound Jack can Long Jump up to 20 feet or High Jump up to 10 feet, with or without a running start.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Bouncing Escape.*** *Trigger:* The Grappled or Restrained condition ends on Box-Bound Jack, or Box-Bound Jack is released from the toymaker's bag. *Response:* Box-Bound Jack jumps up to 20 feet and makes one Cymbal attack.
