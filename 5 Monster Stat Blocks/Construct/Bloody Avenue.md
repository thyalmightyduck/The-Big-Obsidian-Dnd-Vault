>## Bloody Avenue
>*Huge Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 184 (16d12 + 80)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|8 (-1)|20 (+5)|5 (-3)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands one language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Disguised Appearance.*** While the bloody avenue remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal roadway. Other creatures can occupy the avenue's space while it remains motionless. A creature who uses a Search action to examine the roadway and succeeds on a DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation) check spots the blood-tinged mortar with bits of bone and tiny runes on the cobbles, revealing its true nature.  
>
>***Mire.*** The ground within 40 feet of the bloody avenue is Difficult Terrain for creatures of the avenue's choice. A creature that enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The creature has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17). *Failure by 5 or More:* While Grappled, the creature has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***Rise.*** When the bloody avenue rolls Initiative, it rises up from the ground and takes a serpentine form, and each creature in its space is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* The creature is pushed into an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the bloody avenue and has the Prone condition. *Success:* The creature chooses the space it is pushed into and doesn't have the Prone condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The bloody avenue makes two Slam or Hurl Rubble attacks in any combination.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 28 (4d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Hurl Rubble.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 60/120 ft. *Hit:* 24 (4d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Alarum (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Trigger:* A condition set by the bloody avenue's creator, such as a specific creature entering the bloody avenue's space. *Response:* The bloody avenue alerts a creature within 1 mile, sending it a telepathic image of the trigger.
