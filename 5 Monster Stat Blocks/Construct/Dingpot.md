___
>## Dingpot
>*Tiny Construct, Neutral Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 22 (4d4 + 12)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 20 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Medicine +3, Perception +3
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Boil and Bubble.*** While the dingpot doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, it can provide enough heat to boil water within its vessel.  
>
>### Actions
>***Steam Spray.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Fire damage, and the target has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the start of the dingpot's next turn. Being underwater doesn't grant Resistance to this Fire damage.  
>
>***Brew (1/Day).*** The dingpot produces a magical elixir within its vessel. The elixir remains potent for 24 hours. It can choose from the following brews:  
>
>***Healing Draught.*** As a Bonus Action, a creature can drink the elixir to regain 4 (1d6 + 1) Hit Points.  
>
>***Lucky Draught.*** As a Bonus Action, a creature can drink the elixir to gain startling luck for 10 minutes. When the creature fails an ability check or a saving throw, it can reroll the d20 and use the new roll. Once this causes a roll to succeed, the effects of the elixir end early.
