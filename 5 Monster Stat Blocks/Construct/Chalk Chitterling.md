>## Chalk Chitterling
>*Small Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 22 (5d6 + 5)
>- **Speed** 25 ft., Fly 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|4 (-3)|10 (+0)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** None (seems to speak, but it's gibberish)
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Death Burst.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the chalk chitterling when it dies. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Distracting Chatter.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the chalk chitterling and can hear it. *Failure:* The target can't cast or maintain Concentration on spells until the start of its next turn. Constructs are immune to this trait.  
>
>***Water Weakness.*** If the chalk chitterling is doused in water or ends its turn submerged in water, it takes 3 (1d6) Acid damage, or 6 Acid damage if the water is soapy.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dust Whip.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Maddening Chatter (Recharge 6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature within 30 feet of the chalk chitterling that can hear it. *Failure:* 5 (2d4) Psychic damage, and the target can't take Reactions until the end of the chalk chitterling's next turn. *Success:* Half damage. Constructs are immune to this effect.
