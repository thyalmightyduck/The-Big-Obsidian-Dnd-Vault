>## Andora
>*Medium Construct, Neutral Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 84 (13d8 + 26)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +6, Con +4
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Insight +2, Intimidation +3, Investigation +3, Perception +2, Survival +2
>- **Immunities** Lightning; Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Ostoy and five other languages
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Subdermal Plating.*** Critical Hit scored against Andora become normal hits.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Andora makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Force damage plus 2 (1d4) Lightning damage, and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***She's Alive!.*** *Trigger:* Andora takes Lightning damage. *Response:* Andora gains Temporary Hit Points equal to the Lightning damage dealt.
