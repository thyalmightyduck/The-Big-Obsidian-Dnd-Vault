>## Living Thunderwave
>*Medium Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 30 (4d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 25 ft., Fly 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|3 (-4)|6 (-2)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Thunder; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Grappled, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The living spell can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The living spell has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Magical Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Thunder damage.  
>
>***Spell Mimicry (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cube originating from the living spell. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away from the living spell. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* A thunderous boom is audible within 300 feet.
