___
>## Magnetite Armour
>*Medium Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 127 (15d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|1 (-5)|10 (+0)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Cold, Poison, Psychic; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Greatsword*
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Arcanomagnetic Repulsion.*** Attacks against the armour made with ferrous weapons or ammunition are made at disadvantage.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the armour remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a pile of armour plates.  
>
>***Superconductor.*** If the armour takes 10 or more fire damage in a single instance, it falls prone and, until the end of its next turn, it is incapacitated and its flying speed is reduced to 0 feet.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The armour makes two melee attacks or casts Magnetobolt twice.  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) force damage.  
>
>***Magnetobolt.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) force damage and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. A creature wearing ferrous armour makes this saving throw with disadvantage.
