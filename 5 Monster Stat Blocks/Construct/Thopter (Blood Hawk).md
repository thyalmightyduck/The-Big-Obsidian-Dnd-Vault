>*Small Construct, Unaligned*

>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 7 (2d6)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|

>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Sight.*** The thopter has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The thopter has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the thopter's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.  
>
>### Actions
>***Beak.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
