>## Anvilwrought Raptor
>*Tiny Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 18 (4d4 + 8)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|3 (-4)|14 (+2)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands one language of its creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Sight.*** The raptor has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.  
>
>***Recorded Mimicry.*** The raptor can mimic any sound, including voices, it has heard in the last 24 hours. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The raptor makes two attacks with its beak.  
>
>***Beak.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.  
>
> ##### Variant: Anvilwrought Raptor Familiar
>
>Some anvilwrought raptors are created expressly to be familiars. Such creatures have the following trait.
>
>***Familiar.*** The anvilwrought can serve another creature as a familiar, forming a magical, telepathic bond with its willing master. While the two are bonded, the master can sense what the anvilwrought senses, as long as they are within 1 mile of each other.
