>## Tanker
>*Large Construct*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 95 (10d10 + 40)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|8 (-1)|18 (+4)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Acid, Poison; Frightened, Poisoned, Burning|Monstersofdrakkenheim
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Chemical Stench.*** A creature other than a Construct or Undead that starts its turn within 10 feet of the tanker is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Sickened condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to the Chemical Stench of all tankers for 1 hour.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** The tanker is immune to the Burning condition (Acid only).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The tanker makes two Slam or Chemical Glob attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Chemical Glob.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 60/120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Acid, Cold, or Poison damage (tanker's choice).  
>
>***Chemical Spray (Recharge 5–6).*** Each creature in a 30-foot Cone is subjected to one of the following effects of the tanker's choice:  
>- **Sulfuric Acid.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 25 (10d4) Acid damage, and the target has the Burning condition (4d4 Acid). *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Liquid Nitrogen.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Cold damage, and the target has the Frozen condition (DC 14). *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Chlorine Gas.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition (save ends). While Poisoned, the target has the Blinded condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
