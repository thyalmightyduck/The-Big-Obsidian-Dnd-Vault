___
>## Apparmarole
>*Medium Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 14 (4d6)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|4 (-3)|2 (-4)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 7
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Antimagic Susceptibility.*** The apparmarole has the Incapacitated condition while in the area of an *Antimagic Field* spell. If targeted by a *Dispel Magic* spell, the apparmarole must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or have the Unconscious condition for 1 minute.  
>
>***Damage Transfer.*** While grappling a creature, the apparmarole has Resistance to all damage, and the creature Grappled by the apparmarole takes damage equal to that taken by the apparmarole.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the apparmarole remains motionless and isn't flying, it is indistinguishable from normal clothing.  
>
>### Actions
>***Constrict.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12). While Grappled, it has the Restrained and Blinded conditions, and takes 4 (1d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. The apparmarole can grapple one creature at a time.  
>
>***Puppeteer.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature Grappled by the apparmarole. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition for 8 hours. While Charmed, the target wears the apparmarole, shares a space with it, and loses the Grappled condition. The apparmarole and the target have a telepathic link, and the apparmarole can issue the target commands (no action required), which the target does its best to obey.  
>Each time the target takes damage, it repeats the save. On a success, it ends the effect and pushes the apparmarole into the nearest unoccupied space.
