___
>## Living Vitriolic Sphere
>*Large Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 76 (8d10 + 32)
>- **Speed** 25 ft., Fly 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Acid; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Grappled, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The living spell can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The living spell has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The living spell makes two Magical Strike attacks.  
>
>***Magical Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 21 (5d6 + 4) Acid damage.  
>
>***Spell Mimicry (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point within 150 feet. *Failure:* 25 (10d4) Acid damage, and 12 (5d4) Acid damage at the end of the target's next turn. *Success:* Half initial damage only.
