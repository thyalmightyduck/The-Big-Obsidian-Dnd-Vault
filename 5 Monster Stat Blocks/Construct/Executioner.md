>## Executioner
>*Huge Construct (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 22
>- **Hit Points** 250 (20d12 + 140) plus 250 (20d12 + 140) per player character
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|11 (+0)|25 (+7)|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +18, Con +15, Int +8, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Perception +20
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 30
>- **Languages** Understands Common but doesn't speak
>- **Challenge** 25 (XP 75,000; PB +8)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +8
>___
>***Bound to Slaughter.*** If the Executioner begins its turn more than 100 feet away from Slaughterstone Square, it disappears into the Haze and reappears 1 minute later at the center of Slaughterstone Square.  
>
>***Dimensional Anchor.*** The Executioner can't be teleported or sent to another plane of existence unless it chooses to be.  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** The Executioner is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.  
>
>***Necrotic Vents.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the Executioner takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If the Executioner is destroyed, it reforms with all of its Hit Points 1 minute later unless a *Wish* spell is used to stop it. A *Wish* spell used in this manner is always stressful, and the caster must roll to determine whether or not they can cast the *Wish* spell ever again.  
>
>***Stoic.*** The Executioner can't take Reactions.  
>
>### Actions
>***The Executioner always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the Executioner can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the Executioner takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Lumber Forward (1/Round).*** The Executioner moves up to its Speed.  
>
>***Death Grip.*** Spectral chains appear around one creature within 300 feet of the Executioner. The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends. The condition ends if the Executioner uses Death Grip against a different target  
>
>***Guillotine Blade.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +18, reach 10 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 45 (10d6 + 10) Slashing damage which ignores any *Resistance* or Immunity to Slashing damage. Immediately after making a ranged attack, this weapon flies back to the Executioner's hands. Critical *Hit:* The target dies unless it has the Epic Resistance action or Legendary Resistance trait.  
>
>***Destroyer (1/Round).*** The Executioner chooses a Huge or smaller object or structure within 5 feet of it. Alternatively, it can choose a spell or magical effect within 30 feet of it. The object or structure is destroyed, or the spell or effect ends.  
>
>***Summon Grasping Shadows (1/Round).*** The Executioner targets 1d4 corpses within 300 feet of it. Each target's spirit rises as a **Grasping Shadows** in the nearest unoccupied space. The spirits act each round on Initiative count 20, and disappear if the Executioner is destroyed.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The Executioner chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The Executioner can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
