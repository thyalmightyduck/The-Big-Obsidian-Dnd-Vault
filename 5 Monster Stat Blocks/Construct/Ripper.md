>## Ripper
>*Large Construct*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 287 (25d10 + 150)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|11 (+0)|23 (+6)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +11, Wis +7, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +11, Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Dismemberment.*** Creatures killed by attacks made by the ripper can't be restored to life except by a *Resurrection* spell or similar magic.  
>
>***Feed on Fear.*** Frightened creatures have the Helpless and Defenseless conditions against attacks made by the ripper.  
>
>***Insensitive to Pain.*** Critical Hits against the ripper become normal hits.  
>
>### Actions
>***Whirling Bonesaw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 20 (4d6 + 6) Slashing damage, and the ripper can immediately make another Whirling Bonesaw attack against the same target. This attack ignores *Resistance* and Immunity to Slashing damage.  
>
>***Gory Display (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature that can see the ripper. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition (save ends). While Frightened, the target's Speed becomes 0 and can't increase.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Unstoppable Lurch.*** *Trigger:* A creature deals damage to the ripper. *Response:* The ripper moves up to half its Speed toward that creature without provoking Opportunity Attack action. If the ripper ends this movement within reach of the creature, it gains Advantage on its next attack roll against the creature.
