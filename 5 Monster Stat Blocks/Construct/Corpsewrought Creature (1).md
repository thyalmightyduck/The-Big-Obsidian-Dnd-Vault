>## Corpsewrought Creature
>*Medium Construct*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 + PB
>- **Hit Points** 5 + five times your apothecary level (the creature has a number of hit dice [d12s] equal to your apothecary level)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +3 plus PB, Wis +1 plus PB
>- **Skills** Athletics +4 plus PB
>- **Immunities** Poison, Lightning; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals your proficiency bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals your proficiency bonus
>___
>***Lightning Absorption.*** Whenever the corpsewrought creature is subjected to lightning damage, it takes no damage and regains a number of hit points equal to the lightning damage dealt.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  4 plus PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d8 + 4 bludgeoning damage.
