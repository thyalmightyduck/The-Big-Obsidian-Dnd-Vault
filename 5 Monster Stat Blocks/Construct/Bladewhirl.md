___
>## Bladewhirl
>*Medium Construct, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 117 (18d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|3 (-4)|8 (-1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't adamantine
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Grappled Menace.*** The bladewhirl can't impose the Grappled condition. It has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature that has it Grappled.  
>
>***Indiscriminate.*** On each of its turns, the bladewhirl moves to and attacks the closest creature it can sense. If it can't sense any creatures, it takes the Bounce action and moves in a random direction, searching for a creature to attack.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The bladewhirl makes three Blades attacks.  
>
>***Blades.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 7 (1d6 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Bounce.*** The bladewhirl takes the Dash action. At any point during this movement, the bladewhirl can jump a vertical or horizontal distance up to its remaining Speed. It takes no falling damage from this jump.
