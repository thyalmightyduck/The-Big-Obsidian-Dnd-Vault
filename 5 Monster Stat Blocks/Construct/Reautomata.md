>## Reautomata
>*Medium Construct*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 170 (20d8 + 80)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Climb 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +12 (22)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|19 (+4)|19 (+4)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +9, Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Relentless Endurance.*** If damage reduces the reautomata to 0 Hit Points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 plus the damage taken, unless the damage is from a Critical Hit. On a success, the reautomata drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>***Standing Leap.*** The reautomata's Long Jump is up to 50 feet and its High Jump is up to 30 feet, with or without a running start.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The reautomata makes two Arm Blade attacks and one Entrapping Rend attack.  
>
>***Arm Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (3d6 + 5) Slashing damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it is pushed up to 5 feet away from the reautomata and has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Entrapping Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft., one Prone target. *Hit:* 15 (3d6 + 5) Piercing damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the reautomata can't make Entrapping Rend attacks against another target.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The reautomata moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see. This movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attack action.
