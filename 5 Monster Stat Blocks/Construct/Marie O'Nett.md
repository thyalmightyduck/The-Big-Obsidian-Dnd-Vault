___
>## Marie O'Nett
>*Small Construct, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d6 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Immutable Form.*** Marie O'Nett can't shape-shift.  
>
>***On Strings.*** Marie O'Nett can High Jump up to 30 feet. It takes no falling damage when descending from this jump.  
>
>***Spellbinding Movement.*** When Marie O'Nett moves, it can choose one creature within 30 feet that can see it, subjecting the creature to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* 3 (1d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Charmed condition until the end of Marie O'Nett's next turn or until it takes damage. While Charmed, the target's Speed is halved.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Marie O'Nett makes two Whip attacks.  
>
>***Whip.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Hoisted Escape.*** *Trigger:* The Grappled or Restrained condition ends on Marie O'Nett, or Marie O'Nett is released from the toymaker's bag. *Response:* Marie O'Nett High Jumps up to 30 feet and uses Spellbinding Movement.
