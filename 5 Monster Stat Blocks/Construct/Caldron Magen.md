___
>## Caldron Magen
>*Medium Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 67 (9d8 + 27)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Acid, Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands Common plus two other languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Disintegration.*** If the magen dies, it disintegrates into dust, leaving behind anything it was wearing or carrying.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The magen has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The magen makes two attacks, using Extended Fist or Acid Spittle in any combination.  
>
>***Extended Fist.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 5 (1d10) Acid damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) from one of two fists.  
>
>***Acid Spittle.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Acid damage.
