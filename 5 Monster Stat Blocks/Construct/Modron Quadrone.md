>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Force damage.  
>
>***Gears Launcher.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 320 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Force damage.
