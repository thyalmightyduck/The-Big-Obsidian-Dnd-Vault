>## Living Cone of Cold
>*Large Construct, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 105 (10d10 + 50)
>- **Speed** 25 ft., Fly 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Cold; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Grappled, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The living spell can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The living spell has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The living spell makes two Magical Strike attacks.  
>
>***Magical Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 27 (5d8 + 5) Cold damage.  
>
>***Spell Mimicry (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Cold damage. *Success:* Half damage.
