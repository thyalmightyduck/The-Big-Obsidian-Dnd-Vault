# Monsters of Drakkenheim (MoDk)
___
## Allopine
*Small Aberration*
___
- **[[Armor Class]]** 12
- **[[Hit Point|Hit Points]]** 21 (6d6)
- **[[Speed]]** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
- **[[Initiative]]** +4 (14)
___

|  STR   |   DEX   |   CON   |  INT   |   WIS   |  CHA   |
| :----: | :-----: | :-----: | :----: | :-----: | :----: |
| 6 (-2) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) | 13 (+1) | 5 (-3) |
___
- **Skills** [[Perception]] +3, [[Stealth]] +4
- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., [[Passive Perception]] 13, Passive Insight 11, Passive Stealth 14
- **Languages** Understands Deep Speech but can't speak
- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
- **[[Proficiency Bonus]]** +2
___
### Traits
***Alien Intellect.*** The allopine is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom ([[Insight]]) checks made to ascertain the allopine's intentions or sincerity have [[Disadvantage]].  

***Delerium Healing.*** As an [[action]], the allopine can touch a delerium [[shard]] to regain 10 (3d6) [[Hit Point|Hit Points]]. Once the allopine has used a delerium [[shard]] in this way, the [[shard]] can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  

***Fully Contaminated.*** The allopine is immune to [[Contamination]] and has [[Advantage]] on [[Saving Throw|saving throws]] against contaminated spells. The allopine may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain [[Contamination]] levels.  

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The allopine makes three Quill attacks.  

***Quill.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  

### [[Bonus Action|Bonus Actions]]
***Nimble Escape.*** The allopine takes the [[Disengage Action|Disengage]] or [[Hide action]].
