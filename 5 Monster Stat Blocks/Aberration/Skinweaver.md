>## Skinweaver
>*Large Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 68 (8d10 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Any three languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Shocking Visage.*** A Humanoid that starts its turn within 30 feet of the skinweaver and can see it is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. *Failure or Success:* The target automatically succeeds on further saves against Shocking Visage until it loses sight of the skinweaver.  
>
>***Supernaturally Sharp.*** The skinweaver's Tanning Knife attacks ignore Resistance (but not Immunity) to Slashing damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skinweaver make two Tanning Knife attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Web Whip.  
>
>***Tanning Knife.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Web Whip.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 25 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Piercing damage, and the target is pulled up to 20 feet toward the skinweaver.  
>
>***Web Net (Recharge 5–6).*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* The target is covered with leathery webbing and has the Restrained condition. A creature can take an action to free the creature with a successful DC 13 Strength check. The webbing can also be attacked (AC 10; 10 Hit Points; Vulnerability to Slashing damage; Immunity to Bludgeoning, Poison, and Psychic damage).
