___
>## Elder Thing
>*Large Aberration (Mythos), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 161 (14d10 + 84)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|16 (+3)|22 (+6)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +7, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Medicine +6, Perception +6
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Elder Thing plus 1d6 others
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The elder thing has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elder thing makes up to two Grasping Tentacle attacks and uses Spellcasting.  
>
>***Grasping Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, Reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18). While Grappled the target has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***At Will:.*** *Charm Person*, *Scorching Ray*, *Thunderwave*.  
>1/day: *Cone of Cold*, *Fear*, *Ice Storm*.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The elder thing casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Charm Person*, *Scorching Ray*, *Thunderwave*  
>1/day: *Cone of Cold*, *Fear*, *Ice Storm*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Surgical Eye.*** The elder thing assesses the anatomy of a creature it can see within 30 ft. and makes a DC 15 Intelligence (Medicine) check. If it succeeds, its attacks against the target are critical hits on die rolls of 18 or higher for the next minute or until it uses Surgical Eye against a different target. If the elder thing succeeds on this check a second time against the same creature, its attacks against the target are instead critical hits on die rolls of 16 or higher for the next minute or until it uses Surgical Eye against a different target.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 2. Immediately after another creature's turn, the elder thing can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The elder thing regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Attack.** The elder thing makes a Grasping Tentacle attack.
>- **Ghoulish Study.** The elder thing uses Surgical Eye.
>- **Sudden Movement.** The elder thing moves up to its Speed.
