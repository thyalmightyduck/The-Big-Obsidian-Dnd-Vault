___
>## Elder Thing
>*Large Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 209 (22d10 + 88)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 90 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|20 (+5)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +9, Int +10
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Psychic, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Lightning; Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Deep Speech; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The elder thing has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elder thing makes two Soothing Tentacle attacks and uses Psychic Skewer.  
>
>***Soothing Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target has the Charmed condition until the start of the elder thing's next turn.  
>
>***Mind-Scouring Spores (Recharge 6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the elder thing. *Failure:* 55 (10d10) Psychic damage. For 1 minute, whenever the target takes the Magic action, it takes 10 (3d6) Psychic damage. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Psychic Skewer.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 18, one creature within 60 feet. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Psychic damage. If the target has the Charmed condition, it gains 1 Exhaustion level and has the Stunned condition while Charmed.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Eldritch Magic (3/Day).*** The elder thing casts *Command*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Gust of Wind*, or *Nondetection*, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18).
