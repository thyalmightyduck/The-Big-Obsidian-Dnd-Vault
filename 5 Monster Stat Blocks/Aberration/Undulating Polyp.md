___
>## Undulating Polyp
>*Small Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d6 + 40)
>- **Speed** 15 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +3, Wis +5, Cha +0
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Piercing
>- **Immunities** Bludgeoning, Slashing; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Displacement.*** The polyp's form is constantly shifting and moving through reality in unnatural ways. Attack rolls against the polyp have Disadvantage. If the polyp takes damage, this trait ceases to function until the start of its next turn. This trait is suppressed while the polyp has the Incapacitated or Restrained conditions or is otherwise unable to move.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The polyp makes two Mucus attacks.  
>
>***Mucus.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Acid damage, and a 5-foot radius centered on the target is covered in acidic slime for 1 minute. A creature other than the polyp that enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Acid damage, and the target has the Burning condition (3d6 Acid).  
>
>***Slip Space.*** The polyp teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 20 feet. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the polyp when it reappears. *Failure:* 16 (3d10) Force damage. Failure by 5 or More: The target triggers an Arcane Anomalies centered on itself. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shift Step.*** The polyp teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 60 feet.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Confused Realities.*** *Trigger:* The polyp takes damage from a creature it can see within 30 feet. *Response:* The triggering creature is teleported to an unoccupied space the polyp can see within 30 feet of its original space. The destination must be on a surface or liquid that can support the target without the target having to squeeze.
