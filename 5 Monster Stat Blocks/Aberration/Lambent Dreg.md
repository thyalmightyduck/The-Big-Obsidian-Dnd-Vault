>## Lambent Dreg
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Arcane Hunger.*** The dreg has Advantage on attack rolls against creatures that have one or more levels of Contamination.  
>
>***Octarine Glow.*** The dreg sheds Bright Light in a 15-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 15 feet.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dreg makes three Octarine Beam attacks.  
>
>***Octarine Beam.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Radiant damage, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target gains one level of Contamination.  
>
>***Wave of Contamination (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature within 30 feet. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Radiant damage plus 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage and gain one level of Contamination. *Success:* Half damage.
