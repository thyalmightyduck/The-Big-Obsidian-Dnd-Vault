>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage. Critical Hit: If the target isn't infected by a cauchemange larva, the larva burrows into it. Once inside, the larva is a disease. Each time an infected target finishes a Long Rest, it is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 10. *Failure:* The target regains no Hit Points from the rest. After 7 days, the larva erupts from the target as a **Corporeal Cauchemange** or an **Incorporeal Cauchemange**, dealing 20 (4d8 + 2) Slashing damage to the target.
