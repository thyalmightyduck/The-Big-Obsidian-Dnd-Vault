>## Psychophant
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 117 (18d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Persuasion +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Poison
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common (only repeats phrases it has heard)
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The psychophant makes two Psychic Grasp attacks.  
>
>***Psychic Grasp.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage and the target has the Immobilized condition (Wisdom saving throw DC 14 save ends).  
>
>***Final Scream (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 40 (9d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage, and the target is immune to this psychophant's Final Scream for 24 hours.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The psychophant shape-shifts into a Small or Medium Humanoid it has seen or reverts to its true form. Its game statistics are the same in each form.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Life Siphon.*** The psychophant deals 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage to a creature it damaged this turn with Psychic Grasp, then regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>***Teleport.*** The psychophant teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet. It can bring one creature Immobilized by its Psychic Grasp, which teleports to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the psychophant's destination space.
