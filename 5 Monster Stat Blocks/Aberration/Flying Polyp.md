___
>## Flying Polyp
>*Huge Aberration (Mythos), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 189 (18d12 + 72)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|11 (+0)|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Con +8, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +12, Intimidation +4, Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 others
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The flying polyp has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The flying polyp makes up to four Grasping Tentacle attacks and uses Wind Blast (if available).  
>
>***Writhing Tentacles.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, Reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 16 (1d10 + 10) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 24). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***Wind Blast (Recharge 5–6).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 24, each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the flying polyp can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Cold damage and the target is pushed up to 30 feet away from the flying polyp in a straight line. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Grasping Wind.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 24, on creature within 2,000 feet. The flying polyp must have an unblocked line between it and its target, but the line does not need to be straight. The polyp does not need to see the target. *Failure:* The target's Speed is reduced to 0 and cannot be increased until the end of the flying polyp's next turn. *Success:* No effect.  
>
>***Invisibility (Recharge 5–6).*** The flying polyp gains the Invisible condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 2. Immediately after another creature's turn, the flying polyp can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The flying polyp regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Attack.** The flying polyp makes a Writhing Tentacles.
>- **Sudden Movement.** The flying polyp moves up to its Speed.
