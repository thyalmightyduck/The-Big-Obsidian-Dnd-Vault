___
>## Shadow Mauler
>*Large Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d10 + 24)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Radiant
>- **Immunities** Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft. (unimpeded by magical Darkness), Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Deep Speech
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Shadow Shape.*** In Dim Light or Darkness, the shadow mauler can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Sunlight Vulnerability.*** While in sunlight, the shadow mauler loses its Immunities. It also has Disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The shadow mauler makes two Bite attacks.  
>
>***Bites.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 19 (3d10 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shadow Escape.*** In Dim Light or Darkness, the shadow mauler can take the Disengage or Hide action as a Bonus Action.
