>## Haze Hulk
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 95 (10d10 + 40)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|3 (-4)|6 (-2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Mutant Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the hulk to 0 Hit Points, it makes a Constitution saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken) unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. On a successful save, the hulk drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hulk makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Trample (Recharge 5–6).*** The hulk moves its speed. During this movement, it can move through the spaces of Medium and smaller creatures, as well as structures made of wood or stone that are less than 1 foot thick. When the hulk enters another creature's space for the first time during this movement, it can make one Slam attack against that creature. If the target is a creature, it is subject to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The targeted creature has the Prone condition.
