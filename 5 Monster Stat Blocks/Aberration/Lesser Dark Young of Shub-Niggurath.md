>## Lesser Dark Young of Shub-Niggurath
>*Large Aberration (Mythos), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 105 (10d10 + 50)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|9 (-1)|21 (+5)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Con +7, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d3 other languages
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The lesser dark young of Shub-Niggurath has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lesser dark young of Shub-Niggurath makes four Flailing Tentacle attacks. It can replace two attacks with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Flailing Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, Reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13).  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The lesser dark young of Shub-Niggurath casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Dispel Magic*, *Entangle*, *Pass without Trace*, *Plant Growth*  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 1. Immediately after another creature's turn, the lesser dark young of shub-niggurath can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The lesser dark young of shub-niggurath regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Swarming Tentacles.** The lesser dark young of Shub-Niggurath makes two Flailing Tentacle attacks.
>- **Clamor of Hooves.** The lesser dark young of Shub-Niggurath moves up to its Speed.
