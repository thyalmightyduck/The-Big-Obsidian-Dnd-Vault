>## Brainmuncher
>*Tiny Aberration (Bespoke Companion, Crafted Familiar); Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 2 (1d4)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Deception +3, Insight +3, Perception +3
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Deep Speech, telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB equals your bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals your bonus
>___
>***Brainmuncher.*** The brainmuncher likes to eat the brains of Tiny beasts, like household pets. If left unattended, it will attempt to do so (GM's discretion).  
>
>### Actions
>***Tentacles.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) psychic damage.  
>
>***Levitate.*** The brainmuncher casts the *levitate* spell on itself.
