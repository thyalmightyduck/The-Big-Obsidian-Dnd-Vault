___
>## Agog
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 30 (4d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Climb 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Deep Speech but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The agog is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the agog's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the agog can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the agog has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The agog is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The agog may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The agog makes one Claw attack and one Bite attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Acid damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Slashing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Gobble.*** The agog makes a Bite attack against a Prone creature.
