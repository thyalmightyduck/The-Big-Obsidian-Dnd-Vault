___
>## Deep Siren
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Deception +7, Perception +5
>- **Immunities** Cold, Lightning
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common; telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The deep siren can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The deep siren makes two Storm Lash attacks.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The deep siren casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>2/day each: *Fog Cloud*, *Lightning Bolt*  
>1/day each: *Conjure Elemental* (water only), *Control Water*  
>
>***Storm Lash.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 90 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed up to 10 feet away from the deep siren.  
>
>***Discordant Song (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature of the deep siren's choice in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the deep siren. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Psychic damage, and the target must roll 1d6 and subtract the result from each D20 Test it makes. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>
>***Illusory Appearance.*** The deep siren covers herself and anything she is wearing or carrying with a magical illusion that makes her look like another creature of her general size and shape. The illusion ends if the siren takes a Bonus Action to end it or if she dies.  
>The changes wrought by this effect fail to hold up to physical inspection. For example, the siren could appear to have smooth skin, but someone touching them would feel their fishy flesh. Otherwise, a creature must take an action to visually inspect the illusion and succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check to discern that the siren is disguised.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step.*** The deep siren magically teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet.
