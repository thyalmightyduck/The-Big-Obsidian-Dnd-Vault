>## Corporeal Cauchemange
>*Medium Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Intimidation +5, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft, Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** The languages of its host, telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Hallucinatory Hold.*** The cauchemange needs only to touch a creature to deal damage, grappling a creature by force of presence rather than physical strength. The cauchemange has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature it has Grappled. A creature Grappled by the cauchemange can also use Wisdom (Insight) to escape.  
>
>***Happiness Susceptibility.*** A creature within 30 feet of the cauchemange can take an action to inspire happiness or hope and make a DC 13 Charisma (Performance) check. *Success:* The cauchemange has Disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of the creature's next turn.  
>
>***Implant Larva.*** A Humanoid killed by the cauchemange is implanted. A **Cauchemange Larva** emerges from the Humanoid's corpse 8 hours later.  
>
>***Nightmare Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the cauchemange is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to the Nightmare Aura of all cauchemanges for 1 hour.  
>
>***Psychic Teleport (Recharge 5–6).*** As its movement for its turn, the cauchemange can teleport to an unoccupied space within 30 feet, provided the space is within 10 feet of a creature with an Intelligence score of 3 or higher. The cauchemange doesn't need to see the destination.  
>
>### Actions
>***Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d12 + 3) Psychic damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). The target believes the damage to be of a type appropriate to the cauchemange's form.  
>
>***Drain.*** The cauchemange makes a Touch attack against a creature it has Grappled. Hit—*Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target's Hit Points maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a Long Rest. If this effect reduces the target's Hit Points maximum to 0, it dies.  
>
>***Glamor.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, one Humanoid within 30 feet that can see the cauchemange. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition and sees the cauchemange as something or someone desirable. The target regards the cauchemange as a trusted friend to be heeded and protected. The target isn't under the cauchemange's control, but it takes the cauchemange's requests and actions in the most favorable light, it is a willing target for the cauchemange's Touch attack, and it becomes a willing host for a cauchemange larva, which it also sees as something desirable.  
>Each time the cauchemange or its allies do something harmful to it, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 24 hours or if the cauchemange is destroyed, is on a different plane of existence, or ends the effect early as a Bonus Action.
