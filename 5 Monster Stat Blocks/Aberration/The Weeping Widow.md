>## The Weeping Widow
>*Medium Aberration, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 104 (19d8 + 19)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +8, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +8, History +8, Medicine +9, Religion +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish, Giant; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the widow fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Reopened Memories.*** At the start of her turn, if the widow can see a memento of her husband or some other reminder that forces her to realize what she's lost, she has Disadvantage on D20 Tests and can only expend 2 uses of her Legendary Actions until the start of her next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The widow makes two Injection attacks.  
>
>***Injection.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 1 Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage. If the target is a creature, it has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn. While Poisoned, the target suffers one of the following effects of the widow's choice (each option can only be used once per turn):  
>- **Hyperphobic Stimulant.** The target has the Frightened condition. At the start of its turn, the target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or take the Dash action and move away from the widow by the safest and shortest available route, unless there is nowhere to move.
>- **Soporific Infusion.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, the target hit by Injection. *Failure:* The target's Speed is halved. On its turn, it can take either an action or a Bonus Action, but not both, and it can make only one attack if it takes the Attack action. Failure by 5 or More: The target has the Unconscious condition for 1 minute, until it takes damage, or until a creature takes an action to shake it awake.
>- **Thaumaturgic Inhibitor.** If the target casts a spell, it must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or the spell fails, but a spell slot used to cast the spell isn't expended.
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Alacrity.** The widow or a creature she is riding moves up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. Dose. The widow makes one Injection attack. She can't take this action again until the start of her next turn.
>- **Hypertrophic Injections.** The widow targets herself or an ally within 5 feet and grants the target one of the following effects of her choice; she can't take this action again until the start of her next turn:
  - **Aggression.** The target has Advantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
  - **Fortitude.** One of the following conditions of the widow's choice ends on the target: Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Restrained, Stunned.
  - **Vigor.** The target regains 10 Hit Points.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Weeping Widow's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Dulled Memories.** Within 1 mile of the lair, creatures have Disadvantage on Intelligence checks made to recall information related to painful or emotional memories.
>- **Faded Dreams.** Within 1 mile of the lair, creatures who sleep or Trance have only vague impressions of dreams and recall no details.
>
>If the Weeping Widow dies or moves her lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
