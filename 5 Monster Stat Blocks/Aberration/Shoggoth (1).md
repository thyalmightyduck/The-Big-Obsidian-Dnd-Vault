>## Shoggoth
>*Huge Aberration (Mythos), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 276 (24d12 + 120)
>- **Speed** 60 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|24 (+7)|11 (+0)|20 (+5)|8 (-1)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +15, Dex +5, Con +10, Int +4, Wis +6, Cha +3
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Immunities** Acid, Necrotic, Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 other languages
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The shoggoth can move through a space as narrow as 1 foot without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Amphibious.*** The shoggoth can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The shoggoth has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The shoggoth makes four attacks, using Horrid Tendril or Hurled Mucous in any combination. It can't attack the same target more than once.  
>
>***Horrid Tendril.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, Reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 21 (2d10 + 10) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 20), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends. It can Grapple up to four Large or smaller creatures at a time.  
>
>***Hurled Mucous.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +15, range 200 ft. *Hit:* 21 (2d10 + 10) Acid damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Slime Wave (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 44 (8d10) Acid damage. *Success:* Half damage. For 1 minute, the area within the Cone is drenched with slimy acid. When a creature enters the area for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there, it takes 22 (4d10) Acid damage. A creature can take this damage only once per turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Horrid Digestion.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 23, one creature Grappled by the shoggoth. *Failure:* The target takes 32 (4d10 + 10) Acid damage.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 1. Immediately after another creature's turn, the shoggoth can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The shoggoth regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Thrash of Shifting Forms.** The shoggoth makes a Horrid Tendril or Hurled Mucous attack.
>- **Bear Down Upon.** The shoggoth moves up to its Speed.
