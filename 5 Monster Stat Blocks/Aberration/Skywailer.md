>## Skywailer
>*Large Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 112 (15d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|4 (-3)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Air Buoyancy.*** If the skywailer dies or has the Incapacitated condition, it floats upward 10 feet on its turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skywailer makes two attacks, using Tentacle or Ectoplasmic Tendril in any combination.  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The skywailer has ten tentacles, each of which can grapple one target.  
>
>***Ectoplasmic Tendril.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. A creature can take an action to destroy the tendril with a DC 15 Strength check, freeing the target. The tendril can also be attacked (AC 10; 10 Hit Points; Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage). Destroying a tendril deals no damage to the skywailer.  
>
>***Wail (Recharge 6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature Grappled by the skywailer. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Psychic damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Jet.*** While flying, the skywailer can take the Dash action as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Quick Reel.*** The skywailer pulls each creature Grappled by it up to 15 feet toward it.
