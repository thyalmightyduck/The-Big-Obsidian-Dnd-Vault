>***Mythos Fanaticism.*** If the deep one hybrid can see a Mythos creature of size Large or larger, it gains a +5 bonus to Initiative rolls and gains 2 Temporary Hit Points at the start of its turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Trident.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, Reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Light Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +2, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d8) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***My Life for Yours.*** *Trigger:* A Mythos creature without this Reaction is hit by a melee attack. *Response:* This creature becomes the target of the triggering attack if it is in range of the original attack.
