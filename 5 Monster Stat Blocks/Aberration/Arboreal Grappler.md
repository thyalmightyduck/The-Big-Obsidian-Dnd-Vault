# Tome of Beasts 1 2023 Edition (ToB123)
___
## Arboreal Grappler
*Medium Aberration, Neutral Evil*
___
- **[[Armor Class]]** 14 (natural armor)
- **[[Hit Point|Hit Points]]** 90 (12d8 + 36)
- **[[Speed]]** 10 ft., Climb 40 ft.
- **[[Initiative]]** +3 (13)
___

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
___
- **Skills** [[Acrobatics]] +5, [[Stealth]] +5
- **Senses** [[Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[Passive Perception]] 10, Passive Insight 10, Passive Stealth 15
- **Languages** —
- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
- **[[Proficiency Bonus]]** +2
___
### Traits
***Boscage Brachiation.*** The arboreal [[grappler]] doesn't provoke [[Opportunity Attack|opportunity attacks]] when it climbs out of an [[enemy]]'s reach.  

***Mobile Grappler.*** The arboreal [[grappler]] can move at its full [[speed]] when dragging a Medium or smaller creature it has [[grappled]].  

***Spider Climb.*** The arboreal [[grappler]] can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an [[ability check]].  

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The arboreal [[grappler]] makes one Bite attack and two Tentacle attacks.  

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.  

***Tentacle.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the target is [[grappled]] (escape DC 13). Until this [[Grapple Action|grapple]] ends, the target is [[restrained]]. The arboreal [[grappler]] has two tentacles, each of which can [[Grapple Action|grapple]] only one target.
