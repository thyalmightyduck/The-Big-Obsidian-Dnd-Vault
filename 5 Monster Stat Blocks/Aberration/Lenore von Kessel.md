>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Lenore makes three attacks with her Tentacle Hair.  
>
>***Tentacle Hair.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a creature it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target gains one level of Contamination.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Radiant Gaze (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Radiant damage and the target gains one level of Contamination. *Success:* Half damage only. If Lenore sees her reflection within the Cone, she gains the Blinded and Incapacitated conditions for 1d6 hours.
