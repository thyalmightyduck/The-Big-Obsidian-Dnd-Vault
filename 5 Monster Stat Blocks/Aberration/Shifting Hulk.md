>## Shifting Hulk
>*Large Aberration, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 168 (16d10 + 80)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|9 (-1)|11 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands one language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Aberrant Wound.*** A creature that takes damage from the shifting hulk's attacks is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target suffers a wound. The target loses 5 (2d4) Hit Points for each wound it is suffering at the start of each of its turns. A creature can take an action to staunch the wounds, ending this effect with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check. The wounds also close if the target receives magical healing. Constructs and Undead are immune to this trait.  
>
>***Fast Healing.*** While it is Bloodied, the shifting hulk regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. This trait doesn't function while the shifting hulk has 0 Hit Points  
>
>***Vicious Critical.*** When the shifting hulk scores a Critical Hit with an attack, it can roll the attack's damage dice three times instead of twice.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The shifting hulk makes two Claw or Spine attacks and uses Frightening Attacks.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 26 (6d6 + 5) Slashing damage, and the shifting hulk inflicts an Aberrant Wound.  
>
>***Spine.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) Piercing damage, and the shifting hulk inflicts an Aberrant Wound.  
>
>***Frightening Attacks.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature of the shifting hulk's choice within 30 feet that is aware of the shifting hulk. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. Once a creature succeeds on a save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to Frightening Attacks for 24 hours or until it sees the shifting hulk score a Critical Hit.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The shifting hulk shape-shifts into a form that resembles its Small or Medium Humanoid appearance before it became a horror, or it returns to its true form. Its statistics are the same in each form, except for its size. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.
