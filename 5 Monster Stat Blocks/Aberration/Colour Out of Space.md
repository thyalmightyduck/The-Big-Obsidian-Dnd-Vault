___
>## Colour Out of Space
>*Large Aberration (Mythos), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 135 (18d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|22 (+6)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +9
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 other languages
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The colour out of space has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The colour out of space makes two Draining Lash attacks.  
>
>***Draining Lash.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, Reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Absorb Life (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, non-Mythos creatures in a 30-foot Emanation centered on the colour out of space. *Failure:* 44 (8d10) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage. If three or more creatures fail their saving throw against this effect, the colour out of space gains an additional action on its next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The colour out of space casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Dispel Magic*, *Dominate Monster*, *Tongues*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Horrid Burrowing.*** The colour out of space destroys an unattended, non-magical object that it can touch that has Hit Points less than or equal to the colour's maximum Hit Points.
