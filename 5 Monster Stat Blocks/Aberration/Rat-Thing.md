>## Rat-Thing
>*Tiny Aberration (Mythos), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 14 (4d4 + 4)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Int +4, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, History +4, Insight +5, Investigation +4, Religion +4, Stealth +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 30 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 other languages
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Hexing Aura.*** Each creature of the rat-thing's choice in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the rat-thing gains a +1 bonus to its spell save DC.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The rat-thing has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, Reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d3 + 2) Piercing damage.
