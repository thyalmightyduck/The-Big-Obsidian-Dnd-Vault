>## Tachna Gallery
>*Huge Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 250 (20d12 + 120)
>- **Speed** 0 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|22 (+6)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|22 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Con +12, Wis +9, Cha +12
>- **Skills** Deception +12, Stealth +9
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Sleep
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 19 (XP 22,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Demiplane.*** The subject content of the tachna gallery becomes a 60-foot Cube-shaped sentient demiplane designed to lure and trap victims. A creature within a 10-foot Emanation is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 20 (a target touching the tachna gallery automatically fails). *Failure:* The target is pulled into the tachna gallery's demiplane, arriving in a random unoccupied space. The target can still attack the tachna gallery from within it, and can't leave the demiplane by any means until the tachna gallery is defeated. *Success:* The target is immune to this trait for 1 hour.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the tachna gallery fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Psychic Aura.*** creature that starts its turn in the tachna gallery's demiplane takes 21 (6d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The tachna gallery uses Tremor and makes three Tentacle attacks.  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +18, reach 60 ft. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Tremor.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in the tachna's demiplane. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Mind Blast (Recharge 6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation originating from the tachna gallery. *Failure:* 54 (12d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Stunned condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it automatically succeeds.
