___
>## Mukad Warrior
>*Medium Aberration, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 10 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Mukad, telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The mukad has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Psychic Defender.*** Any non-Aberration that starts its turn within 10 feet of the mukad warrior is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target can't attack any creature other than the mukad warrior, another mukad with this trait, or another creature compelling the target to attack it.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The mukad warrior can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Tunneler.*** The mukad warrior can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrowing Speed. When it burrows, it leaves a 5-foot diameter tunnel in its wake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mukad warrior makes one Bite attack and one Claws attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Poison damage, and if the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>If the target drops to 0 Hit Points while Poisoned, it is Stable but has the Poisoned condition for 1 hour and has the Paralyzed condition until it is no longer Poisoned.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Psychic Distraction.*** *Trigger:* A creature the mukad warrior can sense within 30 feet makes an attack roll against a creature that isn't a mukad warrior or another creature compelling the creature to attack it. *Response:* The mukad warrior imposes Disadvantage on the target's roll. If the target has Immunity to the Frightened condition, this Reaction fails.
