>## Displacer Dreg
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|19 (+4)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Displacement.*** Attack rolls against the dreg have Disadvantage. If it is hit by an attack roll, this trait does not apply until the end of its next turn. This trait does not apply while the dreg has the Incapacitated condition or has a Speed of 0.  
>
>### Actions
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Teleport (Recharge 4–6).*** The dreg teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. Before or after teleporting, the dreg can make one melee attack. If this attack hits, it deals an extra 10 (3d6) Psychic damage.
