>## Hazewing Moth Chrysalis
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 28 (3d8 + 15)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Climb 5 ft.
>- **Initiative** -5 (5)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|1 (-5)|20 (+5)|1 (-5)|5 (-3)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Anchored.*** The chrysalis is firmly anchored in place. It can't be forced to move or teleport unless it chooses to be.  
>
>***Haze Aura.*** The chrysalis conjures Deep Haze in a 30-foot Emanation, which renders its area Lightly Obscured. A creature that starts its turn in the area is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level. *Failure or Success:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Harden.*** The chrysalis regains 7 (2d6) Hit Points, gaining any excess healing as Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sudden Emergence.*** *Trigger:* The chrysalis is reduced to 0 Hit Points. *Response:* Roll 1d6. On a 6, a Hazewing Moth Adult emerges from the chrysalis, sharing its Initiative count. On a 1-5, vile goo spills out of the chrysalis, and each creature within 5 feet of it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level. *Failure or Success:* 10 (3d6) Acid damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.
