___
>## Reality Cyst
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 4 (1d8)
>- **Speed** 0 ft.
>- **Initiative** -5 (5)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|1 (-5)|1 (-5)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|13 (+1)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Immutable Form.*** The reality cyst is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.  
>
>***Unhinged Reality.*** The reality cyst emits a 120-foot Emanation of unhinged reality. A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Immobilized condition until it leaves the Emanation. While Immobilized, the target believes itself to be in an altered and confused environment of shifting forms, and can't see or hear anything else. The target does not perceive itself to be stationary, and instead believes it is walking through a bizarre landscape. It can take an action on its turn to repeat the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. If the creature succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to the reality cyst's Unhinged Reality for 24 hours.  
>
>***Unusual Nature.*** The reality cyst doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.  
>
>### Actions
>***Psychic Whip.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target has the Dazed condition until the end of the reality cyst's next turn. The reality cyst can target a creature Immobilized by its Unhinged Reality with this attack even if the creature has Total Cover.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Feed.*** Each creature Immobilized by Unhinged Reality takes 10 (3d6) Psychic damage. The reality cyst regains Hit Points equal to the total damage dealt, and its Hit Point Maximum increases by the same amount.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Fracture.*** *Trigger:* The reality cyst takes damage. *Response—**Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation originating from the reality cyst. *Failure:* The target has the Addled condition (save ends).
