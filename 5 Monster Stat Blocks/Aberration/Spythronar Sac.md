>## Spythronar Sac
>*Tiny Aberration, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 5
>- **Hit Points** 1 (1d4 - 1)
>- **Speed** 0 ft.
>- **Initiative** -5 (5)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|1 (-5)|1 (-5)|8 (-1)|1 (-5)|3 (-4)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Tremorsense 10 ft., Passive Perception 6
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 0 (XP 10; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** The spythronar sac appears to be a tangled ball of string, twigs, and dirt. A creature that can see the sac can identify it with a successful DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana or Nature) check.  
>
>***Fragile.*** A creature who enters the spythronar sac's space must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or the sac is destroyed.  
>
>***Lightning Release.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 10, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the sac when it is destroyed. *Failure:* 2 (1d4) Lightning damage. *Failure or Success:* Each spythronar in the area gains Advantage on its next attack roll.  
>
>***Shocking Birth.*** When a spythronar sac takes Lightning damage from a source other than another spythronar, it hatches, transforming into a **Spythronar Swarm** with 25 Hit Points.
