___
>## Sentient Planetoid
>*Gargantuan Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 330 (20d20 + 200)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 300 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|1 (-5)|30 (+10)|27 (+8)|21 (+5)|23 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +14, Wis +11, Cha +12
>- **Skills** Arcana +14, History +14, Insight +11, Nature +14, Perception +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 1 mile, Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 1 mile
>- **Challenge** 17 (XP 18,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The sentient planetoid is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the sentient planetoid's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Beyond Space and Time.*** The sentient planetoid always acts on Initiative count 20. Additionally, it can't be moved, teleported, or sent to another plane of existence against its will.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the sentient planetoid can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the sentient planetoid has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The sentient planetoid is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The sentient planetoid may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Gravitational Pull.*** The sentient planetoid exerts its own force of gravity in a 100-foot Emanation. A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation falls towards the sentient planetoid, taking falling damage as appropriate if it collides with the planetoid. A creature can make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw to grab onto a fixed object it can reach, avoiding the fall on a success.  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** The sentient planetoid is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The sentient planetoid has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The sentient planetoid makes four attacks, only one of which can be an Unfathomable Knowledge attack.  
>
>***Unfathomable Knowledge.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 5 ft. or range 250 ft. *Hit:* 39 (6d12) Psychic damage, and the target has the Addled and Weakened conditions until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Crashing Comet.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +14, range 150 ft. *Hit:* 27 (6d8) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Burning condition (3d8 Fire).  
>
>***All-Knowing Magic (Recharge 5–6).*** The sentient planetoid casts any spell of level 5 or lower, using Intelligence as its spellcasting ability (spell save DC 22, +14 to spell attacks).
