>## Horror Flit Hunter
>*Small Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d6 + 10)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The horror flit hunter can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The horror flit hunter can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The horror flit hunter makes two Bite attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Piercing damage. If a Small or larger creature is killed by this damage, a **Horror Flit Swarm** emerges from its corpse 1d4 hours later.
