>## Kokela
>*Gargantuan Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 410 (20d20 + 200)
>- **Speed** 0 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|6 (-2)|30 (+10)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|21 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +9, Wis +11, Cha +12
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Incapacitated, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 22 (XP 41,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Eldritch Distortion.*** Each time the kokela is targeted by a *Magic Missile* spell, a Line spell, or a spell that requires a ranged attack roll, roll a d6. On a 1–3, the kokela is affected normally. On a 4–6, the effect rebounds at the caster as if it originated from the kokela.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** f the kokela fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Nightmare Aura.*** The kokela emits a 150-foot Emanation that looks like whatever horrid, squirming vista it dreams of. The Emanation can conceal danger. A creature moving through the Emanation can take the Study action to see through the illusion with a successful DC 20 Intelligence (Investigation) check. Those who succeed on this check see the dream world vaguely superimposed over the real terrain. Such creatures have Advantage on saving throws against the Nightmare Aura and against the kokela's Alien Sun reaction.  
>A creature that enters the Emanation for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 20. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition until the start of its next turn. If the target is within 20 feet of the kokela, it also takes 11 (2d10) Psychic damage and its Speed is halved for the same duration.  
>If a creature drops to 0 Hit Points in the aura, one of its ears detaches and flies away.  
>
>***Sound Reliant.*** While the kokela has the Deafened condition, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and can't use Hum or Swarm Song.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The kokela makes two Slam attacks and uses Hum.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 25 (5d8 + 3) Force damage.  
>
>***Hum.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation. This vibration transcends hearing, ignoring magical silence and affecting creatures with the Deafened condition normally. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Thunder damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell.*** The kokela casts *Counterspell* (level 5 version) in response to that spell's trigger. Charisma is its spellcasting ability for this spell.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the kokela can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The kokela regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Attack.** The kokela makes a Slam attack.
>- **Dream Movement.** The kokela teleports up to 60 feet. It can arrive in a space occupied by other creatures, shunting the creatures into the nearest unoccupied spaces.
>- **Swarm Song (Recharge 6).** The kokela calls for a **Swarm of Ears**, which rolls Initiative and arrives next round in an unoccupied space within 90 feet of the kokela.
>- **Alien Sun (1/Day).** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in the kokela's Nightmare Aura. *Failure:* 39 (6d12) Radiant damage, and the target has the Blinded condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the Blinded condition on itself on a success. *Success:* Half damage only.
