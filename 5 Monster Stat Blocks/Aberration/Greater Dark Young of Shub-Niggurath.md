___
>## Greater Dark Young of Shub-Niggurath
>*Huge Aberration (Mythos), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 261 (18d12 + 144)
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|9 (-1)|26 (+8)|12 (+1)|23 (+6)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Con +12, Wis +10
>- **Skills** Perception +10
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d3 other languages
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The greater dark young of Shub-Niggurath has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The greater dark young of Shub-Niggurath makes four Flailing Tentacle attacks. It can replace two attacks with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Flailing Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, Reach 30 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18).  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The greater dark young of Shub-Niggurath casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18):  
>
>At will: *Dispel Magic*, *Entangle*, *Pass without Trace*, *Plant Growth*  
>1/day each: *Call Lightning*, *Cone of Cold*, *Insect Plague*, *Tree Stride*  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 1. Immediately after another creature's turn, the greater dark young of shub-niggurath can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The greater dark young of shub-niggurath regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Swarming Tentacles.** The greater dark young of Shub-Niggurath makes two Flailing Tentacle attacks.
>- **Clamor of Hooves.** The greater dark young of Shub-Niggurath moves up to its Speed.
