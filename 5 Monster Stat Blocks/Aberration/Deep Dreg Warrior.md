___
>## Deep Dreg Warrior
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 11 (2d8 + 2)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +2, Stealth +3
>- **Gear** *Net*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The deep dreg can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Catch of the Day (1/Turn).*** The deep dreg can deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a Prone or Restrained creature it hits with a weapon attack.  
>
>***Watery Camouflage.*** The deep dreg has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks to Hide in any area Lightly Obscured by murky water or aquatic features.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, or 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage if wielded with two hands to make a melee attack.  
>
>***Net.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3 to hit, range 5/15 ft., one Large or smaller creature. *Hit:* The target has the Restrained condition. A creature can take an action to free itself or another creature in a net with a successful DC 10 Strength check. Dealing 5 Slashing damage to the net (AC 10) frees the target without harming it and destroys the net.
