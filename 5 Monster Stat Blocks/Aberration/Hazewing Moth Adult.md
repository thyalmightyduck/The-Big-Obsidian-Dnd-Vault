>## Hazewing Moth Adult
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 28)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Proboscis.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage. If the target has the Incapacitated condition, its Intelligence score is reduced by 1d4 until it finishes a Short or Long Rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its Intelligence score to 0.  
>
>***Delerium Dust (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the hazewing moth. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Radiant damage, and the target has the Addled condition (save ends).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hypnotic Flutter.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation of the hazewing moth that can see it (hazewing moths automatically succeed). *Failure:* The target has the Slowed condition (save ends). If the target is already Slowed, it instead has the Immobilized condition (save ends). If the target is already Immobilized, it also has the Incapacitated condition (save ends both).
