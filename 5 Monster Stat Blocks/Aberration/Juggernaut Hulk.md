>## Juggernaut Hulk
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|3 (-4)|6 (-2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Out of My Way.*** The hulk moves more than 10 feet in a straight line. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature within 5 feet of its path. *Failure:* The target is pushed 5 feet away from the hulk and has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Regenerate.*** The hulk regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the hulk takes Acid or Cold damage, this trait doesn't function on the hulk's next turn. The hulk dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hulk makes two Rusty Blade attacks. It can replace one Rusty Blade attack with Whirlwind if available.  
>
>***Rusty Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Slashing damage and the creature gains the Bleeding 2d6 condition.  
>
>***Whirlwind (Recharge 5–6).*** The hulk twirls its blades around it in a violent, heavy swing. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature within 10 feet of the hulk. *Failure:* 27 (6d6 + 6) Slashing damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rip and Tear.*** The hulk makes one Rusty Blade attack against a Prone creature.
