# Grim Hallow: Monster Grimoire 2024 (GHMG24)
___
## Mukad Larva Swarm
*Medium swarm of Tiny Aberrations, Lawful Evil*
___
- **[[Armor Class]]** 12
- **[[Hit Point|Hit Points]]** 33 (6d8 + 6)
- **[[Speed]]** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
- **[[Initiative]]** +1 (11)
___

|   STR   |   DEX   |   CON   |  INT   |   WIS   |  CHA   |
| :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :----: | :-----: | :----: |
| 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 3 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 4 (-3) |
___
- **Senses** [[Blindsight]] 10 ft., [[Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[Passive Perception]] 10, Passive Insight 10, Passive Stealth 11
- **Languages** —
- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
- **[[Proficiency Bonus]]** +2
___
### Traits
***Magic Resistance.*** The swarm has [[Advantage]] on [[Saving Throw|saving throws]] against spells and other magical effects.  

***Spider Climb.*** The larva swarm can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an [[ability check]].  

***Swarm.*** The larva swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] or gain [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]].  

### Actions
***Bites.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d6 + 1) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage, or 4 (1d6 + 1) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage if the larva swarm is [[Bloodied]]. If the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution [[Saving Throw]]:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the [[Poisoned]] [[condition]]. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
If the target drops to 0 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] while [[Poisoned]], it is [[Stable]] but has the [[Poisoned]] [[condition]] for 1 hour and has the [[Paralyzed]] [[condition]] until it is no longer [[Poisoned]].
