>## Swarm of Ears
>*Large swarm of Tiny Aberrations, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 125 (14d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|3 (-4)|6 (-2)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +0, Wis +2, Cha +0
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Keen Hearing.*** The swarm of ears has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.  
>
>***Pain Transfer.*** When the swarm takes damage and creatures Charmed by it are in its space, divide the damage in half. The swarm takes one half, and the other half is divided among the Charmed creatures.  
>
>***Sound Dependent.*** While the swarm is Deafened, creatures have Advantage on saving throws against the swarm's Overwhelming Whispers.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The swarm of ears can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gainTemporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Waxy Discharge.*** The swarm's space is covered with viscous wax that dries quickly as the swarm moves. This area is Difficult Terrain for creatures other than swarms of ears and creatures Charmed by them.  
>
>### Actions
>***Overwhelming Whispers.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in the swarm's space. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Psychic damage, or 11 (2d10) Psychic damage if the swarm has the Bloodied condition, and the target has the Charmed condition until the start of the swarm's next turn. While Charmed, the target must use its Reaction to move with the swarm, remaining in its space if possible. Movement used in this way is still expended on the target's next turn.  
>
>***Sacrifice.*** While in the kokela's space, the swarm of ears can destroy itself. The kokela regains Hit Points equal to the swarm's remaining Hit Points, and the swarm dies.
