## Horror Flit Swarm
*Medium swarm of Tiny Aberrations, Chaotic Evil*

- **[[Hit Point|Hit Points]]** 54 (12d8)
- **[[Speed]]** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Swim 40 ft.
- **[[Initiative]]** +3 (13)

- **[[Proficiency Bonus]]** +2

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The horror flit swarm can breathe air and water.  

***Spider Climb.*** The horror flit swarm can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an [[ability check]].  

***Swarm.*** The horror flit swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] or gain [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]].  

### Actions
***Bites.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Piercing damage, or 9 (2d8) Piercing damage if the swarm has the [[Bloodied]] [[condition]]. If a Small or larger creature is killed by this damage, a **Horror Flit Swarm** emerges from its corpse 1d4 hours later.
