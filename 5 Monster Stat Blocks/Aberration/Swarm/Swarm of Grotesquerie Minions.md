## Swarm of Grotesquerie Minions
*Medium swarm of Tiny Aberrations, Chaotic Evil*
___
- **Armor Class** 12
- **Hit Points** 22 (5d8)
- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
- **Initiative** +2 (12)
___
|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|5 (-3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|1 (-5)|7 (-2)|1 (-5)|
___
- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
- **Immunities** Acid; Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
- **Languages** —
- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
___
***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature.  

### Actions
***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 5 (2d4) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Acid damage, or 5 (2d4) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Acid damage if the swarm has the Bloodied condition.
