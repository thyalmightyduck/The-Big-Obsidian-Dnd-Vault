## Spythronar Swarm
*Medium swarm of Tiny Aberrations, Neutral Evil*
___
- **[[Armor Class]]** 13
- **[[Hit Point|Hit Points]]** 45 (10d8)
- **[[Speed]]** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
- **[[Initiative]]** +3 (13)
___

- **Skills** [[Stealth]] +5
- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
- **Immunities** Lightning; [[Charmed]], [[Frightened]], [[Grappled]], [[Paralyzed]], [[Prone]], [[Restrained]], [[Stunned]]

- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
- **[[Proficiency Bonus]]** +2

### Traits
***Spider Climb.*** A spythronar swarm can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an [[ability check]]  

***Swarm.*** The spythronar swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny arachnid. The swarm can't regain [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] or gain [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]].  

***Web Walker.*** The [[spythronar web]] ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing, and it knows the location of any other creature in contact with the same webbing.  

### Actions
***Bites.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 0 ft., one creature in the swarm's space. *Hit:* 10 (4d4) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Lightning damage, or 5 (2d4) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Lightning damage if the swarm has the [[Bloodied]] [[condition]].  

***Weave (4/Day).*** The spythronar swarm weaves webbing, covering a 5-foot Square. The swarm can give up 1 [[Hit Point]] to create a [[spythronar sac]] in that space. If the swarm's webbing covers a 10-foot Square, it can give up 23 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] to instead create a spythronar web with the same number of [[Hit Point|Hit Points]]. The swarm can't drop to 0 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] to create a sac or web.  
If the swarm weaves an incomplete web, that web is a normal web rather than a sentient, monstrous one. A creature that enters the normal web's space must succeed on a DC 10 Strength [[saving throw]] or have the [[Grappled]] [[condition]] (escape DC 10). While [[Grappled]], the target has the [[Restrained]] [[condition]]. Each 5-foot Square of this web has AC 10, the Vulnerabilities, Immunities, and [[Resistance|Resistances]] of a [[spythronar web]], and 5 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]].  

### Bonus Actions
***Furtive.*** The spythronar swarm takes the [[Hide action]].
