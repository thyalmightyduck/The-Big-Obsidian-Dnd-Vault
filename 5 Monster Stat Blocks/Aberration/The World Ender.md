___
>## The World Ender
>*Gargantuan Aberration (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 23
>- **Hit Points** 205 (10d20 + 100) plus 205 (10d20 + 100) per player character
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +20 (30)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|15 (+2)|30 (+10)|12 (+1)|30 (+10)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +11, Int +10, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Athletics +19, Insight +19, Perception +19
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Poison, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 29
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 30 (XP 155,000; PB +9)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +9
>___
>***Delerium Storm Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 120 feet of the World Ender takes 21 (6d6) Lightning damage and is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 24. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level.  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** The World Ender is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.  
>
>***Mutations Abound.*** A creature that gains a Contamination level from the World Ender's Delerium Storm Aura must roll 2d6 and take the lower result to determine if they mutate. Whenever a creature within the aura gains a mutation, that creature must roll twice on the Mutations table and gains both mutations.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If the World Ender is destroyed, it regains all of its Hit Points in 1 hour unless a *Wish* spell is used to prevent it from rejuvenating. This is a stressful use of the *Wish* spell.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The World Ender deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Unbound Arcana.*** Whenever the World Ender is the target of a spell, an Arcane Anomalies is triggered on the caster.  
>
>### Actions
>***The World Ender always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the World Ender can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the World Ender takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Necrotic Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +19, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 37 (5d10 + 10) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Bite (1/Round).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +19, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 26 (3d10 + 10) Piercing damage, and if the target is a creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 27). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the World Ender can't Bite another target.  
>
>***Charge Eradication Beam (Recharge 5–6).*** Octarine energy ripples through the body of the World Ender from its tail up to its head. The World Ender's Eradication Beam recharges, and it chooses the direction it will face to unleash its Eradication Beam.  
>The World Ender can only use this Epic Action if it's the first Epic Action it has taken this round. After using this Epic Action, The World Ender can't use Bite, Bellowing Roar, or Devour until the start of the next round.  
>
>***Eradication Beam (Special Recharge).*** The World Ender can only use this Epic Action if it's the last Epic Action it takes this round. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 27, each creature and object in a 1000-foot long, 20-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 110 (20d10) Force damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* A creature is disintegrated along with anything it is wearing or carrying if this damage leaves it with 0 Hit Points, and can't be revived except by a *Wish* spell.  
>The Eradication Beam destroys any *Wall of Force*, *Forcecage*, or *Prismatic Wall* spell within its area. It also damages unattended magic items in its area, including artifacts.  
>
>***Octarine Lightning Strike.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 27, each creature in a 10-foot radius Sphere centered on a point within 500 feet. *Failure:* 54 (12d8) Lightning damage, and the target has the Shocked condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Bellowing Roar (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 27, each creature in a 120-foot Emanation originating from the World Ender that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Deafened condition (save ends). While Deafened, the target has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.  
>
>***Ground Slam (1/Round).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 27, each creature on the ground in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 26 (4d12) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away from the World Ender and has the Prone condition. *Failure or Success:* The Cone becomes Difficult Terrain until the start of the World Ender's next turn.  
>
>***Devour.*** The World Ender can't take this Epic Action if it has used its Bite Epic Action this round. The World ender makes a Bite attack against a Large or smaller creature it has Grappled. If the attack hits, the target takes the Bite's damage, the target is swallowed, and the grapple ends. While swallowed, the creature has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, it has Total Cover against effects originating from outside the World Ender, and it takes 56 (16d6) Acid damage and gains a Contamination level at the start of each of the World Ender's turns.  
>If the World Ender takes 60 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the World Ender must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which land in unoccupied spaces within 10 feet of the World Ender with the Prone condition. If the World Ender dies, a swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape from the corpse by using 30 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The World Ender chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The World Ender can use this Epic Action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
