___
>## Whipskein Horror
>*Medium Aberration, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4
>- **Skills** Perception +2
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Any two languages
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the whipskein horror hides its tendrils from sight, such as under clothing, it is indistinguishable from the ordinary humanoid it once was.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The whipskein horror makes four Tendril attacks. It can replace any attack with a use of Spit.  
>
>***Tendril.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The whipskein horror has four tendrils, each of which can grapple one target.  
>
>***Spit.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 30 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Poison damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the whipskein horror's next turn. Being within 5 feet of a Hostile creature doesn't impose Disadvantage on the whipskein horror's Spit attack.  
>
>***Tendril Dance (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature within 30 feet of the whipskein horror that can see it. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition until the end of the whipskein horror's next turn. While Charmed, the target has the Incapacitated condition and a Speed of 0. The effect ends early for the target if it takes damage or if another creature uses an action to shake it out of its stupor.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Wriggle.*** The whipskein horror extends the duration of its Tendril Dance. Each creature currently Charmed by the whipskein horror has the Charmed condition until the end of the whipskein horror's next turn. The whipskein horror can't use Wriggle while it has a creature Grappled.
