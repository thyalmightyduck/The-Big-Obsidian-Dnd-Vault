>- **Hit Points** 13 (3d6 + 3)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)

>
>***Stinger.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Piercing damage.
