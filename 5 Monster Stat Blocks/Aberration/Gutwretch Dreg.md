>## Gutwretch Dreg
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Reactive.*** The dreg can take one Reaction on every turn in combat.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dreg makes two Gut Flail attacks.  
>
>***Gut Flail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Flailing Guts.*** *Trigger:* A creature enters a space within 15 feet of it. *Response:* The dreg makes one Gut Flail attack against that creature.
