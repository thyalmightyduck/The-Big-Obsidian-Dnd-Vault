>## Greater Star-Spawn of Cthulhu
>*Huge Aberration (Mythos), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 252 (24d12 + 96)
>- **Speed** 80 ft., Fly 60 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|8 (-1)|19 (+4)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +11, Con +9, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +9, History +9, Intimidation +6, Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 other languages
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The greater star-spawn of Cthulhu can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The greater star-spawn of Cthulhu has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The greater star-spawn of Cthulhu makes two Crushing Fist attacks.  
>
>***Crushing Fist.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, Reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 28 (4d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The greater star-spawn of Cthulhu casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17):  
>
>At will: *Dispel Magic*, *Fear*, *Misty Step*, *Shatter*, *Suggestion*  
>1/day each: *Cone of Cold*, *Forcecage*, *Hold Monster*, *Wall of Force*  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 2. Immediately after another creature's turn, the greater star-spawn of cthulhu can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The greater star-spawn of cthulhu regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Flabby Claws.** The greater star-spawn of Cthulhu makes a Crushing Fist attack.
>- **Eldritch Contradictions.** The greater star-spawn of Cthulhu uses Spellcasting.
>- **Ravening for Delight.** The greater star-spawn of Cthulhu moves up to its Speed.
