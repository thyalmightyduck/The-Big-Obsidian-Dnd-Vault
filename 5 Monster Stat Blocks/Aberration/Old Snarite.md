>## Old Snarite
>*Small Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d6 + 20)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 15 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +6
>- **Immunities** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Muddled Thoughts.*** Although an old snarite cannot control the actions of its host, it can cloud a host's thoughts, inclining the host to make poor or irrational decisions. The host also refuses to cut their hair, thus saving the old snarite from harm.  
>
>***Wisdom Drinker.*** After being infected by a young snarite for a year, the host is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target's Wisdom score is permanently reduced by 2.  
>
>### Actions
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (3d4 + 4) Slashing damage, and the target is Grappled (escape DC 13). While Grappled, the creature is Restrained. A target that begins its turn Grappled by the old snarite take 11 (3d4 + 4) Slashing damage and is infected with 2 (1d4) **Young Snarites**.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Deflect Damage.*** *Trigger:* The adult snarite is hit with a melee attack. *Response:* The adult snarite deflects the damage onto its host instead.
