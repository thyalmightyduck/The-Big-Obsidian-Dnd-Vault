>## Interloper
>*Small Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 70 (20d6)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Int +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, Investigation +6, Perception +5, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Force, Necrotic, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Jinxed|Monstersofdrakkenheim
>- **Senses** Blindsight 90 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Telepathy 90 ft. (conveys simple emotions) understands all languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The interloper is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the interloper's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the interloper can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the interloper has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The interloper is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The interloper may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The interloper is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The interloper may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The interloper has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the interloper's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** Interloper is immune to the Jinxed condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The interloper makes one Spindly Claw attack and one Suckered Bite attack. If the interloper has used Siphon Spell since the end of its last turn, it can instead make three Spindly Claw or Suckered Bite attacks.  
>
>***Spindly Claw.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11, one creature within 5 ft. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage and the target gains one Contamination level. Succes: half damage only.  
>
>***Suckered Bite.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature within 5ft. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage and the target has the Bewildered condition (save ends) and the interloper regains 10 (3d6) Hit Points. *Success:* half damage only.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** The interloper touches a delerium shard and regains 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the interloper has used a delerium shard in this way, that shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Plane Shift (1/Day).*** The interloper casts *Plane Shift* targeting only itself, requiring no components and using Charisma as its spellcasting ability.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Teleport.*** The interloper teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Siphon Spell.*** *Trigger:* A spell is cast by a creature within 60 feet of the interloper. Response - Intelligence Saving throw: DC 14, that creature. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Psychic damage and the spell fails, but no resources are expended by the spell's casting.
