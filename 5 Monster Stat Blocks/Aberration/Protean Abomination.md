___
>## Protean Abomination
>*Huge Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 168 (16d12 + 64)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|8 (-1)|18 (+4)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Absorption.*** When the abomination kills a creature that is neither a construct nor undead, it absorbs the corpse into its body and regains 20 Hit Points.  
>
>***Amorphous.*** The abomination can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The abomination can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The abomination makes two Slam attacks. If both attacks hit a Large or smaller creature, the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16) and the abomination uses Engulf on the Grappled creature.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Engulf.*** The abomination engulfs a Large or smaller creature Grappled by it. Until the grapple ends the target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, is suffocating, and has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the abomination. If the abomination moves, the engulfed target moves with it. The abomination can have only one creature engulfed at a time. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. The engulfed creature repeats the save at the start of each of the abomination's turns Success: The target escapes and enters the nearest unoccupied space.
