___
>## Far Dweller Mastermind
>*Medium Aberration (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24) plus 60 (8d8 + 24) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|21 (+5)|17 (+3)|23 (+6)|19 (+4)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Int +11, Wis +9, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Arcana +16, Deception +9, Perception +9, Persuasion +9, Stealth +10
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 300 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft. Understand all languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The far dweller is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the far dweller's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the far dweller can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the far dweller has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Eldritch Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the far dweller takes 10 (3d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The far dweller is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The far dweller may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Magic Deflection.*** The far dweller has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. If the far dweller is targeted by a spell of level 4 or lower, the caster must succeed on a DC 19 Intelligence saving throw or the spell fails and the caster takes 18 (4d8) Psychic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spellcasting (Psionics) (1/Round).*** The far dweller casts one of the following spells, requiring no components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19, +11 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Thoughts*, *Magic Missile* (level 5 version)  
>1/day each: *Dominate Monster*, *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Mass Suggestion*, *Maze*, *Plane Shift* (self only), *Scrying*, *Slow*, *Telekinesis*  
>
>***The far dweller always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the far dweller can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the far dweller takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Tentacle.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Force damage, and the target has the Dazed condition until the end of its next turn.
>- **Contaminate.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19, one creature within 120 feet of the far dweller. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.
>
>***Mind Scramble (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 19, each creature in a 60-foot Cone (creatures maintaining Concentration have Disadvantage). *Failure:* 42 (12d6) Psychic damage, or 72 Psychic damage if the target was maintaining Concentration. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Pluck the Puppet Strings (1/Round).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 19, one creature within 120 feet of the far dweller. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Psychic damage, and the target must immediately use its Reaction to move up to its Speed toward a creature of the far dweller's choice. If it ends this movement within reach of the creature, it must make a melee weapon attack against the creature with Advantage. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Phase Out (1/Round).*** The far dweller teleports to an unoccupied space within 120 feet of it. Each creature within 10 feet of the far dweller when it arrives is subjected to the following effect. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 19. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Defenseless condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The far dweller chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The far dweller can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Step Through Time and Space.*** *Trigger:* The far dweller takes damage. *Response:* The far dweller takes no damage and teleports to an unoccupied space within 60 feet. The next attack roll made against the far dweller has Disadvantage.
