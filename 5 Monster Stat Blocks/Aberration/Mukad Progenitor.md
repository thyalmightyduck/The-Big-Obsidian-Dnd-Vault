___
>## Mukad Progenitor
>*Large Aberration, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 110 (13d10 + 39)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Burrow 10 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Mukad, telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The mukad has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The mukad progenitor can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Tunneler.*** The mukad progenitor can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrowing Speed. When it burrows, it leaves a 5-foot diameter tunnel in its wake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mukad progenitor makes one Bite attack and one Claws attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Poison damage, and if the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>If the target drops to 0 Hit Points while Poisoned, it is Stable but has the Poisoned condition for 1 hour and has the Paralyzed condition until it is no longer Poisoned.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Aetheric Blast (Recharge 6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13, each non-Aberration in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 18 (3d10 + 2) Psychic damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Aetheric Incubation.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 14, a Paralyzed creature within reach of the mukad progenitor. *Failure:* The target has the Paralyzed condition for 24 hours. An ectoplasmic substance harmlessly cocoons the target. Removing the cocoon takes 1 minute, and escaping it from within takes no effort. If the cocoon remains for 13 (3d8) hours, a number of mukad larvae equal to 1d4 + the target's Intelligence bonus appear on the creature, absorbing the cocoon and attacking the target, feeding on it.
