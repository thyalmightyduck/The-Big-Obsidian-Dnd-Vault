___
>## Mi-Go
>*Medium Aberration (Mythos), Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 104 (16d8 + 32)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +5, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Medicine +7, Perception +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 other languages
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The mi-go has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Space Traveler.*** The mi-go does not need to breathe air.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mi-go makes two Claw attacks and uses Spellcasting.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, Reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The mi-go casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Darkness*, *Hypnotic Pattern*  
>1/day: *Cone of Cold*, *Disintegrate*, *Wall of Force*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hideous Counsel.*** The mi-go confers with a human brain it has sealed away in a jar. The specimen is specifically chosen for the knowledge it can provide to the mi-go, allowing it to adapt to a variety of situations. The mi-go chooses one of the following benefits: It gains Advantage on attack rolls until the end of its turn.  
>It uses the Dodge action as part of this Bonus Action.  
>It gains a +2 bonus to its spell save DC until the end of its turn.
