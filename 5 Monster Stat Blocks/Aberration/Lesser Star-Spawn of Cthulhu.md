>## Lesser Star-Spawn of Cthulhu
>*Large Aberration (Mythos), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 153 (18d10 + 54)
>- **Speed** 60 ft., Fly 40 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +6, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, History +5, Intimidation +3, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 other languages
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The lesser star-spawn of Cthulhu can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The lesser star-spawn of Cthulhu has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lesser star-spawn of Cthulhu makes two Crushing Fist attacks.  
>
>***Crushing Fist.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, Reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The lesser star-spawn of Cthulhu casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Dispel Magic*, *Fear*, *Misty Step*, *Shatter*, *Suggestion*  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 1. Immediately after another creature's turn, the lesser star-spawn of cthulhu can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The lesser star-spawn of cthulhu regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Flabby Claws.** The lesser star-spawn of Cthulhu makes one Crushing Fist attack.
>- **Eldritch Contradictions.** The lesser star-spawn of Cthulhu uses Spellcasting.
>- **Ravening for Delight.** The lesser star-spawn of Cthulhu moves up to its Speed.
