>## Bog Crawler
>*Gargantuan Aberration, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 279 (18d20 + 90)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|26 (+8)|10 (+0)|21 (+5)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +10, Wis +5
>- **Immunities** Acid, Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The bog crawler can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Slimy Movement.*** The bog crawler ignores Difficult Terrain, and magical effects can't reduce its Speed or cause it to have the Restrained condition. It can spend 5 feet of movement to escape from nonmagical restraints or the Grappled condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The bog crawler makes two Tentacle attacks, or one Tentacle attack and one Bite attack or Swallow action.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +13, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 34 (4d12 + 8) Piercing damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 21). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the bog crawler can't Bite another target.  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +13, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 26 (4d8 + 8) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 21). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The bog crawler has six tentacles, each of which can grapple one target.  
>
>***Swallow.*** The bog crawler makes one Bite attack against a Huge or smaller creature it has Grappled with its Bite. *Hit:* The target takes the Bite attack's damage, no longer has the Grappled condition, and is swallowed. While swallowed, the target has the Blinded and Restrained condition and Total Cover from effects that originate outside the bog crawler. It takes 36 (8d8) Acid damage at the start of each of the bog crawler's turns.  
>If the bog crawler takes 30 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the bog crawler must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which land in spaces within 10 feet of the bog crawler and have the Prone condition. If the bog crawler dies, a swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape the corpse using 15 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.
