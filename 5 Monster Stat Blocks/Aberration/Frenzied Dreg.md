>## Frenzied Dreg
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d8)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Reckless.*** At the start of its turn, this dreg can gain Advantage on all melee attack rolls until the start of its next turn, but attack rolls against it have Advantage during that time.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dreg makes two attacks with its Claws.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage. Critical *Hit:* *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target gains one level of Contamination.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bounding Leap.*** The dreg jumps 20 feet. This movement does not count toward its total movement for the round.
