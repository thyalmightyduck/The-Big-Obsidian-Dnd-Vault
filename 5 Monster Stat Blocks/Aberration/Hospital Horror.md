___
>## Hospital Horror
>*Medium Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 30 (4d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|7 (-2)|9 (-1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +6, Dex +5
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Athletics +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Poison
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** Understands Common and Deep Speech but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Aversion to Needles.*** If the horror takes Piercing damage, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The horror has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the horror's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The horror makes two Graft Blade attacks.  
>
>***Graft Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Stim Burst.*** The horror takes the Dash action and can jump up to 30 feet by spending 10 feet of movement.
