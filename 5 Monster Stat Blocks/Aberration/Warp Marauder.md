___
>## Warp Marauder
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 97 (13d8 + 39)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***1-2: Blue.*** The attacker is teleported to an unoccupied space the warp marauder can see within 20 feet. The chosen space must be on a surface or in a liquid that can support the target. The attacker is then subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Immobilized condition (save ends).  
>
>***3-4: Yellow.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Lightning damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***5-6: Red.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Staggered condition (save ends).  
>
>***Alien Intellect.*** The warp marauder is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the warp marauder's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the warp marauder can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the warp marauder has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Extraplanar Gullet.*** The warp marauder's back opens into a large mouth that leads to an extradimensional gullet. Anything it swallows is transported into this extraplanar space, which manifests as a rhizomatic network of slurping tunnels and fleshy passages. This space can hold any number of creatures of any size, and nothing inside the space can harm the warp marauder.  
>A creature within the gullet can take an action to attempt to escape, making a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw. On a success, it reappears in the nearest unoccupied space within 5 feet of the warp marauder. On a failure, it takes 10 (3d6) Psychic damage. If the warp marauder dies, everything inside the extraplanar gullet appears in the nearest unoccupied spaces to its corpse.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The warp marauder is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The warp marauder may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Grasping Tentacles.*** The warp marauder can have up to four tentacles at a time. Each tentacle has AC 20, 10 Hit Points, and Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage. Destroying a tentacle deals no damage to the warp marauder, which can extrude a replacement tentacle on its next turn. A creature within 5 feet can take an action to break a tentacle with a successful DC 15 Strength check.  
>
>***Swirling Color Field.*** A field of shimmering color reverberates around the warp marauder, distorting reality. A creature that hits the warp marauder with an attack suffers one of the following effects, determined randomly (roll 1d6). A creature Grappled and adjacent to the warp marauder isn't affected by the Swirling Color Field.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The warp marauder makes four Tentacle attacks, uses Reel, and makes one Bite attack.  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 60 ft. *Hit:* The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and has Disadvantage on Strength checks and saving throws, and the warp marauder can't use this tentacle against another target.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft, one creature Grappled by the warp marauder. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Piercing damage, and the warp marauder swallows the target. The target, along with everything it is wearing and carrying, appears in the warp marauder's Extraplanar Gullet.  
>
>***Reel.*** The warp marauder pulls each creature Grappled by it up to 30 feet straight toward it.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Dimensional Jump.*** The warp marauder teleports to an unoccupied space within 120 feet. Alternatively, it travels from the Material Plane to the Space Between Worlds or vice versa.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Reality Hunter.*** *Trigger:* A creature Grappled by the warp marauder attempts to teleport. *Response—**Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Force damage, and the teleport fails. *Success:* The teleport fails, but no resources are wasted.
