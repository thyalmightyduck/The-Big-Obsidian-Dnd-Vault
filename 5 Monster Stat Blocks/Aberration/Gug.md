___
>## Gug
>*Huge Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 250 (20d12 + 120)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|15 (+2)|23 (+6)|7 (-2)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Con +10
>- **Skills** Perception +7, Stealth +10
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Understands Deep Speech but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The gug has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the gug's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gug makes two Claw attacks and uses Chew.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16) from one of two claws.  
>
>***Chew.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18, up to two Medium or smaller creatures Grappled by the gug. *Failure:* The gug places the target in its mouth and chews. A chewed creature is no longer Grappled but has the Restrained condition. While Restrained in this way, a target has Half Cover against attacks and other effects not originating from the gug and takes 10 (3d6) Piercing damage at the start of each of the gug's turns. A gug can have a maximum of four creatures in its mouth at a time.  
>A creature in the gug's mouth can take an action to make a DC 16 Strength (Athletics) check. On a successful check, the target is no longer Restrained and moves to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the gug. If the gug dies, a target is no longer Restrained and can escape from the gug using 5 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.
