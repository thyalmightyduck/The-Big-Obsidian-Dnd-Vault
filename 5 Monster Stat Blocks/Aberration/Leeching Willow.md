>## Leeching Willow
>*Huge Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 232 (15d12 + 135)
>- **Speed** 5 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|4 (-3)|28 (+9)|22 (+6)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +10, History +10, Insight +5, Nature +10
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands Deep Speech and all languages those infected by it knew but it cannot speak
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Bell Weakness.*** A bell rung within 30 feet of the tree forces it to uproot, disrupting its False Appearance and suppressing the effects of its Leeching Spores. A loud bell rung near a creature kills any Leeching Spores inside of it.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the leeching willow remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal tree.  
>
>***Leeching Spores.*** TThe leeching willow emits spores from its body in a 1,000-foot Emanation. Each creature that breathes in the leeching willow's spores is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* The spores take root in the target's body, infecting it. *Success:* The creature is immune to being infected for 1 minute, but still has spores inside its body.  
>An infected target's body feeds vigor to the leeching willow. The target gets minimal rest from sleeping, suffering a level of Exhaustion every 7 days (to a maximum of 4 levels of Exhaustion). These Exhaustion levels can't be removed by taking a Long Rest until the spores are removed from the creature's body.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The leeching willow deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The leeching willow makes three attacks, using Slam or Rock in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Psychic Spike.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 23 (5d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The leeching willow casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18):  
>
>At will: *Blur*, *Confusion*, *Modify Memory*  
>3/day: *Dream*  
>
>***Rock.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 60/180 ft. *Hit:* 28 (4d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage  
>
>***Psychic Spike.*** Intelligence Saving Throw: DC 18, one creature with Leeching Spores in its body. *Failure:* 27 (5d10) Psychic damage, and the target can't take a Reaction until the end of its next turn. On the target's next turn, it must choose whether it can move, take an action, or take a Bonus Action; it only gets one of the three. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Psychic Slam (1/Day).*** The leeching willow uses Psychic Spike against each creature with Leeching Spores in its body within 1,000 feet. This special use of Psychic Spike deals 45 (13d6) Psychic damage, kills any Leeching Spores in the target's body, and suppresses the leeching willow's Leeching Spores trait for 1 round. If this damage kills a target, its head explodes, if it has one.
