___
>## Rougaugh
>*Large Aberration, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 175 (14d10 + 98)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|8 (-1)|24 (+7)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +10, Survival +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Siege Monster.*** The rougaugh deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Spiney Defense.*** A creature adjacent to the rougaugh that attacks it with a melee weapon attack takes 5 (1d10) Piercing damage, regardless of whether the attack hits or misses.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The rougaugh makes three attacks: one with its Headbutt and two with its Spines.  
>
>***Head Butt.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Spines.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spine Splinter (Recharge 6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the rougaugh (Prone creatures have Disadvantage). *Failure:* 27 (5d10) Piercing damage. *Success:* Half damage.
