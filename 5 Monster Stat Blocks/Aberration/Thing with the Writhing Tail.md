>## Thing with the Writhing Tail
>*Huge Aberration (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 125 (7d20 + 49) plus 125 (7d20 + 49) per player character
>- **Speed** 60 ft., Fly 120 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +15 (25)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|27 (+8)|21 (+5)|29 (+9)|25 (+7)|23 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +12, Int +16, Wis +14, Cha +13
>- **Skills** Deception +20, Insight +14, Perception +14, Persuasion +13
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 24
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 1 mile
>- **Challenge** 24 (XP 62,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Avoidance.*** If the Thing with the Writhing Tail is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a saving throw to take only half damage, it instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>***Inscrutable.*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the being's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Purring Aura.*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail projects a 30-foot Emanation of madness and dread. A creature that ends its turn in the Emanation has the Frightened condition for 1 hour. A creature can take an action to attempt to overcome its fear, making a DC 25 Wisdom saving throw. *Failure:* The target takes 27 (5d10) Psychic damage. *Success:* The Frightened condition ends for that creature, and it becomes immune to the Purring Aura of the Thing with the Writhing Tail for 24 hours.  
>
>***Quantum Displacement.*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail shifts between realities and timelines. Attack rolls against it have Disadvantage, and it has Advantage on saving throws. If it is hit by an attack or fails a saving throw, this trait is disrupted until it uses Phase Displacement. This trait is also disrupted while the Thing with the Writhing Tail has the Incapacitated condition or a Speed of 0.  
>
>### Actions
>***The Thing with the Writhing Tail always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, The Thing with the Writhing Tail can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, The Thing with the Writhing Tail takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Cosmic Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 29 (4d10 + 8) Force damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting (Psionics; 1/Round).*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 26):  
>
>At will: *Detect Thoughts*, *Dominate Beast*, *Suggestion*  
>3/day each: *Evard's Black Tentacles*, *Dream*, *Greater Invisibility*, *Major Image* (level 4 version), *Mirror Image*, *Project Image*, *Scrying*, *Sending*, *Telekinesis*  
>1/day each: *Maze*, *Teleport*  
>1/year: *Gate*, *Wish*  
>
>***Tail Whip.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 26, one creature the Thing with the Writhing Tail can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Dazed condition (save ends). Failure by 5 or More: 70 (20d6) Psychic damage, and the target instead has the Stunned condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Manifest Duplicate (1/Round).*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail creates a perfect visual illusion of itself in an unoccupied space it can see within 300 feet. The illusion is intangible and doesn't occupy its space. The duplicate lasts for 1 minute, but it ends early if the Thing with the Writhing Tail dismisses it (no action required) or has the Incapacitated condition. The Thing with the Writhing Tail can have a up to eight illusory duplicates at a time. While it has one or more duplicates, it has the following benefits:  
>
>***Attacks and Spells.*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail can cast spells, use Tail Whip, and make Claw attacks as if was in an illusory duplicate's space.  
>
>***Aura Expansion.*** Each illusory duplicate emanates a Purring Aura. The effects of multiple overlapping auras do not stack; a creature is only affected once by the Purring Aura even if it is within range of multiple Emanations.  
>
>***Move.*** Each time the Thing with the Writhing Tail takes this Epic Action, it can move any number of illusions up to 60 feet to unoccupied spaces within 300 feet of itself.  
>
>***Distract.*** When both the Thing with the Writhing Tail and one of its illusory duplicates are within 10 feet of a creature that can see them, the Thing with the Writhing Tail has Advantage on attack rolls against that creature.  
>
>***Sense.*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail can see and hear through each of its illusory duplicates.  
>
>***Phase Displacement.*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail teleports, swapping places with one of its illusory duplicates. If its Quantum Displacement trait was disrupted, it is no longer disrupted.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The Thing with the Writhing Tail chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting it and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The Thing with the Writhing Tail can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
