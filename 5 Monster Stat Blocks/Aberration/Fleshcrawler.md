>## Fleshcrawler
>*Medium Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d8 + 56)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Cold; Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Speaks and understands all languages, Telepathy 120 ft. (can communicate only with other fleshcrawlers or infected creatures)
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Infect Humanoid.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15 A creature Restrained in a fleshcrawler's fleshwebs for 1 hour. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition for 1d4 days. While a creature has the Charmed condition in this way, the fleshcrawler can issue telepathic instructions that the creature does its best to obey. If it completes an order and doesn't receive further direction from the fleshcrawler, it acts and moves as it likes, focusing on protecting itself. Whenever the target takes damage, it repeats the save, ending the Charmed condition on a success. A creature with the Charmed condition can make a save to end the effect at the end of each day.  
>If a creature is still Charmed at the end of the duration, it dies, and its body transforms into a flesh cocoon gestating a new fleshcrawler that hatches in 1 hour.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The fleshcrawler makes two attacks, using Fleshwarp Strike and Necrotic Bolt in any combination.  
>
>***Fleshwarp Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Necrotic damage and the target has the Restrained condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the Restrained target rolls a Constitution saving throw (DC 15) to end this condition.  
>
>***Necrotic Bolt.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Fleshwebs (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15 One creature the fleshcrawler can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Restrained condition until the web is destroyed (AC 15; HP 15; Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage).
