*Medium Aberration, Any Alignment*

- **[[Armor Class]]** 12
- **[[Hit Point|Hit Points]]** 127 (15d8 + 60)
- **[[Speed]]** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
- **[[Initiative]]** +3 (13)

- **[[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]]** Wis +3
- **Skills** [[Perception]] +3
- **Immunities** Acid; [[Prone]]
- **Senses** [[Blindsight]] 30 ft., [[Passive Perception]] 13, Passive Insight 10, Passive Stealth 10
- **Languages** Any two languages
- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
- **[[Proficiency Bonus]]** +3
___
### Traits
***Amorphous.*** The amorphous horror can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  

### [[Action|Actions]]
***Multiattack.*** The amorphous horror makes two Slam attacks.  
***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Acid damage.  

***Shape-Shift.*** The amorphous horror shape-shifts into a form that resembles its Small or Medium Humanoid appearance before it became a horror, or it returns to its true form. Other than its size, its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  

### Reactions
***Split.*** *Trigger:* While the amorphous horror is Medium and has 20+ [[Hit Point|Hit Points]], it becomes [[Bloodied]]. *Response:* The amorphous horror splits into two new **Amorphous Horrors**. Each horror has [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] equal to half the original horror's, rounded down. New horrors are one size smaller than the original horror.
