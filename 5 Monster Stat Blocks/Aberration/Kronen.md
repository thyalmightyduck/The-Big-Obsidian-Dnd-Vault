>## Kronen
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 123 (13d10 + 52)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Stealth +5, Survival +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The kronen makes two Claw attacks and uses Loathsome Presence.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Loathsome Presence.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature that can see the kronen. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition for 1 minute (save ends). If the creature's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, it is immune to the kronen's Loathsome Presence for 24 hours.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Scuttle.*** The kronen takes the Dash action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Infestation.*** *Trigger:* A creature ends its turn within 5 feet of the kronen. *Response—**Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target is covered in cockroaches. It takes 14 (4d6) Piercing damage at the end of each of its turns until a creature takes an action to swipe the cockroaches off its body.
