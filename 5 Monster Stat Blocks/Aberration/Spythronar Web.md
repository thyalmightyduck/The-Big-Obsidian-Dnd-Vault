___
>## Spythronar Web
>*Large Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d10 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|3 (-4)|8 (-1)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Immunities** Lightning; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Tremorsense 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Adhesive.*** The spythronar web adheres to anything it touches. A creature that touches the web can avoid adhering to it by succeeding on a DC 13 Strength saving throw (a creature hit by the web's Crush attack automatically fails). A Huge or smaller creature adhered to the web also has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). If adhered to a target larger than it can grapple, the web can release the adhesive or remain adhered and be dragged along with the target as it moves. A creature can take an action to separate an object or creature from the web with a successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check.  
>
>***Amorphous Form.*** The spythronar web can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the spythronar web remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a large, thick spider web.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** A spythronar web can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Web Walker.*** The spythronar web ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing, and it knows the location of any other creature in contact with the same webbing.  
>
>### Actions
>***Crush.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is subjected to Adhesive. While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the web can't Crush another target.  
>
>***Lightning Surge.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 30 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) Lightning damage. Each creature Grappled by the web also takes this damage.
