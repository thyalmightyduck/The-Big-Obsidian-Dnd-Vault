>## Lurking Dreg
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 18 (4d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +7
>- **Gear** *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Normal Semblance.*** The dreg has Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks to disguise itself as, or impersonate, a humanoid creature.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Furious Ambush (Recharges on a Short Rest).*** The dreg makes two Dagger attacks.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The dreg takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
