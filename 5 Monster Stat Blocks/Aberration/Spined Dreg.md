>## Spined Dreg
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d8)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|19 (+4)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Spider Climb.*** The dreg climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Spiny Body.*** Any creature grappling the dreg or Grappled by it takes 5 (1d10) Piercing damage at the end of that creature's turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dreg makes three Spike attacks.  
>
>***Spike.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft., or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. Critical *Hit:* *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10. *Failure:* The target gains one level of Contamination.
