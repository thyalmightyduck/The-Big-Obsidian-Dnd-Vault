___
>## Mutant Nurse
>*Medium Aberration, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 63 (14d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Wis +5, Cha +2
>- **Skills** Medicine +5, Perception +7, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison, Psychic
>- **Senses** Truesight 30 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Deep Speech
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Anchoring Mask.*** The nurse has a porcelain mask (AC 15; HP 15; Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage). If the nurse starts its turn and isn't wearing its mask, it must move as far as possible to retrieve its mask if possible, or to reach the closest creature it can see or hear, and it must attack that creature. If it can't see or hear any other creatures, it either stands still and does nothing, or it destroys any objects in its vicinity (50 percent chance of either).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The nurse makes two Stab attacks. If it hits a creature with a Stab attack, it can use Inject if available.  
>
>***Stab.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Inject (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, a creature the nurse hit with a Stab attack this turn. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition for 1 hour. Failure by 5 or More: The Poisoned target also has the Unconscious condition until it takes damage or a creature within 5 feet of it takes an action to shake it awake. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Mutation.*** The nurse has one of the following mutation action options:  
>- **Cerebral Graft.** Part of a psionic creature's brain is grafted to the nurse's own. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 30 feet. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Psychic damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Tentacle Lash.** A tentacle writhes beneath the nurse's skin or clothes. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Catalyst (Recharge after Short or Long Rest).*** The nurse injects itself with a stimulant. Until the start of the nurse's next turn, its Speed is doubled, it can make one extra Stab attack when it takes the Multiattack action, and it gains a +2 bonus to AC.
