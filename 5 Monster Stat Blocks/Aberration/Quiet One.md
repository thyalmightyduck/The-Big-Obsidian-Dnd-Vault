___
>## Quiet One
>*Medium Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 110 (17d8 + 34)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Psychic, Thunder
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Deep Speech; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Silent.*** The quiet one emits a 20-foot Emanation with the effects of a *Silence* spell. It does not require Concentration and, if dispelled, the quiet one can restore it as a Bonus Action.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The quiet one makes two Claw attacks and one Bite attack.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) Psychic damage. If the target has the Frightened condition, it takes an extra 9 (2d8) Psychic damage and the quiet one gainsTemporary Hit Points equal to the extra Psychic damage dealt.  
>
>***Fear (3/Day).*** The quiet one casts *Fear* (spell save DC 14), requiring no Material components.
