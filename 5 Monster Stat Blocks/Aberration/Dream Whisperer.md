___
>## Dream Whisperer
>*Medium Aberration (Shadowsteel Curse), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 130 (20d8 + 40)
>- **Speed** 35 ft., Climb 35 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|15 (+2)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** All languages known before the curse
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of Confusion.*** A creature that starts its turn or moves within 25 feet of a Dream Whisperer must succeed on a DC 13 Intelligence saving throw or be affected as if they had failed a save against the *Confusion* spell.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The Dream Whisperer has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The Dream Whisperer can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Dream Whisperer makes three Claw attacks.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage, and the target is subjected to the following effect. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The Dream Whisperer gains Advantage on attack rolls against the target for 1 minute, and the target rolls on the Lost Memories table.  
>
##### Lost Memories
|  d6 | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|:---:|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|  1  | The target becomes deeply suspicious of a character or NPC of the GM's choice. They gain the following flaw: "I know they are seeking to betray me. I have to act first—it's me or them!" |
|  2  | The target forgets all memories of a loved one of the GM's choice. The next time the target sees them, it's as if they are meeting them for the first time.                               |
|  3  | The target becomes stricken with doubt and gains the following flaw: ""The world is out to get me, and I can't win. I may as well give up now."                                           |
|  4  | The target forgets how to speak in one language known, decided by the GM.                                                                                                                 |
|  5  | The target forgets the location of something important to them, of the GM's choice. It may be the location of a goal, their home settlement, or a hidden treasure.                        |
|  6  | The target loses an inconsequential memory such as what they ate a few nights ago or the name of an unimportant NPC.                                                                      |
A *Remove Curse* spell reverses this effect and restores the victim's memory.
