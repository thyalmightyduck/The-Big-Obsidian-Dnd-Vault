___
>## Deep Knight
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Poison
>- **Gear** *Shield*, *Spear*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common; telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Abyssal Aura.*** Allies within 30 feet who can see and hear the deep knight have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed and Frightened conditions. The deep knight can't use this trait if it is Incapacitated.  
>
>***Amphibious.*** The deep knight can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Unwavering Sentinel.*** Creatures provoke Opportunity Attack action from the deep knight even if they take the Disengage action before leaving its reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The deep knight makes one Harpoon attack and one Bite attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Harpoon.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage, and if the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target is pulled up to 20 feet toward the deep knight.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shield Bash.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.
