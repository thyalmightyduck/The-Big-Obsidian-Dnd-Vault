___
>## Blightworm
>*Tiny Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 40 (16d4)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|2 (-4)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands the languages of its original host but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Otherworldly Nimbleness.*** The blightworm can move through the space of any creature larger than it.  
>
>***Slippery.*** The blightworm has Advantage on checks and saving throws to avoid or end the Grappled condition.  
>
>***Symbiotic Proficiency.*** The blightworm has a Proficiency Bonus equal to that of its original host (usually +4).  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage, and the blightworm enters the target's space and attaches to it. While attached to the target, the blightworm moves with the target and can't Bite another target. A creature can take an action to detach the blightworm from the target with a successful DC 15 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. The blightworm can detach as part of its movement and does so automatically if it becomes Incapacitated or dies.  
>If the blightworm hits a Humanoid it is attached to with a Bite attack, the target is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The blightworm burrows into the target. While embedded, the blightworm has Total Cover from effects that originate outside the target and it can't be detached. Subsequent Bite attacks against the target deal 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage, or 16 Piercing damage on a Critical Hit. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. A *Lesser Restoration* or *Protection from Evil and Good* spell ends the effect early. If the effect ends, the blightworm is ejected into a space of its choice within 5 feet of the target.  
>If a Humanoid dies with the blightworm embedded in it, its soul departs at the start of the blightworm's next turn. The blightworm's original host then reincarnates using the target's body, remaking it into a blightworm ascendant with the same appearance as the original host. The ascendant has half of its Hit Points and suffers two Exhaustion levels.
