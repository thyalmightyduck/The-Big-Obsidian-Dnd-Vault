>## Great Race of Yith
>*Huge Aberration (Mythos), Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 210 (20d12 + 80)
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|24 (+7)|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Int +9, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +9, Deception +4, History +9, Nature +9, Perception +6, Religion +9
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 + 2 other languages
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The Yithian has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Time Master.*** When the Yithian rolls Initiative, it may choose to treat the result of its d20 roll as a 5 or 15. It may also apply this effect to up to six willing creatures it can see.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Yithian makes two Snapping Claw attacks.  
>
>***Snapping Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, Reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 29 (4d10 + 7) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The Yithian casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17):  
>
>At will: *Charm Person*, *Banishment*, *Dispel Magic*, *Fly*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Misty Step*  
>1/day: *Dominate Monster*, *Foresight*, *Meteor Swarm*  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 2. Immediately after another creature's turn, the great race of yith can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The great race of yith regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Attack.** The Yithian makes a Snapping Claw attack.
>- **Spell.** The Yithian uses Spellcasting, but can only cast spells it can use at will.
