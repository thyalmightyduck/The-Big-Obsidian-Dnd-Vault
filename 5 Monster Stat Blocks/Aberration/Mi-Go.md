___
>## Mi-Go
>*Medium Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 120 (16d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|21 (+5)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Int +9, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +9, Perception +6, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The mi-go has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Silver Canister.*** The mi-go has a silver canister. While holding or carrying the canister, the mi-go can use its Extract Brain action.  
>The canister has AC 15, HP 40, and Resistance to all damage. The canister breaks if reduced to 0 Hit Points. If the canister is broken, any brains inside it are destroyed. The mi-go can create a new canister after 7 days.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mi-go makes two Pincer attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Dazing Ray if available.  
>
>***Pincer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 16 (3d10) Acid damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) from one of two pincers.  
>
>***Dazing Ray (Recharge 4–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature the mi-go can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Lightning damage plus 10 (3d6) Force damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of the mi-go's next turn.  
>
>***Extract Brain (Requires Silver Canister).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature that is Grappled by the mi-go or that has the Stunned condition. *Failure:* 77 (14d10) Piercing damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, the mi-go kills it, removes its brain, and places the brain in its silver canister.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The mi-go casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17):  
>
>1/day each: *Modify Memory*, *Plane Shift* (self only)
