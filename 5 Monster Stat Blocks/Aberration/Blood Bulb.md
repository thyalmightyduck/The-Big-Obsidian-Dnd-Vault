___
>## Blood Bulb
>*Small Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 11 (2d6 + 4)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Attach.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, the blood bulb enters its space and attaches to it. If the blood bulb has Advantage on the attack roll, it attaches by engulfing the target's head. Such a target has the Blinded condition and begins suffocating.  
>While attached, the blood bulb can only attack the target, but has Advantage on attack rolls against it. On a hit, the target loses an extra 2 (1d4) Hit Points to blood loss.  
>The blood bulb moves with the target, and can detach from it by spending 5 feet of its movement. A creature within 5 feet of the target can forcibly detach the blood bulb by taking an action and succeeding on a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Blood Exchange.*** The blood bulb loses any number of Hit Points and causes a blood bulb or blood brooder within 5 feet of it to regain an equal number of Hit Points. The blood bulb can't use Blood Exchange while it is Bloodied.
