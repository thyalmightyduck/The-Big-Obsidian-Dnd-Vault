>## Du'ulora Quori
>*Large Aberration, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 168 (16d10 + 80)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|13 (+1)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +8, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Intimidation +8, Perception +12, Survival +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 22
>- **Languages** Common, Quori, Riedran; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***All-Around Vision.*** The quori can't be surprised while it isn't Incapacitated.  
>
>***Aura of Fury.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, any creature that starts its turn in a 60-foot Emanation originating from the quori. *Failure:* The target becomes enraged. While enraged, the target has Advantage on all melee attack rolls, and all attack rolls made against the target have Advantage.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The quori has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Track Hatred.*** When the quori deals damage to any creature, it forms a psychic link with the target for 24 hours. During that time, the quori has advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track the target. The quori can attempt to track the target at any range, even if it is on another plane of existence. Abilities such as the *Pass Without Trace* spell or the Ranger's Nature's Veil feature do not impede the quori's ability to track its target. The quori remains alert to danger even when it is tracking while traveling.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The quori makes three Tentacle attacks.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The quori casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):  
>
>At will: *Mage Hand*  
>3/day each: *Crown of Madness*, *Flaming Sphere*, *Shield*  
>1/day each: *Enemies Abound*  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Fire damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17) from one of three tentacles. While Grappled, the target takes 10 (3d6) Fire damage and 10 (3d6) Psychic damage at the start of each of the quori's turns.  
>
>***Possession (Recharge 6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 16, one Humanoid the quori can see within 5 feet. *Failure:* The target is possessed by the quori; the quori disappears and the target has the Incapacitated condition and loses control of its body. The quori now controls the body, but the target retains awareness. The quori can't be targeted by any attack, spell, or other effect, except ones that specifically target Aberrations. The quori's game statistics are the same, except it uses the possessed target's Speed, as well as the target's Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution modifiers.  
>The possession lasts until the body drops to 0 Hit Points or the quori leaves as a Bonus Action. When the possession ends, the quori appears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the target, and the target is immune to this quori's Possession for 24 hours. *Success:* The target is immune to this quori's Possession for 24 hours.  
>
>***Recall Agony (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the quori can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 67 (15d8) Psychic damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Incite Fury.*** *Trigger:* A creature under the effects of the quori's Aura of Fury targets the quori with an attack. *Response:* The target attacks another creature of the quori's choice within the targets range.
