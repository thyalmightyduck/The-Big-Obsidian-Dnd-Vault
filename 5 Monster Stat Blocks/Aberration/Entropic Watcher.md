>## Entropic Watcher
>*Large Aberration (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 73 (7d10 + 35) plus 73 (7d10 + 35) per player character
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 50 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +8, Wis +7, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Perception +12
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic, Poison
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft. (unimpeded by magical Darkness), Passive Perception 22
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft. Understand all languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The entropic watcher is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the entropic watcher's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Corrupting Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the entropic watcher takes 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the entropic watcher can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the entropic watcher has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The entropic watcher is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The entropic watcher may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The entropic watcher has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Withering Gaze.*** The entropic watcher's eye creates a 150-foot long, 10-foot wide Line of withering magic. At the start of each of its turns, the entropic watcher decides which way the line faces and whether the line is active. Creatures in the Line have Vulnerability to Necrotic damage and can't regain Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***The entropic watcher always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the entropic watcher can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the entropic watcher takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Tendrils.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 40 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The entropic watcher has ten tendrils, each of which can grapple one creature.
>- **Insanity Beam (1/Round).** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 22 (4d10) Necrotic damage, and the beam leaps to another target. The entropic watcher makes another Insanity Beam attack against a new target within 30 feet of the first. A creature can be targeted by only one Insanity Beam per turn. For each additional creature the beam hits, its damage increases by 5 (1d10).
>
>***Dimensional Glide.*** The entropic watcher moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Shadow Crash (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 30-foot radius Sphere centered on a point the entropic watcher can see within 150 feet. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The Sphere's area is filled with magical Darkness for 1 minute or until the entropic watcher uses Shadow Crash again. A creature that ends its turn in the area takes 36 (8d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Contaminated Grasp.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, one creature Grappled by the entropic watcher. *Failure:* 16 (3d10) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Force Barrier.*** The entropic watcher creates a shimmering octarine 10-foot-by-10-foot wall of force centered on a point within 150 feet. The wall lasts for 1 minute or until the entropic watcher has the Incapacitated condition or dies. The wall appears in any orientation it chooses, and can be free floating or resting on a solid surface. If the wall cuts through a creature's space when it appears, the creature is pushed to one side of the wall (the entropic watcher's choice).  
>Nothing can physically pass through the wall. It is immune to all damage and can't be dispelled by *Dispel Magic*. A *Disintegrate* spell destroys the wall instantly, however. The wall also extends into the Ethereal Plane, blocking ethereal travel through the wall.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The entropic watcher chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The entropic watcher can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
