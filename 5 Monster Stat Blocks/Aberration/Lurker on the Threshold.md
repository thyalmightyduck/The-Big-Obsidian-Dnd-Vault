>## Lurker on the Threshold
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 168 (16d10 + 80)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 30 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Cha +3
>- **Skills** Perception +8
>- **Immunities** Cold, Necrotic; Charmed, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft. (unimpeded by magical Darkness), Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft. understands all languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The lurker is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the lurker's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the lurker can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the lurker has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Eldritch Blood.*** A creature that deals Piercing or Slashing damage to the lurker while within 5 feet of it takes 5 (1d10) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The lurker is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The lurker may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The lurker has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lurker makes three Tendril attacks. It can replace each Tendril attack with a use of Cosmic Rays.  
>
>***Tendril.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 30 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The lurker has ten tendrils, each of which can grapple one creature.  
>
>***Cosmic Rays.*** The lurker randomly shoots one of the following magical rays at a target it can see within 120 feet (roll 1d4; reroll if the lurker has already used that ray this turn):  
>- **Disintegrating Ray.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Force damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, it disintegrates into dust.
>- **Memory Lapse Ray.** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target's memories become distant for 1 minute. While its memories are distant, the target can't take the Magic action, use class features, or understand language, and has Disadvantage on ability checks. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.
>- **Contaminating Ray.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 16 (3d10) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Existential Dread Ray.** On the target's next turn, each time it takes an action other than the Dodge action, moves more than 5 feet, or takes a Bonus Action, it takes 14 (4d6) Psychic damage.
