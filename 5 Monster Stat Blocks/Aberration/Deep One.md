>## Deep One
>*Medium Aberration (Mythos), Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Int +4
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Perception +2
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The deep one can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The deep one has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The deep one makes two claw and one bite attacks.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, Reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, Reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The deep one casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Charm Person*, *Disguise Self*, *Message*  
>1/day: *Thunderwave*
