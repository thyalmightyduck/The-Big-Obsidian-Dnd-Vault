>## Mukad Droner
>*Small Aberration, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d6 + 6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 10 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Mukad, telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The mukad has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The mukad droner can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Tunneler.*** The mukad droner can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrowing Speed. When it burrows, it leaves a 5-foot diameter tunnel in its wake  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mukad droner makes one Bite attack and one Claw attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Poison damage, and if the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>If the target drops to 0 Hit Points while Poisoned, it is Stable but has the Poisoned condition for 1 hour and has the Paralyzed condition until it is no longer Poisoned.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Aetheric Scream (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11, each non-Aberration in a 30-foot radius Sphere. The Sphere spreads around corners. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target can't take Reactions until the end of the mukad droner's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aetheric Drone.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11, each non-Aberration in a 10-foot Emanation (with Advantage if the target is fighting the mukad droner). *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition. While Charmed, the target has Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to perceive any creature other than the mukad droner. The effect ends if the mukad droner has the Incapacitated condition or doesn't extend its duration as a Bonus Action on each of its turns.
