## Adult Snarite
*Tiny Aberration, Neutral Evil*
___
- **[[Armor Class]]** 14
- **[[Hit Point|Hit Points]]** 18 (4d4 + 8)
- **[[Speed]]** 5 ft., Fly 15 ft.
- **[[Initiative]]** +4 (14)
___

|   STR   |   DEX   |   CON   |   INT   |   WIS   |  CHA   |
| :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :-----: | :----: |
| 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 8 (-1) |
___
- **Skills** [[Stealth]] +6
- **Senses** [[Passive Perception]] 11, Passive Insight 11, Passive Stealth 16
- **Languages** —
- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
- **[[Proficiency Bonus]]** +2
___
### Traits
***Muddled Thoughts.*** Although an adult snarite cannot control the actions of its host, it can cloud a host's thoughts, inclining the host to make poor or irrational decisions. The host also refuses to cut their hair, thus saving the adult snarite from harm.  

***Wisdom Drinker.*** After being infected by a [[young snarite]] for a year, the host is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom [[Saving Throw]]:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target's Wisdom score is permanently reduced by 2.  

### Actions
***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) Slashing damage.  

### Reactions
***Deflect Damage.*** *Trigger:* The adult snarite is hit with a melee attack. *Response:* The adult snarite deflects the damage onto its host instead.
