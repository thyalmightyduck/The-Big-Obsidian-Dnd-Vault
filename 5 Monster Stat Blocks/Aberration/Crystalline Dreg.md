>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)

>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dreg makes two Refracted Beam attacks.  
>
>***Refracted Beam.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 21 (4d8 + 3) Radiant damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the start of the dreg's next turn. If this attack deals a different damage type due to Refract Elements, it has additional effects:  
>
>***Acid or Fire.*** The target is also Burning (2d6) of the corresponding damage type.  
>
>***Cold.*** The target has the Frozen Condition (DC 13).  
>
>***Lightning.*** The target has the Shocked Condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Refract Elements.*** *Trigger:* The dreg takes Acid, Cold, Fire, or Lightning damage. *Response:* The dreg gains Resistance to the triggering damage type, including the triggering damage, until the end of its next turn. During its next turn, its Refracted Beam attack deals damage of that damage type instead of Radiant damage.
