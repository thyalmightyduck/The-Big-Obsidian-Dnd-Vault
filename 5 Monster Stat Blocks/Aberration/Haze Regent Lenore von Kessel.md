>## Haze Regent Lenore von Kessel
>*Medium Aberration (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 76 (8d8 + 40) plus 76 (8d8 + 40) per player character
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|23 (+6)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +11, Insight +6, Perception +6, Stealth +9
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 16 (XP 15,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Haze Aura.*** A creature starting its turn within 30 feet of Lenore is subjected to the following effect: *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19. *Failure:* The target gains a Contamination level. *Failure or Success:* 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Tendrils.*** Lenore's long hair can latch onto surfaces to pull her in any direction. She has a Flying Speed of 40 feet (included in Speed) but cannot move more than 30 feet from a solid surface, including walls and ceilings.  
>
>### Actions
>***Lenore always takes her turn on Initiative count 20.*** On her turn, she can use her movement and interaction as normal. However, she does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Lenore takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn.  
>
>***Tentacle Hair.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d4 + 4) Piercing damage plus 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Contaminated Eye Blast.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Force damage, plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a creature it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19. *Failure:* The target gains one level of Contamination.  
>
>***Grim Reveal (1/Round).*** Lenore removes her mask, revealing her horrific form. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 19, creatures within 60 feet of her who can see her. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition (save ends).  
>
>***Octarine Wall (1/Day).*** Lenore summons a shimmering wall within 120 feet of her. The wall can be up to 90 feet long, 30 feet high, and 1 inch thick. Alternatively, she can shape the wall into a sphere up to 30 feet in diameter centered on a point she can see within range. The wall has three octarine layers. When a creature attempts to reach into or pass through the wall, it does so one layer at a time through all the wall's layers. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19. Failure:  
>Layer 1: 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage and the target gains one level of Contamination and has the Dazed condition; Layer 2: 17 (5d6) Necrotic damage, and the target gains two levels of Contamination and has the Defenseless condition; Layer 3: 35 (10d6) Necrotic damage, and the target gains three levels of Contamination and the Helpless condition. *Success:* Half damage only. The wall can be destroyed by taking at least 100 Radiant or Force damage.  
>
>***Radiant Gaze (1/Round).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 44 (8d10) Radiant damage and the target gains one level of Contamination. *Success:* Half damage only. If Lenore sees her reflection within the Cone, she gains the Blinded and Incapacitated conditions for 1d6 hours and can't take the Epic Resistance Epic Action for the duration.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Lenore chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting her and rolls 1d20. On an 11 or higher, the effect ends on her. Lenore can use this Epic Action even if she would otherwise be unable to take actions, her actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters her game statistics.
