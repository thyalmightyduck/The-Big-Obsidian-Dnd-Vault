>## Cyclopean Hulk
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 20 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hulk makes two Slam attacks. It can replace one of the attacks with its Eye Laser.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Eye Laser.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 60-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Force damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* If the target is a creature and this damage reduces it to 0 Hit Points, it disintegrates into a pile of fine delerium dust.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Wink (Recharge 4–6).*** The hulk vanishes from the Material Plane and is transported to the Space Between Worlds. At the start of its next turn, it returns to an unoccupied space of its choice within 10 feet of the space it vanished from. If no unoccupied space is available within that range, it appears in the nearest unoccupied space (chosen at random if more than one space is equally near).
