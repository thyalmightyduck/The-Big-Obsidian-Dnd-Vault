>## The White Worm
>*Gargantuan Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 330 (20d20 + 120)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Burrow 30 ft., Swim 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|28 (+9)|7 (-2)|22 (+6)|7 (-2)|19 (+4)|21 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +12, Wis +10
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Tremorsense 60 ft., Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands all but can't speak; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 19 (XP 22,000, or 25,000 in lair; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Inscrutable Mind.*** If a creature tries to read the worm's mind, that creature must succeed on a DC 19 Intelligence saving throw or have the Stunned condition. The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Lair).*** If the worm fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Poisoned Knowledge.*** If the worm uses Devour Mind on a creature that knows a special sequence of constellations and their path through the sky, at the start of the target's next turn, the worm takes 50 Psychic damage and Devour Mind ends.  
>
>***Tunneler.*** The worm can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrow Speed and leaves a 10-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The worm makes one Bite attack and uses Psychic Caress on one or two creatures.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 25 (3d10 + 9) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Force damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 19).  
>
>***Psychic Caress.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 19, one creature the worm can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 17 (5d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the start of the worm's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Devour Mind (1/Day).*** The worm vanishes and magically enters the mind of a creature it can see within 60 feet of it. The worm can't target a creature under the effect of  the *Mind Blank* or *Protection from Evil and Good* spells. While in the target's mind, the worm can't take actions, and the target experiences the worm appearing in its memories as it devours each one. At the end of each of the target's turns, it takes 88 (16d10) Psychic damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, the target is Stable but loses all its memories permanently. Only a *Wish* spell can restore the lost memories.  
>Devour Mind ends if the target is reduced to 0 Hit Points and the worm appears in an unoccupied space within 60 feet of the target. Devour Mind can also be ended in the following ways:  
>- As an action, the target mentally duels the worm  and makes a DC 20 ability check using Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (its choice). On a success, the worm must succeed on a Charisma saving throw with a DC equal to the check's total or Devour Mind ends.
>- Devour Mind ends if the target receives an effect  that ends possession, including the *Dispel Evil and Good* spell.
>- If the target takes damage, the worm takes Psychic damage equal to half that amount. It must succeed on a Charisma saving throw with a DC equal to the Psychic damage taken, or Devour Mind ends.
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Slither.** The worm moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
>- **Spawn Parasites.** The worm disgorges a slimy spray of pale, parasitic worms. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* The target is infested with parasitic worms and has the Poisoned condition. At the start of the Poisoned target's turn, it takes 7 (2d6) Necrotic plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage as the worms burrow toward its brain. A creature reduced to 0 Hit Points by this damage dies and then rises 24 hours later as a **Wormhost Surgeon**. The infesting worms are killed by any effect that ends the Poisoned condition on the target, or if 10 Poison damage or more is dealt to it. *Failure or Success:* The worm can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Strike.** The worm makes one Bite attack. It can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the White Worm's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Alien Growth.** Strange life grows rampant within 1 mile of the lair. In that region, **Grick**, **Purple Worm**, and **Wormhost Surgeons** are common.
>- **Psychic Phantoms.** Within 1 mile of the lair, creatures are plagued by horrid nightmares. When a creature sleeps or enters a Trance and finishes a Long Rest in that area, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or gain no benefit from finishing the rest.
>
>If the White Worm dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
