___
>## Nightgaunt
>*Large Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 136 (16d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Wis +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Lightning, Poison, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened, Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands Deep Speech but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Flyby.*** The nightgaunt doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The nightgaunt makes two attacks, using Claw or Barb in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Ascend.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13).  
>
>***Barb.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 21 (6d6) Piercing damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Ascend.*** The nightgaunt flies upward in a straight line, moving up to its Fly Speed. If the nightgaunt is grappling a creature, it ignores the extra movement cost to carry the creature with it.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Tickle.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature Grappled by the nightgaunt. *Failure:* The target has the Incapacitated condition until the start of the nightgaunt's next turn.
