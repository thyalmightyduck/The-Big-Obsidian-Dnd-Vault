>## Wormhost Surgeon
>*Medium Aberration, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 52 (7d8 + 21)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Medicine +7, Perception +7
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Deep Speech
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The surgeon regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If it takes Poison damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn. The surgeon dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Veiled Ignorance.*** A creature whose mind can't be read, or that is under the effect of a *Mind Blank* or *Nondetection* spell, has the Invisible condition toward the surgeon (this ignores the surgeon's Blindsight).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The surgeon makes two Bone Saw attacks.  
>
>***Bone Saw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Psychic damage.  
>Critical *Hit:* If the target is a creature, its Intelligence score decreases by 1d4. The target dies if this reduces that score to 0.  
>
>***Infested Spray (Recharge 5–6).*** The surgeon unleashes a spray of worm-ridden cerebral fluid. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 20-foot Cone. *Failure:* 17 (5d6) Psychic damage, and the target is infested with larval parasitic worms. The target takes 7 (2d6) Psychic damage at the end of each of its turns as the worms burrow toward its brain. The ongoing damage ends when the target takes Poison damage or is targeted by an effect that ends the Poisoned condition. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* If the target is reduced to 0 Hit Points by this Psychic damage, the worms reach its brain and it dies. A Humanoid slain in this way rises 24 hours later as a wormhost surgeon.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Parasite.*** *Trigger:* The surgeon is reduced to 0 Hit Points. *Response:* The parasite worm emerges from the surgeon's exposed brain and spits acid—*Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 20-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Acid damage. *Success:* Half damage.
