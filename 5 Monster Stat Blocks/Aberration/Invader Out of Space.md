___
>## Invader Out of Space
>*Medium Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 156 (24d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +6, Wis +5, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, Intimidation +8, Stealth +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Deep Speech; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Alien Mind.*** If a creature tries to read the intruder's mind or deals Psychic damage to it, that creature must succeed on a DC 16 Intelligence saving throw or have the Stunned condition for 1 minute. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.  
>
>***Gliding Levitation.*** The invader hovers 6 inches above the ground and leaves no tracks. It ignores Difficult Terrain caused by conditions on the ground, can move over liquids, and takes no damage from falling.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The invader has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Weak Constitution.*** The invader has Disadvantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition. If the invader is Poisoned at the start of its turn, it takes 20 Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The invader makes three Disrupting Touch attacks and uses Unnerving Glare if available.  
>
>***Disrupting Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Force damage. A creature killed or an object destroyed by this damage is reduced to a fine gray dust.  
>Magic items, and any equipment worn or carried by a disintegrated creature, don't disintegrate.  
>
>***Unnerving Glare (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the invader can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Incapacitated condition until the start of the invader's next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Otherworldly Slip.*** *Trigger:* The invader takes damage. *Response:* The invader teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 60 feet and can cause the following—*Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature within a 30-foot Emanation originating from the space where the invader vanished or appeared (invader's choice). *Failure:* 16 (3d10) Psychic damage, and the target's Speed is halved until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
