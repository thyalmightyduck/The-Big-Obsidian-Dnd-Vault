___
>## Horror Flit Swarm
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Aberrations, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 54 (12d8)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|8 (-1)|2 (-4)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing; Blinded, Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The horror flit swarm can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The horror flit swarm can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The horror flit swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bites.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Piercing damage, or 9 (2d8) Piercing damage if the swarm has the Bloodied condition. If a Small or larger creature is killed by this damage, a **Horror Flit Swarm** emerges from its corpse 1d4 hours later.
