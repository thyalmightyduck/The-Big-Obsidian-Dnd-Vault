___
>## Chitinous Dreg
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|8 (-1)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***No Escape.*** When the dreg hits a creature with an Opportunity Attack action, that creature's Speed becomes 0 until the end of its turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dreg makes two Bone Blade attacks.  
>
>***Bone Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Force damage, and the target has Disadvantage on attack rolls against any creature other than the dreg until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sentinel Strike.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 5 feet of the dreg takes the Disengage action or makes a melee attack roll against a target other than the dreg. *Response:* The dreg makes an Opportunity Attack action against that creature.
