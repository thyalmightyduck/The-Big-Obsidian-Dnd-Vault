___
>## Effulgent Cnidarian
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 153 (18d10 + 54)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 20 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Radiant
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Altered Reality.*** The cnidarian has the Invisible condition for creatures Charmed by it.  
>
>***Hallucinogenic Bioluminescence.*** A creature that starts its turn within 60 feet of the cnidarian and can see it is subjected to the following effect unless the cnidarian chooses otherwise. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition (save ends). While Charmed, the target regards all other creatures as enemies except the cnidarian, whom it regards as an ally.  
>Whenever the target makes an attack or casts a detrimental spell, it must choose its target at random from the creatures it can see within range. If a creature provokes an Opportunity Attack action from the target, it must make the attack, if able. If the target ends its turn without taking an Attack or Magic action, it takes 14 (4d6) Psychic damage.  
>Unless surprised, a creature can avert its eyes to avoid the saving throw at the start of its turn. If the creature does so, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against the cnidarian until the start of its next turn. If the creature looks at the cnidarian in the meantime, that creature must immediately make the saving throw.  
>
>***Neon Illumination.*** The cnidarian sheds Bright Light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 30 feet.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The cnidarian makes two Tendrils attacks.  
>
>***Tendrils.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 27 (6d8) Necrotic damage, or 48 Necrotic damage if the target is Charmed by the cnidarian.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Reflexive Flash.*** *Trigger:* A creature the cnidarian can see within 30 feet deals damage to it. *Response—**Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the cnidarian that can see it. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Radiant damage and the target has the Blinded condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.
