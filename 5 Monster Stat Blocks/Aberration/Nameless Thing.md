___
>## Nameless Thing
>*Tiny Aberration, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 17 (5d4 + 5)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +5, Perception +2, Stealth +4
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Deep Speech; telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** The nameless thing doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Invisibility.*** The nameless thing casts *Invisibility* on itself, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability.  
>When it casts the spell, the nameless thing can extend the effect of the spell to a creature it is touching. The spell ends early if either creature breaks the spell on itself or if the nameless thing stops touching the other creature. Once the nameless thing extends the spell in this way, it can't do so again until it finishes a Long Rest.  
>
>***Psychic Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d12 + 3) Psychic damage, and if the target is a creature, it has the Frightened condition until the start of the nameless thing's next turn.
