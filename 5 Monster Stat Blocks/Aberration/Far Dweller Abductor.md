___
>## Far Dweller Abductor
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Int +7, Wis +6, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Arcana +10, Deception +5, Perception +6, Persuasion +5, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft. understands all languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The far dweller is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the far dweller's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the far dweller can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the far dweller has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The far dweller is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The far dweller may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Unfathomable Presence.*** A creature that starts its turn within 120 feet of the far dweller and can see it is subjected to the following effect. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded and Immobilized conditions until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to the Unfathomable Presence of all far dweller abductors for 24 hours.  
>Unless surprised, a creature can avert its eyes to avoid the saving throw at the start of its turn. If the creature does so, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against the far dweller until the start of its next turn. If the creature looks at the far dweller in the meantime, that creature must immediately make the saving throw.  
>
>### Actions
>***Mind Warp.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 27 (4d10 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target must roll 1d4 and subtract the result from the next saving throw it makes before the end of the far dweller's next turn.  
>
>***Devour Thoughts.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature within 30 feet of the far dweller. *Failure:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target's Intelligence score is reduced by 1d4. If this effect reduces the target's Intelligence to 0, it has the Paralyzed condition. The reduction lasts until the target finishes a Short or Long Rest.  
>
>***Abduct.*** The far dweller casts *Plane Shift* without components, targeting itself and each Incapacitated creature within 10 feet of it.  
>
>***Spellcasting (Psionics).*** The far dweller casts one of the following spells, requiring no components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Thoughts*, *Dispel Magic*  
>2/day each: *Scrying*, *Sleep*, *Telekinesis*  
>1/day each: *Dominate Person*, *Modify Memory*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Psychic Step.*** The far dweller magically teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 60 feet.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell (1/Day).*** The far dweller casts *Counterspell* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting (Psionics).
