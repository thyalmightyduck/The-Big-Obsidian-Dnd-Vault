>## Wretched Patient
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 32 (5d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Conditioned Obedience.*** While the patient can see its creator, it has Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.  
>
>### Actions
>***Rusty Surgical Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Impulse.*** *Trigger:* The patient's creator is targeted by an attack or spell, and the patient is within 10 feet of it. *Response:* The patient switches spaces with its creator and becomes the target instead.
