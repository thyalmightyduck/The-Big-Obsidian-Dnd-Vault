___
>## Eldritch Dreg
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 82 (15d8 + 15)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +3
>- **Senses** Truesight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common, Deep Speech
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dreg makes two Psychic Blast attacks.  
>
>***Psychic Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 17 (4d6 + 3) Psychic damage and the dreg pushes the target 10 feet straight away from itself.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The dreg casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Mage Armor* (included in AC), *Mage Hand*, *Levitate* (self only)  
>
>***Synaptic Shock (Recharge 6).*** The dreg unleashes a storm of psychic energy in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within 60 feet of it. Each creature in that area must make a DC 13 Intelligence saving throw or take 14 (4d6) Psychic damage and has the Dazed condition (save ends).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hazewarp Hex.*** The dreg chooses a creature it can see within 60 feet of it and one of the following conditions: Addled, Clumsy, Enfeebled, or Sickened. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, the targeted creature. *Failure:* The target becomes cursed by the dreg for one minute or until the dreg dies or uses this ability again. While cursed in this manner, a creature becomes vulnerable to Psychic damage and gains the chosen condition (sane ends). *Success:* The target is immune to the dreg's Hazewarp Hex for 24 hours.
