>## Pale Man
>*Medium Aberration (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 76 (8d8 + 40) plus 76 (8d8 + 40) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|20 (+5)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Int +10, Wis +8, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +10, Deception +6, History +10, Perception +8
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Common and any five languages
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Aura of Fleshy Insects.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature of the Pale Man's choosing who starts its turn within 60 feet of the Pale Man. *Failure:* 22 (5d8) Necrotic damage and the target gains one level of Contamination.  
>
>***Crawling Flesh.*** When the Pale Man is reduced to 0 Hit Points, he breaks apart into a **Swarm of Contaminated Insects** in the same space. Unless the swarm is destroyed, the Pale Man's swarm returns to its cocoon and reforms into the Pale Man 1d6 days later.  
>
>***Insect Body.*** The Pale Man can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the Pale Man can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny insect.  
>
>### Actions
>***The Pale Man always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On his turn, the Pale Man can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the Pale Man takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn.  
>
>***Contaminating Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* The target gains one level of Contamination.  
>
>***Forced Mutation.*** The Pale Man targets a creature within 60 feet who has one or more mutations and magically alters the mutation. The Pale Man chooses one of the target's existing mutations to remove and replaces it with a different one of his choice.  
>
>***Infest the Flesh (1/Round).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation from the Pale Man. *Failure:* 22 (5d8) Necrotic damage, and the target gains the Burning (3d8 Necrotic) condition. A target also has the Dazed condition while it is burning in this way. Failure by 5 or more: The target also has the Restrained condition (save ends) due to masses of swarming fleshy bugs. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Contaminate (1/Round).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature Restrained by the Pale Man's Infest the Flesh action. *Failure:* The target gains one level of Contamination.  
>
>***Abrupt Contaminant (1/Round).*** The Pale Man targets a creature within 60 feet who has one or more levels of Contamination. That creature takes 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage for each level of Contamination it has.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The Pale Man chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11 or higher, the effect ends on him. The Pale Man can use this Epic Action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.  
>
>***Spellcasting (1/Round).*** The Pale Man casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18, +10 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Inflict Wounds*, *Mage Armor* (included with AC), *Magic Missile*, *Shield*, *Delerium Orb*, *Purge Contamination*, *Warp Bolt*, *Bacterial Barrage*, *Caustic Grip*, *Infect*  
>2/day each: *Bestow Curse*, *Evard's Black Tentacles*, *Hold Person*, *Web*, *Witch Bolt*, *Conjure the Deep Haze*, *Delerium Blast*, *Forced Evolution*, *Blood Worm*, *Septic Shock*, *Venomous Aura*  
>1/day each: *Antimagic Field*, *Blight*, *Chain Lightning*, *Giant Insect*, *Invisibility*, *Slow*, *Octarine Spray*, *Siphon Contamination*, *Grievous Wounds*, *Vile Necrosis*
