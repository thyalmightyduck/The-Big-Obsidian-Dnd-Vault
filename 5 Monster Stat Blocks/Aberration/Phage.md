>## Phage
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 61 (11d8 + 11)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|2 (-4)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +3
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Spell Vessel.*** After using its Consume Spell Reaction, the phage stores the spell until the spell is digested or the phage dies. When the phage dies, the spell takes effect. If the original caster of the spell sees the phage die, it can choose the area or target(s) of the spell as if they had cast it from the phage's space. Otherwise, the spell affects a random area or target(s) from those within range.  
>
>***Warped Space.*** The phage magically warps space in a 10-foot Emanation. Attacks targeting the phage from outside the Emanation have Disadvantage, and the phage has Advantage on saving throws against spells and effects that originate from outside the Emanation.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage. If the phage isn't storing a spell, it makes this attack with Advantage and deals an extra 14 (4d6) Piercing damage on a hit.  
>
>***Spit.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 60/120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 3) Acid damage.  
>
>***Digest Spell.*** The phage digests the spell it has stored, teleporting to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet and making a Spit attack with Advantage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Consume Spell.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 120 feet of the phage casts a spell while the phage isn't storing a spell. *Response:* The spell has no effect and is stored in the phage.
