___
>## Horror Flit Breeder
>*Large Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d10 + 24)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The horror flit breeder can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The horror flit breeder can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The horror flit breeder makes three Bite attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Piercing damage. If a Small or larger creature is killed by this damage, a **Horror Flit Swarm** emerges from its corpse 1d4 hours later.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Vomit Swarm (Recharges after a Long Rest).*** *Trigger:* The horror flit breeder becomes Bloodied. *Response:* The horror flit breeder opens its mouth and vomits a **Horror Flit Swarm** into a space within 5 feet of it.
