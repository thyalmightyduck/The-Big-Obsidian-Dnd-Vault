___
>## Rat King
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 170 (20d10 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 15 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|21 (+5)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Rat King makes two Tail Whip attacks, then uses Psychic Blast or Spellcasting.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The Rat King casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):  
>
>At will: *Mage Hand*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>1/day each: *Evard's Black Tentacles*, *Telekinesis*  
>
>***Tail Whip.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 33 (8d6 + 5) Psychic damage. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Dazed and Slowed conditions until the end of the Rat King's next turn.  
>
>***Psychic Blast (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Psychic damage, and the target is Jinxed 1d10 (save ends).
