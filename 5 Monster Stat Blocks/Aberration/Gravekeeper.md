>## Gravekeeper
>*Large Aberration (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 73 (7d10 + 35) plus 73 per player character (7d10 + 35)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 30 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|7 (-2)|21 (+5)|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|21 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +10, Int +9, Wis +8, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Perception +13
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 23
>- **Languages** All but doesn't speak
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Dazzling Aura.*** The gravekeeper sheds Bright Light in a 60-foot radius. A creature that starts its turn in the Bright Light takes 10 (3d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***The gravekeeper always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the gravekeeper can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the gravekeeper takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Lantern Blast.*** Magical octarine beams flash from one of the Gravekeeper's many lanterns at one creature it can see within 120 feet that is not behind Total Cover, subjecting it to one of the following effects (the gravekeeper can only use each blast once per round):  
>- **Dispelling Blast.** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Psychic damage. Any spell or magical effect on the target ends, and it has the Bewildered condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Forceful Blast.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Force damage, and the target is pushed 20 feet away from the gravekeeper and has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Blinding Blast.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Radiant damage, and the target has the Blinded condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Contaminating Blast.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Igniting Blast.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Fire damage, and the target has the Burning condition (10d6 Fire). *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Freezing Blast.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Cold damage, and the target has the Frozen condition (DC 18). *Success:* Half damage only.
>
>***Glide (1/Round).*** The gravekeeper moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Sludge Column (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 10-foot radius, 60-foot tall Cylinder, centered on a point within 120 feet of the Gravekeeper. *Failure:* 42 (12d6) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Animate Walls (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature within 10 feet of any number of walls within 120 feet of the gravekeeper. *Failure:* The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18). The wall sprouts limbs to facilitate this grapple, which disappear when the condition ends.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The gravekeeper chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting it and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The gravekeeper can use this Epic Action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
