>## Tachna
>*Small Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 66 (12d6 + 24)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Stealth +6
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Sleep
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Demiplane.*** The subject content of the tachna becomes a 40-foot Cube-shaped sentient demiplane designed to lure and trap victims. A creature within a 10-foot Emanation is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13 (a target touching the tachnaautomatically fails). *Failure:* The target is pulled into the tachna's demiplane, arriving in a random unoccupied space. The target canstill attack the tachna from within it, and can't leave the demiplane by any means until the tachna is defeated. *Success:* The target is immune to this trait for 1 hour.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The tachna uses Tremor, if available, and makes two Tentacle attacks.  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Tremor (Recharge 5–6).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in the tachna's demiplane. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.
