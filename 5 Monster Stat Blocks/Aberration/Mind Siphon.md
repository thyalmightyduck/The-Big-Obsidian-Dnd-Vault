>## Mind Siphon
>*Large Aberration (Shadowsteel Curse), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 154 (22d10 + 44)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Dex +6, Con +6, Int +8
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +6
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Displacement.*** Attack rolls against the Mind Siphon have Disadvantage, since it projects an illusion that makes it appear near its actual location. This trait is suppressed while the Mind Siphon has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The Mind Siphon has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The Mind Siphon can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Mind Siphon makes two Tentacle Tongue attacks at different targets.  
>
>***Tentacle Tongue.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 26 (5d8 + 4) Psychic damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16) from the Mind Siphon's Tentacle Tongue, and the target has the Stunned condition until the Grapple ends. The Mind Siphon can Grapple up to two creatures at the same time.  
>
>***Consume Head (Recharge 6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature Grappled by Tentacle Tongue. *Failure:* 44 (8d10) Piercing damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, the Mind Siphon kills it and devours its head.  
>
>***Synaptic Eruption (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature within 30 feet of the Mind Siphon. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of the Mind Siphon's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
