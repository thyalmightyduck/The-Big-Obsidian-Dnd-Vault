>## Far Dweller
>*Medium Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 113 (15d8 + 45)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|21 (+5)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Int +8, Wis +6, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Arcana +11, Deception +5, Perception +6, Persuasion +5, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft. understands all languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The far dweller is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the far dweller's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the far dweller can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the far dweller has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The far dweller is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The far dweller may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spellcasting (Psionics).*** The far dweller casts one of the following spells, requiring no components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):  
>
>At will: *Magic Missile*  
>3/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *Misty Step*  
>1/day each: *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Maze*, *Plane Shift* (self only), *Slow*, *Telekinesis*  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Force damage, and the target has the Dazed condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Contaminate.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature within 120 feet of the far dweller. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Mind Scramble (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 60-foot Cone (creatures maintaining Concentration have Disadvantage). *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Psychic damage, or 48 Psychic damage if the target was maintaining Concentration. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Pluck the Puppet Strings.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature within 60 feet of the far dweller. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage, and the target must immediately use its Reaction to move up to its Speed toward a creature of the far dweller's choice. If it ends this movement within reach of the creature, it must make a melee weapon attack against the creature with Advantage. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Vanish to the Space Between Worlds (3/Day).*** *Trigger:* The far dweller takes damage. *Response:* The far dweller takes no damage and vanishes to the Space Between Worlds. At the start of its next turn, the far dweller reappears in an unoccupied space of its choice within 60 feet of the space it left.
