___
>## Taisus
>*Huge Aberration, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 310 (27d12 + 135)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|18 (+4)|20 (+5)|25 (+7)|22 (+6)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +19, Insight +12, Perception +12
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Restrained
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 22
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 19 (XP 22,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Arcane Immortality.*** If the taisus dies, its body crumbles into glittering dust. It gains a new body after 1d10 days, reviving with all its Hit Points, unless a *Wish* spell is cast on its remains to prevent the effects of this trait. The new body appears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of a location designated by the taisus on its creation.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If the taisus fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The taisus has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The taisus makes two Brain Drain attacks.  
>
>***Brain Drain.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +13, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) Force damage plus 16 (3d10) Psychic damage, and if the target is a creature with spell slots, it's subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 21. *Failure:* The target's highest-level unexpended spell slot is expended.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The taisus casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 21):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Fireball* (level 5 version), *Invisibility*, *Mage Hand*  
>2/day each: *Jallarzi's Storm of Radiance*, *Plane Shift*, *Scrying*  
>1/day each: *Befuddlement*, *Disintegrate*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Magic.*** The taisus casts *Counterspell* or *Shield* in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the taisus can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The taisus regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Deadly Drain.** The taisus makes a Brain Drain attack. The taisus can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Teleport.** The taisus teleports itself or a willing creature it can see within 10 feet of itself up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space the taisus can see.
