>## Pyknic Maunder
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 9
>- **Hit Points** 67 (9d8 + 27)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Swim 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|1 (-5)|8 (-1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The maunder makes one Grasp or Bite attack against each creature within its reach. Alternatively, it makes two Eye Beam attacks.  
>
>***Grasp.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). The maunder has countless hands and can grapple any number of creatures.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft., one creature Grappled by the maunder. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Piercing damage. A Dazed target that is Grappled by the maunder is Helpless against this attack.  
>
>***Eye Beam.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Babble.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 10, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the maunder that can hear it. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Dazed condition until the start of its next turn.
