___
>## Hunting Horror
>*Huge Aberration (Mythos), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 184 (16d12 + 80)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 120 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Dex +7, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, Investigation +6, Perception +7, Survival +11
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 90 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d6 other languages
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Light Sensitivity.*** While in Bright Light, the hunting horror has Disadvantage on attack rolls and checks.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The hunting horror has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hunting horror makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack, It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, Reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The hunting horror casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Dispel Magic*, *Feather Fall*  
>1/day each: *Arcane Eye*, *Dimension Door*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Locate Creature*  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, Reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Prey's Doom.*** The horror unravels a target's life force with a bite. The hunting horror studies one creature it can see within 30 feet of itself. For 1 minute, once on each of the hunting horror's turns when it hits the target with an attack roll using its Bite attack, it can cause the target to take an extra 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the end of hunting horror's next turn. On each of the hunting horror's subsequent turns, it can take a Bonus Action to deal 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage to the target automatically.  
>This effect ends if the target is ever outside the range, if it has Total Cover from the hunting horror, if it is on a different plane of existence, or if hunting horror uses this Bonus Action on a different creature.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 2. Immediately after another creature's turn, the hunting horror can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The hunting horror regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Gnashing Teeth.** The hunting horror makes one Bite attack.
>- **Crushing Horror.** A creature Grappled by the hunting horror takes 22 (4d10) Bludgeoning damage.
>- **Pursuit of Prey.** The hunting horror moves up to its Speed.
