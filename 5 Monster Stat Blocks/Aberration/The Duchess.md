>## The Duchess
>*Gargantuan Aberration (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 87 (6d20 + 24) plus 87 (6d20 + 24) per player character
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|21 (+5)|17 (+3)|24 (+7)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +10, Int +11, Wis +9, Cha +13
>- **Skills** Arcana +10, Deception +12, Insight +14, Intimidation +12, Persuasion +12
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Necrotic, Poison, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 18 (XP 20,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The Duchess can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Aura of the Deep.*** A creature that starts its turn within 60 feet of the Duchess takes 14 (4d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, a creature can touch one of the Duchess's Delerium shards to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once a Delerium shard restores Hit Points in this way, it can't do so again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The Dutchess is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The Dutchess may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The Duchess has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Moisture Regeneration.*** The Duchess regains 20 Hit Points at the start of each of her turns if she is submerged at least partially in water. She dies only if she starts her turn at 0 Hit Points and does not regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***The Duchess always takes her turn on Initiative count 20.*** On her turn, the Duchess can use her movement and interaction as normal. However, she does not take an action or Bonus Action on her turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the Duchess takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d6 + 6) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting (Psionics).*** The Duchess casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20):  
>
>At will: *Detect Thoughts*  
>3/day each: *Dispel Magic*, *Dream*, *Polymorph*, *Sending*  
>1/day each: *Chain Lightning*, *Control Water*, *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Project Image*, *Scrying*, *Telekinesis*, Acid Rain *See **Chemist**.*, Biohazard, Controlled Mutation, Storm of Contamination, *Weave the Elder Sign*  
>
>***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 20 (4d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 20). The Duchess can have up to four creatures Grappled at a time.  
>At the start of each of its turns until the grapple ends, the target is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level.  
>
>***Fling.*** One Large or smaller object held or creature Grappled by the Duchess is thrown up to 60 feet in a direction of the Duchess's choice and has the Prone condition. If a thrown target strikes a solid surface, the target takes 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it was thrown. If the target is thrown at another creature, that creature must succeed on a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw or take the same damage and have the Prone condition.  
>
>***Deep Domination (1/Round).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature The Duchess can see. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition. The target is under the Duchess's control and can't take Reactions. The Duchess and the target can communicate telepathically over any distance.  
>Whenever the target takes damage, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. On a failure, it gains one Contamination level. Once every 24 hours, if the creature is at least 1 mile away from the Duchess, it can repeat the saving throw.  
>If the target undergoes a Monstrous Transformation, it becomes a **Deep Dreg Warrior**, a **Deep Knight**, or a **Deep Siren**, and is forever in thrall to the Duchess.  
>
>***Call of the Deep (1/Round).*** The Duchess calls forth up to five **Deep Dregs** or one **Deep Knight**. The creatures appear in unoccupied spaces within 30 feet of the Duchess and remain until destroyed. The creatures act after the Duchess on the same Initiative count and are under her control.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The Duchess chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting her and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on her. The Duchess can use this action even if she would otherwise be unable to take actions, her actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters her game statistics.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Sacrificial Pawn.*** *Trigger:* The Duchess takes damage from an attack. *Response:* The Duchess chooses an ally or a creature Charmed by her to take the attack's damage instead.
