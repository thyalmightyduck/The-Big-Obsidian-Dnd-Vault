>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Bile Blast.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 10-foot cone. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Acid damage and are Burning (2d8 Acid). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Hurl Fetid Flesh (Recharge 5–6).*** The Gutbuster Hulk rips off a chunk of its mutated flesh and hurls it at a point it can see within 120 feet. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature within a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Necrotic damage. *Success:* Half damage.
