___
>## Blood Brooder
>*Large Aberration, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 84 (8d10 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|20 (+5)|4 (-3)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Blood Pouches.*** An adjacent blood bulb can take refuge in pouches in the blood brooder's sides, entering or exiting a pouch using 5 feet of movement. Bulbs in a pouch have Three Quarters Cover, share the blood brooder's space, and move with the blood brooder. The blood brooder can hold up to eight blood bulbs in its pouches.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The blood brooder makes three Spines attacks.  
>
>***Spines.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Blood Exchange.*** The blood brooder loses any number of Hit Points and causes a blood bulb within 5 feet of it to regain an equal number of Hit Points. The blood brooder can't use Blood Exchange while it is Bloodied.
