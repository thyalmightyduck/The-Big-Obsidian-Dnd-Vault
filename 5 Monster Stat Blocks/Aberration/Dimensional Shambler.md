>## Dimensional Shambler
>*Large Aberration (Mythos), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 153 (18d10 + 54)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|17 (+3)|17 (+3)|8 (-1)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Dex +6, Con +6, Int +2, Wis +3, Cha +3
>- **Skills** Perception +3
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** All, but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Dimensional Attunement.*** A dimensional shambler gains 1 Dimension Point at the end of its turn during combat or when it is otherwise threatened. It begins an encounter with 0 points and can have up to 4.  
>
>***Dimensional Warping.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, if a creature within 30 feet of the dimensional shambler attempts to teleport. *Failure:* The creature doesn't teleport. *Success:* The creature teleports as normal.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The dimensional shambler has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dimensional shambler makes up to four Dimensional Claw attacks and uses Disdainful Banishment. It can't attack the same creature more than twice with Dimensional Claw when it uses Multiattack.  
>
>***Dimensional Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, Reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d10 + 5) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16). It can Grapple up to two Medium or smaller creatures at a time.  
>
>***Disdainful Banishment.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature Grappled by the dimensional shambler. *Failure:* The target is teleported up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space the dimensional shambler can see.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Dimensional Flurry.*** A dimensional shambler can spend 3 Dimension Points to gain an extra use of Legendary Action. It must use the additional use before its next turn.  
>
>***Dimensional Rend.*** A dimensional shambler can spend 1 Dimension Point to extend its claws through space and time, increasing its Reach with Dimensional Claw to 30 feet and allowing it to ignore all cover with those attacks until the end of its turn.  
>
>***Dimensional Step.*** A dimensional shambler can spend 3 Dimension Points to teleport itself and all creatures it has Grappled to a chosen destination it has visited within the past month.  
>
>***Teleport.*** The dimensional shambler teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 1. Immediately after another creature's turn, the dimensional shambler can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The dimensional shambler regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Attack.** The dimensional shambler makes up to two Dimensional Claw attacks.
>- **Shift.** The dimensional shambler uses Dimensional Step.
>- **Step.** The dimensional shambler uses Teleport.
