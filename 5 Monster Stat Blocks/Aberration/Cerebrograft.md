___
>## Cerebrograft
>*Large Aberration*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 142 (15d10 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|19 (+4)|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +8, Wis +6, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Telepathy 30 ft. The languages of its creator
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Dual Minds.*** The cerebrograft has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Stunned, and Unconscious conditions.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The cerebrograft makes two Slam attacks and uses Telekinetic Grip.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Telekinetic Grip.*** The cerebrograft reaches out with its mind to hold one creature or object it can see within 120 feet of it in its psychic grasp. If the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target is moved up to 30 feet in any direction and has the Restrained condition until the start of the cerebrograft's next turn or until the cerebrograft has the Incapacitated condition.  
>If the target is an object weighing 300 pounds or less that isn't being worn or carried, it is telekinetically moved up to 30 feet in any direction. The cerebrograft can also exert fine control on objects with its telekinesis, such as manipulating a simple tool or opening a door or a container.  
>
>***Psychic Crush (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the cerebrograft. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Psychic damage, and the target has the Dazed condition (save ends). Failure by 5 or More: The target instead has the Stunned condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.
