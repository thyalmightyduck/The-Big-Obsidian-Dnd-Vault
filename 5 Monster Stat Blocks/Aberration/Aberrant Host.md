- **[[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]]** Int +9, Wis +7, Cha +7

___
### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The host makes two Mind Spike attacks.  

***Mind Spike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) Psychic damage.  

***Psychic Barrage (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence [[Saving Throw]]:* DC 17, up to three creatures the host can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the [[Jinxed]] [[condition]] (1d8; save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.  

***Spellcasting.*** The host casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17, +9 to spell attacks):  

At will: *[[Detect Thoughts]]*, *[[Mage Hand]]* (the hand is [[Invisible Condition|invisible]])  
3/day: *[[Levitate]]*, *[[Suggestion]]*  
1/day: *[[Dominate Person]]*  

### Reactions
***Thought Shield.*** *Trigger:* The host or a creature it can see fails a [[saving throw]] or is hit by an attack. *Response:* The target rolls 1d6 and adds the result to its AC or [[saving throw]], potentially turning a hit into a miss or a failure into a success.
