___
>## Lamplighter
>*Large Aberration, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Radiant
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands Common but does not speak
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Lantern Light.*** The lamplighter sheds Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 10 feet. This light goes out if the lamplighter dies.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) necrotic damage.  
>
>***Lantern Beams.*** The lamplighter emits two different magical beams from their lanterns, choosing two targets they can see within 60 feet.  
>- **Blasting Light.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 60 feet who can see the lamplighter. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Force damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage.
>- **Blinding Light.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 60 feet who can see the lamplighter. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Radiant damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the start of the lamplighter's next turn. *Success:* Half damage.
>- **Burning Light.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 60 feet who can see the lamplighter. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Fire damage, and the target is burning. *Success:* Half damage.
>- **Witch Light.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 60 feet who can see the lamplighter. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition. While Charmed in this way, the target must use all of their movement on their turns to move toward the lamplighter. At the end of each of their turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on themself on a success. The target has advantage on its saves to end this effect if it's taken damage from the lamplighter. After 1 minute, the target is no longer Charmed by the lamplighter.
>
>### Reactions
>***Warding Flare.*** *Trigger:* A creature targets the lamplighter with an attack. *Response:* The lamplighter's lanterns flare, and the target has disadvantage on their attack rolls against the lamplighter this turn.
