___
>## Bigger Linda
>*Gargantuan Aberration (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 74 (4d20 + 32) plus 74 (4d20 + 32) per player character
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +14 (24)
>___

>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|29 (+9)|15 (+2)|26 (+8)|2 (-4)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Con +14, Wis +8, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Athletics +15, Perception +14
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Immunities** Acid, Necrotic
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 24
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 20 (XP 25,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Haze Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of Bigger Linda is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 22. *Failure:* The target gains a Contamination level. *Failure or Success:* 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** Bigger Linda has Immunity to any spell or effect that would alter her form.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** When Bigger Linda starts her turn in an area of the Deep Haze, she regains 20 Hit Points. Bigger Linda dies only if she starts her turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If Bigger Linda is destroyed and her corpse is left within an area of the Deep Haze for 1 hour, she is revived with all of her Hit Points.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** Bigger Linda deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bigger Linda always takes her turn on Initiative count 20.*** On her turn, Bigger Linda can use her movement and interaction as normal. However, she does not take an action or Bonus Action on her turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Bigger Linda takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Bite.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 35 (4d12 + 9) Piercing damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 23). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and Bigger Linda can't Bite another target.
>- **Tail.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d8 + 9) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it is pushed 20 feet away from Bigger Linda and has the Prone condition.
>- **Stomp.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 25 (3d10 + 9) Bludgeoning damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 23. *Failure:* The target has the Staggered condition (save ends).
>
>***Breathe Deep.*** Bigger Linda's Breath Weapon recharges. Bigger Linda can only take this Epic Action as the first Epic Action she uses this round. After using this Epic Action, Bigger Linda can't take her Bite Epic Action until the start of the next round.  
>
>***Breath Weapon (Special Recharge).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature in a 60-foot long, 5-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 31 (7d8) Necrotic damage plus 31 (7d8) Force damage, and the target gains a Contamination level. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Trampling Rampage (Recharges When Bloodied).*** Bigger Linda moves up to her Speed in a straight line, moving through the space of Large or smaller creatures in her path. She makes one Stomp attack against each creature she moves through.  
>
>***Teleport (1/Round).*** Bigger Linger teleports up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space she can see within the Deep Haze.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Bigger Linda chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on her. Bigger Linda can use this Epic Action even if she would otherwise be unable to take actions, her actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters her game statistics.
