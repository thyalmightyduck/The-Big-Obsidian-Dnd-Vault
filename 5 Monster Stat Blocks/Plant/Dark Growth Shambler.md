>*Medium Plant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|5 (-3)|13 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dark growth shambler makes two Claw attacks and one Infectious Bite attack.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Infectious Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target is infected with a disease called Dark Decay. Instead of acting normally on its turn, the target must move toward the closest creature that isn't infected with Dark Decay and make one melee attack against it. If the attack hits, the target of that attack must also make a saving throw against Dark Decay. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save. Subsequent Failures: The target gains one Contamination level. Third Success: The disease ends.  
>An infected creature that dies or undergoes a Monstrous Transformation becomes a Dark Growth Shambler. A *Lesser Restoration* spell or similar magic that removes diseases ends Dark Decay, as does any magic that removes Contamination levels.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Abrupt Decay.*** *Trigger:* The dark growth shambler drops to 0 Hit Points and it hasn't taken Fire damage this round. *Response:* The dark growth shambler creates a Dark Growth in its space.
