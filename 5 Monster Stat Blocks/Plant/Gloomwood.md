>## Gloomwood
>*Huge Plant, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 161 (17d12 + 51)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +7, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Perception +7, Stealth +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Immunities** Necrotic
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Blighted Aura.*** The ground in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the gloomwood is Difficult Terrain from mushrooms and fungus that grow in the area. A creature that ends its turn in the Emanation takes 5 (1d10) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Carved Past.*** As an action, a creature can use a Dagger or Woodcarver's Tools to carve the name in Sylvan of the ancient home of the gloomwoods into the gloomwood's trunk. Until the gloomwood finishes a Long Rest, it can't use its Fungal Fling or Spore Stomp actions.  
>
>***Fungal Step.*** The gloomwood ignores Difficult Terrain caused by plants or fungi.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gloomwood makes two attacks, using Slam or Fungal Fling in any combination.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16) from one of two branches. Until the grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***Fungal Fling.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 60/180 ft. *Hit:* 23 (5d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Spore Stomp (Recharge 5–6).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. *Hit or Miss:* Each creature within a 20-foot Sphere centered on the target must make the following saving throw. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
