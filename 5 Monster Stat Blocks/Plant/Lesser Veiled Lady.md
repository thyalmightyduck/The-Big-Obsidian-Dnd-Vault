>## Lesser Veiled Lady
>*Huge Plant (Hyphan), Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 66 (7d12 + 21)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +4, Insight +4, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Necrotic; Charmed
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Tremorsense 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Communication Spores, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Communication Spores.*** The Veiled Lady's telepathy extends throughout its lair. It communicates using images and emotion.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (2/Day).*** If the Veiled Lady fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Mycelial Form.*** The Veiled Lady can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check, and can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.  
>
>***Sun Sickness.*** While in sunlight, or if it takes 5 or more radiant damage in a single turn, the Veiled Lady has disadvantage on ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws until the end of its next turn. The Veiled Lady dies if it spends more than 1 hour in direct sunlight.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Veiled Lady uses either its Infectious Spores or Shackles of Pain, if available, then makes a Halluciwhip attack.  
>
>***Halluciwhip.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (3d4 + 3) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) poison damage and the target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Infectious Spores (Recharge 5-6).*** The Veiled Lady exhales a cloud of spores at a creature it can see within 30 feet of it. The creature must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or become afflicted by the fungal infection* spell for 1 minute. An affected creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.  
>
>***Shackles of Pain (Recharge 5-6).*** The Veiled Lady exhales a cloud of spores at a creature it can see within 30 feet of it. The creature must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or become afflicted by the shackles of pain* spell. The Veiled Lady can have only one creature afflicted by Shackles of Pain at a time.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Detritivore.*** As a bonus action, the Veiled Lady can target one Small or larger corpse not under the effects of the gentle repose spell within 15 feet of it, draining it of its essence and regaining 10 (3d6) hit points. Once a corpse has been targeted by this feature, it becomes desiccated and can't be targeted again.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>The Veiled Lady can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Veiled Lady regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.
>
>- **Hyphal Tether.** Tendrils of grasping hyphae burst from the ground, grabbing at one creature the Veiled Lady can see within 30 feet of it. The creature must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or become grappled (escape DC 13). Until this grapple ends, that creature is restrained.
>- **Mycelial Reconstitution.** The Veiled Lady dissolves into mycelia, ending any grapples or restraining effects to which it is currently subjected, and reappears in an unoccupied space on the ground it can see within 30 feet of it. This does not end grapples created by the Veiled Lady's Hyphal Tether.
>- **Whip.** The Veiled Lady makes a Halluciwhip attack.
