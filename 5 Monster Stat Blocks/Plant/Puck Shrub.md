___
>## Puck Shrub
>*Medium Plant, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 13 (4d8 + 4)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the puck shrub remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a thorny shrub.  
>
>### Actions
>***Brambles.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (2d4 + 1) Slashing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, the puck shrub can halve the attack's damage and cause the target to have the Grappled condition (escape DC 11). Until the grapple ends, the puck shrub can't make Brambles attacks against another target.  
>
>***Hurl Thorns.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, Range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (2d4 + 1) Piercing damage.
