>## Crooked Effigy
>*Medium Plant, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 45 (6d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|9 (-1)|16 (+3)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +5, Survival +5
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Crooked Thorns.*** At the start of each of its turns, the effigy deals 5 (1d10) Psychic damage to any creature it is grappling or any creature grappling it.  
>
>***Old Bindings.*** The effigy takes an extra 10 Force damage if it's hit by an attack roll using *Smith's Tools* or *Tinker's Tools* (treat as a *Club* or *Light Hammer*).  
>
>### Actions
>***Impale.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13) from one of two branch arms. Until the grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition.
