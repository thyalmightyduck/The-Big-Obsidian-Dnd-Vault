___
>## Sewer Thing
>*Huge Plant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 92 (8d12 + 40)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|20 (+5)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Deafened, Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Common but doesn't speak
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Instinctive Ambusher.*** At the start of the sewer thing's first turn of each combat, if the sewer thing attacks a creature, it can make one additional Tendril Lash attack as part of its Multiattack action.  
>
>***Poison Absorption.*** Whenever the sewer thing takes Poison damage, it takes no damage and regains a number of Hit Points equal to the Poison damage dealt.  
>
>***Toxic Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of the sewer thing is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* 5 (1d10) Poison damage.  
>
>***Unexpected Appearance.*** If the sewer thing is motionless at the start of combat, it has Advantage on its Initiative roll. Additionally, if a creature hasn't observed the sewer thing move or act, that creature must succeed on a DC 18 Intelligence (Investigation) check to discern that the sewer thing is animate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The sewer thing makes two attacks and uses Engulf.  
>
>***Tendril Lash.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 30 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16).  
>
>***Reel.*** The sewer thing pulls each creature Grappled by it up to 20 feet straight toward it.  
>
>***Engulf.*** The sewer thing engulfs a Medium or smaller creature within 5 feet of it that is Grappled by it. The grapple ends. The target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, is suffocating, and is subjected to the following effect at the start of each of the sewer thing's turns. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Poison damage plus 11 (2d10) Acid damage.  
>If the sewer thing moves, the target moves with it. The sewer thing can have up to four creatures engulfed at a time. An engulfed creature can take an action to escape with a successful DC 16 Strength (Athletics) check, landing in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the sewer thing.
