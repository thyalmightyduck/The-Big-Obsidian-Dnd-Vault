___
>## Lightning Vine
>*Large Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 135 (18d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|15 (+2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Lightning; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the lightning vine remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary plant.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lightning vine makes two attacks, using Grasp or Arc in any combination. If it has a creature grappled, it uses Stunning Shock if available.  
>
>***Grasp.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The lightning vine can grapple up to four Medium or smaller targets at a time.  
>
>***Arc.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 30 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d8) Lightning damage, and the target can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Stunning Shock (Recharge 4–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature Grappled by the lightning vine. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Lightning damage.
