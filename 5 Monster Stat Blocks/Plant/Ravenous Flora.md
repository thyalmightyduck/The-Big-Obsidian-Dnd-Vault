>## Ravenous Flora
>*Large Plant (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 42 (4d10 + 20) plus 42 (4d10 + 20) per player character
>- **Speed** 15 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +3, Int +3, Wis +5
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Deafened, Poisoned, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Tremorsense 300 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Poison Ivy.*** If the ravenous flora would take Poison damage, it instead takes no damage and regains Hit Points equal to the Poison damage it would have received.  
>
>***Writhing Aura.*** The ground within 30 feet of the ravenous flora is filled with plant roots and becomes Difficult Terrain. A creature that starts its turn in the aura takes 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***The ravenous flora always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the ravenous flora can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the ravenous flora takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Bite.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). Until this grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition and the ravenous flora can't Bite another target.
>- **Spit Acid.** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Acid damage, and the target has the Burning condition (2d4 Acid). While Burning, the target has the Blinded condition.
>- **Swallow.** The ravenous flora makes one Bite attack against a Medium or smaller target it has Grappled. If the attack hits, the target is swallowed and the grapple ends. The swallowed target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover from attacks and effects outside the flora, and takes 10 (3d6) Acid damage at the start of each of its turns. The flora can have up to four targets swallowed at a time.
  If the flora takes 15 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, it must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which land with the Prone condition in unoccupied spaces within 10 feet of it. If the flora dies, a swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape from the corpse using 5 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.
>
>***Hypnotic Pollen (1/Round).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation originating from the ravenous flora. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Weakened condition (save ends). While Weakened, the target must spend all of its movement on each of its turns to move directly toward the ravenous flora. During this movement, the target ignores Difficult Terrain caused by the flora's Writhing Aura.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The ravenous flora chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The ravenous flora can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
