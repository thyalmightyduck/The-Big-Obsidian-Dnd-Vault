>## Seed Coin
>*Tiny Plant, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 5
>- **Hit Points** 2 (1d4)
>- **Speed** 0 ft.
>- **Initiative** -5 (5)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|3 (-4)|1 (-5)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +4, Persuasion +4
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 6
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** The seed coin is indistinguishable from a normal copper piece. However, a creature that has truesight sees a deep red aura radiating from the seed coin, and a creature within 30 feet of it that is under the effect of a *detect poison and disease* spell senses the insidious nature of the seed coin and knows it is a plant creature.  
>
>***Unusual Nature.*** The seed coin doesn't require sleep. If the seed coin spends 1 minute in direct sunlight or a creature spends 1 hour in direct sunlight while the seed coin is on the creature's person, the seed coin receives enough nourishment to sustain itself for one day. When the seed coin dies, it becomes an ordinary copper piece.  
>
>### Actions
>***Infestation.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +0 to hit, reach 0 ft., one humanoid that has the seed coin on its person. *Hit:* The creature must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw or its mind becomes imperceptibly infected with a disease until the seed coin is no longer on the creature's person or the creature dies. Creatures immune to the charmed condition are immune to this disease, and a creature that succeeds on the saving throw is immune to this disease for the next 30 days. The seed coin's influence invades a diseased creature's mind, killing the creature in a number of hours equal to 1d12 + the creature's Wisdom score, unless the disease is removed. In half that time, the creature becomes charmed for the rest of the duration and refuses to part with the seed coin.  
>If a diseased creature dies while the seed coin is on its person, it immediately regains all its hit points and becomes a **seed coin pollinator**, as the seed coin takes over its body. The seed coin doesn't gain access to the creature's knowledge, class features, or proficiencies while controlling a creature in this way. This infestation lasts until the **seed coin pollinator** dies, killing the seed coin.  
>
> ##### Variant: Silver Seed Coin
>
>A rare breed, these seed coins bear an uncanny resemblance to a typical silver piece. Though less prevalent, they are even more potent than their copper kin. Such a silver seed coin uses the seed coin stat block, with the following changes:
>
>- It has a challenge rating of 1 (200 XP).
>- Its Intelligence and Charisma scores each increase to 14.
>- It has immunity to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical attacks.
>- It is indistinguishable from a normal silver piece, and it becomes an ordinary silver piece when it dies.
>- It has the Innate Spellcasting trait below, which it retains after it becomes a seed coin pollinator.
>- The saving throw for its Infestation increases to DC 12, and it kills a diseased creature in a number of hours equal to 1d6 + the creature's Wisdom score, rather than 1d12.
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The seed coin's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 12). The seed coin can innately cast the following spells, requiring no components:
>
>- At will: *prestidigitation*
>- 3/day each: *comprehend languages*, *misty step*
>- 1/day each: *locate creature*, *suggestion*
