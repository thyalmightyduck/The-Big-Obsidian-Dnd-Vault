>## Dark Growth
>*Medium Plant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 120 (16d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 0 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Grappled, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Mimicry.*** The dark growth can mimic any sounds it has heard, including voices. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The dark growth regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the Dark Growth takes Fire or Radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the dark growth's next turn. The dark growth dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dark growth makes three attacks: two with its Entangling Vines and one with its Infection Spores.  
>
>***Entangling Vines.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 30 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) and the target has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends.  
>
>***Infection Spores.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d6 + 4) Necrotic damage, and if the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target is infected with a disease called Dark Decay. Instead of acting normally on its turn, the target must move toward the closest creature that isn't infected with Dark Decay and make one melee attack against it. If the attack hits, the target of that attack must also make a saving throw against Dark Decay. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save. Subsequent Failures: The target gains one Contamination level. Third Success: The disease ends.  
>An infected creature that dies or undergoes a Monstrous Transformation becomes a Dark Growth Shambler. A *Lesser Restoration* spell or similar magic that removes diseases ends Dark Decay, as does any magic that removes Contamination levels.
