>***False Appearance.*** While the hell's reach remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary mushroom.  
>
>***Fiery Body.*** A creature that touches the hell's reach or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 2 (1d4) Fire damage.
