>## Thunderblossom
>*Tiny Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 63 (18d4 + 18)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|1 (-5)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Thunder; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the thunderblossom remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary flowering plant.  
>
>### Actions
>***Boom Bloom.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the thunderblossom. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Thunder damage and the target has the Deafened condition until the end of its next turn. *Failure by 3 or More:* The target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The explosion can be heard from up to 150 feet away.
