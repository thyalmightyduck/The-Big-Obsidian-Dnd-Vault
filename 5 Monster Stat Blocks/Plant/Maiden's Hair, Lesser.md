>## Maiden's Hair, Lesser
>*Medium Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 16 (3d8 + 3)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Climb 5 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|1 (-5)|10 (+0)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Psychic
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened, Exhaustion, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Enamoring Scent.*** A creature that starts its turn within 20 feet of the maiden's hair is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. Every minute, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 hour. *Success:* The target is immune to the maiden hair's Enamoring Scent for 24 hours.  
>While Poisoned, the target has the Charmed and Incapacitated conditions. The target uses its movement on each of its turns to approach the maiden's hair. It doesn't avoid danger, but before moving into damaging terrain and whenever it takes damage from a source other than the maiden's hair, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.  
>A strong wind (such as one created by *Gust of Wind*) suppresses the scent for the wind's duration.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the maiden's hair remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary plant.  
>
>### Actions
>***Tendril.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition. While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and takes 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns, and the maiden's hair can't make Tendril attacks against other creatures.
