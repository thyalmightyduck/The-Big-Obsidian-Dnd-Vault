>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 3 (1d4 + 1)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)

>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 0 (XP 10; PB +2)

>***Regrowth.*** When the mold spider is killed by damage other than Fire damage, it releases spores that grow a new mold spider in 3 days.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The mold spider can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
