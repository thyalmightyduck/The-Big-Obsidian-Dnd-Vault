>## Shrieking Mandrake
>*Tiny Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 9
>- **Hit Points** 5 (2d4)
>- **Speed** 5 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|3 (-4)|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|1 (-5)|3 (-4)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened, Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 6
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 0 (XP 10; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the shrieking mandrake remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from ordinary mandrake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +1, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1 Piercing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Shriek.*** *Trigger:* The shrieking mandrake emerges from the ground. *Response:*—*Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 10, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the shrieking mandrake that can hear it. *Failure:* 2 (1d4) Psychic damage.*Failure by 5 or More:* The target also has the Deafened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target can make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it automatically succeeds.
