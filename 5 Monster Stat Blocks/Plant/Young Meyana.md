>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) Acid damage, or 5 (2d4) Acid damage if the target is Grappled by the meyana. If the target is Small or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 11). Until the grapple ends, the meyana can't Bite another target.
