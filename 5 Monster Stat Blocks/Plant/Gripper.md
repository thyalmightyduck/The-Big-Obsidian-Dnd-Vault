___
>## Gripper
>*Medium Plant, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 9 (2d8)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Burrow 10 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +2, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Slashing
>- **Immunities** Necrotic; Blinded
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Death Spores.*** If the gripper is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it swells for 1 round, then uses Spores, if able.  
>
>***Echo Navigation.*** If the gripper has the Deafened condition, its Speed is halved  
>
>***Otherworldly Perception.*** The gripper's Blindsight extends into the Ethereal Plane.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Constrict.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the gripper can't Constrict another target.  
>
>***Spores (1/Day).*** The gripper releases spores in a 5-foot Emanation, or a 10-foot Emanation if it is dead. The Emanation spreads around corners and is immediately dispersed by a moderate or stronger wind.  
>*Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10, a creature that starts its turn in the Emanation or moves there for the first time on a turn. *Failure:* 2 (1d4) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to the Incapacitated condition, but not the damage, caused by all grippers' Spores for 1 hour.
