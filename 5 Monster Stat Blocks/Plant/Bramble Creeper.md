___
>## Bramble Creeper
>*Large Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 52 (7d10 + 14)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Climb 5 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|1 (-5)|6 (-2)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +3
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Prone, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Tremorsense 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Ambusher.*** The creeper has advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has surprised.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the creeper remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from normal cluster of vines.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The creeper can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Thorns.*** At the start of each of its turns, the creeper deals 7 (2d6) piercing damage to any creature grappling it, or that it has grappled.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The creeper makes two attacks with its strangling bramble. It can't make both attacks against the same target.  
>
>***Strangling Bramble.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one Large or smaller target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 14). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained and at risk of suffocating. If the creeper is grappling a Large creature, or two Medium or smaller creatures, it can't use this attack against another creature.
