>## Ordeal Tree
>*Gargantuan Plant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 186 (12d20 + 60)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|24 (+7)|7 (-2)|21 (+5)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Corrupt Desire.*** A Humanoid can bind themself to the ordeal tree. Each day the petitioner is bound to the tree, they must succeed on a Constitution saving throw with a DC equal to 15 plus the number of days bound or suffer an Exhaustion level. If they survive for 7 days, the ordeal tree can choose to grant a blessing. The tree only does this if the Humanoid is Evil, once per Humanoid. If the Humanoid is neutral or good, the tree attacks them instead.  
>The blessing is tailored to the petitioner's desires (at the GM's discretion), but might include a +1 bonus to an ability score, a Feat, Expertise in a skill, or the ability to innately cast a spell of level 3 or lower once per day using Charisma as the spellcasting ability.  
>The petitioner also contracts the *Curse of Foul Blight* [the following text has been removed due to homebrew: (see Grim Hallow Campaign Guide]). If the petitioner commits a profoundly evil act every day, the curse never advances beyond Stage 1. If the petitioner fails to commit any evil acts, the curse automatically escalates.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the ordeal tree remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary tree.  
>
>***Heart Sight.*** The ordeal tree knows the alignment and desires of any creature that binds itself to the tree.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ordeal tree makes two Slam attacks and one Vine attack. It can replace the Vine attack with Enervate.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 29 (4d10 + 7) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Vine.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) Slashing damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17).  
>
>***Enervate (2/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature Grappled by the ordeal tree. *Failure:* 44 (8d10) Necrotic damage, and the ordeal tree gains Temporary Hit Points equal to half the Necrotic damage dealt.
