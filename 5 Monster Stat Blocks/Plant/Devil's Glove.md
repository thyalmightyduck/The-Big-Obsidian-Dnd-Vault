>## Devil's Glove
>*Tiny Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d4 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|8 (-1)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Deafened, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Scent of Death.*** Creatures that can smell have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to find the devil's glove.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The devil's glove can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Tendril.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage, and the devil's glove attaches to the target. While attached, the devil's glove can't make Tendril attacks against other targets, and if the target is a creature, the target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn if it can smell the devil's glove.  
>The devil's glove can detach itself by spending 5 feet of its movement. The target or a creature within 5 feet of it can detach the devil's glove as an action.  
>
>***Decaying Bloom (2/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the devil's glove. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the start of the devil's glove's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Symbiosis.*** The devil's glove forms a magical bond with a willing creature it touches. The bonded creature doesn't need to eat or drink, and the creature has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition. The bond ends when the creature dies, the devil's glove dies, the devil's glove ends the bond (no action required), or when the devil's glove uses this Bonus Action again.
