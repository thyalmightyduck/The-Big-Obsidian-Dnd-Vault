>## Seed Coin Pollinator
>*Medium Plant, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 7 (1d8 + 3)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Persuasion +5
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** The languages its host knew in life
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** The pollinator is indistinguishable from a normal commoner, and it appears and sounds exactly like the creature it was before becoming infested. However, a creature that has truesight sees a deep red aura radiating from the pollinator, and a creature within 30 feet of it that is under the effect of a *detect poison and disease* spell senses the insidious nature of the pollinator and knows it is a plant creature.  
>
>***Unusual Nature.*** The pollinator doesn't require sleep. If the pollinator spends 1 hour in direct sunlight, it receives enough nourishment to sustain itself for one day. When the pollinator dies, it becomes an ordinary humanoid corpse.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) damage.  
>
>***Coin Spray (Recharge 6).*** The pollinator hurls a flurry of **seed coins** at one creature it can see within 10 feet of it. The target must make a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (4d4) bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, one of the seed coins gets lodged on the target's body (for example, in a pocket or a fold in its clothing).
