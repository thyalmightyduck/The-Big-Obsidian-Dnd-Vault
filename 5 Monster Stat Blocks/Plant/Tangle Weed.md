___
>## Tangle Weed
>*Large Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 9
>- **Hit Points** 30 (4d10 + 8)
>- **Speed** 15 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|9 (-1)|16 (+3)|1 (-5)|10 (+0)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened, Exhaustion, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the tangle weed remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary plant.  
>
>### Actions
>***Constrict.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* The target takes 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. At the end of the tangle weed's turn, the target is pulled 5 feet toward it.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Whipping Strand.*** *Trigger:* A creature Grappled by the tangle weed is subject to an effect that moves it and ends the Grapple. *Response:* The tangle weed makes a Constrict attack against the creature.
