>## Daemon Oak
>*Huge Plant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 161 (14d12 + 70)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|8 (-1)|21 (+5)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Infernal, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Siege Monster.*** The daemon oak deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The daemon oak makes two attacks, using Lash or Rock in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Spew.  
>
>***Lash.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 23 (5d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The daemon oak can grapple four creatures at a time.  
>
>***Rock.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 60/180 ft. *Hit:* 32 (4d12 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Spew (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Acid damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Tree Thrall (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The daemon oak curses and animates one tree within 30 feet. The tree becomes a **Daemon Oak Thrall**. This thrall is the daemon oak's ally, and it takes its turn just after the daemon oak. The thrall obeys the daemon oak's verbal commands (no action required for the daemon oak). If issued no commands, the thrall attacks a creature that isn't the thrall's ally. A thrall dies after 24 hours, when the daemon oak uses a Bonus Action to sever the connection to the thrall, the thrall is more than 1 mile from the daemon oak, or the daemon oak dies.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Chain.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 10 feet of the daemon oak starts to move. *Response:* The daemon oak makes a Lash attack against the triggering creature.
