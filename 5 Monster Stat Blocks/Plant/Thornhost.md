>## Thornhost
>*Medium Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire, Poison
>- **Damage Resistances** Piercing
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Thorn Rot.*** A Humanoid that takes damage from the thornhost's Thorns attack or Burst is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* 2 (1d4) Piercing damage. For 3 rounds, the target repeats the save at the start of each of its turns as the thorn continues to burrow. Third *Failure:* The thorn takes root, the target can't regain Hit Points lost to Thorn Rot until it is cured, and the target suffers an Exhaustion level.  
>During the first 24 hours, a creature can take an action to remove the thorn rot with a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check. Otherwise, at the end of each Long Rest, the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 12 plus the number of thorns, up to DC 15) or suffer an Exhaustion level. If the target dies from Exhaustion, its soul departs its still-living body and becomes a thornhost.  
>
>### Actions
>***Thorns.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d8 + 1) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Burst.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the thornhost. *Failure:* 12 (4d4 + 2) Piercing damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The thornhust dies.
