>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The meyana makes one Bite attack and one Greatclub attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 5 (2d4) Acid damage, or 10 (4d4) Acid damage if the target is Grappled by the meyana. If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12). Until the grapple ends, the meyana can't Bite another target.  
>
>***Greatclub.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.
