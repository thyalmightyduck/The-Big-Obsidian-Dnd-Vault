___
>## Logger
>*Large Plant (Brute), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +9, Con +6, Wis +3
>- **Skills** Athletics +9, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Lightning; Blinded, Deafened, Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Lightning Absorption.*** Whenever Logger is subjected to lightning damage, they take no damage and instead regain a number of hit points equal to the lightning damage dealt.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Logger makes two Slam attacks. If they are in a fury (see Nature's Fury), they instead make three Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Entangling Vines.*** Vines lash out from Logger's body.  
>Each enemy within 30 feet of them must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or take 9 (1d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage and be grappled (escape DC 17) and pulled up to 30 feet directly toward Logger.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Nature's Fury (1/Day).*** Logger enters a fury for 1 minute.  
>While in a fury, Logger has advantage on Strength checks and saving throws, and they can make an extra Slam attack when they use Multiattack. If Logger ends their turn without damaging another creature on that turn, the fury ends early.
