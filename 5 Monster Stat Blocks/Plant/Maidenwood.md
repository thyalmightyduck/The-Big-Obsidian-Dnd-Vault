>## Maidenwood
>*Huge Plant, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 103 (9d12 + 45)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|8 (-1)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Druidic, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Undoing Gift (1/Day).*** The maidenwood must accept and eat an apple offered to it or to its lure. The maidenwood then takes 55 (10d10) Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The maidenwood makes two Tendril attacks. It can replace one attack with a Chew attack.  
>
>***Tendril.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16) from one of two tendrils. Until the grapple ends, it has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***Chew.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d10 + 6) Piercing damage. If the target has the Restrained condition, it takes an extra 18 (4d8) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Lure.*** The maidenwood shapes its lure into a Small or Medium Humanoid, or it retracts the lure. The maidenwood can speak through the lure and move it anywhere within reach of its tendrils, and it causes the following effect—*Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature within 30 feet of the lure. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition until the start of the maidenwood's next turn. The Charmed target must approach the lure as closely as it can. The maidenwood has Advantage on attack rolls against the Charmed target. If the target takes damage, the Charmed condition ends at the end of the current turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Absorb Magic.*** *Trigger:* The maidenwood succeeds on a saving throw against a spell or other magical effect, or a spell attack roll misses it. *Response:* The maidenwood gains 10 Temporary Hit Points.
