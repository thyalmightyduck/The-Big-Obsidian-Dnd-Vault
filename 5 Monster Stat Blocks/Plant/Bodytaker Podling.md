>## Bodytaker Podling
>*Small or Medium Plant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +4
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Deep Speech plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Semblance of Life.*** The podling is a physical copy of a creature digested by a Bodytaker Plant. The podling has Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks made to pass as the digested creature. The podling melts into a slurry when it dies.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.
