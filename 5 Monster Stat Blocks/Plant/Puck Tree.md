___
>## Puck Tree
>*Huge Plant, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 95 (10d12 + 30)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|6 (-2)|17 (+3)|3 (-4)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the puck tree remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a tree.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The puck tree deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (4d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, the puck tree can halve the attack's damage and cause the target to have the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). The puck tree can grapple up to two creatures at a time.  
>
>***Rock.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 32 (5d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Fling.*** The puck tree throws a Medium or smaller creature it has Grappled up to 30 feet horizontally. The target takes 1d10 Bludgeoning damage per 10 feet it travels and has the Prone condition. If the target is thrown at another creature, that creature must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take the same damage and have the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Deadfall.*** *Trigger:* The puck tree is reduced to 0 Hit Points. *Response:*—*Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 15- foot long, 5-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 17 (5d6) Bludgeoning damage. *Success:* Half damage.
