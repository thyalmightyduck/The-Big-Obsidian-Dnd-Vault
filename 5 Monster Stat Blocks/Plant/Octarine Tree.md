>## Octarine Tree
>*Huge Plant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 147 (14d12 + 56)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|6 (-2)|18 (+4)|8 (-1)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +2, Con +8, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Nature +3
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Cold, Lightning, Piercing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Enraged.*** While Bloodied, the octarine tree gains Advantage on all attack rolls.  
>
>***Plant Camouflage.*** The octarine tree has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to Hide in forests.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The octarine tree deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The octarine tree makes three attacks.  
>
>***Thwack.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 19 (2d12 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it is pushed 10 feet and has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Crushing Roots.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 25 (3d12 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The octarine tree can grapple up to four creatures at a time.  
>
>***Delerium Fruit.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 60/120 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level. Failure by 5 or More: The target triggers an Arcane Anomalies centered on itself.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Take Root.*** If the octarine tree has not moved this round, it can plant itself into the ground. It regains 18 (4d8) Hit Points and has the Immobilized condition until the start of its next turn. While Immobilized, the octarine tree has Immunity to the Grappled, Prone, and Restrained conditions, and its AC increases to 20. These benefits end early if the tree takes Fire damage.
