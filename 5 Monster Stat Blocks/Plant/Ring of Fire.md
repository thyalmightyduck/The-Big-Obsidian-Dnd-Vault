___
>## Ring of Fire
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Plants, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|15 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Fire; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 6
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the ring of fire remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary cluster of mushrooms.  
>
>***Fiery Swarm.*** A creature that touches the ring of fire or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 5 (2d4) Fire damage, or 2 (1d4) Fire damage if the ring of fire has the Bloodied condition.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The ring of fire can occupy another creature's space and vice versa. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +0, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (4d4) Fire damage, or 5 (2d4) Fire damage if the ring of fire has the Bloodied condition.
