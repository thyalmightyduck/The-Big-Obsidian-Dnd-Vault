___
>## Valochera
>*Large Plant (Fungus, Controller); Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 180 (19d10 + 76)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +9, Dex +4
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Acid, Lightning, Poison; Blinded, Deafened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Acid Absorption.*** Whenever the valochera is subjected to acid damage, they take no damage and instead regain a number of hit points equal to the acid damage dealt.  
>
>***Unbearable Stench.*** Any creature who starts their turn within 5 feet of the valochera must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The valochera makes two Mycelium attacks.  
>
>***Mycelium.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is Large or smaller, they are grappled (escape DC 15).  
>Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained.  
>
>***Chow Time.*** The valochera douses a creature they have grappled with acid and then takes a bite. The target must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) acid damage plus 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.  
>
>***Poison Glob (Recharge 5–6).*** The valochera spits a glob of noxious sludge at a point they can see within 30 feet of them.  
>Each creature in a 5-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw, taking 33 (6d10) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
