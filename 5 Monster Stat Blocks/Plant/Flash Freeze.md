___
>## Flash Freeze
>*Huge Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 200 (16d12 + 96)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|10 (+0)|22 (+6)|1 (-5)|15 (+2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Immunities** Cold; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the flash freeze remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary tree.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The flash freeze makes three attacks, using Chill Slam or Ice Boulder in any combination.  
>
>***Chill Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Cold damage.  
>
>***Ice Boulder.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 60/180 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage plus 11 (2d10) Cold damage.  
>
>***Flash Freeze (Recharge 6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the flash freeze. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Cold damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* If this damage reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, it is Stable and has the Petrified condition for 1 minute, turning into a statue of ice.
