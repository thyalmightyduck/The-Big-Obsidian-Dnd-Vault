>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The wood weird uses its Alluring Spores, then makes a Slam attack.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. one target. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Alluring Spores.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11, one Humanoid the wood weird can sense within 10 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition. While Charmed, the target can't take Reactions and uses its turns to approach the wood weird, stopping when it is within 5 feet. The target avoids obvious danger, including Opportunity Attacks. If it can't avoid such danger, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It also repeats the save if it takes damage or at the end of each of the target's turns. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute or if the wood weird is destroyed.
