>- **Hit Points** 84 (8d12 + 32)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)

>- **Languages** Understands Infernal and Sylvan but can't speak

>***Siege Monster.*** The daemon oak thrall deals double damage to objects and structures.
