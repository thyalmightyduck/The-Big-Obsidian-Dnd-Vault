>## Wood Weird Trapper
>*Small Plant, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 5
>- **Hit Points** 13 (3d6 + 3)
>- **Speed** 0 ft.
>- **Initiative** -5 (5)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|1 (-5)|13 (+1)|1 (-5)|3 (-4)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened, Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 6
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While motionless, the wood weird is indistinguishable from a clump of beautiful wildflowers.  
>
>### Actions
>***Restful Spores.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10, one creature the wood weird can sense within 10 feet (wood weirds are immune). *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. While poisoned in the way, the target has the Prone condition and its Speed is 0 and cannot be increased. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the Poisoned condition on a success.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cocoon.*** A Medium or smaller Humanoid that is Prone within 10 feet of the wood weird must succeed on a DC 11 Strength or Dexterity saving throw. *Failure:* The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 11). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The wood weird can cocoon only one creature at a time.  
>At the start of each of the wood weird's turns, the target is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* 3 (1d6) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage. If the target dies while cocooned, it lignifies over 24 hours and then rises as a **Wood Weird Seeder**.
