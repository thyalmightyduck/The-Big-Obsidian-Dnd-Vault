>## Fungusfeaster
>*Small Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 8
>- **Hit Points** 19 (3d6 + 9)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Climb 10 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|1 (-5)|4 (-3)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +2
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The fungusfeaster can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.  
>
>***Fungiphage.*** The fungusfeaster's pseudopod attack deals an extra 4 (1d8) necrotic damage to fungi and fungus creatures such as **shriekers** and **violet fungi**. The fungusfeaster regains hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The fungusfeaster can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) acid damage.
