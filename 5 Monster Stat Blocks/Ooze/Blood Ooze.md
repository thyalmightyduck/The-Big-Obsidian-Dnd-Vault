___
>## Blood Ooze
>*Large Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 150 (20d10 + 40)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened, Exhaustion, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The blood ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Blood Scent.*** The range of the blood ooze's Blindsight is doubled for living creatures missing any of their Hit Points.  
>
>***Infusion.*** A creature engulfed by the blood ooze when it dies is instead infused with the blood ooze's essence, regaining 16 (3d10) Hit Points. It gains any excess healing as Temporary Hit Points, which last for 24 hours. If the target dies within 24 hours of this infusion, it dissolves into a blood ooze over the next hour. Magic that cures a disease or remove the curse prevents this change and removes any Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The blood ooze can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The blood ooze makes two Pseudopod attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Engulf.  
>
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The blood ooze can grapple two Medium or smaller creatures at a time.  
>
>***Engulf.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature Grappled by the blood ooze. *Failure:* The grapple ends and the target is pulled into the blood ooze's body. The target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, is suffocating, and has Total Cover from effects that originate outside the blood ooze. At the start of each of the blood ooze's turns, the target takes 18 (4d8) Acid damage. The blood ooze can have one creature engulfed at a time.  
>An engulfed creature can try to escape by taking an action to make its choice of a DC 14 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. On a success, the creature escapes and uses 5 ft. of movement to enter a space of its choice within 5 ft. of the ooze.  
>The target can escape the blood ooze by taking an action and succeeding on a DC 14 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. Escaping the ooze costs 5 feet of movement and ejects the target into a space of its choice within 5 feet of the blood ooze. If the ooze dies, the target no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape without using an action or making a check.  
>If the target dies while engulfed, its body dissolves and the blood ooze expels anything it was wearing or carrying.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Predatory Lurch.*** The blood ooze makes a Pseudopod attack against a creature missing any of its Hit Points, or takes the Dash action and moves toward such a creature.
