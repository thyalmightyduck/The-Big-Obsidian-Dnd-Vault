___
>## Formless Spawn
>*Large Ooze (Mythos), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 138 (12d10 + 72)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|15 (+2)|22 (+6)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Dex +6, Con +10, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Perception +5
>- **Immunities** Acid, Cold, Lightning, Slashing; Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common plus 1d4 other languages
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The formless spawn can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The formless spawn has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The formless spawn makes up to four attacks, using Bludgeoning Pseudopod or Grasping Tentacle in any combination. It can replace any number of attacks with uses of Crushing Grip or Throw Victim.  
>
>***Bludgeoning Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, Reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Crushing Grip.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 18, one creature Grappled by the formless spawn. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Bludgeoning damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Grasping Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, Reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The formless spawn casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Darkness*, *Locate Object*, *Silence*  
>
>***Throw Victim.*** The formless spawn throws a Medium or smaller creature Grappled by it to a space it can see within 30 feet of itself that isn't in the air. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18, the creature thrown and each creature in the destination space. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only
