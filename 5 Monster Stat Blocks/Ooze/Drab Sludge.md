>## Drab Sludge
>*Huge Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 7
>- **Hit Points** 64 (6d12 + 24)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -3 (7)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|5 (-3)|18 (+4)|1 (-5)|6 (-2)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Acid; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The drab sludge can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The drab sludge can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Stench.*** A creature that starts its turn within 20 feet of the drab sludge is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. While Poisoned in this way, the target is has the Stunned condition. *Success:* The target is immune to the drab sludge's Stench for 1 hour or until it uses Split, whichever comes first.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Acid damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Split.*** *Trigger:* The drab sludge becomes Bloodied. *Response:* The drab sludge splits into two new drab sludges. Each sludge has Hit Points equal to half the original sludge's, rounded down. New sludges are one size smaller than the original sludge.
