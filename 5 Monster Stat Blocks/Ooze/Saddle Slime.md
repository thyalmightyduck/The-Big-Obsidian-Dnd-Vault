___
>## Saddle Slime
>*Large Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 8
>- **Hit Points** 51 (6d10 + 18)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|5 (-3)|6 (-2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Acid; Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** Understands the languages its summoner speaks
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Born for the Saddle.*** The ooze grips firmly onto any object that is inserted into its body to be used as a saddle. A creature riding the ooze on one such saddle has advantage on saving throws made to avoid being dismounted.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The ooze can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) acid damage.  
>
>***Corrode Object (3/Day).*** The ooze touches a Medium or smaller nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried, causing it to corrode and dissolve over the course of 1 minute.
