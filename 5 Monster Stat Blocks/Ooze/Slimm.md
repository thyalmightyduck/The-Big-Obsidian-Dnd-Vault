___
>## Slimm
>*Medium Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|2 (-4)|9 (-1)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Lightning, Piercing, Slashing, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Acid; Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The slimm can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending additional movement.  
>
>***Corrosive Body.*** A creature that touches the slimm or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 5 (2d4) Acid damage.  
>
>***Humanoid Shape.*** While the slimm remains still, it can pass for Humanoid to anyone who fails a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check. If the slimm is in Bright Light, this check is made with Advantage.  
>
>***Nimble Ooze.*** The slimm can move through the space of any creature larger than the slimm. If the slimm moves through a creature's space, that creature must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw to avoid touching the slimm's Corrosive Body.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Bludgeoning damage plus 13 (3d8) Acid damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Explosive Split.*** *Trigger:* While the slimm is Medium and has 10+ Hit Points, and it is subjected to Lightning, Slashing, or Thunder damage or takes 10+ damage in a single turn. *Response:* *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the slimm. *Failure:* 7 (3d4) Acid damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* The slim splits into two new slimms, which appear at opposite edges of the Emanation. Each new slimm is one size smaller than the original slimm and acts on its Initiative. The original slimm's Hit Points are divided evenly between the new slimms (round down).  
>
>***Rejoin.*** *Trigger:* A Small slimm ends its turn within 5 feet of another Small slimm. *Response:* Both slimms use their Reactions to rejoin into a single Medium slimm in one of the two slimm's spaces. The original slimms' Hit Points are combined to determine the Hit Points of the new slimm.
