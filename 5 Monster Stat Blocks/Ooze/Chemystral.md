>## Chemystral
>*Large Ooze*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 168 (16d10 + 80)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|7 (-2)|20 (+5)|1 (-5)|6 (-2)|2 (-4)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Fire, Force, Lightning, Necrotic, Poison, Psychic, Radiant
>- **Immunities** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Acid.*** On a hit, the target has the Burning condition (4d6 Acid).  
>
>***Amorphous Form.*** The chemystral can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.  
>
>***Cold Vulnerability.*** If the chemystral takes Cold damage, it becomes Frozen (DC 15). While it has the Frozen condition, it loses its Immunities to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage and its Resistance to Force damage, and it gains Vulnerability to Thunder damage. In addition to the normal means, the Frozen condition ends if the chemystral takes 20 points of Force, Piercing, Thunder, or Slashing damage.  
>
>***Elemental Reaction.*** If the Chemystral takes Acid, Fire, Lightning, Poison, or Necrotic damage, it takes on elemental properties which change the damage type of its Chemical Spray and Engulf actions to match that damage type. In addition, it causes an additional effect when it hits with its Chemical Spray attack:  
>
>***Fire.*** On a hit, the target has the Burning condition (4d6 Fire).  
>
>***Lightning.*** On a hit, the target has the Shocked condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Necrotic.*** On a hit, the chemystral regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.  
>
>***Poison.*** On a hit, the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The chemystral regains 25 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the chemystral takes Cold or Thunder damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn. The chemystral dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Sonic Neutralization.*** When the chemystral takes Thunder damage, it loses any active benefit from its Elemental Reaction trait.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The chemystral makes two Chemical Spray attacks and uses Engulf.  
>
>***Chemical Spray.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 35 (10d6) Acid damage.  
>
>***Engulf.*** The chemystral moves up to its Speed. While doing so, it can enter Large or smaller creatures' spaces. Whenever the chemystral enters a creature's space, the creature is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Success:* The creature can choose to be pushed 5 feet back or to the side of the chemystral. A creature that chooses not to be pushed suffers the consequences of a failed saving throw.  
>*Failure:* The chemystral enters the creature's space, and the creature takes 35 (10d6) Acid damage and is engulfed. The engulfed creature can't breathe, has the Restrained condition, and takes 35 (10d6) Acid damage at the start of each of the chemystral's turns. The engulfed creature has Total Cover from effects outside the chemystral. When the chemystral moves, the engulfed creature moves with it.  
>The chemystral can hold only one Large creature or up to four Medium or smaller creatures inside it at a time. An engulfed creature can take an action to escape with a successful DC 17 Strength check, entering a space of its choice within 5 feet of the ooze.
