>## Coldfire Ooze
>*Large Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 8
>- **Hit Points** 110 (13d10 + 39)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|17 (+3)|1 (-5)|12 (+1)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Cold; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The coldfire ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Coldfire.*** Cold damage dealt by the coldfire ooze ignores Resistance, and treats Immunity as Resistance instead.  
>
>***Ignite.*** A creature or flammable object that takes Cold damage from the coldfire ooze ignites. Until the target takes Fire damage, it takes 3 (1d6) Cold damage at the start of each of its turns.  
>
>***Illumination.*** The coldfire ooze sheds Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The coldfire ooze can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) Cold damage.  
>
>***Coldfire Flare (Recharge 6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Cold damage. *Success:* Half damage.*Failure or Success:* If a significant amount of flammable material is in the Cone, the area ignites, dealing 3 (1d6) Cold damage to creatures and objects that enter the area for the first time on a turn or end their turn there. A target that takes Cold damage from the area is subjected to Ignite.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Split.*** *Trigger:* The coldfire ooze is Medium or Large, has 10+ Hit Points, and takes Cold and Slashing damage. *Response:* The coldfire ooze splits into two new coldfire oozes. Each new ooze has Hit Points equal to half the original ooze's, rounded down. New oozes are one size smaller than the original ooze.
