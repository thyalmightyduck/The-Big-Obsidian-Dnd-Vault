>## Sovereign Ooze Monarch
>*Huge Ooze, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 238 (19d12 + 114)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|24 (+7)|10 (+0)|22 (+6)|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +12
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Grappled, Incapacitated, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft., understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 17 (XP 18,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The ooze has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The ooze can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ooze makes three Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +13, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (2d10 + 7) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Force damage.  
>
>***Arcane Glob (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 30-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the ooze can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 45 (10d8) Force damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The ooze shape-shifts to resemble a Medium or smaller Humanoid or Beast while retaining its game statistics, or it returns to its true form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Transformation (1/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature the ooze can see within 5 feet that has 0 Hit Points. *Failure:* The target dies. After one minute, the target transforms into a Sovereign Ooze Vassal unless it is restored to life or the ooze is destroyed before a minute passes. Only a *Wish* spell can reverse this transformation.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the sovereign ooze monarch can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The sovereign ooze monarch regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Arcane Spray.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the ooze. *Failure:* 16 (3d10) Force damage. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The ooze can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Manipulate.** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature the ooze can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 26 (4d12) Psychic damage, and if able, the target takes a Reaction to move up to its Speed in a horizontal direction of the ooze's choice, following the safest route. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The ooze can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Strike.** The ooze moves up to its Speed and makes one Slam attack.
