>## Living Toxin
>*Small Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 44 (8d6 + 16)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|1 (-5)|10 (+0)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The living toxin can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Toxic Nature.*** When the living toxin or the host of its Toxic Invasion is subjected to an effect that ends the Poisoned condition, the living toxin takes 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage per level of the effect, if any.  
>
>### Actions
>***Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Poison damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). Until this grapple ends, the living toxin can't Touch another target.  
>
>***Toxic Invasion.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, a creature Grappled by the living toxin. *Failure:* The living toxin invades the target's body. While inside a host, the living toxin has Total Cover from effects that originate outside the host. The host has the Poisoned condition and takes 10 (2d6 + 3) Poison damage at the start of each of the living toxin's turns.  
>At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. The effect ends early if the host dies or is subjected to any effect that ends the Poisoned condition. When the effect ends, the living toxin leaves the host, landing in an unoccupied space within 5 feet.
