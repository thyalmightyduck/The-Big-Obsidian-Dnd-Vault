___
>## The Churning
>*Gargantuan Ooze, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 248 (16d20 + 80)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -4 (6)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|2 (-4)|20 (+5)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 16 (XP 15,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Enormous Space.*** The Churning takes up an entire 60-foot Cube. Other creatures can move through this space, but a creature that does so is subjected to Engulf.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The Churning has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The Churning deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Unstoppable.*** The Churning ignores Difficult Terrain, and nothing can reduce its Speed or stop it from moving on its turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 43 (6d10 + 10) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pulled into a space within 5 feet of the Churning.  
>
>***Engulf.*** The Churning moves up to its Speed and can move through the spaces of other creatures, subjecting each of them to the following effect. Strength or *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 23. *Failure:* 40 (10d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pulled into the Churning's body. The target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, is suffocating, and has Total Cover from effects that originate outside the Churning. At the start of each of the Churning's turns, the target takes 42 (12d6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>The target can escape the Churning by taking an action and succeeding on a DC 23 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. Escaping the Churning costs 15 feet of movement and ejects the target into a space of its choice within 5 feet of the Churning. If the chapped brute dies, the target no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape without using an action or making a check.  
>If the target dies while engulfed, its body breaks up and the Churning keeps anything it was wearing or carrying.  
>
>### Reactions
>The Churning can take up to four Reactions per round but only one per turn.
>
>***Grab.*** *Trigger:* A creature moves while within reach of the Churning's Pseudopod attack. *Response:* The Churning uses Pseudopod.
