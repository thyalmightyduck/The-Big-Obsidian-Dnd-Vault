___
>## Sovereign Ooze Vassal
>*Medium Ooze, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|5 (-3)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Grappled, Incapacitated, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Merge.*** The ooze and one other Sovereign Ooze Vassal within 5 feet of it merge over the course of a Short Rest. When both oozes finish the Short Rest, they merge into a Sovereign Ooze Lord.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The ooze can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ooze makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Arcane Glob (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 30-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the ooze can see within 90 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Force damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Damage Dispersal.*** *Trigger:* The ooze takes damage from a spell or magical effect. *Response:* The sovereign ooze halves the damage it takes (round down), and each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the ooze takes 10 (3d6) Force damage.
