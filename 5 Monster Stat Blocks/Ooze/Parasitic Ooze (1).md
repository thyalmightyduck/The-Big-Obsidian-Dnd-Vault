___
>## Parasitic Ooze
>*Tiny Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 8 + PB (natural armour)
>- **Hit Points** 5 + three times your warlock level
>- **Speed** 5 ft. +5 x PB, Climb 5 ft. +5 x PB
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex -2 + PB, Con +3 + PB
>- **Skills** Stealth -2 + 2 × PB
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands the languages you speak
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB +PB)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +PB
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the ooze remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an oily pool or wet rock.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The ooze can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  your spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d6 + PB bludgeoning damage
