>## Magiplasm
>*Medium Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7, Wis +5
>- **Immunities** Acid; Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Grappled, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands commands given in any language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The magiplasm can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The magiplasm can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The magiplasm makes two Slam attacks and uses Smother.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) Acid damage.  
>
>***Smother.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17, one Medium or smaller creature within 5 feet. *Failure:* The target takes 22 (5d8) Acid damage and has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17). Until the grapple ends, the target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, is suffocating, and takes 9 (1d8 + 5) Acid damage at the start of each of its turns. The magiplasm can only smother one creature at a time.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Transfer Magic.*** *Trigger:* The magiplasm is smothering a creature and takes damage from a spell of level 5 or lower or a magical effect. *Response:* The engulfed target takes the same amount of damage and the magiplasm reduces the damage it takes from the triggering spell or magical effect to 0.
