>## Magebane Ooze
>*Large Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 7
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d10 + 42)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -3 (7)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|5 (-3)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +2, Stealth +0
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Radiant, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Force; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.  
>
>***Corrode Magic.*** Any magical weapon that hits the ooze has some of its magic stripped away. After dealing damage, the weapon takes a cumulative −1 penalty to attack and damage rolls. If the penalty reaches −3, the weapon permanently becomes a mundane version of that weapon type. A weapon affected by this magical draining reduces the penalty by 1 every 24 hours. Magical ammunition that hits the ooze becomes mundane immediately after dealing damage.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the ooze remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an oily pool or wet rock.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 36 (8d8) Force damage. If the target is wearing magical armor, the armor is stripped of some power and takes a cumulative −1 penalty to the AC it offers. If the penalty reaches −3, the armor permanently becomes a mundane version of that armor type. Armor affected by this magical draining reduces the penalty by 1 every 24 hours.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Feed.*** *Trigger:* A creature deals damage to the ooze with a spell. *Response:* The ooze reduces the damage dealt by that attack to 0, and instead regains Hit Points equal to the amount of damage it would've taken.
