>## Sovereign Ooze Lord
>*Large Ooze, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d10 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Grappled, Incapacitated, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft., understands Common but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The ooze has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Merge.*** The ooze and one other Sovereign Ooze Lord within 5 feet of it merge over the course of a Short Rest. When both oozes finish the Short Rest, they merge into a Sovereign Ooze Monarch.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The ooze can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ooze makes three Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Force damage.  
>
>***Arcane Glob (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the ooze can see within 90 feet. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Force damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The ooze shape-shifts to resemble a Medium or smaller Humanoid or Beast while retaining its game statistics, or it returns to its true form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Transformation (1/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature the ooze can see within 5 feet that has 0 Hit Points. *Failure:* The target dies. After one minute, the target transforms into a Sovereign Ooze Vassal, unless it is restored to life or the ooze is destroyed before a minute passes. Only a *Wish* spell can reverse this transformation.
