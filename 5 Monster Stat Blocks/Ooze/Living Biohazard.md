___
>## Living Biohazard
>*Large Ooze*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d10 + 16)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|4 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Grappled
>- **Senses** Blindsight 20 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Hazardous Fumes.*** A creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the ooze has the Burning condition (4d4 Acid). While Burning, the creature also has the Slowed condition. While the creature remains within 10 feet of the ooze, the Burning condition cannot be removed in any way.  
>
>***Liquid Form.*** The ooze can squeeze through a space as narrow as 1 inch, and can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. The first time it enters a creature's space on a turn, that creature takes 10 (4d4) Acid damage. The ooze has Advantage on ability checks it makes to initiate or escape a grapple.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The ooze can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ooze makes two Pseudopod attacks.  
>
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 10 (4d4) Acid damage.
