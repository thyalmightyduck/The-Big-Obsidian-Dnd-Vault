___
>## Urban Ooze
>*Medium Ooze, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 8
>- **Hit Points** 30 (4d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft., Swim 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|1 (-5)|6 (-2)|1 (-5)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Amphibious.*** The ooze can breathe air and water.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the ooze remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a mass of waste.  
>
>***Infection.*** A non-Ooze creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the ooze or touches it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. *Failure by 5 or More:* The target also contracts Sewer Plague. *Success:* The target is immune to the Infection of all urban oozes for 1 hour and to Sewer Plague for 24 hours.  
>
>### Actions
>***Pseudopod.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage, and the target is subjected to Infection.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Diminish.*** *Trigger:* The ooze becomes Bloodied. *Response:* The ooze splits into two **Diminished Urban Oozes**. Each new ooze acts on the original ooze's Initiative. The original ooze's Hit Points are divided evenly between the new oozes (round down).
