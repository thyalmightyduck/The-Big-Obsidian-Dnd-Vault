>## Zabrielite Truthseeker
>*Large Celestial, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 126 (12d10 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|23 (+6)|20 (+5)|19 (+4)|23 (+6)|22 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Wis +9, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Insight +9, Investigation +7, Perception +9
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison, Radiant; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Inscrutable.*** The truthseeker has Immunity to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any Divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to determine the truthseeker's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The Zabrielite truthseeker has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The truthseeker makes two attacks, using Talons or Eye Beams in any combination.  
>
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. Hit 22 (3d10 + 6) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Eye Beams.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +9, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 27 (5d10) Radiant damage. This attack ignores all but Total Cover, and the truthseeker doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack roll while adjacent to an enemy.  
>
>***Transfixing Gaze.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature the truthseeker can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Paralyzed condition. While Paralyzed, the truthseeker can read the target's mind to learn its surface thoughts and gain insight into its motivations, emotional state, and major concerns. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.  
>The truthseeker must maintain Concentration on this effect or it immediately ends. While maintaining Concentration on Transfixing Gaze, the truthseeker can't make Eye Beams attacks.
