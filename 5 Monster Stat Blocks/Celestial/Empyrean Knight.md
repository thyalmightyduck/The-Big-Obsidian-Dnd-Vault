>## Empyrean Knight
>*Medium Celestial, Lawful Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d8 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Perception +6, Survival +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Radiant; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Longsword*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Celestial, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Flyby.*** The knight doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The knight has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Only Forward.*** The knight is unable to flee from battle until all its enemies are destroyed or it is.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The knight makes two Longsword attacks.  
>
>***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Force damage plus 18 (4d8) Radiant damage.
