___
>## Lesser Avarice Seraph
>*Medium Celestial, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 105 (14d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 90 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Wis +5, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Insight +5, Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Radiant
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The seraph has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The seraph makes three Scimitar attacks.  
>
>***Scimitar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Force damage plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The seraph casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material or Somatic components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Detect Evil and Good*, *Detect Magic*, *Light*, *Minor Illusion*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>1/day each: *Identify*, *Suggestion*  
>
>***Tempting Call.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 30 feet that can hear the seraph. *Failure:* The target can't take actions. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats its save, ending the effect on itself on a success but taking 9 (2d8) Psychic damage on a failure. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.
