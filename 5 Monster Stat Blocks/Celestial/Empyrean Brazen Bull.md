>## Empyrean Brazen Bull
>*Huge Celestial, Lawful Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 162 (12d12 + 84)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|26 (+8)|10 (+0)|25 (+7)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|21 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +12, Con +11, Wis +7, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Athletics +12, Perception +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Fire, Necrotic, Poison, Radiant; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Celestial
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Death Throes.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the brazen bull when it dies. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Fire damage plus 14 (4d6) Radiant damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The Empyrean brazen bull deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Soulfire Nimbus.*** At the start of each of the brazen bull's turns, each creature within 5 feet of it takes 10 (3d6) Radiant damage. A creature that touches the brazen bull or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 10 (3d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The brazen bull makes two attacks, using Bite or Hooves in any combination. It can replace one attack with Gore.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 24 (3d10 + 8) Bludgeoning damage plus 18 (4d8) Radiant damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 20). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the brazen bull can't Bite another target.  
>
>***Gore.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 27 (3d12 + 8) Bludgeoning damage plus 18 (4d8) Radiant damage. If the brazen bull moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition and the brazen bull can make a Bite or Hooves attack against it as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 5 feet. *Hit:* 24 (3d10 + 8) Bludgeoning damage plus 18 (4d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Swallow.*** The brazen bull makes one Bite attack against a Medium or smaller creature it has Grappled with its Bite. *Hit:* The target takes the Bite attack's damage, no longer has the Grappled condition, and is swallowed. While swallowed, the target has the Blinded and Restrained condition and Total Cover from effects that originate outside the brazen bull. It takes 14 (4d6) Fire damage at the start of each of the brazen bull's turns.  
>If the brazen bull takes 30 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the brazen bull must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate the swallowed creature, which lands in a space within 10 feet of the brazen bull and has the Prone condition. If the brazen bull dies, the swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape the corpse using 15 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.
