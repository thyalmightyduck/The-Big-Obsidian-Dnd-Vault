>## Angel of Vengeance
>*Large Celestial, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 130 (20d10 + 20)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +9
>- **Immunities** Radiant
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The angel has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The angel makes three Ring attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Unblinking Eye.  
>
>***Ring.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) from one of five rings, and while Grappled, it has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***Cone of Retribution (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each enemy in a 90-foot Cone. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Radiant damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The angel casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Shapechange* (Humanoid form only, no Temporary Hit Points gained from the spell, and no Concentration or Temporary Hit Points required to maintain the spell), *Locate Creature*  
>1/day: *Plane Shift*  
>
>***Unblinking Eye.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature Grappled by the angel. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Psychic damage. *Success:* Half damage.
