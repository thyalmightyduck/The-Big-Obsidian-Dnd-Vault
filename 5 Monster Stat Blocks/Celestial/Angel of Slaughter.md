>## Angel of Slaughter
>*Huge Celestial, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 21
>- **Hit Points** 475 (38d12 + 228)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Fly 50 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +14 (24)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|28 (+9)|22 (+6)|22 (+6)|12 (+1)|22 (+6)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +17, Wis +14
>- **Skills** Athletics +17, Perception +14
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Radiant; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Stunned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 24
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 120
>- **Challenge** 25 (XP 75,000; PB +8)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +8
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If the angel fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The angel has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The angel makes two Engulfing Slam attacks and uses Fling.  
>
>***Engulfing Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +17, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 20 (2d10 + 9) Slashing damage plus 13 (2d12) Radiant damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it is pulled into the angel's space and has the Grappled condition (escape DC 19). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. When the angel moves, the Grappled target moves with it, costing it no extra movement. The angel can have up to three creatures Grappled by this action at a time.  
>
>***Fling.*** The angel throws a Large or smaller creature Grappled by it to a space the angel can see within 30 feet of itself that isn't in the air. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 25, the creature thrown and each creature in the destination space. *Failure:* 17 (5d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The angel casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 22):  
>
>At will: *Shapechange* (Beast or Humanoid form only, no Temporary Hit Points gained from the spell, and no Concentration or Temporary Hit Points required to maintain the spell)  
>1/day each: *Control Weather*, *Plane Shift*  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, the angel of slaughter can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The angel of slaughter regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Fling.** The angel uses Fling. The angel can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Spin and Slam.** The angel moves up to half its Speed, and it makes one Engulfing Slam attack.
>- **Whirlwind of Death.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the angel. *Failure:* 33 (6d10) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the end of the angel's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The angel can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing an angel of slaughter's lair is affected by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Looming Shadows.** Any creature (excluding the angel and its allies) that finishes a Short Rest while within 1 mile of the lair must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or gain no benefit from that rest.
>- **Screams of the Damned.** The tormented souls whom the angel brings back to its lair wail endlessly, reliving the trauma of their deaths. Creatures can't gain or expend Heroic Inspiration while within 1 mile of the lair.
