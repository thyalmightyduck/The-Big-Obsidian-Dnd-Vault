___
>## Goat-Knight Steed
>*Large Celestial, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 19 (3d10 + 3)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands Dwarvish but can't speak it
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Charge.*** If the goat-knight steed moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 5 (2d4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.  
>
>***Shared Spells.*** Any spell the **goat-knight** casts with a range of self also targets the goat-knight steed   
>
>***Summoned Nature.*** When a goat-knight steed drops to 0 hit points, it disappears, leaving behind no physical form. The **goat-knight** can also dismiss the steed at any time as an action, causing it to disappear. In either case, the same steed is summoned when the **goat-knight** casts *find steed*.  
>
>***Sure-Footed.*** The goat-knight steed has advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made against effects that would knock it prone.  
>
>### Actions
>***Ram.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
