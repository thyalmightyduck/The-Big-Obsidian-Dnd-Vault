>## Angel of Empyreus
>*Large Celestial, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 21
>- **Hit Points** 445 (33d10 + 264)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 90 ft.
>- **Initiative** +13 (23)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|29 (+9)|23 (+6)|26 (+8)|25 (+7)|18 (+4)|30 (+10)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +16, Int +14, Wis +11, Cha +17
>- **Skills** Arcana +14, Insight +11, Intimidation +17, Investigation +14, Perception +11, Religion +14
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Lightning, Radiant
>- **Immunities** Cold, Necrotic, Poison; Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Holy Avenger*
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 22 (XP 41,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Celestial Arsenal.*** The angel of Empyreus's weapon attacks deal an extra 27 (5d10) Radiant damage (included in the attack).  
>
>***Divine Awareness.*** The Angel of Empyreus knows if it hears a lie.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If the angel of Empyreus fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The angel of Empyreus makes three *Holy Avenger* attacks. It can replace any attack with a use of Authoritative Mien.  
>
>***Holy Avenger.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +19 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 12) Slashing damage plus 27 (5d10) Radiant damage, or 49 (9d10) Radiant damage if the target is a Fiend or Undead.  
>
>***Authoritative Mien.*** The angel of Empyreus casts *Command* (spell save DC 25).  
>
>***Blazing Radiance (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Fire damage plus 35 (10d6) Radiant damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Holy Aura.*** The angel of Empyreus projects a 60-foot Emanation of holiness with one of the effects listed below. The angel of Empyreus chooses which creatures in the area are affected. A target is affected until it leaves the area, dies, or the angel of Empyreus chooses a new effect. Only one effect can be active at a time.  
>- **Judgement.** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature that starts its turn in the area. *Failure:* The target is overwhelmed by guilt, doubt, remorse, and self-criticism. The target suffers a level of Exhaustion. *Success:* The target is immune to this effect for 1 hour.
>- **Valor.** Each creature that starts its turn in the area gains 15 Temporary Hit Points, can't have the Charmed or Frightened condition, and has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the angel of empyreus can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The angel of empyreus regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Fly.** The angel of Empyreus flies up to half its Fly Speed.
>- **Sword.** The angel of Empyreus makes a *Holy Avenger* attack.
>- **Hurl Sword.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 24, each creature in a 30-foot long, 5-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 23 (2d6 + 9) Slashing damage plus 27 (5d10) Radiant damage, or 49 (9d10) Radiant damage if the target is a Fiend or Undead. *Failure or Success:* The angel of Empyreus can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Recharge.** The angel of Empyreus recharges its Blazing Radiance.
