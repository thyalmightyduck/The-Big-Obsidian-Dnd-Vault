>## Hamingja
>*Small Celestial, Neutral Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 21 (6d6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 20 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Cha +4
>- **Skills** History +3, Insight +5, Perception +5, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Poison, Radiant; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Celestial, Telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The hamingja has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Radiant Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The hamingja shape-shifts to resemble a Medium or smaller Beast or Humanoid. Its game statistics are the same in each form, except its Fly Speed is only available in its true form or a form possessing wings. Any equipment it's wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The hamingja casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Invisibility* (self only), *Light*, *Purify Food and Drink*  
>1/day each: *Cure Wounds*, *Zone of Truth*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Shot of Luck (3/day).*** *Trigger:* A creature the hamingja is bonded to fails an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. *Response:* The creature adds 1d6 to its die roll, potentially turning the failure into a success.
