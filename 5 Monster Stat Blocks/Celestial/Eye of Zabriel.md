>## Eye of Zabriel
>*Tiny Celestial, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 28 (8d4 + 8)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6
>- **Skills** Insight +5, Investigation +5, Perception +7, Stealth +6
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Exhaustion, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Ever Observant.*** This celestial has reduced itself to individual floating eyes that never stop watching. It cannot be surprised.  
>
>### Actions
>***Flash of Light.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the eye. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Radiant damage, and the target has the Blinded condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it automatically succeeds. *Failure or Success:* The eye has the Invisible condition and can teleport to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet.
