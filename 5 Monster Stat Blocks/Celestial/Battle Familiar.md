___
>## Battle Familiar
>*Medium Celestial, Fey, or Fiend; Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11 + the spell's level + 2 (Brute only)
>- **Hit Points** 30 (Brute only) or 20 (Flyer or Stalker only) + 5 for each spell level above 2
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover; Flyer only), Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands the languages you know
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals your Proficiency Bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals your Proficiency Bonus
>___
>***Talented.*** Add half the spell's level (round down) to any ability check or saving throw the familiar makes.  
>
>***Flyby (Flyer Only).*** The familiar doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The familiar makes a number of Rend attacks equal to half this spell's level (round down). The familiar can replace one of these attacks with Prowl if available.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d8 + 3 + summonSpellLevel Force damage.  
>
>***Prowl (Stalker Only).*** The familiar moves up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. At the end of this movement, the familiar can take the Hide action.
