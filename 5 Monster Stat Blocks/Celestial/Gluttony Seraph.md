___
>## Gluttony Seraph
>*Huge Celestial, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 218 (19d12 + 95)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|12 (+1)|20 (+5)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +10, Wis +7, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Insight +7, Perception +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Radiant
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The seraph has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The seraph makes three attacks using Claw or Tongue in any combination. It can replace one attack with Bite.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 24 (3d12 + 5) Force damage plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Force damage plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The seraph casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material or Somatic components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):  
>
>At will: *Detect Evil and Good*, *Detect Magic*, *Light*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>3/day: *Suggestion*  
>1/day: *Create Food and Water* (the food is tasty and the seraph can create wine instead of water)  
>
>***Tongue.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 20 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 5) Force damage plus 9 (2d8) Radiant damage. The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18) and is pulled into a space within 5 feet of the seraph. While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the seraph can't make Tongue attacks or Bite another target.  
>
>***Swallow.*** The seraph makes one Bite attack against a Large or smaller creature it has Grappled. *Hit:* The target takes the Bite attack's damage, no longer has the Grappled condition, and is swallowed. While swallowed, the target has the Blinded and Restrained condition and Total Cover from effects that originate outside the seraph. It takes 18 (4d8) Acid damage and 9 (2d8) Radiant damage at the start of each of the seraph's turns. The seraph can only have one creature swallowed at a time.  
>If the seraph takes 35 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the seraph must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate the swallowed creature, which lands in a space within 10 feet of the seraph and has the Prone condition. If the seraph dies, the swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape the corpse using 10 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.
