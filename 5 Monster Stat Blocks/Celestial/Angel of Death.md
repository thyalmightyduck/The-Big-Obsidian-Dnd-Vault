>## Angel of Death
>*Medium Celestial, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 187 (25d8 + 75)
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +11 (21)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|22 (+6)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +11, Wis +10
>- **Skills** Insight +10, Perception +10
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Radiant; Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The angel has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The angel makes three Soul Scythe attacks.  
>
>***Soul Scythe.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Slashing damage plus 13 (3d8) Necrotic or Radiant damage (angel's choice). If the target is reduced to 0 Hit Points by this attack, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* The target dies, and its soul is trapped in the angel's scythe. The target can't be raised from the dead until the angel is destroyed, releasing all souls trapped in the scythe.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The angel casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18).  
>
>At will: *Disguise Self* (24-hour duration), *Locate Creature*  
>1/day each: *Circle of Death*, *Plane Shift*, *Power Word Kill*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step.*** The angel casts *Misty Step*, requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
