___
>## Sunwraith
>*Large Celestial, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 211 (20d10 + 100)
>- **Speed** 60 ft., Fly 120 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +7, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Perception +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Radiant, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Celestial, Infernal, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The sunwraith has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The sunwraith makes two Greatsword attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Life Burn it available.  
>
>***Greatsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Force damage plus 18 (1d4) Necrotic or Radiant damage (sunwraith's choice).  
>
>***Life Burn (Recharge 6).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft *Hit:* 30 (6d8 + 3) Necrotic or Radiant damage (sunwraith's choice). The target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. A Humanoid killed by this effect rises as a specter at the start of the sunwraith's next turn. The specter is under the sunwraith's direct control. The sunwraith can control seven specters at a time.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Blinding Corona.*** *Trigger:* A creature that can see the sunwraith harms it with an attack or effect from more than 5 feet away. *Response:*—*Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it automatically succeeds.
