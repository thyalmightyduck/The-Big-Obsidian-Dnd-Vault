>## Skein Witch
>*Medium Celestial, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 162 (25d8 +50)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +7, Wis +9, Cha +9
>- **Skills** History +7, Insight +13, Perception +13
>- **Damage Resistances** Radiant
>- **Immunities** Fire, Lightning, Psychic
>- **Senses** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 23
>- **Languages** Celestial, Telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Fate's Resistance (3/Day).*** If the skein witch fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The skein witch has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skein witch makes three Inexorable Thread attacks.  
>
>***Inexorable Thread.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 27 (5d8 + 5) Radiant damage. If the skein witch hits with this attack, it can teleport, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.  
>
>***Destiny Distortion Wave (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17 Each creature of its choice in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 55 Force damage, and the creature has Disadvantage on D20 Tests for 1 minute. A creature can make a save at the end of each of its turns to end this effect. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Bind Fates (1/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17 One creature the skein witch can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target's fate is bound to that of one of its allies of the witch's choice. Any damage or condition the target suffers is inflicted on the ally to which it is bound instead, and vice versa. A creature can be bound to only one other creature at a time. This binding lasts until lifted by a *Greater Restoration*, *Heal*, or *Remove Curse* spell or similar magic.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cutting the Thread.*** The skein witch chooses a creature within 30 feet of it that it can see and that is at 0 Hit Points. If the creature was Stable, it is no longer Stable. In addition, it must make an immediate Death Saving Throw.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry Spell.*** *Trigger:* The skein witch succeeds on a saving throw against a spell of 4th level or lower that targets only the skein witch. *Response:* The spell has no effect. If the skein witch succeeds on the saving throw by 5 or more, the spell is reflected back at the spellcaster, using the slot level, spell save DC, attack bonus, and spellcasting ability of the caster.
