___
>## Avarice Seraph
>*Large Celestial, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 199 (19d10 + 95)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 120 ft.
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +10, Wis +8, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Deception +10, Insight +8, Perception +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Radiant
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Gear** *Scimitar*
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The seraph has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The seraph makes three Scimitar attacks.  
>
>***Scimitar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Force damage, plus 18 (4d8) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The seraph shape-shifts into a Small or Medium Humanoid or a Large Giant, or reverts to its true form. Other than its size, its game statistics are the same in each form. Objects worn and carried by the seraph change size to match its new form until relinquished or the seraph dies.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The seraph casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material or Somatic components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18):  
>
>At will: *Detect Evil and Good*, *Detect Magic*, *Invisibility* (self only), *Light*, *Minor Illusion*, *Thaumaturgy*  
>3/day each: *Identify*, *Suggestion*  
>1/day: *Zone of Truth*  
>
>***Voice of Avarice.*** The seraph presents objects of desire, which can be illusions the seraph creates, to creatures of its choice. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature within 60 feet of the seraph's choice that can see and hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition until the seraph has the Incapacitated condition or fails to extend the Charmed condition as a Bonus Action.  
>While Charmed, the target can't take Reactions and can only take the Dash action to approach the seraph by the safest route.  
>Once within 5 feet of the seraph, it spends its turns staring at the desirable objects. If approaching the seraph would endanger the target, it can repeat the save before doing so. The target also repeat its save if subjected to a harmful effect, if it can't see or hear the seraph, and at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. A target that succeeds on its save is immune to the Voice of Avarice of any avarice seraph for 24 hours.
