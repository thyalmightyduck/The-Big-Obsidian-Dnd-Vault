>## The Bloodied
>*Medium Celestial, Neutral Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 10
>- **Hit Points** 77 (14d8 + 14)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|6 (-2)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|19 (+4)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +6, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Insight +6, Perception +6
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Radiant; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Celestial, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Peacemaking.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 14, up to four Hostile creatures the bloodied can see. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition for 1 minute. While Charmed, the target has the Incapacitated condition and weeps uncontrollably until another creature takes an action to shake it out of its trance.  
>
>***Shared Sacrifice.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature within 60 feet that the bloodied can see. *Failure:* Until the end of the bloodied next turn, the bloodied has Resistance to all damage, and whenever the bloodied takes damage, the target takes the same amount.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Ultimate End.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 60 feet of the bloodied targets it with an attack. *Response:*—*Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target takes Force damage equal to the bloodied's remaining Hit Points, and the bloodied dies. *Success:* Half damage, and the damage can't reduce the target to 0 Hit Points. If the creature would be reduced to 0 Hit Points, it is reduced to 1 Hit Point instead.
