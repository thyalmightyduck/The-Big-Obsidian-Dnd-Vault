___
>## Prismatic Drake
>*Large Dragon, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 136 (16d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Deception +5, Intimidation +5, Perception +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Draconic and one other language
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The prismatic drake has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The prismatic drake makes one bite attack and one attack with its claws.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) slashing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The prismatic drake casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Color Spray*, *Mage Hand*, *Mending*, *Message*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Blur*, *Chromatic Orb* (level 2 version), *Detect Thoughts*, *Gust of Wind*  
>1/day each: *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Confusion*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Quicken Spell (4/Day).*** The prismatic drake uses Spellcasting as a Bonus Action instead of an action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Magic (3/Day).*** The prismatic drake casts *Counterspell* or *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
