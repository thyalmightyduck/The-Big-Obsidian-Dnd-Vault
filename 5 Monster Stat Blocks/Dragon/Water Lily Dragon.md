___
>## Water Lily Dragon
>*Huge Dragon, typically Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 73 (7d12 + 28)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +2, Con +6, Wis +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire, Cold
>- **Immunities** Prone
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands Draconic and one other language but can't speak them
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Aquatic Advantage.*** The dragon has Advantage on attack rolls when it is in the water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (1d10 + 6) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Flipper.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Wave of Defense (2/Day).*** *Trigger:* When an attack is made against a creature on the dragon's back. *Response:* The dragon can use its reaction to smack its front flippers against the water's surface, imposing Disadvantage on the roll. *Hit or Miss:*  The attacker must then succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be pushed 15 feet away from the dragon.
