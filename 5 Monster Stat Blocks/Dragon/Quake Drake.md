___
>## Quake Drake
>*Large Dragon, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 153 (18d10 + 54)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|4 (-3)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Perception +6, Stealth +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Understands Draconic but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The quake drake makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage. If the quake drake burrows 10+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 14 (4d6) Thunder damage and is caught in rubble. The target has the Restrained condition until a creature takes an action to free it with a successful DC 16 Strength (Athletics) check.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Stunned condition until the start of the quake drake's next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Tremor Aura.*** The quake drake emits a 20-foot Emanation that shakes the earth. Each creature that starts its turn in the Emanation is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16 (quake drakes automatically succeed). *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.
