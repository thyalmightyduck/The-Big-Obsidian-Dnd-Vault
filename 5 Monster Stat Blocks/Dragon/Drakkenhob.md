___
>## Drakkenhob
>*Large Dragon, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 136 (16d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6
>- **Skills** Deception +4, Perception +7, Stealth +6 resistances fire
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Irresistible Payment.*** As an Influence action, a creature that offers the drakkenhob alcohol or any amount of gold and succeeds on a DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion) check forces the drakkenhob to accept the offer and immediately take a Reaction to move up to half its Speed to get away from the creature by the safest route possible.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The drakkenhob makes two Rend attacks.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Piercing damage plus 11 (2d10) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Terrifying Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage, and the target drops whatever it is holding and has the Frightened condition. A Frightened target takes the Dash action and moves away from the drakkenhob by the safest route on each of its turns unless there's nowhere to move. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Call the Hounds.*** The drakkenhob lets out a wolf-like howl and magically summons three hunting hounds (use the Wolf stat block) that appear in unoccupied spaces within 60 feet of it. The hounds take their turn immediately after the drakkenhob on the same Initiative count, and they obey the drakkenhob's mental commands. The hounds vanish when they or the drakkenhob drop to 0 Hit Points, or when the drakkenhob uses this Bonus Action again.
