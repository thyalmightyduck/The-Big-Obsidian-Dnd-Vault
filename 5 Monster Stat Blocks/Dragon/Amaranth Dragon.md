___
>## Amaranth Dragon
>*Medium Dragon, Any Neutral Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Con +4
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands any one language but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Floral Appearance.*** While the dragon remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary growth of amaranth. It has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in areas covered in similar plants.  
>
>***Keen Senses.*** The dragon has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes two melee attacks.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.
