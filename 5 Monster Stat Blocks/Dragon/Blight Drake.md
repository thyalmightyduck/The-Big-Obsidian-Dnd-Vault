>## Blight Drake
>*Medium Dragon, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|5 (-3)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands Draconic but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Hearing.*** The blight drake has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Slashing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* 3 (1d6) Poison damage and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the blight drake's next turn.
