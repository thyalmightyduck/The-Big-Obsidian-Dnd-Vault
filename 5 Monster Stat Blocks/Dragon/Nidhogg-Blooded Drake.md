___
>## Nidhogg-Blooded Drake
>*Medium Dragon, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 51 (6d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 20 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +6, Int +6
>- **Skills** Intimidation +6, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 20 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, the Northern Tongue, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Ancient Imprisonment.*** Rage and terror at Nidhogg's imprisonment by Wotan reverberates down the generations within his brood. When rolling saves against effects that cause the Restrained or Grappled condition, as well as any that would entrap it, the Nidhogg spawn drake has Disadvantage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The drake makes one Bite and one Claw attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the start of the drake's next turn.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Venom Spit (Recharge 4–6).*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (4d6 + 2) Poison damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Earth Wyrm.*** The drake burrows 20 feet.
