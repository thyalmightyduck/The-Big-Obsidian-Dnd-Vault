>## Vulmungoth, Doom of the Mountain
>*Gargantuan Dragon, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 22 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 546 (28d20 + 252)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Fly 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|10 (+0)|29 (+9)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|23 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Con +16, Wis +9, Cha +13
>- **Skills** Perception +16, Stealth +7
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 26
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 24 (XP 62,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Vulmungoth fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Vulmungoth can use its Frightful Presence. It then makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +17 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (2d10 + 10) piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) fire damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +17 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (2d6 + 10) slashing damage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +17 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (2d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Frightful Presence.*** Each creature of Vulmungoth's choice that is within 120 feet of Vulmungoth and aware of it must succeed on a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to Vulmungoth's Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.  
>
>***Fire Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** Vulmungoth exhales fire in a 90-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 91 (26d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Vulmungoth can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Vulmungoth regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Detect.** Vulmungoth makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
>- **Tail Attack.** Vulmungoth makes a tail attack.
>- **Wing Attack (Costs 2 Actions).** Vulmungoth beats its wings. Each creature within 15 feet of Vulmungoth must succeed on a DC 25 Dexterity saving throw or take 17 (2d6 + 10) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. Vulmungoth can then fly up to half its flying speed.
