___
>## Morbus Kobold
>*Small Dragon, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 5 (1d6 + 2)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|9 (-1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +3
>- **Skills** Medicine +1, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Sling*, *Pouch*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** Draconic and one other language
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Carrier.*** The morbus kobold can contract a disease but has Immunity to its effects, other than cosmetic symptoms. It has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.  
>
>***Infection.*** A creature that touches the morbus kobold or anything it has touched in the past 24 hours is exposed to a disease, usually Sewer Plague.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** The morbus kobold has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the morbus kobold has Disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.  
>
>### Actions
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage, and the target is subjected to Infection  
>
>***Sling.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is subjected to Infection.  
>
>***Gore Bag.*** The morbus kobold throws its sack of diseased gore at a point it can see within 20 feet. Each creature in a 5-foot radius Sphere of the bag when it bursts is subjected to Infection. A morbus kobold usually only carries one such bag at a time.
