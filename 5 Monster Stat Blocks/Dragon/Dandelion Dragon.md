___
>## Dandelion Dragon
>*Tiny Dragon, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 31 (9d4 + 9)
>- **Speed** 15 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|3 (-4)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|6 (-2)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +2, Con +1, Int -2, Wis +0, Cha +1
>- **Skills** Perception +2, Stealth +4
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands Common and Draconic but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Temporal Sensitivity.*** The dragon has Advantage on Initiative rolls and can choose to reroll its Initiative at the start of each round. If it rerolls, it must use the new result.  
>
>***Time Acceleration (1/Day).*** The dragon takes an additional action on its turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes two bite attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage, and the target suffers an itchy rash for the next 1d4 hours. While afflicted in this way, the target has Disadvantage on Dexterity checks and saving throws. A successful DC 11 Wisdom check with an herbalism kit ends the effect.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Interplanar Jaunt.*** The dragon magically shifts from the Material Plane to another plane of existence of its choice, or vice versa.
