___
>## Wellwyrm
>*Large Dragon, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 153 (18d10 + 54)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 40 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|5 (-3)|14 (+2)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Perception +6, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The wellwyrm can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Hunting Terror.*** If the wellwyrm hears a creature play a horn or similar Musical Instrument, it has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The wellwyrm makes one Bite attack and uses Constrict.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Piercing damage plus 21 (6d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Constrict.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16, one Medium or smaller creature the wellwyrm can see within 5 feet. *Failure:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends. The wellwyrm needn't spend extra movement to move during this grapple.
