___
>## Blightscale Dragon Wyrmling
>*Medium Dragon (Cursed), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +3, Wis +2, Cha +1
>- **Skills** Insight +2, Perception +4, Stealth +3
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Draconic
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Accursed Death.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:*: DC 12, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the dragon when it dies. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats its save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>However, a target that fails two saves is cursed. While cursed, the target can't regain Hit Points. Upon being cursed and every 24 hours thereafter, the target takes 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage, can't regain Hit Points, and its Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken. If this effect reduces the target's Hit Point maximum to 0, it dies.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three Rend attacks.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 +3) Force damage plus 2 (1d4) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Swarm Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage.
