___
>## Fafnir
>*Huge Dragon, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 151 (14d12 + 60)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6
>- **Skills** Deception +3, Perception +5, Persuasion +3
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic, Dwarven, the Northern Tongue
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400, or 10,000 in lair; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Armor of Greed (3/Day).*** When Fafnir is hit by attack, he can choose for the attack to miss instead. If the attack is a Critical Hit, he must spend two uses of this trait to cause it to miss. Each time Fafnir uses this ability, creatures have Advantage on attack rolls against him until the end of his next turn.  
>
>***Aura of Avarice.*** Creatures within 1 mile of Fafnir's lair have Disadvantage on saves against spells and effects that compel them to take or steal things, and they feel deeply protective of their own belongings and covetous of the belongings of others.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Lair).*** If Fafnir fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three attacks using Bite or Constrict in any combination.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Piercing damage plus 11 (2d10) Poison damage.  
>
>***Constrict.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 18 One Huge or smaller creature Fafnir can see within 10 feet of him. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16) and the Restrained condition until the Grapple ends. Fafnir can only grapple one creature at a time with this ability.  
>
>***Misfortune Breath (Recharge 4–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17 Each creature in a 60-foot Line. *Failure:* 31 (7d8) Poison damage, and the creature has Disadvantage on D20 Tests until the end of Fafnir's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, the fafnir can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The fafnir regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Inflame Greed.** Fafnir casts *Compel Avarice*, requiring no Material components or Concentration and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13). Fafnir cannot take this action again until the start of his next turn.  
>
At will: *Compel Avarice*  
>
>- **Toxic Blood Spray.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17 Each creature within 20 feet of Fafnir. *Failure:* 10 (4d4) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the target's next turn. *Failure or Success:* Fafnir cannot take this action again until the start of his next turn. Fafnir can only use this ability if he's missing at least one of its Hit Points.
>- **Entrap.** Fafnir moves up to half his Speed and makes one Constrict attack.
