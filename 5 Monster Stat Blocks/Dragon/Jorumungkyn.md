___
>## Jorumungkyn
>*Gargantuan Dragon, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 230 (20d12 + 100)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Swim 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|25 (+7)|9 (-1)|20 (+5)|5 (-3)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning
>- **Immunities** Cold, Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands Draconic but can't speak it.
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Poisonous Secretions.*** The jorumungkyn constantly oozes poison into its surroundings. A creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of a jorumungkyn (or 30 feet in water) takes 3 (1d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The jorumungkyn makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack and uses Constrict.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 25 (4d8 + 7) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (4d6 + 7) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Constrict.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 19 One Huge or smaller creature the jorumungkyn can see within 15 feet. *Failure:* 21 (4d6 + 7) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage. The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 19) and has the Restrained condition until the Grapple ends.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Glare of the Deep (2/Day).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 14 One creature within 60 feet. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Charmed condition for 1 minute. While Charmed, the target has the Incapacitated condition. If the target is more than 5 feet from the jorumungkyn, the target moves on its turn toward the jorumungkyn by the most direct route, trying to get within 5 feet of the jorumungkyn. Before moving into damaging terrain and whenever it takes damage from a source other than the jorumungkyn, the target repeats the save. *Success:* The target has Immunity to the jorumungkyn's Glare of the Deep for 24 hours.
