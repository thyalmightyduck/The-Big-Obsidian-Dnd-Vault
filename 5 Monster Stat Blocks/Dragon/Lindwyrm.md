___
>## Lindwyrm
>*Gargantuan Dragon, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 211 (14d20 + 70)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|15 (+2)|21 (+5)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +11, Dex +7, Con +10
>- **Skills** Perception +7, Stealth +7
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Tremorsense 120 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Draconic
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Disease Immunity.*** The lindwyrm has Immunity to all nonmagical diseases.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day or 4/Day in Lair).*** If the lindwyrm fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lindwyrm makes one Bite attack and two Claw attacks. It can replace one Claw attack with Swallow.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Piercing damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and the lindwyrm can't Bite another target. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Swallow.*** The lindwyrm makes one Bite attack against a Huge or smaller creature it has Grappled with its Bite. *Hit:*  The target takes the Bite attack's Piercing damage (but not the Poison damage), no longer has the Grappled condition, and is swallowed. While swallowed, the target has the Blinded and Restrained condition and Total Cover from effects that originate outside the lindwyrm. It takes 27 (6d8) Acid damage at the start of each of the lindwyrm's turns. The lindwyrm can only have one target swallowed at a time.  
>If the lindwyrm takes 40 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the lindwyrm must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate the swallowed creature, which lands in a space within 10 feet of the lindwyrm and has the Prone condition. If the lindwyrm dies, the swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape the corpse using 15 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 2. Immediately after another creature's turn, the lindwyrm can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The lindwyrm regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Claw Attack.** The lindwyrm makes a Claw attack.
>- **Burrowing Rush.** The lindwyrm burrows up to its Speed, coming up beneath a target touching the ground and making a Bite attack against it. This movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks. The lindwyrm can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
