>## Ancient Blightscale Dragon
>*Gargantuan Dragon (Cursed), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 21
>- **Hit Points** 420 (24d20 + 168)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|27 (+8)|10 (+0)|25 (+7)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +8, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Insight +8, Perception +15, Stealth +7
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 25
>- **Languages** One national language, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 22 (XP 41,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Accursed Death.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:*: DC 22, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the dragon when it dies. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats its save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>However, a target that fails two saves is cursed. While cursed, the target can't regain Hit Points. Upon being cursed and every 24 hours thereafter, the target takes 22 (4d10) Necrotic damage, can't regain Hit Points, and its Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken. If this effect reduces the target's Hit Point maximum to 0, it dies.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day or 5/Day in Lair).*** If the dragon fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon uses Sickening Presence, then makes three Rend attacks.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +15 to hit, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d8 + 8) Force damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Sickening Presence.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation originating from the dragon. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the dragon's next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to Sickening Presence for 1 hour.  
>
>***Swarm Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature in a 90-foot Cone. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Piercing damage plus 42 (12d6) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, the dragon can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The dragon regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
>
>- **Bursting Pus.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature in a 30-footlong, 5-foot-wide Line. *Failure:* The target is covered in eye-watering pus. It has the Blinded condition and can't ingest anything until a creature takes an action to scrub off the pus. The effect ends early if the creature is doused or submerged in water. *Failure or Success:* The dragon can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Tail Swipe.** The dragon makes one Rend attack.
>- **Take Flight.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the dragon. *Failure:* The target takes 17 (2d8 + 8) Bludgeoning damage and has the Prone condition. *Failure or Success:* The dragon flies up to half its Fly Speed. The dragon can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>
>### Lair Actions
>On Initiative count 20 (losing Initiative ties), an adult or ancient blightscale dragon takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects. The dragon can't use the same effect two rounds in a row.
>- Insects burst from a hive built on a surface that the dragon can see within 120 feet of it. Any creature within a 20-foot Emanation centered on the hive must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) Piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that ends its turn in the cloud takes 10 (3d6) Piercing damage. The insects disperse on Initiative count 20 of the next round. They disperse early if subjected to intense wind or a damaging area effect.
>- Puslike fluid erupts in a 20-foot radius Sphere centered on a point on the ground the dragon can see within 120 feet of it. That area becomes Difficult Terrain, and a creature moving through this terrain must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or have the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) and have the Restrained condition while grappled in this way. Another creature can pull a Restrained target free by taking an action and succeeding on a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check. The ground dries sufficiently to return to normal on Initiative count 20 of the next round.
>- Foul gas belches forth in a 20-foot-radius Sphere from a point the dragon can see within 120 feet of it. The cloud spreads around corners, and its area is Heavily Obscured. Each creature within the cloud at the start of its turn must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature has the Poisoned condition. Creatures that don't need to breathe or are immune to Poison damage automatically succeed on this saving throw. This cloud disperses on Initiative count 20 of the next round. It disperses early if subjected to intense wind.
>
>### Regional Effects
>An adult or ancient blightscale dragon's curse warps the land around the dragon's lair, creating one or more of the following effects.
>- The smell of decay can be detected 6 miles from the lair, growing stronger as one moves nearer.
>- Water sources within 1 mile of the lair have soured, and the water smells and tastes foul. Only the dragon can drink it safely. Any other creature that does so must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw 10 minutes later or have the Poisoned condition. A poisoned creature can repeat the saving throw every hour, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature that succeeds on a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check can cure this condition with 10 minutes of effort.
>- Vegetation within 1 mile of the lair is sickly and nasty. It provides no more than half its normal food value. Within 1,500 feet of the lair, plants are inedible, and no wildlife can be found.
>- Beasts and Plant creatures within 1 mile of the lair are aggressive and hard to reason with. In combat, at the start of its turn, such a creature gains Advantage on all melee attack rolls it makes during that turn, but attack rolls against it have Advantage until the start of its next turn. Effects that cure disease can end this effect for 1 day, but it returns if the creature remains in the area.
>
>If the dragon dies, these effects fade over 2d4 days. Signs of environmental damage remain indefinitely.
