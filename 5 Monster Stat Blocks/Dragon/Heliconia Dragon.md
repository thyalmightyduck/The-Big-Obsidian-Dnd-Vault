>## Heliconia Dragon
>*Small Dragon, typically Neutral Good*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 45 (10d6 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|4 (-3)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|9 (-1)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Performance +7
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Draconic
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** The dragon doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three attacks: two with its beak and one with its talons.  
>
>***Beak.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Charming Song (2/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13 Each creature within 60 feet of the dragon that can hear it. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
