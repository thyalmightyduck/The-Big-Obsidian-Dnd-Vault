# Info:
Playful and full of wonder, Delerium Dragon Wyrmlings do not stray far from where they hatch, as they still must contend with their multidimensional existence. A wyrmling usually does not yet fully understand their transtemporal senses, and experiences memories of their adulthood and future in dreams and visions.

# Delerium Dragons
This unearthly dragon is a crystalline horror, its body formed from angular gem-like scales pulsing with octarine light. Its form is raw delerium. Each scale is a gleaming crystal refracting light into eerie hues of purple, yellow, and green, with an unnatural sharpness that feels both fragile and impossibly durable.

The dragon's wings are translucent and webbed with glowing crystal veins that shimmer, casting kaleidoscopic patterns as it moves. Each beat causes the air to hum, vibrating with a chaotic energy that overwhelms and distorts the senses. Each step the dragon takes leaves delicate motes of crystal growing into jagged formations, tainting the landscape. Its eyes are like multifaceted gems, swirling with chaotic magic pooling within their depths. The beast's crystalline horns and talons are razor-sharp, gleaming like polished jewels, yet crackling with eldritch energy.

Inscrutable beings of the Space Between Worlds, Delerium Dragons are order from chaos incarnate. How such creatures came to be is unknown, and few know of their existence. Some propose that they emerged spontaneously, hatching from crystalline eggs with octarine shells.

A Delerium Dragon is not simply contaminated, but a unique type of gemstone dragon with an otherworldly physiology. These dragons are literally made of the crystals: their claws, bones, and scales are refracting delerium shard. Their flesh and organs are incomprehensible organic matter, and their blood is delerium sludge. When unleashings its breath weapon, it exhales a storm of glittering shards that explode, causing madness, mutation, and wild magic to erupt in the area.

Delerium Dragons are amongst the most powerful beings born within the Space Between Worlds. Capable of perceiving dimensions imperceptible to mortals, these dragons often seem flighty or [[Distracted]], but this is because they experience layers of reality which humans cannot. They are thus strange intermediaries, able to interact with creatures on a transdimensional level. Indeed, Delerium Dragons are unstuck in time, unrestrained by the linear progression of past, present, and future. Some experience their entire lives simultaneously, whereas others live their lives non-sequentially. Most understand that even their own existences is beyond comprehension, and many contemplate the fundamental nature of existence, chaos, and the cosmos. any are nihilistic solipsists who believe that nothing actually exists except themselves, and even then many doubt their own existence, believing they are merely half-remembered dreams caught betwixt a fleeting thought. Others instead revel in their chaotic existence, seeking new forms of art and untamed creation. Powerful mages and talented bards have sought their wisdom and knowledge.

Most Delerium Dragons find the Mortal World, and mortals, rather limited and boring. Throughout history, only one emerged in the Mortal World, the mighty Amaranthyst, archmage of the Amethyst Academy—the dragon's true nature known only to the other members of the Academy Directorate.

Delerium Dragons exert their force of will over the Space Between Worlds and make their lairs realms unto themselves. hese lairs may open into a Thin Place, the yawning cavern of a normal dragon's lair instead leading to the madness of the Delerium Dragon's home. These dragons fill their lairs with treasures and secrets of mysterious significance and anachronistic origin, hoarding items which may have been valuable in the distant past, or of critical importance to the distant future. Like themselves, some of their greatest possessions may exist in a state mortals can't perceive or which are fundamentally hazardous, such as multidimensional art objects, miniature primordial black holes, degenerate matter taken from a neutron star, or magical vessels containing another universe or pocket dimension.

### Study the Foe
Characters proficient in History gain [[Advantage]] on Intelligence (Arcana) checks when they take the [[Study action]] to examine a Delerium Dragon, learning the following based on the result.

**(15+) Empowered by Eldritch Magic.** You understand the dragon's [[Resistance|Resistances]] and senses.

**(20+) Octarine Breath.** You recognize the effects of the Delerium Dragon's breath weapon, as well as noting its Spellcasting capabilities.

**(25+) Few Weaknesses.** You learn its highest and lowest saving throws.

### Combat Tactics
A Delerium Dragon views combat less as a violent encounter and more as a chance to change foes into a new form. It darts about using flight and teleportation, seldom remaining in the same place for more than a turn. Their movement makes it easy for the dragon to find the perfect place to catch many enemies with its breath weapon. Delerium Dragons love to spread [[contamination]] and cause [[Arcane Anomaly]], and delight causing monstrous transformations with their contaminated attacks.

Delerium Dragons use their Bite, Contaminated Rend, and Tail attacks to tear opponents apart in between uses of their breath weapon. When they are unable to close the distance, they mentally assault their targets with Mindfire.
