___
>## Sea Drake
>*Medium Dragon, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Draconic
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The sea drake can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The sea drake makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it is pulled into a space within 5 feet of the sea drake and has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). While grappling the target, the sea drake can't use its Tail on another target.
