>## Necrolisk
>*Huge Dragon, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 178 (17d12 + 68)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Burrow 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|13 (+1)|19 (+4)|7 (-2)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Frightened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Tremorsense 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands Draconic but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Entrapping Saliva.*** The necrolisk ignores Immunity to the Grappled and Restrained conditions of a creature with the Incorporeal Movement trait. Creatures swallowed by the necrolisk can't pass through it using Incorporeal Movement or through the Ethereal Plane, and creatures can't teleport out of its stomach.  
>
>***Herb Allergy.*** If the necrolisk eats a specific concoction of herbs worth 25 GP (such as if a swallowed creature pours them into its stomach as a Utilize action), at the end of the turn, the necrolisk regurgitates anything in its stomach as if it failed the saving throw described in its Swallow Bonus Action.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The necrolisk can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Tunneler.*** The necrolisk can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrow Speed and leaves a 10-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The necrolisk makes one Bite attack and one Claws attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Force damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16). Until the grapple ends, it has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d6 + 6) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Force damage.  
>
>***Ephemeral Spit (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 60-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. *Failure:* 42 (12d6) Force damage, and the target has the Restrained condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Swallow.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18, one Large or smaller creature Grappled by the necrolisk (the necrolisk can only have one creature swallowed at a time). *Failure:* The necrolisk swallows the target, which is no longer Grappled. While swallowed, the creature has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the necrolisk, and takes 21 (6d6) Force damage at the start of each of the necrolisk's turns.  
>If the necrolisk takes 30 damage or more on a single turn from the swallowed creature, the necrolisk must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate the creature, which falls in a space within 10 feet of the necrolisk and has the Prone condition. If the necrolisk dies, a swallowed creature is no longer Restrained and can escape from the corpse by using 15 feet of movement, exiting Prone.
