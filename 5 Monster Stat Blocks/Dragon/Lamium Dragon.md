___
>## Lamium Dragon
>*Gargantuan Dragon, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 264 (16d20 + 96)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|28 (+9)|8 (-1)|22 (+6)|18 (+4)|23 (+6)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Con +11, Int +9, Wis +11
>- **Skills** History +9, Insight +11, Perception +16
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Deafened
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 26
>- **Languages** Draconic, plus any three languages
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Hold Breath.*** The dragon can hold its breath for 1 hour.  
>
>***Sunlight Dependency.*** The dragon gains a level of Exhaustion every 5 days it isn't exposed to sunlight. A level of Exhaustion gained this way is removed if the dragon is exposed to direct sunlight for 5 hours.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon can use its Aquatic Floral Presence or Greater Heart Sight. It then makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its tail.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 20 (2d10 + 9) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (2d12 + 9) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Aquatic Floral Presence.*** Each creature of the dragon's choice that is within 120 feet of it is cured of the Poisoned condition and gains 11 (2d10) Temporary Hit Points that last for 1 minute. As long as these Temporary Hit Points remain, the target can't be Poisoned and can breathe water. Once the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the dragon's Floral Presence for the next 24 hours.  
>
>***Greater Heart Sight.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 16 One creature in range of its Blindsight. *Failure:* The dragon magically learns the target's current emotional state and its alignment. *Success:* Emotional state only. Celestials, fiends, and undead automatically fail this saving throw.  
>
>***Emotion Wave (Recharge 5–6).*** The dragon uses one of the following emotion waves:  
>- **Negative Wave.** The dragon releases a blast of violent psychic energy. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 19 Each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 52 (15d6) Psychic damage. A creature that fails its saving throw by 5 or more is Frightened of the dragon until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Positive Wave.** The dragon releases a burst of blissful psychic energy. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 19 Each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* Target has Incapacitated Condition for 10 minutes. This effect ends early for a creature if a target takes damage. An affected creature can repeat its saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect for itself on a success.
