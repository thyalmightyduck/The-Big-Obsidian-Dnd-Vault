>## Clematis Dragon
>*Small Dragon, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 27 (5d6 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Swim 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|3 (-4)|8 (-1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Intimidation +2, Perception +3
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 5 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The dragon can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Poison Petals.*** A creature that touches the dragon or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 2 (1d4) Piercing damage and must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 9 (2d8) Poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that fails its save is also Paralyzed until the end of its next turn, unless it is immune to the Poisoned condition.  
>
>***Standing Leap.*** The dragon's Long Jump is up to 30 feet and its High Jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Piercing damage.
