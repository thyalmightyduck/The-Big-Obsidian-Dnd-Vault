___
>## Sky Drake
>*Large Dragon, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|17 (+3)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +6, Survival +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Draconic and one other language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** The sky drake doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>***Keen Sight.*** The sky drake has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The sky drake makes two attacks, using Bite or Claws in any combination.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Lightning damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage.
