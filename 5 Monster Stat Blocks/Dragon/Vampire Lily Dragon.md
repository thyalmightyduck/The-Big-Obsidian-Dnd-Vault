___
>## Vampire Lily Dragon
>*Large Dragon, typically Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 252 (24d10 + 120)
>- **Speed** 45 ft., Burrow 15 ft., Swim 45 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|14 (+2)|21 (+5)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +11, Con +10, Int +9
>- **Skills** Deception +8, Insight +9, Perception +9, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic plus any two languages
>- **Challenge** 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The dragon has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Poison Spines.*** A creature that touches the dragon or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it must succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or take 16 (3d10) Poison damage and become Poisoned until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Unsleeping.*** The dragon can't be Surprised, does not need to sleep, and magic can't put it to sleep.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three attacks: two with its bite and one with its tail.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (3d6 + 6) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 19). Until this grapple ends, the creature has the Restrained condition, and the dragon can't grapple another target.  
>
>***Stench Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** The dragon exhales foul gas. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18 Each creature in a 60 foot Cone. *Failure:* Target has the Unconscious condition for 1 minute. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself with a successful save. The effect ends if the creature takes damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The dragon moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature that it can see.
