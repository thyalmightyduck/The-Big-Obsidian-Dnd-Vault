>## Ornithogalum Dragon
>*Huge Dragon, typically Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 225 (18d12 + 108)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 120 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|13 (+1)|22 (+6)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Con +6
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Force, Poison, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands Draconic but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Aerial Agility.*** While flying, the dragon makes Strength and Dexterity saving throws with Advantage.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The dragon has Advantage on Saving Throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Toxic Aura.*** Any creature that starts its turn within 20 feet of the dragon must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or be Poisoned for 1 minute. An affected creature can repeat its saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon can use its Noxious Floral Presence. It then makes four attacks: two with its claws and two with its tail. It can replace two claw attacks with its stomp.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Stomp.*** The dragon slams its forefeet into the ground. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 19 Each creature within 15 feet of the dragon. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition. *Failure or Success:* The ground in a 15-foot radius circle centered on the dragon becomes Difficult Terrain.  
>
>***Noxious Floral Presence (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19 Each creature within 120 feet of the dragon. *Failure:* Target gains a level of Exhaustion. If a target's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the target is immune to this dragon's Noxious Floral Presence for the next 24 hours.
