___
>## Adult Delerium Dragon
>*Huge Dragon (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 95 (7d12 + 49) plus 95 (7d12 + 49) per player character
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Fly 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|27 (+8)|14 (+2)|25 (+7)|23 (+6)|17 (+3)|23 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Con +13, Wis +9, Cha +12
>- **Skills** Arcana +12, Perception +15, Stealth +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Force, Necrotic, Psychic, Radiant
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 25
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 18 (XP 20,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The dragon is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the dragon's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the dragon can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the dragon has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The dragon is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The dragon may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Haze Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the dragon takes 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spellcasting (1/Round).*** The dragon casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20):  
>
>At will: *Dimension Door*, *Telekinesis*  
>2/day each: *Dispel Magic*  
>1/day each: *Contact Other Plane*, *Plane Shift* (self only), *Scrying*  
>
>***The dragon always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the dragon can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the dragon takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Bite.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 34 (4d12 + 8) Force damage, and if the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 22). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.
>- **Claw.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d6 + 8) Force damage.
>
>***Tail.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 22, one creature within 20 feet of the dragon. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Force damage, and if the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Mindfire.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 20, one creature the dragon can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* The target takes 22 (4d10) Psychic damage and has the Burning condition (2d8 Psychic). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Terrifying Roar (Recharges after a Short Rest).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 21, each creature in a 120-foot Emanation originating from the dragon that can hear it. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Thunder damage, and the target has the Frightened condition (save ends).  
>
>***Breathe Deep (1/Round).*** The dragon's Breath Weapon recharges. The dragon can only use this Epic Action if it's the first Epic Action it has taken this round.  
>
>***Breath Weapon (Special Recharge).*** The dragon can only use this Epic Action if it's the last Epic Action it takes this round. It chooses one of the following effects  
>
>***Mutation Breath.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 21, each creature in a 120-foot Cone. *Failure:* 72 (16d8) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level. Instead of rolling to determine if it gains a mutation, the target automatically gains a mutation. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Madness Breath.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 21, each creature in a 120-foot Cone. *Failure:* 66 (12d10) Psychic damage, and the target has the Dazed condition (save ends). Failure by 5 or More: 132 (24d10) Psychic damage, and the target instead has the Incapacitated condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Wild Magic Breath.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 21, each creature in a 120-foot long, 5-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 55 (10d10) Radiant damage, and the target triggers an Arcane Anomalies centered on itself. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The dragon chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The dragon can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Draconic Rebuke.*** *Trigger:* The dragon becomes Bloodied. It can use this Reaction even if it has the Incapacitated condition or is otherwise unable to take Reactions. *Response:* All conditions and spells on the dragon immediately end unless it chooses to be affected by them, and it can move up to its Fly Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action and ignoring Difficult Terrain. The dragon recharges and immediately uses its Terrifying Roar.
