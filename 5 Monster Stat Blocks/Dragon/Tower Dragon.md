___
>## Tower Dragon
>*Large Dragon*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 171 (18d10 + 72)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 80 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Con +8, Wis +5, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Insight +4, Perception +7, Stealth +3
>- **Immunities** Lightning; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The dragon can breathe air and water.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the dragon remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an inanimate bronze statue.  
>
>***Faultless Tracker.*** The creatures that caused the dragon to animate become its quarries. The dragon knows the direction and distance to its quarries as long the quarries remain within Drakkenheim or until the dragon is destroyed.  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** The dragon is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The dragon has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If the dragon is destroyed, it reforms with all of its Hit Points in 1 hour unless a *Disintegrate* spell is cast on its remains.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three Rend attacks.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  + 9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Force damage.  
>
>***Breath Weapons (Recharge 5–6).*** The dragon uses one of the following breath weapons.  
>
>***Lightning Breath.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 60-foot long, 5-foot wide Line. *Failure:* 55 (10d10) Lightning damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Repulsion Breath.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* The target is pushed up to 40 feet away from the dragon.
