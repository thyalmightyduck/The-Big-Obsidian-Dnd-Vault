___
>## Sea Lindwurm
>*Huge Dragon, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 153 (18d10 + 54)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Swim 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|13 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +6, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +9
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Lightning
>- **Immunities** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Draconic
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The sea lindwurm can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The sea lindwurm makes two Bite attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Piercing damage. The target is also subject to the sea lindwurm's paralyzing toxin. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. **First Failure:** The creature has the Restrained condition. The target repeats the save at the end of its next turn if it's still Restrained, ending the effect on itself on a success. **Second Failure:** The target has the Paralyzed condition for 1 hour.  
>
>***Flipper Slap (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16 All creatures in a 10-foot radius Sphere around the sea lindwurm. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the creature has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Deep Dive.*** The sea lindwurm moves up to half its Speed without drawing Opportunity Attacks.
