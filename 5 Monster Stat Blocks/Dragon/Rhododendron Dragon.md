>## Rhododendron Dragon
>*Medium Dragon, Any Lawful Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Con +4, Int +2
>- **Skills** Intimidation +1, Perception +4
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Draconic plus any one language
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The dragon has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage and if the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away from the dragon and has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protector.*** *Trigger:* When a creature the dragon can see attacks a target other than the dragon that is within 5 feet of the dragon. *Response:* The dragon can impose Disadvantage on the attack roll.
