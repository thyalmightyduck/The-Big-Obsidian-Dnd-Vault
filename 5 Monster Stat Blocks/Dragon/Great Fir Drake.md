>## Great Fir Drake
>*Huge Dragon, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19
>- **Hit Points** 162 (18d12 + 54)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +14 (24)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|19 (+4)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|21 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Int +10, Wis +9, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Insight +9, Perception +9, Persuasion +9, Stealth +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Immunities** Radiant; Charmed, Blinded
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft, Darkvision 120 ft, Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000, or 15,000 in lair; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Illumination.*** As a Bonus Action, the drake can produce dim light in a 15 ft radius, or bright light in a 15 ft radius and dim light for an additional 15 ft radius. The drake can end this effect as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Lair).*** If the drake fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Prickly Pines.*** If a creature successfully hits the drake with a melee attack, the creature takes 4 (1d8) piercing damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The drake makes three Rend attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of (A) Incandescent Breath or (B) Spellcasting to cast *Colour Spray*.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>***Incandescent Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 19, each creature in a 90 ft long, 5 ft wide Line. *Failure:* 55 (10d10) Radiant damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The drake casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18, +10 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Color Spray*, *Detect Magic*, *Faerie Fire*  
>1/day each: *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Major Image*, *Plant Growth*  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, the great fir drake can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The great fir drake regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Bright Tail Lash.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17, a creature the drake can see within 15 ft. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Radiant damage, and the target is knocked Prone.
>- **Pounce.** The drake moves up to half its Speed, and it makes one Rend attack.
>- **Twinkling Lights.** The drake uses Spellcasting to cast *Faerie Fire*.
