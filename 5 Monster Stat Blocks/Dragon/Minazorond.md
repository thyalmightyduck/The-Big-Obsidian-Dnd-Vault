___
>## Minazorond
>*Huge Dragon (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 95 (7d12 + 49) plus 95 (7d12 + 49) per player character
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft., Fly 80 ft.
>- **Initiative** +14 (24)
>___

>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|27 (+8)|14 (+2)|25 (+7)|23 (+6)|17 (+3)|23 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Con +13, Wis +9, Cha +12
>- **Skills** Arcana +14, Perception +15, Stealth +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Force, Psychic, Radiant
>- **Immunities** Lightning, Necrotic, Poison, Thunder
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 25
>- **Languages** Common, Draconic; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 18 (XP 20,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Construct Resilience.*** If damage reduces Minazorond to 0 Hit Points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 plus the damage taken, unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. On a success, Minazorond drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>***Protective Aura.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of Minazorond takes 14 (4d6) Lightning damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Minazorond always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, Minazorond can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, Minazorond takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Bite.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 33 (4d12 + 7) Piercing damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.
>- **Claw.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d6 + 7) Slashing damage.
>
>***Tail.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 22, one creature Minazorond can see within 20 feet. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Force damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Shocking Burst.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 20, one target Minazorond can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Lightning damage, and the target has the Shocked condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Terrifying Roar (Recharges after a Short Rest).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 20, each creature in a 120-foot Emanation originating from Minazorond that can hear it. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Thunder damage, and the target has the Frightened condition (save ends).  
>
>***Breathe Deep (1/Round).*** Minazorond's Breath Weapon recharges. Minazorond can only use this Epic Action if it's the first Epic Action it has taken this round.  
>
>***Breath Weapon (Special Recharge).*** Minazorond uses one of the following breath weapons. It can only use this Epic Action if it's the last Epic Action it takes this round.  
>
>***Lightning Breath.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature in a 120-foot Cone. *Failure:* 81 (16d8) Lightning damage, and the target has the Shocked condition. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Thunder Breath.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 22, each creature in a 120-foot Cone. *Failure:* 66 (12d10) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed 20 feet away from Minazorond and has the Dazed condition (save ends). Failure by 5 or More: Double damage, and the target also has the Deafened condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** Minazorond chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. Minazorond can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Draconic Rebuke.*** *Trigger:* Minazorond becomes Bloodied. It can use this Reaction even if it has the Incapacitated condition or is otherwise unable to take Reactions. *Response:* All conditions and spells on Minazorond immediately end unless it chooses to be affected by them, and it can fly up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action and ignoring Difficult Terrain. Minazorond recharges and immediately uses its Terrifying Roar.
