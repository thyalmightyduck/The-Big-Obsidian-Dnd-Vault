>## Hedron
>*Medium Elemental, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 110 (13d8 + 52)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___

>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Blinded, Deafened, Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Understands all languages but can't speak, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of Pure Order.*** The hedron exudes a 30-foot Emanation of pure order, which influences randomness and makes the area conform to a rigid ideal. The Emanation penetrates objects and barriers, but a barrier more than 2 feet thick or made of magical force blocks it. An object that remains in the Emanation for 1 minute becomes perfectly geometric, and Difficult Terrain in the area becomes normal terrain.  
>A creature other than a hedron that starts its turn in the Emanation or moves there for the first time on a turn takes 5 (2d4) Force damage. While in the Emanation, a creature can't shape-shift or have Advantage or Disadvantage on any roll.  
>
>***Immutable Form.*** The hedron has Immunity to any spell or effect that would alter its form.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hedron makes three Telekinesis attacks.  
>
>***Telekinesis.*** *Melee Spell Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 60 ft. *Hit:* 21 (6d6) Force damage, unless the hedron chooses to deal half or no damage. If the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14). While Grappled, the target has the Bloodied condition.  
>Each time the hedron makes a Telekinesis attack against a Grappled target, it can move the target up to 30 feet in any direction, provided the target remains within 60 feet of the hedron. If the target is an object worn or carried by an unwilling creature, it can prevent the movement with a successful DC 14 Strength saving throw. If the hedron deals no damage to the target, it can exert fine control with its telekinetic force, such as manipulating a tool or opening a door.
