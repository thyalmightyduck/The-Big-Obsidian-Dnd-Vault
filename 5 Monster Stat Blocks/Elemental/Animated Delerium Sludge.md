___
>## Animated Delerium Sludge
>*Large Elemental*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 114 (12d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 90 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Invisible in Sludge.*** The elemental has the Invisible condition while fully submerged in delerium sludge.  
>
>***Sludge Form.*** The elemental can enter a Hostile creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.  
>
>### Actions
>***Contaminated Touch.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level.  
>
>***Engulf.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, each Large or smaller creature in the elemental's space. *Success:* The target is pushed out of the elemental's space. *Failure:* The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The elemental can grapple one Large creature or two Medium or smaller creatures at a time.  
>At the start of each of the elemental's turns, the target takes 28 (8d6) Necrotic damage and is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target gains one Contamination level.  
>A creature within 5 feet of the elemental can take an action to pull a creature or object out of it with a successful DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check.
