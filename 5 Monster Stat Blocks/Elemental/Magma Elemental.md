___
>## Magma Elemental
>*Large Elemental, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Cold, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Primordial (Ignan, Terran)
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aversion to Cold.*** If the elemental takes Cold damage, its movement is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn, and its Heat Aura trait doesn't function on its next turn.  
>
>***Earth Passage.*** The elemental can burrow through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone and leaves a 5-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.  
>
>***Heat Aura.*** At the end of each of the elemental's turns, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the elemental takes 7 (2d6) Fire damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two Burning Slam attacks.  
>
>***Burning Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Roiling Lava (Recharge 4–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the elemental can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.
