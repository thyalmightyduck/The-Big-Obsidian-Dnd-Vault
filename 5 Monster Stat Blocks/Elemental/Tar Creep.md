>## Tar Creep
>*Large Elemental, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 114 (12d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +7
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amorphous.*** The tar creep can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Kindle.*** When the tar creep takes Fire damage, it ignites. While burning, the tar creep's Slam attacks adhere flaming pitch to foes, dealing an extra 4 (1d8) Fire damage and igniting the target. Until a burning creature takes an action to douse the fire, the creature takes 5 (1d10) Fire damage at the start of each of its turns.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The tar creep makes two Slam attacks. It can replace one attack with Engulf.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The tar creep can grapple up to two creatures at a time.  
>
>***Engulf.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature Grappled by the tar creep. *Failure:* The grapple ends and the target is pulled into the tar creep's body. The target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, is suffocating, and has Total Cover from effects that originate outside the tar creep. At the start of each of the blood ooze's turns, the target takes 13 (3d8) Bludgeoning damage plus 5 (1d10) Fire damage if the tar creep is burning. The tar creep can have one creature engulfed at a time.  
>The target can escape the tar creep by taking an action and succeeding on a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. Escaping the tar creep costs 5 feet of movement and ejects the target into a space of its choice within 5 feet of the tar creep. If the tar creep dies, the target no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape without using an action or making a check.  
>If the target dies while engulfed, its body dissolves and the tar creep expels anything it was wearing or carrying.
