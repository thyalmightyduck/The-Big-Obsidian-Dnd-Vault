>## Gore Elemental
>*Large Elemental, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 110 (13d10 + 39)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Psychic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Infernal
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gore elemental makes three Gruesome Deluge attacks against separate targets. It can replace one attack with Grisly Mire.  
>
>***Gruesome Deluge.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15).  
>
>***Grisly Mire.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature Grappled by the gore elemental. *Failure:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Slashing damage plus 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Prone condition. Until the grapple ends, the target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions and can't breathe. *Success:* The target is pushed into an adjacent space, ending the grapple.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Swelling Crimson (1/Day).*** *Trigger:* The gore elemental reduces a living creature to 0 Hit Points, and it doesn't have the Bloodied condition. *Response:* The gore elemental's Size increases to Huge. While Huge, the gore elemental gains Advantage on all Strength and Constitution saving throws, its Gruesome Deluge attack can grapple Large targets, and its Grisly Mire action deals 26 (4d10 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage on a failed save. The gore elemental reverts to its original size when it becomes Bloodied.
