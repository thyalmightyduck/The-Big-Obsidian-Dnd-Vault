___
>## Panjaian Morakeshi
>*Medium Elemental, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 35 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Performance +4, Persuasion +4
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Primordial and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Heated Body.*** A creature that touches the panjaian morakeshi or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 5 (2d4) Fire damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The panjaian morakeshi makes two attacks, using Slam and Hurl Fire in any combination.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Bludgeoning damage plus 8 (2d4 + 3) Fire damage.  
>
>***Hurl Fire.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +5, range 30 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Fire damage.  
>
>***Control Flame.*** The panjaian morakeshi chooses a fire it can see within 30 feet and expands that fire by one 5-foot Cube, extinguishes fire in one 5-foot Cube, or makes shapes in the fire in one 5-foot Cube. Expanded fire needs fuel to continue burning.  
>
>***Flame Wave (Recharge 5–6).*** Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Controlled Illumination.*** The panjaian morakeshi sheds Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet. The morakeshi can douse the light (no Action required), provided it doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.
