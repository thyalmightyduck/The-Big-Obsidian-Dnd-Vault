___
>## Bogfoul
>*Large Elemental, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 119 (14d10 + 42)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 90 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Primordial (Aquan)
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blight.*** A creature that touches the bogfoul, hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it, or shares a space with it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Freeze.*** If the bogfoul takes Cold damage, it partially freezes. Its Speed is reduced by 20 feet until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Water Form.*** The bogfoul can move through a creature's space and stop there, and vice versa. The bogfoul can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The bogfoul makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage, and the target is subjected to Blight.  
>
>***Noxious Splash.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 10-foot radius Sphere centered on a point within 60 feet. The Sphere spreads around corners. *Failure:* 17 (5d6) Poison damage and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Swamp (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in the bogfoul's space. *Failure:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage. If the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and can't breathe unless it can breathe underwater. *Success:* The target is pushed out of the bogfoul's space.  
>A creature within 5 feet of the bogfoul can pull a creature or object out of it by taking an action and succeeding on a DC 14 Strength (Athletics) check. Regardless of the result, the creature is subjected to Blight.
