___
>## Eldritch Cataclysm
>*Gargantuan Elemental*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 210 (12d20 + 84)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 120 ft.
>- **Initiative** +12 (22)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|25 (+7)|25 (+7)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +6, Wis +8, Cha +10
>- **Immunities** Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, Thunder; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Air Form.*** The eldritch cataclysm can enter a Hostile creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.  
>
>***Cyclonic Storm.*** The eldritch cataclysm emits a 120-foot Emanation of high winds that disperse gases and vapors and extinguish unprotected flames. Ranged attacks that pass through the area have Disadvantage. Objects that are not worn or carried are blown away 1d6 x 5 feet in a random direction. A creature that starts its turn in the Emanation is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17 (flying creatures have Disadvantage). *Failure:* The target has the Slowed condition until the start of its next turn. Failure by 5 or More: The target is flung 1d6 x 5 feet in a random direction, landing with the Prone condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The eldritch cataclysm uses Chain Lighting or Icy Bolt, then uses Meteor Stike or Quake.  
>
>***Chain Lightning.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +12, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d8 + 7) Lightning damage, the target has the Shocked condition until the end of the cataclysm's next turn, and the lightning leaps to another creature of the cataclysm's choice within 15 feet of the first target. The cataclysm makes a separate *Chain Lightning* attack against the target. The cataclysm can make any number of *Chain Lightning* attacks in this way, but a creature can be targeted only once each time the cataclysm takes this action.  
>
>***Icy Bolt.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +12, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d8 + 7) Cold damage. *Hit or Miss:* The target and each creature within 5 feet of it are subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Cold damage, and the target has the Frozen condition (DC 17).  
>
>***Meteor Strike.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 15-foot radius, 40-foot tall Cylinder centered on a point the cataclysm can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Fire damage plus 18 (4d8) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Quake.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the cataclysm. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Thunder damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
