___
>## Polar Serpent
>*Large Elemental, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 58 (9d10 + 9)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|19 (+4)|12 (+1)|4 (-3)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands Primordial but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Misty Slithering.*** The serpent can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The serpent makes one Bite attack and uses Constrict.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Cold damage.  
>
>***Constrict.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 14, one Medium or smaller creature the snake can see within 10 feet. *Failure:* 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Cold damage. The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends.
