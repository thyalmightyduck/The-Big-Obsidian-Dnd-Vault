>## Mjork Sootling Swarm
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Elementals, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|4 (-3)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Thunder
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Primordial (Ignan) and Primordial (Terran) but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Hot Soot.*** A creature that ends its turn in the sootling swarm's space takes 5 (2d4) Fire damage, or 2 (1d4) Fire damage if the sootling swarm is Bloodied, and is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition and is suffocating until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Smoke Sense.*** A sootling swarm in contact with smoke has Blindsight in that smoke's area, to a range of 120 feet.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The sootling swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Water Susceptibility.*** For every 5 feet the sootling swarm moves in water, or for every gallon of water splashed on it, the sootling swarm takes 2 Cold damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Burns.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 10 (4d4) Fire damage, or 5 (2d4) Fire damage if the sootling swarm is Bloodied.
