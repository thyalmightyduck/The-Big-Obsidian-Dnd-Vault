___
>## Mjork Charger
>*Large Elemental, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 133 (14d10 + 56)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Thunder
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Primordial (Ignan), Primordial (Terran)
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Fiery Body.*** A creature that touches the mjork charger or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 7 (2d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Illumination.*** The mjork charger sheds Dim Light in a 10-foot radius.  
>
>***Momentum.*** If the mjork charger moves on its turn before attacking, creatures have Disadvantage on Opportunity Attacks against it until the end of its turn.  
>
>***Smoke Sense.*** A mjork charger in contact with smoke has Blindsight in that smoke's area, to a range of 120 feet.  
>
>***Water Susceptibility.*** For every 5 feet the mjork charger moves in water, or for every gallon of water splashed on it, the mjork charger takes 1 Cold damage  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mjork chargermakes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Fire damage. If mjork charger moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 4 (1d8) Bludgeoning damage and 4 (1d8) Fire damage and, if it is Large or smaller, it has the Prone condition.
