>## Lava Bastion
>*Large Elemental (Earth, Fire, Retainer); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18 (heavy armor)
>- **Hit Points** Nine times their level (number of d12 Hit Dice equal to their level)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str 3+PB, Dex +PB, Con 1+PB, Int +PB, Wis 1+PB, Cha +PB
>- **Skills** Athletics +3 plus PB, Insight +1 plus PB
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 10 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Dwarvish, Ignan, Terran
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals the mentor's bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals the mentor's bonus
>___
>### Actions
>***Signature Attack (Molten Slam).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  3 plus PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d6 + PB bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) fire damage. A flammable object hit by this attack ignites if it isn't being worn or carried. Beginning at 7th level, the bastion can make this attack twice, instead of once, when they take the Attack action on their turn.  
>
>***3rd Level: Burning Embrace (3/Day).*** As an action, the bastion makes a signature attack against a creature within range. If the attack hits, the target is also grappled (escape DC 10 plus PB). While grappled in this way, a target is restrained and takes PBd6 fire damage at the start of each of their turns.  
>
>***5th Level: Melting Grasp (3/Day).*** When the bastion hits a creature with a signature attack, the creature takes an extra PBd4 fire damage. If the target is wielding a mundane metal weapon, the bastion partially melts the target's weapon, giving it a permanent -2 penalty to attack and damage rolls. If the target is wielding a supernatural metal weapon, the penalty lasts for 1 hour instead.  
>
>***7th Level: Eruption (1/Day).*** As an action, the bastion erupts, shooting a 30-foot cone of earth and lava from their head. Each creature in that area must make a DC 10 plus PB Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes PBd6 bludgeoning damage plus PBd6 fire damage and is knocked prone. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage and is not knocked prone. The lava spreads around corners and ignites flammable objects in that area that aren't being worn or carried.
