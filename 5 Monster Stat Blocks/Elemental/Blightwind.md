___
>## Blightwind
>*Large Elemental, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 120 (16d10 + 32)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Necrotic, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Primordial (Auran)
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blight.*** A creature that touches the blightwind, hits it with a melee attack with 5 feet of it, or shares a space with it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Mist Form.*** The blightwind can move through a creature's space and stop there, and vice versa. The blightwind can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The blightwind makes two Wind Whip attacks.  
>
>***Wind Whip.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage, and the target is subjected to Blight.  
>
>***Blight Vortex (Recharge 5–6).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the blightwind. *Failure:* The target is pulled 20 feet straight toward the blightwind. If it would enter another creature's space, it stops moving and both creatures take 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage and have the Prone condition.  
>If the target enters the blightwind's space, it takes 14 (2d8 + 5) Necrotic damage and is subjected to Blight. If the target fails its saving throw against Blight, it has the Blinded condition for the same duration.
