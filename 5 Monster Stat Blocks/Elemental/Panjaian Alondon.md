>## Panjaian Alondon
>*Medium Elemental, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +4, Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Primordial and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The panjaian alondon can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The panjaian alondon makes two Tidal Slam attacks.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The panjaian alondon casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Create or Destroy Water*  
>1/day: *Control Water*  
>
>***Tidal Slam.*** *Melee Spell Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 30 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, the panjaian can push it up to 10 feet to an unoccupied space within 30 feet.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Mobile.*** The panjaian alondon takes the Dash or Disengage action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Uncanny Dodge.*** *Trigger:* A creature the panjaian alondon can see hits it with an attack. *Response:* The panjaian alondon halves the damage it takes from the attack.
