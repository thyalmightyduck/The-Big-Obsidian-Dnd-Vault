___
>## Salmon of Knowledge
>*Tiny Elemental, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 25 (10d4)
>- **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 30 ft. (hover; Orb of Water Only), Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, History +6, Insight +6, Perception +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire (Orb of Water only); Psychic
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Understands all but can't speak; telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Orb of Water.*** The salmon is surrounded by a sphere of flowing water that moves with it. While inside of the sphere, the salmon has Resistance to Fire damage. A creature that grapples the salmon can choose to pull it out of the orb of water. The orb of water moves up to 20 feet toward the salmon at the start of each of the salmon's turns. If it enters the salmon's space, the Grappled condition ends on the salmon.  
>
>***Water Breathing.*** The salmon can breathe only underwater.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The salmon makes two Water Spray attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Prophetic Guidance.  
>
>***Water Spray.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Cold damage.  
>
>***Prophetic Guidance (2/Day).*** A creature the salmon can see within 30 feet gains a flash of foresight. Once in the next minute, when the creature fails a D20 Test, it can roll 1d4 and add it to the total, potentially turning the failure into a success.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Echo from the Current.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 5 feet makes an Intelligence or Wisdom check. *Response:* The creature can use the salmon's Wisdom modifier (+4) in place of its own ability modifier for the check.
