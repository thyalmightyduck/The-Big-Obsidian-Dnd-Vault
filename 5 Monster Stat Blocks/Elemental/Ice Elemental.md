___
>## Ice Elemental
>*Large Elemental, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Cold, Poison; Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Primordial (Aquan, Auran)
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aversion to Fire.*** If the elemental takes Fire damage, its Piercing Form trait doesn't function on its next turn, and it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Piercing Form.*** At the start of each of its turns, the elemental deals 5 (1d10) Piercing damage to any creature with 5 feet of it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two Ice Claw attacks.  
>
>***Ice Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Cold damage.  
>
>***Shard Spray (Recharge 4–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Cold damage, and the creature's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
