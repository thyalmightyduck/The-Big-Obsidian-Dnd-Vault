___
>## Forgeblight
>*Large Elemental, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 136 (16d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Primordial (Ignan)
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blight.*** A creature that touches the forgeblight, hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it, or shares a space with it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Cinder Form.*** The forgeblight can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so. A creature that touches the forgeblight or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 4 (1d8) Fire damage. In addition, the forgeblight can move through a creature's space and stop there. The first time it enters a creature's space on a turn, that creature takes 4 (1d8) Fire damage.  
>
>***Illumination.*** The forgeblight sheds Bright Light in a 10-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 10 feet.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The forgeblight deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Water Susceptibility.*** For every 5 feet the forgeblight moves in water, or for every gallon of water splashed on it, it takes 1 Cold damage.  
>
>***Weapon Blight.*** Any nonmagical weapon that strikes the forgeblight partially burns away. After dealing damage, the weapon takes a permanent and cumulative −1 penalty to damage rolls. If the penalty reaches −5, the weapon is destroyed. Nonmagical ammunition that hits the forgeblight deals damage and is then destroyed.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The forgeblight makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) Fire damage, and the target is exposed to Blight. If the target is wearing nonmagical armor or a shield, it is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* 4 (1d8) Fire damage, and the target's armor or shield (target's choice) partially burns away. The armor or shield takes a permanent and cumulative −1 penalty to the AC it offers. Armor reduced to an AC of 10 or a shield that drops to a +0 bonus is destroyed.  
>
>***Cinder Burst.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 10- foot radius Sphere centered on a point within 60 feet. The Sphere spreads around corners. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Fire damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn. While Poisoned, the target can't have the Invisible condition.
