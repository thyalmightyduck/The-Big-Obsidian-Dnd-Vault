___
>## Mud Elemental
>*Large Elemental, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 82 (11d10 + 22)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Primordial (Aquan, Terran)
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aversion to Cold and Fire.*** If the elemental takes Cold or Fire damage, its Speed becomes 0, and it can't use Engulf until the end of its next turn. However, a creature Grappled by the elemental has Disadvantage on ability checks made to escape the grapple during that time as well.  
>
>***Mud Form.*** The elemental can move through a space as narrow as 1 foot without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two attacks, using Sticky Slam or Jet in any combination.  
>
>***Sticky Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Jet.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Engulf.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, one Medium or smaller creature within 10 feet. *Failure:* The target is pulled into the elemental's space and has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). Until the grapple ends, the target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, and it takes 10 (3d6) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. When the elemental moves, the Grappled target moves with it, costing it no extra movement. The elemental can have up to two creatures Grappled by this Bonus Action at a time.
