___
>## Bayou Beast
>*Large Elemental, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 105 (10d10 + 50)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Primordial (Aquan) but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Spirits Susceptibility.*** The elemental takes 9 (2d8) Force damage for every bottle of alcohol splashed onto it, and its Speed is reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two Hexed Slam attacks.  
>
>***Hexed Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be cursed until the start of the elemental's next turn. While cursed, the creature subtracts 1d4 from its attack rolls and saving throws.  
>
>***Swamp Grasp (Recharge 5–6).*** Swamp water and plants roil around the elemental. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature on the ground in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the elemental. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Poison damage, and the target has the Restrained condition for 1 minute, until the elemental has the Incapacitated condition, or until the elemental dies. The target or a creature within 5 feet of it can take an action to make a DC 16 Strength (Athletics) check, ending the Restrained condition on the target on a success.
