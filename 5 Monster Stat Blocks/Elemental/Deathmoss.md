___
>## Deathmoss
>*Large Elemental, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 133 (14d10 + 56)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** -3 (7)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|5 (-3)|18 (+4)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Cold
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Primordial (Terran)
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blight.*** A creature that touches the deathmoss or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Earth Glide.*** The deathmoss can burrow through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone. While doing so, it doesn't disturb the material it moves through.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the deathmoss remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a moss-covered mound.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The deathmoss deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The deathmoss makes two Slam attacks. It can replace one attack with Calming Spores.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage, and the target is subjected to Blight. If the target fails its saving throw against Blight, its Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a Long Rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its Hit Point maximum to 0.  
>
>***Calming Spores (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the deathmoss. The Emanation spreads around corners. *Failure:* For 1 minute, the target is Indifferent toward the deathmoss and has no desire to harm it. At the end of each of its turns and whenever the deathmoss harms the target or its allies, the target repeats its save, ending the effect on itself on a success. *Success:* The target is subjected to Blight.
