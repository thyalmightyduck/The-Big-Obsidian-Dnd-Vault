>## Fractal Lepidoptera
>*Huge Elemental*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 172 (23d12 + 23)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +11 (21)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|9 (-1)|25 (+7)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +7, Wis +4, Cha +9
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Force
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Alien Intellect.*** The lepidoptera is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the lepidoptera's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Delerium Healing.*** As an action, the lepidoptera can touch a delerium shard to regain 10 (3d6) Hit Points. Once the lepidoptera has used a delerium shard in this way, the shard can't be used in this manner again for 24 hours.  
>
>***Energy Form.*** The lepidoptera can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so, and it can enter a creature's space and stop there. The first time it enters a creature's space on a turn, that creature is moved 20 feet in a random direction.  
>
>***Fully Contaminated.*** The lepidoptera is immune to Contamination and has Advantage on saving throws against contaminated spells. The lepidoptera may cast contaminated spells even though it does not gain Contamination levels.  
>
>***Incomprehensible Vision.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the lepidoptera and can see it is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Staggered condition until the start of its next turn. *Success:* The target is transported to the Border Ethereal. If the target is already on the Border Ethereal, they are transported to the Material Plane instead.  
>Unless surprised, a creature can avert their eyes to avoid the saving throw at the start of their turn. If the creature does so, it can't see the lepidoptera until the start of its next turn. If the creature looks at the lepidoptera in the meantime, that creature must immediately make the saving throw.  
>
>***Multidimensional Presence.*** If the lepidoptera is reduced to 0 Hit Points but hasn't taken damage from a source on both the Material Plane and the Border Ethereal in the last round, it drops to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>***Phototaxis.*** If a source of Bright Light is within 100 feet of the lepidoptera, it attempts to move closer to the light on its turn.  
>
>***Unraveling Reality.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation originating from the lepidoptera when it dies. *Success:* The target is transported from the Material Plane to the Border Ethereal, or vice versa.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lepidoptera makes two Fractal Wing attacks.  
>
>***Fractal Wing.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 35 (5d10 + 7) Force damage.  
>
>***Tessellation (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the lepidoptera that doesn't have Total Cover. *Failure:* 44 (8d10) Force damage, and the target begins its next turn by moving up to its Speed in a random direction.  
>If the target has the Staggered condition, it instead takes no damage, that condition ends, and the target has the Incapacitated condition (save ends). While Incapacitated, the target mutters rhymes in a singsong voice and dances to an unheard rhythm, moving up to its Speed in a random direction at the start of each of its turns.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Criss Cross.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 60 feet of the lepidoptera touches it or hits it with an attack. *Response—**Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 17, the attacker. *Failure:* The attack deals no damage and the attacker is transported from the Material Plane to the Border Ethereal, or vice versa.
