>## Panjaian Gormadraugon
>*Medium Elemental, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 28)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +5, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Intimidation +5, Perception +4, Persuasion +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Fire, Lightning, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Primordial and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Ignite.*** A creature or object that takes cold damage from the panjaian gormadraugon ignites. Until the target takes Fire damage, it takes 3 (1d6) Cold damage at the start of each of its turns.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The panjaian gormadraugon regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the gormadraugon takes Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn. The gormadraugon dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The panjaian gormadraugon makes two attacks, using Slam or Hurl Coldfire in any combination.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (2d6 + 3) Cold damage, and the target is subjected to Ignite.  
>
>***Hurl Coldfire.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +6, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Cold damage, and the target is subjected to Ignite.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The panjaian gormadraugon casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Constitution as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Faerie Fire*  
>1/day: *Wall of Ice*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Coldfire Crisis.*** The panjaian gormadraugon causes a 30-foot Emanation originating from it to become icy Difficult Terrain. Nonmagical flames in that area go out. Creatures of the gormadraugon's choice that start their turn in that area lose their Resistance to Cold damage or treat their Immunity to Cold damage as Resistance. This loss of Resistance or Immunity lasts until the start of the creature's next turn. The Emanation lasts until the gormadraugon becomes Incapacitated, dies, or uses an action or Bonus Action to end it.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Prismatic Flash.*** *Trigger:* The panjaian gormadraugon fails a saving throw. *Response:* The gormadraugon rerolls the saving throw. If it succeeds, each creature within 30 feet of the gormadraugon is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn.
