>## Ice Gargoyle
>*Medium Elemental, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 20 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|6 (-2)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Cold, Poison; Exhaustion, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Primordial (Aquan, Auran)
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** The gargoyle doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gargoyle makes two attacks, using Claw or Ice Dart in any combination.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Ice Dart.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Piercing damage.
