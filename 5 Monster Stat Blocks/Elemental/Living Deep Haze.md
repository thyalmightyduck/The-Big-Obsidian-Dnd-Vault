___
>## Living Deep Haze
>*Large Elemental*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d10 + 24)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 90 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +11 (21)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Deep Speech
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Deep Haze.*** A creature that enters the same space as the elemental or starts its turn there is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level.  
>
>***Fog Form.*** The elemental can enter a Hostile creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.
