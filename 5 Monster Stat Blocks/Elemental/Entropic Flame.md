___
>## Entropic Flame
>*Large Elemental*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 91 (14d10 + 14)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Deep Speech
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Enduring Flame.*** Flames within 10 feet of the elemental can't be extinguished, and the Burning condition can't end for creatures within 10 feet of the elemental.  
>
>***Fire Form.*** The elemental can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.  
>
>***Illumination.*** The elemental sheds Bright Light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light in an additional 30 feet.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes three Dancing Flame attacks.  
>
>***Dancing Flame.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Fire damage, and the target has the Burning condition (3d6 Fire).  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Flickerjump.*** The elemental teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 60 feet.
