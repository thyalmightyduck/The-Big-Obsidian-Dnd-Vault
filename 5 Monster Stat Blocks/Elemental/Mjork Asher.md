___
>## Mjork Asher
>*Large Elemental, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|7 (-2)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Thunder
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Primordial (Ignan), Primordial (Terran)
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blinding Soot.*** A creature that takes Fire damage from the mjork asher's attacks is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded condition until the end of the mjork asher's next turn.  
>
>***Fiery Body.*** A creature that touches the mjork asher or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 7 (2d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Smoke Sense.*** A mjork asher in contact with smoke has Blindsight in that smoke's area, to a range of 120 feet.  
>
>***Smoke Shroud.*** While the mjork asher isn't Bloodied, it emits a 20-foot Emanation of smoke. The smoke spreads around corners and leaves its area Heavily Obscured. Even while the mjork asher is Bloodied, tendrils of smoke extend up to 60 feet away, providing mjorks in the area with Smoke Sense. The Emanation is suppressed by strong wind (like that created by *Gust of Wind*).  
>
>***Water Susceptibility.*** For every 5 feet the mjork asher moves in water, or for every gallon of water splashed on it, the mjork asher takes 1 Cold damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mjork asher makes two attacks, using Slam and Hurl Soot in any combination.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Fire damage, and the target is subjected to Blinding Soot.  
>
>***Hurl Soot.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +5, ranged 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 12 (3d6 + 2) Fire damage, and the target is subjected to Blinding Soot.  
>
>***Create Sootlings (1/Day).*** The mjork asher creates a 20-foot radius Sphere of smoke centered on a point it can sense within 60 feet. The Sphere spreads around corners and leaves its area Heavily Obscured. The smoke lasts until the start of the mjork asher's next turn or until dispersed by a strong wind.  
>If the smoke isn't dispersed before the duration ends, a mjork sootling swarm forms in a space in the area, favoring a space occupied by a Hostile creature. The swarm takes its turn just after the mjork asher and obeys the mjork asher's verbal commands (no Action required). If issued no commands, it uses its turns to attack Hostile creatures.
