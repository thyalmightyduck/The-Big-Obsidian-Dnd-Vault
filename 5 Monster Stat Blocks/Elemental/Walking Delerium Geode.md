>## Walking Delerium Geode
>*Large Elemental*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 126 (12d10 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|8 (-1)|20 (+5)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Radiant, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Deep Speech
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Death Burst.*** The elemental explodes when it dies and it hasn't taking Force, Radiant, or Thunder damage since its last turn. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the elemental. *Failure:* 42 (12d6) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level. *Failure or Success:* The explosion triggers a random Arcane Anomalies (see Appendix C).  
>
>***Earth Glide.*** The elemental can burrow through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone. While doing so, the elemental doesn't disturb the material it moves through.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two Shard Slam attacks.  
>
>***Shard Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the target gains one Contamination level.
