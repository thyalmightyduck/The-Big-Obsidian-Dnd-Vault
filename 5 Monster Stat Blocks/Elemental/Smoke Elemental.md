___
>## Smoke Elemental
>*Large Elemental, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d10 + 12)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 90 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Primordial (Auran, Ignan)
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aversion to Wind.*** If the elemental starts its turn within a strong wind (such as one created by the *Gust of Wind* spell), it makes a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the elemental has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its turn.  
>
>***Smoke Form.*** The elemental can enter a creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two Smoky Tendril attacks.  
>
>***Smoky Tendril.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) Fire damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Asphyxiate (Recharge 4–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature in the elemental's space. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Fire damage, and the target gains 1 Exhaustion level. All Exhaustion levels gained from this Bonus Action are removed after 1 minute.
