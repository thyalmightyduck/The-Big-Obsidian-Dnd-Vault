___
>## Panjaian Ilharan
>*Medium Elemental, Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Deception +3, Perception +4, Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Lightning, Thunder
>- **Gear** *Dagger*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Primordial and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The panjaian ilharan makes four Dagger attacks. It can draw daggers as part of this action.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Gift of Flight (1/Day).*** The panjaian ilharan touches up to four willing creatures. Each target gains a Flying Speed of 30 feet for 1 hour. When the effect ends, the target descends at a rate of 60 feet per round for 1 minute. If it touches the ground before the effect ends, it takes no falling damage and lands on its feet.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The panjaian ilharan casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Mage Hand* (the hand is invisible)  
>3/day each: *Gust of Wind*, *Invisibility* (self only)  
>
>***Storm Wave (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 6 (2d4 + 1) Lightning damage plus 6 (2d4 + 1) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed up to 15 feet to the edge of the Cone. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Returning Wind.*** The panjaian ilharan chooses up to four objects it can see within 60 feet. The objects must not be worn or carried, and can weigh up to 5 pounds combined. The objects fly up to 60 feet toward the ilharan, delivered into its hands or into open containers within 5 feet of it.
