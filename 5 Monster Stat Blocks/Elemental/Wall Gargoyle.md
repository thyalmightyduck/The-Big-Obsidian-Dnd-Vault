___
>## Wall Gargoyle
>*Medium Elemental*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 52 (7d8 + 21)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|6 (-2)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Immunities** Poison; Exhaustion, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the gargoyle remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an inanimate statue.  
>
>***Faultless Tracker.*** The creature that caused the gargoyle to animate becomes its quarry. The gargoyle knows the direction and distance to its quarry as long the quarry remains within Drakkenheim or until the gargoyle is destroyed.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If the wall gargoyle is destroyed, it reforms with all of its Hit Points in 1 hour unless a *Disintegrate* spell is cast on its remains.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gargoyle makes one Bite attack and one Claw attack.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage.
