>## Dark Mist Stalker
>*Large Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 136 (16d10 + 48)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|21 (+5)|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Perception +6, Stealth +9, Survival +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Elven, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Camouflage.*** The dark mist stalker can Hide while Lightly Obscured by foliage, heavy rain, mist, or other natural phenomena. If it misses with a ranged attack while it has the Invisible condition, it does not lose that condition.  
>
>***Dead Eye.*** The dark mist stalker's attacks with Ranged weapons ignore Half Cover and Three Quarters Cover, and score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.  
>
>***Hunter's Stride.*** The dark mist stalker ignores Difficult Terrain.  
>
>***Night Stalker.*** The dark mist stalker treats Darkness within the range of its Darkvision as Bright Light.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The dark mist stalker makes three attacks, using Skinning Knife or Hunting Bow in any combination.  
>
>***Skinning Knife.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) sSlashing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Bleeding condition.  
>
>***Hunting Bow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Piercing damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet straight away from the dark mist stalker  
>
>***Aim.*** The dark mist stalker chooses a creature it can see and maintains Concentration. While doing so, creatures have Advantage on attack rolls against the dark mist stalker. If the dark mist stalker is still maintaining Concentration when it makes its next Hunting Bow attack against the target, the attack has Advantage and deals an extra 27 (6d8) Piercing damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Skirmisher.*** *Trigger:* A Hostile creature moves within 5 feet of the dark mist stalker. *Response:* The dark mist stalker makes a Skinning Knife attack against the triggering creature, then moves up to 20 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
