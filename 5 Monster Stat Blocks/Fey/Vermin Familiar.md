___
>## Vermin Familiar
>*Tiny Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 31 (7d4 + 14)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 30 ft. (Centipede or Weasel only), Climb 30 ft., Fly 60 ft. (Pigeon only)
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +0, Cha +4
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Perception +5, Stealth +8
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft. (Pigeon only), Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft. (Centipede only), Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Disparate Incarnation.*** The familiar incarnates in a body similar to a pest: Centipede, Pigeon, or Weasel, which determines other traits and actions the familiar has.  
>
>***Fear of Canines.*** If the familiar can see a dog, wolf, or other canine at the start of its turn, it has the Frightened condition until the start of its next turn. While Frightened in this way, it has Disadvantage on saving throws.  
>
>***Vermin Telepathy.*** The familiar can magically control Beasts and swarms that match its incarnation (centipedes, pigeons, weasels, giant versions of each, as well as **Boogleswarms**, **Skitterswarms**, and **Squabswarms**) within 120 feet of itself, using a special telepathy.  
>
>***Flyby (Pigeon Only).*** The familiar doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>***Spider Climb (Centipede Only).*** The familiar can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The familiar makes two Rend attacks.  
>
>***Invisibility.*** The familiar casts *Invisibility* on itself, requiring no spell components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Incarnated Assault (Recharge 5–6).*** The familiar uses the option that matches its incarnation.  
>
>***Confounding Scratch (Weasel Only).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature the familiar can see within 5 feet. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage, and the target is affected by the *Confusion* spell until the start of the familiar's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Infectious Sting (Centipede Only).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature the familiar can see within 5 feet. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the familiar's next turn. While Poisoned in this way, the target has the Paralyzed condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Terrifying Flutter (Pigeon Only).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature the familiar can see within 5 feet. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of the familiar's next turn. The Frightened target uses its movement on its turn to get as far away as possible from the familiar, moving by the safest route. *Success:* Half damage only.
