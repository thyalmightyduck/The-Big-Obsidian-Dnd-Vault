___
>## Mirror Haunt
>*Medium Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 130 (20d8 + 40)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7
>- **Skills** Perception +8, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning
>- **Damage Resistances** Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Radiant
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Common plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The mirror haunt can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object. If a creature within 30 feet of it takes a Utilize action to show the mirror haunt the haunt's own reflection, this trait is suppressed for 1 minute.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The mirror haunt has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mirror haunt makes two Mind Slash attacks.  
>
>***Mind Slash.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Mirror Step (1/Day).*** The mirror haunt turns a normal mirror into a portal between its current location and a similar location on the Feywild, the Material Plane, or the Shadowfell. The portal remains open for 1 minute, and the portal's destination is visible through it.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Twisted Reflection (Recharge 5–6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 16, up to two creatures the mirror haunt can see within 30 feet of itself. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Restrained condition until the end of the mirror haunt's next turn. If the mirror haunt is within 30 feet of a portal created with its Mirror Step, it can also teleport the target to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet of the other side of the portal. *Success:* Half damage only.
