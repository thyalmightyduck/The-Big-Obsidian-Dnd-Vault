___
>## The Tall Man
>*Huge Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 322 (28d12 + 140)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +18 (28)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|22 (+6)|20 (+5)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +10, Con +11, Wis +9, Cha +11
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +12, Perception +15, Stealth +18
>- **Immunities** Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 25
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 20 (XP 25,000, or 33,000 in lair; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day, or 5/Day in Lair).*** If the Tall Man fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The Tall Man has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Memory's Dance.*** As a Bonus Action, a creature can perform some steps from a dance of the Tall Man's court and make a DC 20 Charisma (Performance) check. On a success, the Tall Man takes 20 Force damage and has Disadvantage on D20 Tests until the start of the creature's next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Tall Man makes two Claw attacks and uses Spellcasting to cast *Vicious Mockery* (4d6 Psychic damage).  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) Psychic damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) from one of two claws, and has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The Tall Man casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19):  
>
>At will: *Invisibility*, *Minor Illusion*, *Vicious Mockery* (4d6 Psychic damage)  
>2/day: *Darkness*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Elusive Disguise.*** The Tall Man magically transforms into a Small or Medium Humanoid, or back to his true form. His statistics, other than his size, are the same in each form. Any equipment he is wearing or carrying changes to accommodate his new form. He can take on any features he chooses, but he is always unnaturally tall for whatever guise he takes. He returns to his true form if he dies.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Evasive Blink.*** *Trigger:* The Tall Man takes damage. *Response:* The Tall Man has Resistance to the triggering damage and teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Spellcasting.** The Tall Man uses Spellcasting.
>- **Strike.** The Tall Man makes one Claw attack, then can't take this action again until the start of his next turn.
>- **Teleport.** The Tall Man teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space he can see. When he teleports, he can force a creature he is grappling to succeed on a DC 18 Charisma saving throw or teleport with him, appearing in the closest unoccupied space to the Tall Man's destination.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Tall Man's lair is changed by his presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Deepening Shadows.** Within 1 mile of the lair, shadows stretch far beyond what they should. Creatures can find a sufficiently shadowy spot to hide in, even without cover, 50 percent of the time.
>- **Too Quiet.** Sounds are muffled within 1 mile of the lair, halving the distance at which sounds can be heard.
>
>If the Tall Man dies or moves his lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
