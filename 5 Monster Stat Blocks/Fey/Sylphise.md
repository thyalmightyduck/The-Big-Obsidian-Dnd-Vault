___
>## Sylphise
>*Medium Fey (Skirmisher), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 77 (14d8 + 14)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|20 (+5)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|22 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +3, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +7, Perception +3, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from mundane attacks
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish, Sylvan, telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fey Countenance (1/Turn).*** When a creature who can see Sylphise targets her with an attack, power, or spell, the creature must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be blinded until the start of their next turn (no action required).  
>
>***Speak with Beasts and Plants.*** Sylphise can communicate with Beasts and Plants as if they shared a language.  
>
>***Supernatural Resistance.*** Sylphise has advantage on saving throws against powers, spells, and other supernatural effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Sylphise makes three Claw attacks or uses Sibilant Whispers twice.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) psychic damage.  
>
>***Sibilant Whispers.*** One creature Sylphise can see within 60 feet of her must succeed on a DC 16 Charisma saving throw or take 10 (3d6) psychic damage.  
>
>***Etherealness.*** Sylphise magically enters the Ethereal Plane from the Mundane World, or vice versa.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Too Slow!.*** If a creature misses Sylphise with an attack, she can move up to her speed without provoking opportunity attacks from that creature.
