>## Mothfolk
>*Medium Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 120 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Perception +5
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Radiant
>- **Damage Resistances** Piercing, Slashing, and Bludgeoning from nonmagical attacks
>- **Immunities** Psychic
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Disaster Sense.*** The mothfolk has a natural understanding of when disaster may strike. The mothfolk has Advantage on Initiative rolls and Dexterity saving throws against effects it can see. The mothfolk can't be Surprised.  
>
>***Fey Ancestry.*** The mothfolk has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed condition, and magic can't put it to sleep.  
>
>***Flyby.*** The mothfolk doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mothfolk makes two Kick attacks.  
>
>***Kick.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Mothfolk Curse.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature the mothfolk can see within 300 feet (a Blinded creature automatically succeeds). *Success:* The target is immune to the Mothfolk Curse of all mothfolk for 24 hours. *Failure:* The target is cursed. While cursed, the target the Charmed and Frightened conditions, treating any mothfolk as the effect's source. The target's eyes turn red, and it gains the following compulsion: "Get me away from those monsters hiding everywhere!"  
>After 1 minute, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success but making the curse's duration unlimited on a failure. A *Remove Curse*, *Lesser Restoration*, or similar spell removes the curse.
