___
>## Nacambraan Sorcerer
>*Medium Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 117 (18d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +5, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +4, Deception +6, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** One national language, Abyssal, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Nacambraan Dagger.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) Piercing damage plus 11 (2d10) Poison damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The nacambraan sorcerer casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14, +6 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Mage Armor* (included above), *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Bestow Curse*, *Hold Person*, *Invisibility*, *Ray of Sickness*, *Stinking Cloud*  
>1/day each: *Blight*, *Cloudkill*, *Eyebite*  
>
>***Toxic Splash.*** Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 14, each creature in a 5-foot radius Sphere. *Failure:* 5 (1d10) Acid damage plus 5 (1d10) Poison damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (2/Day).*** The nacambraan sorcerer casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>***Quick Cantrip.*** The nacambraan sorcerer uses Toxic Splash or casts an at-will spell from Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell (2/Day).*** The nacambraan sorcerer casts *Counterspell* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
