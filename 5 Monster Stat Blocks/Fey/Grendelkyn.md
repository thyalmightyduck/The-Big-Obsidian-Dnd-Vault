___
>## Grendelkyn
>*Large Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 76 (8d10 + 32)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|17 (+3)|19 (+4)|9 (-1)|15 (+2)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +6
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Immunities** Cold; Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 90 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The grendelkyn can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Shadow Armor.*** The grendelkyn is surrounded by a writhing mantle of shadows. This mantle adds 3 to its AC (included in its stats) and grants it Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in Darkness or Dim Light.  
>
>***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the grendelkyn has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls, and it loses its Shadow Armor. Additionally, it cannot use its Shadow Snare Action, and any existing Shadow Snare melts.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The grendelkyn makes one Bite and one Claws attack. It can replace its Claws attack with Shadow Snare.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Piercing damage. On a Critical Hit, the target has the Frightened condition until the end of the grendelkyn's next turn.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (1d10 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Shadow Snare.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) Cold damage, and the target is wrapped in shadowy tendrils extending from the grendelkyn's body, and the target is subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions. A creature can make a new Dexterity Saving Throw at the end of each of its turns to free itself. Otherwise, it remains ensnared by the tendrils for 1 minute. The Shadow Snare also vanishes if it is exposed to sunlight, the grendelkyn dies or has the Incapacitated condition, or it chooses to use Shadow Snare against a new target.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shadow Port.*** A grendelkyn in an area of Dim Light or Darkness can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see in Dim Light or Darkness. If the grendelkyn teleports while it has a creature trapped in a Shadow Snare, that creature also teleports 30 feet, to an unoccupied space within 10 feet of the grendelkyn that is in Dim Light or Darkness. If no such space is available, the grendelkyn releases the trapped creature when it teleports.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Frenzied Shriek.*** *Trigger:* The grendelkyn is Bloodied. *Response:* All creatures within 20 feet of the grendelkyn make a *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition for 1 minute. A creature affected by the shriek can make a new Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns to end the effect on itself.
