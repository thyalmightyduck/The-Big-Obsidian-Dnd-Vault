>## Bone Trader
>*Medium Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12, 15 (with Bone Armor)
>- **Hit Points** 22 (5d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +2
>- **Skills** Animal Handling +4, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Sylvan, two other languages
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Speak with Beasts.*** The bone trader can communicate with Beasts as if they shared a language.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bone Club.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Skelesuck.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10. *Failure:* One of the target's bones is sucked out. The target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by the amount of piercing damage it took until magical healing is applied.  
>
>***Fey Charm.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, one Beast, Fey, or Humanoid the bone trader can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition, regarding the bone trader as a trusted friend to be heeded and protected. Although the target isn't under the bone trader's control, it takes the bone trader's requests or actions in the most favorable way. *Success:* The target is immune to the bone trader's Fey Charm for 24 hours.  
>Each time the bone trader or its allies do anything harmful to the target, it repeats its save, ending the effect on itself and becoming immune on a success. Otherwise, the effect lasts 24 hours or until the bone trader dies, is on a different plane of existence from the target, or ends the effect as a Bonus Action.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bone Armor (1/Day).*** While in its lair, the bone trader can summon a temporary suit of bone armor that lasts for 10 minutes or until dismissed using this Bonus Action. While wearing its bone armor, the bone trader has Advantage on Wisdom saving and Charisma (Intimidation) checks.
