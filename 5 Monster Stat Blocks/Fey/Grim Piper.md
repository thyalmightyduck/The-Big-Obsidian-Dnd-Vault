>## Grim Piper
>*Medium Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Performance +5, Stealth +5
>- **Gear** *Dagger*, *Flute*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** Any three languages
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The grim piper makes two Dagger attacks. It can replace one attack with Cruel Notes.  
>
>***Dagger.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Cruel Notes.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 30 feet that is Charmed by the grim piper's Luring Melody. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Psychic damage, and the target has Disadvantage on saving throws for 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Luring Melody.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, each living creature in a 30-foot Emanation that can hear the grim piper. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition. The grim piper must take a Bonus Action on its subsequent turns to continue playing. It can stop playing at any time. The song ends if the grim piper has the Incapacitated condition.  
>While Charmed, the target has the Incapacitated condition. If the target is more than 5 feet from the grim piper, it must approach on each of its turns until it is within 5 feet. The target takes the most direct route and doesn't avoid danger, but before moving into damaging terrain, it can repeat the save. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. If the target succeeds on its save or the effect ends for it, it is immune to the grim piper's Luring Melody for 24 hours.
