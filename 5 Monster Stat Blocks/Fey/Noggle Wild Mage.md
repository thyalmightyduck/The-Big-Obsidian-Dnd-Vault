___
>## Noggle Wild Mage
>*Small Fey, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 22 (4d6 + 8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +4, Cha +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Bloodied Wild Magic.*** While Bloodied, the noggle has a Fly Speed of 30 feet and it can choose for any damage it deals to be Force damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The noggle makes two attacks, using Noggling Stick or Befuddling Ray in any combination.  
>
>***Noggling Stick.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Befuddling Ray.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Psychic damage, and the target subtracts 1d4 from the next saving throw it makes before the end of the noggle's next turn.
