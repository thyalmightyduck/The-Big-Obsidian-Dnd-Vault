___
>## Horror-out-the-Box
>*Medium Fey, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Climb 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +7, Athletics +4, Perception +4, Performance +6, Sleight of Hand +7, Stealth +7
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** One national language, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Spring-Heeled Horror.*** The horror-out-the-box uses its movement to jump up to 50 feet. It takes no falling damage from these jumps.  
>
>### Actions
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 30 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Force damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Leap.*** The horror-out-the-box takes the Dash action.  
>
>### Reactions
>The horror-out-the-box can take up to three Reactions per round but only one per turn.
>
>***Thrashing Limbs.*** *Trigger:* A creature moves into, out of, or within the horror-out-the-box's reach. *Response:* The horror-out-the-box makes a Slam attack against the triggering creature.
