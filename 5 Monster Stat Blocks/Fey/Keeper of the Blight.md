___
>## Keeper of the Blight
>*Large Fey (Druid), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 97 (13d10 + 26)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, Medicine +8, Stealth +6
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Blight Dependent.*** If the keeper is targeted by an effect that ends the Poisoned condition, it takes 10 (3d6) Force damage and has Disadvantage on D20 Tests until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Fungal Step.*** The keeper ignores Difficult Terrain caused by plants or fungi.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The keeper makes two attacks, using Blight Staff or Spore Spray in any combination, and it uses Rotting Ritual.  
>
>***Blight Staff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The keeper casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Druidcraft*, *Ray of Sickness*  
>1/day: *Blight*  
>
>***Spore Spray.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) Poison damage. If the target is a creature, it has the Poisoned condition until the end of the keeper's next turn.  
>
>***Rotting Ritual.*** The keeper magically creates one of the following effects of its choice. It can't use the same effect twice in a row.  
>
>***Frenzying Spores.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, a creature the keeper can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Psychic damage, and the target must immediately take a Reaction to make a melee or ranged attack against a creature of the keeper's choice that is within reach or range of the attack. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Blight Spawn.*** The keeper magically summons a Rotweaver (lacks a Hunting Horn) in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of itself. The rotweaver takes its turn immediately after the keeper on the same Initiative count, and it obeys the keeper. The rotweaver vanishes after 1 minute, or when it or the keeper dies.  
>
>***Devouring Fungus.*** Life-sapping fungus sprouts from the ground in a 60-foot radius centered on the keeper.  
>The area is Difficult Terrain, and a creature has the Poisoned condition while within the area. The fungus remains for 1 minute, until the keeper uses this effect again, or until it dies.
