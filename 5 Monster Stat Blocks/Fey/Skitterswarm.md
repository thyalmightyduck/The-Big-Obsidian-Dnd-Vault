___
>## Skitterswarm
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Fey, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 19 (3d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 20 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Common and Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Body Burrowing.*** If the swarm can see the corpse of a Humanoid at the start of its turn, it must move up to its Speed toward the corpse. Once it enters the corpse's space, it must remain there for 1 hour, after which time the corpse's flesh putrefies.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny centipede. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Vile Vermin.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, any creature that starts its turn in the swarm's space. *Failure:* The target contracts a magical contagion. While afflicted, the target can't regain Hit Points, and it repeats the save when it finishes a Long Rest or when targeted by an effect that ends the Poisoned condition, recovering from the contagion on a success. *Success:* The target is immune to the Vile Vermin trait of all skitterswarms for 24 hours.  
>
>### Actions
>***Infesting Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d6) Piercing damage, or 3 (1d6) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied. If the target is a creature, a centipede infests its body. While infested, the target takes 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns. The infestation ends when the target regains Hit Points or is targeted by an effect that ends the Poisoned condition.
