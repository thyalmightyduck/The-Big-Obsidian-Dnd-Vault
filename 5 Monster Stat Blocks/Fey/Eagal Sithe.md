___
>## Eagal Sithe
>*Tiny Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 88 (16d4 + 48)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +8 (18)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|20 (+5)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Cha +6
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Insight +5, Perception +5, Persuasion +6, Stealth +8
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Sylvan, plus one national language, telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Psychic Feed.*** When the eagal sithe deals Psychic damage to a creature, it regains an amount of Hit Points equal to the Psychic damage dealt. Excess healing is gained as Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The eagal sithe makes two Bite attacks. It can replace one attack with Emotional Torment.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) Piercing damage plus 7 (3d4) Psychic damage. If the target is a Humanoid, it is subjected to the following effect. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition. The target regards the eagal sithe as a trusted friend to be heeded and protected, taking its requests or actions in the most favorable way.  
>If the eagal sithe or its allies harm the target, it repeats its saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 24 hours. The effect ends early if the eagal sithe dies, is on a different plane than the target, or ends it as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Emotional Torment.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 60 feet that is Charmed by the eagal sithe. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Psychic damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The eagal sithe casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Vicious Mockery* (level 17 version; can be telepathic)  
>3/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *Disguise Self*  
>1/day: *Invisibility* (self only)  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Emotional Manipulation.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 60 feet that is Charmed by the eagal sithe. *Failure:* The target must use its Reaction to make a weapon attack or cast a damaging cantrip against a target of the eagal sithe's choice. If the attack hits, it does an extra 7 (2d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Shadow Stealth.*** In Dim Light or Darkness, the eagal sithe can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action.
