>## Potbelly
>*Huge Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d12 + 24)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|8 (-1)|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Survival +3
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 9
>- **Languages** Giant, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The potbelly makes two Slam attacks. It can replace one attack with Boil.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, the potbelly can halve the attack's damage and cause the target to have the Grappled condition (escape DC 16). The potbelly can grapple up to two creatures at a time.  
>
>***Boil.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16, one Medium or smaller creature Grappled by the potbelly. *Failure:* The grapple ends and the target is plopped into the potbelly's belly pot. The target has the Restrained condition and has Total Cover from effects that originate outside the potbelly. It takes 18 (4d8) Fire damage immediately and at the start of each of the potbelly's turns. The potbelly can have up to two creatures boiling at a time.  
>The target can take an action to escape with a successful DC 16 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check, landing in a space within 5 feet of the potbelly. If the potbelly dies, the target is no longer Restrained and can escape from the corpse using 10 feet of its movement.  
>
>***Snack.*** The potbelly grabs the corpse of a Fey, Giant, or Humanoid that died no longer than 1 minute ago in its belly pot and takes a bite, regaining 25 Hit Points. The potbelly can gain this benefit only once from each corpse.
