>## Ketgrinn
>*Medium Fey, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Deception +8, Perception +2, Persuasion +8, Stealth +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan; telepathy 100 ft.
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The ketgrinn has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Mimicry.*** The ketgrinn can mimic any voice it has heard, or a voice it has stolen (see Consume Voice below). A creature that hears the voice can tell it's an imitation with a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check. The DC increases to 19 for a voice the ketgrinn has stolen.  
>
>***Riddle Bound.*** As an Influence action, a creature that poses a riddle to the ketgrinn and succeeds on a DC 16 Charisma (Deception) check gives the ketgrinn the Incapacitated condition until the start of the creature's next turn. Each riddle posed increases the DC for future riddles by 1 for 24 hours.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The ketgrinn makes two Claw attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Consume Voice.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage, and if the target is a creature, it has Disadvantage on the next saving throw the ketgrinn forces it to make before the end of the ketgrinn's next turn.  
>
>***Consume Voice.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 5 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Psychic damage, and the ketgrinn steals the target's voice. The target can't speak or make any verbal sound while its voice is stolen.  
>The target repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If it fails three of these saving throws, the loss is permanent, and its voice only returns when the ketgrinn dies. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Invisibility.*** The ketgrinn casts *Invisibility* on itself, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Teleport.*** The ketgrinn teleports up to 40 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
