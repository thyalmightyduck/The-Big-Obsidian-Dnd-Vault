___
>## Rage-Infected Bugbear
>*Medium Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +5, Intimidation +3, Perception +4, Survival +2
>- **Gear** *Greataxe*, *Hide Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Goblin
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Reckless Attack.*** At the start of its turn, the bugbear can gain Advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against it have Advantage until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Tormach's Rage.*** The bugbear has a disease called Tormach's Rage that changes its alignment to Chaotic Evil, gives it the Reckless Attack trait, and grants it an extra 2 (1d4) damage on Strengthbased attacks (included in the attack).  
>A bugbear that touches the rage-infected bugbear is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* The target contracts Tormach's Rage. Only powerful magic like a *Greater Restoration* spell or a special herbal concoction (see Salvage) can cure this disease.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is a bugbear, it is subjected to Tormach's Rage.  
>
>***Greataxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (2d12 + 1d4 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampage.*** After reducing a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, the bugbear moves up to half its Speed and makes a Bite attack.
