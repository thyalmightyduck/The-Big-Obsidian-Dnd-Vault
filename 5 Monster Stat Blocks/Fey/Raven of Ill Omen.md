>## Raven of Ill Omen
>*Small Fey, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 21 (6d6)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +4, Perception +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Sylvan and one other language
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Baneful Attack.*** The raven of ill omen has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature with a bane from Affect Fortunes.  
>
>***Flyby.*** The raven of ill omen doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>***Mimicry.*** The raven of ill omen can mimic voices and other sounds it has heard. A creature that hears the mimicked sounds can tell they are an imitation with a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The raven of ill omen makes one Beak attack and one Talons attack.  
>
>***Beak.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Affect Fortunes.*** The raven of ill omen chooses up to six creatures it can see within 60 feet. The raven assigns each creature a bane or boon for 1 minute, until the raven dies, or until it uses this Bonus Action again. These effects don't stack with similar effects, such as the Bane and Bless spells.  
>- **Boon.** The target can roll a d4 and add the result to each ability check and saving throw it makes for the duration.
>- **Bane.** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target must roll a d4 and subtract the result from each ability check and saving throw it makes for the duration.
