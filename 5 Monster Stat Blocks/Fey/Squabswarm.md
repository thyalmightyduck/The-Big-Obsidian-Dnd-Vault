___
>## Squabswarm
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Fey, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 19 (3d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|5 (-3)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Survival +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands Common and Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Compelled Delivery.*** As an Influence action, a creature can direct the swarm to deliver a letter or parcel (which the creature must provide) to a recipient the creature knows. The swarm has the Charmed condition until it delivers the item to the specified recipient or until it takes damage. While Charmed in this way, the swarm must deliver the item to the specified recipient if it knows where the recipient is.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny pigeon. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Unerring Delivery.*** If the swarm is tasked to deliver an item to a creature, the swarm knows the distance and direction to the creature while they are on the same plane.  
>
>### Actions
>***Peck.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (2d4) Piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied. If the swarm isn't Bloodied and the target is a creature, the target has the Charmed condition until the start of the swarm's next turn.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Many Eyes, One Voice (1/Day).*** *Trigger:* A creature who tasked the swarm to deliver an item and is on the same plane as the swarm takes an action to see through the swarm. *Response:* The swarm becomes a scrying sensor as if the creature cast *Scrying*. For the duration, the creature can speak through the swarm.
