___
>## Grief Eater
>*Small Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 35 (10d6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|5 (-3)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Stealth +4
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the grief eater remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a person cloaked in grey, sitting and clutching their legs.  
>
>***Grief Sense.*** The grief eater can sense a Frightened or despairing Humanoid or another grief eater within 60 feet. A grief eater using this sense knows the target's location and the direction of its movement.  
>
>***Light Sensitivity.*** While in Bright Light, the grief eater has Disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage, plus 2 (1d4) Psychic damage if the target is Frightened or despairing. The target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the Psychic damage taken, and the grief eater regains an equal number of Hit Points. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a Long Rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its Hit Point maximum to 0.  
>If a Humanoid is slain by this reduction and left unburied, a grief eater emerges from its corpse 24 hours later, provided its corpse is in Darkness.  
>
>***Wail.*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature within 30 feet. The target has Disadvantage on its save if it is despairing. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute. If the target succeeds on its saving throw or the effect ends for it, it is immune to the Wail of any grief eater for 24 hours.
