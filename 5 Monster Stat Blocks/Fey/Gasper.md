>## Gasper
>*Medium Fey, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 27 (5d8 + 5)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The gasper can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Submerged Invisibility.*** The gasper has the Invisible condition while underwater.  
>
>### Actions
>***Water Snare.*** *Melee Spell Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d6) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12) and is pulled 10 feet toward the gasper. While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition and can't breathe, and the gasper can't use Water Snare against another creature. A creature completely submerged while Grappled by the gasper takes 7 (2d6) Acid damage at the start of each of the gasper's turns.
