___
>## Selkie
>*Medium Fey, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12 in Fey form, 14 in seal form
>- **Hit Points** 82 (15d8 + 15)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., 20 ft. (in seal form), Swim 30 ft., Swim 50 ft. (in seal form)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Aquan, Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fey Resilience.*** The selkie has Advantage on saves to avoid or end the Charmed and Unconscious conditions.  
>
>***Hold Breath.*** The selkie can hold its breath for 30 minutes.  
>
>***Speak with Pinnipeds.*** The selkie can communicate with seals, sea lions, and walruses as if they shared a language.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The selkie makes one Bite attack and two Tail attacks, or it makes three Spear attacks.  
>
>***Bite (Seal Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 +2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Spear (Fey Form Only).*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Tail (Seal Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Change Shape.*** The selkie magically transforms into a Medium seal or back into its true form, which is Fey. Its statistics, other than its AC, size, and Speed, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying transforms with it. It reverts to its true form if it dies. If the selkie is in its true form, it can't use this Bonus Action unless it is wearing or carrying its bound sealskin.  
>
>***Charming Escape.*** The selkie takes the Dash or Disengage action. Each creature within 5 feet of a selkie when it takes the Disengage Action in this way must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. *Failure:* The creature has the Charmed condition until the end of the selkie's next turn.
