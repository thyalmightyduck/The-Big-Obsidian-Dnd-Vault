___
>## Magpie of Omens
>*Tiny Fey, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 25 (10d4)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|2 (-4)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** The magpie doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The magpie makes two Beak attacks.  
>
>***Beak.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Radiant damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bonded Voice.*** The magpie can form or dissolve a magical bond with a willing creature within 5 feet. While the bond lasts, the bonded creature can speak through the magpie using the creature's own voice and language, but with a birdlike lilt. The bond ends when the creature dies or if the magpie creates a new bond.  
>
>### Reactions
>***One for Sorrow (1/Day).*** *Trigger:* A creature within 30 feet of the magpie makes a D20 Test with Advantage. *Response:* The target rolls 1d6 and subtracts it from the total.  
>
>***Two for Joy (2/Day).*** *Trigger:* The magpie or another creature within 30 feet makes an ability check or a saving throw. *Response:* The target rolls 1d4 and adds it to the total.
