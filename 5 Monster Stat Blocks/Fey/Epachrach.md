>## Epachrach
>*Huge Fey, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 149 (13d12 + 65)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___

>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|24 (+7)|12 (+1)|21 (+5)|4 (-3)|13 (+1)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Plant Stride (Recharge 5–6).*** If within 5 feet of a Large or bigger area of plants, the epachrach can teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of a second Large or bigger area of plants that is within 60 feet of the previous area. These plants can be animate creatures, such as a treant, or magical, such as the plants the Entangle spell calls forth.  
>
>***Relentless (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** If the epachrach takes 30 damage or less that would reduce it to 0 Hit Points, it is reduced to 1 Hit Point instead.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The epachrach deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Tusk.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 25 (4d8 + 7) Slashing damage. If the epachrach moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition and the epachrach can make a Hooves attack against it as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. one Prone creature. *Hit:* 29 (4d10 + 7) Piercing damage.
