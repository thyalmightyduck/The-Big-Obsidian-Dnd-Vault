___
>## Puck
>*Tiny Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 14 (4d4 + 4)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|3 (-4)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Perception +3, Stealth +7
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Sylvan, telepathy 120 ft. (plants only)
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fey Evasion.*** If the puck is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a saving throw to take only half damage, the puck instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The puck has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Plant Bond.*** If the puck remains within 20 feet of a Huge or smaller plant for 8 hours, it can use special dust to form a bond with the plant, granting it sapience as if by the Awaken spell. The target gains the ability to understand Sylvan and becomes a puck version of an animated plant, such as a puck tree or puck shrub.  
>The puck can have only one plant Charmed in this way at a time. The condition ends if the puck harms the plant, and the plant can make a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw to end the condition if a request from the puck guarantees the plant's destruction. Plants freed from the charm leave the puck's grove, becoming inanimate again after 24 hours. Otherwise, the plant is Charmed by the puck for 30 days, after which the plant becomes the puck's willing servant.  
>The puck is emotionally bonded with each plant it has animated, Charmed, and enthralled. Provided such a plant is within 1 mile, the puck is aware when the plant takes damage and knows its location. If such a plant is destroyed, the puck has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn as it grieves.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spellcasting.*** The puck casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):  
>
>At will: *Dancing Lights*, *Druidcraft*, *Entangle*  
>3/day: *Phantasmal Force*  
>1/day each: *Bestow Curse*, *Charm Monster* (plant creatures only), *Detect Evil and Good*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Dispel Magic*, *Polymorph*  
>
>***Superior Invisibility.*** The puck magically becomes Invisible until its Concentration ends. Any equipment the puck wears or carries is Invisible with it.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Plant Manifestation.*** The puck causes a plantlike duplicate of itself to emerge from a Plant within 120 feet that is Charmed or enthralled by it. The puck can act through this duplicate until the end of its turn, at which point the duplicate disappears.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Uncanny Dodge.*** *Trigger:* A creature the puck can see hits it with an attack. *Response:* The puck halves the damage it takes from the attack.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 1. Immediately after another creature's turn, the puck can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The puck regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Illusionary Menace.** Six images of the puck appear in multiple locations, each moving and speaking independently during the puck's turn. At the start and end of the puck's turn, and one time during the puck's movement, it can teleport to the location of one of these images. The images remain until the next round.
>- **Hostile Nature.** Grasping vines and roots manifest in the lair. Strength or *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 10, each creature Hostile to the puck in the lair. *Failure:* The target is moved up to 20 feet to another unoccupied space in the lair and gains the Prone condition.
>- **Illusionary Menace.** Inanimate plants in the lair twist and sprout thorns. The area becomes Difficult Terrain to creatures Hostile to the puck until the next round, and are subject to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10, when a creature moves more than half its Speed (as dictated by the Difficult Terrain) in the area. *Failure:* The creature gains the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. *Success:* the creature is Immune to the Poisonous Plants effect for 24 hours.
>
>### Regional Effects
>A natural environment containing a puck's lair is warped by the puck's presence, which creates one or more of the following effects:
>- Plants within 1 mile of the lair darken in hue and look underwatered. Sap from these plants has a bloodlike tinge. This sap seeps from oddly shaped holes that resemble parts of despairing faces or wounds. Fruits are deformed and oddly colored but are otherwise normal in taste and nutrition. Traversing this area is hard and time consuming—roots trip, vines block the way, underbrush is dense and thorny.
>- Animals and other wild creatures within 1 mile of the lair are unnaturally aggressive, especially toward creatures hostile to the puck. However, animals are absent within 600 feet of the lair. The puck uses them to fertilize the grove
>- Creatures that spend 10 minutes or more within this area feel their negative emotions heightened. Effects that cause debilitating negative emotions double in duration. Effects that provide bravery, joy, hope, and similar emotions, such as the spells *Aid* or *Heroism*, halve in duration. A *Calm Emotions* spell ends either effect for that spell's normal duration.
