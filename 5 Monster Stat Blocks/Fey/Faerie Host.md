___
>## Faerie Host
>*Medium Fey*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 112 (15d8 + 45)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +8, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Deception +8, Persuasion +8, Stealth +7
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The host makes two Enchanted Strike attacks.  
>
>***Enchanted Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Illusory Assault (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the host. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of the host's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The host casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Disguise Self*, *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Invisibility*, *Major Image*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Faerie Step.*** The host teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Fey Escape.*** *Trigger:* The host takes damage. *Response:* The host has the Invisible condition and teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 60 feet. The host remains Invisible until the start of its next turn or until it makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.
