>## Braerog
>*Small Fey, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 18 (4d6 + 4)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 10 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|6 (-2)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fey Ancestry.*** The braerog has Advantage on saving throws made to avoid or end the Charmed condition, and magic can't put it to sleep.  
>
>### Actions
>***Beak.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Briar Patch (Recharge 6).*** Thorny vines sprout from the ground ina 20-foot Square starting from a point within 5 feet of the braerog. The patch is Difficult Terrain for 1 minute, until the braerog uses this action again, until the braerog dismisses the patch as a Bonus Action, or until the braerog has the Incapacitated condition or dies. The braerog chooses which creatures are affected by the patch when it appears.  
>*Strength Saving Throw:* DC 11, a creature that enters the area or starts its turn there. *Failure:* The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 11). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Tree Stride (1/Round).*** If within 5 feet of a Medium or larger tree, the braerog teleports to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of a second Medium or larger tree that is within 60 feet of the previous tree.
