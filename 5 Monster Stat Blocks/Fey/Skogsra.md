>## Skogsra
>*Medium Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 130 (20d8 + 40)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +6, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Insight +6, Perception +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The skogsra makes three Claw attacks. In place of one Claw attack, it can use Grasping Vines or Spellcasting.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Grasping Vines.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15 One Medium or smaller creature the skogsra can see within 15 feet. *Failure:* The target is pulled up to 10 feet toward the skogsra and has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15).  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The skogsra casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Friends*, *Thunderclap*  
>1/day each: *Call Lightning*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Disguise Self*, *Ice Storm*, *Wind Wall*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Hollow Trap.*** The skogsra pulls a creature with the Grappled condition into its hollow body. While held in this way, the target isn't Grappled but has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, and it takes 7 (2d6) Slashing damage and 7 (2d6) Poison damage at the start of each of its turns. If this damage reduces a Humanoid creature to 0 Hit Points, that creature is considered Stable and has the Paralyzed condition. This condition lasts for 12 hours or until the Paralyzed creature regains Hit Points.  
>The held creature has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the skogsra. While holding a creature within its body, the skogsra can't pull another creature into its hollow.  
>If the skogsra takes 15 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, it must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate that creature, which falls in a space within 5 feet of the skogsra and has the Prone condition.  
>If the skogsra dies, a held creature no longer has the Restrained or Paralyzed condition and can escape from the corpse using 5 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.
