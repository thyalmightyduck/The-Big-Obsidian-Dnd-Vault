>## Boogleswarm
>*Large swarm of Tiny Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 27 (5d10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Intimidation +5, Persuasion +5, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands Common but can only speak if the swarm has gathered at least one Humanoid tooth
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Mimicry (Only if the Swarm Can Speak).*** The swarm can speak in the voice of a creature whose tooth it has implanted (see the Bite action). Listeners must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check to discern the mimicry.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny **weasel**. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>***Sweet Tooth.*** If the swarm can see or smell sugar at the start of its turn, it must use all its movement to move closer to the sugar. If it reaches the sugar, the swarm has the Incapacitated condition until the start of its next turn as it consumes the sugar, and this trait doesn't function again until the swarm finishes a Short or Long Rest.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The swarm makes two Bite attacks that must have different targets.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (4d4) Piercing damage, or 5 (2d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied. If the target is a creature that has teeth, it must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or lose one of its teeth. The swarm immediately implants the tooth into one of its mouths. A creature that has already lost a tooth to this attack automatically succeeds on the saving throw.
