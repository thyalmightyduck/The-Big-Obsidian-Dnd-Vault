>## Rotweaver
>*Medium Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 38 (7d8 + 7)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fungal Step.*** The rotweaver ignores Difficult Terrain caused by plants or fungi.  
>
>***Hunting Horn.*** The rotweaver carries a hunting horn.  
>The horn has AC 16, HP 10, and Immunity to Necrotic, Poison, and Psychic damage.  
>
>***Iron Binding.*** If the rotweaver has iron wrapped around a part of its body, such as an iron bracelet, Manacles, or Chain (treat any of them as applying Manacles), the rotweaver can move, take an action, or a Bonus Action, but not all three.  
>
>### Actions
>***Staff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Call the Rot (Requires Hunting Horn; Recharge 5-6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The horn call is audible out to 300 feet, and any noncreature plants in the Cone die from fungus and rot.
