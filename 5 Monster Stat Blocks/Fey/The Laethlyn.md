___
>## The Laethlyn
>*Medium Fey, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 221 (34d8 + 68)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|22 (+6)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +10, Int +9, Wis +12, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +10, Animal Handling +12, History +9, Nature +9, Perception +12
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing attacks that aren't adamantine; Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Restrained, Stunned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 22
>- **Languages** Druidic, Primordial, Sylvan, three other languages
>- **Challenge** 17 (XP 18,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the Laethlyn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The Laethlyn has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Nature Speech.*** The Laethlyn can understand and be understood by all Beasts and Plants.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Laethlyn makes three Vine Strike attacks.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The Laethlyn casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20):  
>
>At will: *Barkskin*, *Commune with Nature*, *Detect Magic*  
>3/day each: *Entangle*, *Wall of Thorns*  
>1/day each: *Awaken*, *Heal*  
>
>***Vine Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Force damage. If another creature is within 5 feet of the target, the Laethlyn can attack both targets at once, using the same attack roll for both.  
>
>***Thorn Spray.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 20, each non-Plant in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 50 (8d10 + 6) Force damage, and the target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet for 1 minute or until the target receives magical healing. Further reductions to the creature's Speed from Thorn Spray are cumulative. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Slash.** The Laethlyn uses Vine Strike.
>- **Teleport.** The Laethlyn magically teleports, along with anything she is wearing or carrying, up to 90 feet to an unoccupied space she can see.
>- **Vine Capture.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 20, a creature the Laethlyn can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. The Laethlyn can capture two creatures at a time. The Laethlyn can't use this action again until the start of her next turn.
>- **Rejuvenate.** The Laethlyn roots themself in the ground beneath them, reducing their Speed to 0 until she teleports or takes an action to uproot themself. While rooted, healing energy surges into her, restoring 30 Hit Points at the start of each of her turns. Any Beast allies within 30 feet of the Laethlyn regain 30 Hit Points when she takes this action. The Laethlyn can't take this action again until the start of her next turn.
