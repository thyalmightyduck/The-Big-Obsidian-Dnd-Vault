>## Lurker in the Dark
>*Medium Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Wis +3
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Intimidation +5, Perception +3, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning
>- **Immunities** Grappled, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Common plus one other language
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Nightmarish Contortion.*** The lurker can fit into a space as small as a 1-foot Cube and can pass through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The lurker can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Ward of Innocence.*** The lurker can't attack or willingly move to a space within 10 feet of a creature holding a stuffed animal once owned by a child.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lurker makes two Claw attacks.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) from one of two claws. Until the grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Dark Teleport.*** While the lurker isn't in Bright Light, it teleports up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. If the destination space isn't in Bright Light, the lurker doesn't need to see it. A creature the lurker is grappling must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or teleport with the lurker to an unoccupied space closest to the lurker's destination.
