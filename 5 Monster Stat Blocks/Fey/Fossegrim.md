___
>## Fossegrim
>*Small Fey, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 82 (15d6 + 30)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Swim 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Deception +7, Perception +5, Performance +10
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The fossegrim can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The fossegrim has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The fossegrim makes two Discordant Chord attacks.  
>
>***Discordant Chord.*** *Melee  or Spell Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 feet, one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Thunder damage.  
>
>***Illusory Appearance.*** The fossegrim casts *Disguise Self*. It can appear as a Small or Medium Humanoid.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The fossegrim casts one of the following spells with its musical instrument, but requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Fog Cloud*, *Prestidigitation*  
>3/day each: *Charm Monster*, *Water Breathing*  
>1/day each: *Control Weather*  
>
>***Torrential Tune (2/day).*** The Fossegrim plays its musical instrument, conjuring a wave of water to crash down upon its enemies. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15 All creatures in a 20-foot Cone. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Bewildering Harmonics.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15 One creature the fossegrim can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target becomes confused until the end of its next turn. The target can't take Reactions and must roll 1d8 to determine what it does on its next turn:  
>- 1-4. The target does nothing.
>- 5-6. The target takes no action or Bonus Action and uses all its movement to move in a random direction.
>- 7-8. The target makes a melee attack against a random creature within its reach, or it does nothing if it can't make such an attack.
>
>***Ventriloquistic Step.*** The fossegrim teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Cacophonous Note (Recharge 5–6).*** *Trigger:* A creature succeeds on a d20 Test within 30 feet of the fossegrim. *Response:* The creature must roll again and take the new result.
