>## Vooran
>*Tiny Fey, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 2 (1d4)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Fire, Lightning, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Cold, Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Ethereal Sight.*** The vooran can see 60 feet into the Ethereal Plane when on other planes, and vice versa.  
>
>***Etherealness.*** The vooran lives entirely on the Ethereal Plane and can only enter other planes by possessing a Beast. It is not visible outside of the Ethereal Plane.  
>
>***Familiar.*** vooran can connect with another creature to serve as a familiar, forming a telepathic bond with its willing master. While the two are bonded, the master can sense what the vooran senses if they are within 1 mile of each other. When bonded, the master can see the vooran as a ghostly form.  
>
>***Incorporeal Movement.*** The vooran can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.  
>
>### Actions
>***Animal Possession (1/Day).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 12, one Beast of beast of CR 1/8 or lower that the vooran can see within 5 feet. *Failure:* The target is possessed by the vooran. The vooran disappears, and the target has the Incapacitated condition and loses control of its body. The vooran now controls the body, but the target retains awareness. The vooran can't be targeted by any attack, spell, or other effect, except ones that specifically target Fey. The vooran's game statistics are the same, except it uses the target's Speed, as well as the target's Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution modifiers.  
>The possession lasts for 1 hour, ending early if the body drops to 0 Hit Points or the vooran ends it as a Bonus Action. When the possession ends, the vooran is forced back into the Ethereal Plane, and the target is immune to this vooran's Animal Possession for 24 hours. If an animal is killed while the vooran possesses it, the vooran must succeed on a DC 10 Charisma saving throw or it is permanently destroyed.  
>*Success:* The target is immune to this vooran's Possession for 24 hours.
