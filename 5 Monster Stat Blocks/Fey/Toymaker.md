___
>## Toymaker
>*Medium Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +7
>- **Skills** Perception +7, Sleight of Hand +6, Survival +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The toymaker has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Naughty List.*** The toymaker knows if a Humanoid it can see has committed a serious crime and whether it has been punished for it.  
>
>***Winter Footing.*** The toymaker ignores Difficult Terrain imposed by ice or snow.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The toymaker makes three Claw attacks.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16).  
>
>***Beard Spines (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 26 (4d12) Piercing damage. *Failure:* Half damage.  
>
>***Bag of Toys (Recharges after a Toy is Destroyed).*** The toymaker reaches into its bag and pulls out a random toy: **Bloody Teddy**, **Box-Bound Jack**, **Dolly**, or **Marie O'nett**. It tosses the toy into an unoccupied space within 10 feet. The toy is the toymaker's ally and obeys the toymaker's commands, taking its turn just after the toymaker. If issued no commands, the toy attacks a Hostile creature.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The toymaker casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):  
>
>At will: *Enlarge/Reduce*, *Knock*, *Mending*  
>2/day: *Pass Without Trace*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Bag.*** *Trigger:* A creature Grappled by the toymaker fails to escape. *Response:* Strength or *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The creature is stuffed into the toymaker's bag. While in the bag, the target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions and has Total Cover from effects originating outside the bag.  
>When the toymaker uses Bag of Toys, creatures inside the bag are released, landing in unoccupied spaces within 5 feet of the toymaker with the Prone condition. The creatures are also released if the bag takes 25+ damage in a single turn from a creature inside it. If the bag is torn, the toymaker can't use Bag of Toys until the bag is repaired. If the toymaker dies, creatures inside the bag no longer have the Restrained condition and can escape from it using 10 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.
