>## Vermin Infestation
>*Medium swarm of Tiny Fey, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Cha +4
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Perception +5, Stealth +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Infighting.*** As an Influence action, a creature that insinuates that the components of the swarm are conspiring against each other and succeeds on a DC 15 Charisma (Deception) check deals 10 Necrotic damage to the swarm.  
>
>***Spider Climb.*** The swarm can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can fit through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Shred.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., *Hit:* 14 (4d4 + 4) Piercing damage, or 9 (2d4 + 4) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature in the swarm's space, the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14). The swarm can have one creature Grappled at a time. At the start of each of the Grappled target's turns, roll 1d6 to see which of the following effects it suffers:  
>- **1-2: Centipede Sting.** The target takes 4 (1d8) Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn.
>- **3-4: Pigeon Cry.** The target takes 10 (3d6) Psychic damage.
>- **5-6: Weasel Drain.** The target takes 5 (1d10) Necrotic damage, and its Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken.
