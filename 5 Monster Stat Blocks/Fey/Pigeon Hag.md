>## Pigeon Hag
>*Medium Fey, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 228 (24d8 + 120)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|19 (+4)|18 (+4)|22 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +9, Wis +9
>- **Skills** Deception +11, Insight +9, Perception +9, Sleight of Hand +7
>- **Immunities** Charmed
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Common, Primordial (Avian), Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Coven Magic.*** While within 30 feet of at least two hag allies, the hag can cast one of the following spells, requiring no Material components, using the spell's normal casting time, and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability: *Augury*, *Find Familiar*, *Identify*, *Locate Object*, *Lucky Charm* (lasts until the hag casts it again, or chooses to end it with no action required), or *Unseen Servant*. The hag must finish a Long Rest before using this trait to cast that spell again.  
>
>***Disarming Vanity.*** As an Influence action, a creature that flatters the hag forces it to succeed on a DC 17 Charisma saving throw or have the Incapacitated condition until the end of the hag's next turn.  
>
>***Harrying Wings.*** When the hag rolls Initiative, it is surrounded by fey spirits in the form of angry pigeons.  
>The hag's enemies within a 10-foot Emanation originating from it have Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls. This trait doesn't function if the hag has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The hag makes three Claw attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting (at will spells only).  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Agonizing Cackle (1/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 19, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* A target takes 45 (10d8) Psychic damage and has the Incapacitated condition until the start of the hag's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The hag casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19):  
>
>At will: *Command* (level 2 version), *Hold Person*, *Minor Illusion*, *Stinking Cloud*  
>1/day each: *Befuddlement*, *Dominate Person*, *Scrying*, *Word of Recall* (lair as sanctuary)  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shape-Shift.*** The hag shape-shifts into a Small or Medium Humanoid, or it returns to its true form. Other than its size, its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.
