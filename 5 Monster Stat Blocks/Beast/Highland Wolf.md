>- **Languages** —

>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2

>***Pack Tactics.*** The highland wolf has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the highland wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.
