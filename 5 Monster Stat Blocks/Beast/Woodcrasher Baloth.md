>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3

>***Trampling Charge.*** If the baloth moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a gore attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, the baloth can make one stomp attack against it as a bonus action.  
>
>### Actions
>***Gore.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 24 (4d8 + 6) piercing damage.  
>
>***Stomp.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one prone creature. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage.
