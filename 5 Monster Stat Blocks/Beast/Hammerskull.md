>*Large Beast, Unaligned*

>- **Hit Points** 19 (3d10 + 3)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)

>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Charge.*** If the hammerskull moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 5 (2d4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.  
>
>***Sure-Footed.*** The hammerskull has advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made against effects that would knock it prone.  
>
>### Actions
>***Ram.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
