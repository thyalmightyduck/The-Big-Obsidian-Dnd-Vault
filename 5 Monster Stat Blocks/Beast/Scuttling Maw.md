___
>## Scuttling Maw
>*Small Beast, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 10 (3d6)
>- **Speed** 25 ft., Swim 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|11 (+0)|1 (-5)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|
>___
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The scuttling maw can breathe air and water.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Pincers.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 11). Until this grapple ends, the scuttling maw can't use its Pincers on another target.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Gnash.*** The scuttling maw makes a Bite attack against a creature it has Grappled.
