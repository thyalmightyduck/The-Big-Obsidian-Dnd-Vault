### Traits
***Hold Breath.*** The crocodile can hold its breath for 1 hour.  

### [[Action|Actions]]
***Multiattack.*** The crocodile makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.  

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[Grappled|Grappled condition]] (escape DC 15). While [[Grappled]], the target has the [[Restrained|Restrained condition]] and can't be targeted by the crocodile's Tail.  

***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[Prone|Prone condition]].
