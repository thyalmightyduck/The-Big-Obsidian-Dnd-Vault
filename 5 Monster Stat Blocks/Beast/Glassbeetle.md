>
>### Actions
>***Shatterbite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:*  1 piercing damage + 3 (1d6) thunder damage.  
>
>***Glass Song (1/Day).*** The glassbeetle weaves a strange and beautiful melody, which it can maintain for up to 10 minutes, or until its concentration ends (as if concentrating on a spell). Creatures within 100 feet of the glassbeetle who can hear this song must succeed on a DC 12 Intelligence saving throw or become charmed by the glassbeetle for as long as they can hear the music.
