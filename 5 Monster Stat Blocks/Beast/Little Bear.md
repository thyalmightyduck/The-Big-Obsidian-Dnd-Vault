___
>## Little Bear
>*Small or Medium Beast (Brute), Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 30 (4d8 + 12)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Swim 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Survival +3
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Exceptional Smell.*** The bear has a +10 bonus to Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell, and they can smell creatures even through dirt, snow, and water.  
>
>***Natural Camouflage.*** The bear has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made in their native terrain.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage, or maximum damage if the target is a Plant.  
>
>***Swat.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing or bludgeoning damage (bear's choice), and the target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
