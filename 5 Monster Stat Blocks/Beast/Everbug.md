>___

>- **Hit Points** 4 (1d4 + 2)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)

>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 0 (XP 10; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The everbug has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:*  1 piercing damage.  
>
>***Noxious Defense (1/Day).*** The everbug secretes a noxious liquid with a potent stench. For the next minute, each creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of the everbug, or that enters within 5 feet of the everbug for the first time each turn must succeed on a DC 12 saving throw against the stench or become poisoned until the start of its next turn.
