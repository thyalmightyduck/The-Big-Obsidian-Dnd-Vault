>### Actions
>***Strength Drain.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage, and the black stirge attaches to the target. While attached, the black stirge doesn't attack. Instead, the target's Strength score is reduced by 1d4. The target dies if this reduces its Strength to 0. Otherwise, the reduction lasts until the target finishes a short or long rest. The black stirge can detach itself by spending 5 feet of its movement. It does so after it drains 4 points of Strength from the target, or the target dies. A creature, including the target, can use its action to detach the black stirge. This replaces the stirge's Blood Drain action.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Shadow Blend.*** While not in sunlight, the black stirge can become invisible as a bonus action. It can end this effect as a bonus action.
