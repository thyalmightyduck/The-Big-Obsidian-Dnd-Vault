>- **Hit Points** 14 (4d6)
>- **Speed** 10 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)

>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Blood Drink.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage, and the trennensel attaches to the target. While attached, the trennensel uses its Action to drain blood, and the target loses 2 (1d4) Hit Points.  
>The trennensel can detach itself by spending 5 feet of its movement. The target or a creature within 5 feet of it can take an action to detach the trennensel with a successful DC 9 Strength (Athletics) check. It detaches automatically if it dies or has the Incapacitated condition.
