___
>## Skitterling
>*Tiny Beast (Skirmisher), Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 5 (2d4)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|4 (-3)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Flyby.*** The skitterling doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when they fly out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1 slashing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the start of their next turn. Creatures who are immune to poison damage or the poisoned condition succeed on this saving throw automatically.
