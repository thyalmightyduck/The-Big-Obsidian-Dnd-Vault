___
>## Skitterling Companion
>*Tiny Beast (Companion), Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 plus PB (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 6 + six times caregiver's level (number of d8 Hit Dice equal to their caregiver's level)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +3 plus PB, Con +1 plus PB
>- **Skills** Perception +2 plus PB, Sleight of Hand +3 plus PB
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12 plus PB
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals the caregiver's bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals the caregiver's bonus
>___
>### Actions
>***Signature Attack (Claws).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  3 plus PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d6 plus PB slashing damage.  
>
>***1st Level: Destabilizing Attack (2 Ferocity).*** The skitterling makes a signature attack. On a hit, the target also has disadvantage on the next attack roll they make before the start of the skitterling's next turn.  
>
>***3rd Level: Ankle Biter (5 Ferocity).*** The skitterling moves up to their speed without provoking opportunity attacks and makes a signature attack. On a hit, if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, they are also knocked prone and grappled by the skitterling (escape DC 10 plus PB).  
>
>***5th Level: In All Your Faces! (8 Ferocity).*** The skitterling chaotically bounces around, smearing toxin on each enemy within 5 feet of them.  
>Each target must succeed on a DC 10 plus PB Constitution saving throw or take 1d6 plus PB poison damage and be blinded until the end of the skitterling's next turn. A creature within 5 feet of a target blinded in this way can use their action to wipe the toxin off them, ending the condition for that target.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Crafty Distraction (Recharges after a Long Rest).*** The skitterling flutters and screeches distractingly, allowing their caregiver an opportunity to reposition. The caregiver can use a reaction to move up to their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
