>***Hold Breath.*** The whale can hold its breath for 30 minutes.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The whale makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its tail.  
>
>***Flipper.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target not grappled by the whale. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.  
>
>***Tail.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target not grappled by the whale. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
