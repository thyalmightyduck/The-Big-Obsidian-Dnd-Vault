>***Relentless (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** If the goldmane boar take 11 damage or less that would reduce it to 0 Hit Points, it is reduced to 1 Hit Point instead.

>### Actions
>***Tusk.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature and the goldmane boar moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 4 (1d8) Slashing damage and has the Prone condition.
