___
>## Young Wakewyrm
>*Large Beast, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 68 (8d10 + 24)
>- **Speed** 0 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|3 (-4)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Hold Breath.*** The young wakewyrm can hold its breath for 1 hour.  
>
>***Plaintive Cry.*** The young wakewyrm can spend a bonus action to call for its mother. As long as its mother is within 150 feet, and can hear its young, the mother may spend its reaction to swim up to 50 feet toward the young wakewyrm.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The young wakewyrm makes two bite attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage.
