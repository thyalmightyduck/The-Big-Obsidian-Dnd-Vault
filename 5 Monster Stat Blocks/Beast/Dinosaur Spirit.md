___
>## Dinosaur Spirit
>*Huge Beast (Dinosaur), Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11 + the spell's level + 2 (Ankylosaur only)
>- **Hit Points** 60 + 10 for each spell level above 6
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|4 (-3)|12 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Understands the languages you know
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals your Proficiency Bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals your Proficiency Bonus
>___
>***Tough.*** Add half the spell's level (round down) to any Strength or Constitution saving throw the spirit makes.  
>
>***Siege Monster (Ankylosaur Only).*** The spirit deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The spirit makes a number of attacks equal to half this spell's level (round down).  
>
>***Bite (Tyrannosaur Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 2d10 + 5 + summonSpellLevel Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC equals your spell save DC), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends.  
>
>***Gore (Triceratops Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d10 + 5 + summonSpellLevel Piercing damage. If the target is a Huge or smaller creature and the spirit moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 1d10 Piercing damage and has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 1d10 + 5 + summonSpellLevel Bludgeoning damage.
