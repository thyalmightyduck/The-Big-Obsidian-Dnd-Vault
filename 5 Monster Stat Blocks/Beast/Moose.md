>## Moose
>*Large Beast, Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 22 (3d10 + 6)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|2 (-4)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Forest Walker.*** While in forest terrain, the moose has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the moose moved 10+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 3 (1d6) Bludgeoning damage and has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Fury.*** *Trigger:* The moose takes 5 or more damage. *Response:* The moose has Advantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.
