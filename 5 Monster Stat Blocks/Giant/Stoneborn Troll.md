>## Stoneborn Troll
>*Large Giant, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 136 (13d10 + 65)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|20 (+5)|9 (-1)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +8
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The troll has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the troll takes Acid or Thunder damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The troll deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Sure Stance.*** The troll has Advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws to avoid forced movement or the Prone condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes three attacks: one with its Bite and two with its Claws.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (2d12 + 5) Slashing damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Prone condition.
