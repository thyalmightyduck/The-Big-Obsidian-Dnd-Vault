>## Slumbering Titan
>*Gargantuan Giant, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 255 (14d20 + 98)
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|28 (+9)|10 (+0)|24 (+7)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +13, Perception +11
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Keen Smell.*** The slumbering titan has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.  
>
>***Sleepy.*** The slumbering titan has Disadvantage on saving throws to avoid or end effects that put it to sleep. If the titan is the target of a *Sleep* spell, treat its Hit Points as half their current value to determine whether the spell takes effect.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The slumbering titan makes three attacks, using Slam or Rock in any combination. It can replace one of these attacks with Fling or Stomp.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 27 (4d8 + 9) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, the giant can deal half as much damage and grapple the target (escape DC 21). The titan can grapple up to two creatures at a time.  
>
>***Rock.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +14, range 60/240 ft. *Hit:* 31 (4d10 + 9) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Fling.*** The titan throws a Large or smaller creature Grappled by it to a space it can see within 60 feet of itself that isn't in the air. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 21, the creature thrown and each creature in the destination space. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Stomp.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 19, each creature on the ground within 30 feet of the titan. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
