___
>## Sand Giant Monarch
>*Huge Giant, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 195 (17d12 + 85)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Athletics +10, Insight +6, Perception +6, Persuasion +6, Survival +6
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Giant and two other languages
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Desert Hardened.*** The giant has Advantage on saving throws against Dehydration, thirst, and hunger, and it needs half as much food and water as a normal creature of its size. Also, the giant is adapted to Extreme Heat and Cold.  
>
>***Desert Stride.*** The giant ignores Difficult Terrain due to sand, rubble, or similar debris.  
>
>***Sand Camouflage.*** The giant has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to Hide in sandy or sandstone terrain.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The giant makes three attacks. It can replace one attack with War Command even if the Bonus Action is not recharged.  
>
>***Scimitar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 20 (4d6 + 6) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Rock.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 60/240 ft. *Hit:* 20 (4d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***War Command (Recharge 5–6).*** The giant chooses an ally of CR 9 or less that can hear and understand it, commanding it to attack a creature the giant attacked this turn. The ally can use its Reaction to make an attack against the designated creature, adding the giant's Proficiency Bonus to the damage roll.
