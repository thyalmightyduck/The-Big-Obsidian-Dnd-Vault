___
>## Troll Limbjumble
>*Small Giant (Minion), Unaligned*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 9
>- **Speed** 25 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|3 (-4)|5 (-3)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 7
>- **Languages** Understands Giant but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 90; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Minion.*** If the limbjumble takes damage from an attack or as the result of a failed saving throw, their hit points are reduced to 0. If the limbjumble takes damage from another effect, they die if the damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum; otherwise they take no damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Arm and a Leg (Group Attack).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller and if three or more limbjumbles joined the attack, the target is knocked prone.
