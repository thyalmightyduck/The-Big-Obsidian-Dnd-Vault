>## Great Ogre
>*Huge Giant, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 95 (10d12 + 30)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|7 (-2)|9 (-1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +7, Perception +1
>- **Gear** *Greatclub*, *Javelin*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Massive Frame.*** The great ogre has Advantage on Strength saving throws and Strength (Athletics) checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The great ogre makes two greatclub attacks or throws two javelins.  
>
>***Greatclub.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Javelin.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 10 ft. or range 50/200 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Hideous Belch (Recharge 6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13 All creatures in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Poison damage, and the creature has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only. A creature with the Poisoned condition can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns to end the effect upon itself.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Tense Muscles (Recharge 6).*** *Trigger:* The great ogre is hit by an attack that deals Bludgeoning, Slashing, or Piercing damage. *Response:* The great ogre takes half damage from the triggering attack.
