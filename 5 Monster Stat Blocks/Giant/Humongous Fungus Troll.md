>## Humongous Fungus Troll
>*Huge Giant (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 54 (4d12 + 28) plus 54 (4d12 + 28) per player character
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|10 (+0)|25 (+7)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +11, Wis +7, Cha +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +10, Perception +7
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60, Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the troll remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a gigantic mushroom.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 30 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the troll takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Rotting Aura.*** Any creature who starts its turn in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the troll takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage unless the troll has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***On its turn, the troll can move as normal.*** However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the troll takes one of the following Epic Actions of its choice at the end of each player character's turn:  
>
>***Spore Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 33 (6d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Grasping Tendrils.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 60 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Slashing damage and if the target is Huge or smaller, the troll pulls the target up to 20 feet straight towards itself.  
>
>***Entangling Mycelia (Recharge when Bloodied).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature of the troll's choice in a 30-foot radius centered on it. *Failure:* The target has the Restrained condition (save ends).  
>
>***Spawn Trolling (1/Round).*** The troll creates a Fungal Trolling which appears in an unoccupied space within 10 feet of it. The fungal trolling acts immediately after the troll uses this action, then acts after the troll's turn on subsequent rounds.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The troll chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. A troll can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
