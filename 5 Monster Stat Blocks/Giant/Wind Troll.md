>## Wind Troll
>*Small Giant, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 105 (14d6 + 56)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 45 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Lightning, Thunder; Deafened, Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Giant, Primordial (Auran)
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The wind troll regains 15 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the troll takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function on the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Storm Absorption.*** Whenever the wind troll is subjected to Lightning or Thunder damage, it regains a number of Hit Points equal to the Lightning and Thunder damage dealt.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The wind troll makes three attacks, using Rend or Storm Strike in any combination.  
>
>***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The wind troll casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Fog Cloud*  
>2/day each: *Gust of Wind*  
>1/day each: *Wind Wall*  
>
>***Storm Strike.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, range 60 feet, one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Lightning damage plus 4 (1d8) Thunder damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Windstorm (Recharge 5–6).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15 Each creature in a 20-foot radius around the troll. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed up to 10 feet away from the troll. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Flying Charge.*** The wind troll flies up to half its Fly Speed.
