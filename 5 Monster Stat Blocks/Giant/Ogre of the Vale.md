___
>## Ogre of the Vale
>*Large Giant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 171 (18d10 + 72)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|9 (-1)|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +9, Insight +5, Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison
>- **Immunities** Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Lower Burach, Ostoy
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Ferocious Presence.*** Allied creatures of the Ogre who are within 30 feet of the Ogre and can see him gain 5 Temporary Hit Points at the start of their turn unless the Ogre has the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>***Reckless Abandon.*** At the start of its turn, the Ogre and allied creatures within 30 feet who can see or hear the Ogre can gain Advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, and attack rolls against them have Advantage until the start of their next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Ogre makes two War Sword attacks. He can replace one attack with Bellowing Command.  
>
>***War Sword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Bellowing Command.*** The Ogre chooses a creature it can see and commands up to three allies of CR 6 or lower who can see or hear the Ogre. The allies can use their Reactions to make weapon attacks against the target. The allies roll 1d4 and add the result to their attack rolls.  
>
>***Ravenous Appetite (3/Day).*** The Ogre grabs a Medium or smaller allied Humanoid of CR 1/2 or lower within its reach and gnaws on its face and neck. The ally dies and the Ogre regains Hit Points equal to the ally's Hit Point maximum.
