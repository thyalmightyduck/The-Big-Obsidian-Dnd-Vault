>## Skymir Godkiller
>*Huge Giant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 225 (18d12 + 108)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|23 (+6)|8 (-1)|23 (+6)|18 (+4)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +12, Int +10, Wis +11, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Arcana +10, Perception +11
>- **Immunities** Cold
>- **Gear** *Luck Blade*, *Plate Armor*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Truesight 30 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** Giant, Common, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 17 (XP 18,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Divine Vitality.*** Skymir's ability scores and HP maximum can't be reduced except by means of a *Wish* spell.  
>
>***Icy Weapons.*** Skymir's weapon attacks are magical. When he hits with any weapon, the weapon deals an extra 9 (2d8) Cold damage (included in the attack).  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Skymir fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** Skymir has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Skymir makes two attacks using Runesword or Arcane Sphere in any combination. He can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Runesword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Cold damage; the target is Marked with a glowing spectral rune until the end of Skymir's next turn.  
>
>***Arcane Sphere.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +12 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 25 (3d12 + 6) Force damage, and the target has Disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of Skymir's next turn.  
>
>***Icy Sweep (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16 Each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on Skymir. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Slashing damage, 13 (3d8) Cold damage, and the target is pushed up to 15 feet away from Skymir and gains the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage and not pushed or knocked Prone.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Skymir casts one of the following spells, requiring no material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19):  
>
>At will: *Command*, *Fear*, *Speak with Animals*  
>3/day each: *Bestow Curse*, *Divination*, *Gust of Wind*  
>1/day each: *Earthquake*, *Power Word Stun*, *Teleport*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Translocative Word.*** Skymir speaks a word of power, and up to two creatures Marked by Skymir's Runesword are teleported to unoccupied spaces on the ground within 60 feet that he can see. The Mark on those targets then fades.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Protective Word.*** *Trigger:* Skymir is hit by an attack roll. *Response:* Skymir utters a word of power, adding 6 to his AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the skymir godkiller can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The skymir godkiller regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Attack.** Skymir makes a Runesword or Arcane Sphere attack. He can't take this action again until the start of his next turn.
>- **Frightful Presence.** Skymir uses Spellcasting to cast *Command* or *Fear*. Skymir can't take this action again until the start of his next turn.  
>
>- **Life-Draining Word.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16 Skymir utters a word of power, targeting up to three creatures of his choice within 60 feet. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and Skymir regains Hit Points equal to the damage taken from each target. *Failure or Success:* Skymir can't take this action again until the start of his next turn.
