___
>## Grinstagger
>*Large Giant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 266 (28d10 + 112)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** -2 (8)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|7 (-2)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +10, Int +8, Wis +7, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Arcana +8, Deception +10, Perception +7
>- **Gear** *Ring of Mind Shielding*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Elvish, Giant, Sylvan
>- **Challenge** 17 (XP 18,000; PB +6)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +6
>___
>***Accursed Attacks.*** A creature that takes damage from the Grinstagger's attacks is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* The target is cursed for 24 hours. While cursed, the target has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and takes an extra 4 (1d8) Psychic damage from Grinstagger's attacks. *Success:* The target is immune to Accursed Attacks for 24 hours.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day or 4/Day in Lair).*** If Grinstagger fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** Grinstagger regains 10 Hit Points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 Hit Point.  
>
>***Ring of Mind Shielding.*** Grinstagger wears an invisible *Ring of Mind Shielding*. He has Immunity to magic that allows other creatures to read his thoughts, determine whether he's lying, know his Alignment, or know his creature type. Creatures can telepathically communicate with him only if he allows it.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Grinstagger makes three attacks , using Claw and Eldritch Bolt in any combination.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +11, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Force damage plus 4 (1d8) Psychic damage, and the target is subjected to Accursed Attacks.  
>
>***Eldritch Bolt.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +10, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Force damage, and the target is subjected to Accursed Attacks.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** Grinstagger casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18):  
>
>At will: *Charm Person*, *Darkness*, *Detect Magic*, *Faerie Fire*, *Speak with Dead*  
>1/day each: *Calm Emotions*, *Cone of Cold* (level 7 version), *Eyebite*, *Gaseous Form*, *Mislead*, *Sleep*  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** Grinstagger shape-shifts into a Small or Medium Humanoid, a Large giant, or back into his true form. Other than his Size, his game statistics are the same in each form, and any form he takes retains his accursed appearance. Equipment he is wearing or carrying changes size with him, remaining transformed until he relinquishes it or dies.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step.*** Grinstagger casts *Misty Step* using the spellcasting ability in Innate Spellcasting.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, Grinstagger can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Grinstagger regains all expended uses at the start of each of his turns.
>
>- **Move.** Grinstagger moves up to his Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
>- **Attack.** Grinstagger makes a Claw or Eldritch Bolt attack.
>- **Deft Magic.** Grinstagger casts an at-will spell from Spellcasting or uses *Misty Step*.
