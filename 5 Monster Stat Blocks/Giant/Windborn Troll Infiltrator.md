___
>## Windborn Troll Infiltrator
>*Large Giant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 136 (13d10 + 65)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|18 (+4)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +8, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Lightning, Thunder
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Flexible.*** The troll can move through spaces large enough for a Medium creature.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the troll takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Sneak Attack (1/Turn).*** The troll deals an extra 10 (3d6) damage when it hits a target with a weapon attack and has Advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of the troll, the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, and the troll doesn't have Disadvantage on the attack roll.  
>
>***Standing Leap.*** The troll's Long Jump is up to 40 feet and its High Jump is up to 20 feet, with or without a running start.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes three attacks, using Bite or Claw in any combination.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Lightning damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Cunning Action.*** The troll takes the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.  
>
>***Windborn Step.*** The troll teleports to an unoccupied space within 40 feet, provided its starting space and destination space are both obscured by mist or similar airborne particles, Dim Light, or Darkness.
