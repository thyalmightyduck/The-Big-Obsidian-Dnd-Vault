>## Thrudgelmir Giant
>*Huge Giant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 175 (14d12 + 84)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|27 (+8)|10 (+0)|22 (+6)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +10, Wis +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +12, Perception +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Stunned, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Ever Vigilant.*** At least two of the thrudgelmir's heads are awake at all times. The creature has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The thrudgelmir has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Runic Tattoos.*** The thrudgelmir giant's body is covered in magical, runic tattoos. A successful *Dispel Magic* cast on the tattoos (against DC 15) causes them to vanish for 1 minute. During that time, the thrudgelmir loses its Magic Resistance and the energy damage from its Elemental Hammer attack.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The thrudgelmir makes three Elemental Hammer attacks.  
>
>***Elemental Hammer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 26 (4d8 + 8) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) damage (roll 1d6 to determine damage type: 1. Acid, 2. Cold, 3. Fire, 4. Lightning, 5. Poison, 6. Thunder).  
>
>***Faces of Evil (Recharge 5–6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* All creatures within 30 feet of the thrudgelmir that can see it are subjected to one of the following conditions or effects until the end of the giant's next turn.
