>## Lightning Giant
>*Large Giant, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 116 (13d10 + 45)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|18 (+4)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +4
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +7, Arcana +5, Athletics +9, Perception +4
>- **Immunities** Lightning, Thunder
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Auran, Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Electrifying Flesh.*** A lightning giant's flesh constantly sparks with electricity. A creature striking a lightning giant with a melee weapon must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or take 3 (1d6) Lightning damage, or 5 (1d10) damage if the weapon is made out of a conductive metal such as iron. A lightning giant can suppress this ability with a Bonus Action.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lightning giant makes two attacks, using Lightning Staff or Static Fling in any combination.  
>
>***Lightning Staff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Lightning damage.  
>
>***Static Fling.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (4d6 + 4) Lightning damage, and the target is pushed up to 15 feet.  
>
>***Static Discharge (Recharge 6 or Special).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15 All creatures in a 20-foot radius Sphere surrounding the lightning giant. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Lightning Damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Absorb Lightning.*** *Trigger:* A creature the lightning giant can see within 60 feet (other than itself) deals Lightning damage to a creature other than the lightning giant. *Response:* The lightning giant absorbs the Lightning damage into its staff, negating that damage against its original target. This also recharges its Static Discharge ability.
