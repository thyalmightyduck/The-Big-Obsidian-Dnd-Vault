>## Magma Troll

>- **Hit Points** 105 (10d10 + 50)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)

>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Fire; Burning|Monstersofdrakkenheim
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the troll takes Cold damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** The troll is immune to the Burning condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes two Fiery Claw attacks and one Volcanic Bite attack. It can replace its Volcanic Bite attack with Sulphurous Belch, if available.  
>
>***Volcanic Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Fiery Claw.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 120 feet. *Hit:* 18 (4d6 + 4) Fire damage, and the target has the Burning condition (4d6 Fire).  
>
>***Sulfurous Belch (Recharges 5-6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on the troll. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Fire damage and has the Blinded condition until it or another creature within 5 of it uses an action to clean the ash from their eyes. *Success:* Half damage only.
