___
>## Fireborn Troll Flamecaller
>*Large Giant, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 147 (14d10 + 70)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|9 (-1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Nature +3, Perception +10
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Heated Body.*** A creature that touches the troll or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 5 (2d4) Fire damage.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the troll takes Acid or Cold damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes three attacks, using Claw or Spit Fire in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Bite.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Force damage plus 7 (2d6) Fire damage, or 14 (4d6) Fire damage if it has used Call Flame.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Force damage and 7 (2d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Spit Fire.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +6, range 40/120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Fire damage.  
>
>***Control Flame.*** The troll chooses a fire it can see within 30 feet of it and expands that fire by one 5-foot Cube, extinguishes fire in one 5-foot Cube, or makes shapes in the fire in one 5-foot Cube. Expanded fire needs fuel to continue burning.  
>
>***Firebane (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 20-foot radius Sphere centered on a point the troll can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Fire damage and the target is cursed for 1 minute. While cursed, the target must roll 1d4 and subtract the result from its attack rolls and saving throws. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried ignite.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Call Flame.*** The troll ignites a magical fire in its maw, which lasts until the start of its next turn. It can't use Spite Fire or Firebane unless this flame is lit.
