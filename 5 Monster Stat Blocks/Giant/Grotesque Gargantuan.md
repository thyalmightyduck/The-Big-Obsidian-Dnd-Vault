___
>## Grotesque Gargantuan
>*Gargantuan Giant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 186 (12d20 + 60)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|27 (+8)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Con +9, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Athletics +12, Perception +5
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Misty Camouflage.*** The gargantuan has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks to hide in any area obscured by mist or fog, including the Deep Haze of Drakkenheim.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The gargantuan regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn as long as it is within the Deep Haze. The gargantuan dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gargantuan makes two Slam attacks.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 40 (4d10 + 8) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Hurl Delerium Chunk.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +12, range 120/300 ft. *Hit:* 26 (4d8 + 8) Bludgeoning damage and the target has the Prone condition. *Hit or Miss:* The delerium chunk explodes creating the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 20, the targeted creature and each creature within 10 feet of it. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Necrotic damage and gain one level of Contamination. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* An Arcane Anomalies occurs in the space where the delerium chunk exploded.
