>## Fungal Trolling
>*Medium Giant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 15 (2d8 + 6)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|5 (-3)|11 (+0)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Death Burst.*** When the trolling dies, it explodes in a burst of mind-affecting spores. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature that isn't a troll in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the troll. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Psychic damage and has the Weakened condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***False Appearance.*** While the trolling remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a patch of mushrooms.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spore Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Necrotic damage.
