>## Putrid Giant
>*Huge Giant, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 161 (14d12 + 70)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|8 (-1)|20 (+5)|5 (-3)|9 (-1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +2
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Pox Euphoria.*** The putrid giant rolls 1d4 and adds the result to ability checks other than Intelligence checks, and saving throws other than Constitution saving throws.  
>
>***Weeping Pox.*** A Humanoid that comes within 5 feet of the putrid giant or anything it has recently held is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Success:* The target is immune to the Weeping Pox for 24 hours. *Failure:* The target is infected with the Weeping Pox. Every 24 hours, the target repeats the save, suffering an Exhaustion level on a failure. This Exhaustion can't be reduced until the disease is cured, and the disease can only be cured by magic.  
>Until the first Exhaustion level sets in, the target has sharpened senses and a sense of wellbeing. The target can roll 1d4 and add the result to any ability check or saving throw it makes, except saving throws to resist the disease.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The putrid giant makes two Greatclub attacks.  
>
>***Greatclub.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 24 (4d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is subjected to Weeping Pox.
