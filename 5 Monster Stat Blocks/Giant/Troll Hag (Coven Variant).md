>## Troll Hag (Coven Variant)
>*Large Giant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 133 (14d10 + 56)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|13 (+1)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Wis +7, Cha +6
>- **Skills** Deception +5, Perception +6
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120, Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Coven Magic.*** While within 30 feet of at least two hag allies, the hag can cast one of the following spells, requiring no Material components, using the spell's normal casting time, and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks).  
>
>2/day each: *Counterspell*, *Ray of Sickness*  
>1/day: *Cloudkill*, *Greater Invisibility*  
>
>***Fungal Aura.*** The ground in a 10-foot radius of any of the three hags is difficult terrain and grants the hags half cover as it is covered in constant fungal growths and clouds of spores unless the hag has the Incapacitated condition. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, any creature who ends its turn within the area. *Failure:* The target has the Staggered and Bewildered conditions (save ends)  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The troll hag regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the troll hag takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the hag's next turn. The troll hag dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll hag makes one Fungal Staff attack and one Slimeball attack. It can replace either attack with a use of Spellcasting.  
>
>***Fungal Staff.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature within 5ft. *Failure:* 16 (3d8 + 3) Psychic damage and the target has the Addled condition (save ends).  
>
>***Slimeball.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +6, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 19 (3d10 + 3) Acid damage and the target has the Burning condition (3d10 Acid).  
>
>***Garden of Fungal Delights (Recharge 6).*** The coven pours their strange brew upon the mushroom patches around them, immediately spawning 7 (2d6) Fungal Trollings.  
>
>***Rapid Growth (1/Day).*** If all three hags use their action to target the same fungal trolling within 60 feet of them with this ability, that trolling erupts in size and becomes a Humongous Fungus Troll. While the hags are using this ability, they must maintain Concentration. If any of the hags lose Concentration before the third hag takes its action, the ability is not wasted but they must start over. While being targeted by Rapid Growth, the targeted fungal trolling has the Paralyzed condition.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The troll hag casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Druidcraft*, *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*  
>2/day each: *Bestow Curse* (Level 5 version), *Plant Growth*  
>1/day: *Contact Other Plane*, *Scrying* The hag may replace either its Fungal Staff or Slimeball attack with the following:  
>
>***Vexing Curse.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 19 (3d10 + 3) Psychic damage and the target has the Jinxed (1d6) condition (save ends).
