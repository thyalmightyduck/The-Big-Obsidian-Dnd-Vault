>## Stoneborn Troll Contemplative
>*Large Giant, Lawful Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 172 (15d10 + 90)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +5 (15)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|12 (+1)|22 (+6)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +10
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Claw Technique (1/Turn).*** A creature hit by the troll's Claw or Kinetic Claw is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target suffers one of the following effects of the troll's choice:  
>- The target has the Prone condition.
>- The target is pushed up to 15 feet straight away from the troll.
>- The target can't take Reactions until the end of the troll's next turn.
>
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the troll takes Acid or Thunder damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The troll deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Sure Stance.*** The troll has Advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws to avoid forced movement or the Prone condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes four attacks: one Bite attack and three Claw or Kinetic Claw attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 18 (2d12 + 5) Slashing damage, and the target is subjected to Claw Technique.  
>
>***Kinetic Claw.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +6, range 60/120 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d12 + 2) Slashing damage, and the target is subjected to Claw Technique.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Purify.*** The troll ends the Charmed, Frightened, or Poisoned condition on itself.
