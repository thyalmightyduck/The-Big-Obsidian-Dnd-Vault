___
>## Jotun
>*Gargantuan Giant, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 370 (20d20 + 160)
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|8 (-1)|26 (+8)|18 (+4)|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +15, Wis +12, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Arcana +11, History +11, Nature +11, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
>- **Immunities** Cold, Bludgeoning
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 22 (XP 41,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If the jotun fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The jotun has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Material Bound.*** The jotun has Immunity to any spell or effect that would force it to leave the Material Plane, unless the jotun wants to leave. It has Disadvantage on saving throws against effects that would return it to the Material Plane from another plane of existence.  
>
>***Too Big to Notice.*** While the jotun remains motionless in nonmagical, natural terrain, it has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The jotun makes three Greatclub or Rock attacks in any combination. Instead of one of those attacks, it can use Icy Sweep or cast Fear.  
>
>***Greatclub.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +17 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 28 (4d8 + 10) Bludgeoning damage plus 27 (6d8) Cold damage.  
>
>***Rock.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +17 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. *Hit:* 32 (4d10 + 10) Bludgeoning damage plus 27 (6d8) Cold damage.  
>
>***Icy Sweep (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 23 Each creature within 20 feet of the jotun. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Bludgeoning damage and 54 (12d8) Cold damage, and the target is pushed up to 15 feet away from the jotun and has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The jotun casts one of the following spells, requiring no material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20):  
>
>At will: *Bestow Curse*, *Divination*, *Earthquake*, *Fear*, *Gust of Wind*, *Speak with Animals*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Rock Catching.*** *Trigger:* The jotun is hit by a ranged weapon attack. *Response:* The jotun reduces the damage by 1d20 + 30. If this reduces the damage to 0, the jotun can throw the weapon or ammunition at a creature within 60 feet of it, using the same attack roll total and damage total of the originating creature.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the jotun can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The jotun regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Hurl.** The jotun makes a Rock attack.
>- **Smash.** The jotun makes a Greatclub attack.
>- **Titanic Power.** The jotun casts a spell or uses Icy Sweep, if available.
