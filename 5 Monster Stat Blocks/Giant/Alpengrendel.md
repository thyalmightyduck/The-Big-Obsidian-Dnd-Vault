___
>## Alpengrendel
>*Large Giant, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 85 (9d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|9 (-1)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4
>- **Skills** Athletics +8, Perception +5, Survival +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Fire
>- **Immunities** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Common, giant
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aversion to Faith.*** The alpengrendel has Disadvantage on attack rolls while in a consecrated area or on attack rolls against a creature holding or wearing a *Holy Symbol*.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The alpengrendel regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the alpengrendel takes Acid or Lightning damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn. The alpengrendel dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The alpengrendel makes one Claws attack and one Tusks attack.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage plus 5 (1d10) Cold damage.  
>
>***Tusks.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d8 + 5) Piercing damage plus 5 (1d10) Cold damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Frost Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Cold damage. Until the start of the alpengrendel's next turn, the target's Speed is halved, and it has Disadvantage on attack rolls. *Success:* Half damage only.
