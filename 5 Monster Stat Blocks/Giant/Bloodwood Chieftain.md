>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 184 (16d12 + 80)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)

>- **Senses** Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Bleeding Strikes.*** A creature hit by the giant's Blood Spear attack is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target has the Bleeding condition [the following text has been removed due to homebrew: (see Grim Hollow Campaign Guide)].  
>
>***Forest Camouflage.*** The giant has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in forested terrain.  
>
>***Steadfast.*** The giant can't have the Frightened condition while it can see an ally within 40 feet.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The giant makes two attacks, using Blood Spear or Rock in any combination.  
>
>***Blood Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 15 ft. or range 60/240 ft. *Hit:* 23 (3d10 + 7) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The giant casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Druidcraft*  
>1/day each: *Detect Poison and Disease*, *Find Traps*  
>
>***Rock.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 60/240 ft. *Hit:* 23 (3d10 + 7) Bludgeoning damage.
