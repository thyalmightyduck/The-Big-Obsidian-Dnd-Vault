>## Fjord Troll
>*Large Giant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 85 (9d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|9 (-1)|9 (-1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Poison
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Hold Breath.*** The fjord troll can hold its breath for 30 minutes.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The fjord troll regains 15 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the troll takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function on the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The fjord troll makes two Gouge attacks.  
>
>***Gouge.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage. If the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Paralyzed condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>***Water Stream.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14 Each creature in a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Cold damage. If the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it is pushed up to 10 feet away from the fjord troll. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aquatic Charge.*** The fjord troll swims up to half its Swim Speed.
