>## Remorseful Storm
>*Huge Giant, Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 270 (20d12 + 140)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Fly 25 ft., Swim 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +12 (22)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|29 (+9)|14 (+2)|25 (+7)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +12, Wis +9, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Athletics +14, History +7, Insight +9, Perception +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Lightning, Thunder
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 19
>- **Languages** Celestial, Giant, three other languages
>- **Challenge** 16 (XP 15,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Adamant Panoply.*** Any Critical Hit against the Remorseful Storm becomes a normal hit.  
>
>***Amphibious.*** The Remorseful Storm can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Relentless Zeal.*** The first time the Remorseful Storm is reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed, it does not fall unconscious and it does not die. Instead, it is filled with an enduring radiance that allows it to keep fighting with the following benefits until the end of its next turn:  
>- The Remorseful Storm sheds Bright Light in a 30-foot radius.
>- At the start of each of the Remorseful Storm's turns, each creature within 5 feet of it takes 9 (2d8) Radiant damage. A creature that touches the Remorseful Storm or hit it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 9 (2d8) Radiant damage.
>- The Remorseful Storm has Advantage on weapon attacks.
>
>At the end of its turn, if the Remorseful Storm hit a creature with a melee attack, the Remorseful Storm regains 1 Hit Point. Otherwise, it dies.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Remorseful Storm makes two Greataxe attacks  
>
>***Greataxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 35 (4d12 + 9) Force damage plus 18 (4d8) Lightning damage.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The Remorseful Storm casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Levitate*, *Light*, *Protection from Evil and Good*  
>3/day each: *Branding Smite*, *Control Weather*, *Water Breathing*  
>1/day each: *Holy Aura*  
>
>***Lightning Strike (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature within 10 feet of a point the Remorseful Storm can see within 500 feet. *Failure:* 54 (12d8) Lightning damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Retaliate.*** *Trigger:* The Remorseful Storm takes damage from a creature within 10 feet of it. *Response:* The Remorseful Storm makes a melee attack against that creature.
