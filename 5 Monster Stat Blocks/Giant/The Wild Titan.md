>## The Wild Titan
>*Gargantuan Giant (Titan), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 350 (20d20 + 140)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +14 (24)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|11 (+0)|24 (+7)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Athletics +17, Perception +10, Survival +10
>- **Immunities** Cold; Charmed, Frightened, Stunned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** Common, Giant, Primordial (Terran)
>- **Challenge** 21 (XP 33,000, or 41,000 in lair; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day or 5/Day in Lair).*** If the titan fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The titan has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Runic Sever.*** If the titan starts its turn within 50 feet of a stone at least 10 feet high and 10 feet wide that is carved with the glyph representing its forgotten creator, the titan takes 50 Necrotic damage and has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The titan deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The titan makes two Massive Fist attacks. It can replace one or both attacks with a use of Thrown Boulder.  
>
>***Massive Fist.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +17, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 28 (4d8 + 10) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 20) from one of two fists, and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends.  
>
>***Thrown Boulder.*** The titan hurls a boulder at a point it can see within 150 feet. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature in a 10-foot radius Sphere centered on that point. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Wild Roar (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 19, each of the titan's enemies in a 100-foot Emanation originating from the titan. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition until the start of the titan's next turn. While Charmed in this way, the creature must use its action to make its most powerful melee or ranged attacks against the nearest creature it can see.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Arm Sweep.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature in a 20-foot Cone. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The titan can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Crushing Hurl.** The titan hurls an object it is holding, a creature it is grappling, or a Large clod of earth to a space within 100 feet. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature in the projectile's space, including a hurled creature. *Failure:* 19 (2d8 + 10) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The titan can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Stomping Stride.** The titan moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. It can enter a Huge or smaller creature's space, and the creature is pushed to nearest unoccupied space.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Wild Titan's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Whiteout.** Within 1 mile of the lair, there is a 75 percent chance every day of heavy precipitation.
>- **Wild Hunger.** Within 1 mile of the lair, creatures always feel ravenously hungry, no matter how much they've eaten. A creature that finishes a Long Rest in this area must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 Exhaustion level. A creature that doesn't require food succeeds automatically.
>
>If the Wild Titan dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
