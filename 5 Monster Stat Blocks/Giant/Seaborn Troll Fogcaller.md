>## Seaborn Troll Fogcaller
>*Large Giant, Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 181 (18d10 + 90)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|16 (+3)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|9 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5
>- **Immunities** Acid
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Amphibious.*** The troll can breathe air and water.  
>
>***Aquatic Camouflage.*** The troll has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to Hide underwater.  
>
>***Flexible.*** The troll can move through spaces large enough for a Medium creature.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn. If the troll takes Cold or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes three attacks, using Claw or Spit Acid in any combination. It can replace one attack with Bite, or replace two attacks with Call Fog.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage and 7 (2d6) Acid damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Spit Acid.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +5, range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Acid damage.  
>
>***Call Fog (1/Day).*** The troll creates a 30-foot radius Emanation of fog that spreads around corners and renders its area Heavily Obscured. It lasts for 1 minute or until dispersed by a strong wind (like that created by a *Gust of Wind*).  
>The troll can choose to make this fog caustic. If it does, a creature that enters the fog for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there takes 10 (4d4) Acid damage.
