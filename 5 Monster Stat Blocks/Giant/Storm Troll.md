>- **Hit Points** 105 (10d10 + 50)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)

>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Acid
>- **Immunities** Lightning, Thunder; Shocked|Monstersofdrakkenheim
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the troll takes Acid damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** The troll is immune to the Shocked condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes two Claw attacks and one Bite attack. It can replace its Bite attack with Hurl Lightning if available.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Thunder damage, and the target has the Staggered condition until the end of the troll's next turn.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) Lightning damage, and lightning leaps from the target to a different creature of the troll's choice that it can see within 10 feet of the target. The second creature takes 18 (4d8) Lightning damage.  
>
>***Hurl Lightning (Recharge 5–6).*** The troll hurls a magical lightning bolt at a point it can see within 300 feet of it. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Lightning damage and has the Shocked condition until the end of the troll's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
