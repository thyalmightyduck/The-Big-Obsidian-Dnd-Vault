>## Eldritch Troll
>*Large Giant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 105 (10d10 + 50)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|13 (+1)|20 (+5)|7 (-2)|11 (+0)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Psychic
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Poisoned, Stunned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the troll takes Force or Psychic damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes two Claw attacks and one Bite attack. It can replace its Bite attack with Corrupting Blast if available.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage, and the target is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* The target gains one level of Contamination.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Corrupting Blast (Recharge 5–6).*** The troll blasts a corrupting beam at a point it can see within 300 feet. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, all creatures in a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage and gains a level of Contamination. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Forced Mutation.*** The troll is so contaminated that it changes form constantly, growing new appendages or exploding out of its old skin into a new form. The troll may manifest one of the following mutations. The mutation stays in effect until a new one is chosen.  
>
>***Grotesque Wings.*** The troll gains a Fly Speed equal to is Speed, but it falls if it ends its turn in the air.  
>
>***Cyclopean Eye.*** Corrupting Blast now recharges on a 4-6, and the troll gains Truesight 30 ft.  
>
>***Extra Arms.*** The troll can make two additional Claw attacks as part of the Multiattack action.  
>
>***Spider Legs.*** The troll gains a Climb Speed equal to its Speed and can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.  
>
>***Reflective Flesh.*** The troll has the Invisible condition until it makes an attack.  
>
>***Crystalized Carapace.*** The troll's Armor Class becomes 20.
