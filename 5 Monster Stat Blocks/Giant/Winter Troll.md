>## Winter Troll
>*Large Giant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 105 (10d10 + 50)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|20 (+5)|7 (-2)|11 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Immunities** Cold; Frozen|Monstersofdrakkenheim
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the troll takes Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Special Immunities.*** The troll is Immune to the Frozen condition(s).  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes two Slam attacks and one Bite attack. It can replace its Bite attack with Freezing Breath, if available.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Cold damage, and the target has the Slowed condition until the end of the troll's next turn. Critical *Hit:* The target has the Frozen condition (escape DC 16).  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Cold damage.  
>
>***Freezing Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Cold damage and has the Frozen condition (escape DC 16). *Success:* Half damage only.
