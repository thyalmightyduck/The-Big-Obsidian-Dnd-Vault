___
>## Restless Lumberer
>*Huge Giant, Lawful Neutral or Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 250 (20d12 + 120)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Climb 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +13 (23)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|25 (+7)|16 (+3)|22 (+6)|15 (+2)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Con +11, Int +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Athletics +12, Perception +6, Stealth +8
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold
>- **Immunities** Frightened, Unconscious
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Giant, two other languages
>- **Challenge** 16 (XP 15,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Hold Breath.*** The restless lumberer can hold its breath indefinitely while conscious.  
>
>***Lurker's Patience.*** The restless lumberer has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks it makes while motionless.  
>
>***Vice Grip.*** Any creature Grappled by the restless lumberer also has the Restrained condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The restless lumberer makes three attacks, using Grab or Slam in any combination.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 23 (3d10 + 7) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Grab.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +12, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 18 (2d10 + 7) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 22). The lumberer can grapple up to two creatures at a time, and can't make Slam attacks against other targets with a hand it is using to grapple.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Psychic Scar.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 20, one or two grappled creatures. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage, and the target is Stunned until the end of its next turn. *Success:* No damage.
