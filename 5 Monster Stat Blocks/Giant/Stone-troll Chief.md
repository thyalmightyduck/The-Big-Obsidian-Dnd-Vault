>## Stone-troll Chief
>*Large Giant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 76 (8d10 + 32)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** -1 (9)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|8 (-1)|18 (+4)|7 (-2)|7 (-2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +7
>- **Skills** Intimidation +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Poison; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
>- **Immunities** Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Greatclub*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 8
>- **Languages** Westron
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Hideous Toughness (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** If the Stone-troll Chief takes 14 damage or less that would reduce it to 0 hit points, it is reduced to 1 hit point instead.  
>
>***Sunlight Curse.*** The Stone-troll Chief becomes petrified if it ends its turn in sunlight.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The Stone-troll Chief makes two melee attacks. It cannot use its great club if it is grappling a creature.  
>
>***Great Club.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it is grappled (escape DC 16). If the target is Small or smaller, it is also restrained until this grapple ends. While grappling the target, the Stone-troll Chief has advantage on attack rolls against it and cannot use this attack against other targets.  
>
>***Yell of Triumph (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** Each creature of the Stone-troll Chief's choice that is within 30 feet of it and can hear it gains advantage on attack rolls and Charisma (Intimidation) checks until the start of the Stone-troll Chief's next turn. The Stone-troll Chief can then make one attack as a bonus action.
