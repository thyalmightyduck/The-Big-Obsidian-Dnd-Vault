___
>## Oak Troll
>*Huge Giant*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 92 (8d12 + 40)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|21 (+5)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, Giant
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Forest Sense.*** The troll can pinpoint the location of creatures and moving objects within 120 feet of it as long as both it and the anything it is detecting is in a natural forested area.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The troll regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the troll takes Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Shroud of Leaves.*** The troll is covered head to toe in a shroud of thick leaves. While it has this shroud it is immune to all damage except Fire damage. If the troll takes any Fire damage, the leaves all burn away revealing the gnarled oak wood body of the troll and it loses this trait.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The troll makes two Gnarled Claw attacks and one Bite attack  
>
>***Gnarled Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) Piercing damage and if the target is a creature, the target has the Bleeding condition (2d8) as thick splinters pierce and remain in the creature's flesh.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Abundant Growth.*** The troll regains its Shroud of Leaves trait.  
>
>***Leaf Burst.*** As long as the troll has its Shroud of Leaves, it can shed its Shroud of Leaves in a burst, losing the trait in the process. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature within 15 feet of the troll. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Slashing damage and has the Blinded condition until the end of their next turn.
