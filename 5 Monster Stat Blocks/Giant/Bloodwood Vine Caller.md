___
>## Bloodwood Vine Caller
>*Huge Giant, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 136 (13d12 + 52)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|19 (+4)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +6
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Bleeding Strikes.*** A creature hit by the giant's Blood Spear attack is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Bleeding condition [the following text has been removed due to homebrew: (see Grim Hollow Campaign Guide)].  
>
>***Forest Camouflage.*** The giant has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in forested terrain.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The giant makes two attacks, using Blood Spear or Rock in any combination. It can replace one of those attacks with Bloodvine.  
>
>***Blood Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 15 ft. or range 60/240 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Rock.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, range 60/240 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Bloodvine.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:*  +6, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d12 + 3) Piercing damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target makes a DC 14 Constitution saving throw, ending the condition on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** The giant casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Barkskin* (self only), *Druidcraft*, *Poison Spray* (at 5th level), *Spare the Dying*  
>3/day each: *Entangle*  
>1/day each: *Blight*, *Detect Poison and Disease*, *Faerie Fire*, *Find Traps*, *Pass Without Trace*  
>
>### Reactions
>***Bloodvine Drinker.*** *Trigger:* The giant damages a creature with a Bloodvine attack. *Response:* The giant regains 10 Hit Points.
