>*Huge Giant, Chaotic Evil*

>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 172 (15d12 + 75)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___

>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Wis +7
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Giant
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3

>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The giant makes three attacks, using Tree Club or Boulder in any combination.  
>
>***Tree Club.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>***Boulder.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, range 60/240 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Wild Magic Cataclysm (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point within 120 feet. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) damage (roll 1d4 to determine damage type: 1—Acid; 2—Fire; 3—Lightning; 4—Thunder). *Success:* Half damage.
