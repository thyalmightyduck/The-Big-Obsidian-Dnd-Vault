___
>## Mizanoros, the Black Iron Fulcrum
>*Huge Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 19 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 319 (22d12 + 176)
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Burrow 30 ft., Climb 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|26 (+8)|16 (+3)|27 (+8)|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|23 (+6)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +16, Int +12, Wis +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** All
>- **Challenge** 25 (XP 75,000; PB +8)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +8
>___
>***Special Equipment.*** Mizanoros wields Hatred, the Wrathful Edge, a sentient weapon with an Intelligence of 14, a Wisdom of 13, and a Charisma of 17. It has hearing and darkvision out to a range of 120 feet. The weapon communicates telepathically with Mizanoros and can speak, read, and understand Common and Infernal.  
>
>***Aura of Punishment.*** Whenever a hostile creature within 30 feet of Mizanoros takes damage, that creature takes an extra 5 (1d10) necrotic damage.  
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** Mizanoros's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 22, +14 to hit with spell attacks). Mizanoros can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:  
>
>At will: *detect evil and good*, *detect poison and disease*, *inflict wounds* (as a 3rd-level spell), *ray of enfeeblement*  
>1/day each: *dominate person*, *fly*, *suggestion*  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Mizanoros fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** Mizanoros has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Magic Weapons.*** Mizanoros's weapon attacks are magical.  
>
>***Reckless.*** At the start of its turn, Mizanoros can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against it have advantage until the start of its next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Mizanoros makes three attacks: one with its sting and two with Hatred, the Wrathful Edge. It can cast any of its at will spells in place of one Hatred, the Wrathful Edge attack.  
>
>***Sting.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (3d4 + 10) piercing plus 15 (3d8) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 24 Constitution saving throw or become poisoned. The poisoned target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.  
>
>***Boil Blood (Recharge 5–6).*** Mizanoros attempts to magically boil the blood of up to three creatures it can see within 120 feet of it. Each target must make a DC 22 Constitution saving throw, taking 41 (6d12) fire damage and 41 (6d12) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that is poisoned by Mizanoros's sting has disadvantage on this saving throw, and a creature that doesn't have blood is immune to this effect.  
>
>***Hatred, the Wrathful Edge.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +17 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (1d12 + 11) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) fire or lightning damage (Mizanoros's choice). If this damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, Mizanoros gains temporary hit points equal to the fire or lightning damage dealt.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Blood Transfer (3/Day).*** As a bonus action, Mizanoros can cast the *vampiric touch* spell. For the duration, he can repeat the attack on each of his turns as a bonus action.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Mizanoros can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Mizanoros regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Hatred Attack.** Mizanoros makes one attack with Hatred, the Wrathful Edge.
>- **Move.** Mizanoros moves up to his speed.
>- **Cast a Spell (Costs 2 Actions).** Mizanoros casts a spell.
>- **Accursed Step (Costs 3 Actions).** Mizanoros magically teleports, along with any equipment he is wearing and carrying, up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space he can see. Mizanoros can then choose one creature he can see within 5 feet of him. The target must make a DC 22 Constitution saving throw, taking 44 (8d10) necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. Mizanoros regains hit points equal to the amount of necrotic damage dealt.
