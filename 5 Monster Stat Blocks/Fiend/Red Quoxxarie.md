>## Red Quoxxarie
>*Large Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d10 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|5 (-3)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +0, Wis +4, Cha +1
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Athletics +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Infernal
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of Wrath.*** Provided the red quoxxarie doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, it emits a 30-foot Emanation of wrath. Any creature (including another red quoxxarie) that starts its turn in the Emanation can grant itself Advantage on attack rolls until the start of its next turn. Attack rolls against the creature also have Advantage until then. If the creature is Hostile to the quoxxarie and uses this aura, the creature also has Disadvantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma checks and saving throws until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The red quoxxarie has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Slippery.*** The red quoxxarie has Advantage on checks and saving throws made to avoid or end the Grappled condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The red quoxxarie makes one Ram attack and one Hooves attack.  
>
>***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the red quoxxarie moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 11 (2d10) damage and, if it is Large or smaller, has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 20 (3d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Aggressive.*** The red quoxxarie moves up to its Speed toward a Hostile creature it can see.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Get the Horns.*** *Trigger:* The red quoxxarie takes 25+ damage in a single turn from a creature outside its reach. *Response:* The red quoxxarie takes the Disengage action and moves up to half its Speed toward the creature. If it ends this movement adjacent to the creature, it can make a Ram attack against the creature with Advantage on the attack roll.
