___
>## Lenchtahg
>*Medium Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 110 (13d8 + 52)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +0 (10)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison, Radiant; Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Infernal
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Berserk.*** Whenever the lenchtahg starts its turn with the Bloodied condition, roll a d6. On a 1, the lenchtahg recalls its former life and current agony, going berserk. On each of its turns while berserk, the lenchtahg attacks the nearest creature it can see. It treats all creatures as Hostile and makes any Opportunity Attack it can. Also, it has Advantage on attack rolls, but all attack rolls against it have Advantage. If no creature is near enough to move to and attack, the lenchtahg moves as far as it can toward the closest creature.  
>Once the lenchtahg goes berserk, it remains so until it is destroyed or it loses the Bloodied condition. A Fiend can take an action to calm the lenchtahg with a successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check, suppressing this trait for 10 minutes.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The lenchtahg has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Radiant Absorption.*** Whenever the lenchtahg is targeted by Radiant damage from a source other than a lenchtahg, it takes no damage and instead regains Hit Points equal to half the Radiant damage dealt.  
>
>***Rejuvenation.*** If the lenchtahg's head survives its demise, the lenchtahg reappears in 6 days in a fiery explosion. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the lenchtahg when it rejuvenates. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.  
>If the lenchtahg's severed head is targeted by a *Dispel Evil and Good* spell or doused in holy water and ritually consecrated in a 1-hour ritual, it doesn't rejuvenate. The head can't rejuvenate on consecrated ground, like that created by a holy *Hallow* spell.  
>
>***Water Aversion.*** If drenched in 5 gallons or more of water, or any amount of *Holy Water*, the lenchtahg has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lenchtahg makes two attacks, using Claw or Radiant Gaze in any combination.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) Fire damage.  
>
>***Radiant Gaze.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature the lenchtahg can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Radiant damage. Cover provides the target no benefit for this save.
