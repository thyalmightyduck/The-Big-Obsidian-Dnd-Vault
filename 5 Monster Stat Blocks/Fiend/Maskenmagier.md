>## Maskenmagier
>*Medium Fiend, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +7 (17)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|17 (+3)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Int +7, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Deception +9, Insight +6, Perception +6, Sleight of Hand +7
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Devour Soul.*** maskenmagier can consume the soul of a Humanoid it has killed in the past hour by spending 10 minutes adjacent to its corpse. The target's soul is trapped in the maskenmagier for 24 hours. If the maskenmagier dies or the soul is raised from the dead before this duration ends, the soul is freed. Otherwise, it is digested and no mortal magic can restore it. The maskenmagier can digest only one soul at a time.  
>After digesting a soul, the maskenmagier gains a soul mask in the likeness of the devoured soul. The maskenmagier can use this mask with its Shape-Shift action for 13 days.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The maskenmagier has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Teleport (Recharge 4–6).*** As its movement, the maskenmagier can teleport to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The maskenmagier makes three attacks, using Claws or Soul Bolt in any combination  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Slashing damage plus 28 (8d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Soul Bolt.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 60 ft. *Hit:* 26 (4d12) Necrotic damage, or 39 (6d12) Necrotic damage while the maskenmagier is digesting a soul. The target can't regain Hit Points until the start of the maskenmagier's next turn.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The maskenmagier shape-shifts into a Small or Medium Humanoid or reverts to its true form. Other than its Size, its game statistics are the same in each form. If the maskenmagier has a soul mask in the likeness of the Humanoid it is shape-shifting into, it gains Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks to impersonate the Humanoid and instinctively knows the Humanoid's quirks and behaviors. Besides the soul mask, any equipment the maskenmagier is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The maskenmagier casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17, +9 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Mage Hand*, *Silent Image*, *Prestidigitation*  
>2/day each: *Charm Person*, *Greater Invisibility*, *Major Image*, *Mirror Image*, *Phantasmal Killer*  
>1/day each: *Cloudkill*, *Eyebite*, *Glibness*, *Mislead*, *Prismatic Spray*, *Weird*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Quick Casting.*** The maskenmagier makes a Soul Bolt attack or casts an at-will spell from Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Counterspell (3/Day).*** The maskenmagier casts *Counterspell* in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
