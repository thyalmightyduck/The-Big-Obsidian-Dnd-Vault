___
>## Pumpkin Head
>*Medium Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|13 (+1)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +6, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire
>- **Damage Resistances** Necrotic
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Understands Common plus one other language but only speaks using Mimicry
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Mimicry.*** The pumpkin head can mimic the voice of the person it replaced or someone it used Personality Consumption on. A creature that hears the voice can tell it's an imitation only with a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>***Sorrowful Memento.*** If the pumpkin head can see a creature holding a possession of the person the pumpkin head replaced, it has Disadvantage on D20 Tests.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The pumpkin head makes one Claw attack and one Pitchfork attack, and it can use Personality Consumption if available.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Pitchfork.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, the pumpkin head can push or pull it 5 feet.  
>
>***Personality Consumption (Recharge 5–6).*** The pumpkin head consumes a victim's memories. *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature the pumpkin head can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn, and its Intelligence score decreases by 1d4. The target dies if this reduces that score to 0. A Humanoid slain in this way is replaced by a **Pumpkin Head** 1d8 hours later unless its body is burned.
