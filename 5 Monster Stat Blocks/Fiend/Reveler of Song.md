___
>## Reveler of Song
>*Medium Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 97 (15d8 + 30)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Cha +8
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Perception +4, Performance +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison, Thunder; Deafened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 10 ft. (Drone only), Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Compelled Contest.*** The reveler can't refuse a contest of skill with Musical Instruments. As an Influence action, a creature can make a Charisma (Performance) check using a Musical Instrument. The reveler must succeed on a Charisma saving throw with a DC equal to the check's total or take 20 Psychic damage and have the Stunned condition until the end of the creature's next turn.  
>
>***Dancing Step.*** The reveler doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it leaves an enemy's reach.  
>
>***Ensemble Form.*** The reveler takes on a specific form: Drone, Drum, or Bell. The form determines certain traits and actions, as well as the instrument the reveler plays (hurdy gurdy, frame drum, or handbells, respectively).  
>
>***Droning Swarm (Drone Only).*** A droning swarm of bees surrounds the reveler. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature that ends its turn in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the reveler. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The reveler makes two Instrumental Blast attacks.  
>
>***Instrumental Blast.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. or range 90 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Thunder damage.  
>
>***Infectious Rhythm (Drum Only).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, up to two creatures the reveler can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Psychic damage, and the target is affected by the *Irresistible Dance* spell (no Concentration required to maintain the spell). *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Stunning Clangor (Bell Only).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the reveler can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 40 (9d8) Thunder damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of the reveler's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
