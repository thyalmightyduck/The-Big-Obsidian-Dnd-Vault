___
>## Mausgeist
>*Tiny Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 110 (17d4 + 68)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 15 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Int +4, Wis +5
>- **Skills** Perception +8, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't silvered
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Understands Abyssal but can't speak, telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The mausgeist has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The mausgeist uses Bite once and Arcane Whiskers once.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) Piercing damage and 13 (3d8) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The mausgeist shape-shifts into a Small Humanoid or reverts to its true form. Other than its Size, its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.  
>
>***Arcane Whiskers.*** The mausgeist uses one of the following effects at random, targeting a creature the mausgeist can see within 90 feet of itself (roll 1d6):  
>- **Eldritch Bolt.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Force damage.
>- **Fear Blast.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save (with Disadvantage if it can sense the mausgeist), ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.
>- **Fire Orb.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 21 (6d6) Fire damage.
>- **Gripping Shadows.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. It automatically escapes after 1 minute.
>- **Hungry Shadows.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage, and the target can't take Reactions until the end of the mausgeist's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
>- **Hypnotic Phantasm.** Intelligence Saving Throw: DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Charmed condition for 1 minute. While Charmed, the target has the Incapacitated condition and its Speed is 0. The effect ends early if the target takes damage or a creature takes an action to shake the target out of its stupor.
