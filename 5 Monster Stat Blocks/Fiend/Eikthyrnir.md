>### Actions
>***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (2d12 + 4) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Necrotic damage. If the target is Huge or smaller, and the eikthyrnir moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) Piercing damage and has the Prone condition.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Grasping Thorns.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15 A Large or smaller creature within 30 feet is pulled adjacent to the eikthyrnir and has the Grappled condition (Escape DC 15). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.
