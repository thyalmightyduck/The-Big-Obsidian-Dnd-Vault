___
>## Slaghund Pup
>*Tiny Fiend (Bespoke Companion, Crafted Familiar); Chaotic Neutral*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 4 (1d4 + 2)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___

>- **Skills** Perception +2
>- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Cold
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 1/8 (XP 25; PB equals your bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals your bonus
>___
>***Heatable Body.*** As a bonus action, the slaghund can cause its body to heat up, or return to a normal warm temperature. While its body is heated in this way, a creature that touches the slaghund or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 2 (1d4) fire damage.  
>
>***Heated Weapons.*** When the slaghund hits with a weapon attack, it deals an extra 2 (1d4) fire damage (included in attacks).  
>
>***Reactionary Flame.*** When the slaghund is surprised, it ignites everything flammable within 1 foot of it that isn't being carried or worn.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) fire damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) fire damage.
