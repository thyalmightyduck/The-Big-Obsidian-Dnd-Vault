___
>## The Brimstone Behemoth
>*Gargantuan Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 22
>- **Hit Points** 468 (24d20 + 216)
>- **Speed** 60 ft., Burrow 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +16 (26)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|30 (+10)|10 (+0)|28 (+9)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|26 (+8)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Int +12, Wis +11
>- **Skills** Arcana +12, Perception +11, Survival +11
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 21
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 25 (XP 75,000, or 90,000 in lair; PB +8)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +8
>___
>***Burning Sacrifice.*** If a creature willingly sacrifices its life in fire or lava for the purpose of defeating the behemoth, the creature can't be returned to life by any means. For the next 24 hours, the behemoth has Disadvantage on D20 Tests, its Speed is halved, it has Vulnerability to Radiant damage, and it loses its Legendary Resistance trait.  
>
>***Consuming Flame.*** When the behemoth deals Fire damage, it can choose to deal Necrotic damage instead.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day, or 5/Day in Lair).*** If the behemoth fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The behemoth has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The behemoth deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>***Tunneler.*** The behemoth can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrow Speed and leaves a 20-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The behemoth makes one Bite attack, and it makes two Claw or Blazing Boulder attacks in any combination.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +18, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 26 (3d10 + 10) Slashing damage plus 11 (2d10) Fire damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 20). Until the grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +18, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 23 (3d8 + 10) Slashing damage plus 5 (1d10) Fire damage.  
>
>***Blazing Boulder.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +18, range 150 ft. *Hit:* 15 (1d10 + 10) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Fire damage, and the target has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Cataclysmic Spellcasting (1/Day).*** The Brimstone Behemoth casts *Earthquake* and *Meteor Swarm*, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 24).  
>
>### Reactions
>***Hellish Rebuke.*** The behemoth casts *Hellish Rebuke* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Cataclysmic Spellcasting.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Fire Rain.** The behemoth makes one Blazing Boulder attack.
>- **Lava Well.** The behemoth causes lava to cover the ground in its space and in any unoccupied spaces in any tunnels it left by burrowing within 100 feet. A creature that touches the lava for the first time on a turn or starts its turn in the lava takes 55 (10d10) Fire damage. A creature takes this damage only once per round.
  The lava cools and hardens after 1 hour or when the behemoth uses this action again. The behemoth can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Trample.** The behemoth moves up to half its Speed, and it makes one Claw attack. On a hit, if the target is a creature, it has the Prone condition.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Brimstone Behemoth's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Relentless Blaze.** Within 10 miles of the lair, flames become driven to spread and consume. Every hour, contained fires in that area have a 10 percent chance of spreading to a flammable object within 10 feet. Uncontained fires spread out of control in 10 minutes.
>- **Tenacious Lava.** A creature that takes damage from lava within 1 mile of the lair starts burning, and it takes 1d6 Fire damage instead of 1d4 when it burns.
>
>If the Brimstone Behemoth dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
