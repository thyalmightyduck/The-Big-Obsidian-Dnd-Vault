___
>## Gray Quoxxarie
>*Large Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 133 (1214d10 + 56)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|17 (+3)|19 (+4)|8 (-1)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +2, Wis +4, Cha +2
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Athletics +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Infernal
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of Death.*** Provided the gray quoxxarie doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, it emits a 30-foot Emanation of deathly energy. Any Hostile creature that starts its turn in the Emanation has Disadvantage on death saving throws. If a Hostile creature in the Emanation regains Hit Points, halve the amount of Hit Points it regains.  
>
>***Bloody Eye.*** The gray quoxxarie and other Fiends within 120 feet of it know the current Hit Points of creatures within that range. The gray quoxxarie has Advantage on attack rolls against creatures with the Bloodied condition.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The gray quoxxarie has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The gray quoxxarie makes one Ram attack and one Hooves attack.  
>
>***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the gray quoxxarie moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) damage and, if it is Large or smaller, has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Get the Horns.*** *Trigger:* The gray quoxxarie takes 25+ damage in a single turn from a creature outside its reach. *Response:* The gray quoxxarie takes the Disengage action and moves up to half its Speed toward the creature. If it ends this movement adjacent to the creature, it can make a Ram attack against the creature with Advantage on the attack roll.
