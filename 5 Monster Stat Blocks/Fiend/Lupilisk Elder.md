>## Lupilisk Elder
>*Large Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 97 (13d10 + 26)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|6 (-2)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +8, Stealth +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** Can learn languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The lupilisk has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the lupilisk's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Poison damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. While Poisoned, the target has the Restrained condition. At the end of its next turn, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. Second *Failure:* The target has the Petrified condition permanently instead of the Poisoned condition.  
>
>***Pack Attack (Recharge 5–6).*** One lupilisk or lupilisk whelp the lupilisk elder can see within 30 feet can use its Reaction to move up to half its Speed and make a Bite attack. The target must be able to see or hear the lupilisk elder. Then the lupilisk elder makes a Bite attack.
