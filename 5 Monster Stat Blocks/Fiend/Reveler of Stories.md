___
>## Reveler of Stories
>*Large Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15; 17 (Hero only)
>- **Hit Points** 127 (15d10 + 45)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., 60 ft. (Hero only)
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +7, Wis +6
>- **Skills** Arcana +6, History +6, Perception +6, Performance +13
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Poisoned
>- **Gear** *Shield*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Truesight 120 ft. (Witch only), Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Moral Quandary.*** As a Bonus Action, a creature can recite the moral of a story using the same theme as the reveler's Storied Form to weaken it for 1 hour. While weakened, the reveler can't take Reactions, and its AC is reduced by 4.  
>
>***Storied Form.*** The reveler takes on a specific form: Great Beast, Hero, or Witch. The form determines certain traits and the theme of stories the reveler embodies (powerful monsters, brave heroes, or wicked magicians, respectively).  
>
>***Terror Feeding (Witch Only).*** If there is at least one creature with the Frightened condition within 30 feet of the reveler at the end of its turn, the reveler gains 15 Temporary Hit Points.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The reveler makes three Thrash attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Fabled Assault if available.  
>
>***Thrash.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Force damage.  
>
>***Fabled Assault (Recharge 5–6).*** The reveler uses the option determined by its Storied Form:  
>
>***Consuming Bite (Great Beast Only).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature within 5 feet (with Disadvantage if the reveler is grappling the target). *Failure:* 36 (8d8) Necrotic damage. If the target is Huge or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The reveler gains Temporary Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage taken.  
>
>***Heroic Charge (Hero Only).*** The reveler moves up to its Speed in a straight line without provoking Opportunity Attacks, and it can move through other creatures without expending extra movement to do so. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature whose space the reveler enters. *Failure:* 26 (4d12) Force damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Wicked Storm (Witch Only).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Lightning damage, and the target has the Frightened condition. While Frightened in this way, the target's Speed is 0. A Frightened target repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Dramatic Reversal.*** *Trigger:* The reveler is hit by an attack roll. *Response:* The reveler adds 4 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
