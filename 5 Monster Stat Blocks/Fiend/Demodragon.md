>## Demodragon
>*Huge Fiend (Demon)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 102 (12d12 + 24)
>- **Speed** 40 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|10 (+0)|15 (+2)|8 (-1)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +5, Wis +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** —
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Baleful Presence.*** Sources of light in a 120-foot Emanation originating from the demodragon flicker wildly. Nonmagical sources of Bright Light in that area instead shed Dim Light.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The demodragon regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns if it has at least 1 Hit Point  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The demodragon makes two Bite attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Acid damage.  
>
>***Acid Breath (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature in a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. *Failure:* 22 (4d10) Acid damage. *Success:* Half damage.
