>## Lupilisk
>*Large Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 60 (8d10 + 16)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|5 (-3)|13 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Can learn languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The lupilisk has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the lupilisk's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. First *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. While Poisoned, the target has the Restrained condition. At the end of its next turn, the target repeats the save. Second *Failure:* The target has the Petrified condition instead of the Poisoned condition. After 24 hours, the target repeats the save. Third *Failure:* The target has the Petrified condition permanently. If the target succeeds on any of its saves, the effect ends for it.
