>## Herald of Fools
>*Medium Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 17
>- **Hit Points** 153 (18d8 + 72)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|22 (+6)|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +10, Insight +6, Performance +7
>- **Immunities** Exhaustion
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Common
>- **Challenge** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Endless Footwork.*** When the herald's Speed is 0, attack rolls made against it have Advantage, and if its Speed is 0 at the start of its turn, it takes 20 Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Perfect Grace.*** The herald doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it leaves an enemy's reach.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The herald makes three Banner Pole attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Lethal Jape.  
>
>***Banner Pole.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10 (with Advantage if the target's Speed is 0), reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d10 + 6) Slashing damage plus 13 (3d8) Thunder damage, and if the target is a Large or smaller creature, the herald can move it to any other unoccupied space within the Banner Pole's reach.  
>
>***Lethal Jape.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, a creature the herald can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 24 (7d6) Psychic damage, and the target's Speed is 0 until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Wave the Banner.*** *Trigger:* An ally of the herald's within 60 feet misses with an attack roll or fails a saving throw. *Response:* The ally can reroll the d20 and must use the new roll.
