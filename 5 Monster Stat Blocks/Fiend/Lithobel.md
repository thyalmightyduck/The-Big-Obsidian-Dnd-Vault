>## Lithobel
>*Large Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 18
>- **Hit Points** 189 (18d10 + 90)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|20 (+5)|5 (-3)|8 (-1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +4, Wis +3, Cha +2
>- **Skills** Athletics +9, Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Understands Infernal and the languages it knew in life but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Two Heads.*** The lithobel has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Blinded, Deafened, Stunned, or Unconscious conditions.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lithobel makes four attacks using Battleaxe, Mace, Shield Bash, or Spear in any combination.  
>
>***Battleaxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) Force damage.  
>
>***Mace.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (3d6 + 5) Force damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Shield Bash.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (3d6 + 5) Force damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it is pushed 10 feet away from the lithobel.  
>
>***Spear.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Force damage. *Hit or Miss:*  If thrown, the spear reappears in the lithobel's hand immediately after the attack.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampage.*** After reducing a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, the lithobel moves up to half its Speed and makes a melee attack.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Parry.*** *Trigger:* A creature the lithobel can see hits it with an attack. *Response:* The lithobel adds 4 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
