___
>## Malcinder Alpha
>*Large Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 97 (13d10 + 26)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|5 (-3)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +6, Stealth +5
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Understands Infernal but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Fiery Body.*** A creature that touches the malcinder alpha or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 5 (2d4) Fire damage.  
>
>***Pack Leader.*** The malcinder alpha and each **malcinder** within 30 feet of it gains Advantage on attacks against a creature if at least one other allied malcinder is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The malcinder alpha makes three attacks, using Claw or Tail Flame in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Bite  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the malcinder alpha moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition and the malcinder alpha can make a Bite attack against it as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Tail Flame.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. or ranged 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) Fire damage.
