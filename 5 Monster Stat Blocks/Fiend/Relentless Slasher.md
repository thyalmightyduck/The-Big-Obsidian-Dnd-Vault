___
>## Relentless Slasher
>*Medium Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 28)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +4 (14)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|12 (+1)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Dex +7, Con +5, Wis +5
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands all but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 8 (XP 3,900; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (1/Day).*** If the slasher fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The slasher makes two Slasher's Knife attacks.  
>
>***Slasher's Knife.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. or range 30/60 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Slashing damage plus 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the relentless slasher can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The relentless slasher regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Homing Knife.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature within 120 feet that the slasher has hit with its Slasher's Knife attack within the last 24 hours. *Failure:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage.
>- **Vanishing Strike.** The slasher makes one Slasher's Knife attack, then teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. The slasher can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
