___
>## Rat God
>*Huge Fiend (Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 25
>- **Hit Points** 116 (8d12 + 64) plus 116 (8d12 + 64) per player character
>- **Speed** 50 ft., Fly 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +11 (21)
>___

>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|29 (+9)|17 (+3)|26 (+8)|25 (+7)|30 (+10)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +16, Con +15, Wis +17
>- **Skills** Arcana +14, Insight +17, Intimidation +12, Perception +24
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Thunder
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Poisoned, Stunned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 34
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 1 mile
>- **Challenge** 23 (XP 50,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Mass of Tails.*** Ranged attacks against the Rat God from further than 60 feet away have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Verminous Aura.*** The Rat God is surrounded by a 60-foot radius of vermin, pouring out of it and spilling over into the area as they claw and gnash. The area is Difficult Terrain and a creature that starts its turn in the area takes 21 (6d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***The Rat God always takes its turn on Initiative count 20.*** On its turn, the Rat God can use its movement and interaction as normal. However, it does not take an action or Bonus Action on its turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the Rat God takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Flail of Skulls.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +16, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 28 (3d12 + 9) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Burning condition (3d6 Acid).
>- **Great Feeding.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 26, each creature within 60 feet of the Rat God. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Piercing damage, and the target has the Sickened condition (save ends). *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The Rat God regains Hit Points equal to the total damage dealt to all targets.
>
>***Rodent Rifts (1/Round).*** The Rat God teleports to an unoccupied space within 120 feet. Each creature within 15 feet of the Rat God when it arrives is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 26. *Failure:* 28 (8d6) Necrotic damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away from the Rat God.  
>
>***Warp Bolt.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 22, one creature the Rat God can see within 120 feet. *Failure:* 23 (4d12) Necrotic damage, and the target suffers a Contamination level.  
>
>***Infernal Chittering.*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 26, one creature the Rat God can see within 60 feet that can hear the Rat God. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Weakened condition (save ends).  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** The Rat God chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on it. The Rat God can use this action even if it would otherwise be unable to take actions, its actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters its game statistics.
