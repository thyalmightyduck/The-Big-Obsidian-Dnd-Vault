___
>## Corvodaemon
>*Medium Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 84 (13d8 + 26)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|15 (+2)|19 (+4)|12 (+1)|11 (+0)|19 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +5
>- **Skills** Deception +8, Perception +2, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Acid, Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Common; telepathy 90 ft.
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Ashen Forbiddance.*** The corvodaemon can't cross a line of ashes created by burning feathers sprinkled with Holy Water.  
>
>***Demonic Restoration.*** If the corvodaemon dies outside the Abyss, its body dissolves into ichor, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its Hit Points somewhere in the Abyss.  
>
>***Devour Anima.*** A creature the corvodaemon reduced to 0 Hit Points is cursed until it regains any Hit Points. When the cursed creature fails a Death Saving Throw at the start of its turn, it dies regardless of how many failures it has.  
>
>***Mimicry.*** The corvodaemon can mimic the voice of any creature it has heard, or of a creature known to one of the creatures within range of its telepathy. A hearer can discern the voices are imitations with a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The corvodaemon can use Feeding Frenzy if available. It then makes one Beak attack and one Claws attack.  
>
>***Beak.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Feeding Frenzy (Recharge 5–6).*** The corvodaemon enters a voracious feeding frenzy, driven by its insatiable hunger for souls. Until the start of its next turn, the corvodaemon's Speed doubles, and when it damages a creature, it gains Temporary Hit Points equal to half the damage taken.
