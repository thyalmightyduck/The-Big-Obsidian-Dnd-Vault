>## The Whispered Promise
>*Large Fiend (Demon, Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20
>- **Hit Points** 125 (7d20 + 49) plus 125 (7d20 + 49) per player character
>- **Speed** 60 ft.
>- **Initiative** +15 (25)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|20 (+5)|27 (+8)|21 (+5)|25 (+7)|21 (+5)|30 (+10)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +12, Int +14, Wis +12, Cha +17
>- **Skills** Deception +17, Insight +12, Perception +12, Persuasion +24
>- **Immunities** Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 22
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 1 mile
>- **Challenge** 21 (XP 33,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Alluring Aura.*** The Whispered Promise projects a 30-foot Emanation of beguiling enchantment. A creature that ends its turn in the Emanation has the Charmed condition for 1 hour. Each time the creature takes damage other than Psychic damage, it makes a DC 25 Wisdom saving throw. On a success, the Charmed condition ends for that creature, and it is immune to the Alluring Aura of the Whispered Promise for 24 hours.  
>
>***Change Shape.*** The Whispered Promise has no true form and chooses how they manifest to each creature (no action required). Their form can be anything they wish; they can appear like a creature, object, or even as a multisensory representation of an abstract concept. They can appear to be any form that is Large or smaller, but their game statistics are the same in each form.  
>
>***Glibness.*** When they make a Charisma (Deception) check, the Whispered Promise treats any roll of 14 or less on the d20 as 15.  
>
>***Inscrutable.*** The Whispered Promise is immune to any effect that would sense their emotions or read their thoughts, as well as any divination spell that they refuse. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the Whispered Promise's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Unravelling Resolve.*** Creatures grappled by the Whispered Promise can't benefit from Resistance and Immunity to Psychic damage or the Charmed condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Spellcasting (Psionics; 1/Round).*** The Whispered Promise casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 25):  
>
>At will: *Detect Thoughts*, *Suggestion*  
>3/day each: *Dimension Door*, *Dream*, *Invisibility*, *Major Image (level 4 version)*, *Sending*  
>1/day each: *Dominate Monster*, *Mass Suggestion*, *Project Image*, *Scrying*  
>1/year: *Wish*  
>
>***The Whispered Promise always takes their turn on Initiative count 20.*** On their turn, the Whispered Promise can use their movement and interaction as normal. However, they do not take an action or Bonus Action on their turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, the Whispered Promise takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Tendril.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +17, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 28 (4d8 + 10) Force damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 25). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.
>- **Whispers (1/Round).** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 25. one creature the Whispered Promise can see within 300 feet. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Psychic damage, and the target is Dazed (save ends). *Success:* Half damage. Failure by five or more: Double damage, and the target is Stunned until the end of its next turn.
>
>***Promises (1/Round).*** *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 25, one creature the Whispered Promise can see within 300 feet. *Failure:* 35 (10d6) Psychic damage, and the target is Jinxed (1d10) (save ends). *Success:* Half damage. Failure by 5 or more: Double damage, and the target becomes cursed. While cursed, the target can't regain Hit Points and gains no benefits from taking a Short or Long Rest.  
>
>***Unfettered Glide.*** The Whispered Promise moves up to their Speed without provoking Opportunity Attack action.  
>
>***Dominate (1/Round).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 25, one creature the Whispered Promise can see within 300 feet. *Failure:* The creature has the Charmed condition until the end of its next turn. While Charmed, the target obeys the Whispered Promise's commands and can't take Reactions. Failure by 5 or More: The Charmed condition instead lasts for 24 hours or until the Whispered Promise is reduced to 0 Hit Points. Each time the target takes damage, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. *Success:* The target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll against the Whispered Promise.  
>
>***Epic Resistances.*** The Whispered Promise chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting them and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on them. The Whispered Promise can use this action even if they would otherwise be unable to take actions, their actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters their game statistics.
