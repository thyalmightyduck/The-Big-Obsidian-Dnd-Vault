>## Cuddles
>*Large Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14
>- **Hit Points** 85 (10d10 + 30)
>- **Speed** 50 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +7
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17
>- **Languages** Understands Infernal but can't speak, Telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Maggoty Hide.*** A creature attacking Cuddles in melee that is adjacent to Cuddles is subject to the maggots crawling over its rank fur. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target is infested with maggots, taking 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage at the beginning of each of its turns. An infested target can make another saving throw at the end of each of its turns to end the effect, and the maggots are burnt away if it takes at least 5 Acid or Fire damage. After the creature successfully saves or the maggots are burnt away, it has Immunity to this trait for 1 hour.  
>
>***Pack Tactics.*** Cuddles has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of Cuddles's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** Cuddles makes two bite attacks.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage.  
>
>***Earth Rending Howl (Recharge 6).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 13 Each creature in a 20-foot Cone. *Failure:* 17 (5d6) Thunder damage, and the target has the Deafened condition for 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only. Unattended objects and structures in the area are not allowed a saving throw, they take double damage, and the howl ignores their damage threshold.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rotten Shake (Recharge 5–6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13 Each creature within 5 feet of Cuddles. *Failure:* The target is infested with maggots (see Maggoty Hide).
