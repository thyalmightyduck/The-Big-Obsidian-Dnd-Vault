>## Doomcaller
>*Medium Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +13 (23)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|17 (+3)|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|21 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +8, Int +7, Wis +6, Cha +10
>- **Skills** Arcana +7, Deception +10, Insight +6, Perception +6, Persuasion +10, Religion +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Acid, Poison; Charmed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** All
>- **Challenge** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +5
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The doomcaller has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects  
>
>### Actions
>***Flame Burst.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 120 ft. *Hit:* 27 (6d8) Fire damage. If the target is an object that isn't being worn or carried, it ignites.  
>
>***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll. Additionally, the doomcaller's allies have Advantage on attack rolls against the target until the start of the doomcaller's next turn.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The doomcaller casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18, +10 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Detect Magic*, *Hunter Sense*, *Magic Missile*, *True Strike*  
>2/day each: *Banishment*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Bestow Curse*, *Consume Mind*, *Fireball*  
>1/day each: *Arboreal Curse*, *Disintegrate*, *Gate*, *Mind Blank**, *Scrying*  
>
>*The doomcaller casts this spell on itself before combat.  
>
>***Summon Fiend (1/Day).*** The doomcaller chooses what to summon and attempts a magical summoning. A doomcaller has a 60 percent chance of summoning 1d6 **succubi** or 1d4 **barbed devils**. A summoned fiend appears in an unoccupied space within 60 feet of the doomcaller, does as it pleases, and can't summon other fiends. A summoned fiend remains for 1 minute, until it or the doomcaller dies, or until the doomcaller dismisses it as a Bonus Action.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Misty Step (3/Day).*** The doomcaller casts *Misty Step* using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Ill Fortune (3/Day).*** *Trigger:* A creature the doomcaller can see makes a D20 Test. *Response:* The doomcaller rolls a d20 and can choose to use this roll in place of the triggering creature's roll.  
>
>***Magic Mirror (1/Day).*** The doomcaller casts *Magic Mirror* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
