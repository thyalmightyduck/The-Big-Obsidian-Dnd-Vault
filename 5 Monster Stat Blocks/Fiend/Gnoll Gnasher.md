___
>## Gnoll Gnasher
>*Medium Fiend (Gnoll, Retainer); Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (medium armor)
>- **Hit Points** Eight times their level (number of d10 Hit Dice equal to their level)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|12 (+1)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str 3+PB, Dex 1+PB, Con 1+PB, Int +PB, Wis +PB, Cha +PB
>- **Skills** Athletics +3 plus PB, Intimidation +0 plus PB
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Common, Gnoll
>- **Challenge** None (XP 0; PB equals the mentor's bonus)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** equals the mentor's bonus
>___
>### Actions
>***Signature Attack (Bite).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +3 plus damage. Beginning at 7th level, the gnasher can make this attack twice, instead of once, when they take the Attack action on their turn.  
>
>***3rd Level: Frenzied Bite (3/Day).*** When a creature the gnasher can see within 30 feet of them is reduced to 0 hit points, the gnasher can use a reaction to move up to half their speed and then make a signature attack. On a hit, the attack deals an extra PB piercing damage.  
>
>***5th Level: Flurry of Fangs (3/Day).*** As an action, the gnasher makes a signature attack against up to PB creatures within 5 feet of them.  
>
>***7th Level: Horrific Feast (1/Day).*** When the gnasher reduces a creature to 0 hit points with their signature attack, they can consume part of the creature as part of the attack, gaining PBd10 temporary hit points. Each enemy within 30 feet of the gnasher who can see them must succeed on a DC 10 plus PB Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of the gnasher until the end of the gnasher's next turn.
