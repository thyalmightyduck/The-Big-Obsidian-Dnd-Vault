___
>## Lupilisk Whelp
>*Medium Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 12
>- **Hit Points** 22 (4d8 + 4)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|3 (-4)|11 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Can learn languages but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The lupilisk has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the lupilisk's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Poison damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition. While Poisoned, the target has the Restrained condition. At the end of its next turn, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. Second *Failure:* The target has the Petrified condition instead of the Poisoned condition. The effect ends after 1 minute.
