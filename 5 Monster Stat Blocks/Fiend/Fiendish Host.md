>## Fiendish Host
>*Medium Fiend (Demon)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 135 (18d8 + 54)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 40 ft. (Hover)
>- **Initiative** +6 (16)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Wis +6, Cha +9
>- **Skills** Arcana +8, Insight +6, Intimidation +9
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Common, Infernal
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The host makes two Fiery Claws attacks and uses Hellfire Chain, if available.  
>
>***Fiery Claws.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +9, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 19 (4d6 + 5) Fire damage, and the target has the Burning condition (4d6 Fire).  
>
>***Hellfire Chain (Recharge 5–6).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature the host can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Fire damage, and the target has the Restrained condition. While Restrained, the target takes 10 (3d6) Fire damage at the start of each of its turns. At the end of each of its turns, the target makes a DC 17 Strength check, ending the effect on itself on a success.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The host casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17, +9 to spell attacks):  
>
>At will: *Thaumaturgy*  
>2/day each: *Command*, *Darkness*  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Fiendish Agility.*** The host takes the Dash or Disengage action.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Hellish Rebuke.*** The host casts *Hellish Rebuke* (DC 17, level 4 version) in response to that spell's trigger, using the spellcasting ability in Spellcasting.
