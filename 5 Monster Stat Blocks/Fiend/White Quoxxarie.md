___
>## White Quoxxarie
>*Large Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 104 (11d10 + 44)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|17 (+3)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|7 (-2)|12 (+1)|5 (-3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +1, Wis +4, Cha +0
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +5, Athletics +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Infernal
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of Sickness.*** Provided the white quoxxarie doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, it emits a 30-foot Emanation of sickening energy. A Hostile creature that starts its turn in the Emanation is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn.  
>
>***Disease Carrier.*** The white quoxxarie has Immunity to the effects of diseases but can carry and spread them.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The white quoxxarie has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The white quoxxarie makes one Ram attack and one Hooves attack.  
>
>***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the white quoxxarie moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) damage and, if it is Large or smaller, has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Get the Horns.*** *Trigger:* The white quoxxarie takes 25+ damage in a single turn from a creature outside its reach. *Response:* The white quoxxarie takes the Disengage action and moves up to half its Speed toward the creature. If it ends this movement adjacent to the creature, it can make a Ram attack against the creature with Advantage on the attack roll.
