___
>## Malikirian Imp
>*Tiny Fiend, Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11
>- **Hit Points** 15 (6d4)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|7 (-2)|13 (+1)|10 (+0)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Wis +3, Cha +4
>- **Skills** Deception +4, Insight +3, Stealth +3
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Infernal plus two other languages
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Creeping Corruption.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the imp is subjected to the following effect. *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 12 (a creature with Immunity to the Charmed condition automatically succeeds). First *Failure:* The creature acts in accordance with its most selfish, venal, and ruthless impulses to the best of its ability. After 24 hours, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. Second *Failure:* The compulsion lasts until removed by a *Remove Curse* spell or similar magic.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The imp has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  -1, reach 0 ft. *Hit:* 1 (1d4 - 2) Piercing damage. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 10. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Poison damage.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The imp casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12):  
>
>At will: *Invisibility* (self only), *Minor Illusion*  
>1/day each: *Detect Thoughts*, *Sleep*, *Suggestion*
