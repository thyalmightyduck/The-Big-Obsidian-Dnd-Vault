___
>## Gnoll Mage-Gorger
>*Medium Fiend (Gnoll, Minion); typically Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13 (*hide armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 11
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|11 (+0)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Gnoll
>- **Challenge** 4 (XP 220; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Life Eater.*** When an enemy the mage-gorger can see within 30 feet of them regains hit points, the mage-gorger chooses one non-minion ally they can see within 30 feet of them. That ally regains 2 hit points.  
>
>***Minion.*** If the mage-gorger takes damage from an attack or as the result of a failed saving throw, their hit points are reduced to 0. If the mage-gorger takes damage from another effect, they die if the damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum; otherwise they take no damage.  
>
>### Actions
>***Wizard Ripper (Group Attack).*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 slashing damage plus 2 acid, cold, fire, or lightning damage (mage-gorger's choice), and until the start of the mage-gorger's next turn, the target has disadvantage on Constitution saving throws made to maintain concentration, including any Constitution saving throw triggered by this attack. Only one damage type is chosen for this attack regardless of how many mage-gorgers joined it.
