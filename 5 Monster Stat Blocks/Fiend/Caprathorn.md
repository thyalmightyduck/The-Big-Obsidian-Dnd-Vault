>## Caprathorn
>*Huge Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 21
>- **Hit Points** 250 (20d12 + 120)
>- **Speed** 40 ft. fly 40 ft. (hover)
>- **Initiative** +9 (19)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|25 (+7)|14 (+2)|23 (+6)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|10 (+0)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +9, Wis +10, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Perception +10
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** All, telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 21 (XP 33,000; PB +7)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +7
>___
>***Implosion.*** The caprathorn implodes and leaves behind a *Sphere of Annihilation* when it dies. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 21, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the caprathorn when it dies. *Failure:* The target is pulled 10 feet toward the sphere.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If the caprathorn fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The caprathorn has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Thornlamms.*** A caprathorn typically has three inanimate **Thornlamms** attached to its horns with vines, and can have up to six thornlamms at a time. Attached thornlamms don't take damage separately, but whenever the caprathorn takes 50 or more damage in a single turn, an attached thornlamm dies.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The caprathorn makes three attacks using Gore, Stomp, or Thorn in any combination.  
>
>***Gore.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 25 (4d8 + 7) Force damage. If the caprathorn moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 18 (4d8) Force damage and, if it is Large or smaller, is pushed 10 feet away and has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Stomp.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 25 (4d8 + 7) Force damage.  
>
>***Thorns.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +14, reach 10 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 21 (4d6 + 7) Force damage. Being within 5 feet of a Hostile creature doesn't impose Disadvantage on the caprathorn's Thorn attack.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the caprathorn can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The caprathorn regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Attack.** The caprathorn makes a Stomp or Gore attack.
>- **Thornlamm Cultivation.** The caprathorn uses Pluck Thornlamm. If it hasn't taken damage from a silvered weapon this round, it can instead grow a new **Thornlamm**. The caprathorn can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Entomb.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 18, a creature with 0 Hit Points that the caprathorn can see within 30 feet. *Failure:* The target is entombed deep within the earth in a sphere of magical force, as if by the Burial option of the *Imprisonment* spell. *Success:* The target is immune to Entomb for 24 hours. *Failure or Success:* The caprathorn can't use this action again until the start of its next turn.
  The caprathorn can Entomb only one target a time. If the target regains any Hit Points or the caprathorn dies, the effect ends, returning the target to the last space it occupied. If the target dies while entombed, the caprathorn's Annihilation Field recharges.
>- **Annihilation Field (Recharges after a Long Rest).** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the caprathorn. *Failure:* 38 (7d10) Force damage. The Emanation destroys Small and Tiny nonmagical objects and damages Medium or larger ones, except those being worn or carried. Creations of magical force, such as the *Wall of Force* spell, are destroyed by the Emanation. A creature or object reduced to 0 Hit Points by the Emanation is disintegrated, turning to a fine ash. *Success:* Half damage.
