___
>## Jungeist
>*Tiny Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 49 (9d4 + 27)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Burrow 15 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|5 (-3)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|13 (+1)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +4
>- **Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't silvered
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands Abyssal but can't speak, telepathy 30 ft.
>- **Challenge** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The jungeist has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The jungeist uses Bite once and Arcane Whiskers once.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) Piercing damage and 7 (2d6) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Arcane Whiskers.*** The jungeist uses one of the following effects at random, targeting a creature the jungeist can see within 60 feet of itself (roll 1d4):  
>- **Eldritch Bolt.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Force damage.
>- **Fear Blast.** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save (with Disadvantage if it can sense the jungeist), ending the effect on itself on a success. It automatically succeeds after 1 minute.
>- **Sickening Ray.** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* 9 (2d8) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the jungeist's next turn.
>- **Gripping Shadows.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. It automatically escapes after 1 minute.
