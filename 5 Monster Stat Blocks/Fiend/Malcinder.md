>## Malcinder
>*Medium Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +4
>- **Immunities** Fire
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
>- **Languages** Understands Infernal but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The malcinder makes two attacks, using Claw or Tail Flame in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Bite.  
>
>***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the malcinder moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition and the malcinder can make a Bite attack against it as a Bonus Action.  
>
>***Tail Flame.*** *Melee  or Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4, reach 5 ft. or ranged 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 5 (2d4) Fire damage.
