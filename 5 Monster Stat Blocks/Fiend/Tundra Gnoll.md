___
>## Tundra Gnoll
>*Medium Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 38 (7d8 + 7)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +4
>- **Immunities** Cold
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Gnoll
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Arctic Camouflage.*** The tundra gnoll has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in snowy or icy conditions.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bone Axe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Slashing damage.  
>
>***Bone Bow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Freezing Glare.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 12 One creature within 20 feet of the tundra gnoll. *Failure:* The creature's Speed is reduced to 0 feet until the end of its next turn by a supernatural chill.  
>
>### Bonus Actions
>***Rampage (2/Day).*** Immediately after dealing damage to a Bloodied creature, the tundra gnoll moves up to half its Speed and makes one Bone Axe attack.
