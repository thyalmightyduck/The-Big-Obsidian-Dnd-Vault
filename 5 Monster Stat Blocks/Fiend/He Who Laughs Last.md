___
>## He Who Laughs Last
>*Gargantuan Fiend (Demon, Epic Boss)*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 21
>- **Hit Points** 125 (7d20 + 49) plus 125 (7d20 + 49) per player character
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +11 (21)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|27 (+8)|17 (+3)|25 (+7)|23 (+6)|20 (+5)|27 (+8)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Con +15, Int +14, Wis +13, Cha +16
>- **Skills** Deception +24, Insight +13, Perception +13, Persuasion +24
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 23
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 1 mile
>- **Challenge** 27 (XP 105,000; PB +8)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +8
>___
>***Inscrutable.*** He Who Laughs Last is immune to any effect that would sense his emotions or read his thoughts, as well as any divination spell that he refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain the demon lord's intentions or sincerity have Disadvantage.  
>
>***Mortal Coil.*** He Who Laughs Last projects a 150-foot Emanation of death and decay. A creature that ends its turn in the Emanation suffers an Exhaustion level and ages 10 (3d6) years. The creature removes an Exhaustion level gained in this way at the end of its turn, but only if it ends its turn outside the Emanation. A creature's original age can be restored with *Greater Restoration* or similar magic.  
>
>### Actions
>***He Who Laughs Last always takes his turn on Initiative count 20.*** On his turn, He Who Laughs Last can use his movement and interaction as normal. However, he does not take an action or Bonus Action on his turn unless forced to do so by a spell or other effect. Instead, He Who Laughs Last takes one of the following Epic Actions at the end of each player character's turn:  
>- **Scornful Whip.** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +16, reach 120 ft. *Hit:* 30 (5d8 + 8) Force damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it is pulled 30 feet toward He Who Laughs Last.
>- **Walking Shadow (1/Round).** He Who Laughs Last moves up to half his Speed.
>
>***Sleep No More (1/Round).*** He Who Laughs Last casts *Power Word Kill*.  
>
>***Out Brief Candle (1/Round).*** He Who Laughs Last casts *Finger of Death* (save DC 24).  
>
>***Dusty Death (1/Round).*** He Who Laughs Last casts *Disintegrate* (save DC 24).  
>
>***Cage of Sorrows (1/Round).*** He Who Laughs Last creates a wall of bone on a solid surface it can see within 120 feet of himself. The wall appears as a flat surface made up of one 10-foot-square panel. The wall is 1 foot thick and lasts for 1 minute or until the wall is destroyed (AC 15, 20 Hit Points, Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage, Vulnerability to Bludgeoning and Thunder damage).  
>If the wall cuts through a creature's space when it appears, the creature is pushed to one side of the wall (GM's choice) and subjected to the following effect. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Piercing damage.  
>
>***Epic Resistance.*** He Who Laughs Last chooses one condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting him and rolls 1d20. On an 11+, the effect ends on him. He Who Laughs Last can use this action even if he would otherwise be unable to take actions, his actions are being controlled by another creature, or if a spell or effect alters his game statistics.  
>
>***Spellcasting (1/Round).*** He Who Laughs Last casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 26):  
>
>At will: *Animate Dead* (level 9 version), *Speak with Dead*  
>3/day each: *Create Undead* (level 9 version), *Dispel Magic*, *Dream*, *Project Image*, *Scrying*, *Teleport*  
>1/year: *Wish*
