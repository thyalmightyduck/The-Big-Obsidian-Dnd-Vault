___
>## Poppet Fiend
>*Tiny Fiend (Devil, Shapechanger); Lawful Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 31 (9d4 + 9)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|17 (+3)|13 (+1)|11 (+0)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Deception +4, Insight +3, Persuasion +4, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't silvered
>- **Immunities** Fire, Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft. (unimpeded by magical Darkness), Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** One national language, Infernal, telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***False Appearance.*** While the poppet fiend remains motionless, it's indistinguishable from an ordinary doll.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The poppet fiend has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) Slashing damage, and if the target is a creature, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The poppet fiend shape-shifts into a doll or reverts to its true form, that of a Tiny imp-like devil. Its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed. A *Remove Curse* spell cast on it while it is Unconscious or dead forcibly reverts it to its true form, then causes it to vanish in a puff of vile smoke.
