___
>## Gnoll Abyssal Archer
>*Medium Fiend (Gnoll, Artillery); typically Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 14 (*hide armor*)
>- **Hit Points** 18 (4d8)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|10 (+0)|10 (+0)|13 (+1)|8 (-1)|
>___
>- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +4, Survival +3
>- **Gear** *Longbow*
>- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
>- **Languages** Abyssal, Gnoll
>- **Challenge** 1/2 (XP 100; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Bloodscent.*** The abyssal archer knows the location of each creature who doesn't have all their hit points within 60 feet of them.  
>
>### Actions
>***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.  
>
>***Longbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:*  +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage, and the target's walking speed is reduced by a cumulative 5 feet until they regain at least 1 hit point.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Death Frenzy.*** When an ally the abyssal archer can see within 30 feet of them is reduced to 0 hit points, the abyssal archer moves up to half their speed and makes a Bite attack.
