>## The Harvest Terror
>*Large Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 105 (10d10 + 50)
>- **Speed** 30 ft., Fly 90 ft.
>- **Initiative** +10 (20)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|19 (+4)|19 (+4)|20 (+5)|13 (+1)|15 (+2)|18 (+4)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Con +8
>- **Skills** Deception +10, Perception +8, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, and Poisoned (except for its Unraveling Song trait)
>- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 18
>- **Languages** All; telepathy 120 ft.
>- **Challenge** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Demonic Mimicry.*** The terror can mimic voices and sounds it has heard. A listener can discern the mimicry for what it is with a successful DC 18 Wisdom (Insight) check.  
>
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the terror fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The terror has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>***Unraveling Song.*** As an Influence action, a creature can play or sing Jericho Sticks' favorite song, "Coal-Eyed Birds," to give the terror the Charmed condition until the end of the creature's next turn. The condition ends early if the creature damages the terror or forces it to make a saving throw.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The terror makes one Claw attack and one Scythe attack.  
>
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Scythe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Psychic damage.  
>
>***Dirge of Secrets (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature within a 30-foot Emanation originating from the terror. *Failure:* The creature takes 11 (2d10) Psychic damage and has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls. At the end of each of its turns, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.  
>After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. *Success:* Half damage only.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, The can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.*
>
>- **Averting Winds.** The terror casts the *Wind Wall* spell (which can't appear in a creature's space), requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability.  
>
>- **Crow's Swoop.** The terror moves up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
>- **Devour Secrets.** The terror's beak gapes and devours secrets. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the terror (with Disadvantage if the target has given up one of its secrets to the terror). *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Psychic damage, and the target's Speed is 0 until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* The terror can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>
>### Regional Effects
>The region containing the Harvest Terror's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:
>- **Amber Eyes.** Within 1 mile of the lair, amber-eyed crows watch everything. Enemies of the Harvest Terror have Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in the region.
>- **Spilled Secrets.** Within 1 mile of the lair, creatures other than the Harvest Terror have Disadvantage on Charisma (Deception) checks made to keep secrets.
>
>If the Harvest Terror dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
