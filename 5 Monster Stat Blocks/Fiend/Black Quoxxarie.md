>## Black Quoxxarie
>*Medium Fiend, Chaotic Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 105 (14d8 + 42)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|17 (+3)|7 (-2)|12 (+1)|7 (-2)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +1, Wis +4, Cha +1
>- **Skills** Acrobatics +6, Athletics +6
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning
>- **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 11
>- **Languages** Infernal
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Aura of Withering.*** Provided the black quoxxarie doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, it emits a 30-foot Emanation of withering energy. Each time a Hostile creature takes damage in the Emanation, it takes an extra 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage. The Emanation kills tiny inanimate nonmagical plants and damages larger such plants, eventually killing them.  
>The first time a Hostile creature with the Bloodied condition takes damage in the Emanation, it feels horrid thirst and hunger and suffers an Exhaustion level. If such a creature drops to 0 Hit Points in the Emanation, it suffers another Exhaustion level. A creature can gain only two Exhaustion levels from any Aura of Withering in the span of 24 hours.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The black quoxxarie has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The black quoxxarie makes one Ram attack and one Hooves attack.  
>
>***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the black quoxxarie moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage and, if it is Medium or smaller, has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Hooves.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.  
>
>### Reactions
>***Get the Horns.*** *Trigger:* The black quoxxarie takes 20+ damage in a single turn from a creature outside its reach. *Response:* The black quoxxarie takes the Disengage action and moves up to half its Speed toward the creature. If it ends this movement adjacent to the creature, it can make a Ram attack against the creature with Advantage on the attack roll.
