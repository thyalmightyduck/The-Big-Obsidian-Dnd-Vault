>## Relentless Juggernaut
>*Large Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 16
>- **Hit Points** 168 (16d10 + 80)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|22 (+6)|12 (+1)|20 (+5)|8 (-1)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Dex +5, Wis +6, Cha +7
>- **Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** Understands all but can't speak
>- **Challenge** 12 (XP 8,400; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the juggernaut fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
>
>***Regeneration.*** The juggernaut regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the juggernaut takes Radiant damage or damage from a Critical Hit, this trait doesn't function on the juggernaut's next turn. The juggernaut dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.  
>
>***Siege Monster.*** The juggernaut deals double damage to objects and structures.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The juggernaut makes two attacks, using Executioner's Blade or Crushing Stone in any combination.  
>
>***Executioner's Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) Slashing damage, and the target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of the juggernaut's next turn.  
>
>***Crushing Stone.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:*  +10, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition until the start of the juggernaut's next turn.  
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the relentless juggernaut can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The relentless juggernaut regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
>
>- **Implacable Advance.** The juggernaut moves up to half its Speed, ignoring Difficult Terrain. Each creature in its path is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 18. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Prone condition. *Failure or Success:* The juggernaut can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
>- **Sweeping Blade.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 11 (1d10 + 6) Slashing damage, and the target is pulled up to 10 feet straight toward the juggernaut.
