___
>## Hemonculus
>*Tiny Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 13
>- **Hit Points** 27 (6d4 + 12)
>- **Speed** 20 ft., Fly 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|6 (-2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|
>___
>- **Skills** Athletics +2, Stealth +7
>- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire
>- **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
>- **Senses** Blindsight 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Blood Sense.*** The hemonculus knows the location of any Bloodied creature within 60 feet of it, unless the creature is a Construct or Undead.  
>
>***Flyby.*** The hemonculus doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.  
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** The hemonculus has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage, and the hemonculus attaches to the target. While attached, the hemonculus automatically hits the target with its Claw attacks.  
>The hemonculus can detach itself by spending 5 feet of its movement. The target or a creature within 5 feet of it can detach the hemonculus as an action.  
>
>***Exsanguinate (1/Day).*** The hemonculus magically draws blood from nearby creatures. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, each creature of the hemonculus' choice in a 15-foot Emanation originating from the hemonculus. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage, and the target can't regain Hit Points until the start of the hemonculus's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only. *Failure or Success:* If the hemonculus is attached to a creature, the hemonculus regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage taken by that target.
