___
>## Marra
>*Medium Fiend, Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15
>- **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 36)
>- **Speed** 40 ft.
>- **Initiative** +2 (12)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|14 (+2)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|11 (+0)|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|
>___
>- **Skills** Animal Handling +5, Deception +6, Insight +5, Stealth +5
>- **Damage Resistances** Psychic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from attacks that aren't silvered
>- **Immunities** Frightened
>- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 12
>- **Languages** One national language, Abyssal, Infernal, telepathy 60 ft.
>- **Challenge** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +3
>___
>***Magic Resistance.*** The marra has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.  
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The marra makes three Claws attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Crushing Presence.  
>
>***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) Slashing damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.  
>
>***Crushing Presence.*** *Melee Spell Attack Roll:*  +5, reach 5 ft., one Prone creature. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Psychic damage and the marra enters the target's space and sits on its chest. The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) and is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14. First *Failure:* While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition. If the target is still Restrained at the end of its next turn, it repeats the save. Second *Failure:* While Grappled, the target has the Paralyzed condition. *Failure or Success:* Until the grapple ends, the target takes 13 (3d6 + 3) Psychic damage at the end of each of the target's turns.  
>
>***Shape-Shift.*** The marra shape-shifts into a Tiny moth or back into its true form. In moth form, the marra has a Speed of 5 feet, a Flying Speed of 40 feet, and can't speak. Otherwise, its game statistics are the same in each form. Anything the marra is wearing or carrying transforms with it.  
>
>***Spellcasting.*** The marra casts one of the following spells, requiring no components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):  
>
>At will: *Animal Friendship*, *Phantasmal Force*, *Sleep*  
>1/day each: *Dream*, *Phantasmal Killer*
