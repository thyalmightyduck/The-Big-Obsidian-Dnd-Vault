## Giantkin

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (height varies)
- **Speed:** 30 feet 

***Catch and Throw.*** If you are hit by a ranged weapon attack, you can use your Reaction to add 3 to your AC. If that causes the attack to miss, you can catch the missile, provided the attack included a projectile or was a small thrown weapon (GM's discretion), and throw it back at the attacker, using your normal ranged weapon attack bonus and the weapon's normal damage.

***Powerful Build.*** You have Advantage on saving throws that you make to end the Grappled condition. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.

***Giantkin Ancestry.*** Your ancestry grants you supernatural abilities. Choose an ancestry from the Giantkin Ancestry table. You gain a natural resistance to the effects of certain natural environments or a condition, and a trait indicated by your chosen ancestry.

If a benefit grants you the ability to cast a spell, you always have that spell prepared. You can cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.

If a benefit of your ancestry requires a saving throw, the DC is 8 + your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma modifier (choose at character creation) + your Proficiency Bonus.
