## Gargoyle

- **Creature Type:** Elemental
- **Size:** Medium (about 5–7 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 30 feet 

***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

***Elemental Resilience.*** You have Resistance to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.

***False Appearance.*** You have Advantage on any ability check you make to Hide while holding still.

***Immutable Form.*** You have Immunity to Magical Contagions. You also can't be subjected to the Petrified condition against your will.

***Sentry's Rest.*** You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 6 hours if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state. During this time, you appear inert but remain conscious.

***Stoneskin.*** Your thick stone hide provides you with natural armor. While you aren't wearing armor, your base Armor Class equals 13 plus your Dexterity modifier. You can use a *Shield* and still gain this benefit.

***Tireless.*** You don't gain Exhaustion levels due to dehydration, malnutrition, or suffocation.
