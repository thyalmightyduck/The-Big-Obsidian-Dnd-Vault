# Northlands Worldbook (NWB)
## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (about 4-5 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 30 feet, Swim equal to your walking speed

***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision to a range of 90 feet.

***Dwarven Toughness.*** Your Hit Point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 again whenever you gain a level.

***Fjord Warrior.*** You don't take any penalties to your attack rolls while balancing or climbing.

***Wavecunning.*** You have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed and can hold your breath for twice as long as normal.

## Info
Dwarves are one of the most prolific humanoid species of the Northlands, perhaps second only to humans themselves. Dwarves in the Northlands take on many roles, from hard-working wheat farmers to outstanding metal and leather crafters. Environment and circumstance have subtly transformed dwarves over the centuries and grant them different traits.

### Fjord Dwarf
Fjord dwarves are a distinct lineage of dwarves who long ago abandoned the halls and mines traditionally inhabited by their species, instead choosing to sail the fjords and dwell in rough coastal settlements throughout the Northlands. Indeed, many fjord dwarves find the ancient halls of their forebears to be a bit claustrophobic. They much prefer open sea air and the deck planks of a ship beneath their feet. Fjord dwarves, like other Northlands dwarves, are often menacing raiders and fierce warriors, yet they can also be deeply honorable and generous to their friends and allies.
