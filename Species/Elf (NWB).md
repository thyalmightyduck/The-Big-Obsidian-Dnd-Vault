# Northlands Worldbook
## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (about 5-6 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 30 feet 

***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

***Elven Lineage.*** You are part of a lineage that grants you supernatural abilities. Choose a lineage from the Elven lineages Table. You gain the level 1 benefit of that lineage.

When you reach character levels 3 and 5, you learn a higher-level spell, as shown in the table. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.

Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose the ability score when you select the lineage).

***Fey Ancestry.*** You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

***Keen Senses.*** You have proficiency in your choice of two of the following skills: Insight, Perception, and Survival.

***Trance.*** You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 4 hours if you spend those hours in a trance-like meditation, during which you retain consciousness.

## Info
Millennia ago, elves were created in the Vanir War between the gods. Most were driven out of their original Northlands territories by the dwarves, forced to move south and join their kin before the Great Retreat. The elven communities remaining in the Northlands are generally isolationist, choosing to inhabit the farthest reaches of the Bleak Expanse or to hide themselves away from view with powerful magic. Ages of seclusion have subtly influenced the native elven populations of the North, granting them certain kinds of magic.

### Alfar (Hidden Elves)
The alfar are an extremely rare lineage of elves who chose to remain in the North when their kin fled to southern lands. Faced with little to no support, as well as pressure from the dwarven empire, they withdrew to the region's most remote forests. Often known as the hidden elves due to their use of camouflage and illusions to conceal their isolated communities from view, the alfar are suspicious of outsiders and possess customs that are far more insular than those of other elves.

### Ice Elves
The other major lineage of elves in the Northlands roams the tundra and icy hills of the Bleak Expanse in small bands, hunting caribou and mammoths from the backs of reindeer and white-furred saber-toothed tigers. Ice elves are largely insular and fiercely self-sufficient. This has given them the reputation of being secretive, while the truth is more that they have little interest in the larger world. They expect no aid from outsiders, nor do they freely give it. Yet, the ice elves are sometimes willing to trade with humans, dwarves, and trollkin. Despite what ignorant outsiders believe, only a few ice elf communities worship Boreas and do his bidding, and most wish nothing to do with the malevolent deity.
