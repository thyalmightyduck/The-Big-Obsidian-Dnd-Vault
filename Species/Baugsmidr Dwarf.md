# Northlands Worldbook (NWB)
## Traits
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (about 4-5 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 30 feet 

- ***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision to a range of 120 feet.
- ***Arcane Lore.*** You have Advantage on Intelligence (Arcana) checks.
- ***Dwarven Resilience.*** You have Resistance to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
- ***Magical Crafter.*** You have Advantage on any checks made to create magical items.
- ***Sense Magic.*** As a Bonus Action, you can sense the presence of magic within 120 feet until the end of your next turn. You see a faint aura around any visible magic object or active spell, and you know its school of magic. You also detect the presence and location of otherworldly creatures, such as Aberrations, Celestials, Fey, Fiends, and Undead. This ability can be blocked by 1 foot of solid material, such as stone or metal. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Info
Dwarves are one of the most prolific humanoid species of the Northlands, perhaps second only to humans themselves. Dwarves in the Northlands take on many roles, from hard-working wheat farmers to outstanding metal and leather crafters. Environment and circumstance have subtly transformed dwarves over the centuries and grant them different traits.

### Baugsmidr Dwarf
A lesser known dwarven lineage is the baudsmidr dwarf. These dwarves are sometimes called "ring dwarves" by outsiders for their knack with crafting magical rings. Long ago, Gullveig, an obscure deity of magic and gold, lived for a time among an isolated community of dwarves, teaching them the divine art of binding magical runes into precious metals. Though this brought the dwarves great renown and power, it also led to jealousy and greed from others. After a series of raids nearly decimating their community, the baudsmidr dwarves retreated deep within the ground, creating hidden enclaves and redoubts. Today, the descendants of these dwarves remain guarded. Culturally, they consider the location of their communities a closely guarded secret, shared with only those they trust implicitly. Their isolationism has made them leaner than other dwarves, with hair the color of gold or silver. Their eyes are usually large and pale. The gift from Gullveig remains strong in the baudsmidr and many become spellcasters. The ring dwarves who leave the community often travel to find and recover new magical lore or lost artifacts.
