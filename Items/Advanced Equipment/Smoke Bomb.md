#### Smoke Bomb

Advanced Equipment, 50 GP, 3 lb.

---

As a Utilize action, you can throw this smoky glass vial to a space up to 30 feet away. When the vial breaks, the concoction creates a 10-foot-radius Sphere of smoke. The area is Heavily Obscured. The smoke lasts for 1d4 + 2 rounds, or half as long in a windy area.

### Advanced Equipment

The brilliant engineers and crafters who designed and built Etharis's highly advanced weapons and ammunition did not stop there. Other inventions of war—and of peace—became invaluable to the soldiers, guards, and adventurers of Etharis.

Some pieces of Advanced equipment, such as the corrupting *Shadowsteel Focus*, need special training to fully employ. Other items, like the *Retractable Shield*, are usable by anyone proficient with *Shields*. And many other pieces of equipment are usable by anyone—though few would be able to afford and maintain these mechanical, alchemical, or even magical marvels.

As they are expensive and difficult to make, access to Advanced equipment is limited—maybe even as limited as some magic items. The largest cities, or locations housing master crafters, may only have a few items of Advanced equipment.
