## Grim Hallow Player’s Guide 2024 (GHPG24)
#### Breath of Beleth

Advanced Equipment, Poison (Inhaled), 150 GP, ¼ lb.

---

Named after the Arch Daemon of Fear, this poison creates an extremely powerful hallucinogenic effect that evokes a victim's worst fears. The poison is airborne, and victims need only breathe it in to suffer its effects. On the rare occasions it has been used, people in crammed rooms have jumped out of windows or even killed each other out of abject fear.

Rumors say that the poison has been forbidden even by the Ebon Syndicate, deeming it too dangerous, too extravagant, and too conspicuous. Chances are that such a powerful substance wasn't simply abandoned, however, and other rumors say the Ebon Syndicate still uses a milder version during interrogation.

***Breath of Beleth (Inhaled).*** A creature who breathes and is in the same space as an open container of the substance must succeed at a DC 15 Constitution [[saving throw]] or have the [[Poisoned|Poisoned condition]] for 1 minute.

While [[Poisoned]] in this way, a creature has the [[Frightened|Frightened condition]], and it is [[Hostile]] to all creatures it can see. If a creature moves within 5 feet of a creature [[Poisoned]] in this way, the [[Poisoned]] creature must use the [[Dash action]] on its turn to move away. If it cannot move away, it must instead use the [[Attack action]] against the adjacent creature.

### Advanced Equipment

The brilliant engineers and crafters who designed and built Etharis's highly advanced weapons and ammunition did not stop there. Other inventions of war—and of peace—became invaluable to the soldiers, guards, and adventurers of Etharis.

Some pieces of Advanced equipment, such as the corrupting *[[Shadowsteel Focus]]*, need special training to fully employ. Other items, like the *[[Retractable Shield]]*, are usable by anyone proficient with *[[Shields]]*. And many other pieces of equipment are usable by anyone—though few would be able to afford and maintain these mechanical, alchemical, or even magical marvels.

As they are expensive and difficult to make, access to Advanced equipment is limited—maybe even as limited as some magic items. The largest cities, or locations housing master crafters, may only have a few items of Advanced equipment.
