#### Clematis Poison

Adventuring Gear, Poison (Injury)

---

A creature subjected to the poison must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw, taking 13 (3d8) Poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that fails its save is also Paralyzed for 1 hour.

This poison has no effect on creatures that are immune to the Poisoned condition.
