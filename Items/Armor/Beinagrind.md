#### Beinagrind

Heavy Armor, 50 GP, 40 lb.

AC 15

---

This armor is formed by carving disks or short shingles out of whale or giant bones and lacing them to a backing of flexible leather.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

If the wearer has a Strength score lower than 13, their speed is reduced by 10 feet.
