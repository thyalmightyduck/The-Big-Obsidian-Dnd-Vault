#### Cannon

Weapon, Martial Weapon, Ranged Weapon, 500 GP, 90 lb.

3d10 Bludgeoning

Artillery (Range 120/240 ft.; *Cannonball*), Booming, Two‑Handed

Mastery:  Push

---

***Range.*** A Range weapon has a range in parentheses after the Ammunition or Thrown property. The range lists two numbers. The first is the weapon's normal range in feet, and the second is the weapon's long range. When attacking a target beyond normal range, you have Disadvantage on the attack roll. You can't attack a target beyond the long range.

***Artillery.*** You must use Strength instead of Dexterity for the attack rolls and damage rolls of a weapon with the Artillery property, and you can use the weapon to make a ranged attack only if you have ammunition to fire from it. The type of ammunition required is specified with the weapon's range. Each attack expends one piece of ammunition. Weapons with the Artillery property must be reloaded with an action. After a fight, you can spend 1 minute to recover half the ammunition (round down) you used in the fight; the rest is lost.

***Booming.*** The deflagration from firing a Booming weapon makes a stupendous booming sound. The sound can be heard from up to 500 feet away. Making a ranged attack using a weapon with this property makes it impossible to go undetected; you automatically fail Dexterity (Stealth) checks made as part of the Hide action until the start of your next turn. In addition, if a creature is within the area of the sound blast, it automatically succeeds on Wisdom (Perception) checks made against you as part of the Search action.

***Two-Handed.*** A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

***Mastery: Push.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from yourself if it is Large or smaller.
