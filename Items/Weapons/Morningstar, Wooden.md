#### Morningstar, Wooden

Weapon, Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 10 GP, 3 lb.

1d8 Piercing

Mastery:  Sap

---

***Wooden Melee Weapons.*** When you make an attack roll with this Melee weapon and roll a 1 on the d20, the weapon splinters and is destroyed.

***Mastery: Sap.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
