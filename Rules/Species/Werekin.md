## Werekin

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (about 5-6 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 30 feet 

***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

***Claws.*** You have wickedly sharp claws that you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. When you hit with your claws, you deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier Slashing damage, instead of the Bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.

***Predatory Prowess.*** You have proficiency in the Athletics, Intimidation, Perception, or Survival skill (your choice).

***Scent.*** You have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relating to detecting scents and tracking.

***Shift Aspect.*** You can transform as a Bonus Action, gaining the primal power of your lycanthropic forebears. The transformation lasts for 1 minute or until you end it (no action required). Once you transform, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

When you transform, you gain one of the following options (choose the option each time you transform).

- ***Bestial Strength.*** You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Proficiency Bonus. You also have Advantage on Strength checks as well as any checks or saves to resist or end the Grappled condition.
- ***Primal Savagery.*** You channel the raw savagery of your bestial side. When you use this option, and as a Bonus Action on each of your turns while it is active, you can roar. Creatures other than your allies within 10 feet of you must succeed on a Charisma saving throw (DC 8 + your Charisma modifier + your Proficiency Bonus) or have the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn. A creature that succeeds on the save has Immunity to your roar for 24 hours.
- ***Swift Hunter.*** Your body becomes primed for the hunt. Until the transformation ends, your Speed is increased by 10 feet, and you have Advantage on Strength (Athletics) and Wisdom (Survival) checks.
