## Fjord Dwarf

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (about 4-5 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 30 feet, Swim equal to your walking speed

***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision to a range of 90 feet.

***Dwarven Toughness.*** Your Hit Point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 again whenever you gain a level.

***Fjord Warrior.*** You don't take any penalties to your attack rolls while balancing or climbing.

***Wavecunning.*** You have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed and can hold your breath for twice as long as normal.
