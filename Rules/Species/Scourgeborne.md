## Scourgeborne

- **Creature Type:** Monstrosity
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 feet 

***Size.*** Medium (about 4–7 feet tall)

***Born of Madness.*** Your body is not the one you were born with, but rather the consequence to your exposure to eldritch madness. You have one of the following options based on your alignment:

**Good Alignment.** Your conviction grants you control over your darkest impulses. You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition but have Disadvantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks.
**Evil Alignment.** You let the depraved monster within you influence you. You gain a bonus to Dexterity saving throws equal to your Proficiency Bonus but have Disadvantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.
***Eldritch Curse.*** Your curse is not something mere mortals can undo. You are immune to any spell that would alter your form, such as the *Alter Self* or *Polymorph* spells.

***Feral Limbs.*** You can use your horns, claws, or fangs originating from your mutation to make Unarmed Strikes. When you do so, you deal Piercing damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage for your Unarmed Strike. If your alignment is Evil, your feral side is more dominant, and the die size for your Unarmed Strikes increases to a 1d8.

***Monstrous Lineage.*** You are part of a lineage that grants you supernatural abilities. Choose a lineage from the Monstrous Lineages table. You gain the level 1 benefit of that lineage. When you reach character levels 3 and 5, you gain further benefits, as shown on the table.
