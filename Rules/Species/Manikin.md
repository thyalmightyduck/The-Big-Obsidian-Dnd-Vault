## Manikin

- **Creature Type:** Construct
- **Size:** Small (2–4 feet tall) or Medium (about 4–7 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 30 feet 

***Born to Serve.*** Manikins are made to be unable to hate or resent their creators. This lapse in judgment affects you in all circumstances. You have Disadvantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks.

***Electric Heart.*** You have Resistance to Lightning damage.

***Integrated Gold Plating.*** Your body has built-in defensive layers. You gain no AC benefit from wearing armor, but if you are wielding a *Shield*, you apply its bonus as normal. When you select this species, choose one of the following armor presets:

***Infiltrator.*** Your base Armor Class equals 11 + your Dexterity modifier. You are considered unarmored for the purpose of features that require you to not be wearing any armor. You have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

***Sentinel.*** Your base Armor Class equals 13 + either your Dexterity modifier (maximum of 2) or your Strength modifier (maximum of 3). You are considered to be wearing Medium armor. If you don't have training in Medium armor, you suffer the associated drawbacks.

***Tormentor.*** Your base Armor Class equals 16 + your Strength modifier (maximum of 2). You are considered to be wearing Heavy armor. If you don't have training in Heavy armor, you suffer the associated drawbacks. You have Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

***Living Material.*** You don't need to eat, drink, or breathe and have Immunity to the Poisoned condition.

***Service Model.*** You've been created for a specific purpose. Choose one of the following benefits when you select this species:

- **Custodian.** You have Advantage on any ability check you make to end the Grappled condition. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity. Additionally, when a creature within 5 feet of you is targeted by an attack, you can take a Reaction to jump in front of it. If the creature is willing, you switch places with it, becoming the target of the attack instead. You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
- **Handler.** You gain proficiency in the Stealth skill and with the *Disguise Kit*. Additionally, you can have up to two Melee weapons embedded within your body. Each weapon must have either the Finesse or Light property. Over the course of 1 hour, which can be done during a Short Rest, you can remove one or both weapons and exchange them for other appropriate weapons you are holding. You can draw or stow these embedded weapons as you would normal weapons. You can't be disarmed of your embedded weapons, short of having your arms cut off.
- **Thespian.** You gain proficiency in the Performance skill. As a Bonus Action, you can form a connection between yourself and a willing creature you can see within 30 feet via a set of immaterial strings for 1 hour. If the creature you're connected to doesn't use all of its movement on its turn (if it has multiple Speeds, pick the highest one), you can take a Reaction at the end of its turn to move a number of feet equal to its remaining movement. Once you form a connection in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
