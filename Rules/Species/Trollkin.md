## Trollkin

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (about 6-7 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 30 feet 

***Darkvision.*** You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.

***Natural Weapon.*** You have fangs, hooves, or claws, which serve as a natural weapon. Work with your GM to determine what type of natural weapon you possess and decide whether it deals Bludgeoning, Piercing or Slashing damage. You can use your natural weapon to make Unarmed Strikes. When you hit with it, you deal 1d6 + your Strength or Dexterity modifier damage (choose which during character creation) of your chosen type, instead of the Bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed attack.

***Trollish Regeneration.*** The regenerative power in your blood allows you to quickly recover from wounds. As a Bonus Action, you can expend any number of Hit Dice up to your Proficiency Bonus to regain Hit Points. If you take Acid or Fire damage, you lose access to this ability until you finish a Short Rest.

***Natural Adaptation.*** You manifest different traits depending on your non-humanoid ancestor. Choose one of the ancestor types from the Trollkin Ancestry table. You gain a different benefit depending on your choice.

##### Trollkin Ancestry
| Ancestry | Benefit                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|:--------:|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|    Fey   | When you make an ability check that uses Charisma, you may roll a 1d6 and add it to the total. You can use this trait a number of times per day equal to your Charisma modifier, and you regain all uses when you finish a Long Rest. |
|   Ogre   | You have Advantage on ability checks and saves to avoid or escape grapples. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.                                                                                |
|   Troll  | You have Advantage on saves against the Stunned condition. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.                                                                                                 |
