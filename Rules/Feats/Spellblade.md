## Spellblade

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+; Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma 13+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Blade Cantrips.*** You learn two of the following cantrips of your choice: *Arc Blade*, *Burning Blade*, *Frigid Blade*, or *True Strike*. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this feat). Whenever you gain a new level, you can change your cantrip selection.

***Arcane Strike.*** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of the attacks with casting *Arc Blade*, *Burning Blade*, *Frigid Blade*, or *True Strike*.

***Channeled Attack.*** When you make an attack using Strength or Dexterity with a weapon with which you have proficiency, you can gain a bonus to the attack roll equal to your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma modifier (minimum of +1). Choose the ability each time you use this benefit. You can gain this bonus a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
