## Blessing of Jormungandr

Origin Feat

You gain the following benefits.

***Poison Resilience.*** If you take Poison damage, you can use your Reaction to give yourself Resistance to Poison damage until the end of your next turn. You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

***Serpent Kinship.*** You always have the *Animal Friendship* and *Speak with Animals* spells prepared. You can cast each spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. When you cast these spells without a spell slot, you may only target snakes and serpents. You can also cast these spells using any spell slots you have. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this feat).

***Serpent's Tongue.*** You know the Draconic language.
