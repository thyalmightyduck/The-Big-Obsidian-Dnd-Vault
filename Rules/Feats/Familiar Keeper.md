## Familiar Keeper

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Find Familiar.*** You always have the *Find Familiar* spell prepared. Additionally, you can cast it as a Magic action without expending a spell slot or Material components. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat). Once you cast the spell using this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

***Expanded Options.*** When you cast *Find Familiar*, you can choose one of the normal forms for your familiar or one of the following special forms: **Imp**, **Pseudodragon**, **Quasit**, **Sphinx of Wonder**, or **Sprite**.

***Familiar Distraction.*** When a creature within 5 feet of your familiar makes an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to command your familiar to give the attack Disadvantage. You impose Disadvantage in this way a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
