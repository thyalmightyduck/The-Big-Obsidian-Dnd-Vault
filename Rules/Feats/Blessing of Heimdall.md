## Blessing of Heimdall (NWB):
General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Awareness of the Guardian.*** You have [[Proficiency]] in the Wisdom ([[Perception]]) skill. If you already have [[Proficiency]], you gain [[Expertise]] in that skill. In addition, when you make a Wisdom ([[Perception]]) check, you can decide to give yourself [[Advantage]]. You can do this a number of times per day equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

***Herald's Horn.*** You gain [[proficiency]] in the *[[Horn]]*.

***Vigilance.*** When you roll [[Initiative]], you can reroll the check but must use the new result. Once you use this feature, you must finish a [[Long Rest]] before you can use it again.
