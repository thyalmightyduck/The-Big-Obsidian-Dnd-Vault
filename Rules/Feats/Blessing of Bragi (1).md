## Blessing of Bragi

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Wisdom or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Love of the Vanir.*** You gain Proficiency with the Persuasion skill if you don't already have it, and you have Advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks.
