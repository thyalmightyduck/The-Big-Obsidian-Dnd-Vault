## Hunter

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence or Wisdom by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Improved Tracking.*** You have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (Survival) checks to follow or identify tracks.

***Greater Stealth.*** You have Advantage when on Dexterity (Stealth) checks while outdoors.
