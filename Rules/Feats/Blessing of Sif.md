## Blessing of Sif

Origin Feat

You gain the following benefits.

***Gift of Dexterity.*** When you make a Dexterity check, you can add your Proficiency Bonus to the roll. If you already add your Proficiency Bonus to a Dexterity check, you may add it an additional time. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

***Sif's Prowess.*** You know the *True Strike* cantrip. Choose either the *Hunter's Mark* or *Longstrider* spell. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast the spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast this spell using any spell slots you have. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).
