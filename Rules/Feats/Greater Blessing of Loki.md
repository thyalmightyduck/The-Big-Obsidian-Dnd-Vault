## Greater Blessing of Loki

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Blessing of Loki)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Lord of Lies.*** You have Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks.

***Mask Magic.*** You always have the *Alter Self* spell prepared. You can cast it without expending a spell slot. Once you cast the spell in this way, you can't cast it in this way again until you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast it using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spell's spellcasting ability is the one you chose when you took the Blessing of Loki feat.
