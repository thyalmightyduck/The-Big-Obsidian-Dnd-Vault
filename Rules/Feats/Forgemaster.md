## Forgemaster

Crafting Feat (Prerequisite: Expert Forger)

Your deftness with your tools of choice is unparalleled.

- Increase your Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When you make a Manufacturing check with a tool with which you are proficient, you have advantage on the roll.
- You gain proficiency with 2 tools of your choice.
- Each hour you spend forging an item counts as 3 hours towards the item's completion.
