## Nocturnal

Origin Feat

You have spent many formative years in the darkness of night. You gain the following benefits.

***Darkvision.*** You have *Darkvision* with a range of 60 feet. If you already have *Darkvision*, its range increases by 30 feet.

***Slink in Shadows.*** Whenever you make a Dexterity (Stealth) check while in Dim Light or Darkness, you can treat a roll of 9 or lower on the d20 as a 10.
