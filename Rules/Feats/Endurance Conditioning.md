## Endurance Conditioning

General Feat (Prerequisite: Constitution 13+, Proficiency in the Athletics skill)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Quick Recovery.*** Once per day, you can recover from 1 level of Exhaustion after completing a Short Rest. In addition, a Long Rest removes 2 levels of Exhaustion (instead of 1).
