# Aquatic Adaptation
## Exploring Eberron (2024):
_Origin Feat_

You are able to live underwater. This may be a natural feature of your character if you're playing a Malenti or an Elf from Valraea, or it could be the result of arcane transmutation or a divine blessing. Whatever the origin of this gift, you gain the following benefits.

**Amphibious.** You can breathe underwater. If you possess the Limited Amphibiousness trait in addition to this feat, this eliminates your dependence on water.
**Athletic Build.** You gain proficiency in the Athletics skill.
**Natural Swimmer.** You have a [[Swim Speed]] equal to your [[Speed]].
