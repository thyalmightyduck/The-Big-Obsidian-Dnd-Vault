## Combat Flyting

General Feat (Prerequisite: Charisma 13+, Proficiency in the Deception skill)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Taunt.*** As a Bonus Action on your turn, you can demean and bait an opponent within 30 feet of you that can hear and understand you. The target must succeed on a Charisma Saving Throw (DC 8 plus your Charisma modifier plus your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the target takes a -1 penalty to attack rolls against you. You can use another Bonus Action on each of your subsequent turns to impart another -1 penalty; these effects stack to a maximum equal to your Charisma modifier. The target is allowed a save against each of these uses.

This effect ends if you drop to 0 Hit Points, have the Incapacitated condition, you fail to use a Bonus Action each round to maintain it, or if the target moves more than 30 feet away from you. Otherwise, it continues for a maximum number of rounds equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

A creature that succeeds on the saving throw has Immunity to your Taunts for 24 hours.
