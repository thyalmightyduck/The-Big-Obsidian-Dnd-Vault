## Brave at a Pinch

Virtues of the Hobbits Feat (Prerequisite: Hobbit Culture)

One of the Wise once said that you have to put a Hobbit in a tight place before you find out what is in them. You prefer to avoid finding yourself in one, but you are also sure you would be able to take good care of yourself in the circumstances.

- Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Whenever a hostile creature or harmful effect imposes a condition on you, you suffer one or more levels of exhaustion, or you drop to half or less of your maximum hit points, you gain inspiration.
