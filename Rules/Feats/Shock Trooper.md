## Shock Trooper

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***First Strike.*** When you roll Initiative and don't have Disadvantage on the roll, you can draw a weapon and make an attack using it.

***Rapid Advance.*** During the first round of each combat, your Speed is doubled.
