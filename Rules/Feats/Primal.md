## Primal

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Primal Magic.*** Choose two of the following spells: *Animal Friendship*, *Entangle*, *Goodberry*, or *Speak with Animals*. You always have those two spells and the *Barkskin* spell prepared. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast a spell in this way, you can't cast that spell in this way again until you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells' spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
