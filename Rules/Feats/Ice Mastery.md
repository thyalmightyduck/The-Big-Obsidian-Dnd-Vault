## Ice Mastery

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Elemental Adept with Cold Spells)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Fimbulwinter's Grasp.*** Your cold spells carry the strength of Fimbulwinter. Whenever you deal Cold damage to a creature with a spell of at least 1st level, you can choose one of the following additional effects.

- The creature has Disadvantage on Dexterity checks for 1 minute as a terrible cold numbs its body.
- The creature is coated in ice. It has the Restrained condition until it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw to free itself (equal to your spell saving throw DC) or takes at least 10 points of Bludgeoning or Fire damage. It can make the save to end this effect at the end of each of its turns.
- The creature has Vulnerability to Cold damage for 1 minute. It can make a Constitution saving throw (equal to your spell saving throw DC) to end this effect at the end of each of its turns.

You can use Fimbulwinter's Grasp a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all uses when you finish a Long Rest.
