## Blessing of Freyr and Freyja

Origin Feat

You gain the following benefits.

***Wild Knowledge.*** You have proficiency in either the Animal Handling or Nature skill.

***Green Magic.*** You learn the *Druidcraft* and *Thorn Whip* cantrips. Choose either the *Entangle* or *Hail of Thorns* spell. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast the spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast this spell using any spell slots you have. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).
