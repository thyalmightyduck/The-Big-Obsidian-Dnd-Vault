## Metabolistic Magic

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+; Spellcasting or Pact Magic Feature)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Arcane Skill.*** When you fail a D20 Test, you can expend a spell slot to gain a bonus to the roll, potentially causing it to succeed. The bonus equals 2 plus the level of the spell slot expended.

***Vital Fuel.*** When you finish a Short Rest, you can expend a number of Hit Point Dice up to your Proficiency Bonus to recover expended spell slots. Choose spell slots to recover that have a combined level equal to no more than the number of Hit Point Dice expended. Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
