## Brutalizer

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength 16+)

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Brutal Weaponry.*** You can wield a weapon that has the Two-Handed property in one hand, provided your other hand isn't also wielding a weapon that has the Two-Handed property or holding a *Shield*.

***Killing Streak.*** When you take the Attack action on your turn and attack with a weapon that has the Two-Handed property, you can make one extra attack as a Bonus Action later on the same turn. That extra attack must be made with a Light weapon, and you don't add your ability modifier to the extra attack's damage unless that modifier is negative or you have the Two-Weapon Fighting feat.
