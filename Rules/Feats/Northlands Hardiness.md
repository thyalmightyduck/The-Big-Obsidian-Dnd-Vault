## Northlands Hardiness

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Cold Weather Resilience.*** You have Advantage on saving throws against Extreme Cold. If you already have Resistance to Cold damage, you also gain Advantage on saving throws against Cold damage.

***Inexhaustible.*** The first time in a day that you receive a level of Exhaustion, your Speed is not reduced. In addition, you may ignore the effects of Exhaustion on a D20 Test.
