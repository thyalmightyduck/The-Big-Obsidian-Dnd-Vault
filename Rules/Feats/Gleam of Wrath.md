# The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying:
## Gleam of Wrath
Virtues of the Elves Feat (Prerequisite: Elven Culture)

Your folk have seen many defeats, and many fruitless victories in their wars against the Shadow. The deadly rage that your kindred harbour for the Enemy infuses your weapons with a gleam of chill flame.

- Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- As a [[bonus action]], you can make a [[Melee Weapons|melee weapon]] that you are wielding emit [[dim light]] in a radius equal to its reach until the start of your next turn. The light is sunlight. While the weapon gleams, it also deals radiant damage instead of its normal type.
- When you score a [[critical hit]] with a weapon attack, you can roll one of the weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it as extra radiant damage.
