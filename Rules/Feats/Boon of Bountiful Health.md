## Boon of Bountiful Health (FRHoF):
Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

***Augmented Health.*** When you gain [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]], increase the number of [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] you gain by 5.

***Superior Recuperation.*** When you spend one or more [[Hit Point Dice]] to regain [[Hit Point|Hit Points]], you can instead use the highest number possible for each die.
