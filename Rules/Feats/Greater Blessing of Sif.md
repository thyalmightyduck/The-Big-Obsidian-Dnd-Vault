## Greater Blessing of Sif

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Blessing of Sif)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Dexterity or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Sif's Arrow.*** As a Bonus Action, you can channel the power of the god Sif for 1 minute. During that time, when you hit with a ranged weapon attack, you deal extra damage of the weapon's type equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You can deal this extra damage no more than once on each of your turns. You can channel Sif's power in this way a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
