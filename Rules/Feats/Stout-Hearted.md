## Stout-Hearted

Virtues of the Bardings Feat (Prerequisite: Barding Culture)

Living for many a year under the ever-present menace of Smaug the Dreadful has made the Bardings a courageous race. After all, there are not many creatures more fearsome than a great Dragon...

- Increase your Wisdom or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You have advantage on Charisma saving throws, and you cannot be frightened by non-undead creatures.
