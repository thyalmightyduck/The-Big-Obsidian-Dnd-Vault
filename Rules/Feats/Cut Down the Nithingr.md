## Cut Down the Nithingr

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Coward's Bane.*** You have a +2 bonus to damage rolls you make against creatures with the Frightened condition.

***Frightful Visage.*** When you use a spell or effect that causes the Frightened condition, the duration is doubled.
