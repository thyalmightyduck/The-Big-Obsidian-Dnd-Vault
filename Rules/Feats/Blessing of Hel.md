## Blessing of Hel (NWB)

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Forestall the End.*** You have [[Advantage]] on [[Death Saving Throws]].

***Resurgence.*** If you become [[Stable]] following a third successful [[Death Saving Throws|Death Saving Throw]], or if you are stabilized by another creature, you immediately recover [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] equal to 1d6 plus your [[Proficiency]] Bonus.

***The Bell Tolls.*** You know the *[[Spare the Dying]]* and *[[Toll the Dead]]* cantrips. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).
