## Blessing of Skadi

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Hunter's Eye.*** When you use the Attack action to make a ranged weapon attack, you can choose to give yourself Advantage on the attack roll. Once you use this feature you must finish a Short or Long Rest before you can use it again.

***Snowpace.*** Your Speed increases by 10 feet when you move on ice or snow, and you never treat those types of terrain as Difficult Terrain.
