## Envoy Specialist

Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Warforged)

You gain the following benefits.

***Envoy Competence.*** You gain proficiency with one tool of your choice.

***Integrated Tool.*** Choose one tool you are proficient with. You have a working model of this tool integrated into your body, which can't be removed. As long as you have a hand free, you can make use of the integrated tool.

***Innate Talent.*** When you make an ability check with the integrated tool, you can roll 1d4 and add the number rolled to the ability check.
