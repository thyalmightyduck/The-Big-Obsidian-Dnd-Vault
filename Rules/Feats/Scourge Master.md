## Scourge Master

General Feat (Prerequisite: Proficiency with whip or tetherhooks)

Your skill with whips and tetherhooks allows you to manipulate items at a distance.

- Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When you hit a creature with an attack using a *tetherhooks* or *whip*, you can use a bonus action to attempt to disarm the creature of a shield, or of one item it is holding. Make a Strength or Dexterity check (your choice) contested by the target's Strength check. If you succeed, the item is dropped on the ground midway between you and the creature.
- You can use your *whip* or *tetherhooks* to grab items weighing less than 1 lb within the reach of your weapon, pulling them back to your person.
