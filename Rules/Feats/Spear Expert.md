## Spear Expert

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+; Strength or Dexterity 13+; Weapon Mastery Feature)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Deflecting Haft.*** You can use a *Spear* to deflect incoming attacks. While holding a *Spear*, you gain a +1 bonus to your AC.

***Expert Thrower.*** The range of any *Spear* you throw is 30/90.

***Spear Ready.*** If you take the Ready action to attack with the *Spear*, you have Advantage on the triggered attack.
