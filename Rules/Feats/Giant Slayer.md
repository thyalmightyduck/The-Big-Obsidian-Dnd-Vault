## Giant Slayer

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Weapon Mastery Feature)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Duck and Weave.*** If you are within 5 feet of a Large or larger creature, you can take the Dodge action as a Bonus Action.

***The Bigger They Are.*** You're adept at knocking Large or larger creatures off their feet. When you use the Topple Weapon Mastery feature against a creature of Large or larger size, it has Disadvantage on its save.
