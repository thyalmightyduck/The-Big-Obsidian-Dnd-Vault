## Frost Eyed

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Bright Eyed.*** You have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks in Bright Light, and you do not have Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks in Dim Light.

***Ice Tracker.*** You are proficient in the Survival skill. You treat both snow and icy terrain as normal terrain. You have Advantage on Survival checks made in snow or ice.
