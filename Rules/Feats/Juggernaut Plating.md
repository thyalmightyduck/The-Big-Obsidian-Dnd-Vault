## Juggernaut Plating

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Warforged)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Resilient Armor.*** While you remain alive, armor you have donned can't be targeted by any effect (such as the *Heat Metal* spell or the antennae of a rust monster) against your will.

***Adamantine Body.*** Any Critical Hit against you becomes a normal hit.
