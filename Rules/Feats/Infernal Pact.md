## Infernal Pact

Planar Pact Feat (Prerequisite: Can't Have Another Planar Pact Feat)

You gain the following benefits.

***Infernal Resistance.*** You gain Resistance to Fire damage or Poison damage (choose when you select this feat).

***Infernal Sight.*** You gain Darkvision with a range of 30 feet. This Darkvision is unimpeded by magical Darkness.

***Silver-Tongued.*** You gain proficiency in the Deception skill.
