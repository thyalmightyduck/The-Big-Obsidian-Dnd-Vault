## Fisher

Origin Feat

You gain the following benefits.

***Survivalist.*** You gain proficiency in the Survival skill.

***Boat Familiarity.*** You have Advantage on d20 Tests made to operate water vehicles.

***Fishing Prowess.*** When you are exploring and have access to a body of water, you can spend 1 hour to fish and obtain enough food for up to 6 people for 1 day.
