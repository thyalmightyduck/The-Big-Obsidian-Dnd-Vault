## Heroic Rush

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Constitution or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Triumphant Boost.*** When you score a Critical Hit, your Speed increases by 10 feet and you have Advantage on attack rolls. These benefits last until the end of your next turn.

***Victorious Inspiration.*** When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with an attack, you can choose one ally within 30 feet of you to receive Heroic Inspiration. The ally must use this Heroic Inspiration before the end of their next turn or it is wasted. You can use this feature twice, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest.
