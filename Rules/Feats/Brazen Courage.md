## Brazen Courage

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Wisdom or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Staunch Will.*** You have Advantage on saving throws against the Frightened condition. Also, you can use a Bonus Action to end the Frightened condition on yourself a number of times per day equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
