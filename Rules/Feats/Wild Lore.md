## Wild Lore

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Knowledge of the Land.*** You have Advantage on all Intelligence (Nature) checks.

***Provider.*** You can use your knowledge of the land to find food and fresh water for yourself and up to 3 other people each day.
