## Greater Blessing of Thor

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Blessing of Thor)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Storm Strike.*** As a Bonus Action, you can channel the power of the god Thor for 1 minute. During that time, when you hit with a melee weapon attack, you deal extra damage of the weapon's type equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You can deal this extra damage no more than once on each of your turns. You can channel Thor's power in this way a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
