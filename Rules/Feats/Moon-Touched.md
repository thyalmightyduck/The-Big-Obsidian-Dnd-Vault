## Moon-Touched

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

Your connection to the moon and its otherworldly radiance grants you the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Moon Magic.*** You know your choice of the *Dancing Lights* or *Light* cantrip. Choose one level 1 spell that creates or emits light, such as *Color Spray* or *Faerie Fire*, from the Druid, Sorcerer, or Wizard spell lists. You always have that spell and the *Moonbeam* spell prepared. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either spell in this way, you can't cast that spell in this way again until you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells' spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
