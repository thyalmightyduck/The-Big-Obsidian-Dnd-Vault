## Strength of Will

Virtues of the Rangers of the North Feat (Prerequisite: Ranger of the North Culture)

You have been raised in a land dotted with desolate ruins and burial mounds. You have heard the dead whisper with cold voices out of lonely barrows, while ghostly lights dance on haunted hills. When your will strengthens your heart, there is hardly anything in this world that can dismay you.

- Increase your Wisdom or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You have advantage on Charisma saving throws.
- Whenever you must make a Charisma saving throw, you gain a bonus to the saving throw equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum +1).
