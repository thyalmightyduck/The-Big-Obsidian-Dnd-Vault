# Alacrity
## Vampire: The Masquerade -  Bound by Blood:
_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+ Kindred)_

You move at supernatural speeds. You gain the following benefits.

**Burst of Speed.** As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend 1 Blood Point to gain an additional action. That action can be used to take only the [[Attack Action]] (one attack only) or [[Disengage action]].
**Fast.** Your [[Speed]] increases by 10 feet, and you have [[Advantage]] on [[Initiative]] rolls.
