## Mounted Leap

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+; Strength or Dexterity 13+; Proficiency in the Animal Handling skill)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Equestrian Prowess.*** While you're riding a mount, that mount's jump distance is doubled.

***Leaping Charge Attack.*** While jumping from a moving mount, your jump distance is calculated using the mount's Speed in place of yours (if its Speed is better than yours). If you make a successful melee attack roll against a creature after jumping, choose one of the following effects: gain a 1d8 bonus to the attack's damage roll, or make the target have the Prone condition if the target is one size category larger than you or smaller. You can use this benefit only once on each of your turns.
