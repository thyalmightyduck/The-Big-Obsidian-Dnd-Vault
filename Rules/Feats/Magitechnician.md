## Magitechnician

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Magic Item Expert.*** If one of your magic items calls for a saving throw, it uses the following DC if it is higher: 8 plus the modifier of the ability increased by this feat and your Proficiency Bonus.

***Magic Item Recharge.*** When you finish a Short Rest, you can cause a magic item that regains charges or properties to recharge as if it were the next dawn. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
