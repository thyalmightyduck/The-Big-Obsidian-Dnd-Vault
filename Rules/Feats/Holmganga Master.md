## Holmganga Master

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Dueling Feat)

Your experience in dueling gives you the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength, Constitution, or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Battle Primed.*** You have Advantage on Initiative at the start of a Holmganga or other duel.

***Duel Intimidation.*** As a Bonus Action, you can make a Charisma (Intimidation) check against your holmganga opponent. If the check succeeds (GM's discretion), you have Advantage on your next attack against the target, and the target has Disadvantage on its next attack against you.
