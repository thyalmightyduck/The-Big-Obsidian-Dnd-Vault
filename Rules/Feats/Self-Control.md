## Self-Control

Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+  Kindred)

You manage your Beast better than many. You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Reining the Beast.*** When you would be affected by the Hunger of your Beast feature, you can take a Reaction to immediately end the effect.

You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
