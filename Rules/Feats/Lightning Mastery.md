## Lightning Mastery

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Elemental Adept with Lightning Spells)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Thor's Fury.*** Your lightning spells are laced with extra power. Whenever you deal Lightning damage to a creature with a spell of at least 1st level, you can choose one of the following additional effects.

- The creature has the Stunned condition until the end of your next turn, if it failed its save against the spell.
- The creature takes half of the Lightning damage it initially took again at the beginning of its next turn. It can make a saving throw against your spell saving throw DC to negate this additional damage.
- The creature gains Vulnerability to Lightning damage for 1 minute. It can make a Constitution saving throw (equal to your spell saving throw DC) to end this effect at the end of each of its turns.

You can use Thor's Fury a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all uses when you finish a Long Rest.
