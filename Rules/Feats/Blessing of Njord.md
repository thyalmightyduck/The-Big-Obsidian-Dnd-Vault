## Blessing of Njord

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Call Sea Serpent.*** You can cast *Find Steed* without expending a spell slot. You can cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast this spell using any spell slots you have. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat).

***Njord's Protection.*** You can breathe water as well as air, and automatically succeed on saves against Extreme Cold while submerged in water. You also have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.
