## Norn-Touched

Origin Feat

You gain the following benefits.

***Fates' Favor.*** Once per day, when you make a d20 Test, you can add your Proficiency Bonus to the result. You can see the result of the roll before you use this feature.

***Fates' Scorn.*** Once per day, when another creature rolls a d20 for an attack roll or ability check, you can subtract your Proficiency Bonus from the result. You can see the result of the roll before you use this feature.
