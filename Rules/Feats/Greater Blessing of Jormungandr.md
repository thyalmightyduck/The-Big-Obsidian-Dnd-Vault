## Greater Blessing of Jormungandr

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Blessing of Jormungandr)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Serpent's Aspect.*** You can channel the fear inspired by serpents and dragons. You can use an Action to force a number of creatures of your choice equal to your Proficiency Bonus within 30 feet of you that can see you to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 plus your Charisma modifier and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, a target has the Frightened condition for 1 minute. Such a creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
