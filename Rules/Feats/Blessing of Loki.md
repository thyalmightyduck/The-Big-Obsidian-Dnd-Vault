## Blessing of Loki (NWB):

Origin Feat

You gain the following benefits.

***Expert Liar.*** You have [[proficiency]] in the [[Deception]] skill. If you already have [[proficiency]] in it, you gain [[Expertise]] in it instead.

***Trickster Magic.*** You learn the *[[Minor Illusion]]* and *[[Vicious Mockery]]* cantrips. Choose either the *[[Disguise Self]]* or *[[Charm Person]]* spell. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast the spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a [[Long Rest]]. You can also cast this spell using any spell slots you have. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).
