## Blessing of Tyr

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Bravery.*** You have Advantage on saving throws made to avoid or end the Frightened condition.

***Justicar.*** As a Magic action, you can touch a creature and compel it to tell the truth. The creature must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the creature can't speak a deliberate lie for 1 minute. You know whether a creature succeeds or fails on this save. An affected creature is aware of the spell and can avoid answering questions to which it would normally respond with a lie. Such a creature can be evasive yet must be truthful.

You can use this feature at will, but when you use it on a creature, you must finish a Long Rest before you can use it on that creature again.
