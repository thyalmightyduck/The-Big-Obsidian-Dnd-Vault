## Player’s Handbook 2024 (PHB24):
_General Feat (Prerequisites: Level 4+, Medium Armor Training)_

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Constitution or Strength by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Armor Training.** You gain training with Heavy armor.

## Player’s Handbook 2014 (PHB14):
_Prerequisite: Medium Armor Training_

You have trained to master the use of heavy armor, gaining the following benefits:

- Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency with heavy armor.
