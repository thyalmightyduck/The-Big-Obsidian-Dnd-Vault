## Grizzled

Origin Feat

You gain the following benefits.

***Overcome.*** Whenever you make a saving throw against the Frightened condition, you gain a bonus to the saving throw equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

***Survive.*** You require half as much food and water to survive, and you have Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made to forage for food and water.

***Withstand.*** Immediately after you take damage that causes you to be Bloodied, you gain Heroic Inspiration. You can't gain this benefit again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
