## Durin's Way

Virtues of the Dwarves Feat (Prerequisite: Dwarven Culture)

The Dwarves have fought many wars in deep places beneath the earth. You have been taught how to best defend yourself while fighting underground. As a result, you know how to exploit corners, darkness and other natural obstacles to your advantage.

- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When you have half or three-quarters cover, you gain an additional +1 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws.
- Other creatures do not gain advantage on attack rolls against you as a result of being unseen by you.
