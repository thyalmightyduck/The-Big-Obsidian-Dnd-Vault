## Faster Crafting

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Crafter)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Increased Artisan Proficiency.*** You may either gain proficiency with an *Artisan's Tool* of your choice or Advantage on ability checks with a single *Artisan's Tool* with which you are already proficient.

***Combat Quickcraft.*** While in combat, you can use a Bonus Action to craft one piece of gear from the Fast Crafting table, provided you have the *Artisan's Tools* associated with that item, have proficiency with those tools, and do not have the Incapacitated condition. The item lasts until the end of your next turn, or until you craft an additional item, at which point it falls apart.

***Short Rest Crafting.*** You can craft an item from the Fast Crafting table during a Short Rest. It lasts until the end of the day, you use this aspect of the feat again, or your next Long Rest, whichever comes first, at which point it falls apart.
