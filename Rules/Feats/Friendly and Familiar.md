## Friendly and Familiar

Virtues of the Men of Bree Feat (Prerequisite: Men of Bree Culture)

Since you started your adventures, you have realised that your folk's custom of trading with foreigners may help you in your endeavours. Your nice manners and friendly demeanour easily gain the sympathies of those you meet, and your ability to inquire about the doings of strangers without letting curiosity get the better of you provides you with many opportunities to learn from those you meet along the way.

- Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Increase the Fellowship rating of your Company by 1.
- Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any Wisdom (Insight) check you make. In addition, you have advantage on the first Charisma (Persuasion) check you make when interacting with Dwarves, Elves, Hobbits, and Men (other than the Dúnedain).
