## Surtr's Touch

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Touch of Flame.*** As a Bonus Action, you can imbue your touch with the power of fire for 1 minute. During that time, when you hit with a melee weapon attack, you deal extra Fire damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You can use this feat a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
