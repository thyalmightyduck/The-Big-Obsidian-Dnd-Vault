# Crafter
## Player’s Handbook 2024 (PHB24):
_Origin Feat_

You gain the following benefits.

Tool Proficiency. You gain proficiency with three different [[Artisans Tools]] of your choice from the Fast Crafting table.

Discount. Whenever you buy a nonmagical item, you receive a 20 percent discount on it.

Fast Crafting. When you finish a [[Long Rest]], you can craft one piece of gear from the Fast Crafting table, provided you have the [[Artisans Tools]] associated with that item and have proficiency with those tools. The item lasts until you finish another [[Long Rest]], at which point the item falls apart.
