# Blessing of Boreas:
## Northlands Worldbook (NWB):
_Origin Feat_

You gain the following benefits.

**Cold Aegis.** If you take Cold damage, you can use your [[Reaction]] to give yourself [[Resistance]] to Cold damage until the end of your next turn. You can use this [[Reaction]] a number of times equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Winter Magic.** You know the [[Ray of Frost]] cantrip. Choose either the [[Armor of Agathys]] or [[Ice Knife]] spell. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast the spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a [[Long Rest]]. You can also cast this spell using any spell slots you have. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).
