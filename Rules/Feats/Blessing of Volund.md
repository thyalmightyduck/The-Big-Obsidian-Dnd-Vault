## Blessing of Volund (NWB):
Origin Feat

You gain the following benefits.

***Crafter's Knack.*** You gain [[proficiency]] with an *Artisan's Tool* of your choice. If you already have [[proficiency]] with that tool, you instead have [[Advantage]] on checks with it.

***Forge Magic.*** You learn the *[[Elementalism]]* and *[[Mending]]* cantrips. Choose either the *[[Burning Hands]]* or *[[Shield spell]]*. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast the spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a [[Long Rest]]. You can also cast this spell using any spell slots you have. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).
