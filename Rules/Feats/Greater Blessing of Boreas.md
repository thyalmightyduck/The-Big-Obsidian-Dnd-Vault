## Greater Blessing of Boreas

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Blessing of Boreas)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Cold-Forged.*** You have Resistance to Cold damage. Additionally, you can summon a shield of cold to block fire-based effects. As a Reaction, when you would take Fire damage, you can gain Resistance to Fire damage until the beginning of your next turn.
