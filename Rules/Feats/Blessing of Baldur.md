# Blessing of Baldur:
## Northlands Worldbook (NWB):
_Origin Feat_

You gain the following benefit.

**Protective Aegis.** Once per [[Long Rest]], when you or an ally within 10 feet of you takes damage, you can use your [[Reaction]] to reduce that damage by an amount equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]] + your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Radiant Aura.** As a [[Bonus Action]], you emit a bright golden light in a 10-foot-radius and [[Dim Light]] for another 10 feet. You may extinguish this light with another [[Bonus Action]].

**Sunlance.** You know the [[Sacred Flame]] cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell.
