## Blessing of Eir

Origin Feat

You gain the following benefits.

***Vitality Points.*** You have a number of Vitality Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus and can spend the points on the benefits below. You regain your expended Vitality Points when you finish a Long Rest.

***Vitality Surge.*** You can take a Bonus Action and spend 1 Vitality Point to recover 1d4 Hit Points. If you are dying, you can spend 1 Vitality Point to stabilize (no action required).

***Channel Vitality.*** You can take a Bonus Action and spend 1 Vitality Point to cause a creature you touch to regain 1d4 Hit Points.
