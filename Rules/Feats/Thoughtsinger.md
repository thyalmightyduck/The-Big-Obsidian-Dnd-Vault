## Thoughtsinger

Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Kalashtar)

You have honed your telepathic abilities, allowing you to link to and guide your companions as a united group. You gain the following benefits.

***Superior Telepathy.*** The telepathy granted by your Mind Link trait has a range of 120 feet or 10 times your level, whichever is higher.

***Thoughtsong.*** When you use your Mind Link trait to grant a creature the ability to speak to you telepathically, you form a mental connection with that individual. When you communicate telepathically, you may choose to send the message to all creatures you currently have a mental connection to. You may also allow those creatures to speak telepathically with one another. These effects last for as long as the Mind Link remains in effect.

***Telepathic Aid.*** If you can speak telepathically to an ally using your Mind Link trait, you can take the Help action to aid them in attacking a creature, even if you are more than 5 feet away from them.
