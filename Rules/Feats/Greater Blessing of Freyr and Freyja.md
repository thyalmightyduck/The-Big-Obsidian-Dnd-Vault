## Greater Blessing of Freyr and Freyja

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Blessing of Freyr and Freyja)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Wisdom or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Healing Magic.*** You always have the *Cure Wounds* spell prepared. You can cast it without using a spell slot a number of times per day equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all uses when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast it using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spell's spellcasting ability is the ability you chose when you took the Blessing of Freyr and Freyja feat. When you cast the spell on a Beast or Plant creature, the amount healed is doubled.
