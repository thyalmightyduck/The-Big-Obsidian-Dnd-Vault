## Frost-Touched

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

Your connection to the ancient frozen power of the Northlands grants you the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Frost Magic.*** You know your choice of the *Frostbite* or *Ray of Frost* cantrip. Choose one level 1 spell that deals Cold damage from the Druid, Sorcerer, or Wizard spell lists. You always have that spell and the *Gust of Wind* spell prepared. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either spell in this manner, you can't cast that spell again until you complete a Long Rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells' spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
