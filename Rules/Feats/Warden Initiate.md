## Warden Initiate

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)

You have learned some of the secrets and techniques of the Wardens of the Wood druidic order. You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Wisdom or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Warden's Proficiency..*** You gain proficiency in the Animal Handling skill.

***Protect Ally.*** When a creature you can see hits another creature within 5 feet of you with an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to reduce the damage dealt to the target by 1d6 + your Proficiency Bonus.

Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

***Warden Spells.*** The secrets you've learned allow you to always have certain spells at the ready. When you have spell slots of a level specified in the Warden of the Wood Spells table, you thereafter have the spells listed for that level and lower prepared.

##### Warden of the Wood Spells
| Spell Level | Spells                          |
|:-----------:|---------------------------------|
|     1st     | *Sanctuary*                     |
|     2nd     | *Detect Thoughts*               |
|     3rd     | *Blink*                         |
|     4th     | *Mordenkainen's Faithful Hound* |
|     5th     | *Hold Monster*                  |
