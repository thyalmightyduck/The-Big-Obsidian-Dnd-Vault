## Cannoneer

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 8+, Strength 18+)

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Cannon Proficiency.*** You gain proficiency with *Cannons*, and you can reload a *Cannon* as a Bonus Action instead of an action. You can take this Bonus Action only if you haven't moved during your turn, and after you take the Bonus Action, your Speed is 0 until the end of your turn. Starting at character level 11, this doesn't reduce your Speed, and when you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with reloading the *Cannon*. When you reach character level 20, you can ignore the Artillery property of *Cannons*.

***Siege.*** Your attacks with *Cannons* deal double damage to objects and structures.

***Strong Back.*** The weight of *Cannons* and *Cannonballs* count as half for you.
