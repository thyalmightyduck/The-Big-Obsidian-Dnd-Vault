# Battle Cry:
## Northlands Worldbook (NWB):
_General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)_

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Thunderous Cry.** You can use a [[Bonus Action]] to release a frightening battle cry. Roll a Charisma (Intimidation) check. One creature of your choice that can hear you within 30 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw with a DC equal to your Charisma (Intimidation) check result or have the [[Frightened]] condition for 1d4 rounds. The target can make a new saving throw at the end of each of its turns to end the effect. Once you've used this ability, you can't use it again until you've completed a [[Short Rest]].
At higher levels, you can target an additional number of creatures with this ability. At 9th level, you can target two creatures. At 15th level, you can target three creatures, and the range of this ability increases to 60 feet.
