# Boon of Fortitude
## Player’s Handbook 2024 (PHB24):
_Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)_

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Fortified Health.** Your [[Hit Point]] maximum increases by 40. In addition, whenever you regain [[Hit Point|Hit Points]], you can regain additional [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] equal to your Constitution modifier. Once you've regained these additional [[Hit Point|Hit Points]], you can't do so again until the start of your next turn.
