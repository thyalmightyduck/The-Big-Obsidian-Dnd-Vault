## Boon of Generations

Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+  Kindred)

You have become the embodiment of the ancient vampires of your bloodline. You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

***Deep Feeding.*** Whenever you roll a Feed Die for your Feed feature, you can reroll the die if it rolls a 1, and you must use the new roll.

***Potent Blood.*** Your Blood Point maximum increases by 5.
