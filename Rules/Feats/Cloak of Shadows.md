## Cloak of Shadows

Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+  Kindred)

You are adept at supernaturally masking your presence. You gain the following benefits.

***Enshrouded.*** As a Bonus Action, you give yourself Advantage on your next Dexterity (Stealth) check on the current turn.

***Unseen Passage.*** As a Magic action, you gain the Invisible condition until the end of your next turn.

You can use this Magic action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
