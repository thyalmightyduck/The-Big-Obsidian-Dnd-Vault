## Memory of Ancient Days

Virtues of the Elves Feat (Prerequisite: Elven Culture)

Long centuries and uncounted generations of Men seem but a little while to the Elves. Your memories go back to a time before Eriador became desolate, and your knowledge of the land is returning to you now that you are travelling again outside the boundaries of your realm.

- Increase your Intelligence or Wisdom by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You can give yourself a bonus to *pathfinding checks* equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1).
- When you are targeted by a journey event, the roll on the Journey Events table is done as if you were in a Border Land if you are in a Wild Land, and as if you were in a Wild Land if you are in a Dark Land.
