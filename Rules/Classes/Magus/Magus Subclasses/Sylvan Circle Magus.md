#### Core Traits
**Primary Ability:** Intelligence

**[[Hit Point Dice|Hit Point Die]]:** D8 per Magus level
**[[Hit Point|Hit Points]] at Level 1:** 8 + Con. modifier
**[[Hit Point|Hit Points]] per additional Magus Level:** D8 + your Con. modifier, or, 5 + your Con. modifier

**[[Saving Throw]] Proficiencies:** Constitution, Intelligence

**[[Skill]] Proficiencies:** _Choose 2:_ [[Acrobatics]], [[Arcana]], [[Athletics]], [[History]], [[Insight]], [[Investigation]], [[Nature]], [[Perception]], or [[Religion]].

**Weapon Proficiencies:** [[Simple Weapons]] and [[Martial weapons]]

**Armor Training:** [[Light Armor]] and [[Medium armor]] and [[Shields]]

**Starting Equipment:** _Choose A, B, or C:_ (A) [[Studded Leather Armor]], [[Longbow]], [[Arrows (20)|20 arrows]], [[Quiver]], [[Rapier]], [[Component Pouch]], [[Diamond]] worth 50 GP, and 4 GP; or (B) 200 GP

#### Multiclassing
To qualify for a new class, you must have a score of at least 13 in the primary ability of the new class and your current classes.

When you gain a level in a class other than your first, you gain only some of that class's starting proficiencies.

**Weapon Proficiencies:** [[Martial weapons]]
**Armor Training:** [[Light Armor]] and [[Medium armor]] and [[Shields]]

## Level 1: Esoteric Eye
As a [[Bonus Action]], you can observe a creature you can see. Until the end of your next turn, you have [[Advantage]] on your Magus spell attacks against it and it has [[Disadvantage]] on [[Saving Throw|saving throws]] against your Magus spells.

You can use this [[Bonus Action]] a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once). You regain one expended use when you finish a [[Short Rest]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

## Level 1: Spellcasting
With disciplined rehearsal, you can wage war and cast spells by rote. See the core rules for the rules on spellcasting. The information below details how you use those rules with Magus spells, which appear in the [[Magus spell list]] later in the class's description.

**Cantrips.** You know two Magus cantrips of your choice. [[Chill Touch]] and [[Fire Bolt]] are recommended. Whenever you gain a Magus level, you can replace one of your cantrips from this feature with another Magus cantrip of your choice.

When you reach Magus levels 10 and 14, you learn another Magus cantrip of your choice, as shown in the Cantrips column of the Magus Features table.

**Spell Slots.** The Magus Features table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Prepared Spells of Level 1+.** You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose two level 1 Magus spells. [[Chromatic Orb]] and [[Shield]] are recommended.

The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Magus levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Magus Features table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional Magus spells until the number of spells on your list matches the number in the Magus Features table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you're a level 5 Magus, your list of prepared spells can include six Magus spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.

If another Magus feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don't count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Magus spells for you.

**Changing Your Prepared Spells.** Whenever you gain a Magus level, you can replace one spell on your list with another Magus spell for which you have spell slots.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for your Magus spells.

## Level 1: Weapon Mastery
Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have [[proficiency]], such as [[Rapier|Rapiers]] and [[Pistol|Pistols]].

Whenever you finish a [[Long Rest]], you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of [[Longbow|Longbows]] and [[Longsword|Longswords]].

## Level 2: Fighting Style
You gain a [[Fighting Style feat]] of your choice. Instead of choosing one of those feats, you can choose the option below.

**Mystic Warrior.** You gain a bonus on Constitution [[Saving Throw|saving throws]] you make to maintain [[Concentration]] equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1). When you fail a Constitution [[saving throw]] to maintain [[Concentration]] on a spell, you gain [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to twice the spell's level plus your Intelligence modifier.

## Level 2: Mystic Mark
You can take a [[Bonus Action]] to imbue yourself with magical power and resilience. When you do, choose a Magus level 1+ spell you have prepared that has a casting time of an [[action]] or a [[Bonus Action]], expend a spell slot equal to or higher than the spell level, and choose Force damage or a [[damage type]] dealt by the spell. You gain your choice of the Aegis or Enspell benefit.

**Aegis.** You have [[Resistance]] to the chosen [[damage type]].

**Enspell.** When you deal damage with a weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]], you can deal the chosen [[damage type]] instead of the normal [[damage type]] for the attack.

You can only benefit from one Aegis and one Enspell at a time. While you have an Aegis or Enspell, you can use a [[Bonus Action]] to release and cast a spell chosen using this feature. Your Mystic Mark and its corresponding benefit end after 1 hour, or when you release the spell.

## Level 3: Magus Subclass
You gain a Magus subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Magus levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass's features that are of your Magus level or lower.

### Sylvan Circle
_Protect Wild Creatures and Natural Wonders_

The Sylvan Circle acts as the sword and shield of the natural world. You adventure to ensure that the world's natural wonders are preserved and that the diversity of its flora and fauna are not threatened. Although some Sylvan Circle Magi become hardliners that see themselves at war with civilization, most work to strike a balance between the needs of the natural world and the multiverse's various peoples.

### Level 3: Agrestal Spirit
You gain the following benefits.

**Additional Cantrips.** You know two additional cantrips of your choice from the Magus or [[Druid spell list]]. These cantrips count as Magus cantrips for you but don't count against your number of cantrips known.

**Green Hand.** You gain [[proficiency]] in the [[Nature]] [[skill]]. If you are already proficient with [[Nature]], you gain [[Expertise]] in it instead.

**Wild Instincts.** You can take the [[Disengage action]] as a [[Bonus Action]]. When you do, until the end of your turn your [[Speed]] increases by 15 feet and you have [[Advantage]] on Strength ([[Athletics]]) and Dexterity ([[Acrobatics]]) checks.

You can use this [[Bonus Action]] a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

### Level 3: Emerald Mark
The effects of your mystic marks are enhanced by your greater understanding of the Sylvan Circle's esoteric knowledge. When you use your Mystic Mark feature, a verdant green aura surrounds you and you gain the following effects based on the benefit or benefits chosen.

**Aegis.** When you or a willing creature you can see within 30 feet of you fails a [[saving throw]], you can use your [[Reaction]] to give the roll a bonus equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1), potentially turning the failure into a success.

**Enspell.** Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]], you can cause the target to treat all spaces as [[Difficult Terrain]] until the end of its next turn.

### Level 3: Sylvan Circle Spells
You can use a [[Druidic Focus]] as a [[Spellcasting Focus]] for your Magus spells.

In addition, your training in the Sylvan Circle ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Magus level specified in the Sylvan Circle Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

## Level 4: Ability Score Improvement
You gain the [[Ability Score Improvement feat]] or another [[feat]] of your choice for which you qualify.

## Level 5: Arcane Ability
When you fail an [[ability check]], you can imbue your effort with a magical might. You add a bonus to the [[ability check]] equal to your Intelligence modifier plus the highest level unexpended spell you have, potentially turning it into a success.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Short Rest]] or [[Long Rest]] unless you expend a spell slot (no [[action]] required) to restore your use of it.

## Level 5: Sword and Sorcery
When you take the [[Attack action]] and it doesn't benefit from the Extra Attack feature, you can cast a Magus cantrip with a casting time of an [[action]] as part of the same [[action]]. When you take the [[Magic action]] on your turn, you can use a [[Bonus Action]] to make one weapon attack.

## Level 6: Magical Recovery
You can regain some of your magical energy by meditating on the Tapestry and its myriad threads. When you finish a [[Short Rest]], you can choose expended spell slots to recover. The spell slots can have a combined level equal to no more than half your Magus level (round up), and none of the slots can be level 6 or higher. For example, if you're a level 6 Magus, you can recover up to three levels' worth of spell slots, regaining either one level 3 spell slot, a level 2 and a level 1 spell slot, or three level 1 spell slots.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

## Level 7: Subclass Feature
You gain a feature from your Magus Subclass.

### Level 7: Nature's Mask
You always have the [[Polymorph]] spell prepared. You can cast the spell on yourself without expending a spell slot, when you do, the spell does not require [[Concentration]] and its duration changes to 8 hours. You can end the spell at any point prior to the end of its duration (no [[action]] required).

Once you cast the spell in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Short Rest]] or [[Long Rest]].

## Level 8: Ability Score Improvement
You gain the [[Ability Score Improvement feat]] or another [[feat]] of your choice for which you qualify.

## Level 9: Rite of Return
When your Mystic Mark ends without you releasing the spell, you regain the expended spell slot. Additionally, you can end your Mystic Mark as a [[Bonus Action]], regaining the expended spell slot.

## Level 10: All-Seeing Eye
As a [[Bonus Action]] you can expend a use of your Esoteric Eye to gain [[Truesight]] out to a distance of 60 feet for 10 minutes. During this time, you can use the [[Bonus Action]] granted by your Esoteric Eye without requiring or expending any uses.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

## Level 11: Sigil Surge
The benefits of your Mystic Mark are improved.

**Aegis.** At the start of each of your turns, you gain [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to the expended spell slot level plus your Intelligence modifier.

**Enspell.** When you deal damage with a weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]], you deal an additional 1d6 damage of the chosen type.

## Level 12: Ability Score Improvement
You gain the [[Ability Score Improvement feat]] or another [[feat]] of your choice for which you qualify.

## Level 14: Subclass Feature
You gain a feature from your Magus Subclass.

### Level 14: Font of Life
As a [[Bonus Action]], you can manifest sylvan energies in a 15-foot [[Emanation]] that originates from you for 1 minute. It ends early if you dismiss it (no [[action]] required) or have the [[Incapacitated]] [[condition]].

When you manifest the [[Emanation]] and at the start of each of your subsequent turns, creatures of your choice within your [[Emanation]] regain [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] equal to 1d8 plus your Intelligence modifier (minimum of one). Additionally, creatures of your choice within your [[Emanation]] have [[Advantage]] on [[Saving Throw|saving throws]].

Once you use this feature, you must finish a [[Long Rest]] before you can do so again.

## Level 15: Sorcerous Shielding
You gain the following benefits.

**Magic Resistance.** Choose one of the following damage types: Force, Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant. You have [[Resistance]] to that [[damage type]]. At the start of each of your turns, you can change the [[damage type]] affected by this feature to one of the other listed options.

**Spell Parry.** You have [[Advantage]] on [[Saving Throw|saving throws]] against spells and spell attacks against you have [[Disadvantage]].

## Level 16: Ability Score Improvement
You gain the [[Ability Score Improvement feat]] or another [[feat]] of your choice for which you qualify.

## Level 18: Spell Surge
In times of need you can surge with arcane power, grasping at magic typically beyond your ability. Choose one level 6 Magus spell as your surge spell. You can cast your surge spell once without expending a spell slot. When you do, you regain one spell slot of your choice that is 5th level or lower.

Once you cast your surge spell you must finish a [[Long Rest]] before you can cast it in this way again.

## Level 19: Epic Boon
You gain an [[Epic Boon feat]] or another [[feat]] of your choice for which you qualify. [[Boon of Spell Recall]] is recommended.

## Level 20: Archmagus
You gain the following benefits.

bChoose Strength or Dexterity. Your Intelligence and the chosen ability score increase by 2, to a maximum of 23.

**Rapid Recovery.** You can use your Magical Recovery twice, regaining both uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

## Level 20: Subclass Feature
You gain a feature from your Magus Subclass.

### Level 20: Sylvan Savant
You gain the following benefits.

**Emerald Spell Surge.** You can expend the use of your Spell Surge to cast [[Conjure Fey]] or [[Wall of Thorns]].

**Font Overflowing.** When you finish a [[Short Rest]], you can regain the use of your Font of Life. Once you regain the use of it in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].
