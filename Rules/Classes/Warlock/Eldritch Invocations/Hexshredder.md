## Hexshredder

Prerequisite: [[Pact of the Blade]]

Your fingers grow into bestial claws, which you can use to make [[Unarmed Strike|unarmed strikes]] that deal 1d6 points of slashing damage. You may add your Charisma modifier instead of your Strength modifier to attack and damage rolls made with these [[Unarmed Strike|unarmed strikes]]. Your [[Unarmed Strike|unarmed strikes]] count as pact weapons, in addition to any [[melee weapons]] you have chosen, and as light weapons for the purposes of [[two-weapon fighting]].

*Type: [[(Eldritch Invocation)]]*
