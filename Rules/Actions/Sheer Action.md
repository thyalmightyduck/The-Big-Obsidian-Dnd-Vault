## Sheer

Only effective against ships with oars, the crew brings their vessel within 5 feet of the enemy vessel, sheering off all the oars on one side of the enemy vessel. This reduces its Speed to 0 feet for 3 rounds until new oars can be deployed. To succeed, the crew member at the helm must make a successful Strength (Athletics) or Wisdom (Survival) check with a DC equal to the other ship's AC.
