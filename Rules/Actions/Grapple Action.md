## Grapple

A member of the crew leads a number of crew in throwing grappling hooks onto an enemy vessel within 5 feet and tying the ships together. While tied together, neither ship can move, and the crews can't use the Come About, Evasive Maneuver, Force Position, Full Speed Ahead, Hold Her Steady, Ram, or Sheer shipboard actions. This action requires a successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) or Wisdom (Survival) check.
