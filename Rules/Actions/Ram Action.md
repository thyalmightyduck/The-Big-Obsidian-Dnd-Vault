## Ram

If the ship is moving, the crew can attempt to ram another vessel within a number of feet equal to half the ship's Speed by making a successful Strength (Athletics) or Wisdom (Survival) check against the other ship's AC and moving that number of feet. If successful, both ships take 2d10 damage for every size category of the ramming ship. If the ship is equipped with a ram, it takes only half damage. Both ships become hopelessly grappled and can't be separated without several minutes of work. If the attempt misses, the ships graze each other and both take one-quarter damage. You can't take the Ram shipboard action against the direction of the wind unless the ship is being rowed.
