## Patch

A member of the crew leads a damage repair team to put in a temporary patch on the hull or repair a damaged station. The crew has a +4 bonus to this check. This action can only be used once per hole or damaged station (see the Disable Station table). The DC to restore Hit Points to the ships is 10 + 2 per hole the ship has suffered, and DC 15 to repair a station. A successful check restores 2d6 Hit Points to the ship or restores a station to working order.
