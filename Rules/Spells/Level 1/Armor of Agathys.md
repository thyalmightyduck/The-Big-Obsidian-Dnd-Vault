_Abjuration Level 1_

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (A Shard Of Blue Glass)
**Duration:** 1 Hour

Protective magical frost surrounds you. You gain 5 [[Temporary Hit Point]]. If a creature hits you with a melee [[Attack Rolls]] before the spell ends, the creature takes 5 Cold damage. The spell ends early if you have no [[Temporary Hit Point]].

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:** The [[Temporary Hit Point]] and the Cold damage both increase by 5 for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes:** [[Warlock]]
**Subclasses:** [[Forge Adept Artificer]], [[Hand of Dread Pugilist]], [[Oath of Conquest Paladin]]
**Species:** [[Dwarf (Mark of Warding)]], [[Tiefling (Levistus)]]
**Feats:** [[Divinely Favored]], [[Magic Initiate]], [[Mark of Warding]], [[Rune Shaper]]
