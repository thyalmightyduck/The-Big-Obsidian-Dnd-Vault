#### Icewalker
*Level 1 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a shard of ice)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 4 hours
---
For the duration, you radiate an aura in a 30-foot Emanation. While in the aura, you and creatures of your choice gain the following benefits:

- Treat slippery ice as normal terrain
- Treat your weight as one-tenth normal for the purpose of breaking through thin ice

**Classes:** Druid, Ranger
