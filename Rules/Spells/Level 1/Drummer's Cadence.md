#### Drummer's Cadence
*Level 1 Enchantment*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 8 hours
---
For the duration, you radiate an aura in a 60-foot Emanation. Whenever an allied ship in this area moves by oar, its Speed increases by 10 ft. The vessel must be sufficiently crewed for this spell to have any effect.

**Classes:** Bard
