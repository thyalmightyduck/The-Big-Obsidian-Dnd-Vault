#### Loki's Escape
*Level 1 Illusion*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take after taking damage
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** 1 round
---
You have the Invisible condition until the end of your next turn. While the spell lasts, you may use your Bonus Action to move your Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. The spell ends early immediately after you make an attack roll, deal damage, or cast a spell.

**Classes:** Warlock
