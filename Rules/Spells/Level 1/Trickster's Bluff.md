#### Trickster's Bluff
*Level 1 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, M (the petrified tongue of a serpent)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
---
You are graced with a measure of **Loki**'s silver tongue. You have Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks. If you fail such a check, you can choose to deal 1d4 Psychic damage to the creature you failed to deceive, and that creature has Disadvantage on attack rolls until the beginning of your next turn.

**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Paladin, Warlock
