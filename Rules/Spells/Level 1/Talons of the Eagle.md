#### Talons of the Eagle
*Level 1 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (an eagle feather)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
---
You imbue yourself with the power of the eagle. While the spell lasts, you have Advantage on Dexterity checks and Dexterity saves.

As a Magic action, you can briefly transform your hands into a set of talons, then move up to your Speed and make a melee talon attack. On a hit, the target takes Slashing damage equal to 1d8 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for every two spell slot levels above 1st.

**Classes:** Druid
