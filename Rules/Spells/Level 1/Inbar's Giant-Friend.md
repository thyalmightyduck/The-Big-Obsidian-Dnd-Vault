#### Inbar's Giant-Friend
*Level 1 Illusion*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when a Giant sees you
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a hair or nail clipping from a goblinoid)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
Your eyes flash with golden light. The Giant must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, its attitude toward you improves 1 step, such as from Hostile to Indifferent or Indifferent to Friendly. A creature's attitude is dynamic, so how you behave toward the giant from this point on can further affect its attitude. A Giant with Immunity to the Charmed condition has Immunity to this spell. Once you have cast this spell on any given Giant, you cannot cast it again on that Giant.

**Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock
