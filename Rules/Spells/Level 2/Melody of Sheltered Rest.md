#### Melody of Sheltered Rest
*Level 2 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a musical instrument)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 2 hours
---
You sing or hum an upbeat melody. For the duration, an aura radiates from you in a 30-foot Emanation. A creature within the aura (including you) can extend the duration for another 2 hours, as long as it continues singing. This can repeat for as long as a creature within the aura continues singing. While in the aura, creatures have Advantage on Perception checks and cannot be Surprised.

**Classes:** Bard, Druid
