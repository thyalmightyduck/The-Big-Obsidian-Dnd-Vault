#### Disrupt the Wyrd
*Level 2 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take in response to a saving throw being rolled by a creature that you can see within 30 feet of yourself
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a tangle of thread)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
The creature rerolls the saving throw, either adding your spellcasting modifier to or subtracting it from the new roll. It must use the new result.

**Classes:** Cleric
