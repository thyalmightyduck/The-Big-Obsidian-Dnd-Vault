#### Billowing Sails
*Level 2 Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 minute or Ritual
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 8 hours
---
You touch a ship or other wind-powered vehicle and fill its sails with a strong breeze. Whenever the ship travels by sail, its Speed increases by 10 ft. or by 1 mph.

**Classes:** Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
