#### Blunder
*Level 2 Enchantment*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when a creature you can see within range hits a target with an attack roll
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** S, M (a banana peel)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
The attacker must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer one of the following effects of your choice:

***Fumble.*** The target drops one object of your choice that it's holding, with the object landing in a space you choose up to 10 feet away from the target. If the target drops the weapon it is using for the attack roll, it can make an Unarmed Strike for the triggering attack.

***Prat Fall.*** The target has the Prone condition. The Disadvantage from the Prone condition applies against the triggering attack, potentially causing it to miss.

**Classes:** Bard, Wizard
