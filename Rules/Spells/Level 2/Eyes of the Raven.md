#### Eyes of the Raven
*Level 2 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a raven feather)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
---
You call upon the power of **Wotan**'s all-knowing ravens, Huginn and Muninn. While the spell lasts, you have Advantage on Intelligence checks and Intelligence saves.

As a Magic action, you can gain Truesight with a range of 30 feet. This lasts until the start of your next turn. Then the spell ends.

**Classes:** Druid
