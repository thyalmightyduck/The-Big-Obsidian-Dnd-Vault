#### Jam Weapon
*Level 2 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when a creature you can see within range makes an attack using a Ranged weapon
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of wet gunpowder)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
The weapon you target suffers a malfunction and the attack fails. A malfunctioning weapon can't be used to make an attack until a creature takes the Utilize action to fix the weapon malfunction.

**Classes:** Bard, Wizard
