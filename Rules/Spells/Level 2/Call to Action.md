#### Call to Action
*Level 2 Enchantment*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take in response to another creature's turn starting
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
The creature starting its turn may immediately use its Reaction to take the Dash, Disengage, Dodge, or Help action.

**Classes:** Bard, Paladin
