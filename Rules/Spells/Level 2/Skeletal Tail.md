#### Skeletal Tail
*Level 2 Conjuration (osteomancy)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a scorpion's tail)
- **Duration:** 1 minute
---
You grow a long, scorpion-like tail made of sharpened bones. Whenever a creature moves while in a 15-foot Emanation originating from you, you can take a Reaction to try and pierce them with your tail. Make a melee spell attack roll. On a hit the target takes Piercing damage equal to 2d4 plus your spellcasting modifier and is pulled to an empty space adjacent to you as your tail drags them in.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes:** Druid, Ranger
