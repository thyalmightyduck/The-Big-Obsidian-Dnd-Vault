#### Reinforce Hull
*Level 2 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (an iron nail)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
---
A vehicle you touch has its damage threshold increased by your spellcasting ability modifier for the duration. A vehicle with no damage threshold can't be targeted by this spell.

**Classes:** Cleric, Druid, Wizard
