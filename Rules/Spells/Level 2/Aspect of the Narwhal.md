#### Aspect of the Narwhal
*Level 2 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
---
A willing creature that you touch takes on the aspects of a narwhal. It grows flippers and a long horn-like tusk. The target gains Resistance to Cold damage while submerged in water, its Speed becomes 5 feet, and it gains a Swim Speed of 50 feet. The target can take a Bonus Action on any turn in which it moves at least 15 feet directly toward an enemy to make a gore attack that deals Piercing damage equal to 2d6 plus its Strength modifier.

**Classes:** Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
