#### Whiteout
*Level 5 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
You must be outdoors to cast this spell, and it ends early if you go indoors.

When you cast this spell, you create a 20-foot-radius Sphere of heavy fog and flurrying snow centered on you. The Sphere is Heavily Obscured and the terrain within it is Difficult Terrain. Any creature that begins its turn in the Sphere or enters the Sphere on its turn must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or receive 1 Exhaustion level. Creatures that have Resistance or Immunity to Cold damage automatically succeed on this save.

When you cast the spell, you may designate a number of creatures equal to your spellcasting ability modifier. These creatures do not receive levels of Exhaustion from the spell.

When the spell ends, all snow created by it disappears instantly and the Exhaustion levels end.

**Classes:** Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
