#### Sword of Judgment
*Level 5 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a strand of horse hair)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
This spell designates an area of protection in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Within that Sphere, a spectral sword appears over each creature, following the creature as long as it remains within the Sphere.

Whenever a creature within the Sphere makes an attack or casts a spell, you can force that creature to make a Dexterity saving throw, as the sword above it falls. Resolve the Dexterity saving throw before the triggering attack or spell. On a failed save, the target takes 4d8 Force damage, or half as much damage on a successful save. A creature makes this save only once per turn.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.

**Classes:** Cleric, Paladin
