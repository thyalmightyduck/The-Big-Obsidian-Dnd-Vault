#### Bearstormer
*Level 5 Illusion*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of bear fur)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
You create three phantasmal grizzly bears in a 20-foot Cube within range. Until the spell ends, you can use a Bonus Action to move the Cube up to 40 feet.

When the Cube appears, each enemy in it makes an Intelligence saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d8 Psychic damage and has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only. A creature also makes this save when the spell's area moves into its space and when a creature enters the spell's area or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You increase the size of the Cube by 20-feet, the number of phantasmal bears by two, and the damage by 2d8 for each spell slot level above 5th.

**Classes:** Bard, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
