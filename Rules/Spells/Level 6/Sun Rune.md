#### Sun Rune
*Level 6 Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a Tiny stone)
- **Duration:** 8 hours
---
You place a magical effect into the stone used as the spell's material component, creating a visible rune etched into the stone. You can only have one Sun Rune spell active at a time.

Once while the spell lasts, any creature holding the stone may use an Action to invoke the rune's power and throw it at a target up to 60 feet away. It stops early if it impacts against a solid surface. When the stone lands or impacts, the rune triggers.

When the rune triggers, the blazing heat of the sun flashes in a 30-foot-radius Sphere centered on the stone. Each creature in the Sphere makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d6 Fire damage and 4d6 Radiant damage, or half as much on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the Sphere that aren't being worn or carried start burning. Ice or snow within the Sphere vaporizes into a fog that fills the Sphere, making it Heavily Obscured for 1 minute or until a strong wind (such as one created by the *Gust of Wind* spell) disperses it.

After activating the rune's power, the creature cannot activate another Sun Rune again until after a Long Rest.

**Classes:** Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
