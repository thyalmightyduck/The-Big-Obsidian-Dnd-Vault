#### Conjure Giant
*Level 6 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
---
You summon a giant of challenge rating 7 or lower, which appears in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. The giant disappears when they drop to 0 hit points or when the spell ends. The giant is friendly to you and your companions for the duration. Roll initiative for the giant, which has their own turns. They obey any verbal commands that you issue to them (no action required by you) as long as the commands don't violate their alignment. If you don't issue any commands to the giant, they defend themself from hostile creatures but otherwise take no actions. If your concentration is broken, the giant doesn't disappear, but instead, you lose control of them: the giant becomes hostile toward you and your companions and might attack. An uncontrolled giant can't be dismissed by you, and they disappear 1 hour after summoned.

The GM has the giant's statistics.
***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the challenge rating increases by 1 for each slot level above 6th.
