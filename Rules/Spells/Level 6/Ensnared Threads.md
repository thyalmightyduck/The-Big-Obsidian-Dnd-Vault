#### Ensnared Threads
*Level 6 Divination*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a knotted string of wool)
- **Duration:** 1 minute
---
**The Norns** allow you to briefly force a connection between the threads of your fate and those of another creature. A creature you can see within range must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, you form a connection between yourself and the target.

Once per turn, until the spell ends, the first attack that hits you hits the target creature instead, if it can be targeted by the attack. If that creature cannot be the target of the attack, the attack hits you normally.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** If you cast this spell with an 8th- or 9th- level spell slot, all attacks that hit you instead hit the target. Any of the attacks the creature cannot be the target of still hit you normally.

**Classes:** Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
