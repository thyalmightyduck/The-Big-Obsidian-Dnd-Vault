#### Vile Necrosis
*Level 6 Necromancy*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a vial of coagulated blood)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You point your finger towards a creature within range, projecting a pale green beam of debilitating energy at them.

Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 12d6 necrotic damage, and it becomes weakened.

While weakened by this spell, a target deals half damage with weapon attacks and spells.

A weakened target can make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a successful save, the weakness ends.

**Classes:** Apothecary, Sorcerer, Sorcerer, Warlock, Warlock, Wizard, Wizard
