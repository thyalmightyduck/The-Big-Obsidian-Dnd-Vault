#### Hungry Jaws of Fenris
*Level 6 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
As a Magic action, you channel the power of **Fenris**, the Great Wolf, transforming your mouth into a gaping maw. This doesn't impede your movement or actions, but you can't speak or cast spells with verbal components for the duration.

While the spell is active, you can use your jaws to make a bite attack. On a hit, the target takes Piercing damage equal to 4d10 plus your spellcasting ability modifier, and it has the Grappled condition (escape DC equals your spell save DC).

If you bite a target already Grappled by you, you can choose to swallow it, and the Grappled condition ends. A swallowed creature is shunted into a pocket dimension. While there, it has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the dimension, and takes 3d12 Force damage at the start of each of your turns. You can't swallow a creature if you have already swallowed a creature using this spell.

A swallowed creature can make a save at the end of each of its turns. On a success, you vomit the creature to the nearest unoccupied space of your choice. The same thing occurs if the spell's duration elapses.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage dealt by the bite attack increases by 1d12 for each spell slot level above 6th.

**Classes:** Druid
