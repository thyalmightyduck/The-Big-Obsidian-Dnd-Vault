#### Freezing Fog
*Level 3 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
---
You create a 20-foot-radius Sphere of bone-chilling fog centered on a point within range. The Sphere is Heavily Obscured and considered Difficult Terrain. The fog lingers in the air for the duration or until a strong wind (such as one created by *Gust of Wind*) disperses it.

Each creature that starts its turn in the Sphere, or moves into its area, must succeed on a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a target's Speed is halved until the end of its next turn.

When a creature moves into or within the area, it takes 2d4 Cold damage for every 5 feet it travels.

You have Immunity to all of the effects of this spell.

**Classes:** Druid
