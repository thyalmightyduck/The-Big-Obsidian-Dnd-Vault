#### Luckfingers
*Level 3 Enchantment*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
---
You touch one Medium or smaller creature, suffusing it with confidence and skill. Until the spell ends, the creature has Advantage on all ability checks. If such a check results in failure, the creature can choose to end the spell and automatically succeed on the ability check.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 3rd. If a creature chooses to end the spell to automatically succeed on an ability check, the spell ends only for them.

**Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard
