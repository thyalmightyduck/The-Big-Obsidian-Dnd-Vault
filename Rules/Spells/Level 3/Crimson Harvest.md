# The Crooked Moon (2024):
_Level 3 Necromancy_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a vial of blood)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A wave of blood sweeps out from you in a 15-foot [[Emanation]]. Each creature of your choice in the wave must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d10 Necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

If you damage at least one Small or larger creature, one creature of your choice that you can see within the [[Emanation]] regains a number of [[Hit Point]]s equal to half the damage roll.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 3.

**Classes:** [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]]
**Subclasses:** [[Crimson Sorcery Sorcerer]], [[Hand of Dread Pugilist]], [[Occultist Guild Monster Hunter]], [[Sanguine Thief Rogue]], [[Spellslinger Gunslinger]], [[Way of the Arcane Hand Monk]]
