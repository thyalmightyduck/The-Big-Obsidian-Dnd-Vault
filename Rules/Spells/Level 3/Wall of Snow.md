#### Wall of Snow
*Level 3 Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a freshly packed snowball)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
---
You create a wall of packed snow on a solid surface within range. You can make the wall up to 50 feet long, 15 feet high and 1 foot thick. Or you can make a 10-foot-radius, hemispherical dome. You can shape the wall in any way you choose so long as it makes one continuous path along the ground. The wall lasts for the duration.

If the wall cuts through a creature's space when it appears, the creature is pushed to one side of the wall (you choose which side).

The wall is an object that can be damaged and thus breached. It has AC 10 and 10 Hit Points per 10-foot section, and it has Immunity to Cold, Poison, and Psychic damage, and Vulnerability to Fire and Radiant damage. Reducing a 10-foot section of wall to 0 Hit Points destroys it.

Creatures within an undamaged dome have Immunity to the effects of Extreme Cold while the spell lasts.

If you cast this spell in an area of snowy terrain, it blends seamlessly with the existing snow. If a creature takes a Study action to examine the wall, the creature can differentiate the wall from the surrounding terrain with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC.

**Classes:** Druid, Ranger
