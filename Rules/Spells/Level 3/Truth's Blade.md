#### Truth's Blade
*Level 3 Divination*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (A silver pin, which is not consumed)
- **Duration:** 10 minutes
---
You punish one who would speak falsely to you. The creature must make a Wisdom saving throw. If it fails, for the duration, it takes 4d8 Psychic damage whenever it lies.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The amount of Psychic damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 3rd.

**Classes:** Cleric, Paladin
