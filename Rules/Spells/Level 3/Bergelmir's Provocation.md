#### Bergelmir's Provocation
*Level 3 Enchantment*
___
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when you see a creature make an attack or spell attack
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
The creature making the attack must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature makes the attack against a different eligible target of your choice, which cannot include the creature itself. If there is no other eligible target, the creature's attack is wasted.

**Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
