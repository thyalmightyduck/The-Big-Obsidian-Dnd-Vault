#### Giant's Teeth
*Level 3 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a handful of molars)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
Two half-circles of enormous teeth erupt from the ground and slam together to crush a creature of your choice within 90 feet.

The target has the Grappled condition. The escape DC is equal to your spell save DC. At the beginning of each of its turns while it has the Grappled condition, the creature takes 2d10 Bludgeoning damage. If the target escapes, the spell ends.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** When you cast this spell using a 5th-level spell slot, the damage increases to 3d10. When you cast it using a 7th-level spell slot, the damage increases to 5d8. When you cast it using a spell slot of 9th level, the damage increases to 6d8.

**Classes:** Sorcerer, Wizard
