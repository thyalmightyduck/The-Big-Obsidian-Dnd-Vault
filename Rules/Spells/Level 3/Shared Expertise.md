#### Shared Expertise
*Level 3 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of boot leather)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
---
For the duration, you radiate an aura in a 30-foot Emanation. Choose up to 2 skills that you have Expertise in. If you have no Expertise in a skill, this spell has no effect.

While in the area, allied creatures are treated as having Proficiency in the chosen skills. If an ally already has Proficiency or Expertise in the skill, they gain no benefit.

**Classes:** Bard, Ranger
