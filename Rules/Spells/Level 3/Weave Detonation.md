#### Weave Detonation
*Level 3 Divination*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a short piece of thread)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
An invisible, soundless burst of fate erupts at a point you choose. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature has Disadvantage on all D20 Tests for 1 minute.

A creature can repeat the Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the spell on a success.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** For each spell slot level above 3rd, you can exclude up to five creatures within the radius from the spell's effects.

**Classes:** Cleric, Sorcerer, Wizard
