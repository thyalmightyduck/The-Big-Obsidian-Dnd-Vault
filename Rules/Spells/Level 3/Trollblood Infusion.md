#### Trollblood Infusion
*Level 3 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of troll blood)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
A creature you touch is magically infused with the regenerative properties of troll blood. For the duration of the spell, the target regains 5 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns, if it has at least 1 Hit Point, and automatically succeeds on Death Saving Throws. If the creature takes Acid or Fire damage, this effect does not function at the start of its next turn.

**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Druid, Warlock
