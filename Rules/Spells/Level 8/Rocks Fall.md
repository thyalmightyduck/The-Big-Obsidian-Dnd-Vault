#### Rocks Fall
*Level 8 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (ten dice)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
Boulders appear in the air and fall in a 60-foot-radius, 120-foot-high Cylinder centered on a point within range. Each creature in the Cylinder makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 10d8 Bludgeoning damage and is buried under the rubble. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

A buried creature has the Prone and Restrained conditions, and can take an action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC, freeing itself from the boulders and ending the Prone and Restrained conditions on itself on a success.

**Classes:** Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
