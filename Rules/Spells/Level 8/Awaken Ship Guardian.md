#### Awaken Ship Guardian
*Level 8 Conjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 24 hours
---
You summon a protective spirit to live within the hull of a Large or larger ship. While the spell lasts, the ship has the following benefits:

- Immunity to Fire damage.
- Each time the ship takes damage from a Large or larger creature, the attacking creature makes a Wisdom saving throw, taking 5d6 Psychic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
- As a Bonus Action while you are on the ship, you can expend a spell slot, and the ship regains Hit Points equal to three times the slot level plus your spellcasting modifier.

**Classes:** Cleric, Druid
