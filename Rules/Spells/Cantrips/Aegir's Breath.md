#### Aegir's Breath
*Evocation Cantrip*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You blow a cloud of frigid air at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 Cold damage, or 1d12 if the target is at least partially submerged in water.
***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The spell creates two clouds at level 5, three clouds at level 11, and four clouds at level 17. You can direct the clouds at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each cloud.

**Classes:** Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
