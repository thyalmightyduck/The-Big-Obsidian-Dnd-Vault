#### Demiplanar Pitch
*Conjuration Cantrip*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
---
You thrust your hand into empty space in front of you and punch through to a demiplane housing the contents of a random *Bag of Holding* somewhere in the multiverse, then launch whatever you can grab at a creature you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target, dealing 1d8 Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage (your choice) on a hit. After you make the attack, the object teleports back to the demiplane it came from.
***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).

**Classes:** Magus, Sorcerer, Wizard
