#### Ghostly Crew
*Level 4 Necromancy*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 8 hours
---
You summon intangible spirits to crew a touched vessel. For the duration of the spell, as long as you are within 30 feet of the vessel, it is considered to have a sufficient number of sailors to take a full complement of normal ship actions (including movement and using siege weapons). The spirits can't take other actions or engage in any other activities, such as fighting independently or repairing the vessel.

**Classes:** Cleric, Sorcerer, Wizard
