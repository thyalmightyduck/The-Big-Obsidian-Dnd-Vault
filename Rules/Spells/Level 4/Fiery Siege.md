#### Fiery Siege
*Level 4 Transmutation*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of pitch)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
---
You touch a siege weapon, imbuing it with pyroclastic power. For the duration, the siege weapon deals an extra 1d10 Fire damage when it hits. As a Bonus Action, you can touch a different siege engine, transferring the effect to it.

**Classes:** Druid, Paladin, Sorcerer, Wizard
