#### Knife of Fate
*Level 4 Necromancy*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a silver knife)
- **Duration:** Special
---
You speak a word of binding fate and point your dagger. A creature you can see within 60 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target's fate is bound to one random ally of the target. Any damage or condition the target suffers is instead inflicted on the individual to whom they are bound, and vice versa. A creature can be bound to only one other creature at a time. This effect lasts until either of the affected creatures gains a level or are targeted by a *heal* spell or partake of a *heroes' feast* spell, lifting the binding.
