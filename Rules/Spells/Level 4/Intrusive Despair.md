_Level 4 Enchantment_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 90 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a devil's trumpet blossom)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You curse a creature within range, filling its mind with creeping paranoia and despair. When the target takes the [[Attack Action]] or [[Magic action]], it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or waste its action. Once the target succeeds on three saving throws against this spell, the spell ends.

**Classes:** [[Bard]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]]
**Subclasses:** [[Grim Harbinger Ranger]], [[Occultist Guild Monster Hunter]], [[Sanguine Thief Rogue]], [[Spellslinger Gunslinger]], [[Way of the Arcane Hand Monk]]
