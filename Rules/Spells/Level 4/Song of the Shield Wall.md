#### Song of the Shield Wall
*Level 4 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
---
You begin a chant that is magically accompanied by the clanging sound of hammers against iron. Glowing, spectral, circular shields appear before a number of creatures equal to your spellcasting ability modifier who are within 30 feet of you. Each creature gains a +2 bonus to their AC.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** If you cast this spell with a 6th level spell slot, the bonus to AC increased to +3, and if you use an 8th level spell slot, it increases to +4.

**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Paladin
