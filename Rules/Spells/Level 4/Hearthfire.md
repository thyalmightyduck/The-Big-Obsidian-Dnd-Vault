#### Hearthfire
*Level 4 Abjuration*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, M (a bit of charcoal)
- **Duration:** 8 hours
---
You touch one Large or smaller object that isn't being worn or carried by someone else. Until the spell ends, the object sheds Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet. Within the spell's area of Bright Light, you and each creature you choose have Immunity to the harmful effects of Extreme Cold.

**Classes:** Cleric, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
