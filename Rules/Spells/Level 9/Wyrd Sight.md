#### Wyrd Sight
*Level 9 Divination*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (A hag's eye)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour
---
You manipulate the threads of luck and fate to work in your favor. No more than once per turn, you can roll 1d12 and add or subtract it from a d20 roll made by a creature you can see.

**Classes:** Cleric, Druid, Warlock
