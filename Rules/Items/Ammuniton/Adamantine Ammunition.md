#### Adamantine Ammunition

Ammunition, Generic Variant, Minor Tier

---

Ammunition made of or coated with adamantine is unusually effective when used to break objects. Whenever a piece of adamantine ammunition hits an object, the hit is a critical hit.

The adamantine version of ten pieces of ammunition costs 500 gp more than the normal version, whether the ammunition is made of the metal or coated with it.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Arrow* (*Adamantine Arrow*)
- *Arrow, Silver* (*Adamantine Arrow, Silver*)
- *Arrow, Wooden* (*Adamantine Arrow, Wooden*)
- *Bellows Cannister* (*Adamantine Bellows Cannister*)
- *Bolt* (*Adamantine Bolt*)
- *Bolt, Silver* (*Adamantine Bolt, Silver*)
- *Bolt, Wooden* (*Adamantine Bolt, Wooden*)
- *Bullet* (*Adamantine Bullet*)
- *Bullet, Silver* (*Adamantine Bullet, Silver*)
- *Bullet, Wooden* (*Adamantine Bullet, Wooden*)
- *Cannonball* (*Adamantine Cannonball*)
- *Cannonball* (*Adamantine Cannonball*)
- *Dragon Shot* (*Adamantine Dragon Shot*)
- *Explosive Cannonball* (*Adamantine Explosive Cannonball*)

- *Flare* (*Adamantine Flare*)
- *Needle* (*Adamantine Needle*)
- *Needle, Silver* (*Adamantine Needle, Silver*)
