#### Hydrangea Dragon Poison

Adventuring Gear, Poison (Contact or Ingested)

---

This colorless poison is harvested from the blooms of a living hydrangea dragon. A creature subjected to this poison must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 4d12 Poison damage, gains 4 levels of Exhaustion, and has the Poisoned condition until it is treated with *Hydrangea Dragon Antidote*. On a successful save, the creature gains 2 levels of Exhaustion and isn't Poisoned.
