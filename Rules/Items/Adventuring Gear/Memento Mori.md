#### Memento Mori

Adventuring Gear

---

A Memento Mori is a physical remnant of a deceased humanoid typically used as a trophy, to honor the dead, or for some other occult purpose. Spiritualists, Knights Stygian, and Necrodancers can use such an item as a Spellcasting Focus.

Multiple variations of this item exist, as listed below:

- *Humanoid Skull*
- *Necklace of Teeth*
- *Preserved Organ in a Jar*
