#### Hunter's Armor

Advanced Equipment

---

Hunter's Armor is a favorite of monster hunters across the length and breadth of Etharis. Any type of armor can be constructed as Hunter's Armor by paying the listed extra cost. This reinforces the most vital areas with adamantine and mithral threads to turn devastating hits into normal wounds.

If a blow would cause a Grievous Wound, roll a d20. On an 11 or higher, the blow does not deliver a Grievous Wound.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Beinagrind* (*Hunter's Beinagrind*)
- *Breastplate* (*Hunter's Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Hunter's Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Hunter's Chain Shirt*)
- *Double Mail* (*Hunter's Double Mail*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Hunter's Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hardened Mail Shirt* (*Hunter's Hardened Mail Shirt*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Hunter's Hide Armor*)
- *Leather Armor* (*Hunter's Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Hunter's Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Hunter's Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Hunter's Ring Mail*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Hunter's Scale Mail*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Hunter's Splint Armor*)

### Advanced Equipment

The brilliant engineers and crafters who designed and built Etharis's highly advanced weapons and ammunition did not stop there. Other inventions of war—and of peace—became invaluable to the soldiers, guards, and adventurers of Etharis.

Some pieces of Advanced equipment, such as the corrupting *Shadowsteel Focus*, need special training to fully employ. Other items, like the *Retractable Shield*, are usable by anyone proficient with *Shields*. And many other pieces of equipment are usable by anyone—though few would be able to afford and maintain these mechanical, alchemical, or even magical marvels.

As they are expensive and difficult to make, access to Advanced equipment is limited—maybe even as limited as some magic items. The largest cities, or locations housing master crafters, may only have a few items of Advanced equipment.
