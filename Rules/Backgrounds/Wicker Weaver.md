## Wicker Weaver

- **Ability Scores:** Increase one of your starting ability scores by 2 and another by 1, or increase three scores by 1 each.
- **Feat:** Charm Twister
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Insight, Nature
- **Language:** Sylvan
- **Tool Proficiency:** *Weaver's Tools*
- **Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) *Quarterstaff*, *Weaver's Tools*, *Basket* (with dry reeds ready to weave), *Book* (druidic lore), *Iron Pot*, *Traveler's Clothes*, 19 GP; or (B) 50 GP
