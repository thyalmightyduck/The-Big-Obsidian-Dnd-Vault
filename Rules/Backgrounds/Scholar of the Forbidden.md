## Scholar of the Forbidden

- **Ability Scores:** Increase one of your starting ability scores by 2 and another by 1, or increase three scores by 1 each.
- **Feat:** Dread Speech
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Arcana, Intimidation
- **Language:** Choose one (Deep Speech recommended)
- **Tool Proficiency:** *Calligrapher's Supplies*
- **Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) *Calligrapher's Supplies*, *Book* (a topic of arcana), *Ink*, *Ink Pen*, *Lamp*, *Oil* (3 flasks), *Paper* (10 sheets), 2 GP; or (B) 50 GP
