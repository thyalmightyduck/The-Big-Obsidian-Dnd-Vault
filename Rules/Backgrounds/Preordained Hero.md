## Preordained Hero

- **Ability Scores:** Charisma, Intelligence, Strength
- **Feat:** Choose one Origin feat that imparts the blessing of a deity
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Athletics, Persuasion
- **Tool Proficiencies:** Choose one kind of Gaming Set
- **Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Gaming Set (same as above), *Fine Clothes*, *Handaxe*, *Shortbow*, *Arrows (20)*, *Quiver*, *Traveler's Clothes*, and 20 GP; or (B) 50 GP
