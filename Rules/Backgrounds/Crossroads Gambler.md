## Crossroads Gambler

- **Ability Scores:** Increase one of your starting ability scores by 2 and another by 1, or increase three scores by 1 each.
- **Feat:** Fate Gambler
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Deception, Insight
- **Tool Proficiency:** *Choose one kind of *Gaming Set**
- **Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) *Gaming set (same as above*, *Caltrops*, *Fine Clothes*, *Fine Wine (Bottle)*, 23 GP; or (B) 50 GP
