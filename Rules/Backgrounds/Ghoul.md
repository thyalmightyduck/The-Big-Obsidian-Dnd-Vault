## Ghoul

- **Ability Scores:** Strength, Dexterity, Charisma
- **Feat:** Nocturnal
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Perception and Survival
- **Tool Proficiency:** Choose one kind of *Artisan's Tools*
- **Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) *Artisan's Tools* (same as above), *Dagger*, *Kindred Vitae*, *Hooded Lantern*, *Shortbow*, 20 *Arrows*, *Traveler's Clothes*, 10 GP; or (B) 50 GP
