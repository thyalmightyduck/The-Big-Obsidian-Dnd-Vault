## Reflected Wanderer

- **Ability Scores:** Increase one of your starting ability scores by 2 and another by 1, or increase three scores by 1 each.
- **Feat:** Unreflected
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Deception, Investigation
- **Tool Proficiency:** *Disguise Kit*
- **Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) *Disguise Kit*, *Mirror*, *Traveler's Clothes*, 16 GP; or (B) 50 GP
