## Crimson Aspirant

- **Ability Scores:** Increase one of your starting ability scores by 2 and another by 1, or increase three scores by 1 each.
- **Feat:** Crimson Ritualist
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Arcana, Medicine
- **Tool Proficiency:** *Herbalism Kit*
- **Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) *Herbalism Kit*, *Dagger*, *Fine Clothes*, *Healer's Kit*, 3 *Vials*, 10 GP; or (B) 50 GP
