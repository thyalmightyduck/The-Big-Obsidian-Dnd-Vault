## Rest Warden

- **Ability Scores:** Increase one of your starting ability scores by 2 and another by 1, or increase three scores by 1 each.
- **Feat:** Grave Keeper
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Religion, Survival
- **Tool Proficiency:** *Mason's Tools*
- **Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) *Mason's Tools*, *Hooded Lantern*, *Oil* (3 flasks), *Shovel*, Soap, *Traveler's Clothes* (with thick gloves and dark-colored hood), 10 GP; or (B) 50 GP
