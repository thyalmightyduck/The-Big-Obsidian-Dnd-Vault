## Ghostlight Passenger

- **Ability Scores:** Increase one of your starting ability scores by 2 and another by 1, or increase three scores by 1 each.
- **Feat:** Ghostlight Medium
- **Skill Proficiencies:** Insight, Persuasion
- **Tool Proficiency:** *Smith's Tools*
- **Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) *Smith's Tools*, *Bullseye Lantern*, *Fine Clothes*, *Oil* (3 flasks), 5 GP; or (B) 50 GP
