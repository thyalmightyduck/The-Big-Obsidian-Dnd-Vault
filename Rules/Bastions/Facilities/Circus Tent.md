## Circus Tent

*Level 9 Bastion Facility*

- **Prerequisite:** Membership in the Phantasmic Circus
- **Space:** Vast  [36 sq]
- **Hirelings:** 4
- **Order:** Trade

The Circus Tent is a space for musical, acrobatic, comedic, or other performances intended to delight a crowd. The tent includes seating for audience members. Your hirelings are **Performers** whose alignment matches your own. Assign names, personalities, and circus acts to them as you see fit.

### Trade: Circus Performance

When you issue the *Trade* order to this facility, the facility's hirelings turn the Circus Tent into a performance venue for 7 days. At the end of the seventh day, your portion of the proceeds is equal to 2d6 × 10 GP.
