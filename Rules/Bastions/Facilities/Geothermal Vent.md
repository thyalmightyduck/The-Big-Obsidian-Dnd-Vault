## Geothermal Vent

*Level 5 Bastion Facility*

- **Prerequisite:** Membership in the Crucible Keepers
- **Space:** Roomy  [16 sq]
- **Hirelings:** 1
- **Order:** Empower

A geothermal vent draws heat from deep below the surface to provide energy and power simple machines.

### Empower: Withstand Heat

When you issue the *Empower* order to this facility, for the next 7 days, the facility's hireling teaches a series of meditative techniques that allow a character to mitigate the injuries they suffer from flames. Any character who trains here for at least 8 hours on each of those days gains the following benefit at the end of the training period:

- **Heat Tolerant.** When you take Fire damage, you can take a Reaction to reduce this damage by 1d4 plus your level. This benefit lasts for 7 days.

### Hot Water

Your basic facilities benefit from a constant source of adjustable heat. For example, a dining room has a warming table to keep food hot, a kitchen has multiple ovens with varying heat, and a bedroom has a basin that warms water for washing.
