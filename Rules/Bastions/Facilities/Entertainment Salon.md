## Entertainment Salon

*Level 5 Bastion Facility*

- **Prerequisite:** Membership in the Bejeweled Conclave
- **Space:** Roomy  [16 sq]
- **Hirelings:** 2
- **Order:** Research

This fancy salon boasts elegant wall hangings, comfortable reclining couches, and a well-stocked bar.

### Open Pockets

Your eloquent hirelings convince others to pay a little more or to accept a little less. When you roll a die to determine how many Gold Pieces you pay or gain for a *Bastion* order or event, you can roll twice and take either result.

### Research: Learn Secrets

When you issue the *Research* order to this facility, you direct the facility's hirelings to entertain well-informed contacts and learn about a specific creature you name within 50 miles of your Bastion. After 7 days of work, the hirelings gain 1d4 facts about the named creature that aren't widely known and might be amusing, embarrassing, or useful. The hirelings also discover the creature's power relative to yours (such as whether the creature's CR is higher than, lower than, or the same as your level). At the DM's discretion, you might receive limited or incorrect information about an exceptionally private or supernaturally disguised creature.
