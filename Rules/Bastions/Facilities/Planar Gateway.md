## Planar Gateway

*Level 17 Bastion Facility*

- **Prerequisite:** Membership in the Horizon Weavers; Ability to use an *Arcane Focus*, *Holy Symbol*, or tool as a Spellcasting Focus
- **Space:** Roomy  [16 sq]
- **Hirelings:** 1
- **Order:** Empower

The focus of this chamber is an inscribed stone archway filled with mist and strange lights. This archway can connect to other planes of existence.

### Empower: Open Gate

When you issue the *Empower* order to this facility, you instruct the facility's hireling to tune the archway to a place on another plane of existence. Tuning the archway takes 7 days, at the end of which time the gateway opens. If you provide a diamond worth 5,000+ GP as part of the tuning, the gate connects to a place you specify on another plane, as with the *Gate* spell (for transport only, not to draw a specific creature through) and destroys the diamond. If you don't provide a diamond or if a deity or another planar ruler blocks the intended gate from opening, determine the gate's destination randomly on the Random Planar Destination table. In either case, the gateway remains open for 1 minute.
