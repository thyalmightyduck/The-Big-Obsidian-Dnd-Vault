## Lighthouse

*Level 9 Bastion Facility*

- **Prerequisite:** Membership in the Seers of Sea and Sky
- **Space:** Roomy  [16 sq]
- **Hirelings:** 1
- **Order:** Harvest

This tall lighthouse has rotating lenses that make its light visible from a significant distance, even in poor weather. Your hireling operates and maintains the machinery and ignites or douses the light as you direct.

### Vision Charm

Time in your lighthouse inspires you to pierce the darkness. After spending a Long Rest in your Lighthouse, you gain the Charm of Darkvision (see the *Dungeon Master's Guide*).

### Harvest: Illuminate Treasure

When you issue the *Harvest* order to this facility, you instruct the facility's hireling to watch the surrounding area for lost or discarded valuables. The hireling watches for 7 days. At the end of that time, you can hire salvagers to retrieve treasures your hireling spotted. You must spend 100 GP to hire the salvagers, who return with treasure worth 1d4 × 100 GP.
