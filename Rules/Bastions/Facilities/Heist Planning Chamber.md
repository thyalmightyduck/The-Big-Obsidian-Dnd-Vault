## Heist Planning Chamber

*Level 9 Bastion Facility*

- **Prerequisite:** Membership in the Ninth Quill
- **Space:** Roomy  [16 sq] or Vast  [36 sq]
- **Hirelings:** 1
- **Order:** Research

This room has a planning table, pieces of miniature terrain to represent walls or furniture, and tomes on magical security, architecture, and spy craft useful when planning a heist.

### Research: Reconnaissance

When you issue the *Research* order to this facility, your hireling sends spies to evaluate a building within 50 miles of your Bastion. This work takes 7 days. At the end of the seventh day, the hireling provides a model of the building on the planning table. Public areas of the building are depicted accurately, and private areas are depicted only generally. Any area concealed by magic or protected by powerful guardians, at the DM's discretion, might not be depicted accurately or at all. The spies avoid all creatures, so information about the building's inhabitants is sparse.

The model remains until you use the *Research* order on this facility again, but it might become inaccurate over time if the building changes.

### Heist Preparations

A character who prepares for a theft or infiltration using the model for at least 8 hours chooses one of the following roles. The role's benefit applies while the character is within 1 mile of the building depicted by the model and lasts for 7 days or until you prepare again:

- **Enforcer:** Advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks
- **Face:** Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks
- **Lookout:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks
- **Medic:** Advantage on Wisdom (Medicine) checks
- **Sneak:** Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks
- **Thief:** Advantage on Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks

### Enlarging the Facility

You can enlarge your Heist Planning Chamber to a Vast facility by spending 2,000 GP. If you do so, the range of Reconnaissance increases to 500 miles, and a character preparing for a heist can choose two roles, gaining the benefits of both.
