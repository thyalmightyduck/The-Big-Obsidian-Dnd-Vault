## Transmutation Vat

*Level 5 Bastion Facility*

- **Prerequisite:** Membership in the Bringers of Cosmic Dawn
- **Space:** Cramped  [4 sq] or Roomy  [16 sq]
- **Hirelings:** 1
- **Order:** Harvest

This laboratory features a vat or cauldron simmering with transformation potential.

### Harvest: Fluid Transmutation

When you issue the *Harvest* order to this facility, you instruct your hireling to pour a substance into the vat and transform it into something else. You must expend a Common or Uncommon potion, which is destroyed in the vat. At the end of 7 days, the hireling produces your choice of a flask of *Alchemist's Fire*, a vial of *Antitoxin*, a vial of *Basic Poison*, or a Common or Uncommon potion of your choice.

### Enlarging the Facility

You can enlarge your Transmutation Vat into a Roomy facility by spending 2,000 GP. When you issue the *Harvest* order to a Roomy Transmutation Vat, you can expend two Common or Uncommon potions. If you do, your hireling produces two substances of your choice instead of one; the options can be different.
