## Morgue

*Level 5 Bastion Facility*

- **Prerequisite:** Membership in the Covenant of the Grave
- **Space:** Cramped  [4 sq] or Roomy  [16 sq]
- **Hirelings:** 1
- **Order:** Research

A morgue is a location where corpses are stored and studied. You can store up to four Small or Medium corpses in the Morgue.

### Corpse Storage

As long as this facility's hireling is present, each corpse stored in the Morgue is affected by the *Gentle Repose* spell. If the facility lacks a hireling or a corpse is removed, the facility's corpses or the removed corpse loses this protection until returned to a Morgue with a hireling.

### Research: Cadaver Insights

When you issue the *Research* order to this facility, you instruct the facility's hireling to carefully inspect the corpses. This work takes 7 days. The next time you speak with the hireling, the hireling can share the cause of death of each corpse present and answer one question you have for each corpse as though the hireling had cast the *Speak with Dead* spell on it.

### Enlarging the Facility

You can enlarge your Morgue to a Roomy facility by spending 2,000 GP. If you do so, the Morgue can store up to twelve Small or Medium corpses and up to one Large corpse.
