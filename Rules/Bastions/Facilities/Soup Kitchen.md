## Soup Kitchen

*Level 5 Bastion Facility*

- **Prerequisite:** Membership in the Sheltering Hands
- **Space:** Roomy  [16 sq]
- **Hirelings:** 1
- **Order:** Recruit

This kitchen prepares large quantities of food and serves it to hungry people.

### Recruit: Kitchen Operation

When you issue the *Recruit* order to this facility, you instruct your hireling to operate the soup kitchen for 7 days. Ingredients cost you 50 GP. If you would lose a Bastion Defender or a hireling on the next roll on the *Bastion Events* table, someone who believes in your work steps into their place and takes over immediately. You don't count as losing that Bastion Defender or hireling.

### Traveling Snacks

After spending a Long Rest in your Bastion, you gain a magical Charm that lasts for 7 days or until you use it. The Charm allows you to cast *Goodberry* once without expending a spell slot.
