#### Ring of the Glacial Jarl

Ring, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This glittering ring of blue-white crystal bears runes of ice and battle. While wearing the ring, your melee weapon attacks deal 1d6 extra Cold Damage on a hit.

In addition, the ring has **3 charges** and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. You can expend its charges to use the properties below.

***Frozen Wrath.*** You can expend **1 charge** to cast *Ice Storm*. You are immune to any damage dealt by this casting of the spell, and you ignore the Difficult Terrain created by it.

***War Cry.*** You can expend **1 charge** after you hit with a melee weapon attack to grant Advantage on all attacks made by allies who can see or hear you against the creature you hit. This effect lasts until the start of your next turn.

Finally, it has the following property.

***Jarlpact Benefit.*** If an ally within **120 feet** is attuned to and wearing any other Ring of the Jarlpact, you have Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.
