#### Enchantment Breaker

Ring, Rare

---

This ring's tiny chains rotate slowly around your finger while worn. Whenever you are affected by a spell that restores Hit Points, such as Cure Wounds or Healing Word, you can take a Reaction to repeat the saving throw against an Enchantment spell that's currently affecting you.

***Shatter Enchantment.*** When you fail a saving throw against an Enchantment spell, you can take a Reaction to weaken the spell's grip. You and your allies within 10 feet of you that were targeted by the spell can reroll the save, potentially turning the failure into a success. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until 1d4 days have passed.
