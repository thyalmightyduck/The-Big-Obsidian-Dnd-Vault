#### Ring of the Cragtop Jarl

Ring, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This polished granite ring bears runes of strength. While wearing the ring, when you attack with a weapon that deals Bludgeoning damage, you score a Critical Hit on a natural 19 or 20.

In addition, the ring has **3 charges** and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. You can expend its charges to use the properties below.

***Bones of the Mountain.*** You can expend **1 charge** to cast *False Life*.

***Mountain Strike.*** You can expend **1 charge** when you deal Bludgeoning damage with a melee or ranged attack to push the target 10 feet.

Finally, it has the following property.

***Jarlpact Benefit.*** If an ally within **120 feet** is attuned to and wearing any other Ring of the Jarlpact, you have Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.
