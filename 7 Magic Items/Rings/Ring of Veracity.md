#### Ring of Veracity

Ring, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)

---

This golden band is stamped with images of smiling humanoid faces. While attuned to this ring, the wearer has Advantage on Search actions to make a Wisdom (Insight) check.

Additionally, you can take a Magic action to cast *Zone of Truth* (save DC 15) with the ring. The ring can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
