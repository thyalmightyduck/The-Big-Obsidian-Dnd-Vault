#### Starmetal Ring

Ring, Rare

---

This roughly crafted metal ring seems to have an otherworldly pull on other metallic items. While touching an object with the hand that bears this ring, you can spend 1 hour forging a magical bond between the ring and the object, provided the object weighs no more than 25 pounds and is at least 50 percent metal. Only one object can be bound to the ring in this way at a time.

While wearing the ring, you can take a Bonus Action on your turn to magically pull its bound object up to 20 feet toward you, provided the object is within 40 feet of you and isn't behind Total Cover. The pulled object bounces harmlessly off of creatures and other objects in its path. If you pull the object into your space, you can catch it in your free hand if you have one. Otherwise, it falls to the ground at your feet.
