#### Ring of Wind

Ring, Uncommon

---

While wearing this ring, you can use it to cast the *Wind Wall* spell (save DC 13). Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

When cast from the ring, the wall appears in a 10-foot Emanation originating from you, which is stationary. The wall is 15 feet high and 1 foot thick.
