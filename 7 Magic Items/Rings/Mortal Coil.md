#### Mortal Coil

Ring, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This ring is composed of hundreds of rose gold wires woven to coil around the finger. It allows its wearer's spirit to resist the pull of the afterlife.

If you die while wearing this ring, your soul enters the ring and your body remains alive and Stable at 0 Hit Points. When you regain Hit Points, your soul reenters your body and the ring turns into a useless lump of lead.
