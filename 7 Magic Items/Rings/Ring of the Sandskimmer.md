#### Ring of the Sandskimmer

Ring, Uncommon

---

This ring is made entirely of sand that ebbs and flows around your finger. The sand is magically held together by a single, perfectly smooth tiger's eye stone that spins in place as the sand brushes past it. While wearing the ring, you leave no footprints behind when you walk in sand, and you treat Difficult Terrain made of sand as if it were normal terrain.
