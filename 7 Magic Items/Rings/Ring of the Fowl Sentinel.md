#### Ring of the Fowl Sentinel

Ring, Common

---

This plain bronze ring is embossed with a rooster's head at its center. You can take a Magic action to tap the ring to the beak of a chicken or rooster that isn't Hostile toward you, causing it to have the Charmed condition for 8 hours. Until the condition ends, the target has AC 10, HP 1, a Speed of 0, a +3 bonus to Wisdom (Perception) checks, and it remains quiet. The condition ends early if the target sees another Small or larger creature it hasn't seen before. When the condition ends, the target lets out a cry, which can be heard up to 150 feet away. Only one creature can be Charmed by the ring at a time. If you take a Magic action to tap the Charmed target's beak with the ring again, the condition ends and the target doesn't let out a cry.
