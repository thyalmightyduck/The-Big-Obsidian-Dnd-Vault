#### Ring of Ice

Ring, Very Rare

---

While wearing this ring, you can use it to cast the *Wall of Ice* spell (save DC 16). Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

When cast from the ring, the wall appears in a 10-foot Emanation originating from you, which is stationary. The wall is 10 feet high and 1 foot thick.
