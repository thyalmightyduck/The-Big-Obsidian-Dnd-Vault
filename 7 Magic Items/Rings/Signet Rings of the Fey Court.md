#### Signet Rings of the Fey Court

Ring, Very Rare (Requires Attunement By an Elf or Fey Creature)

---

This set of wooden rings once belonged to a powerful Fey who ruled over a domain on the Feywild. You must be wearing all four rings in order to attune to them and use their magic. While wearing the rings, you know the *Druidcraft* cantrip. In addition, you gain the Elf's Trance trait if you don't already have it.

Each ring has an associated season: autumn, spring, summer, or winter. The four rings each contain 2 charges and regain all expended charges when you finish a Long Rest. Once a ring has no remaining charges, the small landscape adorning the top of it withers until it regains its charges.

***Season's Resistance.*** You can expend 1 of a particular ring's charges as a Reaction when you take damage of the type corresponding to that ring: spring (Radiant), summer (Fire), autumn (Necrotic), or winter (Cold). When you do, you have Resistance to that damage type until the start of your next turn, including against the triggering damage.

***Magic Resistance.*** You can expend any 2 charges from the rings as a Reaction when an enemy targets you with a spell or other magical effect. When you do, you have Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects until the start of your next turn, including against the triggering spell or effect. The 2 charges don't need to be from the same ring.
