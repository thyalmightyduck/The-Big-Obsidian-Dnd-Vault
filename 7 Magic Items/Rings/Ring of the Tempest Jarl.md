#### Ring of the Tempest Jarl

Ring, Legendary (Requires Attunement)

---

This ring is made from a twisted piece of wood charred by lightning, and it bears runes of the storm and the sea. While wearing it, you have Resistance to Cold, Lightning, and Thunder damage.

In addition, the ring has **3 charges** and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. You can expend its charges to use the properties below.

***Storm Strike.*** You can expend **2 charges** when you deal damage with a melee attack. In addition to the weapon's normal damage, the target takes 8d6 Lightning damage, and it has the Blinded condition until the start of your next turn.

***Windborne Leap.*** You can expend **1 charge** to cast the *Jump* spell on yourself.

Finally, it has the following property.

***Jarlpact Benefit.*** If an ally within **120 feet** is attuned to and wearing any other Ring of the Jarlpact, you gain Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.
