#### Ring of Gestures

Ring, Common

---

This gaudy ring has a delicately carved pink gem in the shape of a hand at its center. While wearing the ring on your hand, you can cast the *Minor Illusion* cantrip from it to alter the appearance of that hand, such as to make your hand or any of its fingers appear larger, smaller, or a different color. In addition, whenever you make a gesture with the hand wearing this ring, the pink gemstone shifts to mimic the gesture and remains that way until you make a new one.
