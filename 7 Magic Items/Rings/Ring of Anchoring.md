#### Ring of Anchoring

Ring, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

While attuned to this hammered lead ring, you can breathe underwater, and whenever you're pushed, pulled, or otherwise moved against your will, the distance you're moved is reduced by 10 feet.

***Curse.*** This ring is cursed. Attuning to it curses you until you are targeted by a *Remove Curse* spell or similar magic. The curse also ends if the finger that's wearing the ring is severed from your hand. As long as you remain cursed, you can't take off the ring, and the arm that's wearing it becomes unbearably heavy while you're underwater, giving you Disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity checks until you resurface.

Whenever you become submerged in a liquid, you must make a DC 20 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, your arm becomes immobile for 1 minute; for the duration, you have a Speed of 0, and you're dragged 30 feet downward at the start of each of your turns. On a successful save, you can move your arm, albeit sluggishly, for 1 minute; for the duration, your Speed is halved, and you have Disadvantage on attack rolls you make with the arm wearing the ring. If you're still underwater at the end of the minute, you must repeat the save. These effects immediately end once you're no longer submerged.
