#### Ring of Ice-Shielding

Ring, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This ring has **3 charges**, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. You can use a Reaction to form a **5-foot** square, **1-foot-thick** wall of ice when you are the target of an attack or are required to make a Dexterity saving throw. The wall provides Three Quarters Cover. If the attack misses or you succeed on your save, the ice wall explodes. Any enemies adjacent to it take 1d6 Piercing damage and 1d6 Cold damage.
