#### Ring of Nightmares

Ring, Rare (Requires Attunement By a Spellcaster)

---

This ring's crafting requires powdered brain from a restless lumberer. It has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can take a Bonus Action to expend 1 charge to change the damage type of the next spell you cast before the end of your turn to Psychic damage.

In addition, while attuned to the ring, you're  Immune to spells or magical abilities that interfere with your sleep or cause nightmares while you sleep.
