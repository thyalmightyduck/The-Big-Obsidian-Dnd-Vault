#### Rocborne Rod

Rod, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This wooden rod is carved in the likeness of five ancient rocs. Motes of their spirits flow through it and allow you to channel some of their lingering power.

This rod has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding the rod, you can take a Reaction while falling, or a Magic action otherwise, and expend 1 charge to cause two handles to extend from the rod's top and bottom. While holding the handles, a pair of magical wings appear above the rod, slowing your rate of descent to 10 feet per round, or 20 feet per round if you weigh more than 200 pounds. While falling in this way, you can move horizontally or downward as if you had a Fly Speed of 40 feet, but you can't move upward without a significant updraft. If you land while falling in this way, you take no damage from the fall. The wings disappear at the end of the fall, or at the end of your next fall if you summoned them as a Magic action. You can also dismiss them early (no action required).

In addition, while holding the rod aloft, you can take a Magic action and expend 1 charge to summon an instantaneous gust of strong wind. When you do, powerful winds *whip* around you, blowing things skyward in a 20-foot-radius, 60-foot-high Cylinder centered on a point within 5 feet of you. Large and smaller creatures in the area must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be launched 30 feet upward before crashing back to the ground. Objects in the area that weigh less than 200 pounds are also launched 30 feet upward by the wind. You can choose to automatically succeed on or fail the save. If you choose to fail it and immediately take a Reaction to summon the rod's magical wings, or if they were already summoned, you're launched 60 feet upward instead.
