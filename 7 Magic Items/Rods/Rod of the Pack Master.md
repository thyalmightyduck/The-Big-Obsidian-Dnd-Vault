#### Rod of the Pack Master

Rod, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This rod appears to be a piece of driftwood carved with images of wolves and bears. While holding the rod, you can take a Magic action to cast *Polymorph* on any number of willing Humanoids you can see within 30 feet of you. The only forms you can choose for each target are **Black Bear** and **Wolf**, and the targets retain their Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores. The effect lasts for 24 hours and requires no Concentration. You can take a Magic action to end the effect early for any number of targets.

Once you use the rod to cast *Polymorph*, it can't be used this way again until the next dawn.

If you have reached at least Stage 1 of the Lycanthrope Transformation, you can hold this rod in your teeth and use it while in hybrid or kindred form.
