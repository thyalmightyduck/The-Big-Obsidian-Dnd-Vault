#### Rod of Ruin

Rod, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This rod is formed of stacked crystal spheres filled with smoke. The rod has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dusk.

When a creature you can see within 60 feet of you damages you while you are holding this rod, you can take a Reaction to expend 1 charge to force the creature to make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature gains 1 level of Exhaustion. If you have the Lich Transformation, the creature has Disadvantage on its saving throw, and you gain 2d10 Temporary Hit Points when a creature fails the save.
