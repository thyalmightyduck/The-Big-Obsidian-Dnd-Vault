#### Cane of Phillip Druskenvald

Generic Variant (Requires Attunement)

Fabled Heirloom

---

This ornate cane is made from polished black wood and is topped with a silver goat-skull ornament. Even a glance at it sparks greed and envy in all but the truly humble.

While holding the cane, you can use it as a Spellcasting Focus for your spells, you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC determined by the cane's rarity.

***Fabled Heirloom.*** Each Fabled Heirloom is a magic item that grows in strength as its attuned owner gains power. The rarity of each heirloom increases as the character grows in level, as shown on the Heirloom Rarity table. An heirloom's description details the properties it always has, as well as the properties it gains as it increases in rarity. The heirloom has all the properties of its rarity and lower.

Multiple variations of this item exist, as listed below:

- *Cane of Phillip Druskenvald (Rare)*
- *Cane of Phillip Druskenvald (Very Rare)*
- *Cane of Phillip Druskenvald (Legendary)*
