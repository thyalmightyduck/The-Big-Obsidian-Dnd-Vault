#### Rod of the Sovereigns' Servant

Rod, Very Rare (Requires Attunement By a Spellcaster)

---

While holding this rod of blackened bone, you can cast the *speak with dead* spell without expending a spell slot.

Additionally, while you hold the rod, you can cast the following spells using your spell save DC: *animate dead* as a 5th-level spell, *bestow curse*, *geas*, and *raise dead*. Once the rod has been used to cast one of these four spells, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dusk.
