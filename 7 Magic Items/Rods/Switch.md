#### Switch

Rod, Very Rare (Requires Attunement By a Druid)

---

This simple, springy rod is made of twisting vines and twigs. While holding the rod, you can expend a use of your Wild Shape feature as a Bonus Action to activate it, magically transforming it into a weapon. It can take the form of any kind of Simple or Martial weapon of your choice that deals Bludgeoning or Piercing damage and lacks the Loading and Reach properties. If you transform the rod into a weapon with the Ammunition property, it magically creates its own ammunition when you make a ranged attack with it.

Regardless of the weapon's form, you're proficient with it and can use your Wisdom modifier for its attack and damage rolls instead of Strength or Dexterity. On a hit, the weapon deals an extra 1d8 damage of its normal type.

While holding the weapon, you can take a Bonus Action to transform it into another qualifying weapon or temporarily back into its rod form until you transform it into a weapon again. Alternatively, you can expend a second use of your Wild Shape feature as a Magic action to extend the bark of the activated rod or weapon to your body, covering yourself in treelike armor. When you do, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to four times your Druid level, and until the rod deactivates, you have an Armor Class of 17 if your AC is lower than that.

The rod deactivates after a number of hours equal to half your Druid level (round down), immediately reverting to its rod form if it's still a weapon. It deactivates early if you drop to 0 Hit Points or choose to deactivate it as a Bonus Action.
