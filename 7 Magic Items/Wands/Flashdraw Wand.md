#### Flashdraw Wand

Wand, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)

---

This delicate Wand hums with bound potential, and seems drawn to your fingers. While attuned to it, you cannot be Surprised. Additionally, whenever you roll Initiative you can choose to immediately draw this wand (no action required). If you do so, you can roll 1d4 and add the total to your Initiative score, and all creatures within 5 feet of you take 1 Lightning damage.
