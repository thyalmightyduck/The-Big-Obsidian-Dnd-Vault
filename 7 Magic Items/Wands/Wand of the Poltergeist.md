#### Wand of the Poltergeist

Wand, Very Rare (Requires Attunement By a Spellcaster)

---

This wand is a swirl of dark and white wood, topped with a carved screaming skull. The wand has 7 charges.

With the wand in hand, you can expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: *Animate Objects* (4 charges), *Major Image* (3 charges), or *Shatter* (2 charges).

***Regaining Charges.*** The wand regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dusk. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a d20. On a 20, the wand explodes into splinters and is destroyed.
