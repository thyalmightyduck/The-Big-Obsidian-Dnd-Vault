#### Duster

Wand, Common

---

While holding this wand, you can take a Magic action to instantly clean an area within 10 feet of you that is no larger than 1 cubic foot, removing dirt and grime. There are many variations of the Duster; the two-handed Staff of Cleansing covers a five-foot cube with a single Magic action, while the Bright Basin cleans clothing and cloth objects placed within it.
