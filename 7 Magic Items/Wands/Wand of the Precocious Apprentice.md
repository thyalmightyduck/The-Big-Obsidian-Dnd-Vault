#### Wand of the Precocious Apprentice

Wand, Uncommon

---

Once per turn when you deal damage to a creature using a cantrip while holding this wand, one target of that cantrip takes an extra 1d4 damage of the type dealt by the spell.

***Crafting.*** Creating a Wand of the Precocious Apprentice requires an Uncommon Workshop and the following Uncommon Components:

- **[Animus]** Any
- **[Bones]** Bones from an Undead
- **[Dust]** Fey Dust
- **[Fluid]** Ectoplasm
