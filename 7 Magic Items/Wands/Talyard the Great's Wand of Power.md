#### Talyard the Great's Wand of Power

Wand, Uncommon

---

This normal-looking spruce stick is actually the wand of a famous drunkard. A drunken creature that sees the stick perceives it as a magnificent, colorful wand that radiates with an awesome power.

This wand has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dusk. While you're drunk and are holding the wand, you can expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells (+3 spellcasting ability modifier) from it: *Heroism* (1 charge), *Lesser Restoration* (2 charges), or *Thaumaturgy* (0 charges).
