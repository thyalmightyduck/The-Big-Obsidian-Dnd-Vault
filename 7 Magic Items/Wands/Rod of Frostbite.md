#### Rod of Frostbite

Wand, Rare (Requires Attunement By a Spellcaster)

---

This rod is made of blackened, desiccated skin shrunk tightly around a humanoid forearm and hand. This wand has **5 charges** and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

***Withering Cold.*** While holding it, you can take a Magic action to expend **1 charge** to cause a shadowy ray to streak from one of the rod's fingertips toward a creature you can see within **60 feet** of yourself. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or for 1 minute, it takes an additional 2d6 Necrotic damage whenever it takes Cold damage. At the end of each of the target's turns, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***Cast Spell.*** You can cast *Ray of Frost* from it using your spell attack modifier. The damage scales with your level.
