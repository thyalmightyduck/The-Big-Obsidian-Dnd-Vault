#### Wand of Dancing

Wand, Rare

---

Light glistens across this shiny, black wand; the ribbons that flow from its tip twinkle and sparkle in rhythmic patterns.

You can spin and twirl holding this wand to cast the *Otto's Irresistible Dance* spell (save DC 14). The wand can be used to cast the spell as many times as you like each day, however, once a creature fails its saving throw against the spell cast by this wand, the wand cannot be used to cast the spell again until dawn the next day.
