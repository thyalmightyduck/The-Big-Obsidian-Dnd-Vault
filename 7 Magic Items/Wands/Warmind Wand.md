#### Warmind Wand

Wand, Rare (Requires Attunement By an Orc)

---

This wand is made from the shattered hilt and jawbone of a defeated warband leader. It has 7 charges and regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, the wand releases a small gasp of breath as its magic escapes it, leaving the wand as a nonmagical sword hilt.

While holding the wand, you can use it to command and bolster your allies by expending 1 or more charges to use any of the following properties.

***Forced Endurance.*** When an ally within 30 feet of you who can see or hear you is reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, you can take a Reaction and expend 2 charges to cause that creature to drop to 1 Hit Point instead. Once this property is used on a creature, that creature can't benefit from it again until it finishes a Long Rest.

***Form Ranks.*** You can expend 1 charge as a Magic action to maneuver one of your comrades into another position and bolster its resolve. When you do, choose an ally who can see or hear you. That ally gains 2d4 + 2 Temporary Hit Points and can use their Reaction to move up to half their Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

***Unyielding Critical.*** When an ally within 30 feet who can see or hear you scores a Critical Hit, you can take a Reaction and expend 1 charge to amplify the brutality of that attack. Roll 2d4 and add the total to the ally's damage roll.

***Warband Strike.*** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks and expend 1 charge to direct one of your companions to strike. When you do, choose a willing creature who can see or hear you. That creature can immediately use its Reaction to make one attack with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike.
