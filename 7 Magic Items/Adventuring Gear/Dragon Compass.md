# Where Evil Lies: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles (WEL) 
#### Dragon Compass
***Adventuring Gear***

- - -

This brass compass has been calibrated not to point north, but to point toward the magical energy produced by true dragons. When you hold the compass, its needle points in the direction of the nearest Dragon who is at least a century old and on the same plane as you. If you are a Dragon who is at least a century old, the compass doesn't point toward you while you hold it.
