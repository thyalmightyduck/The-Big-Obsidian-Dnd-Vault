#### Trollkin Unguent

Potion, Rare

---

One vial of this oil can cover one Medium or smaller creature, along with the equipment it's wearing and carrying (one additional vial is required for each size category above Medium). Applying the oil takes **10 minutes**. The affected creature then has Resistance to Fire damage for **1 hour**.

If the creature takes Fire damage, it becomes wreathed in ghostly green flames that shed Dim Light in a **15-foot** radius. While ablaze, the creature's melee weapon attacks deal an extra 1d6 Necrotic damage. After **10 minutes**, the flames die out and the oil's effects expire.

This oil is transparent with a rainbow sheen.
