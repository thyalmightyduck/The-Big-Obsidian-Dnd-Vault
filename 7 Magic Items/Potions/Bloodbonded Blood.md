#### Bloodbonded Blood

Potion

---

*This item has been created from Salvage text in the book; it may not be exactly as the author envisioned, some information is assumed.*

One vial of this blood can be harvested from a single **Bloodbonded** with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check. Drinking it within one day causes the user to be under the effect of a *Bless* spell. When the spell ends, the user gains 1 Exhaustion level.
