#### Potion of Near-Death Portent

Potion, Very Rare

---

This potion's cloudy grey fluid has a golden thread suspended in it. Drinking this potion gives you a vague vision of your potential death. The next time within **1 hour** that you would be reduced to 0 Hit Points, you can use a Reaction and roll 1d20. If the roll is 10 or higher, you take no damage. If the roll is less than 10, you are reduced to 1 Hit Point instead.
