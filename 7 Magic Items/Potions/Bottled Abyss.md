#### Bottled Abyss

Potion, Very Rare

---

This small vial has a single dose of a fuming, pitch-black poison inside it. When a creature consumes the poison, it takes 4d6 Psychic damage and must make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature has the Blinded condition and becomes unable to benefit from a Long Rest. While the affected creature sleeps, its dreams are plagued with terrible nightmares. The creature repeats the save every 24 hours. The poison lasts until the creature succeeds on a save against it or is cured by the *Greater Restoration*, Heal, or *Wish* spell.
