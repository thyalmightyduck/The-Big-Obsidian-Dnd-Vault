#### Tincture of Mourning

Potion, Rare

---

When you drink this potion, you weep with inexpressible sorrow for 1 hour. During that time, you have Immunity to the Charmed condition, but your continued wailing prevents you from being silent. If you are Charmed when you drink the potion, the condition is suppressed for the duration. This glass bottle is topped with a small crypt angel carved from stone. The fluid within has the taste and consistency of tears and the colors of a rain cloud.

***Heightened Potency.*** Creatures within 10 feet of you that are Friendly to you also gain Immunity to the Charmed condition.
