#### Potion of Dragon's Breath

Potion

---

Drinking this potion grants you a brief surge of draconic power. Once within the next minute, you can replace one of your attacks when you take the Attack action with an exhalation of magical energy, which has the same effects as the Dragonborn's Breath Weapon trait. The damage type for the exhalation is randomly determined from the Draconic Ancestors table in the Dragonborn species description when this potion is created. The amount of damage the exhalation deals and its save DC are determined by the potion's rarity, as shown in the table below.

|    Rarity   | Damage | Save DC |
|:-----------:|:------:|:-------:|
|  *Uncommon* |  2d10  |    13   |
|    *Rare*   |  3d10  |    15   |
| *Very Rare* |  4d10  |    16   |

When crafting a Potion of Dragon's Breath, you can use the harvested stomach and gizzard of a fallen dragon in place of the raw materials normally needed to craft a magic item. If you do, the exhalation's damage type is determined by the kind of dragon the materials were sourced from, and the potion's rarity is determined by the dragon's age category: Uncommon (young dragon), Rare (adult dragon), or Very Rare (ancient dragon). Once you start crafting a Potion of Dragon's Breath in this way, if you don't spend at least 2 hours working to craft it on a given day before finishing it, roll 1d20. On a 1, the harvested materials are destroyed, and the potion spoils.
