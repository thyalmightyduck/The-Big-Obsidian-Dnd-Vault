#### Ornithogalum Dragon Elixir

Potion, Rare

---

This shimmering, translucent green liquid is distilled from a living ornithogalum dragon's flowers. When you drink this potion, your Exhaustion level is reduced by one. Additionally, you can spend Hit Dice as though you had taken a Short Rest.
