#### Hunter's Zeal

Potion, Rare

---

This glowing green liquid sends a creature's immune system into a hyperactive state. When you drink this potion, you have Immunity to Poison damage and to the Poisoned condition for 1 hour. While the effect lasts, you have Advantage on saving throws to avoid becoming cursed. When the potion's effect ends, you gain 1 level of Exhaustion.
