#### Haint Hooch

Potion, Rare

---

When you drink from this glass jug, you become insubstantial for 1 minute. During this time, you can move through creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain, taking 1d10 Force damage if you end your turn inside an object.

The potion is stored in jugs labeled "XxX," with a skeletal smile drawn on it. The ghastly, blue-green liquor within has an overpowering smell and harsh taste.

***Heightened Potency.*** For the duration, you gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed.
