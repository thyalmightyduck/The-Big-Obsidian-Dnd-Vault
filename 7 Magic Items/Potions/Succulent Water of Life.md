#### Succulent Water of Life

Potion, Rare

---

This thin liquid is light green but becomes dark red when shaken. When you drink this potion, you regain 1d10 Hit Points, and for 1 minute, you regain 1d10 Hit Points at the start of each of your turns, provided you have at least 1 Hit Point.
