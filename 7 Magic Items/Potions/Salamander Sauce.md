#### Salamander Sauce

Potion, Uncommon

---

When you drink this potion, you take 2d4 Fire damage. For 1 hour, you have Resistance to Cold damage and whenever you deal damage with your Unarmed Strike, it can deal your choice of Fire damage or its normal damage type.

This potion is bright red and smells of hot peppers and spices. It is typically drizzled on food prior to eating but it is possible to consume it straight from the bottle.
