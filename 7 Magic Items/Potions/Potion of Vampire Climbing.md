#### Potion of Vampire Climbing

Potion, Minor Tier

---

*This item has been created from Salvage text in the book; it may not be exactly as the author envisioned, some information is assumed.*

Someone proficient with *Alchemist's Supplies* or an *Herbalism Kit* can powder the vampire's claws and mix them with reagents worth 50 GP. Brewing takes 3 hours. The result is a *Potion of Climbing* that also allows the drinker to climb upside down along ceilings.
