#### Inquisitor's Draught

Potion, Legendary

---

Designed to aid in the capture and interrogation of mages, this silver syrup is also an effective method of freeing oneself from a cursed item. After drinking this potion, you are incapable of attuning to magic items for 1 hour. During this duration, you can take an action to sever all your Attunements to magic items (even cursed items). At the end of this duration, if you are attuned to any magic items, you die.
