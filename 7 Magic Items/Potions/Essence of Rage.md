#### Essence of Rage

Potion, Rare

---

This swirling potion tastes like iron and bitter raisins. When you drink this potion, you enter a rage that lasts for 1 minute or until you have the Incapacitated condition. This rage grants you the same effects as the level 1 Barbarian class feature with the following changes:

- You can enter and maintain this rage even if you're wearing Heavy armor.
- If you end your turn and haven't made an attack roll against an enemy, forced an enemy to make a saving throw, or taken a Bonus Action to extend your rage, you take 1d4 Psychic damage, and the rage extends for another round.
