#### Phial of Stoutmilk

Potion, Rare

---

This thick glass bottle holds viscous, white, fizzing liquid. Tied around the bottle's neck is a worn tag that once listed the ingredients and stated dose.

For 1 hour after drinking this potion, when you take bludgeoning damage, reduce the total damage taken by 1d4. Your Strength and Constitution scores both increase by 2 until the potion's effects wear off.
