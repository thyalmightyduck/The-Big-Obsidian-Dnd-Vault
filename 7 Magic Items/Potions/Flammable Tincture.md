#### Flammable Tincture

Potion, Uncommon

---

This swirling mixture of red and white liquid is formed from the remains of a **Coldfire Ooze**. As a Magic action, the vial of Flammable Tincture can be hurled up to 30 feet at a target, which must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or be covered with the liquid. For 1 minute, the creature loses its Immunity or Resistance to Fire damage and has Vulnerability to Fire damage instead.
