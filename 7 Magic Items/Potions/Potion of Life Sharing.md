#### Potion of Life Sharing

Potion, Rare

---

This shimmering liquid changes colors constantly within its vial. Made from the acid glands of the dreaded wolfentroika, this potion allows you to share your life force with others. After drinking this potion, you can take a Bonus Action to choose a creature within 30 feet of you that you can see or hear. That creature regains 4d6 Hit Points, and you take an equal amount of Necrotic damage, which can't be reduced or prevented in any way. The effect ends after 1 minute, at which point you gain 1 level of Exhaustion.
