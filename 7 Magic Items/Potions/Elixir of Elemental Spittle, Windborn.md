#### Elixir of Elemental Spittle, Windborn

Potion, Rare

---

This foul-smelling liquid is made from the saliva of a **Windborn Troll**. For one minute after consuming the potion, you can take a Bonus Action to spit energy at a creature within 5 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 2d6 Lightning damage . No damage is inflicted on a successful save.

If the target rolls a natural 20 on its saving throw, the potion explodes inside you. You take 6d6 damage of the type listed for it, and the potion's duration ends immediately.
