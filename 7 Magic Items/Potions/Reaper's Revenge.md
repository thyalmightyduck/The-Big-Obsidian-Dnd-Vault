#### Reaper's Revenge

Potion, Rare

---

When you drink this potion, you are filled with intense and violent vigor, gaining 3d8 Temporary Hit Points. For 1 minute, when one of your enemies dies within 30 feet of you, you can take a Reaction to regain 1d8 Hit Points.

The base of this glass potion bottle is shaped like a skull, and the images of scythes run up its neck.

***Heightened Potency.*** For 1 minute after drinking this potion, when you hit a Bloodied creature with an attack roll and deal damage to it, the creature takes an extra 1d8 Necrotic damage.
