#### Honey of the Hive Warden

Potion, Very Rare

---

When you consume this honey, you have Resistance to Poison for **1 hour**. Once during the duration, you may cast *Insect Plague* (DC 15). Instead of locusts dealing Piercing damage, the spell creates bees that deal Poison damage.
