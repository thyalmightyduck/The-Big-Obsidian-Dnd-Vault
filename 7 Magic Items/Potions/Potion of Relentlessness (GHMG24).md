#### Potion of Relentlessness

Potion, Very Rare

---

This viscous potion is made from Quoxxarie blood. After drinking this potion, when you are reduced to 0 Hit Points you can expend a Hit Die (no action required) and set your Hit Points to 1. This effect lasts for 1 minute, at the end of which you must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 Exhaustion level for each Hit Die spent during the potion's duration.
