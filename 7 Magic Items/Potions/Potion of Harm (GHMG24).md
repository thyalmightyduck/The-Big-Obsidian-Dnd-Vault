#### Potion of Harm

Potion, Rare

---

This concoction is the result of a failed attempt to make a Potion of Heal from the remains of a **Downcast Vampire**. It is indistinguishable from that potion without using divination magic of level 3 or higher.

When you imbibe this potion, roll 1d4. That becomes your new Hit Point total. If the Potion of Harm is given to a creature with 0 Hit Points, they instantly suffer a Death Saving Throw failure and begin dying if they are Stable.
