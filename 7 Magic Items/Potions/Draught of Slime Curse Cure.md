#### Draught of Slime Curse Cure

Potion, Rare

---

This slightly astringent green liquid is thick and viscous, and is filled with bubbles. Drinking this potion grants you the benefits of the *remove curse* spell as though it were cast on you, as well as advantage on saving throws made to resist being cursed for 1 hour after you drink it.
