#### Potion of Rest

Potion, Rare

---

This pale blue elixir has a jasmine flower suspended within it. It is brewed using **bluebee** venom, which helps the imbiber sleep calmly and deeply.

Drinking this potion before taking a long or short rest causes the imbiber to wake with 1d10 + 5 temporary hit points. The imbiber also awakens healed of one level of exhaustion. This is in addition to what the rest would normally provide.
