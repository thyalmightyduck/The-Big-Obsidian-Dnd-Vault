#### Planchette of Adela Druskenvald

Wondrous Item, Generic Variant (Requires Attunement)

Fabled Heirloom

---

A small, heart-shaped silver pointer hangs from a silver necklace. It is inscribed with occult symbols. When placed on an open book or other assortment of letters, the air nearby feels heavy and cold.

While wearing the planchette, you can use it as a Spellcasting Focus for your spells, you gain a bonus to Intelligence and Wisdom ability checks determined by the planchette's rarity, and you can use the following properties.

***Fabled Heirloom.*** Each Fabled Heirloom is a magic item that grows in strength as its attuned owner gains power. The rarity of each heirloom increases as the character grows in level, as shown on the Heirloom Rarity table. An heirloom's description details the properties it always has, as well as the properties it gains as it increases in rarity. The heirloom has all the properties of its rarity and lower.

Multiple variations of this item exist, as listed below:

- *Planchette of Adela Druskenvald (Rare)*
- *Planchette of Adela Druskenvald (Very Rare)*
- *Planchette of Adela Druskenvald (Legendary)*
