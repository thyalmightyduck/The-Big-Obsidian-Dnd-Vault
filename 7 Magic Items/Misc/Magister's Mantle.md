#### Magister’s Mantle
*Wondrous Item ([[Robe]]), Legendary (Requires [[Attunement]] by a [[Classes/Sorcerer/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]])*

This suspends an engraved metal plate over the wearer’s chest, which bears an enchantment amplifying arcane power and protection from magical attack. 

**Magister’s Wards:** While wearing this robe, you gain a +1 bonus to your AC. This increases to +3  against spell attacks and attacks made with magical weapons
In addition, you gain a +3 bonus to [[Saving Throw|saving throws]] you make against spells.

**Overwhelming Arcanum:** When you cast [[Counterspell]] or [[Dispel Magic]], they are treated as being cast one level higher than the spell slot you expended to cast them. 
When a creature attempts to [[Counterspell]] as spell you cast, you can expend another spell slot of any level. If you do, the [[Counterspell]] treats your spell as being that many levels higher than the level at which you cast it.

##### The Talmanah
The mages of the Carlenian Empire commanded power that no mortal has even begun to approach since the empire's fall. The most powerful of these mages ruled over enclaves, floating cities held aloft by arcane power, and these mage-rulers took on the title of magister. Their titles, arcane power, and floating personal cities all served to display their sense of superiority, with magisters viewing themselves as being above all other mortals

Indeed, their rivals tended to be other magisters, or powerful mages in the upper echelons who were aiming to claim an enclave for themselves. As such, many magisters took precautions to specifically protect themselves from other mages and their machinations, trusting that their natural abilities would be enough to handle any other threat without such extra precautions. This mantle is an example of one such measure, with the wards granting protection from any magical assault, whether it be a spell or an ensnared weapon. The metal plate across the chest is engraved with symbols that amplify the magical power of the wearer, which would allow the magister wearing it to overpower opponents in contests of arcane strength, bolstering their own spells to resist attempts to unmake them, while simultaneously reinforcing the magister's own efforts to tear down opposing magic.

The narrow focus on magical opposition, and the desire to showcase power and crush opposition both showcase the mindset of these powerful mages, who were concerned above all else with adding to their own power and ensuring nobody could challenge them.
