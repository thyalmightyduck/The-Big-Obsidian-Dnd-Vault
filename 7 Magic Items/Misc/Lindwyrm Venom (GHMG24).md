#### Lindwyrm Venom

Potion, Poison (Ingested or Injury), Very Rare

---

This terrible venom is extracted and distilled from the deadly **Lindwyrm**. It can be delivered via ingestion or a wound. A creature exposed to the venom must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or take 36 (8d8) Poison damage and have the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. While Poisoned, the creature has Disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity skill checks and saving throws.
