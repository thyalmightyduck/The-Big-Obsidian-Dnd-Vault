#### Runestone of the Wild Titan

Wondrous Item, Generic Variant (Requires Attunement), 50 lb.

Fabled Heirloom

---

This mighty granite runestone is etched with complex glyphs on much of its angular surface. Though an incredible burden to carry, it thrums with the power of the wild titan. The stone weighs 50 pounds.

While holding the stone, you gain a bonus to Strength and Constitution saving throws determined by the runestone's rarity.

***Fabled Heirloom.*** Each Fabled Heirloom is a magic item that grows in strength as its attuned owner gains power. The rarity of each heirloom increases as the character grows in level, as shown on the Heirloom Rarity table. An heirloom's description details the properties it always has, as well as the properties it gains as it increases in rarity. The heirloom has all the properties of its rarity and lower.

Multiple variations of this item exist, as listed below:

- *Runestone of the Wild Titan (Rare)*
- *Runestone of the Wild Titan (Very Rare)*
- *Runestone of the Wild Titan (Legendary)*
