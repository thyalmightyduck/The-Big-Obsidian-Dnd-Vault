# thefantasticepmorium (Homebrew)
#### Belt of Dancing Flames
***Wondrous Item, Uncommon***

- - - 

**Dancing  Flames:** This belt has 6 charges and regains 1d4+2 charges each day at dawn. As a [[bonus action]], you can expend any number of charges, summoning an equal number of small *[[Dancing Flame]]*. The flames and fly and swirl around you in a 10-foot. [[Emanation]] for 1 minute or until none is left. In addition, they shed [[bright light]] within 20 feet of you, and in [[dim light]] in an additional 20 feet.

**Scorching Defense:** Whenever a creature that you can see moves or starts its turn within the [[emanation]], you can command one of your *Dancing Flames* to attack then and deal 2d6 fire damage to them (no [[action]] requires) the flame then vanishes. A creature can only take this damage once per turn.

**Blazing Projectiles:** As an [[action]], you can command these flames and hurl up them at one or more targets that you can see within 60ft. The number of flames that you can hurl at once is equal to your [[proficiency bonus]]. Make a ranges spell attack or each flame. Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisa is your spellcasting ability modifier (your choose). The flames can be controlled to fly around obstacles and creature, ignoring covers and preventing friendly fire. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 Fire damage and the flame vanishes. On a miss, the flame flies back to dance around you, but you can’t command it again for this turn.
