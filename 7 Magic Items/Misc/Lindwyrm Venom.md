#### Lindwyrm Venom

Wondrous Item, Poison, Very Rare

---

This terrible venom is extracted and distilled from the deadly lindwyrm. It can be delivered via ingestion or a wound. A creature exposed to the venom must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or take 8d8 Poison damage and have the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. While Poisoned, the creature has Disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity skill checks and saving throws.
