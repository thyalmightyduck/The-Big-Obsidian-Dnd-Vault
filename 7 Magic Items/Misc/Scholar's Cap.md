#### Scholar's Cap

Wondrous Item, Generic Variant

---

This hat carries with it the cleverness and knowledge of many previous scholars. While wearing it, you gain a bonus to Intelligence checks and Intelligence saving throws. This bonus is determined by the hat's rarity. In addition, for every 5 days you spend researching a subject, you gain knowledge as if you had been researching it for an additional number of days based on the hat's rarity: 1 day (Uncommon), 2 days (Rare), or 3 days (Very Rare).

Multiple variations of this item exist, as listed below:

- *Scholar's Cap +1*
- *Scholar's Cap +2*
- *Scholar's Cap +3*
