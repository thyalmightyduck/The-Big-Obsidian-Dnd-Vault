#### Sword of the Spelldrinker

Weapon (*Longsword*), Legendary (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 3 lb.

1d8 Slashing

Versatile (1d10)

Mastery:  Sap

---

This sword seems to absorb any light that touches it, making it appear darker than the deepest midnight. You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While holding the sword, you treat any magical light or Darkness within 30 feet of you as if it were nonmagical, and you can breathe normally in any environment.

In addition, this sword has 2 charges and regains 1d2 expended charges daily at dusk. While holding the sword, you can expend 1 charge to cast *Counterspell* from it. When cast in this way, the target automatically fails its saving throw if the triggering spell is level 5 or lower, and it automatically succeeds if the spell is level 6 or higher. If you successfully interrupt a spell in this way, you can choose to absorb it into the sword instead of allowing it to dissipate. The absorbed spell must have a casting time of an action or a Bonus Action. The sword can have only one absorbed spell at a time.

While holding the sword, you can release its absorbed spell. Doing so requires you to take a Magic action or Bonus Action (as determined by the spell's normal casting time), and it uses the slot level, spell save DC, spell attack bonus, and spellcasting ability of the original caster but is otherwise treated as if you cast the spell.

The interrupted spell remains absorbed by the sword for 8 hours or until it's released. Absorbing a new spell replaces the one stored within the sword.

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Mastery: Sap.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
