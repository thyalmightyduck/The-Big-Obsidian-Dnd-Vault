#### Prismatic Steel Weapon

Generic Variant, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This weapon is made of prismatic steel infused with the power of the Bifrost. When you hit a creature that is outside of its home plane with a melee attack using this weapon, they are banished to their home plane for 1 round. If you score a Critical Hit with this weapon against a creature that is outside its home plane, the creature is banished to its home plane indefinitely, though it can leave of its own accord.

While attuned to the weapon, you can take a Bonus Action to summon it to your empty hand, even if it is on another plane of existence.
