#### Magebane Bomb

Wondrous Item, Explosive, Rare

---

This bubbling liquid is distilled from the remains of a magebane ooze. You can throw the container at an enemy's weapon or armor, or at a spellcaster.

Make a ranged weapon attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 Acid damage. If the weapon was targeted, that weapon takes a -1 penalty to attacks and damage as well. If armor was targeted, that armor takes a -1 penalty to the Armor Class. Both effects end after the wielder or wearer completes a Long Rest.

A spellcaster hit with a Magebane Bomb has a -1 penalty to hit with spell attacks, and the DCs of saving throws against spells they cast decrease by 1 for 1 minute.
