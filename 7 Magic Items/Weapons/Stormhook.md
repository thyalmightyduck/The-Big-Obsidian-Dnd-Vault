#### Stormhook

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

When you hit a creature with a ranged attack made with this magic weapon, the target is pulled **10 feet** closer to you. If the target has a Fly Speed, that Speed is reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn.

After making a ranged attack with this weapon, it returns to your hand.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Dart* (*Stormhook Dart*)
- *Javelin* (*Stormhook Javelin*)
- *Spear* (*Stormhook Spear*)
