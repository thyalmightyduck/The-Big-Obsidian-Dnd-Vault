#### Tentacle Whip

Weapon (*Whip*), Rare, Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 3 lb.

1d4 Slashing

Finesse, Reach

Mastery:  Slow

---

This whip is made from the tentacles and guts of aberrant creatures, stitched to the webbed membranes of winged beasts and fused to the bark of a plant creature. Once the animus is placed, the tentacles begin to writhe and wriggle and the whip can latch and drag creatures along the ground with uncanny strength.

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic whip, which has a reach of 20 feet.

Once per turn when you hit a Large or smaller creature with an attack using this weapon, you can pull it 10 feet towards you.

***Crafting.*** Creating a Tentacle Whip requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:

- **[Animus]** Any Animus
- **[Hair]** Stitches
- **[Hide]** Bark
- **[Hide]** Webbed Membrane
- **[Natural Weapons]** Tentacles from an Aberration

***Finesse.*** When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

***Reach.*** A Reach weapon adds 5 feet to your reach when you attack with it, as well as when determining your reach for Opportunity Attacks with it.

***Mastery: Slow.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.
