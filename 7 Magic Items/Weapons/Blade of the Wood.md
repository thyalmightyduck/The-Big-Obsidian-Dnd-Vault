#### Blade of the Wood

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

A finely crafted sword with delicate etchings, it resonates with a faint hum of life.

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

The sword has 2 charges. You can expend a charge and speak the command word to cast *spike growth* (save DC 16) centered on yourself. You can pass through this terrain unaffected.

The sword regains one charge each day at dawn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Double-Bladed Scimitar* (*Double-Bladed Scimitar of the Wood*)
- *Greatsword* (*Greatsword of the Wood*)
- *Greatsword, Silver* (*Greatsword, Silver of the Wood*)
- *Long Sword* (*Long Sword of the Wood*)
- *Longsword* (*Longsword of the Wood*)
- *Longsword, Silver* (*Longsword, Silver of the Wood*)
- *Rapier* (*Rapier of the Wood*)
- *Rapier, Silver* (*Rapier, Silver of the Wood*)
- *Scimitar* (*Scimitar of the Wood*)
- *Scimitar, Silver* (*Scimitar, Silver of the Wood*)
- *Shortsword* (*Shortsword of the Wood*)
- *Shortsword, Silver* (*Shortsword, Silver of the Wood*)
- *Sword* (*Sword of the Wood*)
