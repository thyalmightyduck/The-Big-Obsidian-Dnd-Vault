#### Storrbani

Weapon (*Longsword*) (Requires Attunement By a 5th-Level or Higher Character), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 1 lb.

1d8 Slashing

Finesse, Versatile (1d10)

Mastery:  Sap

---

***Fabled Magic Items.*** Fabled magic items are distinct as a rarity. A fabled item grows in power alongside the character who wields it. Rules for fabled items are summarized here, and a few fabled magic items can be found in this chapter.

A fabled magic item begins as a Common magic item. The item grants a minor, useful trait to its owner that doesn't require Attunement to use.

To access any higher-level trait, a PC must attune to it using standard Attunement rules. Once attuned, a PC can use the fabled item's more powerful properties as they increase in level. If a PC higher than 1st level attunes to a fabled item, its level-dependent properties, up to the character's current level, are immediately available.

At the GM's discretion, an item's more powerful properties might become available only after the character completes a particular quest or event related to the item's story rather than immediately upon reaching each property's required level.

If a PC chooses not to attune to a fabled item, it remains a common magic item, granting only its minor property, regardless of the character's level.

Unless otherwise noted, properties granted by a fabled item are in effect as long at the character is wearing, carrying, or touching the item.

This longsword is supernaturally light and easy to use. Its weight is reduced to 1 pound, and it gains the Finesse weapon property. Sparks trail from Storrbani's blade as though it were a burning branch. When you hit a target with Storrbani, the target takes an extra 1d4 Fire damage as the blade burns upon impact.

As your level increases, you gain the following benefits while wielding or holding this weapon if you are attuned to it:

***5th Level.*** The rune Gebo appears on the blade. As a Bonus Action, you can touch the rune and cast *Shield of Faith* on a creature (besides yourself) you can see within **60 feet**. You don't need to Concentrate on the spell to maintain it. Once used in this way, you must finish a Short Rest before you can use it again.

In addition, the spirit of Thogar Giant-Hewer awakens within the longsword. It becomes a sentient, Lawful Neutral weapon with Intelligence 8, Wisdom 13, and Charisma 10. It has hearing and vision out to a range of 60 feet. It communicates telepathically with its wielder, and it can speak in a gravelly voice much like an angry growl. It can read and understand Common and Giant. Thogar's spirit is passionate but not particularly bright. It wants the wielder to confront giants and trolls, and (secretly) to fall in glorious battle against the forces of Jotunheim so that wielder and blade may feast and fight together in eternal Valhalla. It urges its wielder to seek out and destroy evil trolls and giants, and to never back down from a fight. If its wielder displays cowardice or refuses an opportunity to battle an evil troll or other giant, the spirit sulks, suppressing the magical properties of the weapon until the following dawn.

***9th Level.*** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, and the amount of Fire damage it deals increases to 2d6. In addition, the rune Tiwaz appears on the blade. As a Bonus Action, you can touch this rune and give the extra Fire damage from this blade to the weapon of a creature you can see within **60 feet**. At the end of your next turn, the Fire damage property returns to Storrbani.

***13th Level.*** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon instead of a +1 bonus. In addition, the rune Thurisaz appears on the blade. As a Bonus Action, you can touch this rune. When you do so, your attack rolls with this magic weapon score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20 until the end of your next turn. If the target is a creature of the giant type, Storrbani deals an extra 1d8 damage on a critical hit.

***17th Level.*** You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, instead of a +2 bonus. In addition, the rune Ansuz appears on the blade. As a Bonus Action, you can touch the rune and gain Immunity to all damage until the end of your next turn. Once used, the sword can't be used again in this way until the next dawn.

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Finesse.*** When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

***Mastery: Sap.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
