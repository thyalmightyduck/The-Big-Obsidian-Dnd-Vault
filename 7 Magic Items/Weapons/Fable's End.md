#### Fable's End

Weapon (*Mace*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Simple Weapon, Melee Weapon, 4 lb.

1d6 Bludgeoning

Mastery:  Sap

---

You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Whenever you roll a 20 on the d20 for an attack roll made with Fable's End against a Large or smaller creature, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw or be banished to a harmless demiplane. At the end of its next turn, the target reappears in the space it left or in the nearest unoccupied space if that space is occupied. If you make an attack roll against a Fey with this weapon, you score a Critical Hit and force the save against being banished on a roll of 19 or 20; Fey creatures have Disadvantage on this save. When a creature is banished in this way, the weapon's center emits a brilliant magenta flash of light.

***Mastery: Sap.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
