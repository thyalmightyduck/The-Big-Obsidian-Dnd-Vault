#### Returning Weapon

Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

This magic weapon grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it, and it returns to your hand immediately after you use it to make a ranged attack roll.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Dagger* (*Returning Dagger*)
- *Dagger, Silver* (*Returning Dagger, Silver*)
- *Dagger, Wooden* (*Returning Dagger, Wooden*)
- *Dart* (*Returning Dart*)
- *Dart, Silver* (*Returning Dart, Silver*)
- *Dart, Wooden* (*Returning Dart, Wooden*)
- *Handaxe* (*Returning Handaxe*)
- *Handaxe, Silver* (*Returning Handaxe, Silver*)
- *Javelin* (*Returning Javelin*)
- *Javelin, Silver* (*Returning Javelin, Silver*)
- *Javelin, Wooden* (*Returning Javelin, Wooden*)
- *Light Hammer* (*Returning Light Hammer*)
- *Spear* (*Returning Spear*)
- *Spear, Silver* (*Returning Spear, Silver*)
- *Spear, Wooden* (*Returning Spear, Wooden*)
- *Trident* (*Returning Trident*)
- *Trident, Silver* (*Returning Trident, Silver*)
- *Trident, Wooden* (*Returning Trident, Wooden*)
