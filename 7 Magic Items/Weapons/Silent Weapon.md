#### Silent Weapon

Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

This magic weapon grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. When you are hidden from a creature and miss it with a Ranged weapon attack made with this weapon, making the attack doesn't reveal your position.
