#### Vilesmog Bomb

Wondrous Item, Explosive, Uncommon

---

This smoky concoction is a favorite of poisoners and assassins. You can use a Magic action to throw this vial up to 30 feet away from you, at which point it breaks and releases a dark vapor that fills a 15-foot-radius Sphere centered on it for 2 rounds. A creature that enters the area or starts its turn there loses any Resistance or Immunity to Poison damage and has Vulnerability to Poison damage. This effect lasts for 1 minute.
