#### Blade of Retribution

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement By a Paladin)

---

The metal of this blade is forged in the dust of celestials, with a hilt wrapped in fiend skin leather. The eye of a dragon is placed upon the hilt and polished in monster blood.

When you use this magic weapon to attack a creature and roll  a 20 on the d20 for the attack roll, you can cast *Divine Smite*  as part of the same action used to make the attack without expending a spell slot.

***Crafting.*** Creating a Blade of Retribution requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:

- **[Animus]** Any Animus
- **[Dust]** Celestial Dust
- **[Fluid]** Monster Blood
- **[Hide]** Skin from a Fiend
- **[Organ]** Eye from a Dragon

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Double-Bladed Scimitar* (*Double-Bladed Scimitar of Retribution*)
- *Greatsword* (*Greatsword of Retribution*)
- *Greatsword, Silver* (*Greatsword, Silver of Retribution*)
- *Long Sword* (*Long Sword of Retribution*)
- *Longsword* (*Longsword of Retribution*)
- *Longsword, Silver* (*Longsword, Silver of Retribution*)
- *Rapier* (*Rapier of Retribution*)
- *Rapier, Silver* (*Rapier, Silver of Retribution*)
- *Scimitar* (*Scimitar of Retribution*)
- *Scimitar, Silver* (*Scimitar, Silver of Retribution*)
- *Shortsword* (*Shortsword of Retribution*)
- *Shortsword, Silver* (*Shortsword, Silver of Retribution*)
- *Sword* (*Sword of Retribution*)
