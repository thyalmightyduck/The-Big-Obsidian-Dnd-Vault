#### Resistance Breaker

Generic Variant, Rare

---

This magic weapon has 5 charges. When you hit a creature with it, you can expend 1 of the weapon's charges to negate all of the creature's Resistances to damage until the end of your next turn. The weapon regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.
