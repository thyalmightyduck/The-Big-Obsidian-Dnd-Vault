#### Memento of the Shapeless Longsword

Weapon (*Longsword*), Legendary (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 3 lb.

1d8 Slashing

Versatile (1d10)

Mastery:  Sap

---

This weapon was forged using the preserved remains of an ancient mimic. You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. As a Bonus Action, you can use a command word to magically reshape it into any kind of Melee weapon with which you're proficient that doesn't have the Heavy or Reach property.

When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can attempt to grapple the target with it (no action required), channeling the weapon's natural pseudopod form. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or have the Grappled condition. Until the grapple ends, you can't attack or grapple another creature with the weapon. Releasing the weapon ends the condition on the target.

***Pseuperpod.*** As a Magic action, you can make a melee attack roll against a creature within 30 feet of you using the weapon. The weapon stretches toward the target as a pseudopod, ignoring Half and Three-Quarters Cover, and deals 6d8 Bludgeoning damage on a hit. After the attack, the weapon retracts back into its previous form. If you attempt to grapple the target with the attack, the save DC for the Strength saving throw increases to 15. On a failed save, the target is pulled up to 15 feet closer to you before being released from the weapon's grasp. If the target failed the save by 5 or more, it is instead pulled up to 30 feet closer to you and remains Grappled. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until 12 hours have passed.

***Limb Replacement.*** If you're missing an arm or forearm, you can graft the weapon to yourself in its place. If you do, the weapon changes and reforms to match the missing limb. You can add your Proficiency Bonus to any Strength (Athletics) check you make using the limb; your Proficiency Bonus is instead doubled for such checks if you already have proficiency in the Athletics skill. You can alter the limb's form to turn it into a weapon or revert it to match the missing limb as needed (no action required).

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Mastery: Sap.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
