# [rudokstraven](https://www.instagram.com/rudokstavern) (Homebrew)
#### Scythe of Doom
***Very Rare Weapon (Glaive), Reach, Two-Handed, Vex, (Requires Attunement)***

- - -

- Critical Hits from attacks made with Scythe of Doom deal extra 10 necrotic damage, this damage cannot be reduced in any way.

**Fated Outcome:** Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack made with this magical weapon, you can use your reaction to cast a looming lethal curse upon them that lasts until the end of  your next turn. While a target is cursed by this feature, you can use these extra features against them:
- **Final Moments:** If the cursed target has 30 or fewer Hit Points, you can use your bonus action to make them die instantly
