#### Stormstruck Staff

Weapon (*Quarterstaff*), Rare, Simple Weapon, Melee Weapon, 4 lb.

1d6 Bludgeoning

Versatile (1d8)

Mastery:  Topple

---

This wooden *Quarterstaff* has been scarred by lightning, and an amplifying crystal is embedded at its head. You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, which deals an extra 1 Lightning damage on a hit.

Whenever the *Quarterstaff* takes Lightning damage or you take Lightning damage while holding it (from a source other than the *Quarterstaff* itself), the crystal glows brilliantly and pulses with electrical energy for 1 minute. It also glows in this way while under the effect of the *Shillelagh* spell. While the crystal is glowing in this way, it emits Bright Light in a 10-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 10 feet, and the extra Lightning damage the *Quarterstaff* deals on a hit increases to 1d6.

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Mastery: Topple.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus the ability modifier used to make the attack roll and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature has the Prone condition.
