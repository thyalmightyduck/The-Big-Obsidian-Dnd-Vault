#### Reap and Sew

Weapon (*Light Crossbow*), Rare (Requires Attunement), Simple Weapon, Ranged Weapon, 5 lb.

1d8 Force

Ammunition (Range 80/320 ft.; *Bolt*), Two‑Handed

Mastery:  Slow

---

You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, which deals Force damage on a hit instead of Piercing. While attuned to the weapon, you can cast the *Mending* cantrip from it.

A special, needle-like *Bolt* is magically bound to the crossbow. When fired, this *Bolt* leaves a faint thread of arcane energy behind it. The *Bolt* returns to the crossbow and loads itself after each attack made with it, allowing you to ignore the crossbow's Loading property.

The crossbow has 4 charges and regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn. When you hit a Large or smaller creature with this crossbow's special *Bolt*, you can expend 1 charge to tether the target to a point on the ground within 5 feet of it. If the target attempts to move more than 10 feet away from that point before the end of its next turn, it must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, the target's Speed is reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn.

If the special *Bolt* is somehow destroyed or lost, it reappears loaded in the crossbow after 24 hours. The weapon functions like a nonmagical crossbow while separated from the *Bolt* for longer than 1 minute.

***Curse.*** This weapon is cursed, and becoming attuned to it extends the curse to you. As long as you remain cursed, you must make a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw whenever you attempt to begin a Short or Long Rest. On a failed save, you are overcome with the urge to craft and are forced to sew, knit, crochet, or cross-stitch feverishly for at least 1 hour before being able to rest.

***Range.*** A Range weapon has a range in parentheses after the Ammunition or Thrown property. The range lists two numbers. The first is the weapon's normal range in feet, and the second is the weapon's long range. When attacking a target beyond normal range, you have Disadvantage on the attack roll. You can't attack a target beyond the long range.

***Ammunition.*** You can use a weapon that has the Ammunition property to make a ranged attack only if you have ammunition to fire from it. The type of ammunition required is specified with the weapon's range. Each attack expends one piece of ammunition. Drawing the ammunition is part of the attack (you need a free hand to load a one-handed weapon). After a fight, you can spend 1 minute to recover half the ammunition (round down) you used in the fight; the rest is lost.

***Two-Handed.*** A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

***Mastery: Slow.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.
