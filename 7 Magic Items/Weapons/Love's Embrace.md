#### Love's Embrace

Weapon (*Greatsword*), Legendary (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 6 lb.

2d6 Slashing

Heavy, Two‑Handed

Mastery:  Graze

---

You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The weapon can be attuned to by two allies together over the course of a Long Rest. If two creatures are attuned to the weapon in this way, its bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls becomes +2 instead.

This *Greatsword* is made of two long Scimitars that are held together by a powerful, indeterminate magic. You can use a command word as a Bonus Action to magically separate it into a pair of Scimitars or recombine them into the *Greatsword*. If you are not already holding the *Greatsword* or at least one of the Scimitars when you use the command word and are on the same plane of existence as it, you can choose to summon the *Greatsword* to your free hand or summon one or both Scimitars as individual blades.

The Scimitars have the Thrown property with a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet when separated. They deal Piercing damage when thrown.

Each *Scimitar* also has its own elemental effect—either cold or fire—that deals an extra 1d6 Cold or 1d6 Fire damage on a hit, respectively. The *Greatsword* instead deals an extra 1d6 Cold and 1d6 Fire damage on a hit.

The weapon can be used to cast the *Fire Shield* spell twice, and it regains all expended uses daily at dawn. An attuned creature can't cast the spell in this way more than once each day.

***Heavy.*** You have Disadvantage on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon if it's a Melee weapon and your Strength score isn't at least 13 or if it's a Ranged weapon and your Dexterity score isn't at least 13.

***Two-Handed.*** A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

***Mastery: Graze.*** If your attack roll with this weapon misses a creature, you can deal damage to that creature equal to the ability modifier you used to make the attack roll. This damage is the same type dealt by the weapon, and the damage can be increased only by increasing the ability modifier.
