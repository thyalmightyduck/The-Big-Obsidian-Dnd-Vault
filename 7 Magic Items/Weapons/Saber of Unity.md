#### Saber of Unity

Generic Variant, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This sword has been imbued with the spirit of **Gaspard's** leadership and grants whoever wields it a portion of his courage. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic sword. In addition, you and any allies within 10 feet of you are immune to being frightened while you wield it. Whenever you score a critical hit with this sword against a foe that is a size larger than you, you and all allies within 10 feet of you gain 2d10 temporary hit points.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Rapier* (*Saber of Unity Rapier*)
- *Scimitar* (*Saber of Unity Scimitar*)
