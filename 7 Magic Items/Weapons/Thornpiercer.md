#### Thornpiercer

Generic Variant, Legendary (Requires Attunement)

---

This rose has a stem large enough to hold like the hilt of a weapon. As a Bonus Action while holding it, you can cause the rose to magically grow one of the following weapons from its center: *Dagger*, *Shortsword*, *Rapier*, or *Whip*. If the hilt already has a weapon grown from its center, the existing weapon withers away and disintegrates, allowing the new one to take its place.

You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, which deals an extra 1d4 Piercing damage on a hit. While attuned to Thornpiercer, you are proficient with the weapons it grows. Each weapon option has a unique property, as detailed below.

***Dagger.*** When you throw this weapon, only the blade is thrown from the rose hilt. Hit or miss, the blade erupts into a burst of thorns after reaching its target. The target and up to three other creatures of your choice within 10 feet of it must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d4 + 2 Piercing damage.

***Rapier.*** When you roll a 20 on the d20 for an attack roll with this weapon, the blade's thorns eviscerate the inside of the target, dealing an extra 10 Piercing damage to it. If the target was a Large or smaller creature, it must also make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, its Hit Point maximum is reduced by the amount of damage it took from the attack. On a successful save, its Hit Point maximum is reduced by half that amount instead. This reduction to the target's Hit Point maximum returns to normal when it finishes a Short or Long Rest.

***Shortsword.*** The first creature hit by this weapon on each of your turns must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d6 Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.

***Whip.*** When you hit a Medium or smaller creature with this weapon, you can attempt to grapple it with the *Whip*. When you do, the target must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or have the Grappled condition. The Grappled target takes 1d4 Piercing damage from the *Whip*'s thorns at the start of each of its turns until it's freed. To escape, the target or a creature within 5 feet of it must take an action to make a DC 17 Strength (Athletics) check, freeing the Grappled creature on a success. The *Whip* breaks off from the hilt when you successfully grapple a target in this way, allowing you to move freely while the target remains Grappled.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Dagger* (*Thornpiercer Dagger*)
- *Rapier* (*Thornpiercer Rapier*)
- *Shortsword* (*Thornpiercer Shortsword*)
- *Whip* (*Thornpiercer Whip*)
