#### Couatl Herald's Fang

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement By a Creature of Good Alignment)

---

This vibrant blade carries with it some of the properties of the couatl who either forged it or the whose scales it was crafted from. You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addition, when you roll a 20 on the d20 for an attack roll made with this weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 Poison damage and must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the Incapacitated condition and a Speed of 0 until it takes any damage or someone else uses an action to shake the target out of its stupor.

***Flight of the Couatls.*** While you're attuned to three items with this property, you gain a Fly Speed of 30 feet. If you already have a Fly Speed, it increases by 30 feet instead.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Dagger* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Dagger*)
- *Double-Bladed Scimitar* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Double-Bladed Scimitar*)
- *Greatsword* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Greatsword*)
- *Greatsword, Silver* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Greatsword, Silver*)
- *Long Sword* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Long Sword*)
- *Longsword* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Longsword*)
- *Longsword, Silver* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Longsword, Silver*)
- *Rapier* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Rapier*)
- *Rapier, Silver* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Rapier, Silver*)
- *Scimitar* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Scimitar*)
- *Scimitar, Silver* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Scimitar, Silver*)
- *Shortsword* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Shortsword*)
- *Shortsword, Silver* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Shortsword, Silver*)
- *Sword* (*Couatl Herald's Fang Sword*)
