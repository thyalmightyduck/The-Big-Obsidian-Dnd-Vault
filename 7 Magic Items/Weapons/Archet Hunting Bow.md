#### Archet Hunting Bow

Generic Variant

---

When making a weapon attack roll with an Archet hunting bow, you can use your Wisdom modifier instead of your Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls. In addition, when you score a critical hit with an Archet hunting bow, you can roll one additional weapon damage die and add it to the extra damage.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Longbow* (*Archet Hunting Longbow*)
- *Shortbow* (*Archet Hunting Shortbow*)
