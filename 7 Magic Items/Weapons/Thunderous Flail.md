#### Thunderous Flail

Weapon (*Flail*), Rare (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 2 lb.

1d8 Bludgeoning

Mastery:  Sap

---

This *Flail* has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As long as the *Flail* has at least 1 charge, you deal an extra 1d6 Lightning damage to the first target you hit with it on each of your turns.

While holding the weapon, you can expend 1 charge to cast the *Thunderwave* spell (save DC 15) from the ball of the *Flail*. Alternatively, if you make a melee attack using the *Flail*, you can immediately expend 1 charge to cast *Thunderwave* as a Bonus Action in the direction of the attack's target.

The ball of the *Flail* is attached to its chain with a powerful magnetic connection that can be released at will. The *Flail* can be used to make special ranged attacks by releasing the ball mid-swing. You're proficient with these attacks, which have a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet, use your Strength modifier for their attack rolls and damage rolls, and deal 1d8 Bludgeoning damage on a hit. While the ball is within 60 feet of you, you can magically pull it up to 20 feet toward you as a Bonus Action. It bounces harmlessly off of creatures and other objects in its path. The ball magically reattaches itself to the chain when it enters your space.

While the ball is detached, you can still use the *Flail* to cast *Thunderwave*. When you do, the spell originates from the ball, affecting creatures in a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on the ball instead of the spell having its normal area of effect.

***Mastery: Sap.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
