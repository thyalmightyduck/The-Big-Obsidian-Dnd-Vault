#### Dämglesvir (Adept)

Melee Weapon (*Longsword*), Artifact (Requires Attunement)

---

Few ever saw the gleaming blade Dämglesvir before the Gods' End, as only the emperor was permitted to draw the sword from its sheath. Now its blade and hilt are black, like a weapon dipped in shadow.

***Aspirant.*** At this tier of Attunement, you have the following benefits:

- You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with Dämglesvir.
- Any weapon you wield deals an extra 1d6 Force damage.

You also gain the following curse:

- You become pallid and of ill health. You have Disadvantage on Constitution saving throws.

***Adept.*** At the Adept tier of Attunement, you have the following additional benefits:

- A creature that takes damage from the sword can't regain Hit Points until the start of your next turn.
- When you take the Attack action, you may declare one of your attacks with the sword as a sweeping strike. Instead of a single attack, make an attack with the sword against each creature of your choice within reach. You can use this sweeping strike a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all uses when you finish a Long Rest.

You also gain the following curse:

- Drawing on the power of the sword takes a physical toll on your body. When you hit a creature with the sword, your Hit Point maximum is reduced by 1. This reduction lasts until you finish a Long Rest.
