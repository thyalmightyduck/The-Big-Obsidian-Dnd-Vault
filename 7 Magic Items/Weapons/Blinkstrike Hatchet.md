#### Blindstrike Hatchet
***Weapon ([[Handaxe]]) Rare (Requires [[Attunement]])***

*1d6 Slashing damage*

*Range: 40/120*

- - -

**Blink Back:** When you make a ranged attack with this weapon, it magically returns to your hand at the end you your turn.

**Blinkstrike:** This hatchet can hold up to 4 charges. Whenever you hit a creature with a ranged attack with this weapon, it gains 1 charge. As an [[action]], you can expend 4 charges to teleport to an unoccupied space within 5ft. of a creature you can see within 40ft. of you and make a melee attack against it with this weapon. On a hit, the attack deals and additional 8d6 Force damage
