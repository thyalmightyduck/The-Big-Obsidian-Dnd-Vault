#### Couatl Herald's Reach

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement By a Creature of Good Alignment)

---

This wooden bow is carved to resemble a mighty couatl. You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bow has 4 charges and regains 1d3 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. When you make a ranged attack against a Large or smaller creature with the bow, you can choose a limb to target and expend 1 of the bow's charges as part of the attack. On a hit, the attack deals Radiant damage instead of its normal type, and the target must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the chosen limb is pinned by radiant tethers of magic, holding it to the target's body or an adjacent surface (if any) for up to 1 minute.

Depending on the pinned limb, the target may gain the Prone condition, be unable to move, or make attack rolls with Disadvantage (GM's choice) while the limb remains pinned in this way. To escape, the target or a creature within 5 feet of it must take an action to make a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check, freeing the target on a success.

***Flight of the Couatls.*** While you're attuned to three items with this property, you gain a Fly Speed of 30 feet. If you already have a Fly Speed, it increases by 30 feet instead.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Longbow* (*Couatl Herald's Reach Longbow*)
- *Shortbow* (*Couatl Herald's Reach Shortbow*)
