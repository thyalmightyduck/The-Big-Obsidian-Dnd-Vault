# [the_mithral_canvas](https://www.instagram.com/the_mithral_canvas) (Homebrew)
#### Hook Chain
***Weapon ([[Whip]]), Rare (Requires [[Attunement]])***
- - - 

An iron hook at the end of a spiked chain that seems to swing and strike with a mind of its own.

You have a +1 bonus to [[Attack Rolls|attack]] and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, which deals piercing damage instead of the normal slashing, and you cannot be disarmed of it. While attuned to this weapon, you can command it with your mind, wielding it even if you do not have a free hand. the Whip must be worn or attached to you to command it in this way.

**Pierce Flesh:** When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can choose to [[Grapple Action|Grapple]] the creature with it (using your stats for the escape DC). While a creature is [[grappled]] by this weapon, you cannot make attacks with it. A creature [[grappled]] by this weapon can use an [[action]] to automatically escape from the [[Grapple Action|Grapple]] by taking 3d6 piercing damage.

_The arsenal of those versed in pain and the deliverance of it is without limits. But sometimes, the simplest tools are best for the occasion._
