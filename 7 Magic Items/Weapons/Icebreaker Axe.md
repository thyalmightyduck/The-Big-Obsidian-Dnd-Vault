#### Icebreaker Axe

Generic Variant, Rare

---

This axe is forged from a dark grey metal and has blue-white runes inscribed on the head. This weapon deals an extra 3d8 Thunder damage to any object made of ice that it hits.

The weapon has **3 charges**. As a Magic action, you can strike the weapon against an icy surface to create a 60-foot Line of thunderous energy. Each creature in the Line must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition. Objects in the Line that are made of ice take 3d8 Thunder damage. The weapon regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Battleaxe* (*Icebreaker Battleaxe*)
- *Greataxe* (*Icebreaker Greataxe*)
