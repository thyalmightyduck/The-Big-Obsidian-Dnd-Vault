#### Wave-Swept Weapon

Generic Variant (Requires Attunement)

Evolving Item

---

This weapon is covered in oceanic motifs, from wavelike etchings on its blade to stylized sea monsters embroidered onto its leather hilt.

***Evolving Item.*** *This item is an Evolving Item, see *Evolving Magic Items* for more information.*
