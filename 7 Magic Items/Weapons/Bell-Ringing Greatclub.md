#### Bell-Ringing Greatclub

Weapon (*Greatclub*), Uncommon, Simple Weapon, Melee Weapon, 10 lb.

1d8 Bludgeoning

Two‑Handed

Mastery:  Push

---

This Greatclub is fashioned from the clapper of a giant's bell. When you score a Critical Hit with this Greatclub, you can choose for the target to have the Stunned condition until the start of your next turn instead of dealing the extra damage from the Critical Hit.

***Two-Handed.*** A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

***Mastery: Push.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from yourself if it is Large or smaller.
