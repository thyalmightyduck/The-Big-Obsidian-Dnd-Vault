#### Iced Steel Weapon

Generic Variant, Common

---

This weapon or piece of ammunition is made of iced steel, which legends hold originates in the frozen underworld of Niflheim. Objects made of iced steel always feel cold to the touch. Damage you deal with this weapon is treated as Cold damage rather than the type the weapon normally deals, if it would be advantageous to you.
