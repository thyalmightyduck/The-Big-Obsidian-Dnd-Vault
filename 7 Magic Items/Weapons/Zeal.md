#### Zeal

Melee Weapon (*Warhammer*), Artifact (Requires Attunement)

---

This warhammer glows with an inner light. When the celestial realm nearly fell, a heavenly smith received a vision, to craft a hammer like no other. With the might of Zeal, heaven's armies turned back their foes, restoring the cosmic balance.

Zeal lies lost somewhere in Etharis, waiting to be wielded against the forces of darkness. The celestials don't realize that the forces of darkness hold an equally powerful weapon, as the fates that inspired Zeal's creation sent a similar vision to an infernal weaponsmith to maintain the balance of power.

***Hammer of Heaven.*** This magic warhammer grants a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. When you hit with it, the target takes an extra 2d10 Radiant damage.

***Champion of Good.*** While holding Zeal, you have Immunity to the Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, and Stunned conditions.

***Zealous Pursuit.*** You can take a Bonus Action to toss the hammer into the air, causing it to hover magically in your space. While the weapon hovers, you can take a Bonus Action to cause it to fly up to 50 feet to another spot within 50 feet of you. As part of the same Bonus Action, you can either grasp the weapon if it is within reach, or cause it to attack one creature within 5 feet of the weapon. The weapon uses your attack roll and adds your ability modifier to the damage rolls. The weapon ceases to hover if you grasp it, if you are more than 50 feet away from it, or if you die.

***Spells.*** While the hammer is on your person, you can cast *Beacon of Hope*, *Earthquake*, or *Heroism* (save DC 18) from the weapon. Once you use Zeal to cast a spell, that spell can't be cast again from it until the next dawn.

***Destroying the Hammer.*** The only thing capable of damaging Zeal is its fiendish twin *Ardor*. A creature wielding *Ardor* can choose to attack Zeal, which has an Armor Class of 21 and 300 Hit Points. No other creature or object can damage Zeal.
