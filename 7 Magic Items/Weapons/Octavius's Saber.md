# The Griffon’s Saddlebag, Book 2 (TGS2)
#### Octavius's Saber
***Weapon ([[Rapier]]), Legendary (Requires [[Attunement]] By a [[Rogue]]), [[Martial Weapons|Martial Weapon]], [[Melee Weapons|Melee Weapon]], Major Tier, 2 lb.***

*1d8 Piercing*

*finesse*

---

The swirling guard of this powerful blade is a mesh of floral and serpentine sculpture. You gain a +3 bonus to [[Attack Rolls|attack]] and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you deal Sneak Attack damage with an attack using this weapon, you treat any 1 on a Sneak Attack damage die as an 8.

***Finesse.*** When making an attack with a finesse weapon, you use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.
