#### Shadow Blade

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This sleek magic weapon projects a shadowy double image of itself when wielded. The weapon has **3 charges** and regains 1d3 charges each day at sunset.

***Elusive Strike.*** When you miss with a melee attack made with this weapon, you can spend **1 charge** to reroll the attack roll and use the new result.

***Phantasmal Parry.*** While holding this weapon, you can spend **1 charge** to cast the *Shield* spell.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Longsword* (*Shadow Longsword*)
- *Rapier* (*Shadow Rapier*)
- *Scimitar* (*Shadow Scimitar*)
- *Shortsword* (*Shadow Shortsword*)
