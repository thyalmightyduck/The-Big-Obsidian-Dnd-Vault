#### Starbreaker

Weapon (*Morningstar*), Rare (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 4 lb.

1d8 Piercing

Mastery:  Sap

---

This glowing *Morningstar* sheds Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet while you hold it. You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, which deals an extra 1d4 Radiant damage on a hit. In addition, you can cast the *Daylight* spell using the weapon. Once the weapon has been used to cast this spell, it can't do so again until the next dawn.

***Mastery: Sap.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
