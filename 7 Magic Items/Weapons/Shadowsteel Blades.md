#### Shadowsteel Blades

Generic Variant, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

Crafted from the dark material known as Shadowsteel, these weapons seem to ignore most armor, instead slicing through the spiritual essence of living flesh while delivering excruciating pain to their victims. Survivors of an encounter with a Shadowsteel Blade often report lasting pain that lingers for hours.

Attack rolls made with this magic weapon have Advantage if the target wears Medium or Heavy Armor. A creature hit by a Shadowsteel Blade must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or have the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. Undead creatures automatically succeed on this save.

If you roll a natural 1 on the first attack roll you make with a Shadowsteel Blade during your turn, you must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or have the Stunned condition until the end of your next turn.
