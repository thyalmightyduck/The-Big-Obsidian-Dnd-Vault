#### Rage Hammer

Generic Variant, Uncommon (Requires Attunement By a Barbarian)

---

When you enter a Rage, you can make a melee attack with this weapon as part of the Bonus Action used to enter the Rage. After using this property, you can't use it again until you complete a Short Rest.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Maul* (*Rage Maul*)
- *Warhammer* (*Rage Warhammer*)
