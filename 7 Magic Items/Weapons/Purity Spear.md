#### Purity Spear

Weapon (*Spear*), Legendary (Requires Attunement By a Creature of Good Alignment), Simple Weapon, Melee Weapon, 3 lb.

1d6 Piercing

Thrown (20/60 ft.), Versatile (1d8)

Mastery:  Sap

---

This beautiful *Spear* has a long handle carved of ivory and is tipped with the spiraling horn of a unicorn. You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

While attuned to the *Spear*, you have Immunity to Poison damage and the Poisoned condition. In addition, you have Advantage on Constitution and Wisdom saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

The *Spear* has 10 charges and regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding the *Spear*, you can expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells (save DC 17) from it: *Calm Emotions* (2 charges), *Detect Evil and Good* (1 charge), *Dispel Evil and Good* (5 charges), *Greater Restoration* (5 charges), *Misty Step* (2 charges), or *Purify Food and Drink* (1 charge). In addition, you can take a Magic action to expend 1 charge to touch a creature with the *Spear*. The target regains 2d8 + 2 Hit Points, and the Poisoned condition ends on it if it had that condition.

***Charge.*** If you move at least 20 feet in a straight line toward a target immediately before hitting it with a melee attack roll using the *Spear*, the target takes an extra 2d8 Piercing damage.

***Curse Breaker.*** While attuned to the *Spear*, you are immune to being cursed by spells and other effects. If you are already affected by a curse when you attune to the *Spear* or are attuned to a cursed item, the effects of the curse are suppressed for you as long as you remain attuned to the *Spear*.

***Thrown.*** If a weapon has the Thrown property, you can throw the weapon to make a ranged attack, and you can draw that weapon as part of the attack. If the weapon is a Melee weapon, use the same ability modifier for the attack and damage rolls that you use for a melee attack with that weapon.

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Mastery: Sap.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
