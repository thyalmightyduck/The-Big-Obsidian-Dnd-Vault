#### Planar Rapier

Weapon (*Rapier*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 2 lb.

1d8 Piercing

Finesse

Mastery:  Vex

---

This thin blade seems to toe the line between planes of existence, occasionally allowing it to bend the planes to extend beyond its normal reach. You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

The *Rapier* has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. When you make a melee attack roll with the *Rapier*, you can expend 1 charge to extend the weapon, increasing its reach for the attack by 5 feet.

Alternatively, you can expend 1 charge immediately after making a melee attack roll with the *Rapier* against a target within 5 feet of you, causing the weapon to extend and pierce through the original target. Make an additional melee attack roll with the *Rapier* against a second target 5 feet behind the original one; the second target must be within 10 feet of you. This additional attack is made as part of the initial attack, and the second target doesn't benefit from using the original one as cover.

Whenever you expend a charge from this weapon, the planes around the extended blade begin to tear, causing the target to take an extra 2d8 Force damage on a hit.

After the attack with the extended blade, it returns to its normal size.

***Finesse.*** When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

***Mastery: Vex.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
