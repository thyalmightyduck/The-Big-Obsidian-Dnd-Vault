#### Forgemaster's Might

Generic Variant, Legendary (Requires Attunement)

---

This monumental hammer requires a Strength of 17 or higher to wield. You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

You can take a Bonus Action to use the command words, "light the forge," and cause flames to magically curl up and heat the hammer's anvil head. These flames shed Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet. While the hammer is lit, it deals an extra 1d8 Fire damage on a hit and ignores Resistance to Fire damage. The flames last until you take a Bonus Action to use the command word, "quench," or until you drop or stow the hammer.

***Smith's Blessing.*** You can use the hammer to cast either the *Creation* (level 6 version) or *Fabricate* spell as a Magic action instead of its normal casting time. When you cast *Creation* in this way, you can use its conjured materials as raw materials for the *Fabricate* spell, although the materials still have a limited duration. Once the hammer has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn.

In addition, you know the *Mending* cantrip while you're attuned to the hammer. When you cast it to repair metal, the break or tear can be up to 5 feet in any dimension instead of 1 foot. You have Advantage on ability checks using *Smith's Tools* to craft or repair an object when you use the hammer's head as the anvil.

***Iron Raze.*** The hammer deals an extra 2d8 Bludgeoning damage to Constructs and structures on a hit.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Maul* (*Forgemaster's Maul*)
- *Warhammer* (*Forgemaster's Warhammer*)
