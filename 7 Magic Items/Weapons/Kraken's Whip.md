#### Kraken's Whip

Weapon (*Whip*), Uncommon, Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 3 lb.

1d4 Slashing

Finesse, Reach

Mastery:  Slow

---

This *Whip* is made using the shrunken tentacle of a great sea creature. It still writhes slightly on its own. As a Magic action, you can use the *Whip* to force a Large or smaller creature you can see within 10 feet of you to make a DC 13 Strength or Dexterity saving throw (its choice).

On a failed save, the target has the Grappled condition and takes 1d4 + 2 Bludgeoning damage.

To escape, the target or a creature within 5 feet of it must take an action to make a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check, freeing the Grappled creature on a success. While Grappled, the creature takes 1d4 + 2 Bludgeoning damage at the end of each of its turns.

You can't use the *Whip* to attack while grappling a creature in this way. While holding the *Whip*, you can end the grapple at any time (no action required), and if you move more than 10 feet away from the Grappled creature, you drag it with you, but every foot of movement costs you 1 extra foot unless the creature is Tiny or two or more sizes smaller than you.

***Finesse.*** When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

***Reach.*** A Reach weapon adds 5 feet to your reach when you attack with it, as well as when determining your reach for Opportunity Attacks with it.

***Mastery: Slow.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.
