#### Blade of Insults

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement By a Bard)

---

A bard wielding this weapon is empowered to mix swordplay with witty repartee. If you deal damage with this weapon on your turn, you can use your Bonus Action to cast *Vicious Mockery* at the same creature you damaged. If you wish, as part of that Bonus Action, you can also expend a use of your Bardic Inspiration, which otherwise functions as it normally would.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Dagger* (*Dagger of Insults*)
- *Sickle* (*Sickle of Insults*)
