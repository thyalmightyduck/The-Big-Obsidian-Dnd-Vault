#### Titan's Tooth Claymore

Weapon (*Greatsword*), Very Rare, Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, Major Tier, 6 lb.

2d6 Slashing

heavy, two‑handed

---

This massive two-handed blade is made from the lost or harvested tooth of a tarrasque. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. If it hits an object, the hit is automatically a critical hit. In addition, if you roll a 20 on an attack roll using this weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 slashing damage.

***Heavy.*** Creatures that are Small or Tiny have disadvantage on attack rolls with heavy weapons. A heavy weapon's size and bulk make it too large for a Small or Tiny creature to use effectively.

***Two-Handed.*** This weapon requires two hands to use. This property is relevant only when you attack with the weapon, not when you simply hold it.
