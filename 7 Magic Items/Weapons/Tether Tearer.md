#### Tether Tearer

Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

This silver axe can temporarily bind a creature to its corporeal form. When you hit a creature that has the Incorporeal Movement trait using the axe, the target takes an extra 1d6 Force damage, and it can't benefit from its Incorporeal Movement trait again until the end of its next turn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Axe* (*Tether Tearer Axe*)
- *Battle Axe* (*Tether Tearer Battle Axe*)
- *Battleaxe* (*Tether Tearer Battleaxe*)
- *Battleaxe, Silver* (*Tether Tearer Battleaxe, Silver*)
- *Greataxe* (*Tether Tearer Greataxe*)
- *Greataxe, Silver* (*Tether Tearer Greataxe, Silver*)
- *Handaxe* (*Tether Tearer Handaxe*)
- *Handaxe, Silver* (*Tether Tearer Handaxe, Silver*)
