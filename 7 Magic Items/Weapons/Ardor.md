#### Ardor

Weapon (*Battleaxe*), Artifact (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 4 lb.

1d8 Slashing

Versatile (1d10)

Mastery:  Topple

---

The blades of this axe smolder with an inner light, and the dark wooden handle is accented with silver. After the celestial realm turned back yet another assault from the forces of evil, a fiendish forge master received a vision, allowing them to craft a weapon like no other. Ardor now lies in a hidden vault, waiting to be wielded by a scion of darkness who strikes on behalf of the forces of the Arch Daemons.

The fiends don't realize that an equally powerful weapon rests in the hands of the celestials, who wage eternal war against them. In the interest of maintaining order, the fates who inspired the forgemaster to craft Ardor also sent a similar vision to a smith of heaven.

***Axe of Hell.*** This magic battleaxe grants a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. When you hit with it, the target takes an extra 2d10 Necrotic damage.

***Champion of Evil.*** While holding the battleaxe, you have Immunity to the Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, and Stunned conditions.

***Ardor's Pursuit. Ardor's Pursuit:.*** You can take a Bonus Action to toss the axe into the air, causing it to hover magically in your space. While the weapon hovers, you can take a Bonus Action to cause it to fly up to 50 feet to another spot within 50 feet of you. As part of the same Bonus Action, you can either grasp the weapon if it is within reach, or cause it to attack one creature within 5 feet of the weapon. The weapon uses your attack roll and adds your ability modifier to the damage rolls. The weapon ceases to hover if you grasp it, if you are more than 50 feet away from it, or if you die.

***Spells.*** While the axe is on your person, you can use a Magic action to cast one of the following spells (save DC 18): *Bane*, *Bestow Curse*, or *Incendiary Cloud*. Once you use the axe to cast a spell, that spell can't be cast again from it until the next dawn.

***Destroying the Axe.*** The only thing capable of damaging the battleaxe is its celestial twin, *Zeal*. A creature wielding *Zeal* can choose to attack Ardor, which has an Armor Class of 21 and 300 HP. No other creature or object can damage Ardor.

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Mastery: Topple.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus the ability modifier used to make the attack roll and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature has the Prone condition.
