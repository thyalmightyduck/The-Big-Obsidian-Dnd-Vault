#### Scorching Cleaver

Weapon (*Battleaxe*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 4 lb.

1d8 Slashing

Versatile (1d10)

Mastery:  Topple

---

You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This *Battleaxe* has 8 charges and regains 1d6 + 2 expended charges daily at dawn. As long as the weapon has at least 1 charge, attacks you make with it deal an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit. When you roll a 20 on the d20 for an attack roll made with this weapon, it regains 3 expended charges.

***Erupting Slash.*** As a Magic action, you can expend 3 or more charges to slash the air in front you with the *Battleaxe*. When you do, a 5-foot-wide, 30-foot-long Line of fire erupts from the blade. Creatures in the Line must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 Fire damage for each expended charge. The DC for the save is equal to 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus and the number of charges you expended. If you expend 4+ charges in this way and leave the *Battleaxe* with 0 remaining charges, the weapon overheats. When it overheats, this effect deals an extra 3d6 Fire damage, and creatures that succeed on the save take half as much damage from the effect.

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Mastery: Topple.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus the ability modifier used to make the attack roll and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature has the Prone condition.
