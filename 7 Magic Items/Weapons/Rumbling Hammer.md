#### Rumbling Hammer

Generic Variant, Common (Requires Attunement)

---

Immediately after you score a Critical Hit with this hammer, it casts *Thunderclap*, using your level to set the cantrip's damage. Your allies have Immunity to the damage dealt by this casting of the spell.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Maul* (*Rumbling Maul*)
- *Warhammer* (*Rumbling Warhammer*)
