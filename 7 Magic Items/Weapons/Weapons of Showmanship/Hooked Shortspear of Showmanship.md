#### Hooked Shortspear of Showmanship

Weapon (*Hooked Shortspear*), Common, Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 2 lb.

1d4 Piercing

light

---

*The *base item* can be found in Out of the Abyss, page 224.*

When you reduce a Small or larger creature to 0 Hit Points or score a Critical Hit against a target with this weapon, you can create a harmless sensory effect or illusion, as if by the *Prestidigitation* spell, as part of that attack. The effect can appear on the target of the attack or in the area immediately surrounding it.

On a hit with this weapon, the wielder can forgo dealing damage and attempt to trip the target, in which case the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or fall prone. The DC is 8 + the wielder's Strength modifier + the wielder's proficiency bonus.

***Light.*** A light weapon is small and easy to handle, making it ideal for use when fighting with two weapons.
