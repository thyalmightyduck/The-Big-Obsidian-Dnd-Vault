#### Double-Bladed Scimitar of Showmanship

Weapon (*Double-Bladed Scimitar*), Common, Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 6 lb.

2d4 Slashing

two‑handed, special

---

*The *base item* can be found in Eberron: Rising from the Last War, page 21.*

When you reduce a Small or larger creature to 0 Hit Points or score a Critical Hit against a target with this weapon, you can create a harmless sensory effect or illusion, as if by the *Prestidigitation* spell, as part of that attack. The effect can appear on the target of the attack or in the area immediately surrounding it.

***Special.*** If you attack with a double-bladed scimitar as part of the Attack action on your turn, you can use a bonus action immediately after to make a melee attack with it. This attack deals 1d4 slashing damage on a hit, instead of 2d4.

***Two-Handed.*** This weapon requires two hands to use. This property is relevant only when you attack with the weapon, not when you simply hold it.
