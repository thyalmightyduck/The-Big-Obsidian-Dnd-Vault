#### Bearded Axe of Showmanship

Weapon (*Bearded Axe*), Common, Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 6 lb.

1d10 Slashing

Heavy, Versatile (1d12)

Mastery:  Cleave

---

*The *base item* can be found in Northlands Worldbook.*

When you reduce a Small or larger creature to 0 Hit Points or score a Critical Hit against a target with this weapon, you can create a harmless sensory effect or illusion, as if by the *Prestidigitation* spell, as part of that attack. The effect can appear on the target of the attack or in the area immediately surrounding it.

The hook-shaped "beard" at the lower edge of the head of a bearded axe gives it its characteristic silhouette and provides a larger cutting edge than standard battle axes without excessively increasing its weight.

***Heavy.*** You have Disadvantage on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon if it's a Melee weapon and your Strength score isn't at least 13 or if it's a Ranged weapon and your Dexterity score isn't at least 13.

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Mastery: Cleave.*** If you hit a creature with a melee attack roll using this weapon, you can make a melee attack roll with the weapon against a second creature within 5 feet of the first that is also within your reach. On a hit, the second creature takes the weapon's damage, but don't add your ability modifier to that damage unless that modifier is negative. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.
