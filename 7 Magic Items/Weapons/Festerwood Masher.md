#### Festerwood Masher

Generic Variant, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This festerwood hammer has amber pustules on its end that have enriched the wood with its corrosive properties. You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

In addition, this hammer has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dusk. When you hit a target with the hammer, you can expend 1 charge to magically release some of the corrosive acid onto it. If the target is Large or smaller, it takes a cumulative -1 penalty to AC, which lasts for 1 minute. If the target is Huge or Gargantuan, it takes this penalty only after you release the acid onto it for a second or third time, respectively, within 1 minute. When an affected target is hit by the hammer, it takes an extra 1d4 Acid damage for each -1 penalty to AC it has in this way.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Maul* (*Festerwood Maul*)
- *Warhammer* (*Festerwood Warhammer*)
