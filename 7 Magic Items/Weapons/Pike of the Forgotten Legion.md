#### Pike of the Forgotten Legion

Weapon (*Pike*), Legendary (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 18 lb.

1d10 Piercing

Heavy, Reach, Two‑Handed

Mastery:  Push

---

You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The weapon has 6 charges and regains 1d4 + 2 expended charges daily at dusk. While holding this *Pike*, you can expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells (save DC 17) from it: *Mirror Image* (1 charge), *Phantom Steed* (1 charge), *Speak with Dead* (1 charge), or *Spirit Guardians* (2 charges).

***Spectral Warrior.*** Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with this *Pike*, you can choose to summon a spectral warrior from it. Until the start of your next turn, the target is haunted by this warrior, causing it to have Disadvantage on attack rolls against you. This property has no effect against Undead creatures.

***Haunting Phalanx.*** You can take a Magic action while holding this *Pike* to expend 3 charges and point it forward commandingly. Five spectral, *Pike*-wielding warriors appear before you and march in a 25-foot-wide, 50-foot-long Line in a direction you choose. Any creature within the area must make a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw; Undead creatures automatically succeed on the save. On a failed save, a target takes 3d10 Necrotic damage and 3d10 Psychic damage and is affected by the weapon's Spectral Warrior property. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

***Heavy.*** You have Disadvantage on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon if it's a Melee weapon and your Strength score isn't at least 13 or if it's a Ranged weapon and your Dexterity score isn't at least 13.

***Reach.*** A Reach weapon adds 5 feet to your reach when you attack with it, as well as when determining your reach for Opportunity Attacks with it.

***Two-Handed.*** A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

***Mastery: Push.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from yourself if it is Large or smaller.
