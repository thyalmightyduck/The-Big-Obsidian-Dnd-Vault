#### Fey Blade

Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

This weapon is lightweight and portable and can easily fit into a dagger sheath, even if a two-handed weapon or polearm. You can draw it from the sheath or replace it as a bonus action.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Axe* (*Fey Axe*)
- *Battle Axe* (*Fey Battle Axe*)
- *Battleaxe* (*Fey Battleaxe*)
