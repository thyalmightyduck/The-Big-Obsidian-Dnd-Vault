#### The Rose Basket

Weapon (*Rapier*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 2 lb.

1d8 Piercing

Finesse

Mastery:  Vex

---

This silver *Rapier* has a rose adorning its crossguard and pommel and has thorns lining its hilt. You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. If you make an attack with the weapon against a Fey or a creature that is shape-shifted, the target takes an extra 1d8 Radiant damage on a hit.

***Field of Roses.*** As a Magic action, you can use a command word and touch the *Rapier*'s point to the ground at your feet. When you do, silver rosebushes sprout from the ground in a 25-foot square centered on your location. The area becomes Difficult Terrain until the rosebushes disappear. When a creature moves into or within the area, it takes 2d4 Piercing damage for every 5 feet it travels. While holding the *Rapier*, this Difficult Terrain doesn't cost you extra movement, and you don't take damage from moving into or within the area. The rosebushes disappear after 1 minute or when you dismiss them as a Bonus Action. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

***Finesse.*** When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

***Mastery: Vex.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
