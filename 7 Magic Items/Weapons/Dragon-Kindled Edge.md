#### Dragon-Kindled Edge

Weapon (*Longsword*), Legendary (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 3 lb.

1d8 Slashing

Versatile (1d10)

Mastery:  Sap

---

This magnificent blade is crafted from the remains of an ancient dragon. You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The weapon deals an extra 2d6 damage on a hit if the target is a Dragon. In addition, while you're attuned to this weapon, you know Draconic.

***Kindled Cleave.*** When you're affected by a Hostile dragon's breath weapon, you can take a Reaction to hold the blade in front of you. When you do, the sword splits the breath weapon's area of effect, leaving you and any creature in a 30-foot Cone behind you unaffected by it. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

When you use this property to split a breath weapon that deals damage, the sword gives off an elemental haze corresponding to the dragon's breath weapon. The haze lasts for 1 minute, and it sheds Bright Light in a 10-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 10 feet. The first target you hit with an attack roll using the sword while it has this haze takes an extra 6d6 damage of the breath weapon's damage type. This extra damage ignores Resistance and Immunity.

***Lesser Draconic Resistance.*** If you fail a saving throw by 4 or less, you can choose to succeed instead. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Mastery: Sap.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
