#### Prismatic Javelin

Weapon (*Javelin*), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Simple Weapon, Melee Weapon, 2 lb.

1d6 Piercing

Thrown (30/120 ft.)

Mastery:  Slow

---

This dark crystal *Javelin* has a prismatic head that can refract light into beautiful displays of color. Immediately after you hit a target with a ranged attack roll using the *Javelin*, it disappears in a small burst of light before reappearing in your open hand.

As a Magic action, you can throw the *Javelin* at a point you can see within 120 feet of you and use a command word. The *Javelin* erupts at that point in a dazzling display of color and light, casting painful Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet. Each creature of your choice within the Bright Light that isn't behind Total Cover must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw. For each target, roll 1d6 to determine which beam of light affects it, consulting the table below. On a failed save, a target takes 2d10 damage of the type corresponding to the beam's color for each beam of light it is struck by. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

| 1d6 |  Color  |                                 Damage Type                                |
|:---:|:-------:|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------:|
|  1  |   Red   |                                    Fire                                    |
|  2  |  Orange |                                    Acid                                    |
|  3  |  Yellow |                                  Lightning                                 |
|  4  |  Green  |                                   Poison                                   |
|  5  |   Blue  |                                    Cold                                    |
|  6  | Special | The creature is struck by two beams of light. Roll twice, rerolling any 6. |

***Thrown.*** If a weapon has the Thrown property, you can throw the weapon to make a ranged attack, and you can draw that weapon as part of the attack. If the weapon is a Melee weapon, use the same ability modifier for the attack and damage rolls that you use for a melee attack with that weapon.

***Mastery: Slow.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.
