#### Tooth of the Jinxed Leviathan

Weapon (*Dagger*), Generic Variant (Requires Attunement), Simple Weapon, Melee Weapon, 1 lb.

Fabled Heirloom

---

This tooth is razor sharp and can be wielded as a Dagger. Its "hilt" is wrapped in alligator hide, and the flat of its blade flickers with magenta hex magic in carved glyphs.

You gain a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon determined by its rarity. While holding the tooth, you can use it as a Spellcasting Focus for your spells.

***Fabled Heirloom.*** Each Fabled Heirloom is a magic item that grows in strength as its attuned owner gains power. The rarity of each heirloom increases as the character grows in level, as shown on the Heirloom Rarity table. An heirloom's description details the properties it always has, as well as the properties it gains as it increases in rarity. The heirloom has all the properties of its rarity and lower.

Multiple variations of this item exist, as listed below:

- *Tooth of the Jinxed Leviathan (Rare)*
- *Tooth of the Jinxed Leviathan (Very Rare)*
- *Tooth of the Jinxed Leviathan (Legendary)*
