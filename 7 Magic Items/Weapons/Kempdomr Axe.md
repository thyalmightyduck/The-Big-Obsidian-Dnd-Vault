#### Kempdomr Axe

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This axe channels the might of the jotun. You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

This weapon has **5 charges**. When you hit an enemy with a melee attack using this weapon, you can expend **1 charge** to deal an extra 3d8 Force damage and push the target **10 feet** away from you for every size category it is smaller than Gargantuan (Gargantuan creatures are not pushed). The weapon regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

***Giant's Boon.*** While you are attuned to the weapon and wearing either a *Belt of Giant Strength* or *Gauntlets of Ogre Power* to which you are also attuned, the weapon regains all expended charges daily at dawn. In addition, the bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +2, and the extra Force damage you deal when you expend a charge increases to 4d8.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Battleaxe* (*Kempdomr Battleaxe*)
- *Bearded Axe* (*Kempdomr Bearded Axe*)
- *Breidox* (*Kempdomr Breidox*)
- *Greataxe* (*Kempdomr Greataxe*)
