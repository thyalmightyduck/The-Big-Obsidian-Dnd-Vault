#### Crypt Keeper Glaive

Weapon (*Glaive*), Legendary (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 6 lb.

1d10 Slashing

Heavy, Reach, Two‑Handed

Mastery:  Graze

---

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Whenever you reduce a Small or larger creature that is Hostile toward you to 0 Hit Points with the *Glaive*, you gain 1d10 Temporary Hit Points.

In addition, the *Glaive*'s blade begins to glow with a sickly green haze whenever you gain Temporary Hit Points in this way. While glowing, the next time you hit a target with a melee attack using the *Glaive*, the target takes extra Necrotic damage equal to the number of Temporary Hit Points you gained. Once you deal this extra damage, the haze fades. The haze and any remaining Temporary Hit Points are lost after 1 minute.

Undead creatures killed by this weapon can't be raised from the dead again.

***Six Feet Under.*** The first time on each of your turns that you hit a target with the Prone condition using this weapon, the weapon deals maximum damage.

***Heavy.*** You have Disadvantage on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon if it's a Melee weapon and your Strength score isn't at least 13 or if it's a Ranged weapon and your Dexterity score isn't at least 13.

***Reach.*** A Reach weapon adds 5 feet to your reach when you attack with it, as well as when determining your reach for Opportunity Attacks with it.

***Two-Handed.*** A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

***Mastery: Graze.*** If your attack roll with this weapon misses a creature, you can deal damage to that creature equal to the ability modifier you used to make the attack roll. This damage is the same type dealt by the weapon, and the damage can be increased only by increasing the ability modifier.
