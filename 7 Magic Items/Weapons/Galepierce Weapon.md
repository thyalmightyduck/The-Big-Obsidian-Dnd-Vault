#### Galepierce Weapon

Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

These magic weapons have been tempered to glide through the wind, doubling the normal and long ranges of their Thrown property. Ranged attack rolls made with these weapons ignore air-based hindrances, such as fog and high winds, allowing them to be made without the normal Disadvantage or not automatically miss as a result of those obstructions.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Dagger* (*Galepierce Dagger*)
- *Dagger, Silver* (*Galepierce Dagger, Silver*)
- *Dagger, Wooden* (*Galepierce Dagger, Wooden*)
- *Dart* (*Galepierce Dart*)
- *Dart, Silver* (*Galepierce Dart, Silver*)
- *Dart, Wooden* (*Galepierce Dart, Wooden*)
- *Javelin* (*Galepierce Javelin*)
- *Javelin, Silver* (*Galepierce Javelin, Silver*)
- *Javelin, Wooden* (*Galepierce Javelin, Wooden*)
- *Parrying Dagger* (*Galepierce Parrying Dagger*)
- *Spear* (*Galepierce Spear*)
- *Spear, Silver* (*Galepierce Spear, Silver*)
- *Spear, Wooden* (*Galepierce Spear, Wooden*)
- *Trident* (*Galepierce Trident*)
