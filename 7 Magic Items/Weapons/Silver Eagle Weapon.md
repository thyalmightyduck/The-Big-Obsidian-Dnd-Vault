#### Silver Eagle Weapon

Generic Variant, Uncommon

2d6

---

These silvered weapons are of elven make. You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with these magic weapons, which deal 2d6 damage on a hit instead of their normal damage dice. Scoring a Critical Hit with the weapon doesn't allow you to roll the attack's damage dice twice. Instead, whenever you hit a target and roll the same number on both of the weapon's damage dice, you can immediately roll 1d6 and add it to the weapon's damage dice. You continue rolling additional damage dice until you roll a number that doesn't match the previous rolls.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Greataxe* (*Silver Eagle Greataxe*)
- *Greatsword* (*Silver Eagle Greatsword*)
- *Maul* (*Silver Eagle Maul*)
- *Musket* (*Silver Eagle Musket*)
