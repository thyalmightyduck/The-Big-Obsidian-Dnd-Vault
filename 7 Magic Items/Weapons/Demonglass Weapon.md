#### Demonglass Weapon

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

A demonglass shard is inlaid into this weapon, granting it magical properties. Though the fiendish presence embedded within cannot speak directly to its wielder, its presence is keenly felt by those whose blood it spills.

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. On a Critical Hit, the target is Frightened of you until the end of its next turn, and you gain Advantage on saving throws until the end of your next turn.
