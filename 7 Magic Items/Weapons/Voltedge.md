#### Voltedge

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This split metal sword arcs with electricity between its two halves. This magic weapon deals an extra 1d6 Lightning damage on a hit. While holding the sword, you can take a Magic action to unleash a concentrated beam of lightning from the blade in a Line that is 60 feet long and 5 feet wide. Each creature in the Line must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 Lightning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Double-Bladed Scimitar* (*Voltedge Double-Bladed Scimitar*)
- *Greatsword* (*Voltedge Greatsword*)
- *Greatsword, Silver* (*Voltedge Greatsword, Silver*)
- *Long Sword* (*Voltedge Long Sword*)
- *Longsword* (*Voltedge Longsword*)
- *Longsword, Silver* (*Voltedge Longsword, Silver*)
- *Rapier* (*Voltedge Rapier*)
- *Rapier, Silver* (*Voltedge Rapier, Silver*)
- *Scimitar* (*Voltedge Scimitar*)
- *Scimitar, Silver* (*Voltedge Scimitar, Silver*)
- *Shortsword* (*Voltedge Shortsword*)
- *Shortsword, Silver* (*Voltedge Shortsword, Silver*)
- *Sword* (*Voltedge Sword*)
