#### Keen Weapon

Generic Variant

---

Sharp or better-balanced, this weapon is now more likely to produce a critical hit when hitting its target. Attacks using this weapon score a critical hit on a roll of 19–20.

> ##### Keen Weapons Using The One Ring Dice
>
>If you are using a set of *The One Ring* Success Dice, a Keen weapon scores a critical hit also when the dice show two or more 5 results, or three 5 results if you have disadvantage on the roll. A *Superior Keen weapon* additionally scores a critical hit against bane creatures when the dice show two or more 4 results, or three 4 results if you have disadvantage.
>
