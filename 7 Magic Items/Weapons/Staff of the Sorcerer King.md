#### Staff of the Sorcerer King

Staff, Melee Weapon (*Spear*), Legendary (Requires Attunement)

---

This ebony staff bears a bronze spear tip and additional blades carved to depict a winged scarab. The staff can be wielded as a magic spear that grants a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. You are considered proficient with this weapon while you are attuned to it, even if you aren't proficient with spears. While holding the staff, you gain these benefits:

- **Spell Power.** You gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls.
- **Cull Subjects.** Humanoids have a -2 penalty to saving throws against your spells.
- **Spell Shield.** You have Advantage on saving throws against spells that target only you.

***Cursed.*** This staff is cursed, and becoming attuned to it extends the curse to you. Until the curse is broken with *Remove Curse* or similar magic, you are unwilling to part with the staff, keeping it within reach at all times. In addition, you have Disadvantage on attack rolls made with weapons other than this one.

While cursed, you must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw each day at midnight. On a failed save, your Hit Point maximum is reduced by 10 unless you are Undead. Your Hit Point maximum can only be restored by a *Wish* spell. If your Hit Point maximum is reduced to 0 while cursed, you die and immediately rise as a **Wight**.
