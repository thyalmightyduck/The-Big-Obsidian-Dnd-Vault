#### Ashbraid

Generic Variant, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This axe has a fire-blackened blade, and its haft is wrapped with strands of unbreakable golden hair. You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. The weapon deals an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit.

When you hit a creature with an attack made with this magic weapon, the target has Vulnerability to Fire damage until the beginning of your next turn. If the target has Resistance to Fire damage, it loses that until the beginning of your next turn, though it does not have Vulnerability to Fire damage. This has no effect on creatures with Immunity to Fire damage.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Battleaxe* (*Ashbraid Battleaxe*)
- *Greataxe* (*Ashbraid Greataxe*)
