#### Dispelling Ammunition

Ammunition, Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

When a target takes damage from this magical piece of ammunition, any ongoing spell of level 3 or lower on the target ends. Once it deals damage to a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Arrow* (*Dispelling Arrow*)
- *Arrow, Silver* (*Dispelling Arrow, Silver*)
- *Arrow, Wooden* (*Dispelling Arrow, Wooden*)
- *Bellows Cannister* (*Dispelling Bellows Cannister*)
- *Bolt* (*Dispelling Bolt*)
- *Bolt, Silver* (*Dispelling Bolt, Silver*)
- *Bolt, Wooden* (*Dispelling Bolt, Wooden*)
- *Bullet* (*Dispelling Bullet*)
- *Bullet, Silver* (*Dispelling Bullet, Silver*)
- *Bullet, Wooden* (*Dispelling Bullet, Wooden*)
- *Cannonball* (*Dispelling Cannonball*)
- *Cannonball* (*Dispelling Cannonball*)
- *Dragon Shot* (*Dispelling Dragon Shot*)
- *Energy Cell* (*Dispelling Energy Cell*)
- *Explosive Cannonball* (*Dispelling Explosive Cannonball*)
- *Firearm Bullet* (*Dispelling Firearm Bullet*)
- *Flare* (*Dispelling Flare*)
- *Needle* (*Dispelling Needle*)
- *Needle, Silver* (*Dispelling Needle, Silver*)
- *Paper Cartridge Bullet* (*Dispelling Paper Cartridge Bullet*)
- *Shell* (*Dispelling Shell*)
- *Shot* (*Dispelling Shot*)
