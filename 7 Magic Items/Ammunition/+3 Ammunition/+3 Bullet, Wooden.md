# Dungeon Master’s Guide 2024 (DMG24)
#### +3 Bullet, Wooden
***Ammunition ([[Bullet, Wooden]]), Very Rare, 1.6 oz.***

---

*The *base item* can be found in Vampire: The Masquerade - Bound by Blood.*

You have a +3 bonus to [[Attack Rolls|attack]] and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. Once it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.

***Wooden Ammunition.*** Wooden ammunition is destroyed after hitting or missing a target.
