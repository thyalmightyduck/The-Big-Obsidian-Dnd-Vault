# Dungeon Master’s Guide 2024 (DMG24)
#### +3 Explosive Cannonball
***Ammunition ([[Explosive Cannonball]]), Very Rare, 12 lb.***

---

*The *base item* can be found in Steinhardt’s Guide to the Eldritch Hunt Player Pack.*

You have a +3 bonus to [[Attack Rolls|attack]] and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. Once it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.

A creature hit by this ammunition takes the weapon's normal damage, and each creature within 10 feet of it must succeed on a Dexterity [[saving throw]] (DC 8 plus your Strength modifier and your [[Proficiency Bonus]]) or take 2d8 Fire damage. On a miss, the ammunition fails to detonate.

Once it hits a target, the ammunition is destroyed.
