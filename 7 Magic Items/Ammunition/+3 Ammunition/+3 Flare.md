# Dungeon Master’s Guide 2024 (DMG24)
#### +3 Flare
***Ammunition ([[Flare (Ammunition)|Flare]]), Very Rare, 1 lb.***

---

*The *base item* can be found in The Gunslinger Class: Valda’s Spire of Secrets.*

You have a +3 bonus to [[Attack Rolls|attack]] and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. Once it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.

Ammunition is required by a weapon that has Ammunition. A weapon's description specifies the type of ammunition used by the weapon.

Firearm ammunition is destroyed upon use.
