# Dungeon Master’s Guide 2024 (DMG24)
#### +3 Cannonball
***Ammunition ([[Cannonball]]), Very Rare, 10 lb.***

---

*The *base item* can be found in Steinhardt’s Guide to the Eldritch Hunt Player Pack.*

You have a +3 bonus to [[Attack Rolls|attack]] and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. Once it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.

A type of Ammunition for weapons with the Artillery property.

Ammunition is required by a weapon that has the Ammunition property. A weapon's description specifies the type of ammunition used by the weapon.

Firearm ammunition is destroyed upon use.
