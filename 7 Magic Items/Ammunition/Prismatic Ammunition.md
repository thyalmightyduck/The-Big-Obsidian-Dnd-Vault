#### Prismatic Ammunition

Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

This piece of ammunition is made of prismatic steel infused with the power of the Bifrost. When you hit a creature that is outside of its home plane with a ranged attack with this ammunition, it is banished to its home plane for 1 round.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Arrow* (*Prismatic Arrow*)
