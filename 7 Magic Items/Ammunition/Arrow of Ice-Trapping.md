# Northlands Worldbook (NWB)
#### Arrow of Ice-Trapping
Ammunition (*[[Arrow]]*), Rare, 0.8 oz.

---

This arrow deals an additional 1d6 Cold damage on a successful hit. If a creature takes damage from the ammunition, it must make a DC 15 Strength [[saving throw]]. On a failed save, the target has the [[Restrained|Restrained condition]] due to being trapped in bands of ice. It can make a save at the end of each of its turns to end the [[Restrained|Restrained condition]]. While it has the [[Restrained|Restrained condition]], the target takes 1d6 Cold damage at the start of each of its turns. The target or a creature within reach of it can take an [[action]] to make a DC 15 Strength ([[Athletics]]) check to free it.
