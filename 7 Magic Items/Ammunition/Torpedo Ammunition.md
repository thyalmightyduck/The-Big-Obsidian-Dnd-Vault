#### Torpedo Ammunition

Ammunition, Generic Variant, Common

---

This unique ammunition's spiraling design allows it to travel unimpaired through water. When firing the Arrow or Bolt underwater, you ignore the normal drawbacks of underwater combat. If you fire it while not underwater, the normal and long ranges for the attack are halved.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Arrow* (*Torpedo Arrow*)
- *Arrow, Silver* (*Torpedo Arrow, Silver*)
- *Arrow, Wooden* (*Torpedo Arrow, Wooden*)
- *Bolt* (*Torpedo Bolt*)
- *Bolt, Silver* (*Torpedo Bolt, Silver*)
- *Bolt, Wooden* (*Torpedo Bolt, Wooden*)
