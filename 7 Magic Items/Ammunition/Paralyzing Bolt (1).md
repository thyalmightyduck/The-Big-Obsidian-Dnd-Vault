#### Paralyzing Bolt

Ammunition, Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

These serpent tooth-tipped crossbow bolts cause a target's muscles to become rigid. When a creature takes damage from this bolt, it has the Paralyzed condition until the end of your next turn unless they succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. Once it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.
