#### Phoenix Ammunition

Ammunition, Generic Variant, Rare

---

This Arrow or Bolt is fletched with phoenix feathers and is magically bound to a small *Quiver* or *Crossbow Bolt Case*, respectively. You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. Once fired, the ammunition bursts into flames upon impact, dealing Fire damage on a hit instead of Piercing. At the start of your next turn, the ammunition leaves behind a small pile of ash and reappears in its container.

You can use a command word as a Bonus Action before firing the ammunition to magically set it ablaze. If you fire it on that same turn, the ammunition deals an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit and immediately turns to ash; it turns to ash before reaching its target if fired more than 30 feet away. The ammunition reappears in its container immediately after turning to ash.

You can store up to ten other Arrows or Bolts in the small *Quiver* or *Crossbow Bolt Case*, respectively, but can only have one piece of Phoenix Ammunition bound to it at a time. If the ammunition is ever destroyed, it reappears in its container after 24 hours unless it was destroyed while rendered nonmagical, such as by an *Antimagic Field*.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Arrow* (*Phoenix Arrow*)
- *Arrow, Silver* (*Phoenix Arrow, Silver*)
- *Arrow, Wooden* (*Phoenix Arrow, Wooden*)
- *Bolt* (*Phoenix Bolt*)
- *Bolt, Silver* (*Phoenix Bolt, Silver*)
- *Bolt, Wooden* (*Phoenix Bolt, Wooden*)
