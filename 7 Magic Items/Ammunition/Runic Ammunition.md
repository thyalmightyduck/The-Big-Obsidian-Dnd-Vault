#### Runic Ammunition

Ammunition, Generic Variant, Rare

---

This ammunition is etched with runes that are magically discordant to spellcasters. You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. When a creature that is Concentrating is hit by a piece of Runic Ammunition, that creature has Disadvantage on the saving throw it makes to maintain Concentration.

Immediately after the ammunition hits a target, it becomes nonmagical.
