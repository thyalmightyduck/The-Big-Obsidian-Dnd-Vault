#### Winged Ammunition

Ammunition, Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

Ranged weapon attack rolls made with this ammunition ignore half and three-quarters cover. In addition, attacking at long range doesn't impose disadvantage on ranged weapon attack rolls made with this ammunition.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Arrow* (*Winged Arrow*)
- *Arrow, Silver* (*Winged Arrow, Silver*)
- *Arrow, Wooden* (*Winged Arrow, Wooden*)
- *Bellows Cannister* (*Winged Bellows Cannister*)
- *Bolt* (*Winged Bolt*)
- *Bolt, Silver* (*Winged Bolt, Silver*)
- *Bolt, Wooden* (*Winged Bolt, Wooden*)
- *Bullet* (*Winged Bullet*)
- *Bullet, Silver* (*Winged Bullet, Silver*)
- *Bullet, Wooden* (*Winged Bullet, Wooden*)
- *Cannonball* (*Winged Cannonball*)
- *Cannonball* (*Winged Cannonball*)
- *Dragon Shot* (*Winged Dragon Shot*)
- *Explosive Cannonball* (*Winged Explosive Cannonball*)
- *Firearm Bullet* (*Winged Firearm Bullet*)
- *Flare* (*Winged Flare*)
- *Needle* (*Winged Needle*)
- *Needle, Silver* (*Winged Needle, Silver*)
- *Paper Cartridge Bullet* (*Winged Paper Cartridge Bullet*)
- *Shell* (*Winged Shell*)
- *Shot* (*Winged Shot*)
- *Sling Bullet* (*Winged Sling Bullet*)
