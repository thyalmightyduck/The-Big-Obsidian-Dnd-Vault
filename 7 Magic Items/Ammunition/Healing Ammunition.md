#### Healing Ammunition

Ammunition, Generic Variant, Uncommon

2d4

---

This magic ammunition is made by the elder Clerics of nature and hunting deities. Attack rolls made with this ammunition deal no damage on a hit. When you hit a creature other than yourself with the ammunition, the target regains Hit Points equal to 2d4 plus your Dexterity and Wisdom modifiers (minimum +1). If the target is an ally, you have Advantage on the attack roll. Once it hits a creature, the ammunition dissolves into pure healing magic before it's destroyed.
