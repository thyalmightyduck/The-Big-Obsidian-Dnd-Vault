#### Frostbitten Buckler

Shield (*Shield*), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This icy Shield has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. When a creature you can see hits you with a melee attack roll, you can expend 1 charge as a Reaction to deal 2d4 Cold damage to the attacker. After the attack, you gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to the Cold damage dealt.
