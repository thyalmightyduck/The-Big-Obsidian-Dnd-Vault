#### Shield of Marius Renathyr (Rare)

Shield (*Shield*), Rare (Requires Attunement), 6 lb.

AC +2

Fabled Heirloom

---

Crafted from polished gold and white enamel and adorned with an intricate design of a rose encircled by thorny vines, this Shield was once owned by Marius Renathyr, a knight-errant destined for a terrible fate.

While holding this Shield, you gain a +1 bonus to AC determined by the Shield's rarity, in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC, and you can use the following properties.

***Valorous Guard (Rare+).*** When a creature within 5 feet of you is hit by an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to add 2 + the Shield's bonus to the creature's AC, potentially causing the attack to miss. When a creature within 5 feet of you fails a Dexterity saving throw, you can take a Reaction to add 2 + the Shield's bonus to the creature's total, potentially causing it to succeed.

***Fabled Heirloom.*** Each Fabled Heirloom is a magic item that grows in strength as its attuned owner gains power. The rarity of each heirloom increases as the character grows in level, as shown on the Heirloom Rarity table. An heirloom's description details the properties it always has, as well as the properties it gains as it increases in rarity. The heirloom has all the properties of its rarity and lower.
