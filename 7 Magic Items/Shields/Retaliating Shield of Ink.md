#### Retaliating Shield of Ink

Shield (*Shield*), Uncommon, 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This Shield has a hidden compartment inside it that can fire thick squid ink at attacking creatures. When a Large or smaller creature within 5 feet of you misses you with an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to press a button on the Shield's handle, spraying ink from the front of the Shield. The attacker must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have the Blinded condition until the start of its next turn.

If the Shield is filled with a Vial of squid ink (worth 20+ GP), the effect uses a save DC of 13. If it's filled with a bottle of common Ink (worth 10 GP), it uses a save DC of 10 instead. The Shield can be used in this way up to three times before its ink needs to be refilled, which takes 1 minute.

This Shield is a mechanical item whose purpose and functionality can be discerned by taking a Study action to make a DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check. On a success, you understand what the Shield does, how to use it, and how to take care of it. You also understand that filling the Shield with other volatile or sticky liquids (such as acid, poison, grog, or oil) or any solid material (such as sand) will damage the machinery inside and take 8 hours of work to repair.
