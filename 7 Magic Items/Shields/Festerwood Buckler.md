#### Festerwood Buckler

Shield (*Shield*), Uncommon, 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This festerwood Shield is riddled with amber pustules that glow with a low, yellow light. While holding this magic Shield, the pustules emit Bright Light in a 5-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 5 feet. In addition, while you're holding the Shield, you can take a Reaction when a creature that you can see within 5 feet of you misses you with an attack roll to attempt to bash that creature with the Shield. When you do, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d4 + 1 Acid damage.
