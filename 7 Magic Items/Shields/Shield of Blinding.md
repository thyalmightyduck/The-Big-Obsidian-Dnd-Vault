#### Shield of Blinding

Shield (*Shield*), Uncommon, 6 lb.

AC +2

---

While holding this shield, you can speak its command word and choose one creature that you can see within range to make a Constitution saving throw. If it fails, the target is blinded for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target can make a new saving throw to regain their sight.
