#### Retaliating Bloom Shield

Shield (*Shield*), Rare, 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This steel Shield hides a slim chamber in its center where magical flora grow, accumulating toxic pollen. The chamber is covered with an intricate, interlocking series of metal plates and flaps that can be released, unfurling to a shape that resembles a flower.

While holding the Shield with its plates collapsed, you can take a Reaction when a Large or smaller creature you can see within 5 feet of you misses you with an attack roll to release the interlocked plates and fire the accumulated pollen at the attacker. When you do, that creature must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 3d10 Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk when the flowers accumulate another wave of pollen.

Curious, whirring mechanisms inside the Shield automatically retract the chamber's unfurled metallic plates at dusk. Alternatively, you can manually collapse the Shield's plates over the course of 1 minute.
