#### Couatl Herald's Guard

Shield (*Shield*), Uncommon (Requires Attunement By a Creature of Good Alignment), 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This magic Shield is made from yew and covered in durable couatl feathers. When a creature you can see within 10 feet of you takes damage, you can take a Reaction to magically take that damage instead of the target. When you do so, the damage type changes to Force. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

***Flight of the Couatls.*** While you're attuned to three items with this property, you gain a Fly Speed of 30 feet. If you already have a Fly Speed, it increases by 30 feet instead.
