#### Glamsyni Shield

Shield (*Shield*), Uncommon, 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This round metal shield is emblazoned with the glamsyni rune. While wielding it, you can use a reaction to teleport up to 10 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.
