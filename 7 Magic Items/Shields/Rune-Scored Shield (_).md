#### Rune-Scored Shield (*)

Generic Variant

---

** This generic variant has the same name and source as the item *rune-scored shield**.

- **Craftsmanship:** *Dwarven*

While holding this shield, you gain a +1 bonus to AC (in addition to the shield's normal bonus to AC) and saving throws.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Shield* (*Rune-Scored Shield*)
