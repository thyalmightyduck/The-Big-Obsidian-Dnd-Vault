#### Helmsman's Shelter

Shield (*Shield*), Rare (Requires Attunement), 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This magic Shield offers a degree of control over the seas. While holding this Shield, you have a +1 bonus to Armor Class. This bonus is in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC. While attuned to the Shield, you have a +1 bonus to any ability check you make to control a waterborne vehicle.

The Shield has 5 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. While holding the Shield, you can expend 3 charges to cast the *Control Water* spell (save DC 15) from it. Alternatively, you can take a Magic action and expend 1 charge to take control of a water vehicle you can see within 300 feet of you that has a rudder. If the vehicle has a creature currently controlling its rudder, it must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, you wrestle control away from the creature and can alter the direction of the vehicle by up to 90 degrees. You maintain control of the vehicle until the start of your next turn.
