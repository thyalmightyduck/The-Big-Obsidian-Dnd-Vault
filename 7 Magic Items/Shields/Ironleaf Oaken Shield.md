#### Ironleaf Oaken Shield

Shield (*Shield*), Uncommon (Requires Attunement By a Druid), 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This reinforced wooden Shield is covered with ironleaf vines that grant you extra protection. You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while holding it. This bonus is in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC. In addition, the Shield reforms itself to fit you whenever you transform into a Beast while holding it. When you do, the ironleaf vines and wooden Shield latch onto your bestial form, granting you a +1 bonus to AC. When you revert to your normal form, the Shield also reverts, donning itself upon your arm again.
