#### Starmetal Shield

Shield (*Shield*), Legendary (Requires Attunement), 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This open metal Shield hovers above its paired gauntlet, protecting you but leaving your hand free. While wearing the gauntlet, you are considered to be wielding the Shield, but you have a +1 bonus to Armor Class instead of a Shield's normal bonus to AC. Whenever you're targeted by an attack roll made with a metal weapon or projectile, this bonus becomes +3 instead.

***Shooting Star.*** While wearing the gauntlet, you can take a Magic action to throw the Shield, bouncing it between clustered targets before it returns to your arm. Choose up to three creatures, objects, or points. The first target must be within 30 feet of you, and any additional targets the Shield bounces toward must be within 10 feet of the target before it. You can't select the same target more than once each time you throw the Shield.

Make a ranged attack roll against each target. The targets gain no benefit from Half Cover or Three-Quarters Cover for the attack. You are proficient with these attacks and add either your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice) to their attack rolls and damage rolls. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Slashing damage. The Shield flies back to the gauntlet after reaching its final target. It bounces harmlessly off of any other creatures or objects in its path.
