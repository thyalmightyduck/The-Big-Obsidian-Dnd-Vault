#### Shield of the World Serpent

Shield (*Shield*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This shield is shaped from a scale of Jormungandr. While wielding the shield, you cannot be pushed or pulled against your will. At the start of each of your turns, you may end any Grappled and Restrained conditions on you.
