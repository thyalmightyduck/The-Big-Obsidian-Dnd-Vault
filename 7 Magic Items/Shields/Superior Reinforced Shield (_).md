#### Superior Reinforced Shield (*)

Generic Variant

---

** This generic variant has the same name and source as the item *superior reinforced shield**.

- **Craftsmanship:** *Any*
- **Special:** *Bane* (if Númenórean)

While holding this shield, you have a +1 bonus to AC. The bonus increases to +2 if the shield is of Dwarven or Elven craftsmanship, or to +3 against attacks from bane creatures (if Númenórean). This bonus is in addition to the shield's normal bonus to AC.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Shield* (*Superior Reinforced Shield*)
