#### Infernal Aegis

Shield (*Shield*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 6 lb.

AC +2

---

While holding this Shield, you can use a command word as a Bonus Action to cause it to become wreathed in harmless, magical flames. The flames shed Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet. The flames last until you repeat the command word as a Bonus Action or until you are no longer holding the Shield.

While holding the Shield, you have Resistance to Fire damage and a +1 bonus to Armor Class. This bonus is in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC. In addition, the Shield has 4 charges and regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn. While the Shield is ablaze, you can expend 1 or more charges to cast *Fire Shield* (2 charges, warm shield only) or Hellish Rebuke (1 charge, save DC 16) from it.
