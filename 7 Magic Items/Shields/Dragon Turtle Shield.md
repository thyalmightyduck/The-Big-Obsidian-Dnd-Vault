#### Dragon Turtle Shield

Shield (*Shield*), Rare, 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This Shield is made from a piece of reclaimed dragon turtle shell. The dragon turtle head sculpture adorning its front is made of brass, and it magically creates and heats a small reservoir of water that replenishes daily at dawn. When you take Fire damage while holding the Shield, you can take a Reaction to use a command word. When you do, the water inside it is superheated, reducing the Fire damage you take by half. The superheated water is released as a dense cloud of steam, causing the area in a 45-foot-diameter Emanation originating from you to be Heavily Obscured. The cloud remains stationary and lasts for 1 minute or until a strong wind (like that created by *Gust of Wind*) disperses it.

Once this property is used, the Shield's reservoir is empty and can't be used again in this way until the next dawn when it replenishes or until it's submerged in nonmagical salt water for 1 hour.
