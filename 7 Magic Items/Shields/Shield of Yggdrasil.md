#### Shield of Yggdrasil

Shield (*Shield*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 6 lb.

AC +2

---

This massive tower Shield requires a Strength score of 17 or higher in order to adequately wield it. The Shield is of dwarven make and functions as a small battlement, granting you a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wield it. This bonus is in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC.

***Shield Plant.*** As a Bonus Action, you can plant this Shield on the ground in your space to barricade yourself behind it, granting you Half Cover against ranged attacks from the direction you're facing. While wielding the planted Shield, your Speed is reduced to 5 feet and can't increase, and Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage you take from weapons and Unarmed Strikes is reduced by 3. You can uproot the Shield to end this effect as a Utilize action.

You can doff the Shield when planting it by taking a Magic action to do so instead of a Bonus Action. When you do, the Shield remains upright and in place, and it can be used as Half Cover by any creature standing directly behind it in the space it was left in. The Shield falls down if you have the Unconscious condition, or if a creature within 5 feet of it takes a Utilize action to knock it over with a successful DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check. You can move the Shield while it's planted by donning it again.

This Shield has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. You can expend 1 charge when you plant the Shield, or on a subsequent turn as a Bonus Action, to magically send out metal roots along the ground in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the Shield. The area is Difficult Terrain but it doesn't cost you or your allies extra movement. Moving the Shield causes the roots to shift and move with it. The roots remain until you uproot the Shield or it falls down.
