#### Reinforced Shield (*)

Generic Variant

---

** This generic variant has the same name and source as the item *reinforced shield**.

The shield's structure is reinforced, possibly with a metal rim or a larger iron boss, letting its wearer parry blows with greater ease. While holding this shield, you have a +1 bonus to AC. This bonus is in addition to the shield's normal bonus to AC.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Shield* (*Reinforced Shield*)
