#### Glass-Studded Armor

Light Armor (*Studded Leather Armor*), Rare (Requires Attunement), 13 lb.

AC 12 + Dex

---

While wearing this armor, you have Resistance to Radiant damage. Whenever a creature hits or misses you with a melee attack, it takes 3 Piercing damage. An attacker can choose to have Disadvantage on its attack roll to avoid this damage.
