#### Iridescent Armor

Medium Armor (*Breastplate*), Rare (Requires Attunement), 20 lb.

AC 14 + Dex (max 2)

---

This shimmering breastplate is forged from a giant dragonfly's carapace and adorned with a cape of gossamer fabric. You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wear this armor.

While wearing this armor, you can take an action to transform the cape into dragonfly wings for 1 hour. While this effect lasts, you have a  Fly Speed of 30 feet.

If you have reached at least Stage 1 of the Fey Transformation, the armor's effect lasts for 2 hours, during which time you have a  Fly Speed of 60 feet.

Once this effect is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
