#### Ancient Close Fitting Armour

Generic Variant

---

- **Craftsmanship:** *Elven, Dwarven*

You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armour. While you're wearing it, any critical hit against you becomes a normal hit.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Ancient Close Fitting Breastplate*)
