# [the_mithral_canval](https://www.instagram.com/the_mithral_canvas) (Homebrew)
#### Peripheral Hide
***Armor (Light or Medium), Rare (Requires [[Attunement]]***

- - - 

Space itself seems to bend around this armor, hiding the wearer even in plain sight. You gain a +1 bonus to AC against creatures who lack *[[Blindsight]]*, *Tremorsense*, or *[[Truesight]]*. Additionally, other creatures have [[disadvantage]] on Wisdom ([[Perception]]) checks made against you.

**Uncanny Cloaking:** You can take a [[Magic Action]] to gain the [[Invisible condition]] for 10 minutes or until you end it early on your (no [[action]] required). A creatures that is aware of you can make a DC 17 [[Perception]] check. On a success, the creature can see you normally for the next minute and you become aware of that creature. A creature with *[[Truesight]]* succeeds automatically. You can’t use this property again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Curse: Perception Hunter:** This armor is cursed, a fact that is revealed only when the *[[Identify]]* spell is cast on the armor or you attune to it. Attuning to the armor curses you until you are targeted by a *[[Remove Curse]]* spell or similar magic; removing the armor fails to end the curse.

Whenever a creature succeeds on a Wisdom ([[Perception]]) check made against you while you are hiding, you must make a DC 15 Wisdom [[Saving throw]]. On a failure, you are filled with a compulsion to hunt and kill the creature that perceived you. You must use your turn to move close enough to the creature to attack it until the target is reduced to 0 [[Hit Point|hit points]] or [[incapacitated]]. If another creature perceives you and fail your [[Saving Throw]] for that creature, it becomes the new target of your compulsion on a success. Once you succeed on your [[Saving Throw]], you are immune to this curse’s effects for the next 24 hours.
