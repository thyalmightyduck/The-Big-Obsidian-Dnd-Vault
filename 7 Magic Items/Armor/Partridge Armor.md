#### Partridge Armor

Generic Variant, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)

---

Partridge feathers and a scarlet hood give this predominantly dull-brown leather armour some brighter detailing. Intricate embossing around the waist tells the story of a lonely bird.

You have a +1 bonus to Armour Class while wearing this armour. Twice per day, when an enemy moves within 5 ft of you, you can fly up to 10 ft away from them in a flurry of feathers as a Reaction without provoking opportunity attacks.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Leather Armor* (*Partridge Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Partridge Padded Armor*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Partridge Studded Leather Armor*)
