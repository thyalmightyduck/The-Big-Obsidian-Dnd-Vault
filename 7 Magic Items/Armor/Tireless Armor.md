#### Tireless Armor

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This armor has **3 charges** and regains 1d3 charges daily at dawn. Each time you would receive a level of Exhaustion, you may spend a charge to negate that Exhaustion.

As long as the armor has at least 1 charge remaining, you cannot take penalties to your Speed.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Beinagrind* (*Tireless Beinagrind*)
- *Breastplate* (*Tireless Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Tireless Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Tireless Chain Shirt*)
- *Double Mail* (*Tireless Double Mail*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Tireless Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hardened Mail Shirt* (*Tireless Hardened Mail Shirt*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Tireless Hide Armor*)
- *Leather Armor* (*Tireless Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Tireless Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Tireless Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Tireless Ring Mail*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Tireless Scale Mail*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Tireless Splint Armor*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Tireless Studded Leather Armor*)
- *Walrus Hide* (*Tireless Walrus Hide*)
