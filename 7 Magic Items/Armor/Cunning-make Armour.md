#### Cunning-make Armour

Generic Variant, base weight ×0.5

---

A skilled craftsman has made this armour lighter or less cumbersome than its lesser counterparts. The armour is half the normal weight. If it normally imposes disadvantage on fatigue saving throws, this version doesn't. Otherwise, the maximum Dexterity modifier you can add to your AC when you wear it increases by 1.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Cunning-make Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Cunning-make Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Cunning-make Chain Shirt*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Cunning-make Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Cunning-make Hide Armor*)
- *Leather Armor* (*Cunning-make Leather Armor*)
- *Leather Corslet* (*Cunning-make Leather Corslet*)
- *Leather Shirt* (*Cunning-make Leather Shirt*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Cunning-make Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Cunning-make Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Cunning-make Ring Mail*)
- *Ring-Mail* (*Cunning-make Ring-Mail*)
- *Scale Armour* (*Cunning-make Scale Armour*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Cunning-make Scale Mail*)
- *Spiked Armor* (*Cunning-make Spiked Armor*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Cunning-make Splint Armor*)
