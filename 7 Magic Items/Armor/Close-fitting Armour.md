#### Close-fitting Armour

Generic Variant

---

A skilful smith has made this armour more difficult to overcome with critical hits. While you're wearing it, any critical hit against you becomes a normal hit.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Close-fitting Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Close-fitting Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Close-fitting Chain Shirt*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Close-fitting Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Close-fitting Hide Armor*)
- *Leather Armor* (*Close-fitting Leather Armor*)
- *Leather Corslet* (*Close-fitting Leather Corslet*)
- *Leather Shirt* (*Close-fitting Leather Shirt*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Close-fitting Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Close-fitting Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Close-fitting Ring Mail*)
- *Ring-Mail* (*Close-fitting Ring-Mail*)
- *Scale Armour* (*Close-fitting Scale Armour*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Close-fitting Scale Mail*)
- *Spiked Armor* (*Close-fitting Spiked Armor*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Close-fitting Splint Armor*)
