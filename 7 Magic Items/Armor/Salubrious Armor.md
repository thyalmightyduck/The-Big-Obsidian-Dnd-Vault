#### Salubrious Armor

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class. Whenever you regain Hit Points, the armor takes on a reddish tint, and this bonus increases to +2 until the end of your next turn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Plate Armor* (*Salubrious Plate Armor*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Salubrious Scale Mail*)
