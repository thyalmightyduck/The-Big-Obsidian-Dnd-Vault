#### Iced Steel Armor

Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

This suit of armor is made of iced steel, which legends hold originates in the frozen underworld of Niflheim. Objects made of iced steel always feel cold to the touch. While you are wearing it, you automatically succeed on saving throws against Extreme Heat, and you have Advantage on saving throws against spells involving fire and heat.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Beinagrind* (*Iced Steel Beinagrind*)
- *Breastplate* (*Iced Steel Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Iced Steel Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Iced Steel Chain Shirt*)
- *Double Mail* (*Iced Steel Double Mail*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Iced Steel Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hardened Mail Shirt* (*Iced Steel Hardened Mail Shirt*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Iced Steel Hide Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Iced Steel Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Iced Steel Ring Mail*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Iced Steel Scale Mail*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Iced Steel Splint Armor*)
- *Walrus Hide* (*Iced Steel Walrus Hide*)
