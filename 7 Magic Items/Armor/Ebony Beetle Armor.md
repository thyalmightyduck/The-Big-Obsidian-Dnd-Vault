#### Ebony Beetle Armor

Generic Variant, Rare

---

This glossy black armor is made from enchanted carrion beetle chitin by the **darakhul**. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the ebony beetle version of the armor doesn't. While wearing the armor, you are immune to slashing damage. It is half the weight of comparable metal armor.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Ring-Mail* (*Ebony Beetle Ring-Mail*)
- *Scale Armour* (*Ebony Beetle Scale Armour*)
- *Spiked Armor* (*Ebony Beetle Spiked Armor*)
