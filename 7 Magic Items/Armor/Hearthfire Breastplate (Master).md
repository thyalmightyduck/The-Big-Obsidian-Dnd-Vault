#### Hearthfire Breastplate (Master)

Medium Armor (*Breastplate*), Artifact (Requires Attunement)

---

The Hearthfire Breastplate has been profoundly transformed by its corruption. Once it was emblazoned with a golden sun and radiated light. Now its surface is black and distorted, wriggling as if the metal were alive.

***Aspirant.*** At this tier of Attunement, you have the following benefits while wearing the Hearthfire Breastplate:

- You can cast the *Shield* spell a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
- You gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class and saving throws.

You also gain the following curse:

- Though the breastplate isn't heavy, it feels burdensome, even when you aren't wearing it. You have Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.

***Adept.*** At the Adept tier of Attunement, you have the following additional benefits while wearing the Hearthfire Breastplate:

- Take a Magic action to choose up to six creatures you can see within 30 feet of you. Each creature gains 1d4 + 5 Temporary Hit Points. You can use this property a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
- Creatures other than you have a +2 bonus to Armor Class while they have Temporary Hit Points provided by the breastplate.

You also gain the following curse:

- You feel the pain of the companions you try to protect. When an ally within 60 feet takes damage that reduces their Temporary Hit Points, you take Psychic damage equal to half the number of Temporary Hit Points lost. This damage can't be reduced or avoided in any way.

***Master.*** While at the Master tier of Attunement, you have the following additional benefit while wearing the Hearthfire Breastplate:

- When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can take a Reaction to ward that creature. It has Immunity to all damage until the start of your next turn, including against the triggering damage. You can use this property once, regaining the ability to do so after you finish a Long Rest.

You also gain the following curse:

- Seeing your companions fall fills you with dreadful guilt. When an ally that you can see drops to 0 Hit Points, you have the Frightened condition for 1 minute. If the creature dies, you have the Paralyzed condition instead. At the end of each of your turns, you can make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw, ending the condition on a success.
