#### Venombane Armor

Generic Variant, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)

---

This armor absorbs poison and allows you to traverse webbed areas more easily. While you're wearing the armor, you can't be caught in webs of any sort and can move through webs as if they were Difficult Terrain. In addition, when you take Poison damage while wearing this armor, you can take a Reaction to reduce the damage by an amount equal to 1d8 plus your Constitution modifier.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Leather Armor* (*Venombane Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Venombane Padded Armor*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Venombane Studded Leather Armor*)
