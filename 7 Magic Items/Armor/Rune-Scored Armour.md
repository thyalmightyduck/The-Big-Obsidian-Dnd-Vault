#### Rune-Scored Armour

Generic Variant

---

- **Craftsmanship:** *Dwarven*

You gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws while you wear this armour.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Rune-Scored Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Rune-Scored Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Rune-Scored Chain Shirt*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Rune-Scored Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Rune-Scored Hide Armor*)
- *Leather Armor* (*Rune-Scored Leather Armor*)
- *Leather Corslet* (*Rune-Scored Leather Corslet*)
- *Leather Shirt* (*Rune-Scored Leather Shirt*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Rune-Scored Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Rune-Scored Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Rune-Scored Ring Mail*)
- *Ring-Mail* (*Rune-Scored Ring-Mail*)
- *Scale Armour* (*Rune-Scored Scale Armour*)

- *Spiked Armor* (*Rune-Scored Spiked Armor*)
