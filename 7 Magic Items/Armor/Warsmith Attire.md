> ##### Description
This ensemble is comprised of an armored apron and a pair of gloves. Attuning to multiple parts of the same ensemble counts as only on e attunement for you attunement slot limit.

When attuned and wearing all the elements of the ensemble, you gain the following benefits:

**Tempered Anvil:** The Apron’s *Anvil Magic* feature now allows you to empower two items with a +1 or one item with a +2.

**Raging Furnace:** The Gloves now hold 5 charges instead of 3 and they regain 1d4+1 charges each day at dawn. You can now expend 4 charges to cast the spell [[Fire Shield]] or [[Stoneskin]] on yourself, requiring no costly component.

**Fire & Steal:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals fire damage while holding a [[light hammer]], [[warhammer]] or [[maul]], it becomes overheated or the duration of the spell, or until the end of your next turn if its duration is instantaneous. Dealing an additional 1d4 fire damage on a hit.

## Warsmith Apron:
*Magic Breastplate, Uncommon, Requires Attunement*

**Forged-In-Fire:** While wearing this armor, the time needed to craft with smith’s tools is reduced by half. In addition, you can take 1 hour of your time to craft one of the following items, [[Ball Bearings]], [[Bucket]], [[Caltrops]], [[Grappling Hook]], [[Iron Pot]], [[Bell]], [[Shovel]], [[Tinderbox]], a [[Shield]], a Melee Weapon or 10 pieces of ammunition. Crafting a new item in this way destroys the previous one, as its magic subsides.

**Anvil Magic:** As an action, you can use your smith’s tools to empower a non-magical weapon, shield or armor made primarily of metal. The item provides a +1 to attack and damage rolls for weapons, or a +1 to armor class for the armor and shield. In addition, choose a skill. The item gives twice its bonus to skill checks made by its user involving that skill. This effect lasts until you finish a long rest or you use it no another item.

## Warsmith Gloves:
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon, Requires Attunement*

**Hammer Master:** While wearing these smithing gloves, you become proficient with light hammers, warhammers and mauls if you weren’t and their damage is doubled against constructs as well as any creatures or object made out of metal, and ignore their resistances or immunities to bludgeoning damage, if any.

**Furnace Magic:** This item holds 3 charges and regains 1d3 charges each day at dawn. You can expend charges to cast the following spells (DC14) at a level equal to the number of expended charges (max 3):
- [[Searing Smite]], [[Burning Hands]] (minimum 1 charge)
- [[Heat Metal]], [[Flame Blade]] (Minimum 2 charges)
- [[Elemental Weapon]] (3 Charges)
