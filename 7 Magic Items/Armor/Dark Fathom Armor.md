#### Dark Fathom Armor

Light Armor (*Studded Leather Armor*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 13 lb.

AC 12 + Dex

---

This dark, sea-soaked suit of armor smells of brine and is covered in eldritch runes. While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class and can breathe underwater.

In addition, while wearing the armor, you can cast the *Evard's Black Tentacles* spell (save DC 15) from it. When cast in this way, the spell targets a 15-foot square centered on you; it doesn't follow you when you move. This version of the spell doesn't require your Concentration, and you are immune to its effects. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.
