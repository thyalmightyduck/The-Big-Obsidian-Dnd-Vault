#### Quakescale Armor

Medium Armor (*Scale Mail*), Uncommon, 45 lb.

AC 14 + Dex (max 2)

---

Fashioned from the scales of a quake drake, this armor rumbles softly with the sound of thunder during storms. While wearing it, you have Resistance to Thunder damage.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
