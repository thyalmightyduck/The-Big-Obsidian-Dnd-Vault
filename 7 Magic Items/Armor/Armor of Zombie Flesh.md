#### Armor of Zombie Flesh

Light Armor (*Hide Armor*), Rare (Requires Attunement), 12 lb.

AC 14 + Dex

---

This armor is formed from the tanned flesh of a zombie.

While wearing this armor, your Armor Class becomes 14 + your Dexterity modifier. While attuned to this armor, when you take Necrotic damage, you can use a Reaction to halve that Necrotic damage (round down).
