#### Smoldering Armor

Generic Variant, Common

---

Wisps of harmless, odorless smoke rise from this armor while it is worn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Beinagrind* (*Smoldering Beinagrind*)
- *Breastplate* (*Smoldering Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Smoldering Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Smoldering Chain Shirt*)
- *Double Mail* (*Smoldering Double Mail*)

- *Hardened Mail Shirt* (*Smoldering Hardened Mail Shirt*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Smoldering Hide Armor*)
