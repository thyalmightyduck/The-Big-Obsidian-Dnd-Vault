#### Midnight Armor

Generic Variant, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

Spun of pure shadow and invisible in sunlight, armor of this type is typically of shadow goblin or derro construction. While wearing it, you gain a +1 bonus to AC and resistance to radiant damage. In addition, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Leather Corslet* (*Midnight Leather Corslet*)
- *Leather Shirt* (*Midnight Leather Shirt*)
