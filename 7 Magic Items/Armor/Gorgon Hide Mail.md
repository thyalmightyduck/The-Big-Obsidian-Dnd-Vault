#### Gorgon Hide Mail

Medium Armor (*Scale Mail*), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), 45 lb.

AC 14 + Dex (max 2)

---

This suit of *Scale Mail* is made from the metallic hide of a gorgon. It is particularly valuable to anyone that wishes to visit Cazhaak Draal; while wearing this armor, you have Advantage on saving throws against any magical effect that would cause you to be Restrained or Petrified.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
