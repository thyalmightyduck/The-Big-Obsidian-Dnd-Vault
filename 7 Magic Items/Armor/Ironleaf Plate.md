#### Ironleaf Plate

Heavy Armor (*Plate Armor*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 65 lb.

AC 18

---

You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while wearing this suit of armor, which is lined with ironleaf vines. These vines seem to support and quiet the armor's normally loud movements; it doesn't impose Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. In addition, the vines dampen the impact you receive from blunt force, granting you Resistance to Bludgeoning damage while you wear the armor.

If you take Fire damage while wearing the armor, the vines wither for 1 minute. For the duration, you gain none of their benefits.

***Mantle of the Wild.*** While wearing this armor, you can cast the *Pass without Trace* spell from it. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

If the wearer has a Strength score lower than 15, their speed is reduced by 10 feet.
