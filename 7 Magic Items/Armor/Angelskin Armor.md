#### Angelskin Armor

Light Armor (*Leather Armor*), Legendary (Requires Attunement), 10 lb.

AC 11 + Dex

---

This leather armor is made from the flayed flesh of angels.

While attuned to the armor, you have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage. Any Celestial that sees this armor immediately becomes Hostiles toward the wearer, and any Fiend wants to possess it at any cost.
