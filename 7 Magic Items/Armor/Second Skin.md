#### Second Skin

Light Armor (*Hide Armor*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

AC 14 + Dex

---

This harness is woven from the hides of slain lycanthropes. When you attune to this item, the harness melds into your flesh, making it tough. Your Armor Class is 14 + your Dexterity modifier. When you take Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage from a source that is not silver, you can use a Reaction to halve the damage (rounding down).

While you wear the harness, you have Disadvantage on Charisma checks to interact with lycanthropes.
