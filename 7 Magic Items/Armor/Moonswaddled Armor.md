#### Moonswaddled Armor

Generic Variant, Rare

---

This comfortable set of armor grants additional protection and respite when worn at night. You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while wearing this armor between dusk and dawn. When you would normally roll 1 or more Hit Point Dice to restore Hit Points when taking a Short Rest after dusk, you instead use the highest number possible for each Hit Die spent.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Leather Armor* (*Moonswaddled Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Moonswaddled Padded Armor*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Moonswaddled Studded Leather Armor*)
