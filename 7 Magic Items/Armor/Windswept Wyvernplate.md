#### Windswept Wyvernplate

Medium Armor (*Half Plate Armor*), Rare (Requires Attunement), 40 lb.

AC 15 + Dex (max 2)

---

This armor seems to grow lighter when you inhale. You can hold your breath at any point to both lighten the armor and quiet its clattering, negating the normal Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks imposed by Half Plate.

In addition, if you fall while wearing this armor, you can hold your breath as a Reaction to cast the *Feather Fall* spell, targeting only yourself.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
