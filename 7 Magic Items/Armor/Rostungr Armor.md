#### Rostungr Armor

Generic Variant, Very Rare

---

You have a +1 bonus to Armor Class while wearing this armor. You can take a Bonus Action to cause the armor to envelop you for 1 minute. You grow flippers and a thick layer of blubber, which remain for the duration. While in this state, your Speed is halved, you have Swim Speed of 30, and you have Resistance to Bludgeoning damage. Once this action is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Hide Armor* (*Rostungr Hide Armor*)
- *Leather Armor* (*Rostungr Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Rostungr Padded Armor*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Rostungr Studded Leather Armor*)
