#### Winter's Embrace

Medium Armor (*Breastplate*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 20 lb.

AC 14 + Dex (max 2)

---

This frosty armor is enchanted to keep its wearer at a constant, comfortable temperature. While wearing the armor, you have Resistance to Cold and Fire damage, and Difficult Terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost you extra movement.

In addition, while wearing the armor, you can cast the *Ice Storm* spell (level 6 version; save DC 16) from it. When cast in this way, the spell is centered on a point within 5 feet of you, and you are immune to its effects. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
