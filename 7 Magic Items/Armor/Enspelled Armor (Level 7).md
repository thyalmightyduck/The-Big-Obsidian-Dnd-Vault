#### Enspelled Armor (Level 7)

Generic Variant, Legendary (Requires Attunement)

---

Bound into this armor is a level 7 spell. The spell is determined when the armor is created and must belong to the Abjuration or Illusion school of magic. The armor has 6 charges and regains 1d6 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the armor, you can expend 1 charge to cast its spell.

The spell's saving throw DC is 18, and its attack bonus is +10.
