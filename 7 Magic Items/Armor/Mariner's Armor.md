#### Mariner's Armor

Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

While wearing this armor, you have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed. In addition, if you start your turn underwater with 0 Hit Points, you immediately regain 1d4 Hit Points. The armor can't heal anyone again until the next dawn.

The armor is decorated with fish and shell motifs.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Beinagrind* (*Mariner's Beinagrind*)
- *Breastplate* (*Mariner's Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Mariner's Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Mariner's Chain Shirt*)
- *Double Mail* (*Mariner's Double Mail*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Mariner's Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hardened Mail Shirt* (*Mariner's Hardened Mail Shirt*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Mariner's Hide Armor*)
- *Leather Armor* (*Mariner's Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Mariner's Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Mariner's Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Mariner's Ring Mail*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Mariner's Scale Mail*)

- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Mariner's Studded Leather Armor*)
- *Walrus Hide* (*Mariner's Walrus Hide*)
