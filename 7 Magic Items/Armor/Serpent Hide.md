#### Serpent Hide

Medium Armor (*Hide Armor*), Rare (Requires Attunement), 12 lb.

AC 12 + Dex (max 2)

---

While wearing this armor, you can speak its command word to take on the form of a **giant constrictor snake**. You retain this form as long as you like, but once you return to your normal form, you cannot use it again until after a long rest.
