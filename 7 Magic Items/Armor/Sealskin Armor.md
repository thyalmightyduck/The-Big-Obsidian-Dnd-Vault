#### Sealskin Armor

Generic Variant, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)

---

You have a Swim Speed equal to your normal Speed when wearing this armor. Also, you have Advantage on any checks and saves to avoid or end the Grappled condition.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Leather Armor* (*Sealskin Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Sealskin Padded Armor*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Sealskin Studded Leather Armor*)
