#### Moonstone Banded Armor

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

This armor is made of woven moonlight by the shadow goblins. It weighs half as much as comparable armor and always glows with a gentle radiance. While wearing this armor, you have advantage on saving throws against spells and effects involving radiant, cold, or necrotic damage.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Ring-Mail* (*Moonstone Banded Ring-Mail*)
