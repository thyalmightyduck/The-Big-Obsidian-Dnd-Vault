#### Wooden Chain of the Unbroken Circle

Medium Armor (*Chain Shirt*), Rare (Requires Attunement By a Druid or Ranger), 20 lb.

AC 13 + Dex (max 2)

---

This magic chain shirt is crafted using strange wooden links enchanted by a militant circle of Druids. The wooden chain is reinforced with old, natural magic that responds to the wearer's reverent connection to nature. While wearing this armor, you gain a bonus to Armor Class equal to your Wisdom modifier (maximum of +2). You are considered trained with this armor even if you lack training with Medium armor.
