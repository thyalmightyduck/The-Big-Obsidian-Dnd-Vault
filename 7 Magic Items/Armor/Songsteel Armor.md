#### Songsteel Armor

Generic Variant, Uncommon

---

Metal armor made of songsteel rings like a bell or chime when it clangs against an object. When wearing the armor, you can call out to Volund as a Bonus Action. Until the end of your next turn, you take 5 less damage from all attacks that hit you. Once you use this feature, you must finish a Long Rest before you can use it again.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Beinagrind* (*Songsteel Beinagrind*)
- *Breastplate* (*Songsteel Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Songsteel Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Songsteel Chain Shirt*)
- *Double Mail* (*Songsteel Double Mail*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Songsteel Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hardened Mail Shirt* (*Songsteel Hardened Mail Shirt*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Songsteel Hide Armor*)
- *Leather Armor* (*Songsteel Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Songsteel Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Songsteel Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Songsteel Ring Mail*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Songsteel Scale Mail*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Songsteel Splint Armor*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Songsteel Studded Leather Armor*)
- *Walrus Hide* (*Songsteel Walrus Hide*)
