#### Paragon's Plate

Heavy Armor (*Plate Armor*), Legendary (Requires Attunement), 65 lb.

AC 18

---

This ancient set of armor, which includes boots, greaves, a cuirass, gauntlets, and a helm, fully encases you in a shell of imposing steel, such that no part of your body is visible. While wearing this armor, you have Immunity to the Unconscious condition. If you have 0 Hit Points, you must still make Death Saving Throws, but you can continue to move and act.

If you die while wearing this armor, you can continue to move and act within it for up to 1 minute, though you lose the ability to speak and to cast or concentrate on spells.

***Curse.*** This armor is cursed, a fact revealed only when *Identify* is cast on the armor or you attune to it. While attuned to the armor, it is impossible to remove. Additionally, you automatically fail all Death Saving Throws as the armor's magic puts an immense strain on your body.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

If the wearer has a Strength score lower than 15, their speed is reduced by 10 feet.
