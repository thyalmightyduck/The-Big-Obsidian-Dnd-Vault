#### Prismatic Steel Armor

Generic Variant, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)

---

This suit of armor is made of prismatic steel infused with the power of the Bifrost. While you are wearing it, you automatically succeed on saving throws against effects that would push, pull, teleport, or otherwise move you against your will.

While attuned to the armor, you can take a Magic action to teleport the armor onto or off of your body, even if it is on another plane of existence from you. When you teleport the armor off of your body, it moves to an adjacent square. You can't use this effect again until the next dawn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Beinagrind* (*Prismatic Steel Beinagrind*)
- *Breastplate* (*Prismatic Steel Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Prismatic Steel Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Prismatic Steel Chain Shirt*)
- *Double Mail* (*Prismatic Steel Double Mail*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Prismatic Steel Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hardened Mail Shirt* (*Prismatic Steel Hardened Mail Shirt*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Prismatic Steel Hide Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Prismatic Steel Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Prismatic Steel Ring Mail*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Prismatic Steel Scale Mail*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Prismatic Steel Splint Armor*)
- *Walrus Hide* (*Prismatic Steel Walrus Hide*)
