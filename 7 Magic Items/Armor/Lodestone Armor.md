#### Lodestone Armor

Heavy Armor (*Plate Armor*), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 65 lb.

AC 18

---

This plate armor is lined with magnetic lodestone harvested from a different plane of existence. While wearing the armor, you can take a Bonus Action to enhance its magnetic polarity for 1 minute. Loose stones, soil, and minerals are immediately attracted to the surface of the armor, granting you a +1 bonus to Armor Class and reducing your Speed by 5 feet.

Using this ability while it already active increases the AC bonus by 1 and reduces your Speed by an additional 5 feet (to a maximum bonus of +4 and a maximum reduction of 20 feet). Once you reach this maximum effect, you can't activate the armor again until the duration has ended.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

If the wearer has a Strength score lower than 15, their speed is reduced by 10 feet.
