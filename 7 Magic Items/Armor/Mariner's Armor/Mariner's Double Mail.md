#### Mariner's Double Mail

Heavy Armor (*Double Mail*), Uncommon, 65 lb.

AC 17

---

*The *base item* can be found in Northlands Worldbook.*

While wearing this armor, you have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed. In addition, if you start your turn underwater with 0 Hit Points, you immediately regain 1d4 Hit Points. The armor can't heal anyone again until the next dawn.

The armor is decorated with fish and shell motifs.

Also called tvifold brynja, the rings of this chain mail are doubled, so each pair is passed through by four pairs of other rings. It is very heavy and bulky.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

If the wearer has a Strength score lower than 16, their speed is reduced by 10 feet.
