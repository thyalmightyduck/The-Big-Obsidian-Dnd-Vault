#### Breastplate of the Morning Light

Medium Armor (*Breastplate*), Rare (Requires Attunement By a Cleric or Paladin), 20 lb.

AC 14 + Dex (max 2)

---

While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class. You can also take a Magic action to cause the armor to emit a flash of blinding, radiant light. When you do, all other creatures within 30 feet of you must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn. Undead creatures have Disadvantage on this save and take 2d8 Radiant damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
