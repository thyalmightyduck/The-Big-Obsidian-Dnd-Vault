#### Pigskin Armor

Light Armor (*Hide Armor*), Uncommon, 12 lb.

AC 12 + Dex

---

This strong, flexible armor made from the hide of ogre swine is surprisingly supple. It is considered light armor rather than medium armor. When a Critical Hit is scored on you, roll 1d20. On a 17-20, you treat it as a hit instead.

However, while wearing the armor, you snore uncontrollably while sleeping, and your food intake doubles.
