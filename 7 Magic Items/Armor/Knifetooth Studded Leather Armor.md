#### Knifetooth Studded Leather Armor

Light Armor, Rare (Requires Attunement), Major Tier, 13 lb.

AC 12 + Dex

---

*This item has been created from Salvage text in the book; it may not be exactly as the author envisioned, some information is assumed.*

The tattered, scorched leathers of a **Knifetooth Firebrand** can become infused with magic. Cleaning and fitting them takes someone who has proficiency with *Leatherworker's Tools* 5 days and materials worth 250 GP. The finished armor is *Studded Leather Armor of Resistance (Fire)* that also confers a +1 bonus to AC.
