#### Ithjar Hide Armor

Medium Armor, 12 lb.

AC 13 + Dex (max 9999)

---

*This item has been created from Salvage text in the book; it may not be exactly as the author envisioned, some information is assumed.*

Popular superstition says wearing **Ithjar** teeth or hide imparts some of the creature's nimbleness to the wearer. This belief isn't wholly true, but someone who has proficiency with *Leatherworker's Tools* can make *Hide Armor* from the hide *that has a base AC of 13 and allows a wearer to apply their full Dexterity bonus to AC*. Creating this armor takes 10 days. Such *Hide Armor* fashioned from a stormborn ithjar is *Armor of Resistance (Lightning and Thunder)*. This armor takes no longer to make, but it requires other materials worth 2,000 GP.
