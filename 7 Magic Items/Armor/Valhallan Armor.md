#### Valhallan Armor

Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement)

---

While wearing this armor, you don't have the Unconscious condition when you are at 0 Hit Points. You still make Death Saving Throws normally. You can't use the Help action to make a Medicine check to stabilize yourself.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Beinagrind* (*Valhallan Beinagrind*)
- *Breastplate* (*Valhallan Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Valhallan Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Valhallan Chain Shirt*)
- *Double Mail* (*Valhallan Double Mail*)
- *Half Plate Armor* (*Valhallan Half Plate Armor*)
- *Hardened Mail Shirt* (*Valhallan Hardened Mail Shirt*)
- *Hide Armor* (*Valhallan Hide Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Valhallan Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Valhallan Ring Mail*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Valhallan Scale Mail*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Valhallan Splint Armor*)
- *Walrus Hide* (*Valhallan Walrus Hide*)
