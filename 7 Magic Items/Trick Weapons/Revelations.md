#### Revelations

Trick Weapon, Very Rare (Requires Attunement By a Creature with a Strength of 15 or Higher)

*Trick Weapon: (*Cleaver* & *Whip*)*

---

You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While attuned to it, your sight improves as your vision starts echoing with that of the blade. While holding Revelations, you gain the following benefits:

- You can't be Surprised.
- Other creatures don't gain Advantage on attack rolls against you as a result of being unseen by you.
- You are immune to the Blinded condition, as the sword absorbs the effect and closes two of its eyes instead, before regrowing them.

***Trick Weapon.*** As a Bonus Action, you can switch between the Transformed and Untransformed states of this weapon. In its Untransformed state, the weapon is a *Cleaver* studded with eyeballs. In its Transformed state, the weapon is a *Whip* composed of metal shards held together by a sinew of blood and flesh.

***Untransformed.*** When you hit a creature with this *Cleaver*, you curse them for the next hour. For the duration, you gain a +1 bonus to damage rolls made with the weapon against the target, attack rolls you make with the weapon against the cursed target score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20, and you have Advantage on any Wisdom (Perception or Survival) check you make to find the target. The curse ends early if you transform the weapon or if you have the Unconscious condition.

***Transformed.*** This *Whip* deals 1d6 Slashing damage on a hit, has the Versatile (1d8) property, but does not have the Finesse property. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with this weapon, you can attempt to crush them under the weight of the *Whip*. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC equals 8 plus your Strength modifier and your Proficiency Bonus) or take 1d6 Slashing damage and have the Prone condition.

***Curse: Devour Sight.*** While attuned to this weapon, your sight deteriorates rapidly. For each five days that pass, you gain a permanent -1 penalty to all Wisdom (Perception) checks made using your sight. When this penalty reaches -5, your eyes rot and melt out. You have the Blinded condition, as two new eyes appear on the blade.

The sword's curse is insidious: while you remain attuned to the weapon, you do not suffer the penalty to your Perception checks, and you do not realize that your sight is worsening. The blade's influence makes you perceive the world in a clearer light.
