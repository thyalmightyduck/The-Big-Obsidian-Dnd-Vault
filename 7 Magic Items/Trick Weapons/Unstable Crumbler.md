#### Unstable Crumbler

Trick Weapon, Rare (Requires Attunement)

*Trick Weapon: (*Cannon* & *Maul*)*

---

While holding this weapon, your attacks and spells deal double damage to structures, and Fire damage you take is reduced by an amount equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1).

***Trick Weapon.*** As a Bonus Action, you can switch between the Transformed and Untransformed states of this weapon. In its Untransformed state, this weapon is a roaring *Cannon* with smoke coming out of its mouth. In its Transformed state, the handle extends, turning the weapon into a highly unstable and destructive *Maul*. As part of this Bonus Action, you can do the following:

- When you transform the *Cannon* into a *Maul*, you can shoot at your feet, even if the *Cannon* isn't loaded, using the deflagration to propel yourself and jump up to 30 feet in any direction. This jump doesn't cost you any movement.
- When you transform the *Maul* into a *Cannon*, you can reload the *Cannon* and retain its unstable force. If you reload it in this way, the next time you hit with a ranged attack with the *Cannon* on your turn, you are pushed back 30 feet in a straight line.

***Untransformed.*** The Unstable Crumbler heats up *Cannonballs*, transforming them into infernal ammunition. Whenever you roll a 15 or higher on the d20 when making a ranged attack roll with this weapon, the *Cannonball* becomes overheated and explodes on impact. When the *Cannonball* explodes in this way, it is considered an *Explosive Cannonball*.

***Transformed.*** Whenever you roll an 18 or higher on the d20 when making an attack roll with this weapon, the *Maul*'s head detonates, causing an effect that acts as the *Fireball* spell (save DC 15) centered on the target. If you succeed on this saving throw, you take no damage.
