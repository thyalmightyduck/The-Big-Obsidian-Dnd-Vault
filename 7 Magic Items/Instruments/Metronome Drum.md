#### Metronome Drum

Wondrous Item, Instrument, Uncommon

---

This neatly painted *drum* is attached to a leather shoulder harness, so it can be struck at waist height. Local folklore suggests that this instrument can have an almost hypnotic effect on those in earshot, but only if played at the correct tempo.

You can play this *drum* as an Action. Once a day, up to three Humanoids within a 30 ft Emanation of the *drum* beat must succeed on a DC 14 saving throw or have the Charmed condition and become Friendly towards you. The targets make their saving throws with advantage if you or your allies are fighting them. The Charmed condition ends after 1 hour or when you stop playing the *drum*. When the Charmed condition ends, the targets know they were Charmed by you.

***Tool (Musical Instrument).*** If you have proficiency with a tool, add your Proficiency Bonus to any ability check you make that uses the tool. If you have proficiency in a skill that's used with that check, you have Advantage on the check too.
